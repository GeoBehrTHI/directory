## ABSTRACT

Heritage Preparatory School (Atlanta, GA, 30324) presents a comprehensive CLASSICAL profile across all six categories. The religious and metaphysical foundations are explicitly Christian, with Christ-centered formation, biblical worldview, and liturgical practices anchored to chapel life. The school’s purpose and goal emphasize formation—virtue, wisdom, character—over mere workforce preparation, aligning with the classical telos. The curriculum centers on the Trivium (Grammar, Logic, Rhetoric) and Great Books, supplemented by living books, recitation, and memory-based pedagogy, illustrating a robust liberal-arts orientation. Pedagogy blends contemplative, memory-focused lower-school practices with Socratic discourse, seminars, and rhetoric training in the upper grades, consistent with classical teaching methods. Faculty are characterized as model mentors and living curriculum, with small classes and strong ties to student development and family partnerships. Parent and community engagement is highlighted as a core aspect of Heritage’s model, including open visits, parent education, and family involvement.

Key sources corroborating these assessments include Heritage’s public materials describing Christ-centered formation, the five pillars of the school’s mission, Trivium-based pedagogy and Great Books orientation, and campus-life practices such as weekly chapel and family partnerships.
