# Augustine School (augustineschool.com) — Classicality Audit

URL used: https://www.augustineschool.com/

Executive Summary
- Overall classification: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL
- Why: The site explicitly centers truth, goodness, beauty; Christian theological grounding; prayerful community life; and partnership with parents—all strong classical signals. However, the public pages captured in the crawl do not yet make explicit enough claims about the Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric), Latin or classical languages, Great Books/primary texts, and upper‑school Socratic/rhetoric practices. Strengthening public curricular and pedagogy detail would likely move the school to a clear CLASSICAL rating.
- Highlights:
  - “Discover the Good, the True, and the Beautiful with Classical Christian Education” foregrounds transcendental moral realism (truth, goodness, beauty) as aims of formation .
  - “Biblically-Based,” “Gospel-Centered,” and submission of knowledge to the “Lordship of Christ” show theological integration; the community invites a “Season of Prayer” (liturgical/communal practice)  .
  - Mission language emphasizes “forming wise and virtuous young men and women” who “love goodness, truth, and beauty,” aligning with formation in wisdom and virtue over mere information transfer .
  - Clear parent partnership and community hospitality: “partner with families,” Mommy & Me reading events, Open House, Volunteer, Current Families resources    .
  - ACCS accreditation underscores classical identity, though our rating is based on published keyword evidence rather than affiliation alone .

Overall Assessment
- Aggregate rating: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL
- Rationale: Three categories (1, 2, 6) are clearly CLASSICAL by explicit language; three (3, 4, 5) are SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL due to missing explicit curricular and pedagogy specifics (Trivium/Latin/Great Books/Socratic/rhetoric orations/teacher‑as‑mentor statements) on the public pages captured in the crawl. Strengthening those pages should elevate the rating to CLASSICAL.

What would move the needle
- Curriculum page: Add explicit statements and examples naming “Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric),” “Quadrivium,” “Latin” (or other classical languages), “Great Books/primary sources,” “history (not social studies),” and “integration/wonder.” These are core positive indicators in the “Curriculum” element .
- Pedagogy page: Describe lower‑school “singing/chanting/memorization/recitation,” restful learning/scholé, and upper‑school “Socratic seminars, debates, rhetoric speeches/capstone theses.” These are the expected positive pedagogy indicators  .
- Faculty page: Publish a “Portrait of a Teacher” (model teacher/mentor; living curriculum; collegial faculty) to align with the Faculty element’s positive indicators .
- Community formation: Consider describing parent education offerings (book studies on Great Books/classical education basics) to further reinforce Category 6 positives (parent formation) .
