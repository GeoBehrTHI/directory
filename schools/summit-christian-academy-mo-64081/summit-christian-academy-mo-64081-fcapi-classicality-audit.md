## Executive Summary

Summit Christian Academy (Lee's Summit, MO, 64081) presents as a Christian private school with an explicit emphasis on academic excellence and godly character. Public-facing materials characterize the school as Kansas City’s largest accredited independent Christian school and highlight a mission rooted in faith-centered formation of students. However, the available public materials do not clearly articulate a classical curriculum or classical pedagogical program (e.g., the Trivium/Quadrivium, Great Books, or formal Socratic/seminar-based pedagogy). When mapped against the Six Essential Elements of a Classical School rubric, Summit Christian Academy exhibits limited classical credentials in curriculum content, pedagogy, and community practices, with modest alignment in the religious/metaphysical dimension and virtue-formation language. The aggregate assessment therefore lands in NOT CLASSICAL, with notable RED/ORANGE signals in two categories where foundational classical elements—deep engagement with the liberal arts, Great Books, and classical pedagogy—are not explicitly evidenced on public-facing materials.

Key evidentiary notes drawn from the public website materials and classical-education knowledge framework:

This religious framing is supported by homepage language calling SCA a Christian school and highlighting godly character (turn1file0). 
- There is no explicit public articulation of classical curriculum components (Great Books, Trivium/Quadrivium; Great Conversation; scholé; liturgical practices) in the visible pages. The available crawl content emphasizes recruitment and visitation (shadow-a-student tours, signage about joining) rather than curricular commitments (turn1file3).
