Overall Classification: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL

## ABSTRACT

Brilla Schools Network NY 10454 demonstrates a CLEAR but PARTIAL alignment with classical education. The school embeds a Trivium-oriented framework, liberal-arts curriculum, and a strong emphasis on character formation, consistent with a classical telos. Public materials show a robust parent partnership and a mission framed around formation and virtue, which strongly aligns with the classical model for Parents & Community (Element 6) and School Purpose & Goal (Element 2). The Curriculum category shows substantial classical content through Trivium-based structure, liberal-arts orientation, and CKLA/Wit and Wisdom, but does not consistently foreground Great Books/Great Conversation or canonical Latin/Greek study; thus it is categorized as SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL for Element 3. Pedagogy reveals a balance of rigorous, data-informed instruction and contemplative elements, but lacks explicit, sustained Socratic seminars across all grades in public materials, placing Element 4 as SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL. Faculty signals acknowledge teacher-centered implementation and external partnerships, but public articulation of a formal "living curriculum" or teacher portrait (Element 5) remains less explicit, leading to a SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL designation there. Religious/metaphysical foundations show virtue-centered formation and the classical framing, but explicit theology and liturgical practices are not clearly evidenced in public materials, yielding a SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL rating for Element 1. Overall, the aggregate posture lands in SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL, with meaningful strengths in mission, curriculum structure, and parent/community engagement, and opportunities to deepen canonical content, Socratic pedagogy, and explicit liturgical/theological signaling to move toward full CLASSICAL.
