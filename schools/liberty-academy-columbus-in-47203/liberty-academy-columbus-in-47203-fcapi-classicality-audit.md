## Executive Summary

Liberty Academy Columbus (Columbus, IN 47203) demonstrates broad and deliberate alignment with classical education ideals across all six rubric categories. Public materials emphasize a time-tested, virtue-centered classical curriculum (great books, liberal arts, Latin), teacher-centered pedagogy with Socratic discussion and memory-work, and a strong partnership with parents as co-educators. The school explicitly frames its mission as forming character and civic virtue, with education of the whole child (mind, body, spirit) and limited technology use. Parental involvement is actively cultivated through information sessions, open houses, and parent-practicum events, and the faculty are portrayed as moral exemplars and living curriculum. While the site notes its private, non-parochial status, it also situates its campus within a church setting and references the church in partnership language, which strengthens its religious/metaphysical underpinnings without implying parochial control. Overall, Liberty Academy Columbus is CLASSICAL across the six categories, with a robust, explicit classical program and strong parental partnership.
