## ABSTRACT

Liberty Academy Columbus (Columbus, IN 47203) presents a coherent CLASSICAL model across all six categories. The Religious and Metaphysical Foundations are evidenced by commitment to virtue and the true/good/beautiful telos, with church-contextual campus usage and Western-tradition framing. The School Purpose and Goal foreground formation and wisdom as the telos of education. The Curriculum centers on liberal arts—grammar, logic, rhetoric—alongside Great Books, Latin, and a historically grounded Western curriculum. Pedagogy emphasizes memory-work and contemplative routines in early grades, transitioning to Socratic discussion, seminars, and rhetoric in the upper grades. Faculty are portrayed as moral exemplars and living curriculum, and Parents & Community are actively integrated as partners and co-educators, with multiple events for parental formation and involvement. The school also notes its non-parochial status, though its campus is housed within a church, which adds a nuanced religious-context element to its classical framework. Overall, Liberty Academy Columbus demonstrates a robust CLASSICAL orientation across all six dimensions, with a well-articulated classical pedagogy and strong family partnerships that align with the Six Essential Elements of a Classical School.
