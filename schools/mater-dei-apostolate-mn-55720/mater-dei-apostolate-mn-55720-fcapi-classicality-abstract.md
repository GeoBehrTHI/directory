# ABSTRACT

Mater Dei Apostolate (Cloquet, MN, 55720) demonstrates a strong classical orientation within a Catholic formation framework. The school shows clear Religious and Metaphysical Foundations and a purpose focused on formation, virtue, and wisdom. Its Curricular orientation is classical in its partnership with Kolbe Academy, though explicit articulation of all classical curricular elements (Great Books, Seven Liberal Arts) is less evident in public materials. Pedagogy and Faculty show signs of classical practices (classical curriculum in use, qualified instructors, formation-advisor structure) but lack in publicly documented explicit pedagogical methodologies and living-curriculum storytelling. Parents & Community are a strength, with explicit parental partnership and the role of parents as primary educators.

Key takeaways:

- Strong Catholic identity, liturgical life, and Catholic intellectual tradition (Element 1).
- Formation-centered purpose with virtue and wisdom as central aims (Element 2).
- Classical curricular orientation via Kolbe Academy; available curriculum docs (Element 3).
- Pedagogy documented less explicitly; opportunities to document traditional classical methods (Element 4).
- Faculty presence evidenced by instructors and formation-advisors; living-curriculum aspects could be strengthened (Element 5).
- Strong parental partnership and family involvement (Element 6).
