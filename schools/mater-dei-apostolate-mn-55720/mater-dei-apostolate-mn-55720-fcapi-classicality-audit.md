## Executive Summary

Mater Dei Apostolate (Cloquet, MN, 55720) presents a clearly Catholic, mission-driven program that reaffirms classical education with a Catholic orientation. The school emphasizes formation of character through a Catholic intellectual tradition, daily liturgical life (Mass, Adoration, pilgrimages), and a commitment to making high-quality education affordable and accessible. The public materials show strong Religious and Metaphysical Foundations and a purposeful emphasis on formation over mere information, and they signal a genuine partnership with families (parents as primary educators). However, while the program proclaims classical aims (e.g., a classical curriculum and partnership with Kolbe Academy for the core curriculum), explicit references to core classical pedagogy elements such as explicit Great Books, the Seven Liberal Arts (Trivium/Quadrivium), or visible Socratic/Scholé-centric methods in the public materials are less pronounced than in established classical schools. Taken together, the school demonstrates substantial classical alignment, but certain hallmark pedagogical and curricular specifics are not as explicitly documented in the public materials as in the classical rubric’s strongest demonstrations.

# ABSTRACT

Mater Dei Apostolate (Cloquet, MN, 55720) presents a robust Catholic, formation-focused program with a strong public articulation of religious identity, liturgical life, and a classical curricular orientation via Kolbe Academy. The school’s materials clearly reflect Religious and Metaphysical Foundations and School Purpose/Goal, with explicit references to daily Mass, Adoration, pilgrimages, and a Catholic intellectual tradition anchoring the curriculum. The Curriculum is clearly classical in orientation through the Kolbe Academy link and Full Curriculum references; however, explicit mentions of Great Books, the Seven Liberal Arts, and detailed pedagogical methods (e.g., Socratic seminar, narrations, memory work) are less visible in the public materials, yielding a SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL rating for Curriculum and Pedagogy. Faculty is evidenced through instructor quality and formation-advisor structures, but lacks a fully explicit portrayal of a “living curriculum” faculty ethos in public content, again yielding SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL. Parents & Community are highly engaged, with explicit parent-as-educator framing and openness to parental involvement, firmly CLASSICAL.

Overall, Mater Dei Apostolate (Cloquet, MN, 55720) aligns with classical education in many respects, especially in its religious foundations, formation goals, and family partnership. The aggregate rating is SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL, with concrete steps available to move toward full CLASSICAL alignment (explicit Great Books/Trivium/Quadrivium articulation, explicit pedagogical methods, a more formalized living-curriculum faculty portrayal, and expanded parent education offerings).
