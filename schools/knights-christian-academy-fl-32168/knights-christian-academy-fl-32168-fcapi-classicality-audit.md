The curriculum and pedagogy show substantial classical elements (notably the Trivium framework and a biblically centered approach), while some aspects of pedagogy and curriculum indicators are less explicit (e.g., explicit Great Books emphasis). The school also communicates a strong partnership posture with families and open engagement with parents.

What would move the needle if the school sought to become fully CLASSICAL (minor refinements):
