## ABSTRACT

- Overall classification: CLASSICAL

- Executive summary: Headington Hill Classical Academy (HHCA) presents a robust classical Christian program that centers theological formation, Scholé/restful learning, liturgical pedagogy, a Trivium-based curriculum, and strong family engagement. The six-category analysis shows CLASSICAL signals in Religious & Metaphysical Foundations, School Purpose & Goal, Faculty, and Parents & Community, with strong but only partially explicit indicators in Curriculum and Pedagogy. The explicit Great Books/Great Conversation signals and a fully articulated Socratic pedagogy across all grade levels are less visible in public materials, which yields SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL marks for Curriculum and Pedagogy.
