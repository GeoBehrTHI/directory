## Abstract

Kawartha Classical Christian School demonstrates partial classical alignment, with strong religious/theological foundations and a formation-oriented purpose, coupled with a high-caliber faculty narrative. However, explicit curricular and pedagogical details central to classical education—especially the liberal arts framework (grammar, logic, rhetoric), Great Books/Great Conversation, and explicit Socratic pedagogy—are not clearly articulated in the public materials analyzed. Consequently, the category-by-category assessment yields CLASSICAL in Category 2 (School Purpose and Goal) and Category 5 (Faculty) but NOT CLASSICAL in Category 3 (Curriculum) and Category 4 (Pedagogy), with Category 1 (Religious and Metaphysical Foundations) and Category 6 (Parents & Community) landing as SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL. The aggregate classification for Kawartha Classical Christian School is SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL, with clear opportunities to move toward full CLASSICAL by publicly codifying curricular and pedagogical practices consistent with classical Christian education.

- Overall classification: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL
- Key strengths: Christ-centered mission; formation of virtue and truth/beauty; strong faculty emphasis; active parent engagement.
- Key gaps: explicit articulation of liberal arts curriculum, Great Books/Great Conversation, and formal classical pedagogy; expanded parent-formation programming.
