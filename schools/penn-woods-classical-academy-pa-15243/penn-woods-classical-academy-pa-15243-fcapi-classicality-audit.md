## Executive Summary

Penn Woods Classical Academy (PWCA) demonstrates a robust alignment with classical, Christian education as defined by the Classical School rubric in the knowledge base. The school foregrounds a Christ-centered, liberal-arts approach, emphasizes virtue and wisdom, and embeds classical curricular markers (Hillsdale-aligned curriculum, use of primary sources, emphasis on Western civilization and great works, inclusion of Latin). Public materials consistently articulate a formation-focused telos (true, good, beautiful) and promote partnerships with families and faith-integrated practices. While explicit, high-detail statements about certain pedagogy components (e.g., explicit Socratic seminars or scholé days) are less visible in the public materials, PWCA communicates core classical elements that position it firmly within the Classical spectrum.

Overall classification: CLASSICAL

Key observations

- Religious and metaphysical foundations are explicit and central: Christ-centered mission, Nicene Creed, Biblical literacy, corporate worship, and a shared theological framework (interdenominational, worship-centric culture) are repeatedly foregrounded. Evidence: “Christ-centered Liberal Arts education,” “Soli Deo Gloria,” Nicene Creed, Biblical literacy, prayer and communal worship. 
- Purpose and goal consistently emphasize formation, virtue, wisdom, and humane flourishing over mere job-readiness. Evidence: mission phrased as forming mind, heart, and spirit; emphasis on truth, goodness, beauty; aim for virtuous leaders within faith and community.
- Curriculum shows classical scaffolding: Hillsdale American Classical Curriculum, liberal arts balance, emphasis on primary sources, Western civilization, Great Works, and Latin. 
- Pedagogy contains notable classical signals (memory-wor k, reverence for wonder, primary-source-driven instruction) but specific explicit statements on some canonical practices (e.g., explicit Socratic seminars or scholé days) are less visible in public pages. This yields a robust but not exhaustively explicit Pedagogy profile in published materials. 
- Faculty are presented as trained, virtuous mentors aligned with Hillsdale’s classical approach; leadership and faculty profiles emphasize mission alignment, virtue, and living curriculum. 
- Parents & Community partnerships are foregrounded, with explicit language about co-teaching, parent formation, and accessible campus visits; ongoing parent engagement channels are evident (tours, open houses, podcasts).

What would move the needle

- If PWCA intends to move toward a fully explicit CLASSICAL Pedagogy in public materials, it could publish explicit statements on:
 - Socratic seminars and guided inquiry for upper grades, including sample prompts and assessment structures.
 - Explicit evidence of memory work, chant, and recitation practices (lower- and middle-school levels) and how they integrate with core subjects.
 - Clear articulation of a three-phase grammar-logic-rhetoric sequence with concrete examples of tasks for each phase.
 - Documentation of scholé/restful learning days and contemplation within the weekly schedule, if adopted.
