## ABSTRACT

Covenant Classical Christian School (SC 29204) earns a CLASSICAL rating across all six classical-education categories: Religious and Metaphysical Foundations; School Purpose and Goal; Curriculum; Pedagogy; Faculty; and Parents & Community. The school’s public-facing materials consistently articulate a Christ-centered, theologically integrated classical pedagogy that champions the liberal arts, the Trivium, and Great Books; a learning culture that blends contemplative (Scholé) and Socratic methods; and a living-mentors faculty model coupled with robust parental partnerships and community engagement. Evidence from the Covenant site confirms a deliberate alignment with the classical framework defined in the Six Essential Elements, including emphasis on truth, goodness, beauty; formation of virtue and wisdom; a seven-liberal-arts curriculum with Great Books and ancient/canonical texts; memory work and chant in early grades; Socratic seminars and rhetorical training in upper grades; and dedicated partnerships with families and churches. The overall impression is a mature, deeply classical program aimed at forming virtuous, wise, and capable disciples, scholars, and citizens for Christ and society.

Key sources informing this assessment include Covenant’s explicit Classical Methodology and curriculum (grammar, logic, rhetoric) and Great Books/Western Civilization components, as well as mission statements and admissions pages describing faculty as living exemplars and parents as co-educators.
