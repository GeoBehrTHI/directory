## Executive Summary

Evangel Classical School (Marysville/Mukilteo area, WA 98270) presents a robust classical Christian model centered on the Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric), integrated throughout the curriculum, with explicit emphasis on a Christian worldview, liturgical/hearty communal practices, and strong partnerships with families. Public materials consistently describe education as discipleship, a Christ-centered, philosophically coherent system, and a culture-building enterprise aimed at forming character and culture rather than merely transmitting information. The school frequently notes a commitment to classical practices (Latin study, Omnibus, great-books-style readings, Socratic or dialogic engagement in higher grades, and a rhetoric emphasis), while also highlighting family partnership, worship, and mentorship as core elements of its model. Overall, the school aligns with a CLASSICAL orientation across most categories, with Pedagogy representing the closest area to a potential needle move toward full classical alignment.

Key signals include: Christ-centered worldview in every subject; Trivium-based curriculum with Latin; Great-Books-oriented references (Herodotus, Plato, Homer, Shakespeare, etc.); a formal emphasis on worship, Matins, and a liturgical culture; small classes and mentorship as a core expectation; explicit partnership with families as co-educators; and a mission to carry Christ-honoring culture through education. ## What Would Move the Needle

- Pedagogy (Category 4) could move from SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL to CLASSICAL with targeted documentation of explicit Socratic seminars and formal Scholé-based routines across more grade levels (K–12). Concrete steps:
  - Publish explicit, school-wide documentation of regular Socratic seminars and Harkness-style discussions in upper grades, including sample prompts and assessment rubrics.
  - Provide a public outline of a restive Scholé calendar with intentional pacing and quiet study blocks embedded in the daily/weekly schedule.
  - Highlight explicit memorization/recitation routines and how they connect to the Great Books and primary texts on a grade-by-grade basis.

- Curriculum (Category 3) already aligns strongly; further strengthening Great Books integration could enhance CLASSICAL positioning:
  - Publish a formal “Great Books and Great Conversation” curriculum map that lists core canonical works per grade band, including primary-source reading lists, discussion prompts, and verbal/solicited student responses.
  - Clearly articulate Quadrivium components (arithmetic, geometry, music, astronomy) where applicable, or show cross-disciplinary experiences that reflect Quadrivium-style synthesis.

- Public-facing articulation of Scholé (Category 4) to align with the Lexicon:
  - Add explicit language about Scholé as a central practice, including the rationale for restful learning and contemplative time, in site sections describing pedagogy and daily routines.
