# Classicality Audit: Bethel Classical Academy

Executive Summary
- Overall classification: CLASSICAL
- School website: https://bethelclassical.org
- Top findings:
  - Strong, explicit Christ-centered telos and frequent invocation of truth, goodness, and beauty; biblical worldview prominently stated (e.g., “glorify God,” “God’s Truth,” Scripture citations)   .
  - Robust curriculum statements on the Trivium, Latin, logic, rhetoric, Western Canon, and integrated studies; “complete literary works instead of textbooks” noted .
  - Pedagogy shows discussion-based classes, logic and debate, and rhetorical formation; while “Socratic,” “scholé,” and “chanting” are not explicitly named, the practices are directionally aligned .
  - Faculty framed as Christian exemplars who “live out their faith and walk with Christ daily” .
  - Parent formation and community life are unusually strong (Parent Resources with Perrin videos, parent workshops, tours, information meetings)   .
- Notes on liturgy: Evidence of communal worship and sacred music through a “Christmas Service” and a “Spring concert,” with a dedicated Spiritual Life page emphasizing biblical literacy; daily chapel/liturgy practices are not explicitly listed  .

Method and scoring framework
- The audit uses the Positive/Negative Keyword Indicators from the Six Essential Elements of a Classical School lexicon as the scoring framework, with mandatory keyword matching in each category (e.g., truth, goodness, beauty, virtue, theology, Socratic, scholé, wonder, chanting, project-based, experiential learning)    .
- For each category: (1) keywords found on the school site are listed; (2) they are compared to the lexicon’s positive/negative indicators; (3) positive vs negative hits are quantified; (4) a rating is assigned.

CATEGORY 1: RELIGIOUS AND METAPHYSICAL FOUNDATIONS
1) Keywords and phrases found on the school’s site
- “Time-tested method … centered on truth, goodness, and beauty” (transcendentals) 
- “A classical and Christian education … in the service of God”; “worship God with our minds”; “Western Canon engaged from a biblical worldview” 
- Scripture-anchored formation (Proverbs, 2 Peter); “God’s Truth”; explicit moral formation and stewardship language 
- Spiritual Life: “biblically literate” 
- Communal/liturgical life evidence: “Christmas Service,” sacred music through concerts  
2) Comparison to Six Elements lexicon
- Positive matches: truth, goodness, beauty; virtue; wisdom; moral order; theology/Scripture; Christian worldview; reverence; sacred music/communal worship; formation of the soul; pursuit of wisdom (all listed as positive signals)  .
- Negative signals (e.g., values-neutral, moral relativism, secular humanism) not observed on-site .
3) Quantification
- Positive hits: +12 (truth, goodness, beauty, God/glorify God, biblical worldview, Scripture, wisdom, virtue, reverence/sacred music, spiritual formation, communal worship event, stewardship)
- Negative hits: -0
- percent_positive: 1.00
- percent_coverage: ~0.83 (strong on transcendental and theological grounding; less explicit on routine chapel/liturgical rhythm)
4) Rating: CLASSICAL

CATEGORY 4: SCHOOL PEDAGOGY OR TEACHING METHODS
1) Keywords and phrases found
- “Classes are discussion based … focus on cause and effect”; debate in 7th; formal logic and advanced rhetoric to communicate clearly and persuasively; students read complete works (seminar-friendly) 
2) Comparison to lexicon
- Positive matches: discussion/dialectic, debate, rhetoric/oratory; cultivation of love of learning and wonder (“natural curiosity”) aligns with contemplative/restful learning aims, though “scholé” is not explicit  .
- Explicit markers not found: the terms “Socratic,” “scholé,” “chanting/recitation,” “progymnasmata.”
- Negative signals (e.g., experiential-learning dominance, project-based-only, student-directed constructivism) not present .
3) Quantification
- Positive hits: +5 (discussion, debate, logic formation, rhetoric, integrated reading of complete texts)
- Negative hits: -0
- percent_positive: 1.00
- percent_coverage: ~0.55 (several classical pedagogical signals present; explicit “Socratic/scholé/chanting” terms absent)
4) Rating: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL (pedagogy is clearly moving in the classical direction; explicit classical pedagogical terminology could be strengthened)

Aggregate Assessment
- Category ratings: C1 CLASSICAL, C2 CLASSICAL, C3 CLASSICAL, C4 SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL, C5 CLASSICAL, C6 CLASSICAL.
- Overall: CLASSICAL. The only partial gap is in explicit pedagogical terminology (Socratic, scholé, chanting/recitation), though discussion, debate, rhetoric formation, and reading of complete works are strong and classically aligned . There is no marketing emphasis on “21st‑century skills,” “project‑based learning,” or “social studies” terminology that would signal drift per the lexicon’s negative indicators  .
