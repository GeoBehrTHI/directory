Overall Classification: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL

## ABSTRACT

Brilla College Prep Middle School presents a distinctly virtue-centered mission with a stated commitment to academic rigor and college-readiness. However, its publicly surfaced content largely emphasizes character formation and access rather than the full spectrum of classical education’s six elements. Category-by-category analysis yields two SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL signals (Religious and Metaphysical Foundations; School Purpose and Goal) and four NOT CLASSICAL signals (Curriculum, Pedagogy, Faculty, Parents & Community). The aggregate classification is SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL.

- Faculty portrayal and parent/community partnerships as presented do not yet reflect the “living curriculum” or co-teaching dynamics typical of a classical school (Element 5 and Element 6).
