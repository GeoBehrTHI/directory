## ABSTRACT

Providence Christian Academy in 46311 demonstrates a CLASSICAL orientation across most facets of its public-facing materials. The school’s religious/metaphysical foundations are explicitly Reformed and biblically grounded, with liturgical practices and a mission to pursue truth, goodness, and beauty. The curriculum emphasizes liberal arts, primary texts, Great Books-style engagement, memory work, Latin, and a Trivium-based progression, aligning strongly with classical pedagogy. The pedagogy itself features memory-based learning in the grammar years and Socratic/seminar-based, rhetoric-enriched instruction in the upper grades. Community and parental partnership are strong features, with explicit calls for parents to be co-educators and to participate in the ethos of classical education.

Aggregate rating: CLASSICAL.

The school would move further toward FULL CLASSICAL by explicitly codifying and communicating a formal “faculty as living curriculum” framework and by expanding explicit faculty portraits and norms, joined with enhanced public articulation of teacher formation and collegial development. This combination would round out the Faculty signal while preserving the strong CLASSICAL profile.
