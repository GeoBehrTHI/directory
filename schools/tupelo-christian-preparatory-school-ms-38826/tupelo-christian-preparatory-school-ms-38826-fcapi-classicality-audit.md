# Executive Summary

Overall classification: CLASSICAL

Austin Classical School (https://austinclassical.org) exhibits clear alignment with classical Christian education across all six elements. The school’s public materials emphasize transcendental moral realism (truth, goodness, beauty), theological grounding in Scripture, a Trivium-centered curriculum with Latin, logic, and rhetoric, Socratic and memory-based pedagogy, and a deep partnership with parents via a collaborative “co-teacher” model. Evidence for liturgical practices is indirect (e.g., Christian Studies and rhetoric choir) but robust theological integration and Scripture-centered aims more than compensate. Faculty pages describe a joyful, grace-filled ethos and strong teacher–parent collaboration, though an explicit “portrait of a teacher”/“living curriculum” statement is not prominent.

Aggregate rating: CLASSICAL

Key highlights
- Purpose and Goal: Repeated insistence on cultivating “wisdom and virtue” through contemplation of “Truth, Goodness, and Beauty” fits the classical telos and language exactly .
- Curriculum: Trivium structure, Latin (through Level IV), formal logic, rhetoric with senior thesis and oral defense, and primary sources/Socratic discussion are explicit and detailed    .
- Pedagogy: Memorization, recitation, songs (Grammar stage); debate/discussion (Logic stage); Socratic method and rhetoric training (Rhetoric stage) are all specified  .
- Parents & Community: The collaborative schedule formally makes parents “co-teachers,” with weekly plans from faculty and teacher office hours to support the home days  .
