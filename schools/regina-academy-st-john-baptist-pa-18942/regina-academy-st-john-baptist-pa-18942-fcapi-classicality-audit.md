## Executive Summary

Regina Academy at St. John the Baptist (RASJB) exhibits a robust CLASSICAL Catholic orientation across all six rubric categories. The public materials clearly foreground Catholic formation, virtue, and a theologically grounded worldview; a pedagogy that blends contemplative formation with Socratic inquiry; a curriculum anchored in the grammar–logic–rhetoric progression and exposure to Western thought through primary sources and classical methods; a faculty described as virtuous, formative mentors who embody the learning they teach; and an active partnership with parents and a faith-centered community that welcomes family involvement. Across sources, the school emphasizes truth, goodness, beauty, and a unified vision of education as formation of the whole person in Catholic truth. This alignment is evident in statements about Catholic truth and gospel formation, the stages of classical education, Socratic and rhetoric-focused pedagogy, and explicit calls for parent partnership and community formation. Overall, the school earns a clear CLASSICAL rating across all six categories.

- Element 1: Religious and Metaphysical Foundations — CLASSICAL. Evidence of Catholic truth, liturgical life, Gospel witness, theology, and formation rooted in Catholic anthropology and a supernatural vision.   
- Element 2: Purpose and Goal — CLASSICAL. Emphasis on formation of virtue and wisdom, soul formation, and knowing God, not merely career preparation.  
- Element 3: Curriculum — CLASSICAL. Clear articulation of the grammar–logic–rhetoric progression and engagement with Western intellectual tradition; Montessori to upper-school progression aligned with Great Books/Great Conversation sensibilities in practice.  
- Element 4: Pedagogy — CLASSICAL. Evidence of contemplative/restful emphasis where appropriate, plus lower-school singing/recitation, and upper-school Socratic/seminar-based work and rhetoric training.  
- Element 5: Faculty — CLASSICAL. Faculty described as virtuous, living curriculum, and mentors; bios affirm a Catholic, formation-minded teaching staff.
