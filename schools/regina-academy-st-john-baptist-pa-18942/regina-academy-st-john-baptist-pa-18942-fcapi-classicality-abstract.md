## ABSTRACT

Regina Academy at St. John the Baptist (RASJB) demonstrates a cohesive CLASSICAL Catholic education model across all six rubric categories: Religious and Metaphysical Foundations; School Purpose and Goal; Curriculum; Pedagogy; Faculty; and Parents & Community. Public materials emphasize Catholic formation, truth/goodness/beauty, and a supernatural vision; formation of virtue and wisdom as the school’s goal; a curriculum built on the grammar–logic–rhetoric progression with exposure to Western thought and primary sources; pedagogies that combine contemplative routines with Socratic inquiry and rhetoric training; faculty as living exemplars of virtue and formation; and a strong, faith-centered partnership with parents and community members. The keyword analysis, grounded in the Six Essential Elements, shows abundant positive indicators and no meaningful negative indicators across all categories, supporting a robust CLASSICAL classification. The school consistently frames education as formation in truth, goodness, and beauty within a Catholic anthropology, with a unified vision of educating the whole person in faith and reason.
