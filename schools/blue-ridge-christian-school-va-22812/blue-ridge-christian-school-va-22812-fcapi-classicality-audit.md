# Classicality Audit: Hillsdale Academy

School website used: https://academy.hillsdale.edu

## Executive Summary
Overall classification: CLASSICAL

Why: Hillsdale Academy’s public materials consistently use the core classical lexicon around truth, goodness, beauty; virtue and wisdom; a liberal arts curriculum (including Latin, logic, rhetoric); Socratic dialogue and primary sources; chapel, prayer, and liturgical habits; teacher mentorship; and explicit parent partnership. Evidence appears across mission, academics, and “Faith & Community” pages and aligns with the six-element framework and keyword indicators in Six Essential Elements of a Classical School (1)  . The site explicitly affirms “objective standards of truth, goodness, and beauty,” requires Latin, names logic and rhetoric in the Upper School program, and highlights Socratic dialogue, recitation, capstone theses with public defense, and chapel and corporate prayer  .

Most important insights:
- Religious foundations are explicit and lived (daily prayers, weekly chapel) alongside a robust affirmation of objective truth, goodness, and beauty  .
- Purpose centers on the cultivation of virtue and wisdom, not job preparation; the headmaster states education is about “more than just preparing for college or getting a good job” .
- Curriculum and pedagogy clearly include the Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric), Latin, Great Books/primary sources, Socratic dialogue, recitation/memorization, and capstone defenses that train rhetoric in practice .
- Strong parent partnership: “parents are the primary educators,” with invitations to tour and observe classrooms  .

Scoring summary by category
- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: CLASSICAL

- Curriculum: CLASSICAL
- Pedagogy: CLASSICAL
- Faculty: CLASSICAL
- Parents & Community: CLASSICAL
