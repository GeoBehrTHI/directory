## Executive Summary

Providence Classical Christian School (Oxford, GA 30054) presents a robust classical Christian education as reflected in its public materials. The school consistently foregrounds formation of character, virtue, and a Christian teleology, while embedding classical pedagogy through references to paideia, the trivium, the study of Latin/Greek, and Great Books. Public-facing content emphasizes a theology-centered framework, liturgical/catechetical elements, and an integrated curriculum that seeks the true, the good, and the beautiful. Overall, the school clearly aligns with traditional classical criteria across the rubric’s six domains, and its accountably public materials strongly support a CLASSICAL designation rather than a merely SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL or NOT CLASSICAL one.

Key takeaways:
- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations are explicit and pervasive, anchored by a Christ-centered framework, theology, liturgical rhythms, and references to the Logos and sacred tradition.
- Purpose and Goal emphasize formation, virtue, wisdom, and teleology over mere information or job-readiness.
- Curriculum centers on the liberal arts (grammar, logic, rhetoric), Great Books/historic engagement, and the Great Conversation, with explicit attention to Latin/Greek.
- Pedagogy is clearly classical: contemplative (Scholé) elements balanced with Socratic/seminar-style discourse and memorization/recitation.
- Faculty is presented as model teachers and mentors, with an emphasis on the living curriculum and teacher-student relationship.
- Parents & Community are positioned as partners in the classical project, with explicit resources for parental formation and open campus/community engagement.
