## ABSTRACT

New Braunfels Christian Academy (NBCA) was evaluated against the classical education rubric using two vector stores: a knowledge-base of classical education criteria (the Six Essential Elements, with associated positive/negative keyword indicators) and a school-specific crawl containing NBCA’s public-facing website content. The NBCA public material surfaced in the crawl is severely limited and primarily yields a 404 page for a path associated with “The Classical School,” with no substantive text about curriculum, pedagogy, theology, community practices, or parent partnerships. Consequently, across all six categories (Religious and Metaphysical Foundations; Purpose and Goal; Curriculum; Pedagogy; Faculty; Parents & Community) NBCA received a NOT CLASSICAL rating due to the absence of explicit classical markers in the public-facing content.

Key evidence:
- NBCA crawl shows a 404 page for the classical-school content, with no accessible material to demonstrate classical elements. 
- The classical rubric and keyword indicators define clear positive signals (truth/goodness/beauty, theology, liturgy, Great Books, Great Conversation, scholé, Socratic pedagogy, rhetoric, etc.) and negative signals (values-neutral education, project-based dominance, etc.). NBCA’s public crawl does not surface these signals, yielding 0 positive and 0 negative keyword matches in the accessible material.  

Category-by-category wiring (summary):
- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: NOT CLASSICAL — no public signals of transcendental realism, theology, or liturgical/community practices. Evidence limited to a 404 page. 
- School Purpose and Goal: NOT CLASSICAL — no explicit formation-centered purpose visible. 
- Curriculum: NOT CLASSICAL — no visible Great Books, liberal arts, or Trivium/Quadrivium content. 
- Pedagogy: NOT CLASSICAL — no public Socratic/seminar/Scholé indicators. 
- Faculty: NOT CLASSICAL — no public portrait or living-curriculum statements about faculty as core. 
- Parents & Community: NOT CLASSICAL — no public statements about parent co-teaching, formation, or open campus visits. 

Aggregate: NOT CLASSICAL

Notes on sources:
- The Six Essential Elements of a Classical School (1) provides the canonical category criteria and the Positive/Negative keyword indicators used to score each category. 
- The keyword lexicon for classical education (positive indicators per category, including transcendental realism, theology, scholé, Great Books, etc.) informs the quantitative scoring framework used in each category. 
- NBCA public crawl content used for evaluation is the accessible text surfaced by the site’s Classical School page, which in this case is a 404 page with no substantive classical content.
