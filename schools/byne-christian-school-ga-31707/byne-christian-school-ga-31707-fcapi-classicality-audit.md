## Executive Summary

Byne Christian School (Albany, GA 31707) presents a compelling CLASSICAL alignment across all six classical-school dimensions. Public materials describe a Christian, Trivium-based curriculum with explicit emphasis on formation, virtue, and wisdom, supported by a faculty described as virtuous and mission-driven, and a strong partnership with families/parents. The school foregrounds Christ-centered formation, the centrality of the Bible, and the Trivium (Grammar, Logic, Rhetoric) as the core of its pedagogy and curriculum. Evidence from the crawl of Byne’s public materials shows explicit statements about Christian formation, the Trivium, Socratic/seminar-style engagements in upper grades, and a continuous emphasis on the integration of faith and learning. The positive keywords associated with religious formation, virtue, and classical method are widely present, while negative signals (as defined by the classical rubric) are not evident in the public-facing content. Overall, Byne Christian School is classified as CLASSICAL, with particularly strong signals in Religious Foundations, Purpose/Goal, Pedagogy, Faculty, and Parents/Community; Curriculum demonstrates strong alignment as well, with some room to further emphasize Great Books/Great Conversation as described in the rubric.
