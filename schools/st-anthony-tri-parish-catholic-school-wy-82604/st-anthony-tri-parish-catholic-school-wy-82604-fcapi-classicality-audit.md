## Executive Summary

St Anthony Tri Parish Catholic School WY 82604 appears, in the crawl content provided, to be a school that aligns strongly with classical Christian education principles, though the public-facing materials center on Lutheran confessional education rather than Catholic identity. The site emphasizes formation over information, a liturgical and communal life, a catechetical and gospel-centered curriculum, and a Trivium/Quadrivium-informed structure, all within a confessional Lutheran framework. This combination places the school in the CLASSICAL category across the rubric, with clear strengths in religious foundations, formation-oriented aims, liberal-arts curriculum, traditional pedagogy, engaged faculty, and a model of partnership with parents and church communities.

Key evidence in favor of CLASSICAL alignment includes: explicit statements that the school is a “Premier Classical Lutheran Education” that values the Word of God, Lutheran Confessions, classical languages, and great literature, and that the school seeks to form minds (not merely fill them) while cultivating virtues (Philosophy section) . The accreditation material further anchors this in a liturgical and catechetical framework, noting that worship life uses historic liturgy and hymnody and that theology informs and unites instructional programs, with a confessional commitment shaping faculty and the curriculum . The curriculum explicitly centers religion on Scripture (Bible as the heart of the curriculum, chapel as daily practice), and incorporates classical elements (Latin, logic, rhetoric; the Trivium and Quadrivium) across grade levels, including upper-level rhetoric and dialectical training . Pedagogical practice includes contemplative/scholé-oriented approaches, as well as singing, memorization, and a strong upper-school emphasis on Socratic/dialogic forms and liturgical musical training (Choir program) . Faculty and leadership are presented as models of virtue and as living curriculum within a cooperative, confessional Lutheran community, with a clearly articulated partnership with families and an open-campus posture for parent involvement . Collectively, these signals align with the classical-school rubric’s core dimensions.
