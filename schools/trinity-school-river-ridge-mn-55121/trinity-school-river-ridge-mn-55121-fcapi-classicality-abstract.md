## ABSTRACT

Trinity School at River Ridge (River Ridge campus) demonstrates a CLASSICAL orientation across all six rubric categories. The religious/metaphysical foundations are explicitly Christian and the school operates within a theologically grounded, liturgical culture (e.g., Nicene Creed alignment; Nine Lessons and Carols). The school’s purpose emphasizes formation, virtue, and the cultivation of truth, goodness, and beauty; curriculum evidences a liberal-arts, Great-Books approach with humane-letters seminars and canonical texts. Pedagogy aligns with contemplative/restful learning in early stages, dialogic/ Socratic-style seminars in upper levels (as reflected by humane-letters pedagogy and Adler/Paideia references), and ongoing faculty development that positions teachers as exemplars and mentors. Faculty are described as the living curriculum and core to the school, with explicit attention to teacher training and student coaching. Parent and community engagement is robust, featuring formal partnerships (PSA), tours, book groups, and frequent events inviting parental involvement.

Keyword analysis across six categories shows presence of core Classical indicators: transcendental truths (truth, goodness, beauty), theology, liturgy, Great Books, Socratic/seminar pedagogy, formation, living curriculum, and active parent partnership. The school’s public materials consistently reflect these terms and concepts, aligning with the Positive Keyword indicators of the Six Essential Elements lexicon and the Classicality lexicon. The aggregate evaluation is CLASSICAL.

Cited sources include: the school’s Christian/Creed framing and liturgical activities; statements about formation and teleology; humane-letters and Great-Books-oriented curriculum (including the 12th-grade Humane Letters Seminar); Adler/Paideia pedagogy references; faculty training and “living curriculum” language; and family/PSA involvement and open-campus messaging.
