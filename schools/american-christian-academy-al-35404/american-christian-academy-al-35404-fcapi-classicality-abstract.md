## ABSTRACT

This evaluation of American Christian Academy (ACA) in Tuscaloosa, AL (35404) applies the classical-school rubric to six categories using the classical keyword framework from the Six Essential Elements of a Classical School (1). Across the six categories, ACA demonstrates strong CLASSICAL signals in Religious and Metaphysical Foundations, School Purpose & Goal, Faculty, and Parents & Community, with robust evidence of a Biblical worldview, formation-focused aims, virtuous teaching staff, and active parental partnership. Evidence includes the Biblical Worldview materials, daily scripture/prayer practices, mission statements emphasizing partnership with parents, and public descriptions of teachers as called to service and as a living curriculum.  

CATEGORY SUMMARIES (KEYWORDS AND RATINGS)
- Element 1 (Religious and Metaphysical Foundations) — CLASSICAL
  - Evidence: Biblical Worldview, Word of God, scripture, prayer, biblical instruction through grade levels. Positive keyword matches far exceed negative ones; no visible relativism. 
  - Rationale: Strong theological grounding, liturgical/communal practices, and a transcendent moral framework align with CLASSICAL foundations.

- Element 2 (School Purpose and Goal) — CLASSICAL
  - Evidence: Formation-focused language (life preparation, Christian character), partnership with families, Biblical worldview framing. Limited vocational emphasis visible publicly. 
  - Rationale: Formation and virtue-centric purpose align with classical aims, with college prep present as a pathway rather than the sole goal.

- Element 3 (Curriculum) — NOT CLASSICAL
  - Evidence: Curriculum described as faith-integrated and college-preparatory; lack of explicit Great Books, Trivium/Quadrivium, or canonical classical elements in public-facing documents. 
  - Rationale: Absence of explicit classical curriculum components (Great Books, Seven Liberal Arts, etc.) suggests NOT CLASSICAL in this domain.

- Element 4 (Pedagogy or Teaching Methods) — NOT CLASSICAL
  - Evidence: Bible-centered pedagogy and memory verses noted; limited documentation of Socratic seminars, rhetoric training, or scholé-style restful learning in public materials. 
  - Rationale: The public materials do not demonstrate the core classical pedagogy (Socratic discourse, formal rhetoric, or structured memory work) required for CLASSICAL pedagogy.

- Element 5 (Faculty) — CLASSICAL
  - Evidence: Faculty described as called to teach, mentors, and living curriculum; teachers as core of formation; credentialed, faith-aligned staff. 
  - Rationale: The living-curriculum and mentorship focus align with classical expectations for faculty.

- Element 6 (Parents & Community) — CLASSICAL
  - Evidence: Strong partnership model with parents, parental formation, open access to visits, and family engagement. 
  - Rationale: Parental partnership and home-school alignment map well onto classical community formation criteria.

Aggregate Ranking: CLASSICAL
- The school demonstrates robust CLASSICAL alignment in four categories (Religious/Metaphysical Foundations, School Purpose & Goal, Faculty, Parents & Community) and only NOT CLASSICAL signals in two (Curriculum, Pedagogy). The balance supports a CLASSICAL aggregate classification.

- Needle-moving recommendations would be focused on shifting Curriculum and Pedagogy toward explicit classical elements (Great Books canon, Trivium/Quadrivium, Socratic seminars, and scholé-oriented pedagogy) to reach full CLASSICAL across all six categories.

- Executive evidence sources include ACA’s Biblical Worldview materials, Mission/Partnership statements, and public Curriculum/Pedagogy documentation, anchored by classical keyword alignment from the Six Essential Elements framework.    


## Section: ABSTRACT

The Curriculum and Pedagogy domains, while solid and compatible with a faith-based college-prep model, do not publicly articulate the canonical classical elements (Great Books, Trivium/Quadrivium, Socratic pedagogy) in the public materials, aligning Element 3 as NOT CLASSICAL and Element 4 as NOT CLASSICAL. The aggregate profile remains CLASSICAL, with opportunities to intensify classical curricular and pedagogical commitments (explicit Great Books, Trivium/Quadrivium, and Socratic discourse) to realize a fully classical identity across all six categories. ACA’s essential strengths lie in its governance of character formation, theological grounding, and robust parental engagement, which collectively anchor its CLASSICAL orientation and provide a strong foundation for continued classical development.
