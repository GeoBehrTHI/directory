## Executive Summary

Haw River Christian Academy (HRCA) presents a strong classical Christian profile across most of the Six Essential Elements of a Classical School. Public-facing materials emphasize a Christ-centered, biblical worldview, covenantal partnership with families, incorporation of the Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric), and a curriculum attuned to Great Books and classical pedagogy. HRCA explicitly markets itself as a classical Christian school in partnership with families, with mission statements that foreground truth, goodness, and beauty as real, knowable transcendentals and with pedagogy that includes Socratic dialogue, mnemonic/recitation practices, and formation through imitation. Corporate materials also highlight dedicated governance and faculty expectations aligned with biblically-based integrity and moral formation. Several documents emphasize parental partnership and the school’s covenantal relationship with families, including an admissions policy that requires a credible profession of faith and regular church involvement.

Overall, HRCA aligns with classical education on the majority of the six categories, yielding an overall CLASSICAL assessment. Evidence across the site demonstrates explicit religious/metaphysical grounding, a purposeful formation-oriented aim, a Trivium-centered curriculum, a pedagogy mix that includes Socratic work and memory-based tools, a faculty model rooted in character and mentorship, and a robust parent/community partnership. The most substantial strengths are in Religious & Metaphysical Foundations, Curriculum, Pedagogy, and Parents & Community. The Faculty category shows strong alignment but has comparatively less direct, explicit language in public materials about a formal “living curriculum” portrait of a teacher; this keeps Faculty from being 100% explicit as CLASSICAL, though it remains strongly aligned. The aggregate classification remains CLASSICAL.

Key insights:
- HRCA consistently frames education as formation in a Christian, classical framework (truth, wisdom, beauty; covenant with families; Great Books emphasis). 
- The curriculum and pedagogy explicitly embrace the Trivium, Great Books, memorization/recitation, and Socratic discourse, with lower-to-upper school pathways described (Grammar, Logic, Rhetoric).  
- Parental partnership and a covenantal approach to admissions underscore strong community integration and shared formation aims. 
- While there is no overt liturgical schedule described on the public-facing pages, the explicit Christ-centered, biblical worldview framing and ACCS membership align HRCA with classical, Christian norms.

  - Negative keywords: None evident in public HRCA materials.
- Why this rating
  - HRCA’s public materials establish a clear religious/metaphysical foundation anchored in Christianity, truth/theology, and covenantal family partnerships, with governance language supporting biblically grounded education. The alignment with classical Christian ideals is explicit in mission, admissions, governance, and curricular framing. This supports a CLASSICAL designation for this category.  

### Element 2: School Purpose and Goal — CLASSICAL

- Evidence
  - The mission frames education as formation toward truth, wisdom, and beauty rather than mere information or vocational training. The school’s stated aim is to equip students to know, love, and practice what is true, good, and beautiful. 
  - The curriculum and admissions emphasize formation through a biblical-Christian framework, and a teleology toward human flourishing within a classical tradition. 
  - College-prep materials frame education as part of a broader formation process (character formation plus college guidance). While college prep is present, the explicit formation aim remains evident in mission statements and curricular framing. 
- Positive/Negative keyword analysis
  - Positive keywords present: formation, virtue, wisdom, truth/goodness/beauty, liberal education/paideia, great conversation (as a classical aim), telos, ethical cultivation, moral imagination. 
  - Negative keywords: The site does not emphasize a strictly workforce/career-prep focus as its primary aim; the emphasis remains formation-first. (No explicit negative terms found in public materials.)
- Why this rating
  - HRCA emphasizes formation and virtue within a classical framework and a telos toward truth, goodness, beauty, and human flourishing, consistent with CLASSICAL expectations for School Purpose and Goal. 

### Element 3: Curriculum — CLASSICAL

- Evidence
  - The school presents a Trivium pathway (Grammar, Logic, Rhetoric) in multiple pages, and highlights classical tools such as memorization, recitation, singing, and guided research. The grammar school and rhetoric school curricula illustrate a liberal-arts approach, with Great Books and Western-civilization study featured in the honors track.  
  - Public materials describe Great Books and the Great Conversation, and include references to classical authors and primary texts as part of a Great-Book-based curriculum. The Rhetoric School highlights include "Great Ideas I – Ancient" and "Great Ideas II – Modern." 
  - Curriculum materials emphasize integration across grades with a biblically informed worldview. 
- Positive/Negative keyword analysis
  - Positive keywords present: liberal arts (grammar, logic, rhetoric), Great Books, Great Conversation, primary sources, classical canon, Latin/Greek, history (as Western civilization).  
  - Negative keywords: The classical-lexicon notes a potential negative signal for project-based or STEM-first framing; HRCA’s materials emphasize memory, recitation, and Socratic dialogue rather than project-first approaches, aligning with classical content emphasis rather than the negative signals. Explicit negative terms are not present in HRCA materials. 
- Why this rating
  - HRCA’s curriculum is clearly grounded in the liberal arts and Trivium framework, with explicit Great Books/Great Conversation and classical tools (grammar/logic/rhetoric, memorization, chant, etc.), indicating a CLASSICAL curriculum orientation.  

### Element 4: Pedagogy or Teaching Methods — CLASSICAL

- Evidence
  - HRCA materials emphasize Socratic dialogue and dialectic as a core pedagogy, with upper-grade seminars, debates, and rhetoric training, and include contemplative/Scholé-oriented approaches (restful learning, contemplation, memory work). Lower grades emphasize singing/chanting as part of memory work.  
  - Evidence of a structured pedagogy: seminars, debates, and oral presentations in the rhetoric track; and memory-based tools (recitation, chanting) in grammar and early grades. 
  - Admissions and governance language underscores a biblically framed, teacher-guided formation approach, aligning with classical pedagogy norms. 
- Positive/Negative keyword analysis
  - Positive keywords present: Socratic dialogue, seminars, great conversation, memorization/recitation, chanting, Scholé, formation through imitation, memory work, rhetoric, debate. 
  - Negative keywords: Terms signaling a non-classical pedagogy (e.g., exclusively project-based, constructivist, or experiential-only approaches) are not evident in HRCA’s public materials. The lexicon’s negative signals are not reflected in these HRCA pages. 
- Why this rating
  - The presence of Socratic pedagogy, memory-based learning, and rhetoric-focused upper-level work, together with contemplative auch elements, aligns strongly with classical teaching methods, yielding CLASSICAL. 

### Element 5: Faculty — SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL

- Evidence
  - HRCA materials describe faculty expectations around biblically grounded excellence and integrity, with governance language emphasizing faculty embodying excellence, servant leadership, and alignment with biblical truth. Public materials also provide bios and governance references to leadership, signaling a community of model teachers and mentors, though a formal “living curriculum portrait” of a teacher is not spelled out in a single, explicit line on the site.  
  - The school frames faculty as the core of the school in classical education terms (teacher as exemplar in governance language; mentorship expectations). The HRCA materials emphasize teachers who live their faith and who demonstrate virtuous, professional practice. 
- Positive/Negative keyword analysis
  - Positive keywords present: model teachers and mentors, virtue, excellence, servant heart, living curriculum (conceptually supported by governance language), teacher as exemplar. The explicit phrase “living curriculum” appears in the classical lexicon discussion, and HRCA’s materials reflect that ethos in governance language and faculty expectations. 
  - Negative keywords present: There is no explicit public HRCA text describing a formal “portrait of a teacher” or a deeply codified faculty profile beyond expectations in governance and admissions materials.
- Why this rating
  - HRCA demonstrates strong classical-faculty expectations through governance language about exemplary character, faithfulness, and service, but the explicit pastoral/portrait-like “living curriculum” phrasing is not clearly printed as a standalone HRCA statement in public pages. This yields a robust CLASSICAL orientation with some room for a more explicit, formalized “faculty portrait” to achieve a full CLASSICAL rating. Therefore: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL.

### Element 6: Parents & Community — CLASSICAL

- Evidence
  - HRCA emphasizes partnership with parents in mission statements and admissions, including a covenantal relationship and family involvement in education. Public materials encourage parental participation, tours, and formation. The admissions page explicitly describes the covenantal relationship and parental involvement expectations.  
  - The public site highlights “Partnership with Parents” as a core emphasis, and the school invites parent involvement through tours, book lists, gatherings, and liturgical/family engagement language within the Horrice apparatus. The HRCA site also encourages parents to read and learn about classical education through their website and suggested reading lists. 
- Positive/Negative keyword analysis
  - Positive keywords present: Partnership with Families, co-teachers, parental formation, home life integration, open campus/visitor access (mentions of welcoming parents), parent formation/gatherings, classical family culture. 
  - Negative keywords: None evident in public materials (no consumer-only language; no reduced parental involvement).
- Why this rating
  - HRCA demonstrates an explicit, strong emphasis on parental partnership and family involvement, a hallmark of classical education’s “Partners with Families” principle. This yields CLASSICAL for Parents & Community.

Aggregate Ranking: CLASSICAL

- The school’s public materials consistently align with classical education values across Religious & Metaphysical Foundations, School Purpose & Goal, Curriculum, Pedagogy, and Parents & Community. The Faculty category is highly aligned but lacks an abundantly explicit, formal “portrait of a teacher” language in the public pages, which is why it is judged SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL rather than 100% CLASSICAL in that category. Overall, HRCA is CLASSICAL.

What Would Move the Needle

- If desired to reach a comprehensive CLASSICAL rating for Faculty, HRCA could publish a formal, explicit “Faculty Portrait” or living-curriculum narrative detailing the ideal teacher archetype, virtues, formation processes, and professional development aligned tightly with classical pedagogy.
