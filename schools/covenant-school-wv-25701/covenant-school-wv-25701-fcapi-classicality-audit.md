# Classicality Audit Report: Berean Christian Academy (Katy, TX)

Executive Summary
- Overall classification: CLASSICAL
- Why: The school explicitly centers its mission on a Christ-centered worldview and the transcendentals of Truth, Goodness, and Beauty; it implements the Trivium (Grammar–Logic–Rhetoric), Great Books, Latin, logic, debate/Harkness discussion, and a Senior Thesis capstone; lower grades emphasize memorization via songs and chants; the website outlines theological integration (Bible every year, catechism, hymn studies, scripture memory) and a “Portrait of a Teacher/Graduate/Parent” that stresses wisdom, virtue, and partnership with families. Evidence throughout the site maps tightly to the Six Essential Elements keyword framework.
- Website used: https://bereanchristianacademy.org/ 

Method and Scoring Framework
- Framework: The Six Essential Elements of a Classical School keyword indicators (positive and negative) were used as the scoring backbone for each category, supported by the classicality keyword lexicon across all six elements. Quantification below reflects presence/absence of these terms in the school’s public materials.
