## ABSTRACT

Geneva School Boerne, TX (78015) demonstrates a robust CLASSICAL and Christian educational program. The school’s public materials foreground biblical truth, biblical worldview, Christ-centered framing, and awe-filled engagement with truth, beauty, and goodness. Its Curriculum centers on liberal arts via grammar-logic-rhetoric, Great Books, Great Conversation, Latin (beginning in early grades), chronological history, and a Senior Thesis capstone, all integrated within a Portrait of a Graduate framework. Pedagogy features Socratic dialogue and a long-standing emphasis on memory work, narration, and rhetorical training, with upper-level opportunities for structured debate and public speaking. Faculty are described as deeply invested, living curriculum exemplars within a collegial, mentorship-rich environment, and the school actively partners with parents as co-teachers, inviting open campuses, book studies, and gatherings. The Geneva community also emphasizes a vibrant culture of house systems (e.g., Chaucer, Lewis, Milton, Sayers) and a strong emphasis on character formation and leadership for service to Christ. Public materials from the knowledge vector store (Six Essential Elements) corroborate the Positive Keyword Indicators for each category—especially in religious foundations, curriculum, pedagogy, and community—while the school’s site provides explicit textual anchors to support positive alignment in each category. The aggregate assessment is CLASSICAL, reflecting a coherent and genuine classical Christian educational model across all six categories.
