## Executive Summary

Great Hearts Christos AZ 85015 presents a robust classical Christian education model that aligns strongly with the Six Essential Elements of a Classical School. Public materials from the school emphasize a Christ-centered, truth-seeking formation framework, anchored by a time-tested classical curriculum and liturgical community practices (daily chapel, Scripture instruction, morning prayer). The school’s explicit emphasis on formation (not merely information), Great Books/Great Conversation-style reading, and a partnership with families further signals a classical orientation. Across six criteria, the school demonstrates clear adherence to classical aims, with strong evidence of religious/metaphysical foundations, virtuous formation goals, a curriculum rooted in liberal arts and Great Books, and a pedagogy that blends contemplative practice with Socratic dialogue and rhetoric. Parents and community engagement are also foregrounded, particularly in partnership roles and access to scholarships and campus visits.

Overall classification: CLASSICAL.
