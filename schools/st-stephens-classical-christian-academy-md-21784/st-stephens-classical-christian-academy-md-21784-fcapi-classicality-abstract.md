## ABSTRACT

SSCCA presents a full CLASSICAL alignment across the six categories:
- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: strong Christian/metaphysical framing, prayer, Scriptural truth, and liturgical life. Evidence is drawn from mission statements and Biblically Based materials.  
- School Purpose and Goal: education framed as formation toward virtue and wisdom, rooted in truth/goodness/beauty, not solely job prep; mission emphasizes forming disciples. 
- Curriculum: Trivium-based structure (Grammar, Logic, Rhetoric), Great Books/Great Conversation, history as organizing principle, Latin, and memorization; alignment across K–12.  
- Pedagogy: lower grades emphasize memory, chant, singing, guided discovery; upper grades emphasize Socratic discussion, debates, rhetoric, and public speaking; a balanced mix remains faithful to classical pedagogy.  
- Faculty: teachers as mentors and living curriculum, a governance structure, and a Christ-centered approach that shapes practice.  
- Parents & Community: explicit emphasis on parent partnership, co-teaching, and welcoming parental involvement; parish setting and community governance reinforce alignment with classical aims. 

Overall Ranking: CLASSICAL
