# Classicality Audit: Austin Classical School (Austin, TX)

Website used: https://austinclassical.org

Executive Summary
- Overall classification: CLASSICAL
- Why: Austin Classical School explicitly centers its mission on cultivating “wise, virtuous, and humble” students by pursuing truth, goodness, and beauty under Christ’s lordship; it names and employs the Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric), requires Latin, teaches Great Books and history (not “social studies”), and describes memory work, recitation, debate/discussion, Socratic method, and rhetoric/thesis defense in the upper grades. It articulates theological study (Christian Studies with exegesis, hermeneutics, apologetics, and ethics) as a core throughline and operates a collaborative model that makes parents co‑teachers, with open on‑campus engagement and tours. These align closely with the Six Essential Elements framework and lexicon of positive indicators (truth/goodness/beauty; theology; Trivium; Great Books; Socratic; memorization/recitation; parent partnership) while avoiding negative indicators (e.g., “21st‑century skills,” “college and career readiness,” “project-based learning dominance,” values‑neutral language)    .
- Method note: All categories below include mandatory keyword analysis using the Six Essential Elements/lexicon as the scoring scaffold, per your instruction set. Positive/negative indicators cited from the Six Elements lexicon are included for transparency  .
