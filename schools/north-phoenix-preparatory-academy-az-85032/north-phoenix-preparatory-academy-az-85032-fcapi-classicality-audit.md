## Executive Summary

North Phoenix Preparatory Academy (NPX), as reflected in publicly accessible materials from its Great Hearts/North Phoenix Prep ecosystem, demonstrates robust alignment with classical education principles. The school emphasizes a classical liberal arts curriculum anchored in Truth, Goodness, and Beauty, a strong emphasis on Great Books and the Western tradition, Socratic dialogue and Humane Letters seminars, a well-defined college-preparatory outcome, and a clearly articulated partnership with families and the broader community. Across the six classical elements framework, NPX consistently signals formation of character, rigorous liberal arts study, and a pedagogy that blends contemplation with rigorous inquiry. Evidence from NPX materials shows explicit commitments to classical curriculum, Socratic pedagogy, humane letters, a focus on virtue, and active parent involvement. While the public-facing materials do not foreground liturgical or formal theological practices, they do place moral formation and transcendentals (Truth, Goodness, Beauty) at the center of student formation.

Overall classification: CLASSICAL.

This assessment uses the knowledge-base rubric of the Six Essential Elements of a Classical School, focusing on keywords and phrases that indicate adherence to the six classical categories (Religious & Metaphysical Foundations, Purpose & Goal, Curriculum, Pedagogy, Faculty, Parents & Community) and cross-checks with explicit Positive/Negative Keyword Indicators drawn from the knowledge-document set. The NPX materials show strong positive signals in every category, with explicit articulation of Great Books, humane letters, Socratic dialogue, the Great Conversation, and a formation emphasis consistent with classical education. See category-by-category notes below for the keyword analyses and the justification of each category’s rating.
