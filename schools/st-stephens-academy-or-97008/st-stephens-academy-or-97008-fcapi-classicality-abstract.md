## ABSTRACT

St. Stephen’s Academy (SSA) in Oregon presents a CLASSICAL profile across six rubric categories. Religiously and metaphysically, SSA centers on Christ-centered, biblical worldview education with theological foundations (Westminster Confession of Faith) and liturgical/community practices implied in its culture and curricula. In purpose and goal, the school emphasizes formation, virtue, wisdom, and leadership within a classical framework, aligning with the transcendental aims of truth, goodness, and beauty. The curriculum is explicitly liberal arts-based, built on the trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric) with the quadrivium, and anchored in the Great Books and Great Conversation lineage. Pedagogically, SSA integrates contemplative elements (scholé) with Socratic discussions, seminars, and formal rhetoric, culminating in public speaking and eloquence training. Faculty are portrayed as living exemplars and a core, mentoring presence within the learning community, and parents are positioned as co-educators and integral partners—an emphasis echoed in Covenant Classical Academy and related materials. Together, these factors place SSA squarely within the CLASSICAL rubric with a strong, comprehensive alignment to the Six Essential Elements of Classical Education.

- Pedagogy: contemplative/Scholé in early years; memorization/recitation; Socratic seminars; rhetoric and public speaking; canonical classical methods. 
- Faculty: living curriculum; mentors; pillar teachers; aligned with Christian worldview and classical methods. 
- Parents & Community: strong parent partnership; co-educators; home-school collaboration; open to visits and engagement.
