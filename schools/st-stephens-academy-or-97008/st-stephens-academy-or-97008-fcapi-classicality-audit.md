## Executive Summary

St. Stephen’s Academy (SSA) presents a robust, clearly articulated classical Christian program operating in Oregon with two Beaverton-Portland area campuses. Public materials consistently foreground a Christ-centered, biblical worldview, a Trivium-based curriculum (grammar, logic, rhetoric) with attention to the liberal arts, and a formation-focused mission emphasizing virtue, wisdom, and eloquence. The school presents itself as a classical school with strong emphasis on Great Books tradition, memorization/recitation, and a pedagogy that blends contemplative and Socratic methods across grade bands. Community and family partnership are framed as central, including parental involvement and invitation for visits. Accreditation by major classical bodies is highlighted, signaling alignment with classical standards. Overall, SSA meets the Six Essential Elements of a Classical School criteria in practice and text, yielding a CLASSICAL assessment across the rubric.

Citations to SSA’s public materials and the classical education rubric are interwoven below to ground each category assessment in explicit textual evidence. See the linked SSA content for phrasing such as “Shaping Lifelong Learners Through a Biblical Worldview,” “Christ-centered,” the Trivium and Quadrivium, Great Books references, and explicit pedagogical practices like Socratic discussion and memorization/recitation.

### Element 4: School Pedagogy or Teaching Methods — CLASSICAL

Evidence
- Lower/Grammar Stage pedagogy includes contemplative, restful elements and memory-based learning (singing, chanting, recitation) as memory-building devices. SSA materials discuss memorization and the role of chant/song in the early years. 
- Upper Logic/Rhetoric stages emphasize Socratic discussion, seminars, and debates; explicit training in rhetoric and public speaking is described for upper grades. 
- The curriculum and pedagogy explicitly discuss the five canons of rhetoric and humane letters, with a focus on careful reading, argumentation, and persuasive communication. 
- The school supports a rhythm of contemplative, wholesome learning that aligns with scholé (restful study) principles and a discussion-based, dialectic approach in higher grades. 

Positive/Negative keyword analysis
- Positive keywords observed: contemplative learning (scholé), memorization/recitation, singing/chanting in lower grades, Socratic discussion, seminars, debates, rhetoric, public speaking, humane letters, five canons of rhetoric, dialectic, formal logic.   
- Negative keywords: none evident; no strong mention of project-based, purely teacher-directed, or skills-based reduction in the public materials. (There are some co-curriculars, but they sit within the classical framework.) 

Why this rating
- SSA’s pedagogy aligns with the classical expectation of a balanced approach: contemplative, memory-focused early work; Socratic/seminal discussion in the middle grades; rigorous rhetoric and public-speaking training in the upper grades. This maps to the Positive Keyword indicators in the Six Elements rubric and yields CLASSICAL.

Key references: Lower-to-upper school progression (grammar → logic → rhetoric) and explicit mention of Socratic/seminar formats; preaching/memory recitation; rhetorical canon.
