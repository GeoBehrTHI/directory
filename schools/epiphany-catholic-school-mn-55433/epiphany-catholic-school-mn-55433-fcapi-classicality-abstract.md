## ABSTRACT

Epiphany Catholic School MN 55433 demonstrates strong CLASSICAL alignment across five of six categories—Religious and Metaphysical Foundations, School Purpose and Goal, Curriculum, Faculty, and Parents & Community—with Pedagogy displaying substantial classical orientation but not fully explicit Socratic/ Seminar-based practice in the public materials (SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL). The potential needle-mover is the explicit articulation of upper-grade Socratic pedagogy and formal Great Books dialogue, which would elevate Pedagogy to CLASSICAL and provide a unified, fully explicit classical program across K–8.
