## ABSTRACT

The Field School Chicago (Field School 60644) demonstrates a CLASSICAL orientation in its public materials. The school foregrounds a diverse, classical, Christ-centered identity, with a strong emphasis on formation of virtue and wisdom, and a humanities-forward curriculum rooted in Christian content. Pedagogical practices reflect a balance of contemplative memory work (singing, chanting, habit formation) and discussion-based, primary-text study, with a Charlotte Mason influence shaping character development and moral imagination. Faculty are presented as central to the school’s mission, serving as mentors and core to the living curriculum, and parents are positioned as partners in education through the Family Tree Organization, volunteering, tours, and community events. The curriculum highlights Great Books, Story of the World, Bible-literature-art-music humanities, and a four-element humanities approach, with a clear emphasis on formation, timeless content, and a faith-informed worldview.
