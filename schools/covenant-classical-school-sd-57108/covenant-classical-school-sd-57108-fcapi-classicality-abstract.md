## ABSTRACT

Covenant Classical School (Sioux Falls, SD, 57108) demonstrates CLASSICAL alignment with a Christian, formation-centered approach. The school foregrounds a Christian worldview, virtue formation, and a Great-Books-oriented, historically grounded curriculum. It emphasizes parents as co-teachers and a collaborative model, with learning cohorts and multiple signs of a living, virtuous faculty. The presence of Latin study, a focus on history as the backbone of the curriculum, and explicit aims toward truth, goodness, and beauty support CLASSICAL status across most categories. Pedagogy and Curriculum show strong classical signals, though explicit articulation of the complete Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric) and more explicit Socratic pedagogy in public materials are less overt, resulting in some elements being classified as SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL. The aggregate evaluation remains CLASSICAL, with opportunities identified to enhance explicit Trivium alignment and Socratic pedagogy demonstrations, and to deepen liturgical practices within the school rhythm.
