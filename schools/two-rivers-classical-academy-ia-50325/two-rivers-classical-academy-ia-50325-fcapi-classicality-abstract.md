## ABSTRACT

Two Rivers Classical Academy (Des Moines, IA, 50325) (TRCA) presents a robust CLASSICAL profile across all six criteria of the classical education rubric. Evidence from the school’s public materials shows:

- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: clear Christian formation, Scripture memory, catechesis, and a theology-forward approach embedded in school life.  
- School Purpose and Goal: explicit emphasis on formation of virtue and wisdom, with a teleology rooted in truth, goodness, and beauty, rather than solely job readiness. 
- Curriculum: explicit Trivium-based structure (grammar, logic, rhetoric), Great Books, Latin, Bible, and a “best books” approach; ongoing alignment with classical liberal-arts aims. 
- Pedagogy: a blended pedagogy that includes contemplative, memory-based elements in the lower grades (Scholé-like) and Socratic/seminar-based methods in the upper grades, alongside rhetoric training and opportunities to deliver speeches.  
- Faculty: living curriculum and a visible, mentor-centered staff culture, with named roles and Board oversight, reflecting a classical faculty ethos. 
- Parents & Community: strong emphasis on partnership with families as co-educators, formal parental training, and open-campus engagement consistent with classical community integration. 

Aggregate classification: CLASSICAL.
