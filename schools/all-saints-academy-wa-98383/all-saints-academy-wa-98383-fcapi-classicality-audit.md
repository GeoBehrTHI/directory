## Executive Summary

All Saints Academy Wa 98383 presents a robust classical Christian framework in public-facing materials. The school consistently positions itself as Christ-centered and classical, foregrounding virtues, formation, and communal worship as core drivers of education. It explicitly states a purpose of forming students’ souls and character, not merely preparing them for college or jobs, and it emphasizes partnership with Christian families and churches. Evidence from the site shows a clear emphasis on wonder, worship, virtue formation, and a shared mission with families, with narratives about a Christ-centered, classical education and a culture of memory, ritual, and communal life. The public materials reveal strong alignment with Religious and Metaphysical Foundations and School Purpose/Goal, solid but more limited alignment for Curriculum and Pedagogy (compared to an ideal, text-heavy Great-Book, Trivium-focused program), robust alignment for Faculty, and clear alignment for Parents & Community.

Overall classification: CLASSICAL.

Notes on category-by-category signals:
- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: Strong. Christ-centered language, worship, and paideia references appear in the public materials, including explicit statements about wonder leading to worship. Positive signals dominate; no clear negative signals observed in the public materials.
- School Purpose and Goal: Strong. Emphasis on formation, virtue, and the true/beautiful/good; explicit critique of job- or college-only orientation is present.
- Curriculum: Moderate-to-strong signals but with gaps relative to full Great-Books emphasis and explicit Trivium/Quadrivium articulation. Latin is present, and the classical framing is explicit, but direct Great Books/Great Conversation language is not consistently visible in the public content.
- Pedagogy: Moderate signals. There is evidence of contemplative/restful levers and a holistic approach, but explicit Socratic/seminar/Rhetoric programs are not clearly demonstrated in the public-facing materials.
- Faculty: Strong signals. Descriptions of gifted faculty, small teacher/student ratio, and the living curriculum appear.
- Parents & Community: Strong signals. Deliberate partnership with families, parent involvement, and accessible resources for families are evident.

Aggregate Ranking and Narrative

- Aggregate Ranking: CLASSICAL

- Rationale
  - Across the six categories, All Saints Academy Wa 98383 shows strong alignment with the Classical education rubric in Religious Foundations, Purpose/Goal, Faculty, and Parents & Community; it demonstrates substantial alignment in Curriculum and Pedagogy but with some gaps in explicit Great Books/Trivium/Quadrivium articulation and explicit Socratic pedagogy in public-facing materials. Taken together, the overall profile is CLASSICAL, with some room for needle-moving improvements in Curriculum and Pedagogy (see below).

- What would move the needle (for SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL cases)
  - Explicitly publish and reference Trivium/Quadrivium in Curriculum pages, including explicit statements about grammar, logic, and rhetoric as foundational disciplines.
  - Include explicit Great Books and Great Conversation language and lists within core curriculum pages, plus public commitments to reading primary sources and canonical works.
  - Document explicit Socratic seminar, dialectic, or rhetoric training practices across grades (especially at upper levels) to strengthen Pedagogy alignment.
  - Increase conspicuous display of liturgical practices and communal rituals (e.g., chapel schedules, liturgical calendar, and music program) to reinforce Liturgical/Communal identity in Public materials.
