# ABSTRACT

Grace Point Academy WA 98022 presents a CLASSICAL-leaning profile with strong religious/theological framing and a formation-focused purpose, anchored by Hillsdale College’s classical curriculum and a church-community ecosystem. The school demonstrates a clear teleology toward wisdom, virtue, and the good, with an emphasis on truth, goodness, and beauty and a mission to partner with families.
