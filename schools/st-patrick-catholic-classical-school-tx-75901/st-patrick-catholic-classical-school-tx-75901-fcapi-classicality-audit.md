## Executive Summary

St Patrick Catholic Classical School TX 75901 presents a coherent Catholic, classical-education profile. Public-facing materials emphasize a Christ-centered, faith-formation focus integrated with a classical liberal-arts curriculum, including a stated emphasis on the Trivium (grammar and logic focus) and a Catholic sacramental life. The school’s mission, vision, and governance language repeatedly foreground faith, virtue, prayer, worship, and service as the core aims of education, with explicit reference to formation of faith and family, and a Catholic community formed through worship and sacraments. This strongly aligns with Classical School markers in the religious-metaphysical and purpose-goal domains, and with a curriculum anchored in classical liberal arts. Evidence from the crawl data includes explicit statements about a Christ-centered environment, Catholic curriculum, and a Classical Liberal Arts structure, along with references to faith formation, liturgy, and Catholic identity (e.g., Christ-centered mission, worship, sacraments, and a classical curriculum rooted in the liberal arts). The school attributes professional virtue to its faculty and describes parents as partners in the educational mission. There are explicit classroom-structure elements (grammar and logic focus; Latin instruction) consistent with classical methodology, and governance elements (Head Mistress, accreditation, parental involvement) that reflect a traditional classical school model.

Overall classification: CLASSICAL.
