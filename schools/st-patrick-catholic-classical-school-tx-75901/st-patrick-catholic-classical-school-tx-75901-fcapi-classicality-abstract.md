## ABSTRACT

St Patrick Catholic Classical School TX 75901 is a Catholic, classical-education institution with strong religious-metaphysical foundations, a formation-centered purpose, and a curriculum rooted in classical liberal arts and the trivium (grammar, logic, basic rhetoric). The school’s public-facing materials foreground Christ-centered formation, Catholic theology, liturgy, and a holistic development model, aligning with Classical School criteria in Categories 1 (Religious & Metaphysical Foundations) and 2 (Purpose & Goal). The Curriculum (Element 3) shows clear classical elements—grammar, logic, grammar-focused trivium, and Latin—but the on-site materials do not clearly articulate Great Books/Great Conversation or a robust quadrivium treatment, leading to a SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL score for Curriculum. Pedagogy (Element 4) is anchored in classical method (grammar/logic) but lacks explicit, public references to Socratic seminars, Scholé/restful pedagogy, or explicit upper-grade rhetoric-seminars in the website content, resulting in a SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL classification for Pedagogy. Faculty (Element 5) and Parents & Community (Element 6) are strongly classical: model teachers, living curriculum, Head Mistress leadership, and robust parent/parish involvement—classical signals present and explicit.

Overall, the school earns CLASSICAL for the aggregate evaluation, with particular strength in Religious & Metaphysical Foundations, Purpose & Goal, Faculty, and Parents & Community. The Curriculum and Pedagogy categories are the primary areas where additional explicit signaling would move the needle toward a fully CLASSICAL designation.
