## Executive Summary

Chesterton Academy of the Willamette Valley (Mount Angel, OR 97362) presents a sharply classical profile anchored in a Catholic, culture-of-life orientation. The school foregrounds formation over information, a Great Books–driven curriculum, and pedagogy rooted in Socratic dialogue, memorization, and disciplined study. Public materials from the school emphasize a three-pillar model (Intellect, Character, Spirituality), a mission steeped in truth, goodness, and beauty, liturgical life, and a strong faculty-and-family partnership ethos. Taken together, these signals align strongly with classical education as defined by the Six Essential Elements rubric. The aggregate assessment is CLASSICAL.

Key themes driving the CLASSICAL classification:
- Religious and metaphysical foundations are explicit (joyfully Catholic identity, Cultura Vitae, liturgical life, Mass events, spiritual formation).
- Purpose and goal emphasize formation, virtue, wisdom, and the pursuit of truth, good, and beauty.
- Curriculum centers on liberal arts orientation, Great Books / Great Conversation, and a historically-integrated program.
- Pedagogy emphasizes Socratic discussion, close reading, memory work, and rhetoric; the upper-school emphasis on rhetoric and seminars is evident.
- Faculty messaging positions teachers as mentors, living curriculum, and exemplars.
- Parents and community are actively invited and engaged (open houses, liturgical-life events, family involvement).
