Why this rating
- The pedagogical approach centers on traditional classical methods (recitation, memory, narration, chant, rhetoric, and structured speaking). The occasional inclusion of exordium and an emphasis on public-speaking and memory aligns with classical pedagogy, supporting a CLASSICAL rating. The absence of a dominant, modern constructivist project-based emphasis in the public materials further reinforces this.
