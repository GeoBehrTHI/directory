## ABSTRACT

HVCA demonstrates a Christian classical orientation with strong religious/metaphysical foundations and active parental involvement, signaling a CLASSICAL impulse in those dimensions. However, explicit signaling of core classical curriculum elements (liberal arts via grammar, logic, rhetoric; Quadrivium; Great Books/Great Conversation) and classical pedagogy (Socratic seminars, rigorous rhetoric training) is not strongly evident in the public site content, yielding NOT CLASSICAL signals in Curriculum, Pedagogy, and Faculty categories. The result is a generally SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL overall assessment, driven by clear formation and faith-centered aims and robust family engagement, tempered by the lack of explicit classical curricular and teaching-method signaling. The most impactful needle-movers would be explicit public articulation of a liberal-arts curriculum (including Great Books and Trivium/Quadrivium), explicit Socratic pedagogy, and a public-facing model of faculty as living exemplars of classical virtue and intellectual life, as well as deeper, ongoing, visible co-educator partnerships with parents in daily schooling.

Note: This evaluation draws on HVCA public-facing materials (e.g., About HVCA, Statement of Faith, Spiritual Emphasis Week materials, and Admissions pages) and the classical-school rubric and keyword lists in the knowledge base, including the Six Essential Elements of a Classical School (1) document.
