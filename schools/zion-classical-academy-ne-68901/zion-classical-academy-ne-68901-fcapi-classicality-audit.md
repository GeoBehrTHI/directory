## Executive Summary

Zion Classical Academy Hastings (ZIP 68901) presents a clearly classical Christian profile. Public materials emphasize a Christ-centered, virtue-oriented formation anchored in truth, wisdom, and virtue; a curriculum built around the liberal arts (grammar, logic, rhetoric) with Great Books/Great Conversation at the core; Latin in the early grades; and pedagogical modes that blend Socratic discussion, memory work, recitation, and liturgical rhythm. The school also foregrounds character formation, teacher as mentor, and strong parental partnership. Across the materials reviewed, there is consistent alignment with classical criteria in religious foundations, formative aims, curriculum structure, pedagogical methods, faculty posture, and parent/community engagement. Evidence drawn from Zion Classical Academy’s published content (including mission statements, curricular notes, and program descriptions) supports a CLASSICAL classification across all six categories.

Key highlights:
- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: Clear Christ-centered orientation with Scripture, prayer, and a sacred-frame for learning; a mission centered on truth, wisdom, and virtue.
- Purpose and Goal: Education framed as formation and moral formation for life of service, not primarily workforce preparation.
- Curriculum: Explicit emphasis on the liberal arts (grammar, logic, rhetoric), history/readings integrated with Great Books/Great Conversation, and Latin in the lower/middle grades; a commitment to wonder and integrated disciplines.
- Pedagogy: Use of Socratic methods in upper grades, memory work, recitation, and liturgical rhythm; a restful, contemplative strand alongside rigorous inquiry.
- Faculty: Described as mentors and living curriculum; staff and board engaged in mission; explicit expectations around virtue and professional formation.
- Parents & Community: Strong partnership with families; parent formation and volunteer culture; accessible campus with open communication and involvement.
