## ABSTRACT

Holy Family Academy (Manassas, VA) demonstrates CLASSICAL alignment across six categories, anchored in Catholic faith and a traditional liberal-arts curriculum. The school emphasizes the Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric), Great Books/Western-Civilization study, and formation through virtue, with liturgical life and Catholic identity woven into daily learning. Faculty are portrayed as living exemplars who guide character formation, and there is clear partnership with families and an open, faith-centered community. The public materials reflect a strong classical orientation in Religious Foundations, Purpose and Goal, Curriculum, Faculty, and Parents & Community, with Pedagogy showing classical signals albeit less explicitly documented than other areas. The aggregate classification is CLASSICAL.

Key observations by category:
- Religious & Metaphysical Foundations: Strong Catholic liturgical life (Daily Mass), theology integration, and sacred tradition. 
- School Purpose & Goal: Formation, virtue, wisdom, and the true/good/beautiful telos are central; faith and reason integrated to form humans who know real truth. 
- Curriculum: Trivium emphasis; great-works and Western civilization; Memoria Press-based classical programming in St. Anne’s program.  
- Pedagogy: Classical pedagogy signals (memory work, recitation, contemplation) are present; explicit Socratic seminars are not strongly documented in public copy. 
- Faculty: Dedicated faculty; living curriculum; moral exemplars; strong teacher-pedagogical identity. 
- Parents & Community: Partnership with families; a focus on serving families; open admissions and community life. 

Overall, Holy Family Academy presents a robust CLASSICAL profile, with formation, virtue, and classical pedagogy foregrounded within a Catholic framework, and with practices aligning well to the six essential elements of classical education.
