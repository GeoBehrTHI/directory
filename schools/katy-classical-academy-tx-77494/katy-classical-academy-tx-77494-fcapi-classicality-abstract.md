## ABSTRACT

Katy Classical Academy demonstrates a cohesive CLASSICAL orientation across all six classical-education categories. Religious and Metaphysical Foundations are clearly anchored in a Biblical/Christian framework with explicit references to biblical worldview, Christ-centered teaching, God and prayer, and worship-like community life. The School Purpose and Goal emphasize formation, virtue, wisdom, and a teleological pursuit of truth, goodness, and beauty through a classical education, with partnerships with parents as co-educators. The Curriculum centers on the Trivium (Grammar, Dialectic/Logic, Rhetoric), a four-year world-history cycle, Latin, and classical literature, aligning with Great Books and the Great Conversation ethos. Pedagogy blends memory work, chanting, narration, and Socratic discussion, balancing contemplative scholé moments with active seminar-style learning in upper grades. Faculty are portrayed as engaged, virtuous mentors who form the living curriculum, and Parents & Community are integrated as co-teachers and partners in education. The aggregate rating across all six categories is CLASSICAL.

Key signals include: biblical worldview and theologies embedded in curriculum; Trivium-aligned curriculum and a shared historical framework; memory/chanting pedagogy with Socratic elements; a staff described as a “passionate team” and the living curriculum; and a formal, in loco parentis, co-teaching partnership with parents. These elements collectively satisfy the Positive Keyword indicators across the Six Elements lexicon and show minimal presence of negative indicators in the school’s public materials.

Citations: Shift In Perspective, Who We Are, Mission statements, Curriculum Levels (Grammar/Dialectic/Rhetoric), World History cycle, Latin, Great Books orientation, memory-chant pedagogy, and In Loco Parentis materials.
