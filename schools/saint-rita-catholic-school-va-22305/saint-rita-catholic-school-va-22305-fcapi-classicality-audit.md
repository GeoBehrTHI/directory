## Executive Summary

Saint Rita Catholic School (Alexandria, VA 22305) presents a vivid classical-education profile anchored in a Catholic, liturgical, and formation-focused vision. Public-facing materials consistently describe the school as a preschool–8th grade Catholic institution in the classical tradition, emphasizing faith formation, virtue, and the cultivation of wisdom through a curriculum rooted in liberal arts, Latin, and exposure to great literature and the “big questions” of history and human culture. The school explicitly frames its mission around forming students who seek truth, goodness, and beauty, and it highlights communal liturgical practices (Mass, Holy Hour, Adoration) as foundational to daily life. Evidence of a small, tight-knit faculty and a pedagogy that blends discussion, memory work, and hands-on projects further reinforces a classical profile. The presence of catechesis within the Atrium, Latin study, a commitment to literature and art, and a governance of community that includes active engagement with military families, open houses, and resource supports all contribute to a robust classical alignment.

Overall classification: CLASSICAL.

Key strengths include:
- Religious and metaphysical foundations firmly integrated with daily classroom life (Mass, Adoration, Catechesis of the Good Shepherd; Catholic mission; the soul of education motif).
- Formation-centered purpose and goal emphasizing virtue, wisdom, and the good, true, and beautiful.
- A curriculum that highlights liberal arts foundations, Latin, history across disciplines, great literature, and the cultivation of wonder.
- Pedagogy that blends contemplative/restful elements with discussion, memorization, and ritual/thematic learning, along with upper-level seminars and public speaking opportunities.
- A faculty described as model, collaborative, and central to the living curriculum.

What would move the needle (for a COMMONLY SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL rating) is not needed here; the school already demonstrates a strong classical alignment across the six dimensions.
