## Executive Summary

Chandler Preparatory Academy (AZ 85224) presents a clearly classical, Great-Hearts–influenced model of education. Public-facing materials emphasize a classical liberal arts curriculum built around Truth, Goodness, and Beauty, with strong commitments to Great Books, Humane Letters, Socratic pedagogy, and a college-preparatory trajectory rooted in virtue formation. The school also foregrounds parental partnership and an active, family-oriented community culture. Taken together, these signals align most closely with CLASSICAL/classical-education norms, with notable emphasis on formation, moral purpose, and a robust, dialogic, book-centered pedagogy.

Key evidence points include: a stated commitment to cultivating hearts and minds through Truth, Goodness, and Beauty; a curriculum described as classical liberal arts with Great Books and Humane Letters; explicit use of Socratic seminars and daily seminar culture in upper grades; a strong faculty/staff presence described on the site; and active parent/community engagement through formal parent groups and events. These signals collectively support a generally CLASSICAL classification, with Element 1 (Religious and Metaphysical Foundations) showing strong alignment with transcendentals and virtue, Element 3 (Curriculum) and Element 4 (Pedagogy) clearly CLASSICAL, Element 2 (Purpose and Goal), Element 5 (Faculty), and Element 6 (Parents & Community) also aligning strongly, though with some caveats about theology and liturgical language that are not explicitly foregrounded on public pages.
