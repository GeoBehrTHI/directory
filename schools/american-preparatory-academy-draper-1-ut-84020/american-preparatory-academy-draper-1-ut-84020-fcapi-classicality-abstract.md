## ABSTRACT

APA Draper 1 demonstrates a clear classical-liberal arts orientation with a Trivium-based curriculum, a Core Knowledge foundation, and a mission that foregrounds virtue, wisdom, and human flourishing. The public materials show rigorous academics, character formation, and a “Great Conversation” orientation in practice, with upper grades adopting Socratic discussion and rhetorical training. Yet the school also employs Direct Instruction in elementary grades and lacks explicit theological or liturgical signals in public-facing statements, which tempers a wholesale CLASSICAL label in Element 1. On Curriculum and Pedagogy, the presence of Trivium, Great Conversation elements, and seminar-based practices support CLASSICAL alignments, while DI and mixed pedagogy (K–8) yield Somewhat Classical signals in Element 4. Faculty and Community signals are mid-to-strong classical, with public-facing portrayals of mentorship and parent partnership aligning with classical-school norms, though with room for codified “Portrait of the Teacher” and more explicit faculty development. Overall, APA Draper 1 falls between CLASSICAL and SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL, with the aggregate assessment landing at SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL.

- Element 1: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL — Virtue, truth, beauty present; theology/liturgical practices not explicit publicly.
- Element 2: CLASSICAL — Formation, human flourishing, virtue, and wisdom as core aims.
- Element 3: CLASSICAL — Trivium, Core Knowledge, Western civilization, Latin/Greek, and related curriculum signals.
- Element 4: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL — Socratic/rhetoric in upper grades; DI in elementary; blended pedagogy.
- Element 5: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL — Mentorship and living-curriculum signals with room for a formal faculty portrait.
- Element 6: CLASSICAL — Strong parental-partnership signals and community formation.
