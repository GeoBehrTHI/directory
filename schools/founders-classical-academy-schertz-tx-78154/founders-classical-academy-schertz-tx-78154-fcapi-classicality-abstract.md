## ABSTRACT

Founders Classical Academy – Schertz presents a publicly stated classical orientation with emphasis on truth, goodness, beauty, and virtue, and an overarching goal of forming virtuous, free-citizen citizens. The school describes a traditional liberal-arts curriculum and a general classical approach, with clear evidence of partnerships with families. However, explicit theology, liturgical practices, detailed Great Books/Great Conversation commitments, and a robust set of classical-pedagogical practices (especially for upper grades) are not consistently evidenced in the public materials. When evaluated against the Six Essential Elements’ Positive/Negative Keyword indicators, the school shows partial alignment across all six categories, yielding an overall classification of SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL.

- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL, driven by truth/beauty/goodness and virtue, but lacking explicit theological grounding and liturgical practice in public materials. 
- Purpose and Goal: CLASSICAL, with formation and virtue-centered aims as primary, aligning with telos-oriented classical education. 
- Curriculum: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL, anchored by liberal-arts framing but lacking explicit Great Books/grammar/logic/rhetoric sequencing in public materials. 
- Pedagogy: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL, signals a classical approach but lacks explicit high-school Socratic/seminar documentation in public materials. 
- Faculty: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL, presence of named faculty, but lacking explicit “living curriculum” or ideal-teacher portrait language in public materials. 
- Parents & Community: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL, parent partnership language exists, with some parent involvement signals but not a fully articulated formation program. 

Aggregate ranking: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL.
