## Executive Summary

Leap Academy, Georgia 30103 presents a robustly Christian, classical veneer and positions itself as a covenantal, classical Christian school with a stated aim to cultivate a Biblical worldview and virtue in students. Public materials emphasize a Christ-centered, faith-integrated formation, paideia (the upbringing and education of the soul), and a mission to train students to lead with excellence within a Biblical framework. There is clear language about truth, goodness, and beauty, and explicit partnerships with families and church communities, including a stated belief in forming students for life rather than solely for work.

Overall, Leap Academy demonstrates substantial classical elements—especially around religious/theological foundations, formation-oriented goals, and active parent/community partnership. However, the public materials do not consistently articulate explicit Great Books engagement, a formal Trivium/Quadrivium curriculum, or explicit Socratic pedagogy in the way the strict classical rubric envisions. Taken together, Leap Academy aligns more with SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL than fully CLASSICAL, with some strong classical cues and some gaps that, if addressed with a more explicit, text-based, traditional classical curriculum and pedagogy, would push it toward full CLASSICAL.
