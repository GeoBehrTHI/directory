## ABSTRACT
Kairos Classical Christian Academy NC 28052 demonstrates Christian identity and some liturgical/communal cues in publicly available site content, but explicit classical curriculum, pedagogy, and faculty-formation signals are not clearly documented in the accessible public materials. Using the Six Essential Elements of a Classical School framework, the school receives a SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL classification overall, with Element 1 (Religious and Metaphysical Foundations) showing the strongest alignment due to Christian branding and liturgical cues, and Categories 2–6 showing weaker or non-explicit classical alignment due to lack of explicit classical curriculum, pedagogy, faculty, and parent-community documentation on the site. To move toward a full CLASSICAL designation, the school should publicly articulate a clear classical curriculum (Seven Liberal Arts, Great Books, Great Conversation, and the Trivium/Quadrivium), explicit pedagogy (Socratic dialogue, Scholé, memorization/recitation, formation through imitation), a living-curriculum faculty model, and robust parent/community partnerships, all documented on the website and in official materials.
