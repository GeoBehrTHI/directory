## ABSTRACT

Maranatha Academy Wisconsin 54880 demonstrates a CLASSICAL orientation across most of the classical-school rubric. The public materials foreground Christ-centered, Christian formation within a classical curriculum (notably the Trivium) and a discipleship approach to learning, with explicit emphasis on formation, virtue, and a holistic view of education. Faculty are portrayed as dedicated mentors within a low student-to-teacher ratio, and parents are positioned as partners in a family-formation model. Category 4 (Pedagogy) stands as SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL due to limited explicit public articulation of standard classical methods (Socratic dialogue, Scholé, recitation) within the visible site content. The aggregate assessment places Maranatha Academy as CLASSICAL, with room for needle-moving enhancements in pedagogy and explicit Great Books integration and liturgical/community practices.
