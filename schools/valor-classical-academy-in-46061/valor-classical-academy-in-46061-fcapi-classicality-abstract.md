## ABSTRACT

Valor Classical Academy (Noblesville, IN, 46061) demonstrates a robust CLASSICAL orientation across Curriculum, Pedagogy, and Parent/Community engagement, with strong signals of formation, virtue, and a Great-Books-centric liberal-arts program. The school’s public materials align with a Hillsdale College K-12 framework, emphasize teacher-led instruction and Socratic Seminars, and foreground moral formation and civic virtue. While explicit theology and liturgical practices are not foregrounded in the public content, the core classical commitments—formation, Great Books, Latin, primary sources, warm teacher leadership, and family involvement—signal a fully classical direction in practice. Across the six categories, Valor achieves CLASSICAL in four areas (Curriculum, Pedagogy, and Parents/Community; with Purpose and Goal also strong) and SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL in Religious & Metaphysical Foundations and Faculty. The aggregate read is CLASSICAL, with concrete evidence from Valor’s public materials cited below.

- Evidence highlights:
  - Great Books, primary sources, and a content-rich liberal-arts program, plus Socratic Seminars and teacher-led instruction.  
  - Hillsdale K-12 program framework and Core Knowledge-based curriculum. 
  - Public signals of moral formation, character development, and civic virtue as core aims. 
  - Active family involvement via Get Involved/Volunteer channels and enrollment engagement. 

- The six-category synthesis in brief:
  - Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL, with virtue but no explicit theology or liturgical practice in the public content. 
  - Purpose and Goal: CLASSICAL, formation-centered with virtue and wisdom, not workforce- or college-readiness framed. 
  - Curriculum: CLASSICAL, Great Books, Latin, primary sources, liberal-arts progression. 
  - Pedagogy: CLASSICAL, Socratic Seminars and teacher-led instruction documented. 
  - Faculty: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL, with emphasis on highly qualified teachers; explicit “living curriculum” signaling is less explicit. 
  - Parents & Community: CLASSICAL, strong engagement channels and opportunities for parental involvement.
