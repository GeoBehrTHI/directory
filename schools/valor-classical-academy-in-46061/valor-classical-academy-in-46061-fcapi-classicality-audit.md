## Executive Summary

Valor Classical Academy (Noblesville, IN, 46061) presents a clearly classical orientation in its public materials. The program foregrounds liberal-arts formation, virtue and civic virtue, engagement with Great Books, and a Socratic/seminar-based pedagogy. The school explicitly aligns with a Hillsdale College K-12 curriculum framework, emphasizes teacher-led instruction, and promotes a culture of moral character, decorum, and critical thinking. Based on the six-element rubric, Valor is overall CLASSICAL, with especially strong alignment in Curriculum, Pedagogy, and Parent/Community engagement. Categories with somewhat lesser explicit alignment—Religious and Metaphysical Foundations and some aspects of Faculty—still show classical signals, but without strong expressions of theology or liturgical practice or a fully articulated “living curriculum” identity. The aggregate assessment remains CLASSICAL.

Key takeaways by category (high level):
- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL. Virtue and moral formation are present, but explicit theology, liturgy, or sacred practices are not clearly foregrounded in public materials.
- Purpose and Goal: CLASSICAL. Emphasis on formation, virtue, wisdom, and the true/good/beautiful; not framed as workforce preparation.
- Curriculum: CLASSICAL. Strong alignment to Great Books, Latin, primary sources, and a liberal-arts progression; explicit reference to a content-rich program and Core Knowledge/Hillsdale framework.
- Pedagogy: CLASSICAL. Socratic Seminars, teacher-led instruction, and a memory/recitation orientation evident in public materials; some restful / contemplative elements implied through scholé-y frames in the classical lexicon.
- Faculty: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL. Public statements emphasize highly qualified teachers and teacher-led instruction; however, a fully articulated “living curriculum” persona or explicit faculty-as-curriculum signal is less explicit.
- Parents & Community: CLASSICAL. Active family involvement via Get Involved/Volunteer channels and open enrollment/family engagement signals, with clear opportunities for parents to participate.
