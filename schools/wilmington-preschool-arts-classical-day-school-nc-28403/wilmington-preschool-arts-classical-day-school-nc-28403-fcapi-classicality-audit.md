## Executive Summary

Wilmington Preschool of the Arts (WPA) in Wilmington, NC 28403 demonstrates strengths in parent-partnership and community connection, but its public materials do not align with a fully classical six-element rubric. Across the six categories, WPA shows clear alignment with modern, child-centered, play-based early education in terms of pedagogy, curriculum breadth for early childhood, and familial engagement. However, the site does not present explicit evidence of classical elements such as Great Books, rigorous Socratic dialogue, traditional liberal-arts tria, or explicitly catechetical or liturgical practices. On balance, WPA sits primarily in the Not Classical to Somewhat Classical range, with the strongest alignment in Parents & Community and a more tentative alignment in Faculty.

Element 4: Pedagogy or Teaching Methods — NOT CLASSICAL

Evidence

- WPA emphasizes a warm, relational, contemplative, play-based approach with singing and circle-time in early years, memory and engagement rather than a strictly Socratic/seminar-based approach in practice. The site describes “Joyful and Meaningful Play-based Learning,” with a strong emphasis on relationships, routines, and exploration. There is explicit note of singing in Toddlers and a strong emphasis on memory through song in lower levels, which aligns with a traditional pedagogy but not with the classical pedagogy’s typical emphasis on Socratic discussions and rhetorical training in the upper grades.

Positive/Negative keyword analysis

- Positive keywords present: “singing,” “circle time,” “memory,” “group projects,” “cooperation,” and “memory work” (these terms appear in WPA’s pedagogy and program descriptions and align with a more traditional, musical/memory-based pedagogy, but not the strict classical pedagogy emphasis on Socratic seminars or rational discourse).
- Negative keywords present: absence of explicit Socratic seminars, dialectic discourse, or formal rhetoric training in K-8 within WPA materials.

Why this rating

- While there are elements of contemplative, memory-based pedagogy (e.g., singing, circle-time memory), the absence of explicit Socratic/Paideia-style seminars and formal rhetoric instruction in the lower grades signals a NOT CLASSICAL pedagogy.
