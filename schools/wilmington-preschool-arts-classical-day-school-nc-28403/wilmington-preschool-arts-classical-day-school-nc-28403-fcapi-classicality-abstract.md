# ABSTRACT

WPA exhibits strong parental engagement and a warm, child-centered approach but does not demonstrate classical curriculum or pedagogy signals at the level required by the Six Essential Elements of a Classical School rubric. The school’s public-facing materials emphasize family partnerships, a Golden Rule-centered ethos, and a developmentally appropriate, play-based curriculum across early childhood stages, rather than Great Books, classical languages, Socratic dialogue, or liturgical practices. The strongest classical alignment lies in the Parents & Community category, where WPA shows explicit partnership with families and open, ongoing communications. The Faculty category shows some alignment with teacher mentorship and living-curriculum vibes, but lacks explicit and formal classical signals. The Religious & Metaphysical Foundations, Purpose & Goal, Curriculum, and Pedagogy categories show insufficient classical signals to achieve a CLASSICAL rating. Overall, WPA is NOT CLASSICAL, with some weak-to-moderate classical threads (principally in community/family partnership and some faculty signals).

Key source signals:

- Golden Rule orientation and family partnership emphasis in Our Approach (Evidence: “Golden Rule… We partner with each family to identify individual goals and plans for the children…”) .
- Strong parent communications and on-site community resources (Evidence: “Parent-teacher conferences,” “daily digital communications,” and “community resources”) .
- Early-childhood, play-based curriculum with emphasis on social-emotional development, memory through songs, circle-time, and group activities (Evidence: Toddlers circle-time and singing; learning-through-play language in Preschool/Pre-K) .
