# Classicality Audit Report

Executive Summary
- School: Austin Classical School (ACS)
- Website used: https://austinclassical.org/
- Overall classification: CLASSICAL
- Why: ACS explicitly centers its mission on cultivating wisdom and virtue ordered to the true, the good, and the beautiful; integrates Christian theology across the curriculum; employs the Trivium with Latin, logic, and rhetoric; features memory, singing/chanting, debate/discussion, and oratory; positions teachers as formational models; and operates a collaborative schedule that treats parents as co‑teachers. Evidence appears across mission, academics, and community pages, and strongly aligns with the positive indicators in the “Six Essential Elements” keyword lexicon used for this audit    .
- Notable insights:
  - ACS states its aim is “to cultivate wisdom and virtue” through contemplation of truth, goodness, and beauty, and distinguishes itself from a “traditional college preparatory institution” whose primary aim is college placement; ACS’s goal is “life,” not merely “college”  .
  - Religious and liturgical life is explicit (Christ‑centered, chapel, prayer; instruction “rooted” in the Statement of Faith; integration “under the sovereignty of God through theology”)     .
  - Pedagogy highlights memory, recitation, songs and chants; debate/discussion (logic); rhetoric culminating in thesis defense and eloquent speech; community Recitatio event   .

Note on method: This audit follows the “Six Essential Elements of a Classical School” positive/negative keyword indicators and the classicality lexicon provided, applied across six categories: Religious & Metaphysical Foundations; Purpose & Goal; Curriculum; Pedagogy; Faculty; Parents & Community. Each category lists the school’s onsite keywords/phrases, compares them to the provided lexicon, offers a quantitative positive/negative estimate, and justifies the rating   .

Aggregate Assessment
- Category ratings:
  - Religious & Metaphysical Foundations: CLASSICAL
  - Purpose & Goal: CLASSICAL
  - Curriculum: CLASSICAL
  - Pedagogy: CLASSICAL
  - Faculty: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL
  - Parents & Community: CLASSICAL
- Overall classification: CLASSICAL

Why: ACS hits all core classical distinctives with strong, explicit language across mission, curriculum, and pedagogy, and clearly engages parents as partners. Faculty formation indicators are positive but less fully articulated in public materials; even so, the aggregate profile is decisively classical.

What would move the needle (Faculty)
- Publish a formal “Portrait of a Teacher” and/or “Portrait of a Graduate” linking teacher virtues to student formation outcomes (virtue, wisdom, rhetoric) to increase explicit coverage of faculty criteria from the classical lexicon  .
- Publicize ongoing faculty formation in classical pedagogy (e.g., great‑books seminars for faculty, collegium/retreats) to evidence a “faculty of friends” and formal mentorship structures .
