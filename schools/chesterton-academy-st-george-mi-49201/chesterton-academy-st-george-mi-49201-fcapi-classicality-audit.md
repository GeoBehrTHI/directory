## Executive Summary

Chesterton Academy of St. George (Jackson, MI) presents a clearly Catholic, classical-leaning high school program anchored in a traditional liberal-arts orientation. Public-facing materials emphasize a three-pillar model (Intellect, Character, Spirituality), a Culture of Life, daily Mass and liturgical practices, and a curriculum described as “classical” with Latin study and a liberal-arts emphasis. Taken together, these materials align strongly with classical-education norms in Religious & Metaphysical Foundations, Purpose & Goals, Curriculum, Pedagogy, Faculty, and Parent/Community engagement. Across categories, the school consistently evidences classical signals (Catholic theology, virtuous formation, memorization with memory work in the tradition, Socratic/ritorical elements in some settings, and robust family/community involvement). The aggregate rating is CLASSICAL.

Evidence across the site highlights:
- Religious and metaphysical foundations: Catholic identity permeates the mission, daily Mass, liturgical prayer, and a Culture of Life framing the school’s purpose (e.g., “Our Model of Education” with Intellect/Character/Spirituality; daily prayer and liturgy; Gospel-centered formation)   .
- Purpose and goal: Formation-focused aims (virtue, wisdom, truth/beauty/goodness) with explicit formation through Catholic faith, and a “Culture of Life” orientation rather than job-prep emphasis (Our Mission statements; Catholic-theology focus)  .
- Curriculum: Classical signals include a stated classical curriculum, Memoria Press Latin emphasis, and an orientation toward liberal-arts education; explicit references to Western thought and the classics are present in the model of education descriptions  .
- Pedagogy: Seminal classical-teaching elements appear (Socratic-style opportunities during shadow days; rhetoric training; opportunities for public speaking; and a structure that supports both contemplative elements and rigorous seminars)  .
- Faculty: Visible “Faculty Gallery with Bios” and a culture of mentorship and living curriculum are evidenced on the site, along with notable faculty involvement in shaping the learning environment .
- Parents & Community: Formal partnership with families, home-school partnerships, open houses, and events that invite parental involvement and transparency are promoted (Homeschool partnerships; Crusader-for-a-Day; Open Houses)  .

Overall, Chesterton Academy St. George demonstrates a consistently classical orientation across all six criteria, with explicit religious formation, classical-curriculum signals, contemplative and Socratic pedagogy, and active parental/community engagement. The aggregate classification is CLASSICAL.
