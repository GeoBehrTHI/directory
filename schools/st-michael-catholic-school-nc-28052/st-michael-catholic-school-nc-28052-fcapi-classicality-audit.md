## Executive Summary

St Michael Catholic School NC 28052 (Saint Michael Catholic School) presents a robust classical Catholic program that aligns strongly with the core six elements of classical education. The public materials emphasize the Catholic, Trinity-inspired aim of education, the Seven Liberal Arts (notably the Trivium and Quadrivium), a deliberately formed culture of virtue and wisdom, and a pedagogy that blends memory work and contemplative learning with Socratic inquiry in the upper grades. The school explicitly grounds its mission in truth, beauty, and goodness, with liturgical practices (Mass and Rosary), and a liberal-arts curriculum informed by Ratio Studiorum and Catholic tradition. Faculty are presented as experienced professionals within a relational, community-centered school, and parents are positioned as co-educators within a parish-connected ecosystem.

Overall assessment: CLASSICAL.

Key observations:
- Religious and metaphysical foundations are explicit and pervasive (Catholic identity, liturgical life, sacred time, and Ratio Studiorum). Evidence includes daily Mass, Rosary, and explicit Catholic formation in the curriculum. 
- The school’s explicit aim is the formation of virtue and wisdom through a liberal-arts framework, anchored by truth, goodness, and beauty. 
- Curriculum centers on the Seven Liberal Arts with the Trivium and Quadrivium, plus Latin studies; this is a defining feature of its classical approach. 
- Pedagogy is described in stages (Grammar, Logic, Rhetoric) with opportunities for Socratic discussion and rhetoric in the upper grades, aligning with classical pedagogy. 
- Faculty emphasis is on teachers as mentors within a living-curriculum/community-centered model; current faculty profiles are publicly visible. 
- Parents and community are integrated as partners (parish ties, annual funds, open invitations to visits, scholarship programs).
