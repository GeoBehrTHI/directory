- Purpose and Goal: CLASSICAL (formation of virtue and wisdom; liberal-arts telos; civic virtue and self-governance emphasized).

- Pedagogy: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL (lower grades: direct instruction; upper grades: Socratic methods; memory/chanting in lower grades; lack of explicit scholé/restful pedagogy in public materials).
- Faculty: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL (clear leadership and capable staff; living-curriculum language not explicit in public materials).
- Parents & Community: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL (clear parental access and involvement via enrollment and volunteering; contract language signals broader parent-participation frameworks that could be highlighted more publicly).

Aggregate assessment: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL

This assessment reflects a robust alignment with classical education in several core respects, tempered by gaps in explicit liturgical/theological grounding, explicit Great Books framing, and a fully articulated living-faculty/parent-formation model in the public-facing materials. The school shows promising fidelity to classical aims, with a clear path to full CLASSICAL status via targeted public articulation of the needle-mover items described above.
