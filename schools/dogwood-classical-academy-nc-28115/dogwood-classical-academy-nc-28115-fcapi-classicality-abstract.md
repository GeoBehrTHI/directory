## ABSTRACT

Dogwood Classical Academy NC 28115 demonstrates a fundamentally classical orientation, with strong emphasis on a Truth-Goodness-Beauty moral framework, a Great Books–driven curriculum, and Socratic dialogue as core pedagogy. The school presents a clear formation agenda (mind and character, virtuous living, civic responsibility) and a staged approach to learning that aligns with the Trivium in practice. However, explicit theological grounding, liturgical/community practices, and formal documentation of faculty as the living curriculum and robust parent/community partnership programs are not clearly evidenced in the public materials reviewed. Consequently, the school is best characterized as SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL overall, with Element 2 and Element 4 clearly CLASSICAL and several other categories showing substantial, but not complete, alignment.

Key category takeaways:
- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL (transcendental moral realism evidenced; explicit theology and liturgy not evident in public materials).
- School Purpose and Goal: CLASSICAL (formation-focused, virtue-centered, truth/goodness/beauty oriented).
- Curriculum: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL (Great Books and Trivium-like progression evident; explicit naming of grammar/logic/rhetoric not always explicit in public copy).
- Pedagogy: CLASSICAL (Socratic dialogue, seminars, and rhetoric training evident).
- Faculty: NOT CLASSICAL (no explicit living-curriculum or faculty portraits in public materials).
- Parents & Community: NOT CLASSICAL (no explicit parent-partner programs described).
