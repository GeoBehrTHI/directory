## Executive Summary

Covenant Christian Academy (Harrisburg, PA 17109) presents a clearly CLASSICAL Christian educational model grounded in the liberal arts and traditional forms of instruction. Public-facing materials consistently emphasize a Christ-centered, theologically informed approach that foregrounds the trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric) and quadrivium, the Great Books/Great Conversation tradition, and a liturgical-catechetical ethos (e.g., morning liturgy, psalms, hymns, sacred music). The school routinely describes aims of formation, virtue, wisdom, and character, rather than a narrow focus on job preparation, and it depicts faculty as living exemplars and mentors who model virtue and classical pedagogy. Parent partnership and community life are repeatedly highlighted as essential to the Covenant model. Taken together, the public materials strongly align Covenant with the six classical elements and present a cohesive Classical Christian program.

Overall classification: CLASSICAL.

Key insights:
- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations are explicit and robust: Christ-centered formation, theology as a governing influence on curriculum, liturgical and communal practices, and explicit Christian doctrinal framing are evident across admissions, mission, and curricular descriptions.   
- Purpose and Goal emphasize formation, virtue, wisdom, and human flourishing, with language about the true/good/beautiful and Great Books conversations shaping formation.  
- Curriculum explicitly foregrounds the Seven Liberal Arts (grammar, logic, rhetoric; Quadrivium), Great Books/Great Conversation, and history-integrated pedagogy—central markers of classical education.  
- Pedagogy highlights Socratic questioning in the upper grades, memorization/singing/chanting in the lower grades, and rhetoric/recitation within the upper school, with an intentional move toward disciplined oratory and debate.  
- Faculty are portrayed as mission-oriented mentors, with named roles (Lead Humanities Teacher, Upper School Dean, etc.) and a portrait of teachers as living curriculum and cohort of virtuous educators. 
- Parents and Community are integral, with explicit statements that Covenant serves in loco parentis, invites parental involvement, and provides education for parents about classical education; the campus emphasizes open access and tours.
