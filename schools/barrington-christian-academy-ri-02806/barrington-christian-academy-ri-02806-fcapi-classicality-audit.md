# Classicality Audit: Bethel Classical Academy

Website used: https://bethelclassical.org/ 

Executive Summary
- Overall classification: CLASSICAL
- Why: The school explicitly centers formation on truth, goodness, and beauty, integrates theology across disciplines, practices chapel/catechesis/Scripture memorization, employs the Trivium with Latin and integrated, chronologically taught history, and uses discussion, debate, logic, and rhetoric in the upper grades. Parent partnership and community worship are emphasized. Faculty claims highlight living the faith, though a fuller “portrait of a teacher” and collegial formation language are less explicit on the public site (suggesting room to strengthen Category 5 signals). Evidence is consistent with the Six Essential Elements framework and lexicon.      

Note on method: The keyword audit below uses positive/negative indicators from Six Essential Elements of a Classical School (Religious Foundations; Purpose/Goal; Curriculum; Pedagogy; Faculty; Parents/Community) to quantify hits and coverage before assigning ratings.

- Memory/recitation: catechisms and Scripture memorization in community spiritual life (supports a culture of memorization). 

2) Comparison with Six Elements lexicon
- Positive indicators matched: discussion/dialectic; debate and rhetoric; memory/recitation (catechesis/Scripture memorization). 
- Positive indicators not explicitly observed on academics pages: Socratic seminar language; lower-school classroom “singing/chanting” of content; scholé/restfulness.
- Negative indicator present: “Academic Excellence and Rigor” language can signal less emphasis on scholé/restfulness if not balanced.  

3) Quantification
- Positive hits: +5 (discussion-based, debate, logic study, rhetoric training, memorization via catechism/Scripture)
- Negative hits: -1 (“Academic … Rigor” as a potential tension with restfulness)

- Percent coverage (lower-school singing/chanting; wonder; upper-school Socratic/seminar/debate; rhetoric orations): 0.70 (clear debate/logic/rhetoric; less explicit on K–6 chanting and scholé)

4) Rating: CLASSICAL

Aggregate Rating: CLASSICAL

- Score rationale: 5 categories rated CLASSICAL, 1 category SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL (Faculty). The core of religious/metaphysical foundations, purpose/goal, curriculum, pedagogy, and parent/community practices clearly align with the classical Christian model using the lexicon’s positive indicators. Faculty formation signaling could be more explicit publicly, but current evidence is consistent with a classical ethos.
