# Augustine School (Jackson, TN) — Classicality Audit

Website audited: https://www.augustineschool.com/

ACCS accreditation: affirmed in school news post (“now officially accredited by the Association of Classical Christian Schools”) 

Using the Six Essential Elements of a Classical School keyword framework and lexicon for positive/negative indicators across six categories  

## Executive Summary
Overall classification: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL

Why: Augustine School publicly foregrounds the classical Christian telos (truth, goodness, beauty; wisdom and virtue), biblical/theological grounding, and partnership with families. However, the public site materials in the provided crawl do not yet make explicit the curricular and pedagogical distinctives typical of classical schools (e.g., trivium, Latin/logic/rhetoric, Great Books; pedagogy terms like Socratic seminar, memorization/recitation/chant, scholé). Strengthened curricular and pedagogy pages would likely move this school to a full CLASSICAL rating.

Top signals observed:
- Religious/metaphysical framing is explicit: “Biblically‑Based,” “Gospel‑Centered,” formation “for His Kingdom,” “Season of Prayer” guides; repeated appeal to “goodness, truth, and beauty” and “wise and virtuous” formation  .
- School purpose emphasizes formation (virtue/wisdom) and partnership with families; PK–12 scope, founded 2001, ~210 students; CLT results highlighted (not determinative of classicality, but supportive) .
- Curriculum and pedagogy claims are general (“classical model,” “classically‑informed”) without on‑site specificity (e.g., trivium/Latin/logic/rhetoric/Socratic/recitation) in the crawl captures.

Key data points:
- Grades: PK–12, Students: ~210, Founded: 2001 (as displayed on homepage highlights) .

How this audit was done:
- Keyword analysis grounded in the Six Essential Elements indicators (positive/negative lists) and the companion lexicon; then quantified per category before assigning ratings  .

## Aggregate Assessment
- Category ratings: [1] Classical, [2] Classical, [3] Somewhat Classical, [4] Not Classical, [5] Somewhat Classical, [6] Classical.
- Overall classification: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL

Rationale: The school’s public materials strongly reflect classical Christian foundations and aims and present a healthy parent‑school partnership. To merit a full CLASSICAL rating, the site should explicitly publish curricular specifics (trivium; Latin/logic/rhetoric; Great Books/primary sources; “history” rather than “social studies”) and pedagogy hallmarks (Socratic/dialectic, memorization/recitation/chant, scholé/restful learning, rhetoric orations), which are currently implied but not demonstrated in the crawl captures.

---

## What would move the needle (site‑content improvements)
- Curriculum page:
  - Name the trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric) and quadrivium; list Latin and logic/rhetoric course sequences for grades 7–12; state “history” explicitly and avoid “social studies” language; link to a representative Great Books/primary texts reading list. (All are strong positives in the Six Elements framework)  .
- Pedagogy page:
  - Describe K–6 memory work and recitation/chant; 7–12 Socratic seminars/dialectic; debate/oratory and senior thesis/oration in rhetoric years; publish a sample seminar prompt or capstone description.  .
- Faculty formation:
  - Publish a “Portrait of a Teacher” and/or “Faculty of Friends” statement; note teacher formation in classical pedagogy and ongoing Great Books seminars/collegiality.  .
- Parent formation:
  - Add parent reading groups/book studies or classical education workshops as recurring offerings (if already happening, surface them on the site). .
