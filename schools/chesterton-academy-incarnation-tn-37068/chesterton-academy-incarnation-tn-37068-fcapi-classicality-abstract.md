## ABSTRACT

Chesterton Academy of the Incarnation (Franklin, TN) demonstrates a robust classical education model with strong Catholic formation. Across six classical-category facets, CAI shows a consistent CLASSICAL orientation:

- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: CAI foregrounds Catholic identity, daily Mass, liturgical life, and theology-infused pedagogy, consistent with transcendental moral realism and liturgical practices.
- School Purpose and Goal: The school emphasizes formation, virtue, and wisdom as telos, with public statements framing education as a high-purpose formation in truth, goodness, and beauty.
- Curriculum: The curriculum integrates the liberal arts (grammar, logic, rhetoric) and history across periods, with explicit Great Books emphasis and the Great Conversation, all within a Western-Christian theological frame.
- Pedagogy: Pedagogical methods include Socratic dialogue, memory work, and contemplation (Scholé), balancing rigorous inquiry with restful learning and formation through imitation.
- Faculty: The faculty is portrayed as virtuous, living exemplars who embody the living curriculum and honor fidelity to Catholic teaching, with strong leadership and teacher formation.
- Parents & Community: The school actively engages families through open houses, information nights, and community events, aligning with classical-parent partnership ideals.

Overall classification: CLASSICAL

CAI’s public materials consistently connect education to formation, virtue, and the larger Western-Catholic intellectual heritage, with canonical texts, Socratic inquiry, and a strong liturgical life, all of which align with the core elements of classical education and the Six Essential Elements doctrine.
