## Executive Summary

Holy Rosary Academy (Anchorage, AK; ZIP 99503) presents a cohesive classical-Catholic program that aligns strongly with the Six Essential Elements of Classical Education. Public materials from the school emphasize a Trivium-based curriculum (grammar, logic, rhetoric), engagement with Great Books and Western canonical works, and pedagogical practices such as seminars, oral recitation, and a thesis-based senior project. The school situates education within a Catholic theological framework, incorporating Mass, the Rosary, and liturgical practices as central to daily life. Family partnership and parental formation are foregrounded, with explicit statements about assisting parents as primary educators. Across six categories, Holy Rosary Academy demonstrates a predominantly CLASSICAL orientation, with robust evidence of religious-metaphysical foundations, formation-focused aims, liberal-arts curriculum, Socratic/seminar-based pedagogy, a living-faculty model, and active parent/community partnership. No substantive signals in public materials indicate a drift toward non-classical orientations.
