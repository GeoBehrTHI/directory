## ABSTRACT

Holy Rosary Academy (Anchorage, AK 99503) is CLASSICAL across all six categories: Religious & Metaphysical Foundations, School Purpose & Goal, Curriculum, Pedagogy, Faculty, and Parents & Community. The school foregrounds Catholic formation and liturgical life (Mass, Rosary, Stations of the Cross) integrated with a robust classical curriculum (grammar, logic, rhetoric; Great Books; Western civilization; Latin) delivered through a mix of Socratic seminars, Scholé/restful learning, memorization/recitation, and thesis-driven upper-level work. Faculty are presented as a living curriculum with dedicated roles, and parents are positioned as partners and co-educators in a formation-focused educational project. The aggregate assessment indicates a strong, coherent classical-education program anchored in faith, reason, and virtue, with measurable alignment to the Six Essential Elements of Classical Education.
