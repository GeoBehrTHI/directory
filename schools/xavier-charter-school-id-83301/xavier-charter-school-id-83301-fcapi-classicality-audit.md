## Executive Summary

Xavier Charter School (Twin Falls, ID, 83301) presents a robust classical-education profile across public materials on its site. The school consistently signals a classical frame through explicit statements about a liberal-arts core, mastery-based teaching, memorization/recitation, Socratic dialogue, and an emphasis on Western Civilization and Great Books. Public materials also highlight character formation, community partnerships with families, and a mission to cultivate wisdom and virtue, not merely to prepare students for work. Taken together, these signals place Xavier Charter School firmly in the CLASSICAL category, with especially strong alignment in Curriculum, Pedagogy, Faculty, and Parents & Community. Evidence of a theological or liturgical dimension exists in mentions of religious study and moral formation, though explicit liturgical practices are less foregrounded; the core rationale leans on a theologically-inflected classical framework. The aggregate assessment is CLASSICAL, with only minor ambiguity in the explicit ritual-liturgical dimension.
