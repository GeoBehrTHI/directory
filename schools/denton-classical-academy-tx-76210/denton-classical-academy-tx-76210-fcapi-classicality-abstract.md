## ABSTRACT

Denton Classical Academy (DCA) demonstrates a classical orientation with a clear emphasis on formation, virtue, and a liberal-arts-based curriculum. Category-by-category analysis indicates CLASSICAL strength in School Purpose and Goal, with emerging alignment in Religious and Metaphysical Foundations, Curriculum, Pedagogy, Faculty, and Parents & Community. The aggregate classification is SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL, reflecting a strong classical orientation but incomplete or less explicit demonstration of some canonical elements (notably explicit Great Books/Great Conversation commitments and fully articulated Socratic pedagogy). The school’s public materials show a commitment to truth, goodness, and beauty, and a mission-oriented focus on forming virtuous, capable citizens, while the explicit theological or liturgical dimensions are less clearly evidenced on the public site. The presence of a real, functioning faculty and visible family-partnership language underpins the SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL positioning, with targeted opportunities to move toward CLASSICAL through deeper articulation and implementation of canonical curricular and pedagogical practices and stronger parent-education programs.
