## ABSTRACT

Heart of Ohio Classical Academy (HOCA) – Dublin Campus demonstrates a robust classical orientation through a content-rich liberal arts and sciences curriculum, emphasis on virtue/character formation, Latin and phonics instruction, and teacher-led pedagogy, all anchored in a Western-tradition framework. Public materials reveal a strong focus on moral formation, civic virtue, and the centrality of the Western canon, with explicit commitments to parental partnership and a tech-conscious learning environment. However, explicit theological or liturgical foundations are not publicly foregrounded, and explicit, campus-wide Socratic seminar pedagogy is not consistently documented in the materials provided for HOCA’s Dublin campus. Consequently, HOCA’s overall alignment with classical education is best described as SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL, with several core components strongly present (Curriculum, Purpose/Goal, and Community) and others (Religious/Metaphysical Foundations, Pedagogy breadth, and nuanced Faculty articulation) less explicit in the public materials.

ABSTRACT (concluding)
Heart of Ohio Classical Academy – Dublin Campus exhibits a clear classical orientation in curriculum, purpose, and community engagement, with strong signals of virtue-culture, Great Books-like framing, and Western-tradition emphasis. The school’s public materials demonstrate formation-focused aims, liberal arts grounding, and a robust parental partnership, which align with classical ideals. However, explicit theological-liturgical grounding and a campus-wide, documented Socratic/seminar pedagogy are less evident, placing HOCA in the SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL category across several dimensions. The aggregate assessment yields SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL, with concrete, needle-moving opportunities to deepen Socratic pedagogy, articulate a more explicit living-faculty portrait, and reinforce theological-liturgical integrations where appropriate.
