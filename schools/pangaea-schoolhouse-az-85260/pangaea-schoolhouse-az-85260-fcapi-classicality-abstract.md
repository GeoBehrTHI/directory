## ABSTRACT

Pangaea Schoolhouse demonstrates a CLASSICAL orientation overall, with particularly strong alignment in Religious and Metaphysical Foundations, School Purpose and Goal, Faculty, and Parents & Community. The Curriculum and Pedagogy categories show substantial classical elements but are currently rated SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL due to less explicit public documentation of Great Books/Great Conversation and some traditional memory-based pedagogy components. The school employs Trivium-based curricula, Socratic dialogue, faith-centered formation, and a robust parent-community partnership, all of which align with classical education ideals. The aggregate evaluation is CLASSICAL, acknowledging that two categories (Curriculum and Pedagogy) sit at SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL and would benefit from additional explicit public documentation of Great Books/Great Conversation and explicit memory-work practices to reach full CLASSICAL alignment.

Key evidence includes: explicit Trivium references and integration with modern inquiry (classical foundations with modern application) ; the explicit articulation of truth, goodness, beauty and faith-centered formation (Religious Foundations; Purpose and Goal) ; integrated learning with daily prayer and a faith-centered community ; and a living-curriculum model with mentors and close teacher-student relationships . The public materials also emphasize parental partnership and open engagement with families and communities.
