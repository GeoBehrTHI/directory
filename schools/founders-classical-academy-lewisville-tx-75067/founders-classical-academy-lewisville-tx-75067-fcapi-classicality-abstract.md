## ABSTRACT

Founders Classical Academy Lewisville, TX demonstrates strong CLASSICAL alignment overall, with explicit emphasis on Great Books, grammar/logical/rhetoric, Western Civilization, and a formation-focused rationale for education. Curriculum documentation shows a knowledge-rich approach and a progression from foundational language arts to advanced rhetoric, with Latin/Greek roots and logic in middle/high school, aligning with the liberal-arts core of classical education. Faculty and parent-community signals show explicit virtuous modeling by teachers and a partnership with families, reinforcing a culture consistent with classical paideia.

Category-by-category highlights:
- Religious & Metaphysical Foundations: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL. The school foregrounds virtue, truth, beauty, and formation, but lacks explicit liturgical/theological grounding in public content.
- School Purpose & Goal: CLASSICAL. Formation and wisdom are central; education is framed as formation rather than mere information, with a liberal-arts mission aligned to true/good/beautiful.
- Curriculum: CLASSICAL. Grammar/logics/rhetoric; Great Books; Latin/Greek; Western Civilization; history emphasis; strong alignment with classical curriculum.
- Pedagogy: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL. Upper-grade dialectic/seminar/rhetoric features exist; lower-grade contemplative/recitation/Scholé signals are not clearly documented in public materials.
- Faculty: CLASSICAL. Teachers described as models of virtue and the living curriculum, aligning with classical faculty signals.
- Parents & Community: CLASSICAL. Active parent partnership and community involvement, consistent with classical pedagogy.
