## Executive Summary

Summit Christian Academy (SCA) in Yorktown, VA shows a clearly classical Christian orientation across public materials and internal documents. The school consistently foregrounds a Christian worldview, integration of theology with academics, a Trivium-based curriculum, and a pedagogy built around memorization, disciplined inquiry, and rhetorical communication. Public materials emphasize partnership with families, a chapel-centered life, classical curriculum (grammar–dialectic–rhetoric), Latin, Great Books/the Great Conversation framing, and explicit affirmation of truth, goodness, and beauty as real and knowable. The school also highlights faculty leadership and a strong parent/community partnership, including open communication with families and parental involvement in school life. Taken together, these signals align strongly with classical education as described in the classical education rubric and associated keyword lexicon.
