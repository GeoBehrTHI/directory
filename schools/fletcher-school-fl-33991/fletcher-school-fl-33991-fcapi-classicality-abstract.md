## ABSTRACT

The Fletcher School (Southwest Florida) demonstrates CLASSICAL alignment across several core dimensions—Religious and Metaphysical Foundations, and School Purpose and Goal—with explicit Christian/theological grounding, formation-focused aims, and a divinely centered approach to truth, goodness, and beauty. The Curriculum shows strong classical scaffolding (grammar-logic-rhetoric progression; Latin; Bible; History; Literature), reinforcing a CLASSICAL orientation, though the public materials do not consistently articulate a complete Great Books/Great Conversation program in explicit language for all grade levels, resulting in a SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL rating for Curriculum. Pedagogy also shows classical signals (logic, rhetoric, debate; integrated curriculum), but lacks consistent documentation of Socratic pedagogy or formal Scholé/restful practices across the public materials, leading to a SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL rating for Pedagogy. Faculty descriptions are not explicit in the public materials, which yields a NOT CLASSICAL rating for Faculty. Parents & Community are strongly classical, evidenced by explicit emphasis on parental roles, biblical instruction for families, and partnership-oriented language, earning CLASSICAL.

Overall, the aggregate assessment is SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL, reflecting a robust classical-Christian orientation with clear formation and liberal-arts structuring, while showing gaps in explicit pedagogical practice (Socratic/Scholé) and faculty portrayal in public-facing materials. This suggests Fletcher School is closer to a classical model than to a non-classical one, but with notable room for fuller alignment across pedagogy and faculty articulation.
