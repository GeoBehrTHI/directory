## Executive Summary

Grove City Christian Academy (GCCA) presents a robust Classical Christian profile across its public-facing materials. The school foregrounds a Christian worldview integrated with classical education through the Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric), a strong emphasis on Great Conversations with classical authors, and explicit reference to Latin study, rhetoric, and foundational liberal-arts training. Public materials repeatedly describe GCCA as “a Christian education in the classical tradition” and emphasize a community oriented around Christ, Scripture, and worship-like practices, with notable acknowledgment of Great Books/Great Conversation and the Great Conversation as central to its identity. The school also highlights parent leadership and partnership, a multi-campus structure, and a history rooted in classical Christian pedagogy. Taken together, these signals align GCCA with a Classical education model in a way that meets core criteria across multiple categories.

- Evidentiary anchors include explicit statements about Christ-centered, Word-of-God grounded education, the Trivium, Great Conversation with Homer, Dante, Augustine, Tolkien, Lewis, and the Great Books tradition, and a clearly stated formation-focused aim (learning for a lifetime; virtuous formation through rhetoric and logic). See sources describing the Christian/framework base, Trivium and Great Conversation, and explicit references to Latin, logic, rhetoric, and classical literature.   
- The organization also shows a strong parental-partner model (Parent-Sponsored; parent board governance), which aligns with the classical emphasis on the partnership between family and school in the formation of character. 
- At a glance, GCCA demonstrates consistent alignment with the classical rubric’s core elements (religious/metaphysical, formation-focused purpose, Trivium-based curriculum, contemplative/rigor balance in pedagogy, clearly defined faculty/community norms, and active parent/community engagement).
