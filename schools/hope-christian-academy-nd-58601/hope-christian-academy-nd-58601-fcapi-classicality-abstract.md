# ABSTRACT

Overall Classification: CLASSICAL

Key drivers:

- Religious & Metaphysical Foundations: Strong Christ-centered theology, the Word of God, and liturgical/community practices (Chapel) anchor the school’s philosophy, aligning with transcendental moral realism and theological grounding.
- Purpose and Goal: Formation-focused aims (virtue, wisdom, human flourishing) are explicit, with language around truth, goodness, and beauty as real and knowable, signaling a classical telos.
- Curriculum: Public materials reflect classical leanings (rhetoric, history, Biblical worldview, capstone projects) though explicit universal mention of all Seven Liberal Arts and Great Books could be strengthened in public articulation.
- Pedagogy: Restful and memory-based elements in early grades, plus rhetoric and public speaking in upper grades, with contemplative elements implied, aligning with classical pedagogy though explicit Socratic seminar labeling is less uniform in public content.
- Faculty: The living-curriculum concept and staff-as-mentors language aligns with classical formation.
- Parents & Community: Strong partnership with parents and deliberate family-formation content demonstrates a core classical community approach.

Overall, Hope Christian Academy ND 58601 presents a coherent CLASSICAL model with strong religious foundations, formation-focused goals, and active family engagement, and with room to further crystallize explicit canonical elements in Curriculum and Pedagogy for an even more pronounced canonical alignment.
