## ABSTRACT

Redeemer Classical School (Keezletown, VA, 22832) demonstrates a strong CLASSICAL orientation across most dimensions of the classical-education rubric. The public-facing materials reveal a Trivium-centered curriculum (grammar, logic, rhetoric), engagement with Great Books and the Great Conversation, and pedagogy that includes chanting/memory-work in the grammar stage and Socratic/seminar-style discourse in the upper grades. There is a clear theological and metaphysical foundation, with integrated Christian worldview and explicit statements that “all truth is God’s truth,” aligning with transcendental moral realism and theological grounding. The faculty is described as living exemplars and mentors, reinforcing the living-curriculum ideal.

Category-by-category overview:
- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: CLASSICAL. Evidence includes integrated Christian worldview and explicit references to truth/goodness/beauty as real goods in education.
- School Purpose and Goal: CLASSICAL. Emphasis on formation and virtue over mere information or job-readiness.
- Curriculum: CLASSICAL. Trivium structure, Great Books emphasis, and historic-integration signals align with classical content.
- Pedagogy: CLASSICAL. Scholé/restful learning in lower grades, memorization and chant, and Socratic/seminar-style upper-level pedagogy.
- Faculty: CLASSICAL. Teachers as mentors and living curriculum; portrait of teacher excellence.
- Parents & Community: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL. Public materials indicate Christian community and family-integration, but explicit, explicit parent-partnership language and programs are less visible on the public site.

Overall, Redeemer Classical School VA 22832 is CLASSICAL, with a clear, demonstrated classical core and a credible, if not fully explicit, pathway for fuller engagement of parents as co-teachers. The school’s direction is consistent with classical aims of formation, wisdom, virtue, and the Great Conversation, anchored in a Christian theological framework.
