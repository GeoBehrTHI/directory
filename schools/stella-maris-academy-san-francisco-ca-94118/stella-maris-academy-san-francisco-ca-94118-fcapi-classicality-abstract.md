## ABSTRACT

Stella Maris Academy San Francisco functions as a PK–8 Catholic classical school, with a mission deeply rooted in truth, goodness, and beauty within a Catholic framework. The school demonstrates a consistent CLASSICAL orientation across all six categories:

- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: Strong Catholic identity with liturgical life and integrated theology, supported by mission language centering on truth, goodness, and beauty.   
- School Purpose and Goal: Formation of virtue and wisdom, not mere information or job-readiness, with explicit emphasis on character and moral imagination. 
- Curriculum: Classical liberal-arts emphasis with grammar–logic–rhetoric progression, Latin study, and integrated Catholic education; history is framed across disciplines.  
- Pedagogy: Classical progression (Grammar–Logic–Rhetoric) with contemplative and wonder-centered elements in early years; opportunities for rhetoric and expression as students advance. 
- Faculty: Mentors who embody and deliver the living curriculum; clergy involvement and mission-aligned teaching culture.  
- Parents & Community: Strong partnership with parents, welcoming parent involvement and ongoing education about classical education through admissions and events.  

Overall, Stella Maris Academy San Francisco is CLASSICAL, with each category aligning with the core tenets of classical education as described in the Six Essential Elements framework. The school embodies the transcendental aims of truth, goodness, and beauty; a formation-focused purpose; a robust classical-curriculum structure; pedagogical alignment with grammar/logic/reasoning and contemplative dimensions; a faculty rooted in virtue and living the curriculum; and a community that partners closely with families.

End of ABSTRACT.
