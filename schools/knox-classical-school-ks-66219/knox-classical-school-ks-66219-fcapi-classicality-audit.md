## Executive Summary

Knox Classical School KS 66219 presents a consistently classical, Christian model across public materials and internal communications. The school foregrounds a Christ-centered, biblically integrated framework, explicit engagement with Great Books and the Great Conversation, and a Trivium-based curriculum (grammar, logic, rhetoric) supplemented by the Quadrivium through depth in mathematics, music, and sciences. Pedagogical methods emphasize contemplative (Scholé) rhythms in lower grades and Socratic discussion in upper grades, with strong emphasis on memorization, recitation, and virtue formation. Parents are positioned as co-teachers and partners in a two-day, home-optional pedagogical model. Taken together, these signals align Knox KS 66219 with a Classical, Christian, Collaborative approach and yield a firm CLASSICAL rating overall.

Key signals include explicit statements about:

- Religious & metaphysical foundations: Christ-centered, biblically integrated education, and a stated Mission/Vision with a Statement of Faith driving curricular framing. Evidence and framing appear across the site’s mission pages and theology-focused postings.
- Purpose & goals: Formation of virtue and wisdom through a classical paideia, virtue-centered aims, and the Great Conversation as a core pedagogical aim.
- Curriculum: Liberal-arts emphasis (grammar/logic/rhetoric), Great Books, Great Conversation, and a clear Trivium/Quadrivium framework, with specific notes on memorization and catechesis as core features.
- Pedagogy: Lower grades incorporate singing/chanting and memory work; upper grades employ Socratic seminars and rhetoric training; Bildung via “habits” and virtuous formation is highlighted.
- Faculty: Public materials describe faculty as experienced, committed, mission-aligned, with living-curriculum emphasis and strong teacher leadership.
- Parents & Community: Explicit partnership with parents as co-teachers, open invitation for visits, and family-centered planning, including at-home days and parent formation materials.
