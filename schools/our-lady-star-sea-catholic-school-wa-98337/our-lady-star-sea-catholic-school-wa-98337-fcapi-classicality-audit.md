## Executive Summary

Our Lady Star Sea Catholic School (Our Lady Star Sea Catholic School, Bremerton, WA 98337) presents a robust classical-Catholic profile in public-facing materials. The school foregrounds liturgical life, Catholic theological formation, and a clearly stated mission to form virtuous disciples within a Catholic liberal-arts framework. Evidence from the school’s materials consistently emphasizes Mass, sacraments, liturgy, theology, and a mission to support parents as primary educators, all of which align strongly with classical education’s religious-metaphysical foundations and formation aims. The presence of a Socratic-leaning pedagogy (as evidenced by related public materials) and a clear emphasis on a classical curriculum position the school as CLASSICAL in several key categories; some categories show only partial alignment (SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL), particularly in explicit articulation of canonical Great Books/Seven Liberal Arts references within public materials. Overall, the aggregate classification is CLASSICAL, with noteworthy strengths in religious foundations, formation goals, pedagogy, faculty, and parent/community engagement, and with partial alignment to some curriculum specifics.

Key insights

- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: Strong Catholic liturgical life, theology integration, liturgy in daily practice, and explicit sacred life (Mass, sacraments, Adoration Chapel) map directly to the Positive Keywords for this category. There is little to no presence of Negative Keyword indicators in the public materials.
- Purpose and Goal: The school emphasizes formation, virtue, and a transcendent vision of truth, goodness, and beauty—clear alignment with Classical education aims.
- Curriculum: The public materials signal a classical orientation (Catholic liberal education, recitation, literature, Latin elements, and classical formation), but explicit Great Books/Seven Liberal Arts commitments are less overt in public-facing content. This yields a SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL rating for Curriculum, with room to strengthen public articulation of canonical components.
- Pedagogy: Evidence of Socratic-style dialogue and seminars exists (including a public-facing reference to The Socratic Method), along with recitation and classical-influenced memory practices, which strongly supports CLASSICAL pedagogy.
- Faculty: The presence of Dominican Sisters as teachers and named faculty (e.g., Sr. Maria Caeli, Sr. Mariana) demonstrates a living-mentor model aligned with classical faculty ideals.
- Parents & Community: A strong emphasis on parents as co-educators, parent formation events (First Friday Faith Formation), and open-campus/community involvement reflects a robust CLASSICAL orientation.
