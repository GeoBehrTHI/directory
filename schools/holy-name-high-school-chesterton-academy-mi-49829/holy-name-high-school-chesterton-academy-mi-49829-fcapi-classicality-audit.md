## Executive Summary

Holy Name High School – A Chesterton Academy (Escanaba, MI) presents a robust classical-Catholic program that aligns strongly with the six elements of classical education. Public materials from the school foreground Catholic identity, a mission-driven formation of intellect, virtue, and spirituality, an integrated classical curriculum, and a three-pillar pedagogical model (Intellect, Character, Spirituality). Evidence points to a coherent, liturgically-informed culture (Mass at the start of each day, Catholic worldview, House system) and a curriculum that ties history, literature, philosophy, theology, and languages into a unified classical narrative (including Latin study and dual-enrollment opportunities). While explicit mention of Great Books, Great Conversation, and detailed Socratic pedagogy is not always spelled out in every snippet, the available content strongly signals a classical orientation and formation-focused goals. Overall, the school is CLASSICAL, with some elements that warrant a more explicit articulation of pedagogy and faculty/student formation practices to achieve full, textbook-classical labeling.
