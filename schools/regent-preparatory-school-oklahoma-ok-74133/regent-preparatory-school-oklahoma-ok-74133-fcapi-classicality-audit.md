## Executive Summary

Regent Preparatory School of Oklahoma (Tulsa, 74133) presents a rigorous classical Christian program that aligns strongly with the Six Essential Elements of Classical Education. Across all six rubric categories, Regent demonstrates a coherent classical orientation: a theology-grounded, virtue-forming mission; a trivium-based curriculum with explicit grammar/logic/rhetoric progression and exposure to Great Books and Western tradition; a pedagogy that blends contemplative (Scholé) rhythms with Socratic inquiry and rhetorical training; a faculty culture described in terms of mentorship and living curriculum; and a robust, parent-partnered community with formal statements of faith and covenantal involvement.

- Overall classification by category: CLASSICAL for Categories 1–6.
- Executive takeaway: Regent exemplifies a fully Classical Christian school model rather than a mixed or weakly Classical approach. Its public materials repeatedly foreground truth, virtue, biblical authority, the trivium, Great Books, Socratic/vernacular rhetoric, and a parental partnership—core markers of the classical rubric. Evidence includes explicit mission language, the Portrait of a Graduate, Grammar/Logic/Rhetoric progression, Great Books lists, and formal parent covenants. See supporting sources cited in the respective category notes.
