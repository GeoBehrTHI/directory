## Executive Summary

Cross Classical Academy TX 76801 presents a strongly classical, Christian-oriented program. The website content consistently foregrounds a Biblical worldview, a three-part classical pedagogy (grammar, logic, rhetoric), and a community model built around parental partnership and formation of virtue. Public materials emphasize liturgical practices (chapel, worship), memorization and recitation in early grades, and high–school–level Socratic or rhetoric-oriented opportunities, alongside explicit goals of forming character, wisdom, and love of learning. Taken together, the school aligns with the classical education rubric across all six categories, with clear evidence of Great Books/Great Conversation-like aims, a Trivium-based curriculum, and a strong emphasis on family partnership and community formation.

Overall classification: CLASSICAL.

Key insights:
- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations are clearly centered on a Christian worldview expressed through chapel, Scripture memorization, biblical worldview integration, and theology-informed aims (evidence: Foundations mission; Christian Worldview page; chapel and Scripture emphasis)   .
- School Purpose and Goal centers on formation, virtue, wisdom, and human flourishing through a Biblical framework rather than mere job-readiness (evidence: mission statements; classical formation language)  .
- Curriculum emphasizes grammar–logic–rhetoric (Trivium), engagement with classical curricula, and the study of classical texts and history, with specific PK–8 and some 9–12 evidence (e.g., School of Grammar, Classical Approach)  .
- Pedagogy combines contemplative/restful elements (Scholé memory-work, chanting) with higher-level dialogue and rhetoric as students advance; lower grades visibly use singing/memorization, upper grades offer Socratic/rhetoric opportunities (as described in public materials)  .
- Faculty framework is described as mentors and living curriculum, with a collaborative model that centers parental involvement and teacher-mentored study; there is a visible portrait of teacher as exemplar within the Foundations and Collaborative Model materials  .
- Parents & Community are positioned as co-educators and primary partners in formation; the site highlights parental involvement, home-school partnership, and open-campus ethos   .
