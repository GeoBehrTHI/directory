## Executive Summary

Seale Christian Academy (SCA) demonstrates a robust classical Christian orientation in its public-facing materials. The school foregrounds a biblical worldview, Christ-centered education, and a commitment to classical foundational ideas (e.g., Trivium/Great Books) consistent with ACCS-affiliated classical education. Across six evaluated categories, SCA shows strong alignment in Religious and Metaphysical Foundations, School Purpose and Goal, Curriculum, and Parents & Community. Moderate alignment appears in Pedagogy and Faculty, reflecting partial adoption of classical methodologies rather than a fully explicit articulation of every classical pedagogy element. The aggregate assessment is CLASSICAL, driven by explicit theological grounding, a clear classical curriculum frame (Trivium/Great Books), and active parental partnership, with room for more explicit articulation of memory-based/Scholé practices and a more detailed living-faculty narrative.

Key evidence includes mission statements and doctrinal positions that situate education within a biblical framework, a stated classical Christian mission and worldview, and explicit references to Trivium and Great Books as central to the school’s approach. Notable public materials mention: Christ-centered, biblical worldview, theology as foundational, Great Books and Great Conversation, and ACCS membership. These cues align closely with the Positive Keyword Indicators for multiple classical elements in the knowledge-base rubric. See supporting citations from SCA’s public materials and the Six Essential Elements lexicon for keyword alignment and scoring.

  - Public materials describe the school as promoting a biblical worldview and biblical framework for understanding all subjects (the “Biblical Framework” language). This explicitly ties curriculum and life to theology and sacred truth.

  - Quantitative estimate (presence of keywords): Positive ~5–6 core terms; Negative ~0.

The lexical alignment with positive Religious and Metaphysical Foundations keywords (truth, goodness, beauty; theology; liturgy/worship) is robust in the available texts. See sources describing mission and doctrinal alignment.
