# Classicality Audit Report

Executive Summary
- School: Austin Classical School (Austin, TX) — https://austinclassical.org/
- Overall classification: CLASSICAL
- Why: The school explicitly anchors its mission and methods in “truth, goodness, and beauty,” “wisdom and virtue,” and a Christian theological framework; it implements the Trivium with Latin, logic, rhetoric, great books, history (not “social studies”), memory work, chanting/singing, debate/discussion, and thesis defense; it emphasizes teachers as virtuous Christian mentors and partners closely with parents via a collaborative model and ongoing co‑teacher training (see: mission and basics pages; trivium model; curriculum maps; chapel; collaborative schedule)       .
- Framework used: “Six Essential Elements of a Classical School” keyword indicators (positive/negative) for all six categories, plus the classicality lexicon provided (terms like truth, goodness, beauty; trivium, rhetoric; chanting; Socratic/disputation; partnership with parents; etc.)  .

Note on method: For each category, we:
1) Extracted keywords/phrases from the school’s site/crawl.
2) Compared to the Positive/Negative keyword indicators from “Six Essential Elements” and the lexicon.
3) Counted positive vs. negative matches and estimated percent_positive and percent_coverage.
4) Assigned a rating of CLASSICAL / SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL / NOT CLASSICAL accordingly.
