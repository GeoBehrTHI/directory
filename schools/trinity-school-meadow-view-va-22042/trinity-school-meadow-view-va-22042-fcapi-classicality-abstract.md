## ABSTRACT

Trinity School Meadow View VA 22042 demonstrates a CLASSICAL orientation across five of the six classical elements: Religious and Metaphysical Foundations; School Purpose and Goal; Curriculum; Pedagogy; and Faculty. The public materials affirm a Christian/theological grounding, formation-centered aims, a liberal-arts curriculum with Great Books and Latin, and pedagogical practices aligned with Socratic/seminar-style inquiry and a living-teacher model. The public-facing materials for Parents & Community are less explicit, resulting in a NOT CLASSICAL rating for that category. Taken together, the school projects a robust Classical education with strong intellectual and spiritual formation, and a potential for full CLASSICAL status upon explicit articulation and implementation of a formal parent-community partnership program.

Key category highlights:
- Religious & Metaphysical Foundations: Strong CLASSICAL alignment through Christian/theological framing, scripture study, and reverence for truth/goodness/beauty. 
- School Purpose & Goal: Formation of virtue and wisdom; liberal-arts orientation; pursuit of truth, goodness, beauty as real. 
- Curriculum: Liberal-arts core; Great Books/original texts; Latin study; historical breadth; integration of Scripture and humanities. 
- Pedagogy: Socratic/seminar-based inquiry; memory work and recitation; coaching and discussion; deliberate teacher leadership. 
- Faculty: Living curriculum; mentor-focused, Christian culture; ongoing professional development. 
- Parents & Community: Present in Six Elements as a criterion, but public-facing Trinity Meadow View materials lack explicit, published parent-partnership mechanisms; not CLASSICAL for this category based on current public materials. 

Notes on sources and methodology
- School-specific public materials (crawl) were retrieved from Trinity Meadow View’s publicly accessible pages and related course/mission pages. Evidence was drawn from sections including Educational Environment, Our Mission, Goals of a Trinity Education, Academics/Curriculum, and Course Guides. Representative citations indicate the presence of canonical classical elements such as truth/goodness/beauty, Great Books, Latin, weekly seminars, and the teacher-as-mentor paradigm. See sources noted above for direct content references. 
- Classical criteria and keyword indicators were drawn from the knowledge-base “Six Essential Elements of a Classical School (1)” and related lexicons, which outline the Positive/Negative Keywords across six categories. This rubric provided the framework for keyword analyses and category scoring. Representative mappings include: Great Books, liberal education, paideia, seven liberal arts, Socratic pedagogy, memory work, and living curriculum as positive indicators; and workforce/modern-skills framing as potential negative indicators.
