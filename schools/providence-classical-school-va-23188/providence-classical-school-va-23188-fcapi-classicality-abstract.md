## ABSTRACT

Providence Classical School Virginia (Williamsburg, VA, 23188) demonstrates a robust CLASSICAL orientation across all six rubric categories: Religious and Metaphysical Foundations, School Purpose and Goal, Curriculum, Pedagogy, Faculty, and Parents & Community. The public materials show a Christian, biblically grounded, Christ-centered approach, with liturgical routines (memory work, devotions, chapel) and a strong emphasis on virtue formation and wisdom. The curriculum is clearly Trivium-based (Grammar, Dialectic, Rhetoric) with explicit grade-band structuring (K-5, 6-8, 9-12), Latin/Greek offerings, and engagement with Harkness/Socratic methods and rhetoric—key indicators of classical pedagogy. The faculty is framed as living curriculum and mentors, with leadership structures and faculty/staff engagement documented. PCS also emphasizes family partnerships, parent engagement, and community involvement through open houses, parent committees, and resources for families.
