## Executive Summary

Logos Classical Christian School Lynden, MI (ZIP 48451) presents a coherent classical Christian model across the six classical-education criteria. Public-facing materials emphasize Christ-centered formation, virtue and wisdom, a liberal-arts-driven curriculum built around grammar–logic–rhetoric, a strong focus on Great Books and classical languages, and pedagogy that blends contemplative, memory-learning (scholé) with structured dialogue and rhetoric. Faculty materials describe teachers as mentors and living exemplars who form the school’s core, while the school explicitly partners with parents and invites ongoing family involvement and church engagement. Overall, the public materials align strongly with the Six Essential Elements of Classical Education, yielding a clear CLASSICAL classification.

Key positive signals include explicit Christ-centered and Scriptural framing, a formation-of-character orientation (godliness, wisdom, virtue), a liberal-arts core (grammar, logic, rhetoric; Latin and Greek; Great Books), incorporation of memory and recitation (progymnasmata, chant/memory work), a clearly defined faculty/core-teacher model, and robust parent/church partnerships. There are few to no explicit negative indicators within the visible materials. The aggregate assessment across all six categories is CLASSICAL.
