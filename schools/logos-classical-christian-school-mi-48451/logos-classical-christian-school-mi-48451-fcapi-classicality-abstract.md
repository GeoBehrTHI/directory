## ABSTRACT

Logos Classical Christian School, Linden, MI (ZIP 48451), demonstrates a strong CLASSICAL orientation across all six categories of the classical education rubric. Religious and metaphysical foundations are clearly Christ-centered, anchored in Scripture and a theologically framed worldview. The school’s stated purpose prioritizes formation of virtue, wisdom, and godliness over mere information or career preparation. The curriculum centers on the liberal arts (grammar, logic, rhetoric; Latin and Greek), Great Books, and Western civilization, with a deliberate emphasis on the Great Conversation and the cultivation of wonder. Pedagogy includes contemplative/restful learning (scholé), memory-based learning, and classical seminars or discourse in higher grades, supported by a robust “living curriculum” in the faculty. Faculty are presented as mentors and core to the school’s fabric, and the Parents & Community section emphasizes partnerships with families and local churches, inviting parental involvement and home-school alignment. The presence of Doctrinal Statements, Vision/Mission language, and explicit Christ-centered framing across pages reinforces a consistent classical Christian identity.
