## ABSTRACT

Northeast Ohio Classical Academy (NEOCA) presents a robust classical orientation grounded in a Hillsdale College K-12 curriculum, with explicit emphasis on virtue formation, moral character, and civic virtue. The school’s public content foregrounds formation (virtue, wisdom, moral character) and a content-rich liberal-arts core (grammar/phonics, Latin, Western tradition, literature, philosophy, math, science, history). The religious/metaphysical Foundations category is the most ambiguous in public materials (theology and liturgical practices are not clearly explicit), resulting in a SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL rating for Element 1. The School Purpose and Goal, Curriculum, Faculty, and Parents & Community categories align strongly with CLASSICAL expectations, reflecting formation-centered aims, classical curriculum, and a living-faculty-centered culture and family engagement. Pedagogy sits between CLASSICAL and SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL, as traditional teacher-led methods are evident but explicit Socratic/seminar-based practices are not consistently documented in public materials.
