## Executive Summary

Colorado Classical School Co 80122 (CCS) presents a robust CLASSICAL Christian profile across all six rubric categories. Public materials consistently tie education to a biblical worldview, explicitly referencing the fear of the Lord, theological grounding, and liturgical practices, while affirming classical aims through the Trivium (Grammar, Logic, Rhetoric) and Quadrivium, primary sources, and a commitment to memory work and contemplative pedagogy (scholé). The school describes its mission as nourishing minds, hearts, and bodies with what is true, good, and beautiful so that students glorify God and serve neighbors with joy and faithfulness, and it highlights virtuous faculty, parental partnership, and a structured, memory-based approach to learning. Evidence from CCS materials demonstrates strong alignment with the core elements of classical education, including a fixed liberal-arts curriculum, Socratic discussion in higher grades, and a living, mentored faculty culture, with active parent involvement. Accordingly, CCS receives a CLASSICAL rating across all six categories.

Key evidentiary anchors include: the explicit biblical grounding and Christian practices (prayer, Scripture, worship) and the central claim that all knowledge is rooted in God’s truth, goodness, and beauty; the Trivium and Quadrivium framing with Socratic discussion and primary sources; a rest-filled, contemplative pedagogical ethos (scholé) alongside rigorous memory work; a mission that centers virtue, wisdom, and formation; a curriculum that foregrounds liberal arts and the Great Books/primary texts; a faculty described as virtuous and living curriculum; and a strong emphasis on parental partnership and public-facing openness to families and community.
