## ABSTRACT

Rocky Bayou Christian School’s public materials strongly foreground a Christian formation framework anchored in biblical worldview, theology, worship, and parental partnership, which yields CLASSICAL signals across Religious and Metaphysical Foundations (Element 1), School Purpose and Goal (Element 2), Faculty (Element 5), and Parents & Community (Element 6). The curriculum (Element 3) shows traditional subject offerings and a consistent Christian worldview, but does not explicitly present canonical classical elements (Great Books, grammar-logic-rhetoric, or a stated Great Conversation program) in public-facing materials, resulting in a NOT CLASSICAL rating for that category. Pedagogy (Element 4) demonstrates a blend of contemplative/memory-based elements and some opportunity for discussion-based teaching, yielding a SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL rating. The aggregate assessment places RBCS in CLASSICAL territory due to strong formation-oriented aims, liturgical/community life, and robust parental involvement, with a clear potential to strengthen explicit classical-curriculum and classical-pedagogy signals.

Key sources include:
- Mission & Vision statements, “Mission & Vision” and “Philosophy of Christian Education” materials describing biblical truth, God’s calling, and a biblical worldview as foundational to the school’s approach. 
- Chapel, worship, and liturgical life signals (Praise Band, weekly chapel). 
- Niceville Campus and cross-campus Christian curriculum continuity. 
- Faculty as living curriculum and parental-partnered formation. 
- Classrooms, curriculum offerings, and the emphasis on Biblical Worldview in Homeschool/online and on-campus programs. 

Notes:
- The analysis relies on the school’s public-facing materials explicitly cited above. The classification uses the Six Essential Elements’ Positive/Negative Keyword Framework as a lens to gauge alignment with classical aims, cross-referencing the classical lexicon and the school’s textual signals. The key canonical-classical markers (e.g., Great Books, grammar-logic-rhetoric, and explicit Socratic pedagogy) are present in the knowledge rubric but require stronger public-facing articulation in RBCS materials to elevate Element 3 from NOT CLASSICAL to CLASSICAL.
