## Executive Summary

Chesterton Academy of Blessed Pier Giorgio Frassati (Portland, OR; 98685 slug) presents a clearly classical, Catholic-inspired program. Public materials emphasize a culture of life, a Catholic theological orientation, a liberal-arts core, and a memory-rich, Socratic pedagogical approach embedded in a “classical curriculum” with Great Books and a focus on virtue. Across mission statements, curriculum descriptions, and student-life narratives, the school foregrounds truth, goodness, and beauty as real and knowable, formed through Christian formation, classical study, and communal liturgy. The public-facing materials consistently align with the six classical-elements rubric, with strong signals in religious-metaphysical foundations, formation of virtue, liberal-arts curriculum (grammar-logic-rhetoric and Great Books), Socratic pedagogy, a living faculty model, and a family-centered community partnership. Overall classification: CLASSICAL.

- The school’s religious identity is explicit (Catholic faith, liturgical life, communal prayer), and theology anchors its pedagogy and curriculum. Evidence: mission statements, Catholic identity language, and liturgical elements in public materials.  
- The curriculum emphasizes the liberal arts, Great Books, and a public-facing claim of a classical curriculum; memory/recitation and Socratic dialogue appear in materials, with explicit references to the three-pillars approach (Intellect, Character, Spirituality).
