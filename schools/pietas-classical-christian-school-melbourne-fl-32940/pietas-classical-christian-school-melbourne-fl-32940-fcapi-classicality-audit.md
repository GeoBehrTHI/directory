## Executive Summary

Pietas Classical Christian School Melbourne, FL (32940) demonstrates a clear Classical Christian orientation, with strongest evidence in Curriculum and Religious/Metaphysical Foundations. The school’s public materials emphasize the seven liberal arts, Great Books, primary sources, dialogue and narration, and a Christian theological frame that shapes instruction and community life. Taken together with the school’s stated mission and parent-partnership approach, the aggregate profile is CLASSICAL.

- Element 4: Pedagogy — SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL
- Element 5: Faculty — NOT CLASSICAL
- Element 6: Parents & Community — CLASSICAL

Overall, the six-category synthesis yields a CLASSICAL classification, with one category (Pedagogy) showing partial Classical traits and one category (Faculty) lacking explicit Classical signals in public materials. The strongest needle-movers for further elevation toward a fully CLASSICAL standing are to more explicitly codify pedagogy and faculty as living examples of classical formation, and to publicly articulate liturgical/communal practices and faculty profiles in line with the Classical School lexicon.

What would move the needle (for a move toward full CLASSICAL)
- Pedagogy: Add explicit, public commitments to Scholé/restful learning and Socratic seminars in upper grades; publish a concrete pedagogy plan (teacher training, sample schedules, and exemplar dialogues) that foreground Socratic inquiry and disciplined discourse.
- Faculty: Publish a formal “living curriculum” portrait of teachers; document faculty formation in classical pedagogy; highlight teacher exemplars and mentorship roles.
- Religion/Community: If not already explicit, incorporate a stated liturgical or communal prayer/worship rhythm and clearly describe how theology informs cross-disciplinary work.
- Parents & Community: Deepen parent formation efforts with structured book studies, webinars, and open-campus experiences to reinforce parents as co-educators.
