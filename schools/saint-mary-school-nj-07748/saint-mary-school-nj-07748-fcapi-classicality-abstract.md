## ABSTRACT

Saint Mary School NJ 07748 demonstrates a comprehensive CLASSICAL orientation across all six classical-education categories. Religious and metaphysical foundations are foregrounded through Catholic identity, theology, and liturgical/cultural practices embedded in daily formation. The school’s purpose explicitly centers on formation, virtue, wisdom, and the pursuit of truth, goodness, and beauty, rather than merely vocational outcomes. The curriculum is organized around the liberal-arts framework (grammar, logic, rhetoric) with explicit history-centered study and a commitment to classical language study, alongside a history-rich, faith-informed approach. Pedagogy emphasizes Socratic inquiry in upper grades and memory-based, reverent teaching in the lower grades, with singing and liturgical life weaving into daily learning. The faculty are described as mentors who form the living curriculum and as a cohesive, value-driven community. Parents are positioned as co-educators and engaged partners in education, with open access and opportunities for involvement.
