## ABSTRACT

Sacred Heart Academy Grand Rapids (SHA), ZIP 49504, is classified as CLASSICAL overall, with two categories—Curriculum and Pedagogy—exhibiting SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL characteristics due to blended curricular and pedagogical approaches (notably Montessori), while the other four categories strongly align with classical education norms. The school presents a Catholic, classical formation model focused on formation over information, with daily Mass, liturgical life, and a parish-apostolate identity that centers on truth, goodness, beauty, and virtue. The public-facing materials emphasize a living curriculum, a mission to form saints and scholars, and a robust partnership with families, all aligning with classical education ideals.

Key category takeaways:
- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations (CLASSICAL): Clear Catholic-theological framing; liturgical life; daily Mass; sacred culture integrated with learning. 
- School Purpose and Goal (CLASSICAL): Formation of virtue and wisdom; pursuit of truth, goodness, beauty; telos toward sanctity. 
- Curriculum (SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL): Classical orientation with Great Books/Great Conversation framing, but a mixed curricular approach due to Montessori programming. 
- Pedagogy (SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL): Classical orientation present, but blended with Montessori/other methods; not a strictly canonical classical pedagogy. 
- Faculty (CLASSICAL): Leadership and teachers presented as virtuous exemplars and the living curriculum; strong formation emphasis. 
- Parents & Community (CLASSICAL): Explicit partnership with parents as co-educators; parent formation; open campus; family involvement.
