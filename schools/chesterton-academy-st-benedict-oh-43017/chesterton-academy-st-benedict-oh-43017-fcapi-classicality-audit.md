## Executive Summary

Chesterton Academy of St. Benedict (Worthington, OH 43017) presents a robust CLASSICAL orientation across the Six Essential Elements framework. Public materials on the school's site frame education as a Catholic classical formation centered on truth, goodness, and beauty, pursued through a liberal arts curriculum, the Socratic Method, and a culture of virtue. The school’s mission explicitly ties classical study to Catholic faith, with a three-pillars model centering intellect, character formation, and spiritual truth/practice. Great Books, primary sources, and a historically rooted curriculum are foregrounded, and pedagogy is described as a blend of Socratic inquiry in the upper years with contemplative, restful elements in earlier years. Faculty identity is positioned as mentors and living curriculum, and families are actively welcomed as partners in formation. Open houses, shadow days, and open admissions communications demonstrate ongoing parental involvement and community integration.

Direct textual anchors that support these conclusions include the mission statement tying education to Catholic truth (our public materials articulate a mission grounded in Catholic faith) and references to the Three Pillars and formation over mere information, a curriculum rooted in Great Books and primary sources with Socratic discussion, and clear evidence of liturgical or religious practice and Catholic identity within the school community. These elements align Chesterton Academy St. Benedict with a Classical formation oriented toward truth, goodness, and beauty, rather than a purely workforce-focused or secular program. See evidence excerpts below for category-specific justification.
