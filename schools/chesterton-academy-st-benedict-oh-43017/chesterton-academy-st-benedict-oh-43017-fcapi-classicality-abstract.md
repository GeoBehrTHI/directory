## ABSTRACT

Chesterton Academy of St. Benedict (Worthington, OH 43017) demonstrates a robust CLASSICAL orientation across all six categories of the classical-education rubric. Religious and metaphysical foundations are explicitly Catholic, anchored in truth, goodness, and beauty, with liturgical and communal practices evident in public materials and diocesan designations. The school’s purpose emphasizes formation, virtue, and teleology (pursuit of true, good, and beautiful) over mere workforce preparation. The curriculum centers on liberal-arts with Great Books and primary sources, and pedagogy emphasizes Socratic discussion, memory work, and contemplative learning. Faculty are framed as mentors and living curriculum, and parents/community are treated as co-educators and partners in formation.

Category-by-category analysis confirms CLASSICAL across:
- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: Catholic identity, liturgical life, and metaphysical aims are clearly present. Evidence includes mission statements and liturgical/catholic formation references. Positive keyword signals are strong; negative signals appear minimal or absent.   
- School Purpose and Goal: Formation and virtue-centered aims with teleology; emphasis on truth, goodness, beauty; formation over information. Positive keyword set is strong; workforce/preparation framed as secondary or non-primary. 
- School Curriculum: Great Books/primary sources, liberal arts emphasis, integrated humanities with a historical-philosophical-theological core; use of Socratic method; synchronized with classical canon. Positive keyword presence is clear; absence of social-studies-first framing supports a classical reading.  
- Pedagogy or Teaching Methods: Socratic seminars, structured memory work, and a mix of contemplative/restful elements with rigorous upper-school discourse and rhetoric development. Positive keywords from classical pedagogy lexicon are evident.  
- Faculty: Mentors and living curriculum; portrait of teacher ideals; collaborative culture; public evidence of leadership (Headmaster) and faculty formation. Positive keywords present; teacher-formation alignment evidenced through leadership pages. 
- Parents & Community: Active partnership with families; parental formation opportunities; open visits and events for family engagement. Positive keywords present; evidence for co-educator framing and home-school alignment. 

Aggregate assessment: CLASSICAL.

- The school demonstrates a coherent classical-education program as defined by the Six Essential Elements lexicon, with strong alignment to catechetical, philosophical, and pedagogical classical markers, including the Great Books canon, Socratic teaching, and a formation-centered mission rooted in Catholic faith.

ABSTRACT

Chesterton Academy of St. Benedict (Worthington, OH 43017) is clearly CLASSICAL according to the six-category rubric. Religious and metaphysical foundations are rooted in Catholic truth and liturgical practice; the school pursues formation and virtue as core aims; the curriculum emphasizes liberal arts, Great Books, and primary sources; pedagogy centers on Socratic seminars, rhetoric, and memory work; faculty are framed as mentors and living exemplars; and parents/community are actively engaged as co-educators. The public materials align with the Six Essential Elements’ positive keyword signals (truth, goodness, beauty; virtue; formation; Great Books; Socratic pedagogy; living curriculum; partnerships with families) and largely avoid the negative signals associated with secular or purely workforce-centered models. The aggregate assessment is CLASSICAL, with explicit references to the Catholic intellectual-tradition framework and a robust classical curriculum and pedagogy.
