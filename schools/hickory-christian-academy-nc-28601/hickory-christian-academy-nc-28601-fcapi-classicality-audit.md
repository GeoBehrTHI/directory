## Executive Summary

Hickory Christian Academy (HCA) presents a robust Classical Christian program anchored in the Trivium (Grammar, Logic, Rhetoric), a stated commitment to Great Books and liberal arts, and explicit theological framing around truth, goodness, and beauty. Public materials from HCA emphasize formation of character, formation through faith, and a communal, liturgical dimension (chapel, prayer, worship) that aligns with transcendentals and Catholic/Protestant liturgical sensibilities often associated with classical education. Overall, the school can be ranked CLASSICAL across most of the core categories, with very strong alignment in Religious Foundations, Purpose and Goal, Curriculum, and Pedagogy. Faculty and Parents/Community show meaningful classical signals, but could strengthen explicit articulation of living-curriculum concepts and deeper parental-partnership practices to achieve a fully consistent CLASSICAL rating in those two categories.

Key signals supporting CLASSICAL status
- Explicit Classical Christian framing and Trivium (Grammar/Logic/Rhetoric) in core materials. Evidence includes statements about the Triivium and the classical approach to education (turn0file0). 
- Emphasis on Great Books and liberal arts within a Christian worldview (turn0file0, turn0file1). 
- Religious/metaphysical foundations anchored in theology, worship, and liturgical practices (Chapel; Statement of Faith). See Chapel description and Statement of Faith for explicit references to eternal truth and theological grounding (turn0file2, turn0file9).  
- Purpose statements foreground formation, virtue, wisdom, and the cultivation of “thinkers” who can be competent problem solvers and virtuous communicators (turn0file0, turn0file3). 

What would move the needle
- For Faculty: More explicit articulation of the “living curriculum” concept and teacher-student mentorship culture in public materials (e.g., a formal “Portrait of a Teacher” and “Faculty as Living Curriculum” statement visible on the site).
- For Parents & Community: Stronger, explicit, public articulation of parent-partnership structures (e.g., formal parent-education programs, classical book clubs, and a visible “parents as co-teachers” covenant) to elevate the category to CLASSICAL.

The following category-by-category analysis provides evidence, keyword analytics, and rationales for each classification.

- For Element 5 (Faculty): Add explicit public-facing language about the living curriculum, a formal “Portrait of a Teacher” and “Faculty as Living Curriculum” statements, plus a visible faculty-professional-development pathway grounded in classical methods (e.g., seminars for teachers, great-books study for faculty, mentorship culture).
