## ABSTRACT

Overall Classification: CLASSICAL

Acacia Classical Academy CA 95628 presents strong Classical Christian alignment across multiple dimensions. Its public materials emphasize Christ-centered, theologically grounded education, covenantal communities, virtue-formation aims, and a liberal-arts framing with Latin study. The school’s public-facing content demonstrates:

- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: Christ-centered education; alignment with the Nicene Creed; covenantal community and discipleship. This evidences Transcendental moral realism and theological grounding.  
- School Purpose and Goal: Formation toward virtue and wisdom, the True/Good/Beautiful, and liberal-arts orientation; purpose statements around loving God and serving others.

- Pedagogy: Structured program (two days on campus, three days homeschooling) with tutoring support, but explicit Socratic/Scholé practices are not clearly documented in site text. This yields SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL for Element 4. 
- Faculty: Strong emphasis on teachers as mentors and living curriculum, including staff profiles. CLASSICAL. 
- Parents & Community: Explicit partnership with families and parental formation; open engagement with homeschooling communities. CLASSICAL. 

In sum, Acacia Classical Academy is CLASSICAL overall, with strong alignment in religious foundations, formation-focused goals, and community/family partnerships, and with clearly positive signals in curriculum and faculty. The main opportunities to move from SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL in a couple of dimensions to fully CLASSICAL would be to explicitly document Great Books/Great Conversation, and to articulate full, explicit engagement with traditional classical pedagogy (grammar/logical/rhetoric, Socratic dialogue) in public-facing materials.
