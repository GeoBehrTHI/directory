## ABSTRACT

Overall Classification: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL

St Pius X Catholic School TX 79905 demonstrates a credible classical orientation anchored in Catholic formation. The public materials show a strong religious/theological foundation (Christ-centered environment, sacramental preparation, faith formation) together with a clear curricular commitment to classical education (Latin and classic literature) and a prominent role for parents in formation. The school’s affiliation as a Proud Partner Member of the Classical Latin School Association reinforces its classical identity. However, explicit statements about full classical pedagogy (e.g., Socratic seminars, Great Books/Great Conversation, and a grammar–logic–rhetoric progression) are not consistently surfaced in public-facing materials. Therefore, while the school clearly aligns with many core classical elements, the pedagogy and the explicit articulation of a fully realized classical program across grade bands require further public detailing to be considered CLASSICAL across all six categories.
