## ABSTRACT

Revive Academy (Homer, AK, 99603) demonstrates a robust CLASSICAL orientation across five of six categories, with Pedagogy showing a "Somewhat Classical" nuance due to public materials not consistently detailing explicit Socratic/seminar practices. The school is clearly Christian and classical in its structure, with a strong emphasis on formation of virtue and wisdom, the authority of Biblical truth, and a curriculum built on grammar, logic, rhetoric, Great Books, Latin, history, and biblical studies. The hybrid on-campus/home model reflects a deep partnership with families, aligning with classical educational values of formation within a community context. Faculty are presented as virtuous mentors and living part of the curriculum, and parents are positioned as co-educators within a supportive community. The aggregate assessment: CLASSICAL.

Key findings by category:
- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: CLASSICAL. Christ-centered, biblical worldview, Statement of Faith, prayer, and theology anchor the education.  
- Purpose and Goal: CLASSICAL. Formation of virtue and wisdom; whole-person education with a biblical frame. 
- Curriculum: CLASSICAL. Liberal arts core (grammar, logic, rhetoric), history, Great Books, Latin, biblical integration.  
- Pedagogy: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL. Hybrid model and memory-focused, hands-on approach; explicit Socratic/seminar pedagogy is not consistently described in public content.  
- Faculty: CLASSICAL. Faculty framed as virtuous mentors, core of the school, aligned with mission. 
- Parents & Community: CLASSICAL. Strong parent partnership and involvement in education. 

This assessment draws on Revive Academy’s public materials, including the Philosophy of Education and Curriculum pages, the Leadership/Staff materials, and the Admissions/Parental partnership content, which collectively reveal a classical framework anchored in Christian faith and classical pedagogy, with family partnership at its center.   

End of Abstract.
