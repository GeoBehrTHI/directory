Evidence

- The public materials foreground the Trivium and the integration of subjects (grammar, logic, rhetoric; Great Books; Latin). This aligns with classical pedagogy signals in the rubric.
- There are explicit references to “restful learning” and the conceptual aim of formation through contemplation (Scholé) in the knowledge-base lexicon and in the rubric’s pedagogy indicators; however, thePaideia site materials themselves emphasize traditional methods (memory, recitation, and structured learning) more clearly than explicit, codified Socratic seminars across all grades. The “Socratic/dialogue” and “seminar” language appears in the classical education literature more broadly than in the Paideia public-facing site, which focuses on Trivium and Great Books rather than a prescriptive Socratic schedule for every grade. This yields a SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL rating for Pedagogy.

Positive/Negative Keyword Analysis

- Positive keywords present: Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric); sacred/ancillary classical pedagogy terms like sequencing of memory work, recitation, and memory/chanting concepts found in classical pedagogy rubrics. The classical lexicon emphasizes Socratic seminar; Paideia materials explicitly emphasize Trivium; Great Books; and integrated learning.
- Negative keywords present: Limited explicit statements about universal deployment of Socratic seminars across all grades; fewer explicit descriptions of ongoing Socratic seminars or full “Scola” style conversational pedagogy beyond the Trivium. The site’s public content leans more toward classical structure and formation than toward the full Socratic method at all levels.

Why this rating

- The school demonstrates strong classical pedagogical elements (Trivium, Great Books, memory/recitation, and restfulness) but lacks robust, consistently explicit public statements about pervasive Socratic seminars or advanced seminar-based teaching in every grade, which the rubric uses as a hallmark of a fully Classical pedagogy. This places Pedagogy in SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL.
