## Executive Summary

Faith Academy of Freestone (Freestone, TX 75840) presents a public-facing portrait of a Christ-centered, classical Christian school that emphasizes formation of character and virtue within a family-partnered community. Public materials consistently describe a Christ-centered, biblical worldview-infused approach, a classical framework (grammar, logic, rhetoric), and a pedagogy that foregrounds virtue, reverence, and disciplined study. Evidence from the school’s site shows explicit references to Christian faith as foundational, a classical curriculum structure (grammar/logic/rhetoric; with named classical resources), and faculty highlighted as dedicated mentors. The school also stresses parental partnership and open engagement with families, including family letters, admissions processes, and community-facing communications. Taken together, these elements align Faith Academy with a CLASSICAL orientation, with relatively strong emphasis across most six classical criteria, and fewer explicit signals of non-classical rhetoric or practices.

- Overall classification: CLASSICAL

- Category-by-category snapshot (highlights):
    - Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: CLASSICAL — explicit Christ-centered, biblical framing and prayerful language appear across site content; some elements of liturgical/community practices are not deeply foregrounded but the theological framing is clear.
    - Purpose and Goal: CLASSICAL — emphasis on formation of virtue, wisdom, and character alongside Christian formation; appeals to enduring truths rather than vocationalism.
    - Curriculum: CLASSICAL — explicit grammar, logic, rhetoric references; classical-curriculum orientation with named classical resources; limited explicit Great Books mention but core trivium emphasis is strong.
    - Pedagogy: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL — explicit rhetoric focus and a classical teacher identity present; explicit Socratic/seminar-based pedagogy is not clearly described, though upper-school rhetoric and classical training are evident.
    - Faculty: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL — dedicated teachers and mentors; "classical rhetoric and humane letters" teacher highlighted; explicit “living curriculum” language is not overtly shown, but the faculty is framed as virtuous and competent.
    - Parents & Community: CLASSICAL — strong emphasis on partnership with families, parental involvement, and open access to the school; multiple signals of family engagement and community-minded governance.

- What would move the needle (if/when relevant): Not applicable here since the school is classified as CLASSICAL. No major, category-shifting needle moves are required based on available public materials.
