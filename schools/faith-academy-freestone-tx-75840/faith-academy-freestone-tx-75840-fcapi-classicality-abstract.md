# ABSTRACT

Faith Academy of Freestone presents a CLASSICAL orientation across most of the classical-education rubric. The public materials emphasize Christ-centered, biblical formation; a classical curriculum framed by grammar, logic, and rhetoric; and strong parental partnership. The school highlights dedicated teachers and a rhetoric-focused faculty profile, with explicit evidence of a Classical Christian mission and formation goals. While explicit Socratic pedagogy and a fully developed “living curriculum” narrative across all grades are less clearly documented in public materials, the core elements—classical curricular core, religious foundations, formation-focused aims, and active family engagement—are robust. The aggregate assessment remains CLASSICAL.

- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: CLASSICAL — Christ-centered, biblical framing; faith integration across learning; prayerful, sacred language in mission materials. Evidence: “Christ-centered, Classical Christian education”; biblical principles; gospel/bible/ypr references in curriculum materials.
- Purpose and Goal: CLASSICAL — formation of virtue and wisdom; human flourishing; enduring truths as real. Evidence: formation of virtue/wisdom; spiritual formation; mission statements emphasizing formation over mere information.
- Curriculum: CLASSICAL — grammar/logic/rhetoric; explicit classical program signals (Memoria Press, Veritas Press, etc.); K–4 grammar, 5–11 logic/rhetoric. Evidence: curriculum pages; named classical resources.
- Pedagogy: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL — rhetoric-focused teaching and a classical teacher profile, but full Socratic/Paideia pedagogy is not comprehensively documented in public site materials. Evidence: Marsha Black – Classical Rhetoric and Humane Letters Teacher; general classical framing.
- Faculty: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL — dedicated teachers; leadership roles; a rhetoric-focused faculty member; “living curriculum” language is not publicly explicit across all pages. Evidence: Dedicated teachers; named faculty roles; classical rhetoric teacher.
- Parents & Community: CLASSICAL — strong partnership with families; parental engagement and open access; family letters; admissions; governance communications. Evidence: “partnering with parents”; family letters; admissions communications; open community signals.
