Public-facing materials consistently foreground the cultivation of character through the pursuit of truth, goodness, and beauty, with explicit mentions of virtue formation, Great Books, and a shared mission to form “hearts and minds in virtue and truth” within a classical framework. The school highlights Great Hearts Day activities (e.g., Great Hearts, Great Books Day), a K–5 curriculum infused with classical formative aims, and a strong parent community anchored in an active Parent Service Organization. Taken together, these signals align strongly with a CLASSICAL orientation across all six categories of the classical rubric. Notes of evidence include the school’s stated mission of forming hearts and minds in virtue and truth within a classical education, frequent emphasis on virtue and the fine arts, and pervasive partner-ship with families and community groups that reflect a traditional, morally oriented educational project.
