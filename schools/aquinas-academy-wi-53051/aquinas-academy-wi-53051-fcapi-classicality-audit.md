# Overall Aggregate Rating: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL
Rationale: ACS has publicly committed to a Christian classical direction with clear theological grounding and strong curriculum/pedagogy signals (Trivium, classics, Socratic), plus early evidence of community formation. To be fully CLASSICAL per the Six Elements framework, ACS should surface more explicit transcendental (truth–goodness–beauty) language, liturgical/chapel life, restful learning and chant/recitation in the lower school, teacher formation/portrait, and a clear parent partnership model in its public materials  .

---

## What would move the needle (actionable, public-facing steps)
- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations
  - Add explicit transcendental language (truth, goodness, beauty), moral realism, and ordered loves to the mission and academics pages .
  - Publish chapel/liturgy rhythms (daily/weekly chapel, common prayer, hymnody) to signal the school’s sacred communal life .

- School Purpose and Goal
  - State clearly that education pursues wisdom and virtue and is “formation, not merely information,” and tie this to the school’s telos (human flourishing oriented to the true, good, and beautiful) .

- Curriculum
  - Post a representative “classics/Great Books” list by grade bands; ensure “history” (not “social studies”) is used prominently; show integration/wonder statements across subjects .

- Pedagogy
  - Name restful learning (scholé) as part of the school’s cadence; highlight lower school singing/chanting/recitation practices in daily routines (a hallmark of classical memory culture) . For lower grades especially, routine chant/sing practices are strongly classical and form memory and affections .

- Faculty
  - Publish a “Portrait of a Teacher” describing teachers as models of virtue, mentors, and lifelong learners formed in classical pedagogy; highlight ongoing faculty formation in Great Books/seminars .

- Parents and Community
  - Make the parent partnership explicit (co-teachers in formation), add a parent covenant, and list regular parent formation events (book clubs, seminars, Infosessions on the liberal arts) .
