## What Would Move the Needle (Faculty Category)

Because Category 5 (Faculty) is **SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL**, here are concrete steps that would strengthen it to fully **CLASSICAL**:

1. **Publish a “Portrait of a Teacher” or “Faculty Profile”**  
   - Explicitly describe teachers as:
     - “models of virtue,” “living curriculum,” “mentors,” “servant leaders,” “lovers of truth, goodness, and beauty.”   
   - Connect faculty expectations to the school’s telos: forming virtuous followers of Christ and Kingdom citizens.

2. **Highlight Classical Faculty Formation**  
   - Note any use of classical teacher training (e.g., ClassicalU, ACCS, CiRCE, or similar) and ongoing great-books seminars or colloquia for faculty.   
   - Use lexicon terms like *lifelong learners, classical pedagogy, Socratic training, scholé for teachers*.

3. **Describe Faculty Community and Collegiality**  
   - Add language about a **“faculty of friends”** or **collegial community** that studies, prays, and plans together, modeling the great conversation and ordered loves for students.   

4. **Make Mentorship Explicit**  
   - On the About/Faculty page, state that teachers are **mentors** who guide students in wisdom and virtue, not just content deliverers, echoing classical sources (e.g., “good man speaking well,” “formation of the soul”).   

Implementing these changes in public-facing materials would likely move Faculty from **SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL** to **CLASSICAL**, reinforcing the already strong classical identity.
