## ABSTRACT

Adolesco Preparatory Academy (https://www.adolescoprep.org/) is a **classical, Christ-centered, University-Model school** that strongly aligns with the six elements of a classical school as defined in *Six Essential Elements of a Classical School* and the classicality lexicon.    

1. **Religious & Metaphysical Foundations:** The school explicitly affirms a Biblical worldview, absolute truth, and classical Christian education that forms students as followers of Christ and Kingdom citizens, with no signs of relativism or secular neutrality.    

2. **School Purpose & Goal:** Its stated goal is to produce wholesome, competent, and virtuous followers of Christ who live by truth, goodness, and beauty, clearly prioritizing formation in wisdom and virtue over mere college or career preparation, though it also offers a college-preparatory education.    

3. **Curriculum:** The curriculum is explicitly organized around the **Trivium**—grammar, logic, and rhetoric—with logic taught as the art of reasoning and rhetoric as writing and oration aimed at forming “the good man speaking well,” a classic Quintilian ideal.   

4. **Pedagogy:** Pedagogically, the school emphasizes logical reasoning, questioning, evaluation, critique, and rhetorical expression, aligning with dialectic and rhetoric in the classical tradition. Some hands-on and discovery language appears, but it does not displace the Trivium-centered approach.   

5. **Faculty:** While the ethos implies that teachers are Christian mentors in a covenant, discipleship-oriented environment, the public materials sampled do not yet provide a detailed portrait of teachers as models of virtue or a “living curriculum,” so this category is rated **Somewhat Classical** on coverage grounds.    

6. **Parents & Community:** As a University-Model school, Adolesco strongly emphasizes partnership with parents, who serve as co-educators on School@Home days and primary disciplers of their children, with the school explicitly “partnering with parents” toward a shared goal of forming virtuous followers of Christ.    

With **five categories rated CLASSICAL** and one **SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL**, the overall classification of Adolesco Preparatory Academy is **CLASSICAL**.
