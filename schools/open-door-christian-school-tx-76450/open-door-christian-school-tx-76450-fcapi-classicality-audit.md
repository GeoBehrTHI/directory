## Executive Summary

Open Door Christian School Graham (TX 76450) demonstrates a strong classical Christian orientation across the six classical criteria. Public materials from the ODCS Graham site emphasize a Christ-centered education, integration of biblical truth into all subjects, liturgical/missional practices (chapel, worship, sacraments-like rhetoric), a curriculum that foregrounds biblical worldview, Western civ/history in a classical framing, classical literature and memory work (narration/recitation), opportunities for Latin, and a family-structured, parent-partnered community. The evidence supports a robust alignment with transcendental realism (truths about God, virtue, beauty), formation-oriented goals, a curriculum steeped in the liberal arts and Great Books sensibilities, a pedagogy that uses memory work, chanting, narration, and occasional Socratic-style inquiry in upper grades, a faculty profile described as exemplary and forming the living curriculum, and a strong parent/community partnership. Taken together, the school rates CLASSICAL across all six categories, with explicit textual anchors in the site’s mission, curriculum, chapel/worship, memory/recitation practices, and parent involvement.

Key sources informing this assessment include the ODCS Graham public materials (mission statements, curriculum descriptions, enrichment of chapel and Latin/foreign language, and explicit statements about biblical worldview and formation) as well as the knowledge-base rubric for classical education (Six Essential Elements) which provides the Positive/Negative keyword framework used to ground the category-by-category scoring. See citations to evidence of biblical grounding, chapel, classical literature references, grammar/logic/rhetoric emphasis, memorization practices, Latin instruction, and parent/community partnership in the ODCS materials.
