## Executive Summary

Coram Deo Classical School (CDCS) in Peachtree City, GA (Zip 30269) presents a robust classical Christian program with strong religious foundations, a formation-focused purpose, and a curriculum centered on liberal arts, Great Books, and a Trivium-based structure. Public materials from the school's site emphasize Christ-centered education, Scripture-informed pedagogy, liturgical and communal practices, and a clear commitment to truth, goodness, and beauty. The public materials also reveal a community-supported model that actively engages parents as partners and centers faculty as living exemplars of classical formation. Overall, the school aligns strongly with classical aims across most rubric categories, with Element 4 (Pedagogy) showing some nuances that merit a closer look, but not enough to undermine the overall classical orientation.

- Aggregate classification: CLASSICAL
- Key strengths: explicit Christian/theological integration; trivium-based curriculum; great books and classical rhetoric emphasis; liturgical and communal practices; explicit parent partnership; living-faculty model present in public materials.
- The one category with relative nuance: Pedagogy (Element 4) shows substantial classical elements (memory work, chant, hymn-like practices, and structured recitation) but less explicit evidence of Socratic seminars or high‑school-level seminar culture in the public-facing materials. This yields a Somewhat Classical signal for that single category, while the others strongly align with classical aims.

If the school publicly articulates a dedicated seminar or Paideia-style approach at the high school level, that would push Pedagogy toward full CLASSICAL alignment.
