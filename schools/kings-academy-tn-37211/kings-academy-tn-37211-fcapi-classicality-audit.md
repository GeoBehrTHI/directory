## Executive Summary

Kings Academy Nashville (ZIP 37211 area) presents a clearly Christian, classically influenced program. Public materials emphasize a Christ-centered community, classical framing, and a commitment to formation of character alongside academic growth. Evidence from the school’s site and related materials shows explicit religious foundations (Christ-centered, Biblical worldview, prayer/chapel life), an emphasis on formation and virtue, and a curriculum and pedagogy aligned to classical intuitions (Paideia program presence, emphasis on a storied cultural inheritance). However, explicit, detailed articulation of canonical classical elements such as sustained Great Books study, a formal Trivium/Quadrivium curriculum, or full implementation of scholarly Great Conversation in the public presentation remains less explicit. Overall, the school sits squarely within the classical-informed tradition but with elements more clearly aligned to a Christian formation and Paideia-inspired pedagogy than a fully explicit, mature classical program across all six elements. Therefore, the aggregate classification is SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL, with strong classical-adjacent and faith-anchored elements, and room to further foreground the full classical lexicon in curriculum, pedagogy, and governance.
