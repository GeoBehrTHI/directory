## Executive Summary

Christ Classical School California (CA 93422) presents a robust classical Christian program anchored in the Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric), explicit Christ-centered formation, and a culture that integrates biblical faith with classical educational aims. Public materials emphasize a Christ-centered culture, a distinctly classical methodology, and a curriculum rooted in the Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric) with Latin and biblical resources. These features align strongly with classical education principles, particularly in its focus on formation, virtue, wisdom, and a communal, liturgical ethos around worship and biblical study. The school additionally communicates a clear partnership model with families, admissions processes, and a public-facing faculty profile, all of which support a coherent classical framework.

Overall, based on the six-category rubric drawn from the Six Essential Elements of a Classical School, Christ Classical School CA 93422 is CLASSICAL across the evaluated areas. The strongest signals come from explicit catechetical emphasis on God, Scripture, virtue formation, the Trivium-driven pedagogy, and a community-oriented, parent-inclusive culture. Some opportunities for further alignment (e.g., explicit textual emphasis on Great Books / Great Conversation or more explicit statements of contemplative pedagogy as scholé) would move the rubric further toward an even more unambiguous CLASSICAL reading, but there are no substantive gaps that would suggest a non-classical designation at this time.
