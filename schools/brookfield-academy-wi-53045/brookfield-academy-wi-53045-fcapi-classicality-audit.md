# Classicality Audit Report

Executive Summary
- School: Hillsdale Academy
- Website used: https://academy.hillsdale.edu/
- Overall classification: CLASSICAL
- Why: The school explicitly identifies itself as a classical Christian K–12 school, grounding its aims in virtue and wisdom, the pursuit of truth, goodness, and beauty, and a content-rich liberal arts curriculum with Latin, logic, rhetoric, Socratic dialogue, primary sources, and regular communal worship. Faculty are described as stewards of the tradition who model virtue and use teacher-led methods; parents are affirmed as primary educators and explicit partners (multiple direct statements across the site). These features align tightly with the Six Essential Elements framework and its keyword lexicon for classical schools    . The positive signals match the “Six Essential Elements” lexicon across all categories, with no substantive negative signals such as “project-based learning dominance,” “experiential learning focus,” or “college-and-career ready” framing dominating the narrative  .

Method
- Framework: Six Essential Elements of a Classical School, including the Positive/Negative Keyword Indicators and the consolidated classicality lexicon (Religious/Metaphysical Foundations; Purpose/Goal; Curriculum; Pedagogy; Faculty; Parents & Community)  .
- Mandatory keyword analysis: For each category, we list exact words/phrases found on the school’s site and compare them to the Six Elements positive/negative indicators before scoring (Classical / Somewhat Classical / Not Classical).

Note on the website sources: All quotations/phrases below are from the school’s public pages and profile documents included in the crawl dataset (e.g., “Mission Statement & Philosophy,” “Academic Program,” “Portrait of a Graduate,” “School Profile,” and homepage hero text)     .
