## Executive Summary

Whitefield Academy KC (public materials publicly displayed on whitefieldacademykc.org) presents itself as a Christ-centered, PK–12 classical Christian school operating in Kansas City. Public content emphasizes a strong classical orientation, explicit Christian formation across subjects, and a partnership with families. The school repeatedly uses language such as “Christ-centered,” “classical education,” and “integrate Christian teaching in every subject,” and it highlights liturgical/communal practices (prayer gatherings) and the role of families in education. Notable curricular features include the presence of Latin & Greek instruction and a deliberately classical framing of schooling (Pre-K through 12th grade). Faculty pages emphasize credentialed teachers and subject specialization. The school also foregrounds parent involvement and community—evidenced by family testimonials, admissions communications, and events that invite family participation.

Overall classification (six-category rubric): SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL.

- Rationale: The school clearly centers Christian formation and classical framing (Religious Foundations; Curriculum; Purpose & Goals; Faculty; Parents & Community). It demonstrates several hallmark classical elements (Christ-centered classical approach, Latin & Greek, PK–12 classical program, Great Books-like framing is implied though not spelled out on the site, and a strong partnership with families). However, explicit, comprehensive evidence of some core pedagogy markers (e.g., explicit Socratic seminars across upper grades, explicit Great Books programs and “Great Conversation,” the explicit, systematic use of the Trivium in the public-facing materials) is less overt. The absence of clear, on-site, public declarations of some key pedagogy and Great Books practices means the school falls into Somewhat Classical rather than fully Classical by the rubrics, though it leans strongly toward the Classical Christian model.

## What Would Move the Needle

- Make Great Books and Great Conversation explicit across curriculum: The public materials foreground classical education but do not consistently articulate a full Great Books program or Great Conversation across all grades. Publicly articulating a structured Great Books initiative (with reading lists, teacher guides, and recurring Great Conversation seminars) would push Element 3 (Curriculum) toward CLASSICAL and strengthen the overall CLASSICAL rating.

- Publicly spell out Socratic pedagogy and memory work: The classical lexicon emphasizes Socratic dialogue, memory work (recitation/chanting), and Scholé. The site would benefit from explicit articulation of Socratic seminars and structured memory work in upper grades (Element 4: Pedagogy). This would move Pedagogy from SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL to CLASSICAL.

- Expand explicit integration of the Trivium across all grades: The lexicon foregrounds the Seven Liberal Arts (grammar, logic, rhetoric) and Quadrivium (arithmetic, geometry, music, astronomy). Public materials that explicitly map the K–12 curriculum to grammar-logic-rhetoric progression (and demonstrate a systematic progression through Trivium stages) would elevate Element 3 and Element 4.

- Strengthen faculty narrative around the “living curriculum”: While the site shows credentialed faculty, more explicit language tying faculty to the living curriculum and mentoring culture would push Element 5 toward CLASSICAL (e.g., statements like “our faculty embody and model the virtues we teach,” “the faculty are the living curriculum,” and formal faculty collaboration/mentorship). The knowledge-base lexicon supports this framing (Element 5), and making it explicit would raise the overall score.

- Document liturgical rhythm as a formal school practice: The liturgical language appears in community prayers; making liturgical routines (calendar, communal worship, morning prayer) an explicit, formalized practice within the school’s daily schedule would strengthen Element 1 (Religious Foundations) and Element 6 (Parents & Community) as well as Element 2 formation.

- Increase explicit messaging about Great Books integration and “truth, goodness, beauty” as real ideals: The lexicon emphasizes transcendental realism and objective truth; the site can strengthen its public messaging around these core transcendental ideals to reinforce Element 1 and Element 2.
