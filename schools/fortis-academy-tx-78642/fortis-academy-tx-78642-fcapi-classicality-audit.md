## Executive Summary

Fortis Academy (Liberty Hill, Texas; 78642) presents a robust Classical Christian program anchored in a Trivium pedagogy, strong Great Books orientation, and a University-Model school structure that emphasizes active parental partnership. Public materials from the Fortis site consistently frame the school as Christ-centered, biblically informed, and committed to forming character (truth, goodness, beauty) alongside intellect. Across multiple sections (School of Grammar/Logic/Rhetoric, admissions, and parent partnership pages) Fortis foregrounds virtue, wisdom, and a public-facing claim of “great conversation” with classical texts. The school also emphasizes a collaborative university-model (teacher + parent co-teaching) and a defined structure for grades K–12 that integrates at-home mentorship with on-campus instruction. On balance, the Fortis materials align strongly with the six elements of classical education.
