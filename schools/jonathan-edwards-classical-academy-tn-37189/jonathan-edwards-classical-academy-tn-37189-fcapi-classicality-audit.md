## Executive Summary

Jonathan Edwards Classical Academy (JECA), Nashville, TN 37189, presents a robust Classical Christian program that aligns strongly with the Six Essential Elements of a Classical School. The public materials consistently articulate a God-centered, Christ-focused formation that integrates theology with classical pedagogy (Trivium and Quadrivium), a Great Books approach, and explicit emphasis on virtue, wonder, and the cultivation of wisdom. The school features a clearly defined mission to form lifelong learners who pursue truth, goodness, and beauty as revealed in Scripture, with explicit attention to the Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric) across grade bands, a capstone senior thesis, and Latin study. Faculty are described as professional, virtuous mentors who embody the curriculum, and parents are positioned as partners in a Christian formation process. Taken together, JECA’s public-facing content demonstrates a consistent Classical Christian orientation across religious foundations, purpose, curriculum, pedagogy, faculty, and parent/community engagement.

Key evidence points:
- Religious and metaphysical foundations are foregrounded through God-centered formulation, Biblical integration, theology, and worship-centric language across admissions, mission/vision, and program descriptions.  
- The school’s stated purpose centers on formation, virtue, and love of the true, good, and beautiful, with explicit reference to God’s Word and worldview. 
- Curriculum highlights explicitly include the Seven Liberal Arts (grammar logic rhetoric), Great Books/primary sources, Latin, and an integrated historical worldview. 
- Pedagogy emphasizes the Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric), contemplative/restful elements, and Socratic/seminar-style discourse at upper levels, with memorization and recitation as memory-building practices. 
- Faculty language centers on professional, virtuous teachers who model character and serve as the living curriculum; the mission/vision page highlights teachers who “ascribe everything to God.” 
- Parent/community partnership with families, including homeschool avenues and open engagement, is integral to JECA’s model. 

Aggregate classification: CLASSICAL

Given the breadth and consistency of classical-content across all six categories, JECA meets the core criteria of a classical school with alignment to liberal arts education, Great Books, theological grounding, and a Trivium-centered pedagogy, while maintaining a distinctive Christian frame. The site does not present countervailing signals (e.g., secular or purely job-prep framing) to indicate a move away from classical aims. Therefore, the overall assessment is CLASSICAL.

What would move the needle
- No major needle-moving changes are required since this is already a CLASSICAL orientation across all six categories. If desired, the school could further strengthen explicit articulation of Great Conversation and Great Books in every department (beyond mentions) to reinforce the catechetical integration of classical texts with contemporary disciplines.
