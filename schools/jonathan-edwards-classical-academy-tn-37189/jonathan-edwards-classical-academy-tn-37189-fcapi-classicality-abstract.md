## ABSTRACT

JECA demonstrates a coherent CLASSICAL profile across all six categories, anchored in a Christian formation framework and a robust classical curriculum and pedagogy. The Religious and Metaphysical Foundations category shows God-centered theology, scriptural authority, and liturgical-communal language. The School Purpose and Goal category centers on formation, virtue, and the pursuit of truth, goodness, and beauty. The Curriculum category highlights the Seven Liberal Arts, Great Books/primary sources, Latin, and Western civilization. Pedagogy emphasizes the Trivium and a progression from grammar to rhetoric, with contemplative and Socratic elements. The Faculty category emphasizes virtuous mentors and living curriculum, while the Parents & Community category emphasizes partnership with families, parent formation, and openness to engagement, including homeschool partnerships.

Overall, JECA is CLASSICAL, with explicit signals for Great Books, Christian integration, and a teacher-centered, formative approach that aligns with the classical canon. The public materials consistently use language of truth, beauty, and goodness in a theologically grounded educational project, and there are no evident countervailing signals that would indicate deviation toward NOT CLASSICAL or SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL.
