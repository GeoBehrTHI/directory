## Executive Summary

Chesterton Academy of Albuquerque (ABQ) presents a coherent classical Catholic model of education. Public materials on their site emphasize a liberal-arts oriented, faith-informed curriculum, with explicit focus on formation (virtue, wisdom, and beauty), the integration of theology with the humanities, and a classical cadence across history, literature, philosophy, and the arts. The school foregrounds Catholic identity (daily Mass, theology texts, liturgical life) and anchors its pedagogy in a three-pillar framework (Intellect, Character, Spirituality) that centers on formation through a culture of life. Evidence of a robust curriculum (two years of Latin, four-year history sequence, and an integrated classical approach) and a faculty described in terms of mentors and living exemplars further supports a CLASSICAL rating across most categories. The primary area with comparatively weaker explicit public signals of classical pedagogy is the explicit mention of Socratic seminars or similar high-school seminar formats; nonetheless, the explicit emphasis on classical inquiry, Great Books-style formation, and the liberal-arts curriculum keeps Category 4 in the SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL range rather than NOT CLASSICAL or fully CLASSICAL.

Overall classification: CLASSICAL.

- Evidence across categories shows a consistent classical Catholic orientation: daily Mass and theology as central, formation-focused aims, a structured liberal-arts curriculum, Latin study, a four-year history sequence, and a faculty described in terms of virtue and mentorship.

- Key public signals include: Catholic theology and liturgy (Bible, Catechism, Mass, liturgy), the Three Pillars and Cultura Vitae, a classical curriculum with Latin and history across grades, integrated humanities (literature/history/philosophy/theology), and a parent-led/community orientation (volunteer culture, founding/parent-led model). Citations illustrate these signals: daily Mass and Catholic theology (Mass daily); three-pillar model and Cultura Vitae; integrated classical curriculum and Latin requirements; explicit humanities and theology focus; and parent-led/volunteer community language.

The aggregate rating thus stands as CLASSICAL.
