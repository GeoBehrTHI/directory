# ABSTRACT

Chesterton Academy of Albuquerque embodies a Catholic, classical education in which formation, virtue, and wisdom anchor a liberal-arts curriculum across history, literature, philosophy, theology, and the arts. The school’s public materials emphasize daily Mass and liturgy, foundational Catholic texts, a Three Pillars formation model (Intellect, Character, Spirituality), and a curriculum that includes Latin, a four-year history sequence, and integrated humanities. Faculty and parent-community signals reinforce a traditional classical milieu, with a strong emphasis on mentorship and family partnership. The aggregate assessment is CLASSICAL, with Pedagogy identified as SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL due to limited explicit high-school Socratic signaling in public materials.
