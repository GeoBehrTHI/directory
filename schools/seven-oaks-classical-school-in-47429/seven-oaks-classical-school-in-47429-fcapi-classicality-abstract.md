## ABSTRACT

Seven Oaks Classical School, Ellettsville, Indiana (47429), operates as a Hillsdale-aligned public charter school emphasizing classical education, moral character, and civic virtue. Public materials confirm a formation-first aim (whole-child formation) and a transparent non-religious stance, while foregrounding truth, goodness, and beauty as real and knowable ends. The six-category analysis yields: Element 1 (Religious & Metaphysical Foundations): SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL, Element 2 (Purpose & Goal): CLASSICAL, Element 3 (Curriculum): SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL, Element 4 (Pedagogy): SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL, Element 5 (Faculty): CLASSICAL, Element 6 (Parents & Community): CLASSICAL. Aggregating these signals produces an overall classification of SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL.

Key conclusions:
- The school meets core classical aims in formation, virtue, and liberal-arts curriculum, with robust faculty and parental/community engagement signals.
- Public materials lack consistent, campus-wide Great Books/Great Conversation language and universal Socratic pedagogy, which keeps the overall classification from reaching CLASSICAL.
- The public religious/metaphysical foundations are nuanced by a stated non-religious stance, which supports a SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL rating in Element 1.

Cited evidence incorporates official statements on religion and formation, Hillsdale alignment signals, Latin & Western-tradition curriculum, Lyceum dialogue framing, Master Teacher and faculty signals, and parent/community engagement statements:
- Non-religious stance and the true/good/beautiful transcendental framing. 
- Formation emphasis and meaningful-life goals. 
- Liberal-arts/curriculum scope and Western-tradition orientation; Latin emphasis; Lyceum/dialogue.   
- Master Teacher/Hillsdale alignment and faculty-centric living curriculum.  
- Parent/community engagement and openness to visits.
