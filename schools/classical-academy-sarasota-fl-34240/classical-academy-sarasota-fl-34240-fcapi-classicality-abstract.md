# ABSTRACT

The Classical Academy of Sarasota (TCA) presents a CLASSICAL alignment across all six rubric categories. The school’s public materials foreground canonical classical education: a Trivium-focused curriculum (grammar, logic, rhetoric) with a Quadrivium component, Latin instruction from early grades, and history taught as a throughline across grades with first-source documents and great literature. The pedagogical approach blends contemplative memory work and chant in the lower grades with Socratic seminars and rhetoric training in the upper grades. Virtue and character formation anchor the school's purpose, with a motto that explicitly foregrounds truth, goodness, and beauty. Faculty are positioned as mentors and living exemplars within a small-community culture, and parents are regarded as co-educators and partners with ongoing formation opportunities. The school’s materials also emphasize a robust partnership with Hillsdale College and canonical Western heritage, consistent with classical aims. Overall, the evidence supports CLASSICAL across religious/metaphysical foundations, purpose, curriculum, pedagogy, faculty, and parent/community. The public materials reflect a coherent classical mission that centers virtue, wisdom, and the liberal arts, with strong emphasis on tradition, memory, and teacher-led formation.
