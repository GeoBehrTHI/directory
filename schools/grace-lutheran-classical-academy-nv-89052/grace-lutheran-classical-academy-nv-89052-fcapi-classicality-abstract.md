## ABSTRACT

Grace Lutheran Classical Academy (GLCA) in Henderson, NV demonstrates a robust CLASSICAL orientation anchored in Lutheran theology, a classical liberal-arts curriculum, and a collaborative community model with strong parental partnership. The religious and metaphysical foundations are explicit through Lutheran catechesis, liturgy, and chapel; the school’s stated purpose emphasizes formation, virtue, wisdom, and service, aligning with a classical telos. The curriculum centers on the Seven Liberal Arts (Trivium and Quadrivium), and historical content is treated as a core curricular focus rather than solely social studies. The faculty are positioned as moral exemplars and living curriculum, with collaborative practices that involve parents as co-educators. The parental-community axis is particularly strong, with open engagement, formation opportunities, and a welcoming environment for family involvement.
