## Executive Summary

Grace Lutheran Classical Academy (GLCA) in Henderson, NV (service region aligned with 89052) presents a distinctly classical, faith-driven program that centers Lutheran theology, collaborative home-schooling dynamics, and a liberal-arts-oriented curriculum built around the Trivium and Quadrivium. Public-facing materials emphasize a collaborative educational model anchored in Christian faith, Lutheran catechesis, and a two-days-per-week on-campus structure with home-based study the remaining days. The school frequently ties its mission to formation of character, virtue, wisdom, and service in church, home, and community, rather than solely workforce preparation. Taken together, the school earns a CLASSICAL classification overall, with one category (Pedagogy) showing more nuanced alignment that leads to a SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL assessment in that domain. The aggregate assessment places GLCA as CLASSICAL, with concrete opportunities to further strengthen the pedagogy in the direction of a fuller classical pedagogy.

Key signals of CLASSICAL orientation
- Strong religious and metaphysical framing: Lutheran theology, catechesis, liturgy, chapel, and confessional commitments are repeatedly foregrounded. Evidence includes explicit references to Lutheran doctrine, catechesis, liturgy, and chapel, with integration into daily academic life.  
- Curriculum anchored in classical liberal arts: Clear emphasis on the Seven Liberal Arts with Trivium and Quadrivium, plus engagement with a structured classical curriculum and CCLE/SCL affiliations. 
- Formation/virtue orientation: Texts describe formation of faith, virtue, wisdom, and service as core aims, with Gospel-centered framing and a focus on character. 
- Family/community partnership: A collaborative model places parents as co-educators and co-planners with on-campus teachers, aligning with a classical-community ethos. 

Nuanced signal (Pedagogy)
- Pedagogy shows a hybrid, microschooling approach (two-days-on-campus with home-learning), which, while classical in intent, does not explicitly foreground Socratic seminars, Great Books reading mandates, or formal high-school-level classical discourse in the public-facing materials. This yields a SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL placement for Pedagogy, even as other domains are clearly CLASSICAL.
