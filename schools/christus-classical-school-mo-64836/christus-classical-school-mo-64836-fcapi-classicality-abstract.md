## ABSTRACT

Christus Classical School (Carthage, MO, 64836) presents a CLASSICAL orientation across core classical education dimensions in its public materials. The school frames education as formation through the liberal arts within a biblically anchored worldview, emphasizing truth, goodness, and beauty, and it partners with families in a covenantal, Christian framework. The curriculum is explicitly classical (grammar, logic, rhetoric; seven liberal arts; Great Books/Great Conversation framing; integration across disciplines; history as part of a liberal-arts approach). Parental involvement and community signals are strong, including “In Loco Parentis” language and family-centered events. Pedagogy and Faculty signals show robust classical alignment in terms of content and formation, but public materials lack explicit, widespread articulation of high-school Socratic seminar practices and a fully-developed living-curriculum faculty narrative, which positions Categories 4 and 5 as SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL. The aggregate ranking remains CLASSICAL.

- Core thematic signals supporting CLASSICAL: biblical/transcendentals framing; formation-focused purpose; Seven Liberal Arts and Great Books orientation in curriculum; explicit emphasis on truth, goodness, and beauty; strong parental partnership signals. Citations to school content and classical rubric materials support these conclusions.
