## ABSTRACT

Thales Academy Raleigh NC 27616 demonstrates a robust CLASSICAL profile across six standard criteria:

- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: Judeo-Christian framing, natural law, and moral philosophy underpin the curriculum, with explicit attention to objective truth, beauty, and goodness, and to Western intellectual inheritance. This aligns with transcendental moral realism and communal liturgical sensibilities found in classical schooling.

- Purpose and Goal: The mission centers on virtue formation and the cultivation of wisdom, rather than solely job preparation, with a clear emphasis on truth and the good life.

- Curriculum: The school champions the liberal arts through grammar, logic, and rhetoric, the study of Great Books and the Western canon, Latin, and a feature emphasis on the development of wonder and intellectual inheritance.

- Pedagogy: A blended approach combines contemplative and rigorous instruction, lower-division singing/chants with memory work, and upper-division Socratic discussions, seminars, debates, and rhetoric training.

- Faculty: The faculty is portrayed as a living curriculum and mentors, with a visible faculty directory and school leadership emphasizing virtuous teaching and partnership with students.

- Parents & Community: The school foregrounds partnerships with families, parent engagement, and accessible opportunities for parent involvement, visits, and shared educational mission.

Core textual anchors include the Thales First Principles (Natural Order, Human Dignity, Moral Philosophy, Intellectual Inheritance) and the Three Phases of Classical Education (Grammar, Logic, Rhetoric). The Great Books and Western canon are repeatedly highlighted as central to the learning enterprise, and the school explicitly commits to a Judeo-Christian framework guiding its approach to moral philosophy and human dignity. These elements collectively signal a coherent Classical Education model as applied at Thales Academy Raleigh NC 27616.
