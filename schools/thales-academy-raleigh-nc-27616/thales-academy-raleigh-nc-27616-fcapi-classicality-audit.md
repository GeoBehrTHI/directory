## Executive Summary

Thales Academy Raleigh NC 27616 exhibits a robust classical education profile that aligns strongly with the Six Essential Elements of a Classical School. The campus materials consistently foreground truth, goodness, beauty, and virtue; emphasize formation over mere information; deploy Trivium-informed curriculum (grammar, logic, rhetoric) with a focus on the Great Books and the Western canon; employ a pedagogy that blends direct instruction in the early years with Socratic dialogue, seminars, and rhetorical training in the upper grades; present a visible faculty core and mentoring culture; and actively engage parents as partners in a joint educational project. Core phrases such as pursuing truth, beauty, and goodness; Moral Philosophy within a Judeo-Christian frame; a structured grammar-logic-rhetoric pathway; and a Western-canon-centered curriculum recur throughout the site content, newsletters, and program descriptions. The school frequently references the Great Books, Latin study, classical leadership, and a family- and community-oriented ethos, all of which map squarely onto classical expectations.

Overall, the school earns CLASSICAL across all six categories, with especially strong signals in Religious and Metaphysical Foundations, Curriculum, Pedagogy, and Community. The website materials consistently discuss transcendental aims (truth, goodness, beauty), formation of character and virtue, canonical Western learning (Great Books, Western canon), Trivium-based structure, Socratic pedagogy in upper grades, and active parental partnership. There is clear evidence of liturgical/communal sensibilities via Judeo-Christian framing and shared intellectual heritage, combined with a strong public-facing emphasis on classical identity and lifelong learning.
