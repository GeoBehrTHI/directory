## Executive Summary

Grace Academy Springfield (Grace Academy MA 01104) presents a clear Classical Christian program anchored in the Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric), a Biblically centered worldview, and a strong emphasis on Western classical heritage, Great Books, and traditional liberal arts study. Public materials from the school repeatedly frame education as formation in service of God, family, and virtuous living, with explicit statements about fostering truth, goodness, and beauty, and about cooperation with parents as co-educators. The curriculum emphasizes grammar, logic, and rhetoric; history is taught as a traditional, chronological study within Western civilization; Latin and language mastery are foregrounded; and memory work, recitation, and singing are cited pedagogical practices. The school positions itself as part of Sovereign Grace Church, uses in loco parentis language, and invites parental involvement and partnerships.
