## ABSTRACT

Great Hearts Corporate Office AZ 85034 demonstrates a CLASSICAL classification across all six classical education categories. The school articulates a Christian, theological foundation with liturgical facets and a pursuit of Truth, Goodness, and Beauty, aligning with transcendental moral realism and theology-driven aims. The purpose emphasizes formation and virtue rather than utilitarian job preparation, and the curriculum is anchored in the Seven Liberal Arts (Trivium and Quadrivium) with Great Books, Great Conversation, and integrated, interdisciplinary study designed to foster wonder. Pedagogy blends contemplative, memory-focused learning in the lower grades with Socratic dialogue and rhetoric training in the upper grades. Faculty are positioned as living embodiments of the curriculum, and parents/community are actively engaged as co-educators through information nights, enrollment processes, and family engagement initiatives. Collectively, these signals affirm a CLASSICAL orientation in alignment with the Six Essential Elements model and its keyword indicators (e.g., Truth, Goodness, Beauty; liturgical/communal practice; Great Books; Socratic pedagogy; living curriculum; partnership with families).
