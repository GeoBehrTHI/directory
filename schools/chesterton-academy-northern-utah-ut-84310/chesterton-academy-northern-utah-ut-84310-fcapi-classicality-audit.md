## Executive Summary

Chesterton Academy of Northern Utah (CANU) presents a strong classical education model with a clearly articulated Catholic identity embedded in its mission, curriculum, pedagogy, and community framework. Public materials consistently emphasize formation in virtue, the liberal arts tradition, and a pedagogical approach rooted in Socratic inquiry and rhetorical training, with theology and liturgical life integrated into the school’s life. Across the six categories of the Classical rubric, CANU demonstrates robust alignment with classical aims—especially in Religious and Metaphysical Foundations, Curriculum, Pedagogy, and Community. The school’s public communications frame education as formation in truth, goodness, and beauty within a Catholic and Incarnational context, oriented toward character formation and intellectual formation rather than mere information transfer. In short, CANU reads as CLASSICAL across categories, with minimal signals of anti-classical tendencies in the publicly available materials.

Key anchors from CANU materials include: a mission rooted in a Culture of Life and Catholic faith; explicit articulation of the Three Pillars (Intellect, Character, Spirituality) and a curriculum centered on the classics and Western thought; references to Great Books, a liberal-arts orientation, and the Great Conversation in spirit; a pedagogy that includes Socratic seminars, rhetoric training, and memory-based methods; faculty culture and norms reflected in a faculty-focused job description that foregrounds formation and Catholic practice; and sustained parental engagement and community partnerships expressed through events, open houses, and a family-centric ethos. These signals align CANU with the Positive Keyword Indicators in the Six Essential Elements of a Classical School framework, and with the broader classical lexicon’s emphasis on virtue, truth, and the transcendentals.

Executive Reasoning at a glance:
- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: CLASSICAL
- School Purpose and Goal: CLASSICAL
- Curriculum: CLASSICAL
- Pedagogy: CLASSICAL
- Faculty: CLASSICAL
- Parents & Community: CLASSICAL

Abstract view: CANU embodies a coherent classical vision with Catholic formation at its core and visible Great-Books/liberal-arts orientation, combined with Socratic pedagogy and a living-Faculty/parent-partnership culture. The aggregate ranking is CLASSICAL.
