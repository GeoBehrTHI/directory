## Executive Summary

New Life Christian School (Ephrata, WA 98823) presents a strongly classical Christian education orientation across its public materials. The school publicly emphasizes a Christ-centered, Biblical worldview and a commitment to classical pedagogy (grammar–logic–rhetoric), the Great Books/Liberal Arts tradition, and formation of virtue and wisdom. Its admissions, mission, and philosophy statements repeatedly foreground partnership with families, a liturgical/catechetical context (chapel/opening liturgy and worship practices), and a memory- and recital-leaning pedagogy in the grammar stage, all of which align with classical ideals. The school explicitly frames its purpose as formation and character development (not solely information or workforce preparation) and signals a deliberate structure for upper-school Socratic discussion and rhetoric. The public-facing faculty pages show teacher profiles and board involvement, reinforcing the sense of mentorship and the school as a lived, communal curriculum. Public materials also depict a strong parents-and-community orientation, including documented parent involvement expectations and ongoing opportunities for families to engage.
