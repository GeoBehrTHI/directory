## ABSTRACT

APA WV1 demonstrates a strong classical orientation with solid alignment across Purpose & Goals, Curriculum, Pedagogy, Faculty, and Parents & Community, culminating in a GENERAL assessment of SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL. The school’s public materials emphasize virtue, human flourishing, mastery of fundamentals, and the Trivium, alongside Socratic dialogue and parent engagement, consistent with classical education expectations. However, the Religious and Metaphysical Foundations dimension is not as clearly evidenced—there is limited explicit theology and liturgical/community practice information in public-facing content—limiting a full CLASSICAL designation in Element 1. The most important opportunities to push toward full CLASSICAL include: (1) articulating theology or metaphysical grounding in public materials, (2) adopting and publicly describing a formal Great Books program, and (3) expanding explicit contemplative dimensions (scholé) and teacher-mentored formation. If these needle-moving changes are implemented and communicated, APA WV1 could shift to CLASSICAL across all six categories.
