## Executive Summary

American Preparatory Academy West Valley 1 (APA WV1) presents a clearly classical-leaning program. The public materials emphasize a classical, liberal-arts orientation, high academic rigor, virtue and character formation, and a strong emphasis on Socratic dialogue, the Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric), and memory work. There is explicit acknowledgement of the Trivium, mastery of fundamentals, and debates/seminars as pedagogy, along with a robust parent/community engagement framework. However, the public materials do not consistently foreground explicit theology, liturgical/community worship, or a formal Great Books program. Taken together, APA WV1 demonstrates substantial alignment with classical education in most domains, but falls short of the fuller religious/metaphysical grounding and the explicit Great Books/Great Conversation emphasis that some classical schools foreground. Overall classification: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL, with clear needle-moving opportunities identified below.

Key takeaways from the six categories:

- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: Strong on virtue, character, and classical aims, but lacking explicit theological grounding or liturgical practice in public-facing materials. Category score leans toward SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL.
- Purpose and Goals: Emphasizes human flourishing, virtue, and formation over mere information or job preparation. Category score: CLASSICAL.
- Curriculum: Centered on the liberal arts and Trivium; explicit Great Books references are less evident in public materials. Category score: CLASSICAL.
- Pedagogy: Socratic/dialogue-based upper-school pedagogy with rhetoric training; some lower-school memory/chanting concepts discussed in overarching classical frameworks. Category score: CLASSICAL.
- Faculty: Public materials present a professional, mission-aligned faculty and a “living curriculum” idea via leadership and staff; Category score: CLASSICAL.
- Parents & Community: Explicit emphasis on parent partnership, volunteer expectations, and family engagement policies. Category score: CLASSICAL.

What would move the needle section follows after the SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL ranking, with concrete steps to reach full CLASSICAL status.

What would move the needle

- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: Explicitly state theology or liturgical/community practices on the WV1 site, linking the classical education with a grounded theological or metaphysical framework. Include statements about doctrinal commitments, liturgical practices (even if non-denominational), and how worship or shared contemplative practices integrate with the curriculum. This would shift Category 1 from SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL toward CLASSICAL.

- Curriculum: Publicly articulate a formal Great Books program or “Great Conversation” commitments across grades (even if staged gradually by grade level). If Great Books are already used in practice, make explicit on the site how students access primary texts, authors, and the conversations surrounding them.

- Pedagogy: Emphasize and document explicit contemplative/scholé moments (restful learning) alongside rigorous Socratic inquiry, including explicit references to quiet, contemplative passages or moments of reflection as part of daily routines.

- Faculty: Expand the public-facing articulation of the living-curriculum concept by providing specific faculty portraits, exemplars of teacher modeling virtue, and descriptions of mentorship roles (model teachers and mentors) as part of the public materials.

- Parents & Community: Strengthen and publicize formal opportunities for parent education about classical education (e.g., book studies, lectures, or community discussions about the classical model) to deepen parent understanding and participation.

- Great Books / Interdisciplinary Integration: Broaden interdisciplinary and integration languages tied to curriculum topics by clearly describing cross-disciplinary projects and integrated units (history, literature, philosophy, science) that demonstrate unity of truth across subjects.

- Public narrative around formation and character: More explicit articulation of how virtue, wisdom, and beauty guide daily practice and assessment, with clear metrics and examples tied to classroom activities.

# ABSTRACT

APA WV1 demonstrates a robust classical orientation, with strong alignment in Purpose and Goal, Curriculum (Trivium emphasis), Pedagogy (Socratic dialogue, seminars, and rhetoric), Faculty (named leadership and living-curriculum framing), and Parents & Community (active parent engagement). The Public materials strongly emphasize virtue, character, human flourishing, and a classical liberal-arts approach to education, supported by explicit references to mastery of fundamentals and the Trivium. However, the Religious and Metaphysical Foundations category shows only partial alignment: theology, liturgical practices, and overt metaphysical grounding are not consistently foregrounded in public content, which prevents a full CLASSICAL designation in Category 1. The aggregate assessment across all six categories lands at SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL, with meaningful opportunities to move toward full CLASSICAL status.
