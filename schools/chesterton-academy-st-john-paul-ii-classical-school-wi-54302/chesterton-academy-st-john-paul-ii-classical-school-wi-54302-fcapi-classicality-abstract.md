## Abstract

Chesterton Academy of St. John Paul II Classical School (Green Bay, WI 54302) is a Catholic, classical high school embedded in the GRACE system. Its public materials emphasize liturgical life (Mass, confession, adoration), a culture of life, and the Catholic tradition (St. John Paul II as patron). Pedagogy integrates contemplative life with rigorous inquiry, including rhetoric and public speaking, with an aim toward virtue formation and the Great Conversation in Western thought. Faculty are presented as mentors forming a living curriculum, and parents are considered true partners in education. Across categories, the school demonstrates CLASSICAL alignment, with Element 4 showing SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL alignment due to partial explicit articulation of Socratic-style pedagogy in public materials. The aggregate assessment is CLASSICAL.

## ABSTRACT

Chesterton Academy of St. John Paul II Classical School, Green Bay, Wisconsin (54302), presents a robust CLASSICAL profile across five of six categories, with Pedagogy (Element 4) reaching a SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL alignment. The school’s Catholic liturgical life, culture-of-life formation, and explicit focus on virtue and wisdom, together with a liberal-arts curriculum centered on grammar/logic/rhetoric, Great Books, and integrated studies of history, philosophy, and theology, align closely with the classical education rubric outlined in the Six Essential Elements documentation. The Headmaster-led faculty and strong parental/community engagement reinforce the living-curriculum model vital to classical schooling. Public materials underscore a mission to form saints and cultivate a culture of truth, goodness, and beauty, with explicit communal practices such as Mass, confession, adoration, and pilgrimages.

In sum, Chesterton Academy of St. John Paul II Classical School demonstrates CLASSICAL alignment with strong Catholic formation, a liberal-arts curriculum, and a teacher-mentored, community-engaged approach to classical education. The singular area for growth—Categories 4 (Pedagogy)—would be to provide more explicit, codified public documentation of upper-grade Socratic seminars and a grade-by-grade blueprint for the classical pedagogy to lift it to a fully CLASSICAL status.
