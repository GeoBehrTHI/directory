## Executive Summary

Chesterton Academy of St. John Paul II Classical School (Green Bay, WI, ZIP 54302) presents itself publicly as a Catholic, classical high school embedded in the Green Bay Area Catholic Education (GRACE) system. The public-facing materials emphasize a culture of life, a liturgical rhythm (Mass, confession, adoration, retreats), and a curriculum centered in the liberal-arts tradition with emphasis on virtue, beauty, and truth. Notable features include: daily Mass and other liturgical practices, a House System inspired by Oxford/Cambridge, a patron-centered Catholic identity, and a curriculum explicitly oriented toward reading great works and forming character through rhetoric, logic, and memory work. Taken together, these signals align strongly with the classical education rubric in the knowledge base, particularly around formation, virtue, and a liberal-arts curriculum anchored in the Great Books and classical methods. The school’s Catholic identity and emphasis on formation over vocational outcomes further reinforce a Classical orientation.

Overall classification: CLASSICAL

Key evidence highlights:
- Religious & Metaphysical Foundations: daily Holy Mass, confession/adoration, catechesis and Catholic formation; GRACE affiliation; explicit Catholic liturgical life (e.g., pilgrimage to Rome) and culture of life framing.  
- Purpose & Goal: formation of virtue and wisdom through a Catholic classical framework; emphasis on truth, goodness, and beauty as real; saintly formation via Catholic identity.  
- Curriculum: classical liberal-arts orientation, grammar/logic/rhetoric framing, reading of great works, integrated across disciplines, and an emphasis on wonder and beauty. 
- Pedagogy: blend of contemplative/liturgical life with rigorous inquiry (Socratic-like discussion, seminars, debates, rhetoric, and public speaking); explicit emphasis on memory work and wonder. 
- Faculty: public materials reference the Headmaster and teaching staff in a manner consistent with mentorship and living curriculum (Catholic formation as central). 
- Parents & Community: strong parental partnership ethos (pastoral/formation focus, co-educators with parents), opportunities for parent involvement (Fireside Chats, lectures, family faith events), and welcoming tours/visit options.
