## Executive Summary

Cincinnati Classical Academy (Oh 45215) presents a robust classical-education program across its public-facing materials. Using the Six Essential Elements framework, the school demonstrates clear classical commitments in most domains, with Element 1 (Religious and Metaphysical Foundations) and Element 2 (School Purpose and Goal) and Element 3 (Curriculum) and Element 4 (Pedagogy) and Element 5 (Faculty) and Element 6 (Parents & Community) aligning strongly with classical aims. The site emphasizes virtue, truth, goodness, beauty, Great Books, liberal-arts pedagogy, Socratic dialogue, memorization/recitation, and a living, teacher-led culture. The school also stresses formation of character and civic virtue, a content-rich liberal-arts curriculum, and partnerships with families. Some elements—such as explicit liturgical/theological practices—are less overt on public pages, though the mission and pedagogy strongly reflect classical aims. Overall classification: CLASSICAL.

Key insights:

- Strong emphasis on virtue, wisdom, and formation over mere information; frequent allusions to truth, goodness, beauty, and the liberal arts (gra mm ar, logic, rhetoric) and Great Books. This aligns with classical aims in Categories 2, 3, 4, 5, and 6, yielding CLASSICAL across most domains. Evidence includes explicit statements about moral character, civic virtue, and a content-rich classical curriculum, supported by pages such as What is Classical Education? and Mission/Headmaster communications.
- Pedagogy is distinctly classical (Socratic dialogue, memorization/recitation, rhetoric, teacher-led instruction, and conscious cultivation of memory and virtue). Public materials repeatedly describe these methods (e.g., “we diagram sentences, we read the great and difficult books, we memorize and recite poetry, we do mathematical proofs”).
- Curriculum centers on Great Books, primary sources, grammar–logic–rhetoric (Trivium), Latin, and a liberal-arts approach, with explicit emphasis on the Classics and Western tradition. Positive indicators include references to Great Books, primary sources, Latin, and classical curricula.
- Family/Community partnership and parent involvement are foregrounded (e.g., Our Mission/Our Vision emphasize parental partnership and moral formation at home, plus a culture of parent engagement). This supports Element 6 as CLASSICAL.

Aggregate Assessment

- Overall classification: CLASSICAL.
- Rationale: Across all six categories, Cincinnati Classical Academy’s public materials substantially align with the classical-education rubric: virtues and formation; content-rich liberal-arts curriculum; memory-based and dialogic pedagogy; a faculty-centered living curriculum; and robust parental partnership. The strongest alignment appears in Curriculum, Pedagogy, Faculty, and Parents & Community, with formation-focused language pervading School Purpose and Goal and Religious/Metaphysical Foundations. While explicit liturgical/theological practices are not widely foregrounded on public pages, transcendental aims (truth, goodness, beauty) and virtue-based formation are consistently emphasized.
