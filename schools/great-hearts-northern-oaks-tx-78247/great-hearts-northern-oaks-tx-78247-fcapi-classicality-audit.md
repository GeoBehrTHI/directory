## Executive Summary

Great Hearts Northern Oaks (GHNO) demonstrates a clearly classical orientation consistent with the Six Essential Elements framework. Across most categories, GHNO’s public materials reveal strong commitments to liberal arts formation, truth-forming aims, Great Books and a classical curriculum, Socratic pedagogy, a teacher-led “living curriculum,” and deep parent/community partnership. The school explicitly centers education on the pursuit of truth, goodness, and beauty, and presents a curriculum built around classical liberal arts (grammar, logic, rhetoric), Latin study, and engagement with great books and Western tradition. Public materials repeatedly emphasize formation of character, memory and virtue, and communal life rooted in a shared classical ethos. The aggregate assessment is CLASSICAL.

ABSTRACT

Great Hearts Northern Oaks (GHNO) demonstrates a robust CLASSICAL orientation across most of the classical education rubric. The school centers formation and virtue within a liberal-arts framework, grounded in the pursuit of truth, goodness, and beauty. Curriculum features include Seven Liberal Arts, Latin study, engagement with Great Books/Great Conversation, and a Western-tradition curriculum. Pedagogy combines Socratic dialogue, seminars, memory/recitation, and rhetoric-focused opportunities, with the lower-school program fostering memory and wonder. Public materials portray faculty as living mentors and a core culture, and parent/community life centers around partnership with families, PSO involvement, and ongoing home-school collaboration.
