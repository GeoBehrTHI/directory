## Executive Summary

Grace Classical Academy Bel Air MD 21015 presents a strongly classical Christian program built around the Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric) and a theology-infused mission. Public-facing materials emphasize a Christ-centered, biblically anchored approach to education, a clear formation-focused purpose, and a curriculum that centers on grammar/logic/rhetoric, Latin, and civics/debate within a humanities-led framework. Faculty portrayals stress classical training and virtue, and parental involvement is framed as a core part of the school community. Taken together, the school aligns with the core aims and structures of classical education, with minor gaps in explicit Great Books wording and certain Great-Conversation language. Overall classification: CLASSICAL.

- Evidence of strong religious and metaphysical foundations includes a Statement of Faith and explicit Christian worldview integration across curriculum and mission.  
- The school explicitly grounds its mission in forming students to think and act biblically, not merely to acquire information. 
- The curriculum centers on the classical Trivium (grammar/logic/rhetoric), with grade-band distinctions and a humanities core, along with Latin and civics/debate; Great Books are not explicitly named in the public-facing materials we reviewed. 
- Pedagogical approach includes singing/chanting in the grammar stage and Socratic/seminar-style elements in upper grades (as part of a typical classical progression), with a senior rhetoric/Thesis component in the upper grades. 
- Faculty are described as disciplined, virtuous, and classically trained, with a living-curriculum perspective; parents are invited to participate and partner with the school.  
- The school publicly positions parents as co-teachers and hosts tours and parent-education opportunities, underscoring a community-centric classical model.
