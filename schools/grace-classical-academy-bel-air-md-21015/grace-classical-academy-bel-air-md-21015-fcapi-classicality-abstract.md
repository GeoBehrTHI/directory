## ABSTRACT

Grace Classical Academy Bel Air MD 21015 exhibits a strong CLASSICAL orientation anchored in a Christian Trivium framework (grammar, logic, rhetoric) and explicit formation of character in a biblically grounded context. The school presents a clearly defined purpose focused on virtue and wisdom, a curriculum built around grammar/logic/rhetoric, Latin, civics/debate, and a senior rhetoric/thesis component, and a pedagogy that includes memory work in the grammar stage and Socratic/seminar-style activities in the later grades. The faculty are described as classically trained and virtuous, and the community emphasizes parent partnership and open engagement. While the Great Books/Great Conversation language is not overtly named in public-facing materials, the overall structure and language align with classical education’s core aims across five of the six categories. The aggregate assessment is CLASSICAL, with Category 3 (Curriculum) showing strong classical architecture but with room to explicitly name Great Books/Great Conversation to reach a higher classical-certification standard.
