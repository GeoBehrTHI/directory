## Executive Summary

Heritage Christian Academy (Lexington, SC 29072) presents a classical Christian program with clear religious foundations, a grammar-logic-rhetoric curriculum structure, visible faculty engagement, and active parental involvement. Public materials on the school site emphasize a Christ-centered worldview, integration of Scripture across disciplines, and a Trivium-based sequence (grammar/logic, with rhetoric-adjacent training implied). Public signals of community and governance through chapel, weekly Bible study, and pastoral references further reinforce a religious-metaphysical framework and community orientation.

-preliminary classification: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL

- Strengths: strong religious/metaphysical foundations; explicit Trivium-oriented curricular scaffolding; visible, long-tenured faculty and leadership; consistent parent involvement and open-campus ethos; clear articulation of mission to form virtue, wisdom, and love of truth through classical methods.

- Areas for needle movement toward a fully CLASSICAL designation: explicit articulation of Great Books/“Great Conversation” in daily practice; more explicit mention of Socratic pedagogy or seminars in upper grades; a more explicit statement tying curricula to the Great Books canon and universal liberal arts curriculum beyond standard grammar/logic/rhetoric phrasing; stronger public articulation of the “living curriculum” role of faculty in day-to-day teaching; and a more explicit, public acknowledgment of ongoing partnerships with families as co-educators beyond volunteer roles.

Key evidence (highlights)
- The school frames itself as “private education that is both classical and Christian,” with explicit Biblical worldview integration and a mission to know, love, and practice truth in service of God and man. This signals a robust Religious and Metaphysical Foundation and a Purpose/Goal aligned with formation and virtue. 
- University-level articulation of a Trivium-based program (Grammar and Logic stages described; rhetoric-oriented aims implied) appears in site materials, showing a curriculum scaffold consistent with classical pedagogy. 
- Weekly chapel, Bible readings, and a stated belief in the Bible as final authority reflect Liturgical/Communal Practice and Grounded Theology within the institution.
