## Executive Summary

Founders Classical Academy – Carrollton (Carrollton, TX 75006) demonstrates a clear orientation toward classical education, anchored in a liberal arts tradition and a character-formation ethos. Public materials emphasize forming wise, virtuous citizens through a curriculum rooted in truth, beauty, and goodness, with a heavy focus on liberal arts disciplines (Latin, logic, philosophy, classical literature) and a Trivium-informed pedagogy. The school explicitly presents itself as a partnership with families and the broader community as co-educators. However, several canonical features of a fully classical framework (notably explicit Great Books/Great Conversation language, explicit theological grounding, liturgical/community formation practices, and a fully articulated “living curriculum” built around teacher-mentorship as the core of the pedagogy) are less visible in the public-facing materials. Taken together, the school sits to a strong degree in the Classical camp but with room to deepen certain elements to be considered fully Classical.

Aggregate classification: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL

Key insights:
- Strengths across categories: clear emphasis on formation (virtue, wisdom), a rigorous liberal-arts curriculum (Latin, logic, philosophy, classic literature), and parish/community partnership with parents as co-educators. These align with core classical criteria and are well evidenced in public materials. See evidence of truth, beauty, goodness, virtue, and formation in the school’s mission and curricular language, and the explicit “co-educator” stance with families. 
- Gaps to reach full Classical status: theology/the liturgical dimension is not overtly evidenced in the public materials; Great Books/Great Conversation language is not consistently explicit in site copy; explicit “living curriculum” statements about the faculty and mentoring role of teachers are not clearly articulated in the public materials. These gaps keep Category ratings from achieving CLASSICAL rather than SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL.

ABSTRACT

Founders Classical Academy – Carrollton exhibits a strong classical orientation, particularly in School Purpose & Goal and in Curriculum content (liberal-arts emphasis, Latin, logic, philosophy, and classic literature) and in Parents & Community (parents as co-educators). The school also demonstrates elements of a classical pedagogy (Trivium-focused framing and technology-free classrooms) and maintains a community ethos with a headmaster-led faculty and student-life expectations. However, certain canonical elements of a fully classical school—explicit Great Books and Great Conversation language, explicit theological grounding, liturgical/community practices, and a clearly stated “living curriculum” faculty narrative—are less visible in the public-facing materials. On balance, FCA Carrollton sits in the CLASSICAL family in several respects but remains more accurately described as SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL across the six-category rubric, with clear opportunities to deepen theological integration, explicit Great Books pedagogy, and faculty-lifecycle narratives to reach full CLASSICAL status.
