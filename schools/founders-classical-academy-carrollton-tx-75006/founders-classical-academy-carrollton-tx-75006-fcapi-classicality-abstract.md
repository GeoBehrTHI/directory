## ABSTRACT

Founders Classical Academy Carrollton (TX 75006) demonstrates a classical-education orientation with strong virtue/formation rhetoric and a liberal-arts curriculum, plus clear parent/community partnerships. Across the six classical categories, the school most strongly evidences CLASSICAL signals in School Purpose & Goal (virtue/wisdom formation) and Parents & Community, and Faculty. Curriculum and Pedagogy show solid classical alignment but lack explicit public materials for some canonical elements (e.g., explicit Great Books/Great Conversation and Trivium/Quadrivium articulation, and explicit Socratic/Scholé pedagogy). Religious & Metaphysical Foundations show robust transcendental aims (truth, beauty, goodness, virtue) but do not publicly articulate theology or liturgical practices in the accessible copy. On balance, the aggregate classification is SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL.
