## ABSTRACT

- Overall classification: NOT CLASSICAL
- Rationale: The Bishop Machebeuf site content, particularly the public-facing pages, emphasizes closure status and administrative/transcripts functions rather than a classical education framework. There is no explicit or implicit signaling of Great Books, Trivium-based curriculum, scholé pedagogy, or a teacher-mentored “living curriculum,” which are core indicators in the Six Essential Elements rubric. The school’s public materials thus fail to meet the key positive indicators across the six categories outlined in the classical rubric and align with a NOT CLASSICAL designation. The classical-education knowledge-base lexicon provides the benchmark for these indicators (truth/goodness/beauty; theology; liturgy; Great Books; scholé; Socratic pedagogy; formation through imitation). The presence of a Catholic governance context is noted but does not, in itself, establish classical-education criteria; the site content does not articulate the canonical classical markers necessary for a CLASSICAL rating. The closure status further limits the opportunity for ongoing classical formation within public materials.
