ABSTRACT

Idaho Novus Classical Academy demonstrates a strong CLASSICAL orientation across six core categories: Religious and Metaphysical Foundations (SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL), School Purpose and Goal (CLASSICAL), Curriculum (CLASSICAL), Pedagogy (CLASSICAL), Faculty (CLASSICAL), and Parents & Community (CLASSICAL). Evidence from the school’s public materials shows a classical curriculum anchored in Great Books and liberal arts (with Socratic pedagogy, memory work, and public-speaking emphasis), a formation-focused mission (virtue, civic responsibility), and a robust family-community partnership (PTCA, information sessions). Category 1 shows the most nuanced alignment, featuring transcendental values and virtuous formation, but not explicit theological or liturgical practices in public content. The aggregate assessment remains CLASSICAL, with the strongest signals in Curriculum, Pedagogy, Faculty, and Community, and solid signals in Mission/Goal and Curriculum. The school’s alignment with the classical-education rubric is clear, positioned to deliver a rigorous, virtue-centered liberal-arts education for a K-12 trajectory via Hillsdale’s curriculum framework and a community-focused model.
