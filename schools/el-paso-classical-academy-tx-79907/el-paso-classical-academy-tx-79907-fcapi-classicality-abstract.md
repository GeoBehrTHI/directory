# ABSTRACT

El Paso Classical Academy presents a robust Classical-education orientation in its public materials, with clear commitments to a liberal-arts curriculum, virtue formation, and parent partnership. The site emphasizes truth, goodness, beauty, and virtue as core ends and shows explicit curricular anchors (Great Books, Greek/Latin, Western tradition) aligned with classical aims. However, certain classical-practice dimensions are not consistently documented in public content—particularly explicit liturgical/theological grounding, rest-based Scholé pedagogy, and the “living curriculum” faculty model. As a result, the school earns a SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL overall rating: strong alignment in Curriculum and some in Pedagogy, Purpose/Goal, and Community, but weaker in Religious Metaphysical Foundations and Faculty, with a mixed signal in Pedagogy. The aggregate picture shows a school deeply committed to classical aims, yet with room to more fully realize the full spectrum of classical practices in public documentation.
