## Executive Summary

Ridgeview Classical Schools Co 80525 demonstrates a robust alignment with classical education principles, anchored in a liberal-arts orientation, Socratic pedagogy in the upper grades, strong faculty qualifications, and active engagement with parents and community. The public materials show a coherent emphasis on formation, virtue, wisdom, and the lifelong pursuit of truth, goodness, and beauty. The school communicates a clear mission of forming character and intellect rather than solely pursuing college readiness or workforce preparation. Overall, the school earns a CLASSICAL classification, with particularly strong signals in Curriculum (liberal arts orientation) and Pedagogy (Socratic practice), and solid signals in Faculty and Parents & Community. Element 1 shows partial alignment due to some missing explicit liturgical/theological commitments, but other foundational signs keep the overall profile firmly Classical.

Key strengths
- Clear formation focus and pursuit of virtue, wisdom, and beauty as guiding aims (evidence of formation and virtue emphasis; long-term life outcomes emphasized).  
- Liberal-arts curriculum framing and classical mission stated in public materials (charter materials; reference to liberal arts and classical aims).  
- Pedagogical signals aligned with classical methods, notably Socratic/seminar-style work in upper grades. 
- Active parental and community engagement is evident (parent reading groups, broad community reading, etc.). 
- Faculty qualifications and living-curriculum aspects are described in ways that reinforce a mentor/theory-laden classical model.
