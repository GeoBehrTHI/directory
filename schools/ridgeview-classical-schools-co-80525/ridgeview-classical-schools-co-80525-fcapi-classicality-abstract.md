## ABSTRACT

Ridgeview Classical Schools Co 80525 demonstrates a CLASSICAL orientation with strong signals in Curriculum, Pedagogy, Faculty, and Parents & Community. The school’s public materials emphasize formation, virtue, wisdom, and a liberal-arts curriculum, along with the use of Socratic pedagogy in upper grades and a living-curriculum faculty ethos. Parental partnership and a culture of lifelong reading are notable strengths that reinforce classical aims. There is a partial alignment in Religious and Metaphysical Foundations, primarily through references to the Good, the True, and the Beautiful, and formation-oriented aims; explicit liturgical/theological practices are less visible in the site excerpts, placing Category 1 in SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL. Category 3 (Curriculum) also sits at SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL due to a strong liberal-arts framing and evidence of a reading culture, but with less explicit Great Books/Great Conversation listings in public materials. Overall, Ridgeview’s public materials present a robust classical education profile, with room to further articulate explicit theological grounding and canonical Great Books engagement to achieve a fully CLASSICAL designation across all six categories. 

Key sources underpinning this evaluation include public materials describing the Ridgeview Experience (virtue, character, wonder) and formation-focused aims, the charter language referencing liberal arts and the Good/True/Beautiful, explicit evidence of Socratic pedagogy, faculty qualifications, and strong parent/community engagement.
