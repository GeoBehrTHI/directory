## Executive Summary

Chesterton Academy of Our Lady of Guadalupe (Clinton Township, MI 48035) presents a robust Classical Catholic program that aligns strongly with the Six Essential Elements of Classical Education. Public materials consistently emphasize the indivisible integration of faith, virtue, and reason; a curriculum built on Great Books; liberal-arts foundational skills; Socratic pedagogy; a formation-focused faculty model; and an explicit partnership with parents and the wider parish/Community. Across six categories, theschool demonstrates pervasive Classical characteristics (Religious & Metaphysical Foundations, Purpose & Goal, Curriculum, Pedagogy, Faculty, Parents & Community), with especially strong signals around metaphysical aims, formation over information, and a three-pillar formation model. The aggregate assessment is CLASSICAL.

Key strengths include:
- A Catholic, Trinity-formed orientation (Culture of Life; Cultura Vitae), with explicit aims toward truth, goodness, and beauty and a goal of sainthood for students.
- A clearly stated formation-centered purpose (virtue, wisdom, human flourishing) articulated through the Three Pillars (Intellect, Character, Spirituality).
- A curriculum built on Great Books, liberal arts, Latin, history as Western civilization, and explicitly Quintessentially classical strands (grammar, logic, rhetoric; Socratic method; minimal technology; contemplation/wonder).
- Pedagogy that foregrounds Socratic inquiry, memory work, and rhetoric in upper grades; a concrete discipline in which students engage in dialogue, memorization, and public speaking.
- A living-faculty model aligned with classical norms (virtuous, knowledgeable mentors; living curriculum; Catholic fidelity).
