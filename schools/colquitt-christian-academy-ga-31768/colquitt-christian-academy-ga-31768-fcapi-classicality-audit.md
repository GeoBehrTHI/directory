## Executive Summary

Colquitt Christian Academy (CCA) in Moultrie, GA 31768 presents a robust Classical Christian program across the six classical domains. Public-facing materials consistently frame education as formation of virtue and wisdom integrated with Scripture, theology, and liturgical/community life. The school clearly foregrounds the Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric) and the Great Books framework (e.g., great books in upper grades) and deploys a House-based pedagogical and social structure to cultivate character, mentorship, and community. Public materials describe worship, prayer, and a biblical worldview as integral to learning, and frequently highlight the role of teachers as mentors and living curriculum, with parents positioned as co-educators and engaged community partners. Together, these signals align strongly with classical criteria, including transcendental truths (truth, goodness, beauty), formation over information, integration of theology, the liberal arts core, Socratic style inquiry in upper grades, and a family-centered community model that includes active parental involvement. Evidence drawn from CCA’s site and materials shows explicit mentions of Trivium, Great Books, memory work, singing/chanting in early grades, Socratic discussion in upper grades, and a theology-centered, worship-inclusive approach to schooling.   

- Executive-level classification: CLASSICAL.
- Confidence: high, given multiple explicit alignments across all six categories.
