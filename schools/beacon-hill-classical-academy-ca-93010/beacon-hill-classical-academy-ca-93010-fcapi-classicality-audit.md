## Executive Summary

Beacon Hill Classical Academy (BHCA) demonstrates a robust classical Christian program across all six rubric categories. Public materials consistently foreground a Christian worldview, the Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric), engagement with Great Books and primary sources, Socratic dialogue and rhetoric, and strong parental involvement. The site also highlights liturgical and communal elements (e.g., Psalms, Latin motto, Statement of Faith) and a mission to form virtuous, wise Christians rather than merely prepare students for work. These elements align closely with the Positive keyword indicators in the classical rubric and show few, if any, Negative keyword indicators. Overall, BHCA earns CLASSICAL across all six categories.

Key evidentiary anchors include: the motto “Truth. Beauty. Goodness.” on the homepage; the four pillars including Christian Education and Parental Involvement; explicit Trivium pedagogy (Grammar/Logic/Rhetoric) and memorization; Integrated Humanities with primary sources and Great Books emphasizing dialogue and the Logos; and explicit statements about forming students through Christian discipleship and a coeducative parental partnership.
