## ABSTRACT

Mary Seat Wisdom Academy Kansas (67213) is a Catholic classical school that presents a coherent classical-education model across the six classical categories. Religious and Metaphysical Foundations are foregrounded through explicit Catholic identity, theology, virtue formation, and a mission to know, love, and glorify God through Christ and His Church.
