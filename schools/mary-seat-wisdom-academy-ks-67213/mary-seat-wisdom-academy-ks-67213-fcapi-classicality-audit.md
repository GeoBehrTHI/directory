## Executive Summary

Mary Seat Wisdom Academy Kansas (mswwichita.org content identified in the crawl store) presents as a strongly classical, explicitly Catholic, liberal-arts oriented program. Public materials emphasize formation of virtue and wisdom through a Catholic framework, a curriculum centered on the liberal arts (grammar, logic, rhetoric) and the Great Books, and pedagogy that includes Socratic seminars, chanting/recitation, and contemplative routines (Scholé). The school also communicates a clear partnership with families and an open, hospitable community posture (open houses, parent involvement, and family testimonials). Across six classical elements, the materials align with the classical rubric (transcending truth, goodness, beauty; formation over information; liberal arts curriculum; Socratic pedagogy; living faculty; and active parent–community partnerships). Overall classification: CLASSICAL.

- The Religious and Metaphysical Foundations are clearly stated through Catholic identity, theology, and virtue formation. The mission and vision consistently frame education as formation in truth, goodness, and beauty within a Catholic-theological frame. Evidence includes explicit statements about Catholic classical education and formation toward God through Christ and His Church.  
- The School Purpose and Goal centers on virtue and wisdom rather than job preparation, with language of formation of the whole person and the pursuit of truth, goodness, and beauty. 
- The Curriculum foregrounds the liberal arts (grammar, logic, rhetoric) and Great Books, with references to the Great Conversation and primary texts. 
- Pedagogy features Socratic seminars, restorative/Scholé-inspired approaches, and memorization/recitation; lower and upper-school practices are described publicly (e.g., Great Books, Socratic seminars, progymnasmata).
