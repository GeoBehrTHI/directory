## ABSTRACT

Charleston Classical School (North Charleston, SC, 29406) demonstrates a robust classical Christian orientation with strong Religious and Metaphysical Foundations and a clear formation-focused purpose, supported by a community-centered faculty and parent partnership. Across Curricular and Pedagogical dimensions, CCS shows substantial classical alignment, particularly through liturgical practices (Morning Lauds), theology-informed curriculum, and a Logic-focused faculty presence (Logic School). While categories 1, 2, 5, and 6 clearly meet classical criteria, Curriculum and Pedagogy (Categories 3 and 4) show partial alignment, yielding a SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL designation in those areas while maintaining CLASSICAL overall. The aggregate ranking is CLASSICAL.

Key strengths:
- Christian formation integrated with classical aims (truth, goodness, beauty; liturgical rhythm). 
- Formation as central to the educational mission (virtue, moral formation). 
- Explicit staffing and governance (Logic School; staff/board presence). 
- Active parent and community engagement (volunteer program; admissions/family involvement). 

Key areas for needle-moving improvements:
- Explicit Great Books/Great Conversation articulation in curriculum materials to satisfy a full liberal-arts curriculum criterion. 
- Documented, ongoing Socratic/seminar pedagogy across more grade levels to push Pedagogy toward CLASSICAL. 
- Broader explicit articulation of the “living curriculum” concept in faculty materials and professional development to strengthen Element 5. 

Overall, Charleston Classical School (North Charleston, SC, 29406) is CLASSICAL in orientation, with tangible classical elements reflected in its religious foundations, formation-focused aims, and community structure, and with targeted opportunities to elevate two core dimensions (Curriculum and Pedagogy) to full classical status through transparent, public-facing documentation of canonical components and teaching methods.
