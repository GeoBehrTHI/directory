## ABSTRACT

Faith Community Academy Tucson (FCA) demonstrates a CLASSICAL orientation in Religious & Metaphysical Foundations, School Purpose & Goal, and Parents & Community, driven by a robust Christ-centered mission and a formation-first approach that seeks to integrate faith and character with academics. The school presents a strong liturgical and communal life (chapel/worship) and explicit biblical/theological framing, including a vision to educate students “for the glory of God” and to equip them to pursue truth within a biblical worldview. The school’s public materials emphasize formation and virtue, which aligns with classical aims in Category 2.

However, the Curriculum and Pedagogy signals show Moderate to weak canonical-Classical alignment. Public materials describe Abeka-based curriculum with DeepRoots Bible and include Latin/Spanish offerings, but there is no explicit Great Books/Great Conversation program or a canonical Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric) framework. Pedagogy emphasizes liturgical and traditional instruction rather than a sustained Socratic/Paideia-based approach, which reduces alignment in Category 3 and Category 4. The Faculty category shows strong relational and mentorship-oriented language but lacks explicit canonical classical-faculty vocabulary, yielding a SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL signal in Category 5. Category 6 shows strong parental-partnership language and a community-centric model, which strongly supports CLASSICAL.

Aggregate classification across the six categories: CLASSICAL.

Key sources informing the assessment include FCA’s public mission statements (Christ-centered, biblical worldview, formation through virtue and wisdom), curriculum notes (Abeka and DeepRoots Bible Curriculum), pedagogical signals (worship, chapel, and structured learning), and explicit parent/community partnerships. These items are drawn from FCA’s website text and related public-facing materials (including Home/Mission statements, Values, Leadership, and Curriculum pages) and are cross-referenced with the classical education framework and keyword lexicon (Positive/Negative Keyword Indicators) from the Six Essential Elements of a Classical School (1) to ground the analysis.
