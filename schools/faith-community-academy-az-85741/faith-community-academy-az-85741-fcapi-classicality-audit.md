## Executive Summary

Faith Community Academy Tucson (FCA) presents a clearly Christian, Christ-centered approach to education with a strong emphasis on faith integration, moral formation, and community partnership. Public materials from FCA consistently emphasize a religious frame (Christ-centered, biblical worldview, worship) and a partnership with families, in addition to traditional, structured curriculum choices (Abeka and DeepRoots Bible Curriculum) and a stable leadership structure anchored in faith communities. This combination yields a classical orientation in terms of religious/metaphysical foundations and a virtue-formation orientation in the school’s stated goals, while the explicit Great-Books/grammar-logic-rhetoric trinity and the classic Paideia/Socratic pedagogy are not evidenced as centrally implemented across FCA’s K–5 program.

Overall, FCA tilts toward CLASSICAL in several dimensions (Religious Foundations; School Purpose/Goal; and Parents & Community) but does not consistently demonstrate core classical curriculum and pedagogy elements (notably Great Books orientation, explicit grammar-logic-rhetoric curriculum, and Socratic/seminar pedagogy) across its published materials. The aggregate picture is best described as CLASSICAL, with notable CLASSICAL-leaning elements in Element 1, Element 2, and Element 6, and with SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL to NOT CLASSICAL signals in Categories 3 and 4. Element 5 shows some classical alignment in teacher roles and mentoring, but the site does not consistently claim the living-curriculum or fully classical faculty model. See category-by-category notes for details and the supporting website evidence.
