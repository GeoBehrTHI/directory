## ABSTRACT

This evaluation analyzes Trinity Christian Academy (FL 32725) using two vector stores: a knowledge-base with classical-school criteria (Six Essential Elements of a Classical School) and a school-specific crawl containing the public-facing content from Rafiki Classical Academy (RCA)-related materials that appear connected to the Trinity Christian Academy domain. The keyword-analytic process leverages the Six Essential Elements as the rubric, examining six categories: Religious and Metaphysical Foundations; School Purpose and Goal; Curriculum; Pedagogy; Faculty; and Parents & Community.

Overall classification: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL

- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: CLASSICAL. Evidence shows Bible-centered routines, a biblical worldview, and a formal theology-based foundation via a Statement of Faith. Positive signals include truth/goodness/beauty within a Christian metaphysical frame.  
- School Purpose and Goal: CLASSICAL. Formation and virtue-focused aims with truth/goodness/beauty as central ideals; a clear non-vocational telos aligned with classical paideia. 
- Curriculum: CLASSICAL. Explicit Trivium-related language and Great Books/Great Conversation framing per RCA materials support classical curriculum structure. 
- Pedagogy: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL. The public-facing materials emphasize canonical classical elements but do not consistently document the full range of classical pedagogical practices (e.g., Socratic seminars, scholé/historically restful pedagogy) in visible pages. More explicit public detail would elevate this to CLASSICAL. 
- Faculty: CLASSICAL. Faculty portrayal emphasizes virtue, mentorship, and a living-curriculum posture, aligning with classical expectations. 
- Parents & Community: CLASSICAL. Evidence of partnership with families and parent-resource access reflects the classical approach to parent involvement and formation. 

General takeaway: Trinity Christian Academy’s public-facing materials demonstrate a robust classical Christian orientation across most categories, with Pedagogy—the area most sensitive to explicit practice—showing room for stronger, more explicit articulation in public messaging. The aggregate signal remains CLASSICAL in spirit, with Element 4 driving the SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL designation.
