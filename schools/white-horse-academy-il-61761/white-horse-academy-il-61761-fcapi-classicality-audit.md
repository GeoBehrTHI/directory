## Executive Summary

White Horse Academy Illinois (61761) presents a coherent and explicit Classical Christian program. Public-facing materials repeatedly frame education as formation in truth, goodness, and beauty through a Classical Christian lens, with clear anchors in the liberal arts, Great Books, and the Trivium/Quadrivium. The site consistently emphasizes partnering with families, liturgical/worship-like elements in the learning environment, and a pedagogy that blends contemplative rest (Scholé) with Socratic inquiry and rhetoric at higher levels. Together, these signals map closely to the classical rubric: a robust Religious and Metaphysical Foundations orientation, a purpose focused on formation and human excellence, a curriculum centered on Great Books and liberal arts, a pedagogy that blends memorization, chant, lecture with Socratic dialog, a faculty viewed as living exemplars and mentors, and an active, shared commitment with parents and the broader faith community.

Key public cues include: Paideia of God as the core educational anthropology; explicit references to Great Books, Great Ideas, Great Men; emphasis on Trivium/Quadrivium; liturgical elements in instruction; a half-day schedule to protect family time; explicit Christian formation and leadership aims; and a clear partnership with parents as co-educators. The school’s narrative consistently links classical education with Christian formation and leadership for culture, aligning with core Classical Christian ideals in the knowledge-base rubric.
