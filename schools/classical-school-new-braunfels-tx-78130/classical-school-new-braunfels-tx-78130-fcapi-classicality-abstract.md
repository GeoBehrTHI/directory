## ABSTRACT

The Classical School NB presents a robust classical education anchored in a biblical worldview, with formation as the central aim. The school emphasizes a formation-centric purpose (virtue, wisdom, beauty) and a curriculum built around grammar, logic, rhetoric, Great Books, Latin, and history-integrated content. Pedagogy includes memory work, chanting, and contemplative elements in lower grades, with open dialogue and critical thinking emphasized in upper grades. Faculty are framed as mentors and living exemplars, and parents are viewed as co-teachers with the school. Overall, the school aligns with CLASSICAL principles across five of six categories; Pedagogy sits near the border as SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL due to explicit, ongoing articulation of Socratic seminars across grades being less uniformly stated. The aggregate assessment is CLASSICAL.
