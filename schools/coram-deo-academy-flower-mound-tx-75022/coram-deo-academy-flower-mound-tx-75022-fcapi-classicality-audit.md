## Executive Summary

Coram Deo Academy Flower Mound (CDA FM) presents a robust classical Christian program deeply oriented around formation of character, virtue, wisdom, and a biblically framed worldview. Public materials across the CDA FM site repeatedly describe a Christ-centered, classical, and collaborative model in which theology is woven into every subject, chapels and worship spaces are integrated into daily life, and parents are treated as co-educators within a unified learning ecosystem. The school explicitly centers on truth, goodness, and beauty as real and knowable, with explicit emphasis on great books, the trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric), and the liberal arts as the architectural spine of its curriculum. Pedagogical practices include lower-school singing/chanting that fortifies memory, upper-school Socratic dialogue and rhetoric, and a deliberate, staged progression from Grammar to Logic to Rhetoric. The faculty is portrayed as mentors and a living curriculum, and the school actively fosters parental partnership and community involvement. Taken together, CDA FM aligns very strongly with classical criteria across all six categories.

- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: Strong Christ-centered, biblical, and liturgical orientation; chapel and biblical worldview embedded in daily life. Evidence includes explicit statements about Christ-centered education, biblical truth woven into academics, chapel, and coram Deo (presence of God).   
- Purpose and Goal: Formation of virtue, wisdom, and character with a commitment to truth/beauty, not merely job preparation. Evidence includes statements about training ethical servant leaders, pursuit of truth/beauty, and formation-oriented aims.  
- Curriculum: Distinct Grammar/Logic/Rhetoric structure, integration of Great Books-style study, history across the curriculum, and explicit Latin study. Evidence includes Grammar/Logic/Rhetoric progression, Latin in the curriculum, Bible in curriculum, and emphasis on great works/history.   
- Pedagogy: Blended on-campus and at-home collaborative model; lower-school singing/chanting and memory-building; upper-school Socratic/dialogue-based seminars; rhetoric training and public speaking opportunities. Evidence includes lower-school chant/memory, Socratic dialogue in Logic, rhetoric/seminars in Rhetoric, and collaborative model with parent involvement.   
- Faculty: Faculty described as living curriculum and mentors who know each student; teachers praying for students; collaborative faculty-student relationships. Evidence includes “the living curriculum,” teachers who know every student, and prayerful mentorship language. 
- Parents & Community: Explicit partnership with families; parents as co-educators; at-home components and resources; campus visits and family involvement. Evidence includes statements about parents as primary educators, collaborative model, at-home learning materials, and a large supportive community.  

Abstract assessment across the six classical categories yields a consistent CLASSICAL rating for Coram Deo Academy Flower Mound. The school provides a coherent classical framework that foregrounds virtue, wisdom, transcendental truth, and a liturgical sense of community, while maintaining a clearly defined liberal arts curriculum and a pedagogy rooted in memory (chant/singing), Socratic inquiry, and rhetorical training. The public materials repeatedly map onto the six-element rubric, with explicit alignment to the Classical Christian tradition and the Great Conversation.
