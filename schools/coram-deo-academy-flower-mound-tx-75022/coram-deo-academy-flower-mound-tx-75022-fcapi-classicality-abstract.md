## ABSTRACT

Coram Deo Academy Flower Mound (CDA FM) demonstrates a coherent and robust CLASSICAL profile across all six categories of the classical schooling rubric.

- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: CDA FM emphasizes a Christ-centered, biblical worldview integrated into all aspects of learning, with chapels, worship, and coram Deo (presence of God) shaping the educational experience. Evidence includes explicit references to biblical truth woven into academics and chapel-based spiritual formation. 
- School Purpose and Goal: The school targets formation—virtue, wisdom, and character—in addition to intellectual growth, with language around “ethical servant leaders” and shaping culture for God’s glory. This aligns with transcendental aims (truth, goodness, beauty) recognized in classical education. 
- Curriculum: A clear grammar/logic/rhetoric progression is present, along with Latin, biblical studies, and a Great Books/Great Conversation orientation, and a history-spanning, integrated curriculum.  
- Pedagogy: Pedagogy blends contemplative/restful elements with active Socratic dialogue, debates, and public speaking, plus a collaborative model that engages parents in at-home reinforcement of concepts.  
- Faculty: The faculty is presented as the living curriculum and as mentors who know each student, praying for students and modeling virtue and skill. 
- Parents & Community: The school positions parents as co-educators and partners, with at-home learning guidance and frequent opportunities for parental involvement and visits. 

Overall, Coram Deo Academy Flower Mound satisfies the Six Essential Elements of Classical Education with a strong Christian lens and a transparent, formation-centered model. The public materials consistently reflect the transcendental aims (truth, goodness, beauty), the trivium-based curriculum, the Great Books sensibility, the liturgical and communal practices, and the parent-partnered community.
