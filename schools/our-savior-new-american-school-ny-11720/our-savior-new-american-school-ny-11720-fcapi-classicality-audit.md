## Executive Summary

Our Savior New American School NY 11720 (OSNAS) presents a strongly theist, Christ-centered classical education with clear commitments to formation of character, virtue, wisdom, and metaphysical truths. Public materials repeatedly frame education as formation of the whole person (mind, heart, body, spirit) in a theologically rooted framework, with explicit emphasis on a biblical worldview, liturgical life, and communal worship. Pedagogy leans into both contemplative elements and Socratic, rhetorical, and memorization-based practices, including singing, chanting, and memory work in the lower grades, and Socratic dialogue and rhetoric in the upper grades. The curriculum shows classical threads—Latin study, grammar/logic/rhetoric through language study and classical literature sensibilities—while also integrating Bible-centered courses and theology as a unifying lens. Parental partnership and church-family-school alignment are foregrounded.

Overall classification: CLASSICAL

- The school demonstrates robust Religious and Metaphysical Foundations (catechetical/theological framing, liturgical practices, Bible-centered formation) and a mission explicitly oriented to true, good, and beautiful through formation. Evidence includes explicit statements of knowing God and making Him known, daily prayer, Biblical authority, and Christ-centered identity (Our DNA, Bible content, Purpose of Education) .  
- The School Purpose and Goal likewise emphasize formation, virtue, wisdom, and transcendent realities, rather than solely vocational outcomes (The Purpose of Education, The True Purpose of Education) .  
- Curriculum and Pedagogy reflect a classical orientation with Trivium-like features (grammar/logic/rhetoric through language study and public speaking; Latin instruction; engagement with historical literacy and dialectic discussions) alongside strong integration of theology and liturgical rhythm (Latin/Foreign Language, History, English & Literature, Bible content, Classical pedagogy references in “What is Classical Education?”) , , , .  
- Faculty, Parents & Community are framed in terms of mentor-ship, living curriculum, parental partnership, and a church-family-school unity, aligning with classical expectations for mentorship and formation (Our DNA; The Beliefs; Our Mission) , .  
- The aggregate pattern is consistently Classical, with some nuanced areas that could be more explicit (e.g., explicit Great Books/“Great Conversation” terminology is less visible in public OSNAS pages, though dialectic, memorization, and classical pedagogy are present). This yields a robust CLASSICAL rating overall.
