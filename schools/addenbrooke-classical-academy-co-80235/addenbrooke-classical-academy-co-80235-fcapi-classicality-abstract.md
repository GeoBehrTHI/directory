## ABSTRACT

Overall classification: CLASSICAL

- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: Strong CLASSICAL alignment through explicit Christian framing and emphasis on truth, virtue, and beauty as transcendent ideals; the mission centers on formation and the glory of God. Evidence shows explicit Christian framing and virtue-centered formation. 

- School Purpose and Goal: CLASSICAL. The program foregrounds formation of virtue and wisdom and the reality of truth, goodness, and beauty as real. The school emphasizes formation over mere information and character formation consistent with classical aims. 

- Curriculum: CLASSICAL. Clear Trivium structure (Grammar, Logic, Rhetoric) across K–12, with explicit naming of the Grammar/Logic/Rhetoric tracks. 

- Pedagogy: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL. Pedagogy is rooted in Trivium structure and rhetoric training, with explicit Rhetoric School offerings; however, explicit Socratic/seminar formats, Great Books engagement, and scholé/restful pedagogy are not consistently spelled out in public site content. 

- Faculty: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL. There is a visible administration and teaching staff, and a culture of community; however, explicit language about the faculty as the living curriculum or a formalized virtue-based profile is not clearly present in the public site materials. 

- Parents & Community: CLASSICAL. The school strongly emphasizes partnership with families, parents as co-teachers, family involvement, and open access/visits to the school. 

Aggregate assessment
- Primary alignment: CLASSICAL
- Some Category-level caveats (Pedagogy and Faculty) suggest SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL as potential growth areas, particularly around explicit Socratic engagement, Great Books integration, and a formalized “living curriculum” language for faculty. With targeted enhancements in those areas, the school could reach a full CLASSICAL alignment across all six categories.
