## ABSTRACT

Redeemer Classical Christian School Maryland demonstrates a robust CLASSICAL orientation across all six categories of the classical education rubric.

- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: CLASSICAL. The school integrates a Christian worldview and theology as a core governing lens for all disciplines (truth, goodness, beauty; God and Christ-centered learning; biblical standards). Evidence includes explicit statements about God’s existence, the imago Dei, and the lordship of Jesus Christ over all learning.  

- School Purpose and Goal: CLASSICAL. The mission emphasizes formation of wisdom and virtue, transformation of the whole person, and education rooted in truth, goodness, and beauty, rather than mere preparation for work. 

- Curriculum: CLASSICAL. The curriculum centers on the liberal arts (grammar, logic, rhetoric), Great Books, Latin studies, and a thesis-based culmination, all within a theocentric framework.  

- Pedagogy: CLASSICAL. Pedagogy blends memory-based early instruction (singing, recitation, memory work) with Socratic discussion, seminars, and rhetorical training in upper grades.  

- Faculty: CLASSICAL. Faculty are described as living curriculum and mentors, with small classes and an emphasis on character formation and exemplary teaching. 

- Parents & Community: CLASSICAL. The school foregrounds partnership with families, parent formation, and open campus visits as core programmatic elements. 

In sum, Redeemer RCCS MD 21087 aligns strongly with classical education criteria across all six categories, supported by textual evidence that highlights Trivium-based pedagogy, Great Books canon, holistic formation in a Christian framework, a living-faculty model, and deep parental involvement. The public materials show a cohesive Classical orientation in both theory and practice, with explicit commitments to the transcendental aims of truth, goodness, and beauty, formation over information, and a community rooted in faith and virtue.
