## ABSTRACT

The Lafayette Academy materials reviewed indicate a robust classical-education orientation, with mission statements emphasizing formation of virtue, truth, beauty, and goodness; a curriculum built on the liberal arts (grammar, logic, rhetoric) and Great Books; pedagogy centered around Socratic discourse, dialectic, and rhetoric; a faculty culture framed as mentors and living curriculum; and a parents-and-community model that actively engages families through open houses, tours, and events. Collectively, these characteristics align with CLASSICAL across Curriculum, Pedagogy, Faculty, and Parents & Community categories; PURPOSE/GOAL evidence formation and virtue formation; while RELIGIOUS/METAPHYSICAL FOUNDATIONS shows strong transcendental and formation language but does not consistently proclaim theology-centered integration or liturgical practices across all disciplines. The aggregate assessment is CLASSICAL, with a minor potential area for enhancement in the explicit articulation of theology-led integration and liturgical-community practices to strengthen Category 1 to a firm CLASSICAL.
