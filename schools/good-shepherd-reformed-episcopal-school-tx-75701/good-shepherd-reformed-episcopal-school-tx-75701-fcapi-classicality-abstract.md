## ABSTRACT

The school’s materials emphasize truth, goodness, and beauty as real, knowable ends; a disciplined, liberal-arts curriculum with Great-Books and Latin; worship-centered daily life; and a pedagogy that blends memory work, singing, contemplation, and Socratic discussion/debate. The public materials present a Strong CLASSICAL orientation in Categories 1 (Religious Foundations), 2 (Purpose/Goal), 3 (Curriculum), and 4 (Pedagogy), with Category 6 (Parents & Community) also aligning strongly with classical formation through parent formation and community engagement. Category 5 (Faculty) shows a generally classical orientation but is not as explicitly articulated in terms of a formal “living curriculum” or faculty portrait in public materials, making it the only category that is not fully Classically aligned. Overall, the school is CLASSICAL with a standout emphasis on liturgical worship, the Trivium, Great Conversation, and Anglican-Christian formation; the Faculty category would benefit from more explicit classical-faculty articulation to achieve full CLASSICAL status across all categories.
