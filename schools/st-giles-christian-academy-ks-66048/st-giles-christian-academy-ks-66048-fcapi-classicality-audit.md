## Executive Summary

St. Giles Christian Academy (Leavenworth, KS 66048) presents a clear and sustained Classical Christian orientation across its public materials. The school foregrounds the Trivium (Grammar, Logic, Rhetoric), the liberal-arts framework, Latin, and a Great-Books approach (Omnibus programs) within a Protestant, Reformed, biblically grounded worldview. The public materials consistently articulate aims of character formation, formation of virtue and wisdom, and the cultivation of a lifelong love of learning anchored in Scripture and Christian doctrine. Several elements routinely cited in classical education rubrics appear in abundance: explicit references to Truth, Goodness, and Beauty; theology as foundational to all learning; a commitment to Great Books and the Great Conversation; a chapel/creedal (Apostles’ Creed, Nicene Creed, Chalcedon) tradition; and a pedagogy that blends memorization/recitation with Socratic potential through seminars and disciplined discussion (as evidenced in course descriptions and faculty materials). Overall, the school qualifies as CLASSICAL, with evidence of robust classical commitments across six key domains.
