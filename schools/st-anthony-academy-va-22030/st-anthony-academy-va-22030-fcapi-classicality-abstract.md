# ABSTRACT

St Anthony Academy, Fairfax, VA (ZIP 22030), presents a coherently classical-Catholic program as evidenced by explicit Trivium-based curriculum (grammar, dialectic/logic, rhetoric), widespread use of Great Books (Homer, Virgil, Dante, Shakespeare), and a strong emphasis on Latin, memorization, and communal liturgical/spiritual formation (daily prayers, Rosary, Traditional Latin Mass). Public materials also describe the school’s mission to form virtue and wisdom within a Catholic framework and to partner with families in the educational process. The school’s public statements indicate alignment with the Six Essential Elements of Classical Education in all six evaluation categories: Religious & Metaphysical Foundations; School Purpose & Goal; Curriculum; Pedagogy; Faculty; Parents & Community. The Positive Keyword Indicators (e.g., Catholic tradition, liturgy, theology, Trivium, Great Books, Latin, memory work, scholé, Socratic dialogue, rhetoric, faculty as living curriculum, parent partnership) dominate the public-facing materials, while Negative Keyword Indicators are not evidenced as driving characteristics in the sources reviewed. Overall, St Anthony Academy qualifies as CLASSICAL across the rubric, with strong evidence of formation-focused education, canonical classical content, and a robust Catholic cultural framework that shapes both curriculum and community engagement.
