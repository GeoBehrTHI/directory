# ABSTRACT

The Geneva School in Casselberry, FL (ZIP 32707) presents a robust Christian classical education model across all six rubric categories. The school explicitly frames itself as a Christian classical institution with a biblical worldview and a theologized aim for student formation, not mere information delivery. The curriculum is clearly Trivium-centered (Grammar, Dialectic, Rhetoric) with grade-band divisions indicating a coherent classical structure. Pedagogy is aligned with classical methods (dialectic and rhetoric in higher grades; formation through exemplary models and memory/repetition in foundational stages implied by the classical framework). Faculty roles and leadership denote mentorship and virtue-based education, and parents are positioned as co-educators within a prayerful, collaborative community.

Category-by-category summary:

- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: CLASSICAL. Evidence includes Christian identity, biblical worldview, historic Christian faith, and chaplain leadership.
- School Purpose and Goal: CLASSICAL. Formation and life-long cultivation of beauty and thought, with Christ-centered teleology.
- Curriculum: CLASSICAL. Explicit Trivium structure (grammar/dialectic/rhetoric) across K4–12; classical tools and formation-centric framing.
- Pedagogy: CLASSICAL. Trivium-based pedagogy with upper-level rhetoric and Socratic-style engagement implied by the dialectic/rhetoric emphasis.
- Faculty: CLASSICAL. Leadership roles (Head, Chaplain, Deans) signal mentorship and a living curriculum approach.
- Parents & Community: CLASSICAL. Explicit partnership with families and parental engagement in the educational process.

Abstract conclusion: The Geneva School clearly embodies CLASSICAL education according to the Six Essential Elements rubric, with explicit Christian formation, a Trivium-centered curriculum, classical pedagogy, mentorship-focused faculty, and strong parental partnership. The public materials yield a coherent, textbook-classical profile rather than a mixed or “somewhat classical” orientation.
