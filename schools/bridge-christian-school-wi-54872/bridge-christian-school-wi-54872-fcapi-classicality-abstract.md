## ABSTRACT

Classification: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL

The Bridge Christian School (Siren, WI, 54872) presents a clearly classical, theology-infused framework. Its public materials foreground a Classical Christian Education model and a church-based community, with explicit statements about truth, God, and faith. The curriculum is framed as classical and integrated around a divine truth, and there is evident parent involvement and church-community partnership. However, explicit articulation of several core classical pedagogy and curricular hallmarks—such as robust Great Books/Great Conversation language, detailed Socratic pedagogy, and a fully developed “living curriculum” portrayed through faculty exemplars—are not clearly demonstrated on the public site copy. This places the school in the SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL tier across most categories, with Element 1 and Element 6 reaching CLASSICAL strength due to explicit religious foundations and parent-community alignment.

Overall: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL

Bridge Christian School’s public-facing materials reflect a strong religious and classical orientation and a robust parental/community role, while leaving room for a fuller public articulation of the classical pedagogy, Great Books engagement, and faculty living-curriculum narratives that would push the school into a fully Classical classification.
