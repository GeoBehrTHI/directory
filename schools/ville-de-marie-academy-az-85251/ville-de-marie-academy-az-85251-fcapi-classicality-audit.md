## Executive Summary

Ville de Marie Academy AZ 85251 (Scottsdale, AZ) presents a robust classical, Catholic liberal-arts program that is deeply anchored in religious formation, traditional pedagogy, and family partnership. Public-facing materials emphasize a Catholic worldview, formation of character through truth, goodness, and beauty, and a Trivium-informed curriculum (grammar, logic, rhetoric) taught through Great Books and Socratic dialogue at the upper grades. Liturgy, sacraments, daily prayer, and a strong sense of community are repeatedly foregrounded, together with explicit attention to parental partnership in education. Taken together, these elements indicate a CLASSICAL orientation across all six categories (Religious and Metaphysical Foundations; Purpose and Goal; Curriculum; Pedagogy; Faculty; Parents & Community).

Key insights:
- Religious and metaphysical foundations are explicit and pervasive, with Mass, Eucharistic adoration, liturgical life, sacred imagery, and a Catholic anthropology foregrounded in mission and practice. This aligns strongly with the Positive Keywords in the classical lexicon and signals a robust theological framing of education.  
- The school’s stated purpose and goals center on formation, virtue, wisdom, and a Catholic understanding of truth, goodness, and beauty, rather than mere job preparation. This is consistent with the “telos” and paideia language in classic formulations. 
- The curriculum is explicitly Trivium-based (grammar, logic, rhetoric) with reference to Great Books and the Great Conversation, and a documented high-school emphasis on Socratic dialogue. This maps cleanly to the core curricular markers of classical education. 
- Pedagogy describes a mix of contemplative/restful and rigorous modes, with upper-level Socratic discourse and rhetoric opportunities, as well as a liturgical/ritual dimension. The public materials provide sufficient evidence of a classical pedagogy in structure and method.  
- Faculty signals emphasize virtue, leadership, and mentorship roles, and the school evidences senior advisory structures and boards composed of scholars and theologians—consistent with a classical model where teachers function as living witnesses of a virtuous life.  
- Parents & Community are positioned as co-educators and partners, with explicit statements about parental primacy in education and opportunities for parent formation and visits. This aligns with the classical model’s emphasis on a robust home–school partnership. 

Overall classification: CLASSICAL
