## Executive Summary

Covenant Academy Georgia (CA GA 31210) presents a robust Classical Christian model that integrates the Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric) with a Christ-centered worldview. Public-facing materials emphasize a Biblical framework, a philosophy of classical education, and a commitment to great books, Latin, and high-level rhetoric, all anchored in a mission to glorify God and form servant leaders. The school describes itself as the only classical Christian school in Middle Georgia, with explicit references to Christian formation, virtue, and a community partnership with families. These elements align strongly with the classical education rubric, particularly in the areas of religious/metaphysical foundations, curriculum, pedagogy, and the overall formation-focused mission. Evidence from Covenant’s materials highlights a shared emphasis on truth, goodness, beauty, and the Great Conversation as organizing ideals, while also underscoring a commitment to the formation of character and leadership within a biblical framework. Based on the public materials available, Covenant Academy Georgia scores CLASSICAL across all six categories.

- Overall classification: CLASSICAL
- Notable strengths: Christ-centered integration of theology with all learning; explicit Trivium framework; Great Books approach; Senior Capstone with formal argument and defense; strong emphasis on virtue, arete, and servant leadership; substantial family-partnership emphasis.
- Potential needle-movers (if targeted): Make liturgical/community practices explicit on the public site (to satisfy the Liturgical and Communal Practices criterion more transparently) and more clearly connect Great Conversation language to public-facing materials.

Evidence and keyword-based analysis for each category are summarized below, with explicit references to the school’s materials and the classical-education keyword framework drawn from the Six Essential Elements of a Classical School (1).
