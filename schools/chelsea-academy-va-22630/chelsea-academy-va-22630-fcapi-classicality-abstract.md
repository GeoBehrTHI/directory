## ABSTRACT

Chelsea Academy Va 22630 presents a robust CLASSICAL orientation with a clearly Catholic, liberal-arts-based mission. It emphasizes formation of virtue, wisdom, and character within a Catholic framework, supported by a liturgical life (Mass, chapel, Atrium) and policy that positions teachers as living exemplars and mentors. The school demonstrates strong parent/community engagement and a visible commitment to Western-civilization canonical study (e.g., Great Books ethos via literature lists and course design). While public materials strongly embody classical signals in several categories, Curriculum and Pedagogy show partial classical alignment—strong in formation and liturgical life, but with less explicit articulation of the full classical curriculum framework (grammar/logic/rhetoric) and Socratic pedagogy in every public-facing page.
