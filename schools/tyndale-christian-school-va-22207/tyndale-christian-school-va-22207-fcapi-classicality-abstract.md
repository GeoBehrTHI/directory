## Abstract

Tyndale Christian School Virginia (ZIP 22207) demonstrates a comprehensive CLASSICAL profile. Religious and metaphysical foundations are explicitly Christian and theologically integrated into the fabric of the school (Christ-centered formation; biblical worldview; Lordship; wisdom). The school’s stated purpose and goals emphasize formation, virtue, and wisdom aligned with transcendental truths (truth, goodness, beauty) rather than mere job preparation. The curriculum is clearly Classical (trivium/quadrivium, Great Books, Latin, living ideas) and anchored in a liberal-arts sensibility that ties together history, literature, and philosophy within a Christian framework. Pedagogy reflects classical methods through memory work, narration, recitation, short lessons, and nature study, with Charlotte Mason influences that emphasize formation and habit. Faculty are portrayed as mentors and living curriculum, with strong leadership (Head of School) and a focus on teacher formation within a Christian/classical paradigm. Parent and community engagement is explicit, with partnership as a foundational value and open-door practices for involvement.

- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: CLASSICAL — Christ-centered formation, biblical worldview, Lordship, and theological grounding visible in mission and program descriptions.
- School Purpose and Goal: CLASSICAL — Formation and virtue/wisdom emphasis; transcendent values and a telos toward human flourishing are explicit.

- Pedagogy: CLASSICAL — Narration, dictation, recitation, memory work, short lessons, and nature study; Charlotte Mason influence present; some Socratic/seminar methods implied by classical framework but not overtly enumerated.
- Faculty: CLASSICAL — Experienced leadership; commitment to gifted teachers and living curriculum; mentorship and modeling of virtue.
- Parents and Community: CLASSICAL — Strong partnership with families; parent formation and open engagement.
