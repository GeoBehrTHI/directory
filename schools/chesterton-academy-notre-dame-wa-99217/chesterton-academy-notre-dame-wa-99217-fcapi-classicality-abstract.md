- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: The school is firmly anchored in Catholic theology and liturgical life, with a culture anchored by Cultura Vitae (culture of life) and daily Mass, which map to transcendental moral realism and liturgical practices. Evidence includes explicit Catholic framing, liturgical life, and the Three Pillars tradition.

- Parents & Community: The school emphasizes a strong partnership with families, parent formation activities, and active donor-community structures (Headmaster’s Club, Notre Dame Society), signaling a robust classical-education ecosystem.
