## Executive Summary

Chesterton Academy of Notre Dame (Spokane, WA 99217) presents a clearly classical orientation visible across multiple core areas: a Catholic, liturgical culture embedded in daily life; a liberal-arts curriculum with strong emphasis on language study (Latin) and the liberal arts framework; a pedagogy that includes Socratic methods within humanities; and a faculty and community structure that models virtue and partnership with families. Public materials from the school’s site emphasize a joyfully Catholic, classical education centered on formation of character, virtue, and wisdom, not merely information or job prep. This combination places the school solidly in the CLASSICAL category across all six elements of the classical education rubric used here.

Key evidence includes: the Three Pillars model and Cultura Vitae motto, daily Mass and liturgical life, an integrated Liberal Arts Curriculum with Latin requirements, a humanities program explicitly incorporating Socratic method, and active teacher formation and family/community partnerships (including board leadership and donor societies) described on the site.
