# Classicality Audit: Bishop Louis Reicher Catholic School (Waco, TX)

School website used: https://bishopreicher.com/

## Executive Summary
Overall classification: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL

Bishop Louis Reicher Catholic School presents a robust Catholic identity with clear metaphysical grounding in virtue, theology, and liturgical life. The site evidences frequent use of positive classical keywords such as virtue, prudence, justice, fortitude, temperance, Eucharist, Mass, sacred liturgy, and theology, and it integrates a four‑year theology sequence in high school. These strongly satisfy Religious and Metaphysical Foundations. The purpose statement frequently invokes forming students in what is true, good, and beautiful and emphasizes virtue formation; however, the site also markets college‑prep priorities (PSAT, AP), and STEM/Robotics programming, which introduce mixed signals relative to classical aims. The curriculum includes “classical literature,” Latin, and Great Books authors (Homer, Sophocles, Shakespeare, Austen), but also foregrounds “Social Studies,” AP/college‑readiness, and STEM initiatives. Pedagogy shows little explicit reference to classical methods (Socratic seminar, scholé, chanting/memorization), and includes experiential/hands‑on/project language in Robotics. Faculty philosophy is not articulated in classical terms on the site. Parent partnership is explicit and hospitality is strong, though parent formation is not evident.

In sum: very strong Catholic formation + partial classical content + limited evidence of classical pedagogy and faculty formation ⇒ SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL.

References for the classical lexicon and scoring framework were taken from the Six Essential Elements of a Classical School keyword indicators and lexicon documents used in this audit   .

## What would move the needle
- Pedagogy: Publish explicit commitments to classical methods (Socratic seminar in upper school humanities; chanting/recitation/memory work in lower school; restful learning/scholé language; progymnasmata/rhetoric orations). Concrete program statements would transform Pedagogy from NOT CLASSICAL to SOMEWHAT/CLASSICAL per the lexicon  .
- Curriculum framing: Keep Latin/classical literature prominent and add explicit trivium/quadrivium language, Great Books lists by grade, and “History” (not “Social Studies”). De‑emphasize college‑prep/STEM as primary aims and frame them as means under the telos of wisdom/virtue and the pursuit of truth, goodness, and beauty  .
- Faculty formation: Add a “Portrait of a Classical Teacher,” emphasize teachers as models of virtue, and describe ongoing faculty formation in classical pedagogy (e.g., faculty colloquia on Great Books) .
- Parent formation: Offer parent book studies or seminars introducing classical education and the liberal arts tradition; articulate parents as co‑educators in the classical project .
