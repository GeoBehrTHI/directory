## ABSTRACT

Sequitur Classical Academy La 70808 demonstrates clear CLASSICAL alignment across all six rubric categories: Religious and Metaphysical Foundations, School Purpose and Goal, Curriculum, Pedagogy, Faculty, and Parents & Community. The public materials portray a Christ-centered mission that explicates transcendent values (truth, goodness, beauty) and liturgical practices (Promptus, Scripture, hymns) as foundational to teaching. The curriculum follows the Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric) with a historically rooted curriculum, Great Books orientation, classical languages, and integrated history, and is taught through both contemplative and Socratic pedagogies culminating in rhetorical mastery. Faculty are portrayed as living exemplars who mentor students, while parents are recognized as co-educators and partners in education, with ongoing opportunities for parental formation. The overall assessment is CLASSICAL, with strong signals of formation, virtue, and cultural transmission through Western classical heritage.

- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: CLASSICAL. Evidence includes Christ-focused mission, liturgical practices (Promptus, Scripture recitation, hymns), and a theologically grounded aim to form virtue and wisdom. 
- School Purpose and Goal: CLASSICAL. Evidence includes formation toward wisdom and virtue, education as formation (not merely information), and a teleological view of truth, goodness, and beauty as real and knowable. 
- Curriculum: CLASSICAL. Evidence includes grammar–logic–rhetoric progression, Great Books ambitions, Latin, history, and a Western-civilization orientation, integrated with classical literature and philosophy. 
- Pedagogy: CLASSICAL. Evidence includes lower-grade memorization/chanting and memory work, upper-grade Socratic discussion and seminars, and a shift to rhetoric training and public speaking. 
- Faculty: CLASSICAL. Evidence includes a living-curriculum model, a named head of school, and a community of teachers organized around virtue and expertise. 
- Parents & Community: CLASSICAL. Evidence includes explicit partnership with families, parental formation opportunities, and open-campus/community involvement. 

Overall Classification: CLASSICAL

Notes:
- The school’s public materials align strongly with the classical education tradition, including the Trivium, Great Books orientation, liturgical practices, and a fully articulated partnership with families. The presence of a robust Catholic/Christian frame and liturgical life does not detract from a classical orientation; rather, it embodies the integration of faith and reason characteristic of many classical schools.
