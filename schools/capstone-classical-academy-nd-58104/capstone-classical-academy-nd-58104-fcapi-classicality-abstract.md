## ABSTRACT

Capstone Classical Academy (Fargo, ND, 58104) demonstrates a solid CLASSICAL orientation in Religious and Metaphysical Foundations and School Purpose and Goal, with a strong emphasis on virtue formation, Biblical grounding, and the pursuit of truth, goodness, and beauty. The public site shows vivid articulation of virtuous character through The Gryphon’s Weapons and Scriptural anchors, aligning with transcendental moral realism and theology that classical education seeks.
