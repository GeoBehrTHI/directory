## Executive Summary

Chesterton Academy of Fort Worth (CAFW) presents a robust classical-Catholic model that aligns strongly with the core Six Essential Elements of Classical Education. Public-facing materials describe CAFW as a Catholic, independent classical high school that embeds truth, goodness, and beauty into a unified liberal-arts curriculum, emphasizes formation of character and virtue, and employs a Socratic, mail-strengthened pedagogy anchored by the Incarnation. The school explicitly places Catholic theology, liturgical life (including daily Mass), and a culture of life at the center of its mission, curriculum, and community life. The school also foregrounds architecture of community through a House system, active parent engagement, and a governance/advancement model that includes parent involvement and regular communications with families.

Key evidence from CAFW materials includes: “an independent classical high school in the Catholic tradition… Grounded in the Truths of the Catholic Faith” and “By introducing them to great thinkers such as Socrates, Thomas Aquinas and Dante, and by forming them in a distinctly Catholic culture” (public materials). The curriculum is described as classical and integrated, braided humanities across theology, history, literature, philosophy, languages, and the sciences, with explicit notes that the humanities use a Socratic Method. The school’s mission centers on forming joyful leaders and saints within a Culture of Life; three pillars (Intellect, Character, Spirituality) guide program design; daily Mass and Catholic formation are part of faculty and student life. The leadership and faculty sections describe named leaders and a living-curriculum ethos, and there is explicit emphasis on parent partnership and open campus/community engagement.

Overall category-by-category snapshot indicates CAFW is CLASSICAL across all six categories. The public materials explicitly reference religious foundations, formation-focused purpose, liberal-arts curriculum with classical content, Socratic pedagogy, a living/fixed faculty ethos, and strong parent/community partnerships. Therefore, the aggregate rating is CLASSICAL.
