## ABSTRACT

Chesterton Academy of Sacramento demonstrates a strong CLASSICAL orientation across all six criteria. The school presents a Catholic, liturgically integrated framework (Religious and Metaphysical Foundations) with a focus on truth, beauty, and virtue within a Catholic metaphysical and theological frame. The mission emphasizes formation, virtue, wisdom, and the pursuit of true, good, and beautiful within a classical telos, rather than purely instrumental outcomes.

Curriculum and pedagogy are decisively classical: a liberal-arts framework, Trivium/Quadrivium-oriented structure, integrated humanities (history, literature, philosophy, theology, languages), and a robust Socratic Method with seminar-based and rhetoric-focused upper-level work. The lower-school emphasis on chant and memory work complements upper-school Socratic inquiry, aligning with scholé and contemplative facets. Faculty are presented as central, mission-driven mentors across disciplines, reinforcing the living-curriculum approach. Parents and community are actively engaged through information nights and parental formation opportunities, with graduates and post-graduate pathways showcased to emphasize ongoing community ties.

Overall classification: CLASSICAL.
