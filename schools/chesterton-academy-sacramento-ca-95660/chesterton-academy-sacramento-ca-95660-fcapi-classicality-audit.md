## Executive Summary

Chesterton Academy of Sacramento (CA 95660) presents a robust Catholic, classical educational model that centers formation in virtue, wisdom, and reverence for (and engagement with) the transcendentals of truth, goodness, and beauty. Public materials consistently frame the school as a Catholic, classical institution built around a triadic core—Intellect, Character, Spirituality—and emphasize liturgical life, a strong humanities emphasis, and a Socratic, discussion-based pedagogy. The school foregrounds a liberal-arts oriented curriculum, integration of Great Books/Western thought, and a strong emphasis on virtue formation, beauty, and prayer. Community and family partnership are also central (e.g., parent information nights, open access to campus, and public-facing statements about forming leaders and saints). Based on the publicly available public materials, the school scores highly on all six classical criteria, indicating a CLASSICAL classification overall.

Key evidence:
- Public articulation of the Three Pillars (Intellect, Character, Spirituality) and Cultura Vitae, with explicit Catholic theological framing and liturgical life. 
- A mission emphasizing formation, virtue, wisdom, and Catholic truth/beauty, including a stated aim to form joyful leaders and saints within a classical-Catholic tradition. 
- A curriculum and pedagogy that foregrounds liberal arts, the Trivium/Quadrivium mindset, Great Books/Western thought, and the Socratic Method, with explicit reference to history, philosophy, theology, and languages braided together. 
- Liturgical music, sacred music, and specific Catholic liturgical practices (e.g., Gregorian chant, Schola Cantorum), reinforcing religious foundations. 
- Faculty and staff presence with a public-facing "Faculty & Staff" structure and a narrative of teacher leadership, mentoring, and formation. 
- Active parent/community engagement (e.g., Parent Information Nights) and public statements about graduates and community life. 

Overall Category Ranking (aggregate): CLASSICAL
