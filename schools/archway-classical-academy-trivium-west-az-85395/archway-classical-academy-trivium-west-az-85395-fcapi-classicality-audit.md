## Executive Summary

Archway Classical Academy Trivium West (Goodyear, AZ 85395) demonstrates a robust classical-education posture across all six rubriced dimensions. Public materials consistently foreground the transcendental aims of truth, goodness, and beauty, with explicit emphasis on intellectual and moral formation, the Trivium (grammar, dialectic, rhetoric), the Quadrivium, and the liberal-arts framework that underpins a Great-Books–driven, Great-Conversation–oriented curriculum. Pedagogy frequently centers Socratic dialogue, memorization practices (including singing and recitation), and contemplative rhythms (Scholé), while faculty ideals align with living-mentors and character formation. Parent engagement is explicitly elevated as a core facet of the school’s community, with clear invitations for family participation and formation. Taken together, the affiliated materials align strongly with the Six Essential Elements model of classical education, supporting a final assessment of CLASSICAL across all six categories.

Key evidentiary anchors include: the mission language that centers Truth, Goodness, and Beauty and explicitly names Great-Hearts–style formation (intellectual and moral) (Evidence: archway Trivium West materials describing the mission and Great Hearts’ aims) ; the explicit articulation of intellectual and moral formation as core aims (Evidence: Archway Great Hearts materials) ; the clearly stated Trivium framework (grammar, dialectic, rhetoric) and liberal-arts foundations (including the Quadrivium) as curricular scaffolds (Evidence: ICE/Trivium materials; Liberal Arts Tradition content) ; the presence of Great-Books / Great Conversation framing in the curricular lexicon as a goal, and the integration of history and the liberal-arts pedagogy (Evidence: Six Elements lexicon and related documents) ; pedagogy emphasizing Socratic discussion, memorization through chant/singing, and scholé/restful learning (Evidence: ICE pamphlets and pedagogy documents) ; and explicit parent/community-partnership language, including formation and involvement, as well as family-engagement initiatives (Evidence: Six Elements parent/partner keywords; Archway community content) .

- Why this rating

The presence of theology-like scaffolds appears in the broader Great Hearts/Archway lexicon and pedagogy materials, and the school’s framing of formation as a primary aim (not merely information transmission) strongly supports a classical ground-truth orientation.

Citations: Great Hearts/Archway mission language and formation emphasis are evidenced in the public materials (archway triv West/Great Hearts content) and the “pursuit of truth, goodness, and beauty” phrasing.

  - The public-facing materials emphasize the formation of virtue through pedagogy—teachers as moral exemplars, living curriculum, and a teacher-led, inquiry-driven approach. 

- Positive/Negative keyword analysis

  - Negative keywords: The sources include explicit cautions against non-classical, project-based-only, or constructivist approaches; these negative indicators are present as counterpoints in the knowledge-base lexicon, and the public materials consistently favor classical methods over those alternatives. 

- Why this rating
  - The pedagogical vocabulary and practices align strongly with classical pedagogy (Socratic/Lectio/Scholé, memorization via chant, grammar/dialectic/rhetoric progression, and rhetoric training in upper grades), satisfying Element 4.

Citations: ICE Pamphlet/SECTARIAN and related Great Hearts pedagogy materials provide explicit details on the pedagogy, including Schoé and chant-based memory practices, and Socratic seminary/dialogue.
