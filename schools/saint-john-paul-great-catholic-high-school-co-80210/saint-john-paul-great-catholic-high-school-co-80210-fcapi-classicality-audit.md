## Executive Summary

Saint John Paul the Great Catholic High School (Denver) demonstrates a strong alignment with classical education principles across religious foundation, purpose, curriculum, and community. Public-facing materials portray a Catholic, virtue-centered formation anchored in truth, goodness, and beauty, with emphasis on liberal-arts pedagogy, mastery of classical disciplines, and a partnership with parents as co-educators. The school explicitly frames its mission around formation and wisdom rather than job-readiness, and highlights elements such as the Great Books / liberal arts orientation, memorization and recitation in pedagogy, and a faculty culture that models virtue and mentorship. Taken together, the school ranks as CLASSICAL overall, with pedagogy showing some areas that could be strengthened to reach full alignment. The following analysis details how each category stacks up against the classical rubric and keywords.
