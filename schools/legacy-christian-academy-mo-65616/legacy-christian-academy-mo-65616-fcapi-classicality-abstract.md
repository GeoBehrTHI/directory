## ABSTRACT

Legacy Branson Academy (Branson, MO) is CLASSICAL across all six rubric categories. It clearly situates education within religious/metaphysical foundations (Christian, gospel-centered formation; liturgical practices such as chapels and devotionals), purpose and goals oriented toward virtue, wisdom, and true/beautiful knowledge, a curriculum centered on the Trivium and Great Books, a pedagogy that blends memory-based grammar instruction with Socratic/seminar work and rhetoric training, a faculty described as mentors and the living curriculum, and a robust partnership with parents and community involvement programs. The school’s ACCS accreditation and explicit articulation of formation, virtue cultivation, and the Great Conversation position it solidly within the Classical canon as defined by the Six Essential Elements of a Classical School.

- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: CLASSICAL — Christ-centered formation, daily prayer, chapels, Scripture integration.  
- Purpose and Goal: CLASSICAL — Formation of virtue and wisdom; true/good/beautiful. 
- Curriculum: CLASSICAL — Trivium, Great Books/Great Conversation, Latin, integrated history across subjects.  
- Pedagogy: CLASSICAL — Grammar-stage memory/recitation; upper-grade Socratic/seminar work; rhetoric; multi-modal learning.  
- Faculty: CLASSICAL — Living curriculum; mentors; sacred vocation of teaching; collaborative culture. 
- Parents & Community: CLASSICAL — Parental partnership; parent education; open access to visits; community culture.
