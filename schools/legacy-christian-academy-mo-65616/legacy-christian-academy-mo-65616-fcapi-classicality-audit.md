## Executive Summary

Legacy Branson Academy (Legacy Academy Branson, MO) presents a cohesive Classical Christian program that aligns with the Six Essential Elements of a Classical School rubric. Public-facing materials consistently emphasize a Trivium-based curriculum (grammar, logic, rhetoric), Great Books and the Great Conversation, formation of virtue and character, and a robust partnership with families in a liturgical, Christ-centered community. The school explicitly anchors education in theology and Scripture, integrates chapels and daily devotionals, and foregrounds both memory-led pedagogy in the grammar stage and Socratic/seminar-style discourse in the upper grades. The faculty are described as mentors and living embodiments of the school’s aims, with accreditation through ACCS and a stated mission to cultivate truth, goodness, and beauty in students. Parental involvement is positioned as central, with a formal emphasis on parents as co-educators and a culture of family partnership.

### Element 4: Pedagogy or Teaching Methods — CLASSICAL

Evidence
- Grammar-stage pedagogy emphasizes singing, memory, recitation, and multisensory learning (memory-anchored and chant-based methods). 
- Upper-school pedagogy includes Socratic discussion, seminars, debates, and rhetoric training, alongside opportunities for public speaking and senior-level theses. 
- A multi-modal approach (visual, auditory, kinesthetic learning) is described as accommodating various learner strengths, with a focus on gentle review and memorization as well as rigorous argumentation in higher grades. 
- The program includes daily devotionals, chapels, and faith-integrated instruction, aligning pedagogy with religious formation. In higher grades, there is explicit emphasis on logic, rhetoric, and research-based writing, including primary sources.

- Negative keywords observed: None indicating non-classical methods (e.g., pure project-based without classical scaffolding); the materials emphasize classical stages (grammar–logic–rhetoric) with associated methods.

Why this rating
- The pedagogy aligns with CLASSICAL expectations: contemplative/restful elements in early years, memory-focused grammar instruction, Socratic seminars, and rhetoric-oriented performance in upper grades.
