## Executive Summary

Crossroads Christian Academy AZ presents a robust classical-Christian profile, with explicit commitments to Christ-centered classical education, the Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric), Great Books and the Great Conversation, and a biblically grounded worldview embedded in all classroom life. Public materials on the school’s site emphasize a formation-focused aim (virtue, wisdom, human flourishing) and a pedagogy that blends contemplative, memory-rich lower-division practice with Socratic/seminar-oriented upper-division work. The school also foregrounds partnership with families, reverence and church-life integration, and a clearly defined code of ethics and governance structure. Taken together, these elements align strongly with a classical education framework, particularly one that is explicitly theological and liturgically articulated.

Overall classification: CLASSICAL.

Key evidence highlights include: mission statements that describe a Christ-centered, classical education (with emphasis on formation and lifelong learning) and the explicit framing of truth, goodness, and beauty as transcendent standards rooted in God (supported by direct statements in FAQ materials and a formal Statement of Faith) . The curriculum is published to center grammar-logic-rhetoric (the Trivium), the Great Books approach, and Western civilization—elements central to classical education (e.g., “BEGINNING GRAMMAR … GRAMMAR SCHOOL … LOGIC SCHOOL … RHETORIC SCHOOL” and Great Books references) . Faculty and governance materials (board portraits and staff statements) reinforce the living-lesson/church-mentorship model, while parent-partnership and devotional formation are repeatedly foregrounded in policy and mission materials . These signals collectively underpin an overall CLASSICAL rating, with notable strengths in explicit religious/metaphysical foundations, curriculum, pedagogy, and community involvement.
