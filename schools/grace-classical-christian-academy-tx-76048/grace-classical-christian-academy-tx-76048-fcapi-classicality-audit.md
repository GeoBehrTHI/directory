## Executive Summary

Grace Classical Christian Academy (GCCA) in Granbury, TX 76048 presents a robust classical-Christian profile. Public-site materials emphasize the trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric), the Great Books/Great Conversation tradition, and explicit biblical/theological framing of education, coupled with a culture of worship, discipleship, and family partnership. Across categories, GCCA demonstrates strong alignment with classical aims, especially in Religious & Metaphysical Foundations, Curriculum, Pedagogy, and Community/Parents. The overall assessment falls into CLASSICAL, with clear signals of a consistently transmitting classical literature, dialogic/retorically grounded pedagogy, and a formation-focused ethos anchored in Christian faith.

Key takeaways
- Religious and metaphysical foundations are explicit: Scripture-centered instruction, theology, liturgical/communal practices, and a discipleship ethos are repeatedly foregrounded. This strongly supports the “truth, goodness, beauty” transcendentals in the classical lexicon. 
- Curriculum centers the Trivium, Great Books, and a history-rich, Western-civilization-centered approach, with explicit mention of the Great Books and the Great Conversation. 
- Pedagogy blends contemplative (Scholé) elements with rigorous rhetoric, memory work, and Socratic/seminal-style opportunities, particularly at upper levels. 
- Faculty and community orientation emphasize living curriculum, mentorship, and parent partnership, with explicit references to teacher formation and family involvement. 
- The school’s public materials actively promote parent involvement, open-campus orientation, and a relational school culture anchored in Christian community.
