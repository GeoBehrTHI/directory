## Executive Summary

Coram Deo Academy (Or 97385) presents as a preschool-through-middle-school effort anchored in Classical Christian education. Public-facing materials portray a deliberate integration of Christian discipleship with a classical frame, emphasizing the pursuit of wisdom and virtue within a Christ-centered, Bible-informed culture. The school operates as an independent nonprofit and locates lessons and activities in church facilities, openly courting families as partners in formation and offering a pathway toward K–12 classical education in the long term. This combination of explicit Christian formation, explicitly classical language, and a vision of formation over mere information supports a robust Classical orientation overall, with some curricular and pedagogical details still maturing for a full K–12 classical program.

Executive evidence from the public materials shows:
- A stated mission of partnering with families to provide a Christian, classical education centered on wisdom and virtue (and a Christ-centered culture) .
- Clear statements of discipleship, Bible-centered pedagogy, and Protestant, non-denominational identity with a specific emphasis on Scripture and a Christ-centered worldview .
- Long-term vision to build a K–12 Classical Christian school serving the mid-Willamette Valley, with language about “rigorous academic instruction” and “pursuit of wisdom and virtue” within a Christian framework .
- Evidence of a location and community orientation through a church partnership (Emmanuel Bible Church) and public events that invite parental involvement and school visits, signaling a community-centric model rather than a “customer” model .
- Faculty and staff narratives emphasize teachers as virtuous, mission-driven mentors and living exemplars of Christian formation, reinforcing the “living curriculum” ideal in classical pedagogy .

Overall aggregate: CLASSICAL. The public materials reveal a sustained classical Christian frame (formation-focused, virtue- and wisdom-oriented) with explicit religious/metaphysical grounding, a formation-centric purpose, and a visible emphasis on a teacher-led living curriculum and parent partnership. The school’s current K–6 scope aligns with classical practice, and the planned expansion to K–12 would complete the classical cycle in line with the rubric in the knowledge-base materials.
