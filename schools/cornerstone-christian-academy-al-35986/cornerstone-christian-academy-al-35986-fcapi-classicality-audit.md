## Executive Summary

Cornerstone Christian Academy (CCA) in Rainsville, Alabama presents a robust CLASSICAL Christian orientation across most dimensions of the Six Essential Elements framework. Public materials from the school consistently emphasize a Christ-centered environment, biblical worldview, and theological grounding; they articulate a curriculum and pedagogy aligned with classical aims (Latin, logic, rhetoric; Great Books; Trivium; Paideia). The school foregrounds partnership with families and strong faculty leadership as core features of its educational model. Where the pedagogy includes explicit formation through memory work and historical-literary study, it aligns clearly with classical practice. One category shows partial alignment (pedagogy emphasizing some traditional, contemplative elements but not always foregrounding the full Socratic/seminar model in the public materials), placing the school in a largely CLASSICAL standing with a note of “Somewhat Classical” in the pedagogy dimension. Overall, Cornerstone Christian Academy is classified as CLASSICAL.

Key insights at a glance:

- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: Explicit theology, biblical worldview, and Christ-centered mission are central to the school’s identity.
- Purpose and Goal: Education is framed as formation in a Christian context, oriented toward wisdom and virtue rather than mere job preparation.
- Curriculum: Strong emphasis on liberal arts (grammar, logic, rhetoric), Great Books, Latin, and paideia-inspired formation.
- Pedagogy: Evidence of active, discussion- and memory-based elements; partial alignment to contemplative Scholé and classical recitation, with more explicit Socratic methods suggested in classical literature than in explicit public statements.
- Faculty: Public materials describe faculty as a core asset and as living exemplars of virtue; a “top-quality faculty” emphasis is present.
- Parents and Community: School positions parents as partners/co-educators and highlights community engagement.

# ABSTRACT

Cornerstone Christian Academy (CCA) demonstrates a strong alignment with classical education principles through its explicit Christian-theological grounding, formation-focused purpose, and liberal-arts–driven curriculum (Trivium, Great Books, Latin). Public materials emphasize a Christ-centered environment, biblical worldview, and a theology-centered approach to all disciplines, supporting a predominantly CLASSICAL classification in the Religious and Metaphysical Foundations category as well as School Purpose and Goal, Curriculum, and Parents & Community. Element 4 (Pedagogy) shows partial alignment, reflecting active, discussion-rich and memory-based elements, but without fully detailed Socratic/Scholé practices in public-facing content; this yields a SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL designation for that dimension. Faculty and Community categories show strong CLASSICAL signals, with references to top-quality faculty and robust parent partnership. Collectively, the aggregate assessment places Cornerstone Christian Academy in the CLASSICAL category.

- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: CLASSICAL
    - Evidence: Christ-centered mission; biblical worldview; Statement of Faith; theologic grounding. Positive keywords include God, Bible, Scripture, theology, biblical worldview, Creator, etc. No strong presence of secular or relativist language observed.

- School Purpose and Goal: CLASSICAL
    - Evidence: Formation and virtue-centered aims, biblical worldview; spiritual/academic-objectives; foundational Christian formation rather than vocational emphasis.

- Curriculum: CLASSICAL
    - Evidence: Explicit Trivium/Quadrivium framing, Great Books/Paideia, Latin; integrated approach across history and disciplines.

- Pedagogy: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL
    - Evidence: Active learning, analysis/discussion; memory-work; classical emphasis present but full Socratic/Scholé detailing not consistently foregrounded in public materials.

- Faculty: CLASSICAL
    - Evidence: Top-quality faculty; living curriculum; mentor-like role implied.

- Parents & Community: CLASSICAL
    - Evidence: Strong parent partnership, co-teaching language, open communication, community involvement.
