## ABSTRACT

Cornerstone Christian Academy’s public materials reveal a nearly full CLASSICAL alignment across the six classical-elements categories, with Pedagogy showing partial alignment. The school presents a strong Christian theological foundation, a formation-centered purpose emphasizing virtue and wisdom, and a curriculum centered on the liberal arts (Trivium/Quadrivium), Great Books, and Latin. Faculty are portrayed as central to the living curriculum, and parents are engaged as partners in education. While the pedagogy demonstrates many classical elements (active learning, memory work, classical language and rhetorical training), explicit detailing of Socratic seminars and Scholé-based contemplative practices in public content is less evident, resulting in a Somewhat Classical designation for pedagogy. Overall, Cornerstone Christian Academy is CLASSICAL.

- Primary strengths: explicit Christ-centered theology and biblical worldview; classical curriculum (Trivium/Paideia/Great Books/Latin); formation emphasis (virtue, integrity, biblical principles); strong faculty and parental partnership.

- Opportunities to strengthen toward a fully CLASSICAL designation: more explicit public articulation of Socratic/seminar-based pedagogy and Scholé practices; more detailed listing of Great Books and primary texts used in grade bands; explicit “portrait of a teacher” in public materials.

End of ABSTRACT.
