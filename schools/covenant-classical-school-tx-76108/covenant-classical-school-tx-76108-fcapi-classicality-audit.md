## Executive Summary

Covenant Classical School Fort Worth (76108) presents a clearly classical Christian education model across its public materials. The school consistently foregrounds a biblical, Christ-centered worldview, the liberal arts framework, and a curriculum designed around the grammar–logic–rhetoric tradition, coupled with rigorous study of great books, primary sources, and historical breadth. Evidence shows explicit reliance on the Great Books/Great Conversation paradigm, Socratic inquiry in upper grades, and a rhetoric-focused upper-school program. Faculty are described as the “living curriculum,” and there is a notable emphasis on parental partnership and community formation through chapel, advisory structures, and parent resources. Overall, the school scores CLASSICAL across the six categories, with strong alignment in religious/metaphysical foundations, purpose/goal, curriculum, pedagogy, faculty, and parent/community engagement.

Key signals include: a biblically centered mission, daily chapel/advisory practices, explicit statements about classical methodology and the Great Books approach, presence of liberal-arts–driven history and languages (Latin, Wheelock’s Latin), Socratic discussion formats, senior thesis options, a faculty-as-curriculum framing, and active parent partnership with access to resources and visits (including a non-discrimination policy and a clearly stated role for parents as co-educators). These elements collectively map to the Six Essential Elements framework for a classical school.
