## Executive Summary

Logos Academy York (Logos Academy, PA 17401) presents a robust alignment with classical education principles wrapped in a distinctly Christian, Kingdom-centered frame. The public-facing materials consistently foreground Christ-centered formation, a strong classical curriculum (notably the liberal arts, grammar–logic–rhetoric sequence, and the seven liberal arts), and a community-oriented, family-partnered approach. Evidence of a clearly articulated formation goal—intelligence plus character, a biblically informed worldview, and a commitment to virtue and wisdom—permeates admissions, curricula, and mission statements. While the school is clearly classical in its aims and methods, it also foregrounds college/career readiness as a practical outcome, which introduces a subtle tension with the historical “formation over information” emphasis characteristic of some classical schools. Overall, Logos Academy is CLASSICAL, with only a notable nuance around explicit emphasis on college/workforce preparation within its stated objectives.
