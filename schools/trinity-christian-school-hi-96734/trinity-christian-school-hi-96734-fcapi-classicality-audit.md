## Executive Summary

Trinity Christian School Kailua (Hi 96734) presents a robust classical Christian program anchored in a Christ-centered worldview, a classical pedagogy, and a strong partnership with families. Public materials from the school consistently frame education as formation of character and virtue in alignment with truth, goodness, and beauty, rather than mere information or job preparation. The school explicitly identifies as “Christ-centered classical” and discusses a progression from Grammar School (memory and chant) to Logic School (Socratic discussion and debate) and onward toward rhetoric-based upper-school work. It also emphasizes liturgical and communal practices (weekly chapel, parent involvement, and a shared liturgical rhythm) and treats the teacher as a mentor and exemplar, i.e., part of the living curricular fabric. Parental partnership is foregrounded as essential and expected, with the school inviting ongoing parent engagement and visits.

On balance, the publicly available materials strongly meet the Six Essential Elements of Classical Education. Across the six categories, Trinity Christian School Kailua demonstrates a consistent orientation toward classical aims, particularly in religious/metaphysical foundations, formation-focused goals, and the liberal-arts curriculum (grammar/logic/rhetoric) with Socratic pedagogy. The aggregate classification is CLASSICAL.

Evidence for this assessment is drawn from the school’s About, Our Story, and Academics pages and related Community content, which together show: Christ-centered mission and theology integration; a mission of formation and virtue; explicit use of classical methods (grammar/logic/rhetoric) with memory work and chant in the Grammar phase and Socratic discourse in Logic; a pedagogy that includes restful or contemplative moments (Scholé) alongside rigorous discussion and public speaking; faculty described as dedicated mentors; and strong partnerships with parents and active parental involvement in community life.
