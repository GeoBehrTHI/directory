## Executive Summary

Community Christian Academy Wisconsin (CCA-WI) presents a robust classical, Christian-formed program embedded within the West Bend Community Church ecosystem. Public materials emphasize classical education as the intended model, a church-supported staffing framework, and explicit resources that position the school within the classical-tradition ecosystem (e.g., conversations about what classical education is, notes about curriculum staffing, and a “portrait of an educator”). Public-facing pages repeatedly reference classical education, and the site foregrounds church involvement (e.g., pastors on staff and church-affiliated branding). This alignment supports a CLASSICAL overall assessment, with strongest signals in Religious and Metaphysical Foundations, Curriculum, and Faculty, and sustained signals in Parents & Community. Signals for Pedagogy are present but less explicit on the public site (no clear, stated use of Socratic seminars on the site itself). The aggregate profile thus lands firmly in CLASSICAL, with only minor, localized ambiguities in pedagogical phrasing.

Key public signals observed on the site:
- Explicit classical-education framing and links to classical-education resources (What is Classical Education?; Classical Guide for Parents; Well-Trained Mind references; etc.). 
- Church affiliation and church-led staffing references (West Bend Community Church; Senior Pastor; North Site Pastor; etc.). 
- Staffing portrayal and “portrait of an educator” themes within the public materials. 
- Emphasis on assembling a culture that centers the classical-education dialogue (e.g., education consultation, volunteer opportunities, and parent-facing classical resources). 

Abstract-level takeaway: The school’s public materials strongly align with classical education undergirded by Christian/theological framing, with curriculum emphasis and faculty culture consistent with classical practice, and with a stated mission of formation through a classical model.
