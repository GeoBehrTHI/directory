## ABSTRACT

Palm Beach Christian Academy North Stuart demonstrates a predominantly classical profile, anchored in a distinctively Christian, Trivium-based curriculum and a Great Books/Socratic dialogue emphasis, with liturgical and communal practices (chapel, worship) reinforcing a religious-metaphysical foundation. The school foregrounds truth, beauty, and goodness as real, transcendent aims, with formation of character and virtue as a central objective. The public materials also emphasize parental partnership and a community-based culture that aligns with classical education’s emphasis on the formation of the whole person in a community anchored by faith.

Core thematic alignments:
- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: CLASSICAL, evidenced by Scripture-centered learning, Christ-centered formation, and liturgical worship. Positive keywords centered on biblical worldview, scripture, and chapel support this alignment.
- School Purpose and Goal: CLASSICAL, evidenced by virtue/character formation and pursuit of truth within a biblical framework.
- Curriculum: CLASSICAL, evidenced by Trivium framing (grammar, dialectic, rhetoric), Great Books, and Socratic dialogue as central curricular modalities.
- Pedagogy: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL, with explicit Socratic dialogue and rhetoric emphasis but not uniformly documented as a grade-spanning, formalized program across all grades in public materials.
- Faculty: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL, evidenced by staff mentorship and community-oriented culture, but lacking explicit public articulation of the faculty as the living curriculum.
- Parents & Community: CLASSICAL, with strong parental partnership, community engagement, and testimonials illustrating ongoing collaboration.

Overall: CLASSICAL.

PBCA North Stuart evidences a robust classical footprint with definite Christian formation and classical curriculum; the main opportunities to elevate to a fully classical designation lie in explicitly codifying and publicizing the faculty as living curriculum and ensuring grade-spanning, consistent articulation of a full high-school-level Socratic/seminar pedagogy across all levels.
