## Executive Summary

Hillcrest Lutheran Academy (Hillcrest Academy; Hillcrest Lutheran Academy; Hillcrest Academy, Fergus Falls, MN area) presents a robust Classical Christian educational model. Public-facing materials describe a deliberate fusion of faith formation and rigorous liberal-arts pedagogy, organized around the Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric) and a Great-Books-informed approach, with explicit mentions of Great Books, Socratic discussion, and a three-stage progression from grammar through logic to rhetoric. The school positions itself as Christ-centered, with liturgical and communal practices (chapel, Bible study, worship) integrated into the curriculum, and it highlights mentorship by adults as a core component of the living curriculum. Evidence from Hillcrest’s site and blog materials shows a consistent rhetoric of forming virtuous character, unity of faith and learning, and a family/community orientation that frames parents as partners in the educational mission. Overall, the school demonstrates alignment with Classical education principles across all six rubric categories.

Key signals include:

- Religious and metaphysical foundation embedded in curriculum and practice (Christ-centered, worship, chapel, Bible study, theology integrated with learning) .
- Purpose and goals framed as formation in virtue and wisdom, grounded in timeless truths (truth, goodness, beauty; character formation; worship of God) .
- Curriculum reflecting liberal-arts structure (Trivium/Quadrivium-era cadence; Great Books; historical breadth; emphasis on wonder; Latin; classical texts) .
- Pedagogy combining contemplative elements with Socratic/rigorous discourse and memory-education practices (Scholé-like contemplative rhythm; singing/memory in lower school; Socratic seminars; upper-school rhetoric) .
- Faculty described as mentors and living curriculum; adult mentors central to student formation; long-standing tradition of teacher-student partnership .
- Parents and community engagement evidenced by historical partnership with families and ongoing emphasis on parental involvement and formation within the school’s mission (e.g., “Serving Parents” and family/discipleship themes) .

# ABSTRACT

Hillcrest Lutheran Academy MN 56537 demonstrates a coherent Classical Christian profile across all six rubric categories. Religiously and metaphysically, the school foregrounds Christian theology, liturgical practice, and God-centered formation. Its school purpose emphasizes formation, virtue, wisdom, and the true/good/beautiful telos, consistent with classical aims. Curriculum shows a Trivium- and Great-Books-oriented structure, with Latin, world/history Bible history, and a Master Curriculum Map that binds faith formation to academic goals. Pedagogy blends contemplative (Scholé) approaches with Socratic dialogue and rhetoric, particularly in upper grades, accompanied by a lower-school emphasis on memory and liturgical-inflected learning. Faculty are portrayed as mentors who embody and transmit the living curriculum, and there is clear evidence of sustained parent and community involvement, including historical engagement with families. Collectively, Hillcrest Academy meets the Six Essential Elements criteria at a robust CLASSICAL level.
