## Executive Summary

Calvary Christian Academy NM 88062 (Silver City, NM) presents a robust CLASSICAL orientation anchored in a Christ-centered framework. Public-facing materials repeatedly emphasize a classical triad (grammar, logic, rhetoric) and a curriculum built around liberal arts, primary sources, and historical study integrated with theology. The school explicitly names its mission as Christian and classical, with a governance of learning that centers Scripture, theological formation, and virtuous character. Across six evaluative categories, Calvary Christian Academy demonstrates strong alignment with classical education principles, with only minor ambiguities in the public-facing materials that do not undermine the overall classical integrity.

Key evidence includes: a clear “Statement of Faith” grounding the school in biblical authority; a public articulation of being “CLASSICAL” with emphasis on grammar, logic, and rhetoric; a curriculum that includes Latin/Greek, history taught through primary sources, and a “Great Books / Great Conversation” orientation; pedagogical practices featuring Socratic discussion in upper grades and memory-based recitation and chant in lower grades; a faculty model focused on virtue and living the curriculum; and a robust, ongoing partnership with parents and the wider church community. Citations drawn from school handbooks, mission statements, and curricular documents illustrate these elements.
