## ABSTRACT

Alachua Learning Academy (ALA) demonstrates clear strengths in virtue-formation, Lifelong learning, and a values-based, small-school experience that supports humane education. Public messaging emphasizes character development, leadership, and a nurturing, family-centric environment, which aligns with some classical aims (formation and moral education). However, explicit classical curriculum orthodoxy—such as Great Books, the trivium/quadrivium, Scholé, and clearly defined classical pedagogy and faculty archetypes—are not clearly documented in public-facing materials. The school explicitly identifies itself as nonsectarian, which means it does not present a theological or liturgical foundation in its public materials, limiting alignment with the Religious and Metaphysical Foundations criteria of the classical rubric. Thus, the overall classification is SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL, with notable strengths in School Purpose & Goal and partial alignment in Faculty and Parents & Community, and weaker alignment in Curriculum and Pedagogy.

Key evidence:
- Nonsectarian stance and explicit “not a religious school” messaging. 
- Formation-focused language, moral character, lifelong learning, and values-based approach.
