## ABSTRACT

Overall classification: CLASSICAL

Austin Classical School’s website demonstrates strong alignment to classical Christian education across the Six Elements.

- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: Transcendentals (truth, goodness, beauty) and Scripture-centered learning; All-School chapel indicates liturgical rhythms; Statement of Faith grounds instruction   .
- Purpose and Goal: Education aims at the cultivation of wisdom and virtue, with formation prioritized over mere information and an “eternal purpose” orientation  .
- Curriculum: Trivium structure (Latin, logic, rhetoric), great books, Scripture central, history/lit sequences; Socratic method and senior thesis are explicit   .
- Pedagogy: Grammar-stage memory/recitation/songs; logic-stage debate and discussion; upper-school rhetoric, imitation, and thesis defense; Socratic method named  .
- Faculty: Faith commitment and classical approach are clear, though a public “portrait of a teacher,” mentoring language, and collegial formation could be made more explicit.
- Parents and Community: Deep parent partnership via collaborative (University-Model) structure; parents participate in tours with chapel, and frequent on-campus parent events  .
