# Executive Summary

Overall classification: CLASSICAL

School: Austin Classical School (Austin, TX)  
Website used: https://austinclassical.org/

Highlights:
- Strong theological grounding and explicit transcendental moral realism (truth, goodness, beauty; Scripture central; chapel) consistent with the Six Elements framework   .
- Clear purpose: cultivation of wisdom and virtue; formation over mere information; the Great Conversation explicitly invoked .
- Robust classical curriculum: trivium structure, Latin through middle school, logic and rhetoric (incl. thesis defense), great books, Bible and history sequences; explicit use of Socratic method   .
- Pedagogy matches classical patterns across grades: memorization/recitation/songs in grammar years; debate/discussion in logic; rhetoric, imitation, and thesis defense in upper school   .
- Parents are true partners (“co-teacher” model; collaborative schedule; chapel on tours; ongoing parent events)  .
- ACCS accreditation announced Spring 2025, reinforcing classical Christian fidelity and accountability .

Note: Faculty language is generally positive (faith subscription; serve God; degreed professionals) but lacks an explicit “portrait of a teacher” or “faculty of friends/living curriculum” articulation on the public site; we score that category as Somewhat Classical (see Category 5).
