## Abstract

Whitmore Classical Academy (Florence, OR, 97439), as evidenced by publicly crawlable site content, does not yet demonstrate the core indicators of classical education across the six assessed categories. The available site materials emphasize enrollment, governance, and location rather than a classical curriculum, pedagogy, faculty model, or parent-community formation. The knowledge-base rubric (Six Essential Elements) and its keyword indicators identify Great Books, Great Conversation, Trivium-based curriculum, scholé pedagogy, and active parent partnerships as primary classical signals; none of these appear clearly in the school’s public materials. Therefore, the aggregate classification is NOT CLASSICAL.

Key insights from the knowledge base:
- Positive keyword indicators for each category include transcendental realism (truth, goodness, beauty), theology, liturgy, Great Books/Great Conversation, Socratic pedagogy, living faculty, and parent partnerships. The school’s crawl content does not reveal these signals. See the Six Essential Elements document for the explicit indicators and the classical lexicon used to frame these signals.
