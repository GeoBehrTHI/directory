## Executive Summary

Leman Academy Excellence Marana AZ 85743 positions itself publicly as a tuition-free K–8 charter school delivering a “classical education” that foregrounds knowledge, character, virtue, and timeless values. Its Marana campus materials emphasize a strong academic foundation, smaller class sizes, and a collaborative partnership model with families and the broader school community. The site identifies itself as an A-rated public charter school and frames its mission around forming students through a classical curriculum and a virtuous formation rather than a narrow job-preparation focus.

Assessment against the classical rubric shows partial alignment across the six elements. The school clearly articulates classical aims (classical foundation, character formation, parental partnership, and a broader “whole-child” enrichment approach). However, explicit commitments to several core metaphysical or liturgical dimensions of classical education (theology; liturgical or communal worship; explicit Great Books/Great Conversation framework; Socratic pedagogy with formal seminars) are not prominent in the available public materials. As a result, the overall evaluation lands in the Somewhat Classical range, with stronger alignment in Curriculum and some facets of Faculty and Parents & Community, and more modest alignment in Religious & Metaphysical Foundations and Pedagogy.
