ABSTRACT

Rivertree Academy (Fort Worth, TX) is a Christian independent school with a clear religious foundation and formation-oriented goals. The school publicly emphasizes serving Christ, character formation, and communal aspects (e.g., Psalm 1:3 and other biblical framing). While these signals align with Classical Foundation elements, explicit classical curriculum elements (Seven Liberal Arts, Great Books, Great Conversation), classical pedagogy (Scholé, Socratic seminars), and a fully articulated living-faculty/parent-partnership model are not clearly evidenced in the accessible public materials. Consequently, the school scores as NOT CLASSICAL overall, with partial CLASSICAL signals in Religious Foundations and some positive signals in Parents & Community, and SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL signals in School Purpose & Goal. To move toward a full Classical designation, Rivertree would need explicit public documentation of a liberal-arts curriculum, explicit Great Books/Great Conversation commitments, classical pedagogy, and a clearly articulated parent-as-co-teacher model in its public-facing materials.
