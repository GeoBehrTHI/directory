## ABSTRACT

Cornerstone Academy Florida demonstrates a robust classical Christian orientation across most rubric categories, with clear evidence of religious/metaphysical foundations (biblical worldview; weekly chapels), a formation-focused purpose (virtue, wisdom, godly character), and a Trivium-centered curriculum (grammar, logic, rhetoric; Latin/Greek). The school exhibits strong faculty quality language (carefully selected teachers; teachers as mentors) and a well-developed parent/community partnership ethos (parental involvement; family-oriented admissions culture). Public materials show explicit classical commitments in Categories 1, 2, 5, and 6, and partial alignment in Category 3 (Curriculum) and Category 4 (Pedagogy), with notable evidence of Trivium components but less explicit Great Books and Socratic/seminar pedagogy within the publicly available content. Taken together, the school’s public-facing materials meet the core expectations of a classical education in a significant portion of the rubric, but the absence of explicit Great Books/Great Conversation and Socratic-seminar signals places CA in the SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL category overall.
