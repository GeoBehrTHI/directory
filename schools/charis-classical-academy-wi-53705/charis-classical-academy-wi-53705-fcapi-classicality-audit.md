## Executive Summary

Charis Classical Academy Madison (Charis Classical Academy (Madison, WI, 53705)) presents a robust classical, Christian, university-model approach rooted in the Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric), an emphasis on the Great Books, and a clear emphasis on formation of character through a gospel-centered framework. Public materials consistently frame education as formation of virtue and wisdom within a theologically grounded worldview, with explicit statements about Truth, Goodness, and Beauty as transcendent ideals and about partnership with families in the educational task. The school foregrounds serious classical content (Great Books, Latin, classical languages, history through the curriculum, and a staged progression in Grammar–Logic–Rhetoric) and a pedagogy that blends contemplative/purposive study (Joyful Discovery, living books, nature study) with Socratic discussion, debates, and rhetoric in the upper grades. Public materials also emphasize the role of parents as co-educators and a collaborative, family-centered model (home/school partnership, satellite classroom concepts, and weekly/seasonal arts offerings). Overall, Charis Classical Academy Madison meets the core indicators of classical education across six rubric categories and is best classified as CLASSICAL.
