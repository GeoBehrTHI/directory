## Executive Summary

Valley Classical School (Blacksburg, VA, 24062) presents a clearly Christian, classical-trajectory program embedded in a local Christian community. Public-facing materials emphasize theology, virtue formation, and a mission oriented toward forming character in service to God. Evidence from the site and its associated blog posts shows explicit use of a Nicene Creed statement of faith, partnerships with churches, and a rhetoric-leaning upper school pathway. The school’s materials also reference a growing Rhetoric School, Latin assessment, and a portrait-of-a-graduate framework, signaling a move toward traditional liberal arts training. Taken together, the school demonstrates a solid CLASSICAL orientation with meaningful room to demonstrate deeper Great Books engagement and expanded explicit pedagogy around contemplative “Scholé” practices and the full Great Conversation. Overall classification: CLASSICAL.
