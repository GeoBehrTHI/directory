## ABSTRACT

Valley Classical School (Blacksburg, VA, 24062) demonstrates a strong CLASSICAL orientation with a clearly Christian theological framing. The public materials present The Nicene Creed as the school’s Statement of Faith, church-based community context, and a mission oriented toward formation and virtue in alignment with classical aims. Leadership and faculty structures are explicit, and there is a visible Rhetoric School pathway and Latin assessment, signaling a classical curriculum spine. While curricular and pedagogical documentation shows classical elements (grammar-logic-rhetoric progression, rhetoric emphasis, and a growing portrait-of-a-graduate for the rhetoric track), explicit public articulation of Great Books/Great Conversation and fully fleshed Scholé-style pedagogy is less consistently documented online. Overall, the school is CLASSICAL, with room to strengthen explicit Great Books integration, contemplative pedagogy documentation, and cross-discipline integration to secure a fully robust Classical profile across all six categories.
