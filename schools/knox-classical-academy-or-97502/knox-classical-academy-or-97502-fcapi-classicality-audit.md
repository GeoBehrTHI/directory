## Executive Summary

Knox Classical Academy (Medford, OR 97502) presents a clear, multi-layered classical Christian program. Across the six classical elements, public-facing materials consistently foreground a Christian worldview, formation of character and virtue, engagement with the liberal arts (grammar, logic, rhetoric) and Great Books traditions, collaborative pedagogy rooted in Socratic/Scholastic sensibilities, a living-faculty model, and a strong parent/community partnership. Hands-on evidence from the school’s own site shows explicit commitments to: a Christian paideia and theology-informed education; formation through virtue and wisdom; liberal-arts pedagogy (grammar, logic, rhetoric) integrated with Great Books and Western civilization; and active parent involvement as co-educators. Taken together, these signals place Knox Classical Academy solidly in the CLASSICAL category across all six rubric categories.
