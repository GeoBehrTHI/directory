# Classicality Audit: Carden Memorial School (Salt Lake City, UT)

Website audited: https://www.cardenmemorialschool.com/ 

## Executive Summary
Overall classification: CLASSICAL

Carden Memorial School presents a robust classical Christian identity and practice. The website repeatedly affirms a classical purpose (cultivating “wisdom” and “virtue”) and transcendental aims (“truth” and “beauty”), integrates theology across the curriculum, and maintains liturgical/communal devotional practices. The curriculum foregrounds the Trivium, Latin, Great Books/primary texts, and chronological history; pedagogy includes Socratic discussion, structured debate, and substantial use of memorization/recitation and singing. Parent partnership is explicit and active (weekly devotionals open to parents, community events, parent education content). The faculty section is the least explicit relative to the “teachers as models/mentors” and “faculty collegium” language in the classical lexicon, but testimonials and tour pages still show strong teacher formation and care. On balance, the school’s public materials satisfy the Six Elements framework for a CLASSICAL judgment. Key pages: homepage and hero (“Classical Education for Children PreK-8th”), Trivium/logic/Socratic content, Latin and Great Books, weekly devotionals, parent resources and events.     

Note on method: Keywords and criteria are drawn from “Six Essential Elements of a Classical School,” including positive/negative indicators (e.g., truth, goodness, beauty, theology, liturgy; vs. values-neutrality, project-based/experiential-only), and the classicality lexicon referenced herein.
