## Executive Summary

Legacy Christian Academy (Texas, 75034) presents a clear Christian formation orientation with explicit references to Biblical worldview, faith integration, and covenantwith-family community as core drivers of its program. Public materials emphasize a faith-based mission, mission statements, and parent engagement, which align strongly with the Religious and Metaphysical Foundations and Parents & Community elements of classical education. Evidence of formal classical curriculum components (e.g., Great Books, liberal arts framework) and explicit Socratic or memory-based pedagogy is less visible, which pulls the overall profile toward Not Classical in some categories. Taken together, the school demonstrates substantial classical-readiness in Religious & Metaphysical Foundations, and in its emphasis on formation of character and family partnership, but shows limited explicit documentation of core liberal-arts curricula and pedagogy commonly associated with classical schools. The aggregate assessment places Legacy Christian Academy as SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL.

Key insights:
- Religious & Metaphysical Foundations are strongly represented via explicit faith language, Statement of Faith, integrated biblical worldview, and Covenant-family framing. Evidence includes explicit references to faith requirements and covenant partnership with families.   
- School Purpose & Goal centers on formation and virtue within a Christian frame, supported by references to covenant relationships and community-oriented goals. The school’s materials emphasize formation, faith, and character through its Promise and Portrait of a Graduate context. 
- Curriculum documentation is less explicit about liberal-arts foundations (grammar, logic, rhetoric; Great Books; Great Conversation) within public-facing materials, limiting a Strong Classical Curriculum signal. Public pages emphasize program areas (e.g., Journey/Professional Schools, Spanish Immersion) rather than classical-trivium/quadrivium content. 
- Pedagogy signals (Socratic seminars, memory-work, scholé) are not clearly evidenced in the public materials; the site focuses more on program offerings, family involvement, and events than explicit classical pedagogical methods. 
- Faculty signals are present (Faculty & Staff pages), and there is an emphasis on community and covenant; however, explicit descriptions of faculty as “living curriculum” or virtue-models are not overtly documented in public content. 
- Parents & Community are robustly represented through explicit partnership language (co-teachers, parent involvement, LCA Cares, PTG/PTF, and community-engagement content). This aligns strongly with classical education’s emphasis on parental formation and partnership. 

ABSTRACT: Legacy Christian Academy demonstrates CLASSICAL alignment in Religious & Metaphysical Foundations, and in Parents & Community through strong parental partnership and formation signals, while Curricular and Pedagogical signals are weaker and limit a full Classical classification. The overall assessment is SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL.
