# Classicality Audit: Austin Classical School

Website audited: https://austinclassical.org

## Executive Summary
Overall classification: CLASSICAL

Austin Classical School presents strong, explicit classical-Christian identity across mission, aims, curriculum, and pedagogy. The site foregrounds formation in wisdom and virtue ordered to truth, goodness, and beauty; integrates Scripture and theological study throughout the program; implements the Trivium, Great Books, Socratic practice, memorization/recitation, and senior thesis; and articulates partnership with families (“co-laboring with families”). Evidence for formal liturgical practices (e.g., daily chapel) and explicit parent-formation programming is less visible on public pages, but the core signals firmly meet classical standards. Key strengths include:
- Purpose and goal centered on formation in wisdom and virtue and the transcendentals (truth, goodness, beauty) ; aligned to “Six Elements” positive indicators for Purpose/Goal .
- Deep theological grounding (mission to “glorify Jesus Christ,” Scripture as “holy, inerrant, infallible” and central to wisdom) with substantial Christian Studies (exegesis, apologetics/ethics, church history)   ; aligned to Religious & Metaphysical positive indicators in the “Six Elements” document .
- Robust curriculum (Trivium, Great Books/Great Conversation, Latin, history-by-name, rhetoric and senior thesis) and pedagogy (Socratic method, debate/discussion, imitation, memorization/recitation)   ; matching Curriculum and Pedagogy positives in “Six Elements”   .
