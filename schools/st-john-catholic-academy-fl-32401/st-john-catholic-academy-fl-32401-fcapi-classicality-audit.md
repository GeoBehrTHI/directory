## Executive Summary

St. John Catholic Academy (Panama City, FL) presents a robust Classical Education profile that aligns strongly with the Six Essential Elements framework. Public materials consistently foreground Catholic religious foundations, classical liberal arts, Great Books/primary sources, Latin, and a mission centered on virtue formation and, broadly, human flourishing. Evidence of liturgical life, daily prayer, sacramental imagination, and explicit theology-infused pedagogy anchors Element 1 (Religious and Metaphysical Foundations) as CLASSICAL. The school articulates a formation-focused purpose (virtue, wisdom) and a liberal-arts curriculum (grammar, logic, rhetoric; Great Books; Latin), supporting CLASSICAL scores in Categories 2 (Purpose and Goal) and 3 (Curriculum). Pedagogy is clearly enhanced by memory and contemplative elements in early grades (chanting, recitation, restfulness) and by rhetoric/public-speaking emphasis, but explicit high-school-level Socratic seminars are less overt in public-facing materials, yielding a Element 4 rating of SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL. Element 5 (Faculty) is CLASSICAL, with explicit statements about virtuous, skilled teachers and biographies/credentials; Element 6 (Parents & Community) shows strong alignment with parental partnership and formation (parental primacy and open engagement), yielding a SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL rating due to more limited explicit evidence of ongoing parent-formation programs in public materials. Aggregating across all six categories, the school is CLASSICAL overall, with some category-level nuance.

Key strengths

- Religious foundations: explicit Catholic theology, liturgy, daily prayer, Eucharist, and sacramental education integrated with curriculum (CLASSICAL).
- Curriculum and Great Books orientation: classical core, liberal arts scaffold (grammar/logical/rhetoric), Latin, and unabridged literature emphasis (CLASSICAL).
- Faculty: strong teacher profiles, small-class structure, and living-curriculum signals through staff bios and accreditation narrative (CLASSICAL).
- Parental partnership: parent involvement is foregrounded, with the school identifying parents as primary educators and offering family/community engagement modes (SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL).

What would move the needle (for SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL categories)

- More explicit articulation of high-school–level Socratic/seminar pedagogy and public speaking curricula in public-facing materials.
- Expanded, publicly stated parent-formation programs (reading groups, classical book clubs, family liturgy alignment) to solidify the Parents & Community category as CLASSICAL.
