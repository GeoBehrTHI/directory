## Abstract

Victory in Christ Classical Lutheran Academy demonstrates a robust classical-leaning program. Religious and metaphysical foundations are clearly central (daily chapel, Christian worldview, creedal statements) along with a formation-focused aim (servant-leaders grounded in faith and wisdom). The curriculum centers on the Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric), with integrated history/literature/theology and Latin elements; upper-level pedagogy emphasizes Socratic discussion and rhetoric, complemented by memory work and chant in the grammar stage. Faculty structure is aligned with classical models (teacher-mentors, pastoral leadership, living curriculum concept), and the school fosters strong parent and church-community partnerships.

Overall classification: CLASSICAL

- The six categories largely align with classical criteria, with Curriculum showing strong classical components but a minor gap in explicitly naming Great Books/Great Conversation, which places Element 3 at SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL rather than fully CLASSICAL. Even so, the aggregate picture is CLASSICAL due to the dominant presence of Trivium-based curriculum, Scholé/restful pedagogy, liturgical/theological grounding, and strengthened family/church partnerships.

- The school’s public-facing materials consistently show truth, goodness, and beauty realized through a Christian, classical formation; the Great Books/great-conversation explicit designation appears less prominent, but the other classical pillars (grammar/logic/rhetoric; memory work; Socratic seminars) are well represented.

What would move the needle
- To reach a fully CLASSICAL labeling in Curriculum, the school could explicitly document a formal Great Books program and a stated Great Conversation component within the curriculum; further explicit articulation of Quadrivium elements and connections to primary sources would strengthen the “Great Books” emphasis.
- Expanded public articulation of the telos of education (truth, goodness, beauty) with explicit examples and text references in course descriptions would further align with the six-element rubric.
- Additional explicit statements about the public-facing "Living Curriculum" concept and a more formalized parent formation program could further solidify category alignment.

ABSTRACT
Victory in Christ Classical Lutheran Academy presents a CLASSICAL orientation across most categories, with the strongest alignment in Religious & Metaphysical Foundations, Purpose & Goal, Pedagogy, Faculty, and Parents & Community. The Curriculum shows robust classical elements (grammar/logic/rhetoric, Omnibus integration, Latin, history/literature/theology) but lacks explicit Great Books/Great Conversation articulation, yielding a SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL designation for Element 3. The aggregate assessment remains CLASSICAL, indicating a highly classical-leaning program with room for explicit Great Books integration to achieve full alignment.
