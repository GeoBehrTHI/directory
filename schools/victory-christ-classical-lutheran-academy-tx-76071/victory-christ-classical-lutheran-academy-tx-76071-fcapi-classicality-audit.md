## Executive Summary

Victory in Christ Classical Lutheran Academy (Newark, TX, 76071) presents a robust classical framework anchored in a Lutheran Christian worldview. The public materials clearly foreground religious foundations (daily chapel, explicit statement of faith, a Christian worldview woven through all subjects) and a Trivium-based curriculum (grammar, logic, rhetoric) with an integrated history-literature-theology emphasis and a clear structure for upper-level rhetoric and logic training. The pedagogy emphasizes contemplative/restful learning (Scholé), memorization and chant in the grammar stage, and Socratic/seminar-style inquiry in the upper grades. Faculty are depicted as a core, virtuous leadership team (pastor/teacher model, principal/teacher, staff) and there is explicit emphasis on parental and church/community alignment. While the program clearly meets many hallmarks of classical education, explicit public articulation of Great Books/Great Conversation and some phrasing around education as a universal telos (beyond formation and virtue) are less overt in the materials available. Overall, the school scores CLASSICAL in aggregate, with CATEGORY 3 (Curriculum) showing a notable but not fatal gap that shades toward SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL.

Key takeaways by category:
- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: CLASSICAL. Strong, public integration of a Christian worldview, liturgical practice (chapel), and creedal/theological framing are explicit and consistent with classical criteria.
- Purpose and Goal: CLASSICAL. Formation of virtue and wisdom rooted in Christian formation, with explicit formation over mere information.
- Curriculum: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL. Core Trivium elements (grammar, logic, rhetoric) are present and integrated with history, theology, and language study; however, explicit Great Books / Great Conversation and certain liberal-arts emphases are not clearly named in public materials.
- Pedagogy: CLASSICAL. Memorization/chanting in the grammar stage; contemplative/restful blocks; Socratic seminars and rhetoric-focused opportunities in the upper school.
- Faculty: CLASSICAL. Staffing structure centers on pastor/teacher leadership, living curriculum framing, and clearly defined teacher roles.
- Parents & Community: CLASSICAL. Visible partnership with families and church connections; emphasis on parental involvement and open access to information.
