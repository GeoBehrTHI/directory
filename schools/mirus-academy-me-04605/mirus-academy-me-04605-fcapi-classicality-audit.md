## Executive Summary

Mirus Academy (Ellsworth, ME; 04605) presents a robust classical Christian program that aligns strongly with the Six Essential Elements of a Classical School. Public-facing materials consistently emphasize a Christ-centered, classical core, a Trivium-based curriculum, contemplative pedagogy alongside Socratic discourse, and a strong emphasis on virtue formation, mastery by master teachers, and active parental partnership. The admissions and handbook materials underline a deliberately classical philosophy embedded in mission, curriculum, and faculty culture, including explicit references to the Trivium, Great Books-era sensibilities, and a vision to lead culture through truth, goodness, and beauty. The school’s public narrative repeatedly foregrounds the True, the Good, and the Beautiful, and asserts a mission to mature students toward wisdom and virtue within a Christian metanarrative. Taken together, these signals place Mirus Academy solidly in the CLASSICAL category across all six rubric domains, with no significant red flags indicating a drift toward non-classical practices. Evidence of this alignment appears across multiple sources, including direct statements of faith and Christ-centered aims, Trivium-based pedagogy, and a faculty culture described as a “living curriculum” and masterful mentorship.
