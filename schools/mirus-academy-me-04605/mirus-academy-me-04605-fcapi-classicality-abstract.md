## ABSTRACT

Mirus Academy (Ellsworth, ME 04605) demonstrates a coherent and well-implemented CLASSICAL model across all six rubric categories. The Religious and Metaphysical Foundations are anchored in a Christ-centered mission and a Christian metanarrative, with explicit references to a Statement of Faith and Jesus Christ as central to formation. The School Purpose and Goal centers formation, virtue, and wisdom, with a telos to lead culture through truth, goodness, and beauty. The Curriculum centers on Trivium-based pedagogy (Grammar, Logic/Dialectic, Rhetoric) and related classical elements (memory work, Latin beginnings, and emphasis on historical and literary canon), though the public materials do not always explicitly quote Great Books; the overarching classical approach remains clear. Pedagogy spans contemplative/restful elements for the lower grades along with Socratic discussion and rhetoric training in upper grades, meeting the classical expectations for teaching methods. Faculty are portrayed as master teachers, mentors, and living curriculum—central to the school’s identity. Finally, Parents & Community are positioned as co-educators and partners in formation, with explicit parent formation opportunities and open-campus access. Collectively, the signals from the knowledge base and the school’s public materials align with the Positive Keyword Indicators in the Six Essentials Lexicon and yield CLASSICAL across all six categories.

- Overall classification: CLASSICAL
- Key supporting signals include: explicit Christ-centered education and a Statement of Faith; Trivium-based curriculum and the “three stages” pedagogy; a public emphasis on memory, chant, and rhetoric; a living-faculty and master-teacher model; and a strong, ongoing partnership with parents and the broader community.
