# Classicality Audit Report: Austin Classical School

Website audited: https://austinclassical.org 

## Executive Summary
Overall classification: CLASSICAL

Why: Austin Classical School explicitly frames its aim as the cultivation of “wisdom and virtue” through a “classical, Christian education,” anchored in the contemplation of “Truth, Goodness, and Beauty,” and employing the Trivium with stage‑appropriate pedagogy (memorization/recitation/songs in the lower grades; debate/discussion in logic; rhetoric in upper grades). These statements and practices strongly align with the Six Essential Elements framework and keyword indicators for classical schools. Evidence for faculty formation/portrait and robust parent formation is thinner in the crawl sample, but there is clear language about “co‑laboring with families,” which supports classical parent partnership. 

Method note: Scoring and keyword checks are benchmarked against the Positive/Negative Keyword Indicators in the Six Essential Elements reference set and the classicality lexicon (truth, goodness, beauty; liberal arts; Trivium/Quadrivium; Great Books; Socratic; scholé; wonder; chanting; project‑based/experiential learning, etc.)
