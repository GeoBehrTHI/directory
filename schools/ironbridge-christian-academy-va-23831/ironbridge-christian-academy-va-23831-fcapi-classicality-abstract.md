## ABSTRACT

Ironbridge Christian Academy (ICA) is a Christ-centered, classical Christian school serving preschool through elementary levels, with explicit biblical integration, a classical curriculum, and a strong family/community orientation. The six-category rubric yields a CLASSICAL ranking for Religious & Metaphysical Foundations, School Purpose & Goal, Curriculum, Faculty, and Parents & Community. Pedagogy is rated SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL due to partial evidence of Socratic/seminar-based practices and an emphasis on a more blended approach in the available materials. The school’s mission to form virtue and character within a biblical framework, and its explicit commitment to biblical instruction, chapel, and service, collectively place ICA within the classical tradition. The public materials emphasize mission, virtue, and spiritual formation, as well as a strong teacher- and parent-centered community, aligning ICA with classical education standards and practices.
