# ABSTRACT

New Covenant Christian Academy (Interlochen, MI 49643) demonstrates a robust CLASSICAL orientation in religious and community life, with explicit Evangelical/Christian theology, liturgical practices, and a mission framed as “Glorifying God through Wisdom and Virtue.” The school presents a Trivium-centered curriculum (Grammar/Logic/Rhetoric) with Latin study and Quadrivium elements in its public materials, signaling a strong classical structure, though explicit Great Books/Great Conversation emphasis is not consistently documented. Faculty are portrayed as virtuous mentors and living exemplars of the curriculum, and parents are positioned as co-educators and active partners in formation.

Category-by-category assessment highlights:

- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: CLASSICAL — clear theological grounding, liturgical practices, weekly chapel, and a Christian worldview in daily education.
- School Purpose and Goal: CLASSICAL — formation of virtue and wisdom within a faith-based framework; tools of learning emphasize independence and lifelong formation.
- Curriculum: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL — Trivium/Quadrivium and Latin are explicit; Great Books/Great Conversation are not explicitly named in public content.
- Pedagogy: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL — Trivium-based pedagogy is central, with language study; explicit Socratic/seminar memory-work signals are not clearly documented in public content.
- Faculty: CLASSICAL — teachers described as mentors and living curriculum; staff are aligned with Christian faith and content expertise.
- Parents & Community: CLASSICAL — strong partnerships with families, open access to school life, and active parental involvement.

What would move the needle (if the school is to strengthen to a fully CLASSICAL designation)

- More explicit Great Books/Great Conversation documentation within Curriculum.
- More explicit articulation of Socratic methods and scholé practices in Pedagogy, including example lesson sequences.
- Additional explicit ties between transcendentals (truth, goodness, beauty) and course-level outcomes and assessments across disciplines.

In sum, New Covenant Christian Academy exhibits strong classical Christian characteristics with clear religious foundations, formation-focused aims, and Trivium-centered pedagogy, earning an overall CLASSICAL designation with room to strengthen explicit Great Books/Great Conversation emphasis and documented classical teaching methods.
