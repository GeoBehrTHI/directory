## ABSTRACT

Overall Classification: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL

Rationale:
- Element 1 (Religious and Metaphysical Foundations) is NOT CLASSICAL due to a lack of explicit theology, liturgical life, or transcendental metaphysical grounding in public materials; the evidence centers on social-justice-oriented education and IB alignment rather than religious metaphysics.

- Element 4 (Pedagogy) is NOT CLASSICAL, with pedagogy framed by IB and inquiry-based models rather than classical Socratic seminars and rhetoric training. 
- Element 5 (Faculty) is SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL, evidenced by a living-curriculum concept and the presence of model-teacher elements within departmental content and staff structure. This aligns with classical notions of teacher exemplarity and mentorship. 
- Element 6 (Parents & Community) is SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL, supported by Families/Partnerships pages that show engagement with parents and the community in a structured manner, though not explicitly as co-teachers in the classical sense. 

Overall, the school sits in the SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL band. Concrete steps outlined in “What would move the needle” would push this toward CLASSICAL, especially through incorporation of Great Books, a formal grammar-logic-rhetoric ladder, Socratic pedagogy, and explicit liturgical/theological grounding aligned with classical formation.
