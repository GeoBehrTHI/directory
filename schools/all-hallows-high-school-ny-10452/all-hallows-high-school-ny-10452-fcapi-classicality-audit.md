## What Would Move the Needle

- Introduce explicit Great Books and Great Conversation components into the core curriculum (and map them across history, literature, and philosophy) to strengthen Category 3.
- Integrate a formal grammar-logic-rhetoric sequence (classical liberal arts ladder) within the curriculum, with explicit memorization/recitation components and a structured memorization program (Category 3 and Category 4 alignment improvements).
- Add explicit liturgical or theological/metaphysical grounding (where appropriate) to address Category 1, including communal practices and a clear metaphysical framework that complements virtue formation.
- Expand Socratic pedagogy across upper grades (Category 4) with structured Socratic seminars, debates, and rhetoric training, including student speeches and formal rhetoric work (Category 4 and Category 5 alignment).
- Deepen the “living-curriculum” model with explicit portraits of teacher-mentors and a defined teacher-philosophy for how faculty embody classical virtues (Category 5).
- Strengthen the parents-as-co-teachers narrative with structured training, book studies, and regular parent collaboration opportunities (Category 6).

These steps would move All Hallows from SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL toward CLASSICAL across the six categories and yield a stronger overall Classical classification.
