## Executive Summary

Chesterton Academy Twin Cities (Hopkins, MN 55343) presents a robust, explicitly Catholic, classical education model. Public-facing materials emphasize a transfiguring blend of Catholic faith, classical liberal arts, and character formation, with daily Mass, sacraments, catechesis, and pilgrimages integrated into school life, alongside a curriculum framed by Great-Books–oriented, Socratic, and liberal-arts aims. The school markets itself as a joyful, affordable, Catholic classical high school with a strong emphasis on virtue, wisdom, and beauty, aligning closely with the Six Essential Elements of Classical Education.

Overall classification across the six classical criteria: CLASSICAL. The school consistently foregrounds religious metaphysics (Catholic theology, liturgy, sacraments), a purpose oriented to virtue and human excellence, a liberal-arts curriculum with Great Books sensibilities, classical pedagogy (Socratic/seminar-oriented, memory work, rhetoric), a faculty modeled as mentors and living curriculum, and a strong parent/community partnership. Evidence spans daily Mass, catechesis, pilgrimages, a three-pillar model (intellect, character, spirituality), narratives about the best of what has been thought and said, and visible engagement from faculty and parents in formation and classical activities. The material suggests the school adheres closely to the classical model while remaining mindful of accessibility (tuition emphasis) and parish life.
