## ABSTRACT

Windsor Christian Academy (Eagle, PA, 19335) demonstrates a coherent CLASSICAL alignment across the six classical education categories:

- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: Strong Christian framing anchors truth and virtue as real, knowable, and God-centered, with daily Bible study and chapel, aligning with transcendental realism and a theology-grounded curriculum. No explicit secular/normative counterpoints observed.

- School Purpose and Goal: Formation, virtue, wisdom, and lifelong learning are central, with a clearly stated classical framework and Christ-centered telos. The school’s language supports a formation and liberal-arts mission rather than mere job prep.

- Curriculum: Clear liberal-arts orientation with grammar/logic/rhetoric progression; early Latin from kindergarten; history-integrated instruction; Great-Books-like reading lists in upper grades; a sense of wonder in learning. The Great Books and Trivium/Quadrivium elements are present in the public materials, though sometimes implied rather than named in every sentence.

- Pedagogy: A phase-based pedagogy (grammar, logic, rhetoric) with chant/memory work in early years, narration/recitation, and Socratic-like seminars in upper grades. The memory-based lower school and rhetoric-focused upper school align with canonical classical pedagogy.

- Faculty: Public materials emphasize model teachers, small classes, mentoring, and a living curriculum; the faculty culture appears to be character- and faith-centered, with a collaborative, supportive environment.

- Parents & Community: Open houses, tours, and family involvement opportunities, plus service initiatives (Sow Grace) demonstrate a strong partnership with families and a community-centered approach consistent with classical education.

Overall, Windsor Christian Academy is CLASSICAL in its orientation, with a well-articulated classical Christian framework across all six categories. The public materials reflect the key elements—truth-guided formation, Trivium-based pedagogy, liberal-arts curriculum, living-teacher model, and family/community partnership—that characterize a robust classical program.

Citations across the six categories anchor points:
- Religious Foundations: daily Bible and chapel; Jesus Christ-centered curriculum. 
- Purpose/Formation: lifelong learners; classical three-phase approach. 
- Curriculum: grammar/logic/rhetoric; Latin from kindergarten; Great Books-style readings. 
- Pedagogy: memory/recitation; progymnasmata; Socratic/seminal discussion in upper grades. 
- Faculty: small classes; mentors; living curriculum. 
- Parents & Community: tours/open houses; Sow Grace; parent-friendly approaches.
