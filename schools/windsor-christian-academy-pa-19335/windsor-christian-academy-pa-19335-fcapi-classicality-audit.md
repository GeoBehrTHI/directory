## Executive Summary

Windsor Christian Academy (WCA), a K–8 classical Christian school in Pennsylvania, exhibits a robust alignment with classical education as defined by the Six Essential Elements rubric. Public materials from the school emphasize a Christ-centered, liberal-arts-informed education with explicit attention to the Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric), Latin instruction from kindergarten, Great Books-oriented reading in upper grades, memorization and chant in the early years, Socratic and dialectic elements in the upper grades, a living, mentoring-leaning faculty, and an explicit partnership with families in formation. Chapel and daily Bible study frame the religious underpinnings, while the Three-Phase (grammar–logic–rhetoric) progression, Great Books readings (e.g., Mere Christianity, The Hobbit, Pilgrim’s Progress, Shakespeare, etc.), Latin from early grades, and memory-based pedagogy anchor the curriculum and pedagogy. The school also foregrounds parental involvement and community service (e.g., Sow Grace), aligning with the “Parents & Community” dimension of classical education.

### Element 4: Pedagogy or Teaching Methods — CLASSICAL

Evidence
- The site clearly describes a three-phase pedagogy corresponding to the Trivium:
  - Grammar stage: memorization through songs, chants, and rhymes; a focus on memory and formation. 
  - Logic stage: emphasis on argument, debate, and defense of views; development of logic and critical thinking in the Middle School. 
  - Rhetoric stage: emphasis on rhetoric, speaking, and performance (public speaking, Shakespeare readings, musicals). 
- Additional pedagogy cues include:
  - Memorization/narration and chant in lower grades; progymnasmata in some grades (e.g., Progymnasmata in 4th grade). 
  - Socratic-like discussion and seminars are described at the upper levels (logic/dialectic in middle school; rhetoric in upper grades). The Middle School description emphasizes defending views, argumentation, and logical discourse as core to upper-level teaching. 
  - Integrated, “living curriculum” approach and the emphasis on memory work, catechesis, and liturgical-integration pedagogy align with classical pedagogy indicators in the Six Essential Elements lexicon. 

Positive/Negative keyword analysis
- Positive keywords present: Socratic dialogue/dialectic (as reflected in the logic-stage emphasis), narration/recitation, chanting, copywork, progymnasmata, memory work, catechesis, liturgical calendar integration, and rhetoric-training (speech/declamation). The site’s pedagogy content aligns well with classical pedagogy indicators listed in the lexicon. 
- Negative keywords present: the site does not foreground a project-based-only or purely experiential constructivist pedagogy; rather it emphasizes a memory/recitation-dominant approach in early years and dialectic/seminar approaches in later years.

Why this rating
- The explicit phase-based structure (grammar → logic → rhetoric), along with listening to memory-based practices (chant/memory work) and rhetoric training, marks a strong CLASSICAL pedagogy alignment. The presence of upper-grade Socratic discussion/seminars and a memory-centric lower-grammar approach supports CLASSICAL status for Pedagogy.

Citations
- Pedagogy anchors: grammar-stage chant/memory; logic-stage debate; rhetoric-stage public speaking; progymnasmata; memory work; catechesis.
