## Executive Summary

First Baptist Academy TN 37849 presents a clearly classical, Christian-influenced approach to education. The public materials emphasize a biblical worldview, a triadic Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric), and liturgical/community practices (chapel, daily assemblies, worship) that align with the religious/metaphysical and communal aspects of classical education. The curriculum is described as integrated with biblical faith, and upper-level pedagogy centers on rhetoric and inquiry-oriented discussion within a historically rooted framework. While the school demonstrates strong alignment across most classical criteria, explicit, widely-prominent statements about certain elements (e.g., a fully articulated “living curriculum” or formal, explicit Socratic/Seminar practices in upper grades beyond the Trivium framework) are less conspicuous in the online materials. On balance, the school is best categorized as CLASSICAL, with some room for further explicit articulation in a few categories.
