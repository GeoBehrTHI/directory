## Executive Summary

Logos Academy Harrisburg (Logos Academy PA 17102) presents as a Christian, independent school that explicitly foregrounds a classical-leaning, formation-focused approach. Public materials frame education as a holistic formation of mind and soul, emphasizing Christ-centered character, virtue, and service, with a strong commitment to accessibility through scholarships and community partnerships. The school markets a private, holistic, trauma-informed environment and notes open-enrollment that welcomes students from any faith background, while repeatedly tying its mission to faith-informed aims of truth, goodness, and beauty.

Overall, the school demonstrates clear classical orientation in spirit and aims (formation, virtue, intellect, public-facing language of “classical education,” and a private school identity). However, explicit, textbook-level classical curriculum markers (notably the Seven Liberal Arts, Great Books, Great Conversation, and explicit use of grammar, logic, and rhetoric as a structuring framework) are not consistently evidenced in public materials. Given the balance of evidence across the six categories, Logos Academy Harrisburg is best characterized as CLASSICAL, with meaningful classical elements present and a few gaps that prevent a wholesale “fully classical” label in every dimension.

Key insights by category (high level):

- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: Strong Christian grounding, explicitly Christ-centered, with language of formation through faith. Evidence of theology-inflected aims but not overt liturgical practice on the public site. CLASSICAL, with high confidence.
- School Purpose and Goal: Formation of intellect and character, virtue, and human flourishing foregrounded; service and compassionate education highlighted. CLASSICAL.
- Curriculum: Public materials emphasize classical framing and private/holistic aims but do not consistently list Great Books, Trivium/Quadrivium, or direct Great Conversation commitments. SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL.
- Pedagogy: Mixed signals; the site foregrounds compassionate, trauma-informed, community-oriented practices and Christ-centered ethos, but explicit Socratic/seminar-based pedagogy is not clearly evidenced in public-facing content. NOT CLASSICAL (on explicit pedagogical method signals).
- Faculty: Strong portrayal of teachers as caring, known by name, and forming the living curriculum; donor and parent-partner language appears. CLASSICAL.
- Parents & Community: Explicit partner-education language, parent involvement expectations, and open-campus/visit-friendly posture; substantial emphasis on parent partnership and community engagement. CLASSICAL.
