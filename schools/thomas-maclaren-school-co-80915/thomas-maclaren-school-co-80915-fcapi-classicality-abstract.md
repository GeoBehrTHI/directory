## ABSTRACT

Thomas MacLaren School (Colorado Springs, CO 80915) demonstrates a predominantly classical orientation grounded in liberal-arts education, Great Books/Great Conversation, Socratic pedagogy, and a sequenced K–12 curriculum with no formal tracking. The school foregrounds formation, virtue, and wisdom as central aims, with a strong emphasis on memory work, recitation, and rhetoric, aligned with classical pedagogical methods. In Categories 1–6, the school presents a robust classical profile in categories 2, 4, and 5, with strong signals in 3 (Curriculum) and 6 (Parents & Community) that are tempered by nuanced considerations in 1 (Religious and Metaphysical Foundations) and 3 (Curriculum) due to references to Core Knowledge and related curricular signals. The overall assessment leans CLASSICAL, with some areas that are SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL due to the mixed curricular signals and the relatively tempered signaling of explicit theological and parent-formation commitments. To move toward a uniformly CLASSICAL profile, the school could more explicitly foreground Trivium/Quadrivium structures, convert Core Knowledge references toward canonical Great Books framing, and strengthen explicit parent-formation and theological/metaphysical signaling within public materials.
