# Classicality Audit Report: Berean Christian Academy (Katy, TX)
Website audited: https://bereanchristianacademy.org 

## Executive Summary
Overall classification: CLASSICAL

Berean Christian Academy presents strong, explicit alignment with classical, Christian education across mission, goals, and core academic model. Public materials prominently emphasize truth, goodness, and beauty; the cultivation of wisdom and virtue; and the Trivium and Seven Liberal Arts. Upper school pedagogy includes formal logic, debate, Harkness-style conversation, and a senior thesis capstone. The school also states a partnership with parents and lists memberships in ACCS and SCL. Evidence for liturgical practices, scholé/restful learning, and lower-school chanting/singing is not explicit in the crawl, but nothing suggests progressive/constructivist or workforce-first framing.

- Strongest signals: “Christ-centered worldview,” “Truth, Goodness, and Beauty,” “seven liberal arts,” “Grammar/Logic/Rhetoric,” “Latin,” “great books,” “formal logic and debate,” “Harkness Table,” “Senior Thesis,” and “partnership between faculty and parents” (mission/profile pages) , plus “Classical education … trains the mind … seeking out truth, goodness, and beauty” (school’s classical-education explainer) .
- Not observed in crawl: explicit references to chapel/liturgy, scholé/restful learning, or lower-school chanting/singing.

## What would move the needle (targeted improvements)
Because several categories scored SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL on coverage, the following public-facing clarifications would strengthen the evidence base:
- Curriculum
  - Add explicit “history” language (not just “social studies”) and highlight chronology, primary sources, and Western civilization survey to match the framework’s positive signals .
  - Include “wonder” as an explicit curricular aim (e.g., “cultivating wonder”) to satisfy the framework’s Integration and Wonder dimension .
- Pedagogy
  - Publish lower school practices that include “memorization, chanting, and singing” and describe routine review cycles; name “recitation” explicitly .
  - Add language about “restful learning/scholé” or contemplative learning rhythms to demonstrate balance (not merely rigor) .
- Faculty
  - Publish a “portrait of a teacher” and/or “portrait of a graduate/student” describing modeled virtues and collegial culture, which are positives in the faculty element .
- Religious/Liturgical life
  - If applicable, describe chapel, daily prayer, hymnody, or the liturgical calendar to establish liturgical practices publicly .
