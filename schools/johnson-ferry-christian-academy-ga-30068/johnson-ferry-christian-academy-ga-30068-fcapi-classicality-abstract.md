## ABSTRACT

Johnson Ferry Christian Academy (JFCA) demonstrates a strong CLASSICAL orientation overall, anchored by a clear Religious and Metaphysical Foundations (Christ-centered, biblical worldview, Scripture-guided instruction) and robust Parents & Community engagement (parents as co-teachers, home/Church extension). The Faculty category shows a solid CLASSICAL emphasis on mentors and living curriculum, while the School Purpose and Goal, though clearly formation-focused and Christ-centered, reveals a SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL alignment due to its emphasis on godly character and leadership within a faith context rather than explicit classical aims or canonical Great Books. Curriculum and Pedagogy, as publicly described, lean toward a Christian hybrid-model with limited explicit classical content (absence of Seven Liberal Arts, Great Books, or Socratic pedagogy) and thus fall into NOT CLASSICAL for Category 3 and Category 4. The aggregate assessment across the six categories is CLASSICAL, though with notable opportunities to deepen canonical classical elements (curriculum content and pedagogy) to achieve a fully classical program.

- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: CLASSICAL
  - Evidence: Christ-centered education, Scripture-based instruction, worship, discipleship, and theology-influenced formation. 

- School Purpose and Goal: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL
  - Evidence: Formation of godly character and leadership within a biblically grounded framework; home/Church extension and parental partnership. 

- Curriculum: NOT CLASSICAL
  - Evidence: Hybrid Christian model with biblically integrated content; no explicit classical canon (Great Books, trivium/quadrivium) referenced in public materials. 

- Pedagogy: NOT CLASSICAL
  - Evidence: Described hybrid schedule with faith-based instruction; no explicit classical pedagogical methods (Socratic seminars, memorization recitation) publicly documented. 

- Faculty: CLASSICAL
  - Evidence: Mentorship-oriented faculty culture, living curriculum feel, teacher leadership. 

- Parents & Community: CLASSICAL
  - Evidence: Strong parent partnership, co-teacher model, and open campus; active family formation and home-school alignment.
