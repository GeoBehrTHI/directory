# Classicality Audit: Berean Christian Academy (Katy, TX)

Website audited: https://www.bereanchristianacademy.org

Executive Summary
- Overall classification: CLASSICAL
- Why: The school explicitly frames education around Truth, Goodness, and Beauty; identifies as a classical Christian school; uses the seven liberal arts (Trivium and Quadrivium elements in practice); employs Socratic/Harkness discussion, logic, debate, Latin, Great Books, and a 12th-grade Senior Thesis; and emphasizes partnership with parents, including parent formation requirements. These align strongly with the Six Essential Elements framework and its positive keyword indicators.  
- Key strengthening areas: Public evidence of liturgical/chapel life, explicit lower-school memory/chant/recitation practices, and published “portrait of a teacher”/faculty formation statements would further solidify the case.
- Sources guiding this audit: “Six Essential Elements of a Classical School” (positive/negative keyword indicators) and the classicality lexicon, used here to anchor keyword analysis and scoring.
