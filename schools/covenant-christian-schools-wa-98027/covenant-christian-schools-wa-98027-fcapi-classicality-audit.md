## Executive Summary

Covenant Christian Schools Wa 98027 demonstrates a robust classical orientation across most of the six classical criteria. Public-facing materials emphasize a Christian, classical framework with explicit attention to virtue formation, theology, and liturgical/community life. The school presents a clearly stated purpose focused on character formation, wisdom, and the ability to articulate Christian faith. Pedagogy leans into logic and rhetoric, with high school offerings explicitly listing Rhetoric, Logic, and Literature as core components. The lower/middle school materials reference grammar, logic, and rhetoric as evolving stages, aligning with classical pedagogy. Faculty are described as living models of virtue and mentors, and there is explicit emphasis on parent and community partnership. The curriculum includes grammar–logic–rhetoric foundations and a history-centered approach taught through a Christian worldview, though explicit, sustained mention of Great Books/Great Conversation in public materials is not evident. Overall, the school is classified as CLASSICAL.
