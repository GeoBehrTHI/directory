## Executive Summary

Covenant High School (Tacoma, WA, 98465) presents a strongly classical, Christian, college-preparatory program with explicit emphasis on formation of character, Virtue, and intellectual rigor. Public materials consistently foreground Christ-centered education, a classical pedagogical posture (Socratic discussion, logical reasoning, emphasis on “great works” and a trans-civic historical consciousness), and robust parental/community partnership. Taken together, the school aligns very closely with the classical model in most dimensions, particularly Religious & Metaphysical Foundations, Curriculum, Pedagogy, and Faculty. Public materials also emphasize formation over mere information, a hallmark of classical aims. Based on the rubric’s six categories, Covenant High School is CLASSICAL across the board, with a few nuances (e.g., explicit Great Books language sometimes rendered as “great works” in site copy) that still map cleanly to the classical canon and its aims.

Key insights:

- The school explicitly places Christ at the center, emphasizes “Holding Christ over all,” and presents a mission to deepen relationship with Jesus while forming students to serve God and neighbor. This anchors Element 1 (Religious & Metaphysical Foundations) and Element 2 (Purpose & Goal) in a strongly classical frame. Evidence: About page and Mission text.
- The curriculum and pedagogy foreground the Trivium-like emphasis (logic, rhetoric, reading broadly; Socratic discussion; focus on great works) and a classical, liberal-arts orientation. Evidence: “Fostering Socratic discussion, logical thinking, and skilled argument and analysis, with a focus on great works”; “We teach our students to think logically, read broadly, communicate eloquently, and appreciate artistic beauty.”
- There is explicit attention to formation (truth, goodness, beauty; moral imagination; the Logos; Virtue) in the Portrait of the Graduate and mission statements. Evidence: Portrait of the Graduate text; Mission statements.
- Parent and community partnership is visible (parent associations, admissions process, community events, tours). Evidence: Admissions/Parent association materials; tuition/fees sections.
