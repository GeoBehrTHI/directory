## Executive Summary

Covenant Christian Academy (Colleyville, TX 76034) presents a consistently classical Christian profile across its public materials. Public materials repeatedly frame education as a formation of wisdom and virtue within a biblically grounded, theologically informed, three-phase model (grammar, logic, rhetoric). The school foregrounds a Christian worldview, biblical faith integration, and liturgical/community practices (chapel, worship, choir) as core elements of its educational approach. Evidence from the site shows explicit references to biblical truth, a Christ-centered community, and a triadic maturation model (grammar–logic–rhetoric) with Latin study and rhetoric training. The site also publicly emphasizes parent partnership, a house system, and strong student life traditions, which align with the classical lexicon’s positive indicators for formation through community.

In short, Covenant Christian Academy clearly meets the six-element classical rubric in practice, with robust alignment in Religious/Metaphysical Foundations, School Purpose/Goal, Curriculum, Pedagogy, Faculty, and Parents/Community. The balance of positive indicators substantially outweighs any negative indicators in the available materials, yielding an overall classification of CLASSICAL.

Key insights:
- Strong religious/metaphysical underpinnings: biblical truth, the Bible as authoritative, liturgical and communal practices (chapel, worship), and a clearly Christian mission.
- Purpose geared toward formation: emphasis on virtue, wisdom, and a classical paideia approach (grammar–logic–rhetoric) with a biblically anchored worldview.
- Curriculum anchored in the Trivium and classical aims: grammar/logic/rhetoric, Latin, and a focus on foundational classics; explicit articulation of “grammar, logic, rhetoric” across the curriculum.
- Pedagogy features a mix of contemplative/restful elements and discussion-driven practice in upper grades, alongside memory-work and liturgical/arts integration.
- Faculty presentations emphasize mentorship, virtue, and living curriculum; leadership pages and staff bios illustrate a campus-wide ethos aligned with classical formation.
- Parents/Community engagement is foregrounded as partnership, with parent councils, information packets, and open campus orientation.

Cited sources reflecting these patterns include Mission/ Vision and Statement of Faith, the Academics page describing grammar/logic/rhetoric and biblical worldview, admissions pages detailing the classical approach and university-prep trajectory, and the liturgical/community orientation evidenced by chapels, worship ensembles, and choir programs.
