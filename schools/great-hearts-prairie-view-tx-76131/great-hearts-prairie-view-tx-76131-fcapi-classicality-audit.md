## Executive Summary

Great Hearts Prairie View (Fort Worth, TX 76131) presents a strong classical-education profile across most rubric categories. Based on its publicly shared materials (curriculum, pedagogy, mission, and community engagement) and drawing on the Six Essential Elements of a Classical School rubric, the school is generally CLASSICAL with Category 1 (Religious and Metaphysical Foundations) showing only partial alignment. The aggregate assessment places Great Hearts Prairie View solidly in CLASSICAL territory, driven by explicit commitments to the liberal arts, Great Books, Socratic pedagogy, character formation, dedicated faculty, and active parent/community partnership.

Key drivers of CLASSICAL status:

- Explicit emphasis on Truth, Goodness, and Beauty as guiding ideals, formation-minded aims, and moral education (mission statements, outcomes, and the school’s public narrative) .
- Liberal arts curriculum with grammar, logic, rhetoric, Latin, and a strong Great Books/Western-tradition emphasis (Curriculum pages, Classics to Keep, Humane Letters Seminars) .
- Pedagogy centered on Socratic dialogue, seminars, and memory-based practices (Socratic Method, Humane Letters Seminars, classical recitation/memory culture) .
- Faculty as models of virtue and living curriculum; teachers described as mentors and moral exemplars, with a clear “portrait of a teacher” in the materials .
- Strong parent/community partnership rhetoric and practices (parents as co-teachers, formation of families, open campus/visits) .

Category-by-category signals (keyword analysis below) show that every category except one meets the core indicators very well; the overall mix supports a CLASSICAL classification for the school as a whole. See the category snapshots for detailed keyword evidence and the quantitative keyword analysis.

Citations: Truth/Goodness/Beauty and formation language appear in the mission and school narrative, including “pursuit of truth, goodness, and beauty” and formation language . ## Overall Assessment

Aggregate classification: CLASSICAL

Rationale: Across categories 2–6, the Great Hearts Prairie View materials demonstrate robust alignment with classical education principles: a formation-centered purpose; a liberal-arts-curriculum anchored in Great Books and the Western tradition; Socratic pedagogy and memory-based practices; faculty as moral exemplars; and a strong, ongoing parent/community partnership. Category 1 (Religious and Metaphysical Foundations) shows partial alignment—centered on Truth, Goodness, and Beauty and a strong formation/virtue emphasis but lacking explicit theology, liturgical practices, or formal worship in public-facing materials. Taken together, the school’s profile is CLASSICAL with one area of partial alignment, thus the aggregate assessment remains CLASSICAL.

Citations for the aggregate view draw from mission and curriculum materials, pedagogy statements, faculty descriptions, and parent/community language cited above .

Note on the knowledge-base alignment

- The evaluation leverages the Six Essential Elements of a Classical School (1) as the primary rubric backbone (e.g., Truth/Goodness/Beauty; formation vs. information; classical curriculum; Socratic pedagogy; living curriculum; parental partnership). The Great Hearts Prairie View materials consistently align with the Positive Keyword set across categories 2–6 and partially with Category 1, enabling a predominantly CLASSICAL classification with a note of partial alignment in the religious/metaphysical foundation area.
