## ABSTRACT

St. Monica Catholic School (SMS) on Mercer Island, WA 98040, presents a robust CLASSICAL education model grounded in Catholic theology and the Western intellectual tradition. Across six rubric categories, SMS demonstrates:

- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: Explicit Catholic grounding, liturgical life, catechesis, and theology, aligned with classical transcendentals (truth, goodness, beauty) and a sacramental worldview.
- School Purpose and Goal: Formation in virtue and wisdom; a telos-oriented aim consistent with classical education, rather than job-prep.
- Curriculum: Liberal arts core (grammar, logic, rhetoric) with Great Books, Latin, Western Civilization, and a time-period-based curriculum, integrated with Catholic studies.
- Pedagogy: Classical methods including Socratic seminars, narration, memory work, and rhetoric; a balanced approach that includes contemplative aspects.
- Faculty: Living curriculum with mentors and a collaborative, faith-filled, and expert faculty.
- Parents & Community: Strong partnerships with parents as co-educators, parent formation opportunities, volunteerism, and open-campus accessibility.

Overall, SMS is CLASSICAL with clear signals in each category of the Six Essential Elements rubric, supported by a public materials corpus and site content detailing classical pedagogy, Great Books, and Catholic formation.
