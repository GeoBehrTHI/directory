## Executive Summary

St. Monica Catholic School (SMS), located on Mercer Island, WA (ZIP 98040), presents itself publicly as a PreK–12 Catholic classical liberal arts school. Across its public-facing materials, SMS foregrounds the Catholic intellectual tradition, Western Civilization, and a classical curriculum that centers on the trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric) and the great books, integrated with liturgical life and catechesis. The school emphasizes Socratic seminars, narration, and a pedagogy oriented toward truth, goodness, and beauty, with explicit focus on formation of character and virtue rather than merely information transfer. Faculty are portrayed as mentors and the living curriculum, and there is a strong emphasis on parental partnership and community life within a Catholic framework. The overall signal from SMS is CLASSICAL across all six categories of the Six Essential Elements rubric.

Key textual anchors include SMS descriptions of itself as a Catholic classical school offering a liberal arts curriculum grounded in the Educational Plan of St. Jerome, engagement with Great Books and Western Tradition, and explicit pedagogy such as Socratic seminars and narrations. The school also stresses liturgical life, catechesis, and a shared mission with parents as co-educators. These elements appear consistently from elementary through high school materials and admissions pages, and are reinforced by testimonials and program descriptions on the school site.
