# ABSTRACT

- Coram Deo Academy (Kennewick, WA 99336) demonstrates a strong classical Christian orientation through its mission, vision, and curricular framing. Public materials emphasize a Christ-centered education, a Trivium-based structure (Grammar/Logic/Rhetoric), and formation-oriented aims focused on truth, goodness, and beauty, aligning well with core classical criteria. The school explicitly frames education as formation (virtue, wisdom) and foregrounds family partnerships and community involvement as central facets of its classical program. Evidence includes explicit statements about Jesus Christ, Scripture, and Coram Deo as organizing ideas, the Grammar-Logic-Rhetoric stages, senior rhetoric work, and a robust parent/community engagement model (including a volunteer policy and multiple family-facing events). The school’s campus materials also show Shakespeare/Great Books-related content and classical pedagogy signals, though some elements (e.g., explicit Quadrivium articulation, universal Great Books integration, and fully systematized Scholé-based pedagogy across all grades) are less explicit in public pages.
