## ABSTRACT

St Peter the Apostle Catholic Classical School TX 76108 demonstrates a clear Catholic classical orientation with strong religious foundations, virtue-centered mission, and active parental/community engagement. Public materials emphasize Truth, Beauty, and Goodness, liturgical life, and formation in virtue, aligning with Classical Education’s Religious and Metaphysical Foundations and School Purpose/Goal. The Curriculum exhibits core classical strands (grammar, logic, Latin, Western Civilization) and a unified Catholic worldview, though explicit Great Books / Great Conversation language is not clearly documented in the public site copy. Pedagogy signals are less explicit about the full spectrum of classical teaching methods (e.g., Socratic discussion, scholé) in public materials, placing Pedagogy as SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL. Faculty signals are present in spirit (teacher formation and living-education ideals) but are not consistently explicit in public pages, yielding a SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL rating for Faculty. Parent and Community engagement is robust and clearly CLASSICAL, with parochial identity, volunteering, and family formation integrated into school life. Aggregate assessment across the six categories places the school as SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL overall.

Key evidence anchors:
- Religious & Metaphysical Foundations: truth/goodness/beauty; Catholic theology; liturgical life (Adoration, Mass, daily prayer). 
- Purpose & Goal: formation in virtue; holistic education grounded in Catholic tradition. 
- Curriculum: grammar, logic, Latin, Western Civilization; integrated Catholic curriculum. 
- Pedagogy: classical framing present, but explicit pedagogy methods less visible in public materials. 
- Faculty: formation-oriented language present in classical ideals; explicit “living curriculum” signals are not consistently documented in public text. 
- Parents & Community: strong partnership with families and parish connections; open opportunities for parent involvement.
