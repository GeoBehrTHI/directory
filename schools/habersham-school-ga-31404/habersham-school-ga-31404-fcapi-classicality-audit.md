## Executive Summary

Habersham School GA 31404 (Savannah, GA) presents a robust classical Christian orientation across its public-facing materials. The school explicitly describes itself as a PK3–12 classical, Christ-centered program rooted in the classical trinity of grammar, logic, and rhetoric, with a liturgical rhythm and a covenantal partnership with families. Public materials emphasize formation, virtue, and the cultivation of wisdom within a Christian frame, and they foreground practices such as Harkness-style discussions, senior capstone work, Latin study, and a three-phase learning model. Evidence from the school’s materials aligns with key classical indicators in the Six Essential Elements framework (especially in religious foundations, curriculum, and pedagogy), and it shows substantial alignment in faculty and parent/community engagement as well.

- Purpose and Goal: CLASSICAL
- Curriculum: CLASSICAL

Aggregate assessment: CLASSICAL

Key insights:
- The school’s mission foregrounds Christian faith, theology, liturgy, and imago Dei concepts, and it integrates these into its curriculum and culture (Evidence: mission statements, liturgy, Christ-centered framing). 
- The curriculum explicitly follows the classical triad (grammar, logic, rhetoric) and includes Latin study, senior capstone work, and Harkness-style discourse, indicating a strong alignment with core classical curricula and pedagogy. 
- Faculty profiles and professional development reflect a classical formation ethos (e.g., advanced degrees among faculty; living curriculum concept) and a teacher-lead culture.
