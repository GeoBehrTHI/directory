## Executive Summary

Imago Dei Classical School Wisconsin (53144) presents a strongly classical Christian program anchored in the Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric), Great Books and the Great Conversation, formation of character (virtue, wisdom, beauty), and liturgical, communal practice. Public materials consistently emphasize a Christian enculturation framework—formation over information, truth-goodness-beauty as real and knowable, and a partnership with parents in educating children. The public-facing content also highlights a living, mentorship-focused faculty, a pedagogy that includes memorization, narration, chant, and Socratic discussions in the upper grades, and a commitment to catechesis and liturgical rhythms. Taken together, these elements align closely with classical education norms and signal a CLASSICAL rating across categories. Across all six categories, the school demonstrates clear fidelity to the classical rubric, with plentiful textual evidence for formation, Great Books, Trivium pedagogy, liturgical and catechetical practices, and robust parental/community partnership.
