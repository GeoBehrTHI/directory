## Executive Summary

Chesterton Academy of St. Louis, MO (63146) presents a clearly classical, Catholic, high-school program anchored in a tripartite vision—Intellect, Character, and Spirituality—that foregrounds formation over mere information. Evidence from the school’s own materials demonstrates a robust religious-metaphysical orientation (Incarnation, daily Mass, liturgical culture, culture-of-life ethos), a stated goal of formation and virtue, a curriculum described as liberal-arts oriented with a commitment to the Great Conversation and the tools of classical pedagogy (Socratic seminar, memory work, house system), and a faculty and parent/community structure aligned with classical ideals (faculty as living curriculum, active parental partnerships, community events and symposia). The public materials emphasize truth, goodness, beauty; insist on formation in virtue and wisdom; and consistently tie learning to faith, worship, and sacramental life. Taken together, Chesterton Academy of St. Louis aligns strongly with classical education principles across all six categories.

Overall classification: CLASSICAL.
