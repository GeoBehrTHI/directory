## Abstract

Founders Classical Academy – Conroe presents a robust classical-education orientation, with a strong curriculum emphasis on the Trivium, Great Books, and a Western-tradition framework. Evidence from the public site shows explicit focus on virtue, formation, and a liberal-arts-centered mission, with Trivium elements (grammar/phonics; logic; rhetoric), Greek/Latin, and canonical Great Books in the curriculum. The Parents & Community narrative is particularly strong, highlighting a genuine partnership with parents and a culture of parent formation.

However, the site has limited explicit religious-theological or liturgical language, and pedogogical/faculty narratives that fully realize the classical model (Socratic pedagogy, Scholé, living-curriculum faculty archetypes) are not clearly articulated in the available public content. As such, the school falls into SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL overall, with Category 2 (Purpose and Goal), Category 3 (Curriculum), and Category 6 (Parents & Community) more solidly CLASSICAL, while Category 1 (Religious & Metaphysical Foundations), Category 4 (Pedagogy), and Category 5 (Faculty) show partial alignment.
