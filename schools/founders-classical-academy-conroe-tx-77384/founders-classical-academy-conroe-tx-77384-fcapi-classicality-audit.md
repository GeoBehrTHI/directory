## Executive Summary

Founders Classical Academy – Conroe (77384) demonstrates strong alignment with classical education tenets, notably in curriculum emphasis on Great Books, the Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric), and a West- traditions-centered liberal-arts approach. Public materials on the school’s site highlight virtues-based formation, a culture of inquiry, a knowledge-rich curriculum, explicit grammar/logic/rhetoric instruction, and Greek/Latin as foundational languages, all of which map closely to classical criteria.

However, certain religious-metaphysical foundations elements (explicit theology, liturgical/c communal practices) are less evidenced on the public materials, and explicit articulation of Socratic pedagogy or a fully articulated “living curriculum” by faculty is not clearly stated in the site content itself. Taken together, the school shows a robust classical orientation, with some gaps in explicit religious-liturgical language and certain pedagogical narratives. Overall assessment: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL, with clear strengths in curriculum and values, and room for fuller alignment in religious-metaphysical language and pedagogy.
