## Executive Summary

Mars Hill Academy (MHA) presents a consistently classical, explicitly Christian model of education. Public materials show a robust integration of classical method (grammar, logic, rhetoric), liberal arts emphasis, and a biblical worldview anchored in explicit theology (e.g., Westminster Confession; Lost Tools of Learning). The school foregrounds transtemporal ideals such as truth, goodness, and beauty, and emphasizes formation of character alongside intellectual achievement. It also centers parental partnership, liturgical rhythms, and a communal life rooted in Christian witness. Overall, the school aligns strongly with the six elements of classical education across categories, with clear evidence of Great Books/Reading canon, memory-oriented pedagogy, teachers as living curriculum, and parent partnership. Consequently, the aggregate classification is CLASSICAL.

Key across-category patterns
- Religious/metaphysical foundations are explicit and theological, with a biblically centered, Christ-focused framework and doctrinal touchpoints (Westminster Confession; biblical interpretation as core to instruction). Evidence: explicit statements about Christian distinctiveness, biblical worldview, and theological grounding.  
- Purpose/goals center formation, virtue, wisdom, and human flourishing rather than job-readiness alone. Evidence: formation/telos language, emphasis on truth/beauty, and the goal of wise, virtuous graduates. 
- Curriculum emphasizes the liberal arts (grammar, logic, rhetoric; Latin/Greek/Hebrew; Great Books; senior thesis). Evidence: explicit Trivium/Quadrivium framing and advanced classical coursework. 
- Pedagogy includes contemplative/restful elements (scholé-like rhythm) alongside Socratic/seminar-style upper-level work and rhetoric training in later years. Evidence: mentions of rhetoric, senior theses, and a pedagogical approach rooted in classical methods. 
- Faculty described as model teachers; living curriculum; strong mentoring and partnership with students. Evidence: faculty pages, stories of teachers modeling virtues, and references to teachers as the core of the living curriculum. 
- Parents & Community emphasize partnership with parents, parent formation, and open campus visits. Evidence: explicit statements about partnering with parents, in loco parentis, and parent formation activities. 

Abstract classification: CLASSICAL

### Element 4: School Pedagogy or Teaching Methods — CLASSICAL

Evidence
- The materials describe a pedagogy that blends restful, contemplative elements with rigorous rhetoric and Socratic-like discourse at the upper levels. The sources indicate that lower grades involve singing/memory and recitation-related practices; upper grades employ seminars, debates, and rhetorical training (including public speaking). The Schole/Scholé-inspired language appears in the broader classical-learning literature referenced on the site, indicating a pedagogy that values rest, contemplation, and memory. 
- Explicit statements about the classical method emphasize a memory-rich approach (grammar stage, recitation, proggyma) and a structured progression toward rhetorical mastery and public speaking. The Rhetoric School literature and the Senior Thesis program illustrate upper-level oral argument and persuasion as a core practice. 
- Note: The site also emphasizes engagement with literature, debate, and worldview topics (Worldview Summit), consistent with classical pedagogy. 

Positive/Negative Keyword Analysis
- Positive keywords present: Socratic discussion/seminars; rhetoric; memory/recitation; singing/chanting in early grades; restful/scholé pedagogy; public speaking; debates; master-teacher model (as implicit through faculty literature). 
- Negative keywords present: contextual references to project-based learning as a dominant approach in some modern schools; Mars Hill materials do not position project-based learning as their primary method (rather, they center classical methods and memory-based recall). The presence of “project-based learning” as a negative indicator is not evidenced as a public stance of MHA (the materials lean classical rather than constructivist/case-based only). 

Why this rating
