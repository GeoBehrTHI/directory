## ABSTRACT

Mars Hill Academy presents a coherent CLASSICAL profile across all six evaluation categories. The Religious and Metaphysical Foundations are rooted in a robust Christian-theological framework (Biblical worldview; Westminster Confession; Lost Tools of Learning). The School Purpose and Goal emphasize formation, virtue, wisdom, and the teleology of classical education over mere information or job-preparation. The Curriculum centers on the liberal arts (grammar, logic, rhetoric; Quadrivium hints; Latin/Greek/Hebrew; Great Books; Senior Thesis) and integrates faith with the humanities and sciences. Pedagogy combines contemplative/restful learning (Scholé) with rigorous classical practices (Socratic dialogue/seminars, memorization/recitation, and rhetoric). Faculty are presented as model teachers, mentors, and living curriculum, while Parents & Community are positioned as co-educators and partners in formation with accessible visits and family learning opportunities. This alignment with Positive Keyword Indicators in the Six Essential Elements document supports CLASSICAL status with strong evidence of the Great Conversation, liturgical/communal rhythms, and a robust Classical-Christian ecology.
