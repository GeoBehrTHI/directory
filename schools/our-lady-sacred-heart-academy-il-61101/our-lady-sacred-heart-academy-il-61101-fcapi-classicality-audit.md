## Executive Summary

Our Lady of the Sacred Heart Academy (OLSHA) in Rockford, IL 61101 presents a clearly classical orientation across its publicly documented mission, pedagogy, and curriculum. The school operates as a Pre-K–12 Catholic classical school within the Chesterton Schools Network, emphasizing the liberal arts, Great Books, Socratic inquiry, memory work, Latin, and liturgical Catholic life. Public materials consistently articulate formation of virtue and wisdom through singularly classical aims (truth, goodness, beauty) and integrate theology, liturgy, and worship as foundational to daily learning. Evidence indicates strong alignment with classical benchmarks in religious foundations, purpose/goal, curriculum, pedagogy, and, to a substantial extent, faculty and parental/community partnership. The aggregate assessment is CLASSICAL, with robust signals in each category and minimal to no explicit evidence of non-classical framing in the materials reviewed.

Key indicators across the six categories include: liturgical life and Catholic doctrine embedded in daily routines; explicit statements about forming saints and leaders through a Catholic classical program; the documented use of Great Books, the Trivium/Quadrivium framework, Socratic dialogue, and memory work; and a modeled faculty culture aligned with a living curriculum and mentorship. The public content also reveals family and community engagement through Catholic formation, school events, and homeschool options, aligning with classical expectations in Parents & Community.
