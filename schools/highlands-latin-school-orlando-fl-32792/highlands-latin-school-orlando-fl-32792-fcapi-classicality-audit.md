## Executive Summary

Highlands Latin School Orlando (HLS Orlando) presents a clearly Classical orientation in its public materials, with explicit emphasis on classical language study, the liberal arts, and a Christian, formation-centered approach. The school identifies itself as a classical, Christian institution using Memoria Press curricula and aligning with the Classical Latin School Association. It foregrounds the Good, the True, and the Beautiful as central aims and embeds Christian frames (love of the Lord) across admissions, academics, and community life. However, while the public materials strongly articulate classical aims and virtues, some elements typically emphasized in a full classical rubric—such as explicit Great Books programs, extensive Socratic pedagogy in upper grades, and a fully articulated “living curriculum” in faculty—are less clearly spelled out in the site texts. Taken together, Highlands Latin Orlando is best characterized as SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL, with substantial classical-formation features and Christian grounding, but with room to more fully demonstrate a few canonical elements (notably explicit Great Books/Great Conversation commitments, and a more explicit Socratic/seminar pedagogy in upper grades).
