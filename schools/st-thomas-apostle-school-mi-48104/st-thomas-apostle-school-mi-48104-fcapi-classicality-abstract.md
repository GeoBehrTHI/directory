## ABSTRACT

St. Thomas the Apostle Catholic School (Ann Arbor, MI 48104) demonstrates a robust CLASSICAL alignment across all six rubric categories: Religious and Metaphysical Foundations, School Purpose and Goal, Curriculum, Pedagogy, Faculty, and Parents & Community. The school foregrounds Catholic liturgy, catechesis, and a sacramental life as foundational religious practices; frames education as formation toward truth, goodness, and beauty; anchors the curriculum in the Seven Liberal Arts (Trivium and Quadrivium), Great Books, and Western Christian tradition; employs a pedagogy blending memory, chant, recitation, and contemplative rest with Socratic-style discussion and rhetoric training; situates faculty as mentors in a living curriculum; and actively partners with parents and the wider parish community. The Positive keyword indicators from the Six Essential Elements lexicon are strongly evidenced in the public materials, while Negative keyword indicators (e.g., secularism, workforce-tilted framing, or diminished formation) are not present. The aggregate classification is CLASSICAL.

Key grounded signals include:
- Theological and liturgical foundation: All-school Mass, adoration, catechesis, Catholic identity; Catechesis of the Good Shepherd in the Atrium; explicit statements of worship and sacred life in daily school life.  
- Formation through the liberal arts and Great Books: Seven liberal arts emphasis, Trivium/Quadrivium, Great Books, primary sources, Latin, Western civilization framing.  
- Pedagogy blending memory, contemplative practices, and rhetoric: Memory work, chanting, recitation, and rhetorical training in upper grades, with opportunities for discussion in small classes.  
- Faculty as living mentors within a Catholic community; parent partnership and parish integration are explicit.  

Overall, St. Thomas the Apostle Catholic School presents a consistent Classical education profile, anchored in Catholic tradition, classical pedagogy, and a community that partners with families and parish life.
