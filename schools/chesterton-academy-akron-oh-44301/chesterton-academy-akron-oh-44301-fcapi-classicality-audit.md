# Classicality Audit: Chesterton Academy of Akron

Website audited: https://akronchestertonacademy.org/ 

## Executive Summary
Overall classification: CLASSICAL

Chesterton Academy of Akron presents clear textual evidence of a classical, Catholic educational model. The site explicitly anchors its mission in the true, the good, and the beautiful; integrates theology across subjects; lists Great Books authors; and names the Socratic method as a primary pedagogy. Liturgical life (daily Mass, Adoration, Confession) is present, and a House System fosters virtue and community. The new St. Thomas More Junior Classical (grades 7–8) extends this model into the middle school years with catechism memorization, Latin prayers, and contemplative practices like journaling and silence. On balance, positive indicators from the Six Essential Elements outnumber negatives by a wide margin in every category, with only a mild “college outcomes” emphasis appearing in a few places.

Key highlights:
- Mission and identity highlight classical formation in truth, goodness, and beauty, “in partnership with families and the Church.” 
- Classical curriculum featuring integrated humanities, explicit Great Books authors, history (not “social studies”), and the Socratic method. 
- Substantial liturgical and devotional life for students, especially in grades 7–8 (daily Mass, Adoration, Confession), plus catechism memorization and Latin prayers. 
- Faculty expectations emphasize participation in daily Mass and leadership in spiritual formation and House life. 

Scoring framework used: Six Essential Elements of a Classical School (Positive/Negative Keyword Indicators) and the classicality lexicon.
