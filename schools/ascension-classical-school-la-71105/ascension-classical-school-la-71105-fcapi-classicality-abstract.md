## ABSTRACT

Ascension Classical School (Shreveport, LA, 71105) is classified as CLASSICAL across all six classical-education criteria. The school’s public materials foreground a Christian, biblical foundation (Christ-centered mission; chapel/devotionals), a Trivium-based curriculum (Grammar, Logic, Rhetoric), and a Great Books / Great Conversation approach (e.g., explicit references to “Great Conversation” and canonical literature). Pedagogy transitions from memory-oriented, contemplative lower-division activities (singing, chants, memory work) to Socratic discussion and rhetoric-focused upper-division work, consistent with the classical progression. Faculty are portrayed as mentors and the living curriculum at the core of the school’s culture, and parents are positioned as partners and co-educators in a strongly collaborative community. These features collectively align with the classical education rubric’s six elements, confirming ASC’s CLASSICAL designation and confirming alignment with the Six Essential Elements of a Classical School (1).
