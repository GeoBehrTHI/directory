## ABSTRACT
Austin Classical School is a classical Christian institution with an integrated, Trivium‑centered curriculum, Latin, extensive primary texts, and a pedagogy emphasizing memory work, Socratic discussion, and rhetoric culminating in a senior thesis defense. Its mission (“glorify Jesus Christ… cultivate wise, virtuous, and humble students”) underscores transcendental moral realism and the cultivation of wisdom and virtue. Daily chapel and Christian Studies anchor theological formation, and the collaborative co‑teacher model enacts a true partnership with parents.
