# Classicality Audit: Austin Classical School (Austin, TX)

Website used: https://austinclassical.org

## Executive Summary
Overall classification: CLASSICAL

Austin Classical School’s public materials consistently articulate a classical, Christian vision: a mission to “glorify Jesus Christ,” the cultivation of “wise, virtuous, and humble” students, and an education oriented to “truth, goodness, and beauty.” They implement the Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric), Latin, extensive primary sources, Socratic discussion, recitation and chanting in the lower grades, and a capstone senior thesis with oral defense. Daily chapel, explicit Christian studies, and an integrated curriculum “under the sovereignty of God through theology” are prominent. The collaborative (co‑teacher) model demonstrates a deep partnership with parents through required co‑teacher training and regular support. Evidence across all six elements is strong and unambiguous.      

Method note: This audit applies the “Six Essential Elements of a Classical School” keyword framework (positive/negative indicators by category) as the scoring backbone, supplemented by the lexicon file.
