## ABSTRACT

Mt Royal Academy Nh 03782 demonstrates a comprehensive CLASSICAL profile across all six evaluation categories. Religious and metaphysical foundations are firmly Catholic, with explicit emphasis on truth, goodness, and beauty as real, knowable ends; the school’s purpose emphasizes formation over information and a telos oriented toward human excellence within a Christian framework. The curriculum is explicitly liberal-arts-based, centered on the Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric), Great Books, and a canon of Western thought, with Latin and classical-language study appearing in a canonical humanities program. Pedagogy blends contemplative/restful approaches (scholé, memory work, chant) with Socratic seminars, debates, and rhetorical training, particularly in the upper grades. The faculty are described as living exemplars and a collegial community, and parents are integrated as co-educators in a formation-focused partnership. Public materials consistently present the school as a Newman Guide-recognized Catholic institution with a formation-first mission, aligning strongly with the Classical educational tradition.
