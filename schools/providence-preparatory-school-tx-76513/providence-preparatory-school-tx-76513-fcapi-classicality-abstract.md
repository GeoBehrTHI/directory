## ABSTRACT

Providence Preparatory School (Belton, TX) earns a CLASSICAL rating across all six canonical categories of classical education (Religious & Metaphysical Foundations, School Purpose & Goal, Curriculum, Pedagogy, Faculty, Parents & Community). The school’s public materials present a Christ-centered, classical framework anchored by paideia, wisdom/virtue, and the pursuit of truth, goodness, and beauty. The curriculum foregrounds grammar/logic/rhetoric, Great Books, canonical literature, Latin, and cross-disciplinary history; pedagogy blends memory-based, contemplative practices (Scholé) with Socratic and rhetoric-based seminars for higher grades; faculty are featured as living exemplars and mentors, and parents are explicitly positioned as co-teachers in a collaborative school community. The aggregate evidence supports a robust classical identity with a clearly defined mission aligned with the Great Conversation.

Key evidentiary themes
- Religious/Metaphysical Foundations: Christian, theologically grounded, God-shaped wonder; discipleship language; biblical worldview integration; paideia framing.
- Purpose/Goal: Formation of virtue and wisdom; telos toward truth, goodness, beauty; liberal-arts orientation.
- Curriculum: Trivium; Great Books; canonical literature; Latin; integrated history; cross-disciplinary connections.
- Pedagogy: Scholé in lower grades; memory/recitation; Socratic seminars; rhetorical training in rhetoric school; cross-subject integration.
- Faculty: Living curriculum; mentorship; collaborative culture; portraits of teacher ideals.
- Parents/Community: Parental co-teaching; formation for parents; collaborative school culture; open-campus opportunities.
