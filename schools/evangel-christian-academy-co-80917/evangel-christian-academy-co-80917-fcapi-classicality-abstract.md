## ABSTRACT

Evangel Christian Academy (Colorado Springs) demonstrates a robust CLASSICAL orientation across all six rubric categories. Religious and Metaphysical Foundations are grounded in Christ-centered theology, biblical worldview integration, chapel/community worship, and a Statement of Faith; the school foregrounds spiritual formation and moral formation. The School Purpose and Goal center formation, virtue, wisdom, and a biblical vocation to serve God, family, church, and society. The Curriculum is explicitly classical (Grammar-Logic-Rhetoric), aligned with liberal arts traditions, and reinforced by resources used in classical education (Logic of English, Story of the World, Purposeful Design), with accreditation by ACCS and a focus on education through the Great Conversation ethos. Pedagogy reflects both the contemplative/restful aspects and the active Socratic/seminar-based practices appropriate to the Trivium’s stages, including memorization and recitation in the Grammar Stage and Socratic dialogue/rhetoric training in the Rhetoric Stage. Faculty are described as living curriculum and mentors, and there is strong emphasis on community, and parental partnership, positioning parents as co-educators and participants in a shared mission. Overall, Evangel Christian Academy is CLASSICAL in alignment, with coherent integration of religious formation, classical curriculum, and community partnership.

In sum, the school meets or exceeds the core signals of classical Christian education in all six categories, with explicit location-based evidence for theology, formation, Trivium-based curriculum, Socratic pedagogy, virtuous faculty modeling, and parent-community partnership. The aggregate is CLASSICAL, with no category requiring a downgrade based on the present public materials.
