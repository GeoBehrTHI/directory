## ABSTRACT

The school’s public materials foreground a liturgical life (daily Mass, sacraments) and a formation-focused mission (virtue, wisdom, truth, goodness, beauty) anchored in Catholic teaching. Pedagogical practice references Socratic dialogue and rhetoric, supported by a public emphasis on classical education. Parental partnership and community integration are emphasized as core to the school’s model.

 Six-category synthesis:
- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: CLASSICAL — evident through direct Catholic identity, liturgical life, sacramental practice, and the objective of forming virtue and understanding of transcendentals (truth, goodness, beauty). 
- School Purpose and Goal: CLASSICAL — formation and virtue-centered aims, not merely job preparation; emphasis on truth, goodness, beauty as real and knowable. 
- Curriculum: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL — strong classical framing and evidence of classical pedagogy (Socratic seminars, classical texts, rhetoric) but not always explicit about every canonical curriculum component (e.g., explicit Great Books/Seven Liberal Arts listing in public copy). 
- Pedagogy: CLASSICAL — explicit classical methods (Socratic dialogue, rhetoric, history/theology integration) evidenced in the public materials. 
- Faculty: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL — capable, classical-aligned faculty with named leaders; however, explicit articulation of a formal “living curriculum” portrait is less clear in the public copy. 
- Parents & Community: CLASSICAL — strong emphasis on partnership with families and parental engagement; activities and openness to parents are foregrounded. 

Overall, Chesterton Academy – Queen of Martyrs demonstrates a robust classical Catholic orientation, with strongest alignment in religious foundations, overall purpose, pedagogy, and community involvement. The curriculum and faculty categories show solid classical alignment but lack explicit, fully enumerated canonical elements in the public copy, resulting in a SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL designation for those two areas. The aggregate assessment remains CLASSICAL.
