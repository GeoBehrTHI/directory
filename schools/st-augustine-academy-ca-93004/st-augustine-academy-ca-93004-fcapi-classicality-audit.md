## Executive Summary

St Augustine Academy CA 93004 presents a robust Catholic classical education anchored in liberal arts formation. Public-facing materials describe a curriculum centered on the trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric) and quadrivium (arithmetic, geometry, music, astronomy), the integration of Great Books and primary Western literature, explicit emphasis on theology as the crowning discipline, and a pedagogy that blends memory work, recitation, and disciplined rhetoric with higher-level inquiry (including thesis work in the upper grades). The school identifies itself as a Catholic culture embedded in the liberal arts tradition, with a clear aim to form virtue and wisdom through a classical formation rather than a primarily job- or college-prep focus. These signals align strongly with the Classical School rubric, with only minor ambiguities in pedagogy momentum that reduce it from a perfect CLASSICAL to a SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL assessment in a single category.

Key themes observed across public materials:
- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: explicit Catholic identity; theology as central; liturgical/mass life (Mass, Advent programs) and communal religious practices.
- Purpose and Goal: formation of virtue and wisdom; contemplation of truth, goodness, and beauty; allegiance to the liberal arts tradition and the Great Conversation.
- Curriculum: Trivium and Quadrivium; Great Books; Latin; history and Western civilization; memory work and recitation.
- Pedagogy: upper-school emphasis on logic and rhetoric; lower- and middle-school memory and recitation; thesis work in upper grades; some contemplative/restful elements noted.
- Faculty: named faculty with a living-curriculum flavor; visible leadership and teacher profiles.
- Parents & Community: emphasis on family/community orientation; local families; structured admissions and tuition processes; some indicators of parent involvement, with potential to grow toward more explicit parent-formation signals.

Evidence-derived classification across the six categories (category-by-category snapshot is provided below; aggregate rating at the end labels the school as CLASSICAL, with one category identified as SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL).

## What Would Move the Needle

- Element 4 (Pedagogy) is the sole SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL rating. Concrete steps to reach full CLASSICAL pedagogy:
 - Expand explicit articulation of Socratic/seminar-based inquiry across more grade bands, including more structured seminar-work and dialectic practice in middle and lower grades, not only upper grades.
 - Increase explicit mentions of “scholé” days and contemplative pedagogy in more public materials, linking them to student memory work and slow, reflective study.
 - Provide explicit language about how projects or experiential learning fit within a classical framework (ensuring they remain anchored in the liberal arts traditions rather than dominating the pedagogy with non-classical project imperatives).
 - Document and publish case examples of student-led rhetorical exercises, debates, and public speaking beyond thesis-level work, to demonstrate sustained rhetorical training as a core pedagogy.

Citations: Pedagogy emphasis on logic/rhetoric (turn0file4); thesis defense as a rhetoric capstone (turn0file7).
