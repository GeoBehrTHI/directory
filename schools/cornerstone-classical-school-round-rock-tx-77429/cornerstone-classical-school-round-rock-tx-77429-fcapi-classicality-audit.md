## Executive Summary

Cornerstone Classical School Round Rock, TX (77429) demonstrates a robust classical-Christian orientation across most of the six classical-school criteria. Public materials consistently frame a Christ-centered, virtue- and wisdom-forming mission, with explicit theology and biblical foundations, a Trivium-aligned curriculum (grammar, logic, rhetoric), and pedagogy that blends memory work and liturgical/chanting elements in lower grades with Socratic, seminar-style, and rhetorically oriented upper-level work. Leadership development, family partnership, and open engagement with parents are prominent. The strongest alignments are Religious & Metaphysical Foundations, School Purpose & Goal, Pedagogy, Faculty, and Parents & Community. The Curriculum category is present and substantial but displays partial alignment due to the absence (in public-facing materials) of explicit Great Books/Great Conversation language beyond classic-canon resource lists; this keeps Curriculum at a “Somewhat Classical” level rather than fully Classical. The aggregate assessment places Cornerstone as CLASSICAL overall, with a clearly articulated classical framework, explicit theological grounding, and structured classical pedagogy, while leaving room to more explicitly foreground Great Books/Great Conversation in curriculum messaging.

Observations at a glance:
- Religious & Metaphysical Foundations: Clear Christ-centered theology, biblical grounding, and liturgical/community orientation evidenced by mission, theology statements, and faith-language in public materials. CLASSICAL.
- Purpose & Goal: Formation in virtue and wisdom, pursuit of truth, goodness, and beauty, rather than mere job prep; deep formation language present. CLASSICAL.
- Curriculum: Grammar, Logic, Rhetoric emphasized; history integrated; curriculum list includes classic/Christian scholarly resources; however explicit Great Books/Great Conversation messaging is less explicit in public text. Somewhat Classical.
- Pedagogy: Lower grades emphasize memory work, chanting, and wonder; upper grades engage Socratic discussion and rhetoric training; clearly multi-Modal classical pedagogy. CLASSICAL.
- Faculty: Faculty described as collaborators with families, living the curriculum, and modeling virtue; intentional portrayal of faculty as core to the school’s culture. CLASSICAL.
- Parents & Community: Strong emphasis on partnership with families, co-teaching, family discipleship, and open campus/visits; explicit parent formation and community engagement. CLASSICAL.

What would move the needle: For a fully CLASSICAL rating, it would help to more explicitly foreground Great Books and Great Conversation language in Curriculum messaging (e.g., explicit Great Books lists, Great Conversation framing, and primary-source reading emphasis) and to more directly articulate liturgical/cultic practices as part of daily school life (if not already evident in local culture). See section: What would move the needle.
