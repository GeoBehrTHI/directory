## Executive Summary

Founders Christian School TX 77373 demonstrates a robust alignment with classical education as defined by the Six Essential Elements framework. The school presents a clearly articulated Christian worldview embedded across its mission, philosophy of education, and academics, and it operates explicitly as a ministry of a church. Its curriculum foregrounds the liberal arts (including classical language study and logic/rhetoric pathways) and integrates biblical instruction throughout, with weekly chapel, Bible classes, and church involvement underscoring liturgical and communal practices. Public materials repeatedly emphasize formation of character, virtue, and a biblical understanding of truth, goodness, and beauty, rather than career-focused outcomes. Collectively, these signals place Founders Christian School in the CLASSICAL category across all six rubric categories.

Key takeaways by category:
- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: CLASSICAL. Strong emphasis on biblical truth, theology, worship, and church partnership. Evidence includes explicit biblical worldview framing, chapel/music as worship, and Bible-centered academics. 
- Purpose and Goal: CLASSICAL. Education framed as formation through a biblical worldview, cultivating virtue and wisdom rather than job-readiness alone. Evidence includes mission statements and philosophy of education that center wisdom, virtue, and the fear of the Lord as basis for learning. 
- Curriculum: CLASSICAL. Liberal-arts oriented with grammar/logic/rhetoric through a historically grounded curriculum; classical languages; integration of Bible and catechesis; stated aim of biblical/classical curriculum. 
- Pedagogy: CLASSICAL. Evidence of memory-based/recitation and liturgical aspects (singing, chanted/memorizational elements via Bible memory and music classes), plus opportunities for rhetoric and disciplined discussion within a Christian, classical framework. While explicit Socratic seminars are not extensively documented in public materials, the presence of logic/rhetoric and classical pedagogy signals alignment with classical methods. 
- Faculty: CLASSICAL. Public materials emphasize a committed, virtuous faculty and a “living curriculum” through which teachers model virtue and mentoring. Examples include statements about dedicated faculty and a ministry of teaching within a church context. 
- Parents & Community: CLASSICAL. Strong partnership with families, explicit invitation for parental involvement and formation, and a campus that welcomes parent engagement, consistent with the “partnership with families” and “parents as co-teachers” signals.
