## ABSTRACT

Founders Christian School TX 77373 presents a consistently CLASSICAL profile across all six categories of the classical-education rubric. Evidence from public-facing materials shows a school deeply committed to a biblical worldview, theology-integrated pedagogy, and liturgical/community practices (Element 1). Its stated purpose and goals emphasize formation, wisdom, and virtue within a biblical framework rather than merely job preparation (Element 2). The Curriculum is explicitly described as biblical and classical, with liberal-arts coherence (grammar, logic, rhetoric), classical languages, and biblically integrated coursework (Element 3). Pedagogy features memory work, worship-based components, and a classical structure that includes rhetoric and memory-based practices, with contemplative/restful elements in balance (Element 4). The Faculty signals show mentors and a living curriculum with a ministry orientation (Element 5). Finally, Parents & Community materials reveal strong partnerships with families, open access to campus, and active parental formation activities (Element 6).

Overall, Founders Christian School exemplifies CLASSICAL education with a coherent integration of theology, formation of virtue, classical curriculum, and visible parental-community engagement. The absence of explicit Great Books language is noted as a minor gap relative to some classical models but does not substantially detract from the school’s CLASSICAL alignment given the strong presence of liberal-arts content, theology, and classical pedagogy signals.

Cited materials include: the biblical worldview mission and philosophy (formation and biblical truth) and weekly chapel/music (Religious Foundations) ; Bible classes, chapel, and church involvement (Religious Foundations) ; curriculum describing biblical/classical integration and liberal-arts emphasis (Curriculum page) ; statements about ministry of the church and dedicated faculty (Faculty & Church partnership) ; parental partnership and campus accessibility (Parent/Community) ; and admissions/vision statements tying the school to biblical truth and a classical approach (Vision/Mission) .
