## Executive Summary

Chesterton Academy of Holy Trinity (LA 70360) presents a robust Classical-Catholic profile consistent with a primarily CLASSICAL orientation. Public materials emphasize a Catholic formation mission built on truth, goodness, and beauty, a formation-focused educational aim, and a clearly articulated Socratic, integrated humanities approach. The school foregrounds the Incarnation and Catholic Magisterial fidelity as central to formation, with evidence of liturgical life and Catholic culture anchored in events such as Cultura Vitae Fest and Mass celebrations. Pedagogy emphasizes Socratic discussion in the upper grades, with a broader emphasis on formation through classical disciplines and memory work. Faculty is described as master teachers and role models, and parental engagement is evidenced through open houses, shadow days, and family-oriented events.

Overall, the school scores CLASSICAL across most categories, with CATEGORY 3 (Curriculum) and CATEGORY 6 (Parents & Community) showing only SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL signals — primarily due to explicit Great Books/Quadrivium language and explicit “co-teaching” partnerships with parents being less overt in public-facing materials. The aggregate assessment leans CLASSICAL, with explicit steps noted below for targets that would elevate it across all six categories.

- Evidence highlights include: a Catholic context with Magisterium fidelity and Christ-centered formation (evidence of religious/metaphysical commitments) ; emphasis on formation through mind, body, and spirit with Truth, Goodness, and Beauty (formation-focused teleology) ; liturgical life and Catholic culture (Mass, liturgy; Cultura Vitae Fest) ; integrated humanities pedagogy with Socratic Method (upper school) and a public-facing focus on the Great Conversation and Great Books language in the classical tradition (where explicit public references appear) ; master-teacher model for faculty (public statements about faculty as master teachers and role models) ; openness to parents through shadow days, open houses, and family-oriented events (evidence of family involvement) .
