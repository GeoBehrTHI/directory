## ABSTRACT
- Overall: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL. TCPS exhibits strong religious/theological integration and a living chapel culture, with Latin and Logic (MS) and Harkness discussion (US) indicating classical elements. The public purpose narrative, however, is primarily “college preparatory,” with limited articulation of classical ends (virtue, wisdom, truth/goodness/beauty) or of a complete classical curriculum (Great Books, trivium/quadrivium, rhetoric/thesis). Pedagogical signals beyond Harkness (e.g., lower-school chanting/recitation; scholé) are not demonstrated, and faculty formation language is absent. Parent partnership exists (FTO and “partner with your family”), but not explicit “parent formation.”
- Six categories:
  1) Religious/Metaphysical: CLASSICAL; worship, chapel, theological integration are robust.
  2) Purpose/Goal: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL; formation language exists, but “college prep” dominates.
  3) Curriculum: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL; Latin/Logic strong; Great Books/rhetoric/quadrivium not published.
  4) Pedagogy: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL; Harkness (Socratic) appears; chanting/recitation/scholé not shown.
  5) Faculty: NOT CLASSICAL; no “portrait of a teacher,” mentors/living curriculum language lacking.
  6) Parents & Community: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL; partnership/community present; little parent formation.
