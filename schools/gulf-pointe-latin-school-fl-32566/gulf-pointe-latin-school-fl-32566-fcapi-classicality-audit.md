- Positive/Negative keyword analysis

  - Negative keywords observed: absence of terms that would signal an overemphasis on project-based or STEM-first pedagogy; lack of “social studies” framing in place of history in the public materials.
  - Frequency/coverage: Strong presence of the core classical curriculum keywords across multiple pages (curriculum sections, classical overview, language study pages).

- Why this rating
  - The explicit and recurring emphasis on grammar/logic/rhetoric, Latin/Greek, Great Books, and Western civilization, together with history-focused, classical-lit content, supports CLASSICAL. The material demonstrates a robust liberal-arts orientation and a clearly defined Great Books-like curriculum.

  - The school demonstrates a classical pedagogy mix: contemplative memory and chant in the early years, paired with Socratic/seminar and rhetorical training in the upper years. This aligns with CLASSICAL expectations.
