## Executive Summary

Arrows Christian Academy AZ 85286 (as reflected in the crawl content for Lincoln Prep within the Great Hearts network) demonstrates a robust classical-education orientation that aligns strongly with the Six Essential Elements rubric. Public-facing materials from the school’s site emphasize a mission grounded in Truth, Goodness, and Beauty; a curriculum centered on liberal-arts foundations, Great Books, and Western cultural heritage; pedagogy that foregrounds Socratic dialogue, memorization/recitation, and contemplative “Scholé” practices; and a faculty and parent-community framework that presents teachers as mentors and parents as co-educators. Across six classical categories, the material evidence points toward CLASSICAL alignment with only minor room for triangulating theology or liturgical practice more explicitly in some public materials. For the six-category rubric, the aggregate assessment is CLASSICAL.

Key evidentiary signals include:

- Mission and aims: An explicit statement that the academy cultivates minds and hearts through Truth, Goodness, and Beauty, with emphasis on liberal arts and Great Books within a Western-tradition curriculum. This is reflected in mission sections and introductory statements that frame education as formation through classical principles.
- Curriculum and Great Books: The site foregrounds liberal arts (grammar, logic, rhetoric; history; mathematics; natural science; philosophy) and a sustained emphasis on Great Books and the Great Conversation within a Western-tradition curriculum.
- Pedagogy: Public-facing materials highlight Socratic dialogue, memory work (recitation/chanting), and Scholé-like rest in learning, with opportunities for rhetoric and public speaking in upper levels.
- Faculty: The materials present teachers as models of learning and character, a living curriculum, and a collegial, mentor-based approach.
- Parents & Community: The materials emphasize parent involvement, partnerships with families, and parent-education/formation opportunities, with an open-campus ethos and active parent governance/support structures (e.g., PSO).
- Great Hearts framework and Western canon: Across several pages, the site describes a classical Liberal Arts curriculum anchored in Western canon, Great Books, and classical languages (Latin, Greek) with an emphasis on culture, virtue, and moral imagination.

Overall classification: CLASSICAL
