## ABSTRACT

The school emphasizes a Christ-centered framework, a telos-driven formation of virtue and wisdom, and a curriculum anchored in the Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric) alongside Great Books and primary sources. The evidence supports a CLASSICAL classification across five of the six categories (Religious & Metaphysical Foundations; Purpose & Goal; Curriculum; Faculty; Parents & Community), with Pedagogy described as strongly Trivium-based and formation-oriented but less explicitly documented in universal Socratic practice, which places Category 4 as SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL. The aggregate assessment is CLASSICAL.
