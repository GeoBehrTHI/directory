## Executive Summary

Greenleaf Friends Academy (GFA) in Idaho presents a robust Classical Christian program that foregrounds formation in a Christ-centered framework, with explicit branding around being “Joyfully Classical” and “academically excellent.” Public materials consistently position education as formation oriented toward Truth, Goodness, and Beauty, and emphasize the trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric), Great Books, and a deliberately formation-focused telos. The school frames its mission around character, virtue, and a broad liberal-arts curriculum, while integrating aspects of a Great-Books tradition and a House program that structures student life around community and leadership. Overall, based on the school’s public materials, GFA aligns strongly with Classical Christian aims across most rubric categories, with particularly clear strengths in Religious & Metaphysical Foundations, School Purpose & Goal, Curriculum, Faculty, and Parents & Community. The Pedagogy category shows strong classical elements but is somewhat less explicit about certain Socratic/seminar practices, placing it in the realm of SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL rather than purely CLASSICAL. The aggregate assessment is CLASSICAL.

Key evidence points include:
- Public emphasis on Christ-centered education, Truth/Goodness/Beauty, and formation (e.g., “Christ-centered … Joyfully Classical” and the telos orientation)   .
- Explicit articulation of the telos and formation emphasis, with statements about forming the whole person and destiny in Christ and creation  .
- The Trivium, Great Books/Great Conversation, and a classical curriculum focus (grammar, logic, rhetoric; history; primary sources) evidenced in program pages and articles   .
- A School House/House Program and parental/community engagement themes (parents as partners, open campus-style messaging, parent-student portal) visible on multiple pages   .
