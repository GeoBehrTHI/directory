## ABSTRACT

Providence School Santa Barbara (CA 93103) demonstrates strong religious and formation-focused alignment with classical education, particularly through its explicit theological grounding and emphasis on virtue, wisdom, and habits of mind. The mission, motto, and theology-oriented materials show CLASSICAL alignment in Category 1 (Religious and Metaphysical Foundations) and Category 2 (Purpose and Goal). In Category 3 (Curriculum) and Category 4 (Pedagogy), the school displays partial alignment—evident in rhetoric-focused activities and formation-driven aims, but lacking a clearly articulated Great Books/grammar-logic-rhetoric curriculum spine and explicit Great Books/Great Conversation components in the public materials, yielding a SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL rating. Category 5 (Faculty) and Category 6 (Parents & Community) show supportive classical-adjacent features (virtue-motivated faculty and family-oriented community), but with insufficient explicit public articulation of the classical faculty-as-living-curriculum and formal parent-co-teacher frameworks, resulting in SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL for those categories. Overall, the school’s public representation leans toward SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL, driven by strong formation and rhetoric-oriented elements alongside a need for explicit articulation of the traditional Classical curriculum framework and explicit parent/teacher-community structures.

Key evidence pillars include: Our Mission and Motto; Christian Education Philosophy; Intellectually oriented formation and habits of mind; Senior Thesis and Recess & Rhetoric; and explicit theological framing of truth, beauty, and excellence.
