## Executive Summary

Commission Leadership Academy (CLA) presents a strongly classical, Christian educational orientation anchored in the University-Model framework that partners with families. Public-facing CLA materials consistently emphasize a Christian worldview, the formation of character, and a curriculum and pedagogy aligned with classical aims (grammar/logic/rhetoric; quadrivium; great books; memorization; and a living, faith-integrated pedagogy). The school explicitly frames its mission around partnership with parents, catechesis at home, and a shared mission to form virtuous, wisdom-seeking students under a Christ-centered framework. This aligns CLA with a Classical / Highly Classical orientation in several key areas, though some pedagogical and community facets reflect a modern, home-education-centric implementation (University-Model) that may diversify exposure to traditional “Socratic” and scholé-oriented practices.

Key evidence from CLA materials shows:

- A stated identity as a Classical, Christian, University-Model School that partners with parents and centers the home as part of discipleship and schooling. This is explicitly stated in CLA’s public messaging.
- A curriculum and worldview that foregrounds classical education (grammar/logic/rhetoric; quadrivium), plus the integration of Biblical worldview and theology across disciplines.
- Direct articulation of Trivium/Quadrivium pedagogy and a Christian formation aim, with emphasis on virtue, moral imagination, and the pursuit of truth, goodness, and beauty.

Overall assessment: CLASSICAL.
