## Executive Summary

The Saint Constantine School (Houston, TX 77036) presents a robust Classical Christian program with explicit emphasis on virtue, wisdom, and beauty, integrated with Orthodox Christian formation. Public materials consistently frame education as formation of character and intellect within a liturgical, communal, and great-books-driven framework. The Upper School foregrounds Socratic dialogue and mastery of Great Books, while Lower/Middle School incorporate contemplative and memory-focused elements (Scholé-like rhythms, singing, and chant). The school also foregrounds liturgical practices ( Divine Liturgy, morning prayers) and a strong sense of Church life, which strongly anchors its religious metaphysical foundations.

Overall classification: CLASSICAL.

Key drivers of this classification include:

- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations are clearly centered in Orthodox Christian formation, liturgical practice, and sacred aims (truth, beauty, virtue in a theologically oriented framework) as reflected in mission statements and liturgical life. Evidence: emphasis on Eastern Orthodox alignment and liturgical prayer, divine liturgy, and mission statements tying education to salvation and God’s glory.  

- Purpose and Goal emphasize formation, virtue, and wisdom, with a stated aim of formational education alongside preparation for college. The core values explicitly name Wisdom, Virtue, and Joy; the mission ties education to piety and service, while still acknowledging college readiness via dual credit programs.   

- Curriculum centers on the liberal arts, Great Books, and a Trivium/Quadrivium framework; explicit mention of Great Books and a block-schedule approach to integrate classics across disciplines. Latin/Greek and Christian-liturgical content are part of the program.  

- Pedagogy includes both contemplative/restful elements and Socratic/seminar-based discussion, with upper-level rhetoric and public speaking opportunities, and lower-level memory/chanting practices. This aligns with the classical pedagogy spectrum. 

- Faculty is presented as a central asset (facilitating a living curriculum and mentorship; examples of faculty pages and a robust full-time faculty), with a family/house system that reinforces the community.  

- Parents & Community are positioned as partners (house system, parent involvement, and campus life designed to engage families and encourage visits).
