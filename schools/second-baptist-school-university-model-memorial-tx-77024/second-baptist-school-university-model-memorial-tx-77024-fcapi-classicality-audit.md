## Executive Summary

Second Baptist School University-Model Memorial (SBSUM Memorial) presents a predominantly classical framework with a clear Christian theological orientation. Public materials emphasize a biblically grounded mission, a classical curriculum (grammar–logic–rhetoric), and a university-model pedagogy that blends campus days with structured home-based study. Evidence shows explicit commitments to truth, beauty, and excellence within a biblically centered worldview, a strong partnership with families, and a robust classical arts emphasis. Taken together, SBSUM Memorial aligns with the core elements of classical education across all six categories, yielding a GENERAL CLASSICAL assessment.

Key insights:

- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations are strongly indicated by biblically-centered language, theology-infused curriculum, and worship/choral arts embedded in school life.
- The School Purpose and Goal centers on formation, moral character, and the pursuit of truth, goodness, and beauty, not merely job preparation.
- The Curriculum is explicitly built on classical trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric) and includes emphasis on the great books and a Christian worldview.
- Pedagogy combines Socratic/Harkness-style discussion and rhetoric training in the upper grades with contemplative, memory-oriented elements in earlier years, aligned with scholé and classical practice.
- Faculty materials highlight a professional, mission-driven teaching force, with a living-curriculum concept and a clear emphasis on mentorship and character formation.
- Parents & Community are positioned as co-educators through a formal family-school partnership, parent trainings, and a campus network that welcomes parental involvement.
