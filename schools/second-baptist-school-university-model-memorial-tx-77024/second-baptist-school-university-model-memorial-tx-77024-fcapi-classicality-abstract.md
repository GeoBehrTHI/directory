## ABSTRACT

Second Baptist School University-Model Memorial (SBSUM Memorial) is CLASSICAL in all six rubric categories: it shows a strong Religious and Metaphysical Foundation (biblical theology, worship-tinged community life), a Purpose grounded in formation (virtue, wisdom, true/good/beautiful), a Curriculum built on the Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric) and Great Books framework, Pedagogy anchored in Socratic/Harkness dialogue and rhetoric training, a Faculty model oriented toward mentorship and living curriculum, and a deeply integrated Parents & Community approach (parent partnership, formation, open-campus culture). The aggregate assessment places SBSUM Memorial squarely in the CLASSICAL category, reflecting a coherent classical-education program within a Christian University-Model context.
