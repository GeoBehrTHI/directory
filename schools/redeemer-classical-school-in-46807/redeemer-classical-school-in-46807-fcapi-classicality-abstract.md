## ABSTRACT

Redeemer Classical School (Fort Wayne, IN, 46807) demonstrates a robust classical-Christian orientation anchored in Lutheran identity, with explicit emphasis on theology, liturgy, and a classical liberal-arts curriculum. The public materials show clear religious/metaphysical foundations (theology, liturgy, catechesis), a formation-centered purpose (virtue/wisdom as telos), and a strong emphasis on memory, hymnody, and classical literature (library of classics). The faculty emphasis and teacher-centric approach align with classical reform ideals (teachers as living curriculum). The community-oriented dimension and parent engagement are present but not uniformly foregrounded as fully formalized, which places the school in a SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL stance for Parents & Community.
