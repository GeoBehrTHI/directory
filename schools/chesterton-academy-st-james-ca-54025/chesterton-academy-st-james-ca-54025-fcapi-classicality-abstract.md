## ABSTRACT

Chesterton Academy of St. James in Menlo Park, CA presents a CLASSICAL educational model anchored in a distinct Catholic identity. Public materials describe a formation-focused mission that seeks to cultivate virtue, wisdom, and love of truth, goodness, and beauty within a liturgical and communal Catholic framework. The curriculum emphasizes classical canon and Great Books-like engagement (sourced from Homer, Dante, Socrates, Aquinas, Euclid, Newton) and stresses an integrated, liberal-arts approach designed to form the whole person. The school also showcases a living-faculty model and a House-system culture that mirrors enduring Christian educational traditions, while emphasizing partnerships with families as co-educators. Public evidence points to widespread alignment with Classical keywords in Religious & Metaphysical Foundations, School Purpose & Goal, Curriculum, Faculty, and Parents & Community. Pedagogy, while rooted in classical aims, lacks consistently explicit public articulation of Socratic seminars and formal rhetoric/scaffolded memory-work in the shown materials, thus yielding a SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL signal in Category 4. Nevertheless, the aggregate classification remains CLASSICAL.
