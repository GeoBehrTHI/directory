## Executive Summary
Overall classification: CLASSICAL

Austin Classical School (https://austinclassical.org/) exhibits a strong classical, Christian identity across mission, aims, curriculum, and much of its pedagogy, with clear commitments to the Trivium, the Great Books/Great Conversation, Scripture, virtue formation, and robust parent partnership in a collaborative model. Evidence is abundant for:
- Religious and metaphysical foundations grounded in Christ and the transcendentals (truth, goodness, beauty).
- Purpose and goals focused on wisdom and virtue, formation of the whole person, and contemplation of truth, goodness, and beauty.
- Curriculum explicitly structured by the Trivium, engaging the Bible and great works of the Western tradition, plus Latin and history.

Pedagogy shows strong Lower School memory work (songs, recitation) and Upper School discussion/debate; however, explicit mention of “scholé/restful learning” and formal rhetoric deliverables (e.g., capstone orations) is not prominent in the sample crawl. Faculty pages give limited explicit statements about “teachers as mentors/living curriculum,” and while parent partnership is emphatic, parent formation opportunities are less explicit in the crawl sample.

Aggregate result: CLASSICAL, with “Somewhat Classical” marks in Pedagogy, Faculty, and Parents & Community due to partial coverage. Keyword analysis was performed using the “Six Essential Elements of a Classical School” lexicon and cues, applying positive/negative indicators in each category to justify ratings    .

Key site sources used:
- Mission/purpose and basics: “About Us” and “Classical Education Basics” pages .
- Educational approach (Trivium, Bible, great works, Latin, history, music): “Our Educational Approach/The Trivium” page  .
- Collaborative model and parent co-teaching: “Collaborative Schedule” page  .
- House system evidence: “Our First Annual Spirit Week … House of Hildegard” post .

## What would move the needle (targeted improvements)
Note: Overall classification is CLASSICAL; these are optional refinements to strengthen weaker categories.
- Pedagogy: Publish explicit references to “Socratic seminar,” “scholé/restful learning,” and formal Upper School rhetoric deliverables (e.g., defenses, theses, capstone orations) with examples, to remove ambiguity and dampen the “efficiency” signal in pedagogy framing  .
- Faculty: Add a public “Portrait of a Teacher” and “Portrait of a Graduate,” highlight teacher mentorship/collegiality and classical faculty formation (e.g., Great Books seminars for faculty) to signal “living curriculum” and virtue modeling .
- Parents & Community: Surface parent-formation offerings (book clubs, classical education workshops) and explicit hospitality/access practices to bolster coverage across all community sub-elements .
