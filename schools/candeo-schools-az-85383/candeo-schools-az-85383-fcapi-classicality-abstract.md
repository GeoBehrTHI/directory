## ABSTRACT
Austin Classical School is CLASSICAL overall. Key strengths:
- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: Strong, explicit Christian mission (“glorify Jesus Christ”), with a focus on virtue, wisdom, and the transcendentals (truth, goodness, beauty), and references to Scripture and the imago Dei. Category: CLASSICAL.  
- Purpose and Goal: Aim is formation in wisdom and virtue; method is contemplation of the True, Good, Beautiful; education conceived as holistic formation. Category: CLASSICAL. 
- Curriculum: Trivium-centered structure; Great Books/Great Conversation; Bible, history (not social studies), Latin, music; integrated knowledge. Category: CLASSICAL.  
- Pedagogy: Clear memory work (songs, recitation) and dialogic learning (debate/discussion). Less explicit on scholé and formal rhetoric theses; “efficiency” language appears in the collaborative schedule framing. Category: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL.  
- Faculty: Christian educators referenced, but little public articulation of “model teachers/mentors” and faculty formation. Category: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL. 
- Parents & Community: Robust parent partnership via collaborative co-teaching model; fewer visible parent-formation programs in crawl sample. Category: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL. 

Overall: CLASSICAL, with targeted opportunities to make pedagogy, faculty formation, and parent formation more explicit online.
