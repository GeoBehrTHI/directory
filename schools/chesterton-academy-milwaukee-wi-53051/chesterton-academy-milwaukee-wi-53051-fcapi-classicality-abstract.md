## ABSTRACT

Chesterton Academy of Milwaukee presents a robust CLASSICAL profile anchored in a Catholic, liturgical, and formation-centered approach. The school’s public materials emphasize a religious-metaphysical foundation (daily Mass, sacramental life, Catholic faith), a purpose focused on formation in Faith, Reason, and Virtue, and a pedagogy rooted in Socratic discussion and classical liberal-arts education. Parental partnership is strongly foregrounded, with open houses, shadow days, and explicit messaging about parents as co-educators. The curriculum signal is classical in orientation (liberal arts framework and formation emphasis), but explicit, sustained Great Books articulation is less visible in public documents, resulting in a SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL rating for Curriculum.
