## Executive Summary

Watermark Classical Academy (Sewickley, PA, 15143) presents clear evidence of a religiously oriented preschool/kindergarten program operating within a church context. Public materials available for Watermark’s site emphasize devotion to God, biblical framing of education, and parental involvement. However, the materials do not demonstrate core classical education hallmarks such as the liberal arts curriculum (grammar, logic, rhetoric), engagement with Great Books or the Great Conversation, or the distinctive pedagogical practices (Socratic dialogue, Scholé/restful learning, memorization, and imitation) that typify a classical school according to the Six Essential Elements rubric.

Overall, based on the six-category classical rubric, Watermark Classical Academy (Sewickley, PA, 15143) leans NOT CLASSICAL, with a notable partial alignment in Parent/Community relations and a strong Religious & Metaphysical Foundations signal. The aggregate assessment places Watermark outside the classical framework, though with some elements that would align with a classical vision if expanded.

## What Would Move the Needle

- To achieve a full CLASSICAL designation, Watermark would need to publicly articulate and implement a classical liberal-arts curriculum, including:
 - Explicit Great Books engagement and the Great Conversation framework.
 - Structured liberal-arts progression (Trivium and Quadrivium) and classical languages as foundational elements.
 - Adoption of classical pedagogies (Scholé/restful learning, Socratic seminars, memory work, copywork, and imitation with master exemplars).
 - Development of a formal “living curriculum” where faculty members embody classical virtues and mentor students as part of a coherent classical culture.
 - Establishment of formal partnerships with families as co-educators, with explicit parent-education programs and community liturgical/seasonal practices consistent with classical-Christian education.
- These steps align with the positive keyword indicators and the core structure described in the Six Essential Elements framework. See the keyword indicators for the classical framework and recommended transitions to CLASSICAL status.
