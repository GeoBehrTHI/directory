## Executive Summary

Overall classification: CLASSICAL

School: Bloomfield Christian School (BCS)  
Website used for audit: https://www.bloomfieldchristian.com

BCS articulates an explicitly classical, Christian vision featuring strong metaphysical/theological grounding, a clearly stated telos centered on the cultivation of wisdom and virtue, and a curriculum ordered by the liberal arts and the Trivium. The school directly references “truth, goodness, and beauty,” the Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric), Socratic teaching, memorization, and scholé (leisure) as part of its pedagogy. Evidence for faculty formation language and parent formation exists but is less fully developed in public materials compared to the other categories.

## Overall Assessment

Overall rating: CLASSICAL

BCS demonstrates an integrated Christian classical identity: a Scripture-centered and Christ-centered metaphysical foundation; the aim of formation in wisdom and virtue guided by truth, goodness, and beauty; a liberal-arts/Trivium curriculum with classical languages; and hallmark pedagogies (Socratic, memorization/recitation, and scholé). Faculty and Parent/Community evidence is positive but lighter in detail than the other categories based on public-facing materials surfaced by the crawl.

This would add explicit matches to “faculty of friends,” “mentor,” and “living curriculum” indicators.

These would score additional positive keywords (parent formation, book clubs, seminars).
