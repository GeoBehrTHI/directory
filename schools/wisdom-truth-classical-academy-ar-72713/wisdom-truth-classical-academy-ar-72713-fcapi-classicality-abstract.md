## ABSTRACT

Wisdom Truth Classical Academy (AR 72713) shows strong Religious and Metaphysical Foundations and School Purpose & Goal signals consistent with classical education, anchored in biblical truth and virtue. The public materials foreground formation and virtue, and present a clear Christian-classical telos. These signals align with CLASSICAL in Categories 1 and 2. However, explicit, publicly articulated curricular elements (Great Books, Trivium/Quadrivium, Great Conversation), explicit pedagogical methods (Socratic dialogue, Scholé, memory/recitation, rhetoric training), a visible living-curriculum of faculty, and formal parent/community partnership language are not clearly evidenced in the crawl of WTCA’s public site. Consequently, Categories 3–6 show partial alignment rather than full classical embodiment, yielding an overall assessment of SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL.
