## ABSTRACT

Horizon Prep School CA 92067 demonstrates a strong orientation toward classical education, anchored in a Christ-centered worldview. In six classical-criteria categories, the school shows CLASSICAL strength in Religious and Metaphysical Foundations, Pedagogy, and Faculty, with SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL positioning in School Purpose & Goal, Curriculum, and Parents & Community. The school communicates a robust religious formation through Christ-centered instruction, biblical worldview integration, and liturgical practices such as chapels and family worship. The pedagogical core—grammar, logic, and rhetoric—along with Harkness-deliberation and rhetoric training—aligns with classical educational paradigms. Faculty are portrayed as credentialed, dedicated, and integral to the living curriculum. Parental engagement exists through portals and family events, but the explicit framing of parents as co-teachers and deeper parent-formation programs could be strengthened to achieve CLASSICAL across all six categories.

Category-by-category highlights:
- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: CLASSICAL due to Christ-centered instruction, biblical worldview, liturgical life (Bread & Bible, chapels). Evidence emphasizes theology and sacred scripture as ongoing anchors.  
- School Purpose and Goal: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL, with formation and wisdom oriented aims and triadic Trivium emphasis, but explicit public statements about formation over information and the Great Conversation are not consistently explicit. 
- Curriculum: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL, given grammar/logic/rhetoric emphases and Harkness pedagogy, Latin study, and historical framing, yet explicit Great Books/Great Conversation articulation is limited in public materials.  
- Pedagogy: CLASSICAL, with clear Harkness-based, Socratic/seminar practices, memory work, rhetorical training, and a progression through Grammar-Logic-Rhetoric. 
- Faculty: CLASSICAL, with emphasis on credentialed teachers and the living curriculum, supported by pages detailing faculty/staff and small classes. 
- Parents & Community: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL, with observable parental involvement and access (Family Portal, events, and PTF), but a more explicit framing of parents as co-teachers and formal parent-formation programs would further strengthen alignment.  

In sum, Horizon Prep CA 92067 aligns with CLASSICAL education in several core dimensions, while maintaining a few areas that could be fortified to reach a full CLASSICAL designation across all six categories. The school’s Christian formation and classical pedagogy are its defining strengths, with tangible opportunities to more explicitly articulate Great Books, Great Conversation, and deeper parent partnership as the needle-moving steps toward full CLASSICAL status.
