## Executive Summary

Horizon Prep School CA 92067 presents a clearly identifiable classical-leaning program anchored in a Christ-centered framework. The school emphasizes a Trivium-inspired structure (grammar, logic, rhetoric), uses Harkness-style instructional approaches, and integrates biblical worldview throughout the curriculum and student life. Evidence across the site shows a robust religious/metaphysical orientation (Christ-centered education, biblical truths, chapel life) and a strong emphasis on formation through virtue, wisdom, and leadership development. Pedagogy and curriculum align strongly with classical practice (grammar/logic/rhetoric, rhetoric training, Socratic/seminar-style discussions, memorable memory-work, and formal rhetoric experiences). However, explicit references to Great Books, the Great Conversation, or a universal emphasis on Great Books as core curriculum are less visible, though the Harkness/Table approach and the triadic Trivium suggest alignment with classical methods. Parental partnership exists (Family Portal, family chapels, PTF), but the explicit framing of parents as “co-teachers” and a fully open-parent-campus ethos could be strengthened. Taken together, Horizon Prep is best characterized as SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL: strong classical grammar/logic/rhetoric pedagogy, explicit religious formation, and active family engagement, with room for more explicit Great-Books-focused articulation to reach full CLASSICAL status.

Consider highlighting Great Books integration on the curriculum pages in addition to the existing grammar/logic/rhetoric framing and Harkness methodology.

Expand open-campus opportunities and create a published “co-teacher” framework that makes parental partnership explicit in the public materials.
