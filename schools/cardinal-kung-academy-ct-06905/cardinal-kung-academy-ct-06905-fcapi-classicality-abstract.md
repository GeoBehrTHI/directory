## ABSTRACT

The public materials demonstrate formation-focused pedagogy and a liberal-arts curriculum framed within a classical tradition, with a substantial faculty presence and strong parent-community partnerships.

- Faculty: CLASSICAL, with a strong faculty presence and emphasis on mentorship/living curriculum; site language frames teachers as central to formation. 
- Parents & Community: CLASSICAL, strong parental partnership and community involvement language; open visits and parent formation are evident.
