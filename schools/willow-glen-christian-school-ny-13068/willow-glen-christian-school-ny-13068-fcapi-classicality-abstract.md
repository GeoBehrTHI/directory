## ABSTRACT

Willow Glen Christian School (WGCS) presents a primarily CLASSICAL orientation across the six classical-element categories, with notable strengths in religious/metaphysical foundations, purpose/goal, faculty, and parent/community engagement. Evidence shows explicit Christian underpinnings (Triune God, biblical foundation, liturgical practices such as chapel), a formation-focused telos (virtue, wisdom, moral imagination), and a classical framework (grammar → logic → rhetoric) supported by liberal-arts emphasis and place-based/project-based components. While the school demonstrates strong classical alignment, Element 3 (Curriculum) and Element 4 (Pedagogy) show blended approaches that include place-based and project-based methods alongside classical structure; explicit Great Books/Great Conversation framing and systematic Socratic pedagogy are not uniformly documented in public site copy, which places these categories at a level of SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL. The overall aggregate ranking is CLASSICAL.

Key sources evidencing WGCS’s classical/classical-Christian orientation include:
- Vision, Mission, and Theology: Vision to the glory of God; Triune God; biblical truths; liturgical worship; chapel access for community involvement.  
- Classical curriculum references: Grammar → Logic → Rhetoric; liberal arts; place-based and project-based learning integrated within a classical framework.  
- Pedagogical and philosophical orientation: engagement with truth/virtue/beauty as transcendentals; engagement with a Christian worldview in education. 
- Faculty and community: living curriculum, model teachers, and a strong partnership with parents and community organizations (PTF, open-campus policy).  

Additional contextual documents from the knowledge base (Six Essential Elements of a Classical School) provide the rubric’s keywords and categories, including the emphasis on liberal arts, Great Books, and liturgical/community dimensions as core signs of classical orientation. These elements underpin the keyword framework used to score each category and to justify determining WGCS as CLASSICAL overall.  

Executive Summary — Element-by-Element Details (selected quotes)
- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: WGCS’s creed and mission texts emphasize Christ-centered formation, biblical truth, and liturgical practice (chapel, worship). Public materials explicitly affirm the Triune God and a Christian worldview as foundational to education. “We affirm the historic Christian faith: ... the Triune God” and “Rooted in Christ” are repeated themes.  
- Purpose and Goal: The school’s mission/vision foreground virtue, wisdom, and formation (not mere information transfer or job prep). The philosophy of education emphasizes formation and the development of virtuous character. 
- Curriculum: Classical elements are present (grammar → logic → rhetoric; liberal arts; place-based learning). The site highlights “The elements of a classical education” and “Place-Based Learning” and “Project-Based Learning.” However, explicit Great Books/Great Conversation framing is not publicly documented in the site copy, leaving Element 3 as SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL.  
- Pedagogy: Classical pedagogy is evident in structure and aims, with evidence of memory work and rhetorical aims, yet the full Socratic/seminarial pedagogy is not uniformly documented across grades in the site copy. This yields a SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL rating for Pedagogy. 
- Faculty: The “living curriculum” approach and strong mentorship culture align with classical expectations. 
- Parents & Community: Active partnership with parents; robust PTF and open-campus ethos; parent formation and community events documented.
