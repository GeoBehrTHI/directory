## Executive Summary

Willow Glen Christian School (WGCS) presents a robust Classical Christian profile across multiple dimensions, anchored in explicit religious foundations, virtue-oriented aims, and a curriculum and pedagogy that align with classical education principles. Public materials consistently foreground a Christian worldview, the Triune God, and a formation-focused purpose (truth, goodness, beauty) anchored in biblical faith. The school emphasizes a liberal-arts orientation (grammar, logic, rhetoric), place-based learning, and project-based experiences integrated within a Christian framework. Overall, WGCS demonstrates a strong Classical orientation, with some category-specific nuances that are clearly “classical” in intent but also reflect more contemporary, place-based practices. Based on the six-category rubric, the school lands primarily CLASSICAL, with Element 3 (Curriculum) and Element 4 (Pedagogy) showing elements that are mixed or evolving within a classical frame.

What would move the needle
- If WGCS aimed for a fuller “Classical” alignment in Curriculum (Element 3) and Pedagogy (Element 4) without sacrificing place-based and project-based strengths, they could explicitly foreground and document a Great Books/Great Conversation framing and a more explicit, structured Socratic pedagogy continuum across grades, along with a clear articulation of scholé/restful practice in class routines. Concrete steps might include:
  - Publicly publish and reference a Great Books/Great Conversation list or core readings aligned with the three-stage Trivium framework.
  - Create explicit articulation of Socratic seminars and case-based seminars in upper grades with exemplar transcripts or rubrics.
  - Publish a formal “Scholé”/restful learning plan and schedule that components of meditation, reflection, and contemplation are embedded across the grade levels.

- No additional needle-movers are required if the school remains Classically oriented in most dimensions; however, the above would strengthen the Curriculum and Pedagogy dimensions toward a more unambiguously CLASSICAL classification.
