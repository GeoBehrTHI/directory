# ABSTRACT

St. Johns Classical Academy Florida presents a classical education profile with strong alignment to truth, virtue, and beauty, and a clear formation-driven mission. Foundational signals are CLASSICAL in Nature, with exceptional emphasis on character formation and civic virtue, and a strong faculty/community orientation. The curriculum and pedagogy areas show partial alignment (SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL), as explicit Great Books, Trivium elements, and full Scholé-based pedagogy are not uniformly documented in public materials. Parent/community signals are present but could be strengthened with explicit co-teaching and parent-formation programs. Overall, SJCA is SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL, with tangible strengths and targeted opportunities to achieve full CLASSICAL status through deeper articulation and public documentation of core classical curricular, pedagogical, liturgical, and family-engagement practices.
