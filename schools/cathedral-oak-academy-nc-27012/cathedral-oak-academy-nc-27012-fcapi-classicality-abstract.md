## ABSTRACT

Cathedral Oak Academy NC 27012 demonstrates a robust CLASSICAL orientation across all six categories. The Religious and Metaphysical Foundations category is strongly Christian and liturgical, embedding worship and Biblical worldview into the daily life and curriculum. The School Purpose and Goal category centers formation in wisdom and virtue, not merely job preparation, aligning with the classical ideal of truth, good, and beauty as real and knowable. The Curriculum category emphasizes liberal arts education (grammar, logic, rhetoric), Great Books and the Great Conversation, and a formation-first approach. Pedagogy shows a Trivium-based framework with memorization and chant in early years, and Socratic-style inquiry and rhetoric development expected in upper grades. Faculty are described as virtuous mentors and living curriculum, and Parents & Community reflect a partnership model with families as co-educators and supporters of the school’s classical mission.

Overall, Cathedral Oak Academy presents a consistent CLASSICAL profile with clear alignment to the Six Essential Elements of Classical Education. Evidence from the school’s materials includes explicit references to Great Books and the Great Conversation, the Trivium, Biblical worldview, liturgical practices, formation-based goals, partnered with families, and faculty as mentors within a small-class environment. The Positive Keyword Indicators dominate across categories, with little to no signal from Negative Keyword Indicators in public materials.
