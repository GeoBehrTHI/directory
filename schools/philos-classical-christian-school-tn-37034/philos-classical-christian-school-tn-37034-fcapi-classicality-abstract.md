## ABSTRACT

Philos Classical Christian School (TN 37034) exhibits a CLASSICAL orientation in Religious and Metaphysical Foundations and in School Purpose and Goal, with strong Christian/theological framing and formation goals. It demonstrates a robust Parents & Community orientation, emphasizing partnerships with parents and a biblically anchored, classical curriculum. However, in Curriculum, Pedagogy, and Faculty, the public materials show partial alignment with classical ideals, lacking explicit, widely-cited Great Books/Great Conversation commitments, detailed Socratic pedagogy across grades, and clearly stated “living curriculum” language for faculty. Consequently, the overall classification is SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL.
