## ABSTRACT

- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: Christ-centered, Word of God, formation-focused, and virtue-oriented language align with transcendental realism and theology-based grounding.
- School Purpose and Goal: Formation of virtue and wisdom as the telos; education as formation rather than workforce training, with a clear Christian telos.
- Curriculum: Grammar/Logic/Rhetoric structure (K–8; 9–12), Great Books/Great Conversation implications, and integrated liberal-arts framing (history and other disciplines) evidenced by explicit program names and Great Books mention.
- Pedagogy: Classical progression through grammar/logic/rhetoric, formation-focused pedagogy, and discourse-oriented aims; implied Socratic-style inquiry via classical methods.
- Faculty: Public-facing statements of mentors/teachers and the living curriculum, plus named faculty.
- Parents & Community: Strong emphasis on parent partnership and co-education, with explicit invitations for family engagement and formation.

Overall, Samuel Fuller School appears to be a robust CLASSICAL educational institution by design, with public materials consistently reflecting core classical elements such as formation, Great Books, a Trivium-focused curriculum, teacher-student mentorship, and active parent partnership. The explicit Christian framing and integration with a stated mission to form students who love and serve God further reinforce its classical, faith-informed orientation.
