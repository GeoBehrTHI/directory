## Executive Summary

Samuel Fuller School (SFS) presents a clear classical Christian orientation across its public materials. The site/profile emphasizes Christ-centered classical education, formation of character, and a triadic structure of the liberal arts (grammar, logic, rhetoric) through a Grammar & Logic School (K-8) and a Rhetoric School (9-12). It foregrounds formation of the whole person, virtue, wisdom, truth, goodness, and beauty, and it explicitly ties education to a Christian telos and the Word of God. Public statements about a Portrait of a Graduate, partnership with parents, and strong emphasis on Great Books and integrated formation align closely with the Six Essential Elements framework for classical education.

Overall classification: CLASSICAL.

Key evidentiary notes
- Public proclamations of a Christ-centered, classical Christian education for K–12, with explicit aim to form students who love and serve God with wisdom, grace, truth, and courage. 
- Explicit reference to classical pedagogy markers (Great Books immersion; Grammar & Logic vs. Rhetoric School structure). 
- Portrait of a Graduate emphasizes core intellectual and moral aims (listening, critical thinking, eloquent communication, service, faithfulness). 
- Strong parental partnership ethos and open-campus sensibilities; explicit parent-teacher collaboration language.
