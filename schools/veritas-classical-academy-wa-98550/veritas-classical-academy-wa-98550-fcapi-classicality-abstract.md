## ABSTRACT

Veritas Classical Academy Grays Harbor (WA 98550) demonstrates strong Classical signals, anchored by a Christ-centered mission, Scriptural grounding, and a Trivium-focused pedagogy. Across the six categories, the school scores CLASSICAL in Religious and Metaphysical Foundations, School Purpose and Goal, Pedagogy, and Parents & Community, with SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL signals in Curriculum and Faculty. The Faculty signals are present via board/staff structures and mentorship language, but explicit “living curriculum” framing could be strengthened. The Parents & Community signals are strong, with explicit in loco parentis policy and a partnership orientation. Overall, the school is CLASSICAL, with actionable opportunities to push Element 3 (Curriculum) and Element 5 (Faculty) toward full CLASSICAL alignment.
