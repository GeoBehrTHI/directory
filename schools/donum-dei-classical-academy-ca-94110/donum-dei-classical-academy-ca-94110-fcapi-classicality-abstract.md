## ABSTRACT

The Trivium-based Curriculum and a pedagogy that blends contemplative and Socratic approaches align with Classical criteria. Faculty are positioned as living exemplars and mentors, and Parents & Community are integrated as co-educators in partnership with the school. The aggregate classification is CLASSICAL, with Category 3 (Curriculum) showing SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL due to less explicit Great Books labeling across all core curriculum pages, though Trivium progression, history integration, and liberal-arts framing are clearly present. The overall impression is of a classical Christian program that emphasizes formation, virtue, and a shared life of inquiry anchored in biblical truth and a Paideia approach.

- Categories rated CLASSICAL: Religious and Metaphysical Foundations; School Purpose and Goal; Pedagogy; Faculty; Parents & Community.
- Category rated SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL: Curriculum (explicit Great Books labeling is not uniformly present on public pages; however, Trivium and history integration are evident).
