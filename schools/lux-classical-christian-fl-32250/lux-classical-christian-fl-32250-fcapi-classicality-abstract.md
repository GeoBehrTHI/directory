## ABSTRACT

Lux Classical Christian Jacksonville Beach, FL (32250) presents a robust CLASSICAL education anchored in a Christian worldview, with a clear Trivium-based curriculum (Grammar, Logic, Rhetoric), Latin instruction, Bible/history focus, and a strong parental-partnership model. The school’s public materials consistently emphasize formation, virtue, wisdom, and leadership within a biblically framed, collaborative community. The use of Veritas Press as the classical curriculum provider, the explicit Trivium progression, and the structured upper-stage emphasis on rhetoric and argumentation are decisive indicators of classical pedagogy. The school’s stated pillars—God’s Word, Classical Education, and Parental Involvement—ground the program in theology while maintaining a rigorous classical framework. Parental engagement is not only encouraged but integrated into the learning model, aligning with the classical education tradition of the “living curriculum.”
