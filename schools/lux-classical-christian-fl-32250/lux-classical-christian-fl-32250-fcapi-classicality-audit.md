## Executive Summary

Lux Classical Christian Jacksonville Beach (FL 32250) presents a clearly classicalChristian approach anchored in a biblically grounded, Trivium-informed program and a strong emphasis on parental partnership. Public materials consistently frame Lux as a classical program integrated with a Christian worldview, centered on character formation, virtue, truth, and wisdom, and built around collaboration with families rather than a purely school-centric model. Evidence across mission statements, curricular descriptions, and admissions practices shows a commitment to the classical rubric’s core elements: grammar/logic/rhetoric through grade levels, Latin and classical history study, and a pedagogy that blends contemplative elements with Socratic/Discursive opportunities at higher levels. Parental involvement is foregrounded as a structural, intentional component of the education, not an afterthought, and the school positions itself as a Christ-centered community that seeks to partner with families in the formation of virtuous, well-educated young people.

Overall classification: CLASSICAL

Key insights by category (brief):
- Religious & Metaphysical Foundations: Strong, explicit Christian framing (biblical worldview, God’s Word, Christ-centered leadership). The school embraces theology as a core driver of formation.
- Purpose & Goal: Formation and cultivation of virtue and wisdom are foregrounded; education is portrayed as formation aligned with truth, goodness, and beauty through a Christian lens.
- Curriculum: Core classical elements are present (Grammar, Logic, Rhetoric; Latin; History; Bible). Use of Veritas Press places curriculum in a classical framework; however, explicit Great Books/Great Conversation emphasis is not clearly stated in public site content.
- Pedagogy: Evidence of classic pedagogy (Trivium progression, memory-rich early years, rhetoric-focused upper years). The site emphasizes collaboration with parents and a structured in-class schedule.
- Faculty: The site emphasizes collaboration, mentorship, and the idea of a shared “living curriculum,” with a founder/leadership voice and in-school teacher roles implied through mission and admissions content.
- Parents & Community: Strong emphasis on parental partnership, co-teaching model, and ongoing opportunities to engage (tours, conversations, events like Kindergarten Coffee).
