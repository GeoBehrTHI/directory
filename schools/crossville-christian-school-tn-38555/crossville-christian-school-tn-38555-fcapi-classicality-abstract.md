## ABSTRACT

Crossville Christian School (Crossville, TN, 38555) demonstrates strong Religious and Metaphysical Foundations and robust Parent/Community engagement, with formation and theology clearly anchored in public materials. The Curriculum and Pedagogy signals, however, do not align fully with classical education—public content reflects a biblically-based, Christian-curriculum approach (ABEKA) rather than a liberal-arts canon or Great Books framework, and explicit classical pedagogy (Socratic seminars, Scholé, memory work) is not evident in the public-facing materials. The Faculty signal is present but not foregrounded as a classical “living curriculum,” while the Parents & Community dimension aligns well with classical expectations of parental partnership and formation.

Overall classification: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL.

- Primary strengths: Religious foundations and doctrinal clarity; strong parent partnership; formation language in mission and values.
- Key gaps: Explicit classical curriculum (Great Books, Trivium/Quadrivium), explicit classical pedagogy (Socratic method, memorization, scholé), and a clearly articulated living-curriculum framework for faculty.
