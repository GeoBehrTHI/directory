## Method and Lexicon Basis

This audit uses the **Six Essential Elements of a Classical School** keyword framework (positive and negative indicators) across six categories: Religious & Metaphysical Foundations, Purpose & Goal, Curriculum, Pedagogy, Faculty, Parents & Community . It also uses the condensed **classicality lexicon** that highlights core terms such as *truth, goodness, beauty, virtue, Trivium, Quadrivium, Socratic, scholé, wonder, chanting, project-based learning, experiential learning,* etc., as positive or negative signals .

For each category below:

1. I list **keywords/phrases actually present** in the Annapolis Christian Academy crawl.    
2. I compare them with the **positive/negative keyword lists** from the Six Elements document and lexicon.    
3. I estimate **positive vs. negative keyword frequency** (approximate proportions).  
4. I assign a **category rating**: CLASSICAL / SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL / NOT CLASSICAL, with justification.
