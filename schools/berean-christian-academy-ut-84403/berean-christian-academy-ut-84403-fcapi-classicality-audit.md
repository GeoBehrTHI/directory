## Executive Summary

Berean Christian Academy Utah (UT 84403) demonstrates strong alignment with classical education across all six canonical categories of the classical rubric. Public-facing materials emphasize the grammar, logic, and rhetoric approach; a curriculum that includes primary sources and Latin; pedagogy that moves from chant and memory in the grammar years to Socratic/debate forms in the dialectic years and rhetoric-focused instruction in the upper grades; a faculty oriented toward mentorship and living the curriculum; and a robust emphases on parental partnership and community formation grounded in a biblical framework. Sources from the school’s site clearly reference classical methods, theology, liturgical and communal practices, and explicit parent partnership, all of which map closely to the Positive Keyword indicators in the Six Essential Elements framework. There is little to no explicit negative-language signaling anti-classical orientations in the school’s public materials. Overall classification: CLASSICAL.

Key evidentiary anchors include:
- Direct statements of a classical method (grammar, logic, rhetoric) and a K–12 commitment, with emphasis on Latin, primary sources, and memorization (grammar, memory, and recitation) across grades, plus upper-grade Socratic/debate elements.  
- Theological and liturgical orientations tied to a biblical framework, including explicit statements about Biblical instruction for families, theology as a unifying discipline, and communal worship/formation.  
- Explicit references to virtue, truth, and beauty as transcendent aims, with affirmations of “true, noble, just” thought and a liturgical-aesthetic vision.  
- Public materials indicating a cooperative parent-school formation model and parent involvement as co-teachers or partners.
