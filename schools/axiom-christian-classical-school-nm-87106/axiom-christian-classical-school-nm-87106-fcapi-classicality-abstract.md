## ABSTRACT

Axiom Christian Classical School NM 87106 presents a Christian-classical orientation with a strong formation and virtue emphasis. The public materials demonstrate a robust Religious and Metaphysical Foundations alignment (theology, Church Fathers, orthodox-theology references) and a formation-centered School Purpose and Goal. The Curriculum, Pedagogy, Faculty, and Parents & Community dimensions show mixed alignment: the public content most strongly signals formation and classical posture at leadership and mission levels, but explicit classical curricular and pedagogical practices (Great Books, Great Conversation, Trivium/Quadrivium, Socratic pedagogy) are not clearly documented in accessible public pages. Consequently, the school is best categorized as SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL overall, rather than fully CLASSICAL. Key opportunities to move toward CLASSICAL include making explicit commitments to Great Books, Trivium/Quadrivium, and Socratic pedagogy; codifying a living curriculum and parent-education program; and publishing concrete examples of contemplative Scholé practices and memory work across grade bands. 

- Category 1: CLASSICAL
  - Evidence: Theology, Church Fathers, Orthodox orientation; timeless-truth framing; Christian/classical identity across leadership and messaging.   
- Category 2: CLASSICAL
  - Evidence: Formation-focused language; virtue-oriented teleology; “form[ing] the Virtuous Scholar.”  
- Category 3: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL
  - Evidence: Classical framing exists; explicit Great Books/Great Conversation and Trivium/Quadrivium are not publicly documented. 
- Category 4: NOT CLASSICAL
  - Evidence: No explicit, public Pedagogy signatures (Socratic, scholé, memorization, etc.). 
- Category 5: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL
  - Evidence: Named administrators and faculty with classical orientation; living-curriculum language is implied but not fully published. 
- Category 6: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL
  - Evidence: Community/family engagement signals exist; formal parent-education programs and explicit open-campus language are not clearly documented. 

End of ABSTRACT.
