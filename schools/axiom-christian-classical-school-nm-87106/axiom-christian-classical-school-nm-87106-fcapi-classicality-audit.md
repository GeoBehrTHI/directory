## Executive Summary

Axiom Christian Classical School NM 87106 presents a clearly Christian-classical orientation embedded in a K–12 program, with explicit emphasis on forming virtuous, thinking individuals within a classical framework. Public-facing materials stress formation over mere information, a timeless-truth orientation, and a strong Christian/theological lens. Evidence from the site shows direct references to forming the “Virtuous Scholar,” a Christian/classical backbone, and connections to Church Fathers and Orthodox theology. However, explicit pedagogy and curriculum practices that align with all six classical elements (notably explicit Great Books/Great Conversation, heavy use of the Trivium, and documented Socratic pedagogy in the upper grades) are less visible in the public-facing materials. Taken together, the school is best characterized as SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL: it demonstrates substantial classical-Christian orientation and formation aims, but lacks explicit, citable articulation of several core classical practices (especially in curriculum and pedagogy) that would push it to a full CLASSICAL rating.
