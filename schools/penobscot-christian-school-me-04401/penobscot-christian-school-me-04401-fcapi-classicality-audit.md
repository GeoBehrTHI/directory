## Executive Summary

Across categories, PCS demonstrates clear classical markers, especially in religious/theological foundations, formation-focused goals, and Socratic/seminar-style pedagogy in the upper grades. The school consistently emphasizes faith, virtue, truth, and beauty as real and knowable; it also frames parents as co-educators in a traditional, faith-driven community. Overall, the school aligns closely with classical criteria, with particularly strong signals in Religious and Metaphysical Foundations, Purpose and Goal, Pedagogy, and Parents & Community. Some curricular signaling is strong (grammar/logic/rhetoric) but there is less explicit evidence of certain Great-Books/Quadrivium components within publicly available site materials, which places Curriculum at a level of SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL rather than fully CLASSICAL.
