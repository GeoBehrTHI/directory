## ABSTRACT

Penobscot Christian School demonstrates a strong Classical Christian orientation across most criteria. The school presents a biblically-grounded, theology-infused foundation (Religious and Metaphysical Foundations) and a formation-focused Purpose/Goal that emphasizes virtue, wisdom, and human flourishing (Category 2). The Curriculum is anchored in the Trivium (grammar/logic/rhetoric), supplemented by a clearly articulated classical pedagogy that combines contemplative memory work with Socratic discussion and rhetorical training (Categories 3 and 4). Faculty structure and culture align with classical expectations (Category 5), and the Parents & Community context strongly supports parental partnership and involvement (Category 6). While Category 3 (Curriculum) signals a robust classical framework, the site content provides less explicit documentation of Great Books and Quadrivium elements, which tempers the Curriculum signal to SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL rather than FULL CLASSICAL. Overall, PCS ranks CLASSICAL with notable strength in theology, formation, pedagogy, faculty strength, and parent-community engagement.
