# Classicality Audit: Austin Classical School (Austin, TX)

Website used: https://austinclassical.org

## Executive Summary
Overall classification: CLASSICAL

Austin Classical School (ACS) explicitly frames its mission as “to glorify Jesus Christ…[by] cultivat[ing] wise, virtuous, and humble students through an excellent classical, Christian education,” and consistently grounds its model in truth, goodness, beauty, the Trivium, Latin, logic, rhetoric, and the Great Tradition. The site demonstrates robust theological integration (Christian Studies sequence including Doctrine, Exegesis/Hermeneutics, Apologetics & Ethics), memory-intensive pedagogy (memorization, recitation, songs), dialectical practices (debate, discussion), and rhetorical culminating work (thesis defense). Parent partnership is explicit (“co‑laboring with families”) and the school encourages parent education with reading lists and information sessions. Evidence for a formal “portrait of a teacher” or faculty-as-living‑curriculum is thinner than other areas but still directionally aligned. On balance, ACS meets the classical benchmark across nearly all elements, with strongest evidence in Religious Foundations, Purpose, Curriculum, and Pedagogy.
