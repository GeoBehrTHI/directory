## Executive Summary

Aquinas Academy (Bear, DE, 19701) presents a distinctly Catholic, liberal-arts oriented program rooted in a robust religious worldview. Public materials emphasize a Catholic mission, liturgical life, and a “liberal arts” curriculum integrated with faith. Evidence across the site shows explicit religious-theological framing (Catholic environment, liturgy, sacraments, gravitating around the Divine Liturgy and Catholic doctrine) coupled with a clear commitment to classical-inspired aims (liberal arts education, emphasis on formation, virtuous living, and eloquent rhetoric). The school foregrounds formation of character and moral imagination within a Catholic, tradition-rich framework, while implementing a widely-applied graduation structure and a broad PK–12 program.

Overall classification by category:

- Element 2 (Purpose and Goal): CLASSICAL
- Element 3 (Curriculum): SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL
- Element 4 (Pedagogy): SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL
- Element 5 (Faculty): SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL

Aggregate: CLASSICAL

What stands out most is a strong religious foundation and a clearly articulated formation-focused mission. What would move the needle toward a more fully CLASSICAL profile are explicit, public statements of core classical curricular components (grammar, logic, rhetoric) and more explicit evidence of Socratic/seminar pedagogy in upper grades, along with a more explicit articulation of the “Great Books” / “Great Conversation” framework and a more developed portrait of the faculty as a living curriculum.

- Liturgical and communal practices are highlighted (twice-monthly Masses, confession, Eucharistic Adoration, pilgrimages, retreats).
- The Gravissimum Educationis framework is invoked to relate all human culture to the good news of salvation, and to glorify God in education. This includes references to Divine Liturgy and the Sacraments as integral to formation.
- Public materials affirm a theological grounding (Our Philosophy, Spiritual Life, and theology credits in graduation requirements).
