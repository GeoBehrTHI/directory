Overall Classification: CLASSICAL

## ABSTRACT

This evaluation concludes that Awakening Academy (Smithfield, RI, 02917) is CLASSICAL across all six categories of the classical school rubric. Evidence from the school’s public materials indicates a Christ-centered, biblically grounded program, with a Trivium-based curriculum (grammar, logic, rhetoric), Latin study, and engagement with Great Books. The school emphasizes virtue, wisdom, and Christian leadership as the telos of education, and it indicators of formation rather than mere information. Pedagogy features include Socratic discussion and rhetoric training, while faculty are portrayed as mentors who model virtue and serve as the living curriculum. Parental partnership and local church involvement are central to the school’s community model.

Six-category keyword analysis shows robust alignment with the Positive Keyword indicators for Religious & Metaphysical Foundations, Purpose & Goal, Curriculum, Pedagogy, Faculty, and Parents & Community, with little to no explicit negative keyword presence in the publicly available content. The aggregate assessment is CLASSICAL, with a clear articulation of the Great Books/literary emphasis, Trivium-based pedagogy, virtue formation, and a community framework built around family and church involvement. The primary opportunities to further strengthen CLASSICAL status would be to foreground the Scholé/restful dimensions more explicitly in public materials and to provide additional explicit high-school-level Socratic/seminar evidence if the school expands beyond 8th grade.
