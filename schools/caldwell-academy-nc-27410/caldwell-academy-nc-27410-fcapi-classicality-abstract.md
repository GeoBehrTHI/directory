## ABSTRACT
- Overall: CLASSICAL. The school’s public materials consistently affirm transcendental moral realism (truth, goodness, beauty), articulate education as the cultivation of wisdom and virtue, and show integration of theology across the curriculum. The Trivium structure, Latin, logic, rhetoric, great books, and an integrated, theologically informed curriculum are explicit. Pedagogy reflects memory work, dialectic discussion, and rhetoric/oratory. Faculty ethos is Christian and classical but would benefit from more explicit presentation of teachers as mentors and a “portrait of a teacher.” Parent partnership is clear; formation is supported by recommended readings; community structures include a house system.
- Category summaries:
  1) Religious & Metaphysical: CLASSICAL (moral realism + theology integration; liturgical practices not explicit)  .
  2) Purpose & Goal: CLASSICAL (formation toward wisdom/virtue; transcendental aims; de-emphasis on job training)  .
  3) Curriculum: CLASSICAL (Trivium; Latin/logic/rhetoric; great books; integrated study; history as history)  .
  4) Pedagogy: CLASSICAL (memorization/recitation, debate/discussion, thesis/orations; no explicit scholé language)  .
  5) Faculty: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL (virtuous/Christian faculty noted; limited explicit language about mentor-modeling and faculty collegium) .
  6) Parents & Community: CLASSICAL (explicit partnership; parent formation via reading list; house system community)   .
