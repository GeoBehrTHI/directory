# Classicality Audit Report

Executive Summary
- School: Austin Classical School (austinclassical.org)
- Overall Classification: CLASSICAL
- Highlights:
  - Mission explicitly frames education as the cultivation of wisdom and virtue in Christ and orients learning toward truth, goodness, and beauty, grounded in Christian theology (glorify Jesus Christ; classical, Christian education; cultivate wise, virtuous, humble students)  .
  - Clear Trivium-anchored curriculum (Grammar, Logic, Rhetoric), with Latin, formal Logic, Rhetoric, senior thesis, and an integrated approach that treats knowledge as interconnected under God’s sovereignty   .
  - Pedagogy demonstrates memorization/recitation/songs in the lower school, debate/discussion in the logic years, and oratory/thesis defense in the rhetoric years—hallmarks of classical method   .
  - Parent partnership is explicit (“co-laboring with families”), parent formation is encouraged via a recommended classical reading list, and a house system is part of the community life   .

Note on method: Keyword indicators and rating criteria are taken from Six Essential Elements of a Classical School (positive/negative signals across the six categories) and the classicality lexicon used by this audit  .
