## ABSTRACT

Aquinas Classical Academy WA 98332 demonstrates a cohesive Classical Christian program with a pronounced Catholic identity and a robust classical curriculum. The school’s public materials reflect a strong Religious and Metaphysical Foundations orientation (Catholic theology, Magisterium, Our Lady Seat of Wisdom, Aquinas) and a clear formation-centric purpose (faith, reason, virtue; truth as knowable; eternal telos). The Curriculum is anchored in liberal arts: grammar, dialectic, rhetoric; Great Books/primary sources; history within Western civilization; Latin; sacred theology. Pedagogy combines contemplative/restful elements with Socratic discourse and recitation-based methods, aligning with scholé and memory traditions. Faculty are positioned as living exemplars and mentors, and Parents & Community are presented as co-educators and essential partners in formation, with active pathways for involvement and decision-making.
