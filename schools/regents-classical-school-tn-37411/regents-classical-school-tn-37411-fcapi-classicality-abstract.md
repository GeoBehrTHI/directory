## ABSTRACT

Regents Classical School Chattanooga (ZIP 37411) exhibits a partial classical orientation, anchored by a Christian/theological foundation and a formation/teleological aim. The public materials clearly frame education within a Christian worldview (“Raising up citizens of the City of God” and “grounded in Christian faith”) and emphasize formation and virtue in pursuit of a life of purpose through wisdom and eloquence. This signals alignment with Religious and Metaphysical Foundations and School Purpose & Goal, placing the school in the SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL category for those areas.

However, explicit curricular and pedagogical signals central to the classical rubric—such as a clearly articulated liberal-arts curriculum (grammar, logic, rhetoric), heavy emphasis on Great Books and Great Conversation, explicit Socratic pedagogy, and a living-curriculum faculty model—are not clearly demonstrated in the observable public content. As a result, Category 3 (Curriculum), Category 4 (Pedagogy), and Category 5 (Faculty) lean NOT CLASSICAL, while Category 6 (Parents & Community) shows genuine engagement but not yet fully formalized parent formation at scale, yielding a SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL rating in that area.

Overall, the aggregate assessment falls into SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL, with clear opportunities to move toward CLASSICAL through explicit curricular and pedagogical commitments, expanded faculty modeling language, and stronger articulation of Great Books and liberal-arts sequences. The school’s strengths lie in its theological grounding and community/parent engagement, while its gaps lie in explicit classical curriculum and pedagogy signals.
