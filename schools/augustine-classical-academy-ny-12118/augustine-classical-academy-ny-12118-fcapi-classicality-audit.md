# Classicality Audit: Augustine Classical Academy (Mechanicville, NY)

Website used: https://augustineca.org/ 

## Executive Summary
- Overall classification: CLASSICAL
- Why: The school explicitly articulates a classical Christian philosophy grounded in Scripture and the liberal arts; it implements the Trivium across Grammar, Dialectic, and Rhetoric stages; and it evidences hallmark pedagogies (chanting/recitation in lower grades; Socratic dialogue, debates, speeches, a thesis and viva voce in upper school). Parent partnership and community formation are unusually strong. Some marketing emphasis on AP/SAT metrics and a stated “project-based learning” emphasis are mild divergences from traditional classical signals but do not outweigh the substantial classical evidence.    

Highlights:
- Christ-centered, Scripture-integrated academics; repeated references to “wisdom,” “truth, goodness, and beauty,” and a “biblical worldview” across pages.  
- Trivium structure with formal logic, rhetoric, great ideas/books, Great Works of Theology, Latin (3rd grade) and Greek through senior year, primary sources, and a capstone thesis with viva voce.  
- Robust pedagogy: chanting/singing/recitation in the Grammar School; graded Socratic discussions, debates, speeches, and a 30‑page thesis defense in the Upper School; explicit cultivation of “wonder.”   
- Parent partnership is explicit and practiced: in loco parentis framing, parents present during the day, and a classical book club with parents and faculty.
