## ABSTRACT

Our Lady of the Rosary Catholic School (OLR), Greenville, SC, presents a coherent CLASSICAL educational model grounded in Catholic theology and Western classical education. The school frames its mission around formation, truth, goodness, and beauty, with a decidedly Catholic worldview integrated throughout its curriculum and pedagogy. The curriculum emphasizes liberal arts and Latin, plus a Western-civilization orientation and a Great-Conversation mindset, complemented by memory-based pedagogy (recitation, chanting) and Socratic discussion in the upper grades. The faculty is portrayed as living exemplars and mentors, with a robust parental partnership structure and community integration through a parish-centered model and advisory roles. The public materials consistently align with the six classical-category indicators: religious/metaphysical foundations; formation-focused goals; liberal-arts curriculum; contemplative and dialectic pedagogy; model faculty; and active parent/community partnership. Overall classification: CLASSICAL across all six categories.
