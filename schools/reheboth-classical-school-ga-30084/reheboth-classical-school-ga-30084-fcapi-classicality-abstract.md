# ABSTRACT

- This evaluation classifies Reheboth Classical Christian Academy Ga 30084 as CLASSICAL across all six categories of the classical-school rubric (Religious & Metaphysical Foundations; Purpose & Goal; Curriculum; Pedagogy; Faculty; Parents & Community). The assessment rests on the Positive Keyword Indicators and criteria in the Six Essential Elements document, as well as the Positive Keyword signals for religious/metaphysical, curricular, and pedagogical themes typical of classical Christian education. The presence of theology, liturgical or communal practice, the liberal-arts-based curriculum (grammar, logic, rhetoric; trivium; quadrivium; Great Books; Great Conversation), contemplative pedagogy (Scholé), and a strong emphasis on virtue and formation (virtue ethics; ethical cultivation; formation of character) are aligned with the classical educational ideal. The public-facing signals—especially the Christian framing, historical/Great Books emphasis, and a pedagogical balance of memory work, Socratic inquiry, and contemplative learning—are consistent with a CLASSICAL classification in all six categories. The literature confirms these practices as central to classical Christian education and the rubric supports a CLASSICAL rating for a school adopting these elements (with parent/community involvement and a living-faculty model as essential features).

End of # ABSTRACT
