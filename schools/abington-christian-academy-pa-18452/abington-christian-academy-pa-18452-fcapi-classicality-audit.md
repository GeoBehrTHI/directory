# Abington Christian Academy — Classicality Audit

Executive Summary TL;DR
- Overall classification: CLASSICAL
- Why: The school’s public materials repeatedly affirm a Christian classical vision centered on truth, goodness, and beauty; theology as the integrating “logos” across subjects; a liberal‑arts curriculum with Latin, Great Books, history (not “social studies”), and a memory‑rich grammar‑stage pedagogy with singing/chanting/recitation. These directly match the Six Essential Elements lexicon’s positive indicators (truth/goodness/beauty; sacred scripture; logos; trivium; Great Books; recitation/chant) and avoid the negative indicators (e.g., “college and career readiness,” “project‑based learning” dominance)      .
- Watchouts: Fewer explicit statements on faculty formation (portrait of a teacher, mentorship) and on parent partnership/parent formation. Strengthening these would round out an otherwise strong classical profile.

How to read this report
- Each category below shows: (1) keywords we found on the school’s site, (2) how they map to the classical lexicon from Six Essential Elements, (3) a positive/negative hit count and coverage estimate, and (4) a category rating.
- Lexicon and scoring framework: Six Essential Elements of a Classical School and the provided classicality lexicon (positive and negative keyword indicators)    .

- Liturgical/communal practice terms (chapel, liturgy, daily prayer) were not explicit.

3) Quantitative estimate

- Percent positive: 100%
- Percent coverage: 67% (2 of 3 sub‑areas strongly evidenced: transcendental moral realism and theological grounding; liturgical/communal practice not evidenced)

4) Rating: CLASSICAL

- Dorothy Sayers’ “Poll Parrot Stage” (Grammar Stage page) emphasizes repetition and memory work, singing/chanting/recitation 

2) Mapping to lexicon
- Positive matches: recitation, chanting, singing, memory work; strong alignment for K–6 pedagogy (Pedagogy positives) .
- No explicit “Socratic”/seminar claims; however, the site appears focused on PK–6/Grammar Stage, which is not penalized per guidance (upper‑school Socratic begins later).

3) Quantitative estimate
- Hits: +5/-0
- Percent positive: 100%
- Percent coverage: 67% of relevant K–8 items (strong lower‑school memory/chant; no explicit “restful learning/scholé” statement; upper‑school Socratic/rhetoric not applicable here)

4) Rating: CLASSICAL (for the school’s grade band)

Aggregate Rating: CLASSICAL
- Strengths aligned with classical essentials: explicit transcendental aims (true/good/beautiful) and theological integration (logos/Christ‑centered); liberal‑arts/trivium content with Latin and Great Books; robust grammar‑stage pedagogy of recitation/chant/singing and Bible‑anchored memory work (matching Six Elements keyword lists)      .
- Growth areas: publish explicit statements on faculty as mentors/models of virtue and on structured parent partnership/parent formation to strengthen Categories 5 and 6 (per Six Elements’ Faculty and Parents & Community indicators) .

- Several spammy blog artifacts (e.g., “casino” pages) appeared in the crawl; these are irrelevant to school mission and were excluded from scoring  .
