## Executive Summary

Emmanuel Christian School (WV 26301) presents a robust classical Christian profile across all six classical elements. Public materials repeatedly foreground a Christian worldview anchored in Scripture, a formation-focused aim (virtue, wisdom, human flourishing), a liberal-arts curriculum (grammar, logic, rhetoric) with explicit use of Great Books and classical texts, and pedagogical commitments that include both contemplative Scholé-oriented practices and higher-level rhetoric and Socratic-style inquiry. The school publicly aligns with the Association of Classical and Christian Schools (ACCS), emphasizes partnership with families, and situates its mission within a church context (Emmanuel Baptist Church), all of which reinforce a classical, formation-first approach rather than purely information-transfer. The evidence strongly supports CLASSICAL across all six categories.

Key evidentiary threads include: the triad of truth, goodness, and beauty framed as transcendent ideals; a purpose framed as formation and character formation rather than mere workforce preparation; a curriculum that foregrounds grammar–logic–rhetoric, Great Books and the liberal arts, and explicit attention to writing through Progymnasmata; a pedagogy that blends contemplative/restful practices with Socratic/seminar-style and rhetoric training; a faculty stance that emphasizes mentorship, virtue modeling, and a living curriculum; and an explicit parent/community partnership approach that treats families as co-educators and seeks ongoing engagement with classical-education formation.”

Evidence snippets and sources:
- The Logic & Rhetoric School page anchors the religious/metaphysical foundation with a clear statement that students grow to delight in the truth, goodness, and beauty of God as revealed through Scripture. That foundational stance is reinforced by explicit emphasis on a Christian, Scripture-saturated learning environment. 
- The Home page notes the foundation of Emmanuel Baptist Church as foundational to the school, illustrating a grounded theological and liturgical framework. 
- The Eagle Council description emphasizes formation “to live a godly and productive life” with an emphasis on edification, education, and evangelization, illustrating the school’s religious mission and communal practices. 
- Curriculum materials repeatedly reference a classical approach (grammar, logic, rhetoric), a Great Books orientation, and explicit classical aims (e.g., “great books and liberal arts,” “progymnasmata,” Senior Thesis, etc.). These elements are visible across multiple ECS materials, including curriculum-focused blog posts and program descriptions.  
- ACCS affiliation is stated, signaling formal alignment with classical Christian schooling norms. 

Overall category ratings (as detailed in the section below) support CLASSICAL for Emmanuel Christian School.
