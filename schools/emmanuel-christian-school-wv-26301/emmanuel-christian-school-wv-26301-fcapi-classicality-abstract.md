## ABSTRACT

Emmanuel Christian School (WV 26301) is classified as CLASSICAL across all six classical-element categories: Religious/Metaphysical Foundations, School Purpose and Goal, Curriculum, Pedagogy, Faculty, and Parents & Community. The school presents a Christ-centered, Scripture-saturated educational vision that emphasizes truth, goodness, and beauty as transcendent ideals; formation and virtue are core purposes; a classical curriculum (grammar–logic–rhetoric, Great Books, classical languages) underpins teaching; pedagogy combines contemplative Scholé practices with Socratic/seminar and rhetoric education; a living, mentorship-rich faculty structure anchors the learning environment; and a robust partnership with families and active church/community involvement supports the holistic formation of students. Evidence includes explicit statements about the Great Books/ liberal-arts mission, Progymnasmata-based writing, language study (Greek now, Latin planned), ACCS affiliation, and explicit parent-community partnership language. The aggregate assessment indicates Emmanuel Christian School fulfills the core criteria of classical education as defined by the Six Essential Elements framework.

Key primary sources referenced in this evaluation include the Logic & Rhetoric School content (foundation in Scripture and God-centered learning), the school’s home/church affiliation, ACCS membership, the Eagle Council mission, curriculum writings (Great Books, Progymnasmata, grammar–logic–rhetoric progression), pedagogy discussions (Socratic/recitation/choral elements), faculty leadership (headmaster and grammar-school principal), and parent/community partnership materials.

Citations throughout the analysis reference the ECS public materials and associated classroom/leadership documents cited above.
