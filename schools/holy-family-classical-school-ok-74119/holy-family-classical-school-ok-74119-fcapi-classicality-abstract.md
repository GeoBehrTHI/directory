## ABSTRACT

 Holy Family Classical School Ok 74119 demonstrates CLASSICAL alignment across all six categories of the classical education rubric with minor curricular nuances in early grades. The school’s public materials consistently articulate a Catholic, formation-first mission anchored in Truth, Goodness, Beauty, and Unity. The curriculum foregrounds liberal arts (grammar, logic, rhetoric), Great Books, and canonical Western civilization within a classical frame, supported by seminar- and rhetoric-infused pedagogy and a faculty described as a living curriculum and a community of “faculty of friends.” Parental partnership (Martin Guild), open engagement with families, and robust financial-aid programs further reinforce the classical community ethos. The Montessori presence in the early years introduces a non-classical element that slightly tempers the Curriculum category, resulting in a SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL rating for that category while the remaining five categories strongly meet classical criteria. The aggregate classification is CLASSICAL.
