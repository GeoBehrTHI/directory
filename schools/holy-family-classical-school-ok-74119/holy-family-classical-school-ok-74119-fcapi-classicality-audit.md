## Executive Summary

 Holy Family Classical School Ok 74119 (HFCS) demonstrates a consistent classical orientation across most categories of the Six Essential Elements framework. Public materials emphasize a Catholic, truth-centered curriculum oriented toward formation in virtue, wisdom, and beauty; the mission explicitly centers on forming Christian disciples through a classical, Catholic education. Evidence across the site highlights Truth, Goodness, Beauty, Unity, and a strong Catholic identity, reinforced by accreditation through OCCSAA/OPSAC and a leadership team with classical-education credentials. The school also foregrounds parental partnerships (e.g., Martin Guild) and robust financial-aid programs to enable access to a classical education. Notable classical markers include explicit references to liberal arts (grammar, logic, rhetoric), exposure to great books, and seminar- and rhetoric-inflected pedagogy. The primary caveat is the presence of Montessori programming in the early grades, which introduces some non-classical elements into the overall curriculum mix. Overall, HFCS falls into the CLASSICAL category with minor caveats (SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL in specific curricular moments).

## Aggregate Assessment

Overall, the school is CLASSICAL with a notable SOMETHING-OF-A-MIX in early grades on curriculum structure, but the predominant features—Catholic identity, formation-focused mission, liberal-arts curriculum framing with Great Books, seminar- and rhetoric-focused pedagogy, a “living curriculum” faculty, and active parental/community engagement—collectively render HFCS CLASSICAL.

This would help shift Element 3 from SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL toward CLASSICAL, reducing any perceived tension between Montessori-style early learning and the classical curriculum framework. No major shifts are required to maintain the current CLASSICAL status across categories.
