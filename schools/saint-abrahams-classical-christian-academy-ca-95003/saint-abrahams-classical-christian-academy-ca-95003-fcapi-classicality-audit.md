## Executive Summary

Saint Abraham’s Classical Christian Academy (St. Abe’s) presents a pronounced classical Christian program rooted in the Liberal Arts Tradition. Public materials from the school emphasize a discipleship-centered, virtue-focused education anchored in theology, worship, and a commitment to forming character through a structured Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric) approach. The school explicitly frames education as formation rather than mere information, highlighting the pursuit of truth, goodness, and beauty, the Great Books tradition, and a pedagogy that blends memory work, singing, and liturgical practices with Socratic inquiry and rhetoric in the upper grades. Overall, the school aligns strongly with classical education principles across religious foundations, purpose/goals, curriculum, pedagogy, faculty, and parent/community engagement.

- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: Strong theological grounding; explicit emphasis on the Triune God, Word, and World; liturgical/worship practices noted.
- Purpose and Goal: Formation-centered and virtue-oriented; commitment to truth, goodness, and beauty; lenses of liberal arts and Great Books.
- Curriculum: Grammar-Logic-Rhetoric, Great Books/Great Conversation, Latin study, biblically-informed history, and a robust liberal arts orientation.
- Pedagogy: Balanced approach including contemplative/restful elements (scholé-like) and Socratic/seminar-style discourse in upper grades, plus memorization and chant in early grades.
- Faculty: Headmaster-led community with a living-curriculum ethos; clear teacher-centrist modeling; active governance by a parent-involved board.
- Parents & Community: Emphasis on partnership with parents, campus visits, and family involvement; open invitation to engage with the school.
