# Executive Summary

Overall classification: CLASSICAL

- Atlanta Classical Academy (ACA) presents a comprehensive, textually grounded classical program with clear evidence of the liberal arts (Trivium and Quadrivium), great books and primary sources, language study (Latin), memory and recitation, and an upper-school rhetoric capstone (senior thesis with oral defense). These align tightly with the positive indicators in the Six Essential Elements framework and the classicality lexicon. Website: https://www.atlantaclassical.org
- Strongest areas: Purpose/Goal, Curriculum, and Pedagogy—ACA explicitly pursues the cultivation of virtue and the pursuit of truth, goodness, and beauty; it names and uses the Trivium/Quadrivium; it practices memory, recitation, public speaking, teacher questioning, fine arts (including choir), and an hour-long senior thesis defense.    
- Mixed/limited areas: Religious and Metaphysical Foundations (as a public charter, the school does not integrate theology or liturgy) and more explicit “portrait of a teacher” language and parent-formation programming. Nonetheless, clear moral realism language (truth, goodness, beauty; virtue; objective standards) is abundant.  

Below is the full six-category audit with mandatory keyword analysis, comparisons to the Six Essential Elements keyword lists, quantitative tallies, and category ratings.
