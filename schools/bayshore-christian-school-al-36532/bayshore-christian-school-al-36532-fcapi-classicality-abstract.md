## ABSTRACT
Berean Christian Academy (https://bereanchristianacademy.org) is classified as CLASSICAL. Its public materials strongly affirm classical telos (virtue/wisdom; truth, goodness, beauty) and present a robust Trivium-centered curriculum (Latin, logic, rhetoric, great books, theology/philosophy) culminating in senior thesis, with dialectical (Harkness, debate) and rhetorical practices evident. Lower-school pedagogy features wonder, chanting, songs, and memorization. Parent partnership is explicit. Evidence for explicit liturgical/communal worship rhythms, a formal portrait of a teacher, and structured parent formation is thinner in public pages but does not detract from the school’s strong classical center.
- Category 1 (Religious & Metaphysical Foundations): SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL—strong moral realism and theology; limited explicit liturgical practice language. 
- Category 2 (Purpose & Goal): CLASSICAL—virtue, wisdom, TGB prominently featured. 
- Category 3 (Curriculum): CLASSICAL—Trivium, Latin, great books, original texts, rhetoric/thesis, history (not “social studies”). 
- Category 4 (Pedagogy): CLASSICAL—chant/memorization, wonder, Harkness/debate, rhetoric. 
- Category 5 (Faculty): SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL—implicit mentorship; limited formal portraits/faculty formation detail. 
- Category 6 (Parents & Community): SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL—explicit partnership with parents; fewer details on parent formation or open-visit culture.
