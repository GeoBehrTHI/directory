# Classicality Audit: Berean Christian Academy (Katy, TX)
Website used: https://bereanchristianacademy.org

Executive Summary
- Overall classification: CLASSICAL
- Why: Berean Christian Academy self-identifies as a “Discipleship-Driven Classical Christian” school and clearly aligns with core classical markers: transcendental ideals of truth, goodness, and beauty; a Trivium-centered curriculum (grammar, logic, rhetoric) with Latin, great books, theology and philosophy; lower-school chanting/memorization; upper-school debate/Harkness and senior thesis in rhetoric; and explicit partnership with parents. The site consistently uses classical vocabulary and practices aligned with the Six Essential Elements framework and the supplied lexicon. Evidence is robust for Purpose/Goal, Curriculum, and Pedagogy; solid but less explicit for Liturgical practices and Faculty formation; and good for Parent Partnership.    
- Most important insights:
  - Strong alignment to the Trivium and Great Books tradition, with debate, Harkness seminars, rhetoric, and a senior thesis capstone. 
  - Lower school emphasizes wonder, chanting, songs/memorization—core classical pedagogy. 
  - Clear moral realism and Christian orientation (Truth, Goodness, Beauty; Christ-centered worldview), though website shows limited explicit liturgical rhythms (chapel, daily prayer) on public pages. 
  - Explicit parent partnership language appears in multiple places.  

How this audit works
- Benchmarks: The “Six Essential Elements of a Classical School” and its positive/negative keyword indicators were used across six categories: Religious & Metaphysical Foundations; Purpose & Goal; Curriculum; Pedagogy; Faculty; Parents & Community. 
- Lexicon: Keyword matches were evaluated against the provided classicality lexicon to quantify and rate each category.
