## Executive Summary

Clear Lake Classical (Clear Lake, IA, 50428) (CLC) presents a clear classical Christian orientation across most elements of classical education, with strong religious/theological foundations and a curriculum that centers on grammar, logic, and rhetoric within a biblically grounded framework. Public materials emphasize a gospel-centered, Scripture-first approach, a partnership with families, virtue/character formation, and a commitment to a liberal, classical education. While the school demonstrates robust Classical alignment in Religious Foundations, Purpose/Goal, Faculty, and Parents/Community, certain elements of the Curriculum and Pedagogy show partial alignment (notably less explicit emphasis on Great Books, Great Conversation, and Socratic teaching structures in public materials). Overall, the school trends CLASSICAL, with some provisions that are strongly classical and others that are somewhat classical or intermediate in emphasis.

- Aggregate classification: CLASSICAL

- Key insights:
 - The school’s public materials explicitly foreground biblical/theological foundations, Scripture-centered pedagogy, and a gospel-driven formation of the whole person, including character formation and virtue-focused aims. This strongly supports Category 1 (Religious and Metaphysical Foundations) as CLASSICAL.
 - The stated aim to graduate students who love what is worth loving and to develop minds and hearts toward virtue, wisdom, and human flourishing supports Category 2 (Purpose and Goal) as CLASSICAL.
 - Core curriculum emphasis on grammar, logic, and rhetoric is explicit; however, explicit references to Great Books and the Great Conversation are less evident in site materials, making Category 3 (Curriculum) SOMEW HAT CLASSICAL rather than FULLY CLASSICAL.
 - Pedagogy evidence is positive (emphasis on rhetoric, logic, and classical training in upper grades), but explicit, explicit use of Socratic seminars or scholé-centric restful pedagogy is not consistently spelled out in the site materials, yielding Category 4 as SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL.
 - Faculty portrayal in the site materials aligns with classical ideals (teachers as mentors, living curriculum, model virtues), supporting Category 5 as CLASSICAL.
 - Parent and community engagement is a strong explicit feature (parents as co-educators, partnership with families, visible involvement). Category 6 is CLASSICAL.
