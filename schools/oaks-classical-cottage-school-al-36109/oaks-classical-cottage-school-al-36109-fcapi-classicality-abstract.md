## ABSTRACT

Overall assessment: CLASSICAL

The Oaks Classical Cottage School demonstrates a robust classical-Christian alignment across most criteria. It exhibits a strong religious-metaphysical foundation via a formal Statement of Faith and gospel-centered education (theology integrated into daily life). The school’s purpose emphasizes formation, virtue, wisdom, and a transcendent sense of truth, goodness, and beauty, consistent with transcendental moral realism. The curriculum shows classical core elements (grammar, logic, rhetoric) and a Latin emphasis, with history treated as a through-line rather than mere “social studies.” The pedagogical approach includes contemplative and devotional practices, a three-day model, and an emphasis on culture-specific elements that enrich memory and moral formation; however, explicit documentation of universal Great Books/Great Conversation programming and public-facing Socratic seminars across grades 9–12 is less evident. The faculty is clearly organized and accessible, with a defined leadership and robust teaching staff. Parent involvement is a strong hallmark, with active partnership between families and the school.

- Element 4: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL

- Element 5: CLASSICAL

Executive takeaway: The Oaks demonstrates a materially classical program with a Christian formation core, strong curriculum pillars (grammar/logic/rhetoric; Latin), and active parental partnership. The principal gaps relate to explicit presentation of Great Books/Great Conversation and fully explicit Socratic pedagogy in public-facing materials. Addressing those gaps would move the school toward a fully CLASSICAL designation across all six categories.
