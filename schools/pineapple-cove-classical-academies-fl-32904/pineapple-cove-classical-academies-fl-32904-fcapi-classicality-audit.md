## Executive Summary

Pineapple Cove Classical Academy (Palm Bay/West Melbourne area; Brevard County, Florida) demonstrates strong alignment with classical education principles across all six rubric categories. Public materials from the school consistently emphasize a virtues-centered, philosophy-informed, content-rich approach, with explicit references to Great Books, Socratic dialogue, Latin, and a civically engaged liberal education. The school presents a mission of forming minds and character through a classical curriculum and is connected to Hillsdale College’s Barney Charter School Initiative, signaling a formal classical pedagogy and governance framework. Several curriculum elements are highlighted (Core Knowledge foundation, Great Books, Moral Philosophy, Senior Thesis, Latin, etc.), and pedagogy is described in terms of Socratic discussion and a memory-rich, formation-focused approach. There is explicit parent and community involvement via governance structures, parent information channels, and campus-specific parent resources.
