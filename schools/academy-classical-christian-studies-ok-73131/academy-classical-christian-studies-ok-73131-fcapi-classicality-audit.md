## Executive Summary

The Academy of Classical Christian Studies (OKC) serving 73131 demonstrates a robust classical-Christian orientation across the six classical criteria. Public-facing materials emphasize Truth, Goodness, and Beauty as trans-cendent aims; theology is integrated into the educational program (Nicene Creed anchoring identity); liturgical rhythm and prayer ground daily life; the curriculum centers on the Trivium (grammar, dialectic, rhetoric) and the Great Books/Great Conversation through an integrated, imitative, and formation-focused model; pedagogy blends contemplative/restful elements with Socratic-discussion dynamics in upper grades; the faculty are presented as mentors and living embodiments of the school’s ideals; and parents are positioned as co-educators and partners in a transdenominational Christian community. Evidence across multiple pages (mission, theology, liturgical practice, the Trivium, Lyceum, Capstone, faculty leadership, and parental partnership) strongly supports CLASSICAL classification across all six categories. Notable textual anchors include the mission to cultivate Truth, Goodness, and Beauty; the Nicene Creed statement of faith; daily prayer and liturgical rhythm; explicit Trivium framing and pedagogy; a public portrait of a graduate oriented to forming affections toward the True, the Good, and the Beautiful; and explicit parental partnership through structures like the Giving Society and family-engagement programming.
