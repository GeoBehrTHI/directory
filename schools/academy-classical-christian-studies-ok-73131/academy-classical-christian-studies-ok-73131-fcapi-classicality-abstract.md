## ABSTRACT

The Academy of Classical Christian Studies (OKC, 73131) demonstrates a comprehensive classical education model across Religious and Metaphysical Foundations, School Purpose and Goal, Curriculum, Pedagogy, Faculty, and Parents & Community. The school anchors its mission in Truth, Goodness, and Beauty, with explicit theology (Nicene Creed) and liturgical practice (prayer and Hours) shaping daily life. The Trivium-centered Curriculum (Grammar, Dialectic, Rhetoric), Great Books/Great Conversation orientation, and integrated Lyceum/CAPSTONE structures support deep formation over mere information transfer. Faculty are presented as mentors and living exemplars, while Parents are co-educators in a transdenominational Christian community. Public materials show a coherent alignment to classical education ideals, and the Six Essential Elements framework supports a CLASSICAL rating across all six categories.
