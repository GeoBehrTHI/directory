## ABSTRACT

Lake Country Classical Academy demonstrates a strong classical orientation centered on the core aims of truth, goodness, and beauty, with formation and virtue as central educational goals. The public materials reveal a mission oriented toward the flourishing of every student in heart and mind, a curriculum framed by liberal arts and a Hillsdale College partnership, and active parent engagement through book clubs and testimonials. While foundational classical cues (formation, virtue, and a public framing of a classical curriculum) are robust, explicit public articulation of several hallmark classical practices—such as a fully named Great Books program, explicit Trivium/Quadrivium pedagogy, and explicit Socratic/Scholé-based teaching modalities—are less visible. This places LCCA in a SOMEWHT CLASSICAL position overall. The strongest category signals are in School Purpose & Goal and Parents & Community, with solid potential to elevate to CLASSICAL by making explicit and publicly documenting additional classical pedagogical and curricular elements.
