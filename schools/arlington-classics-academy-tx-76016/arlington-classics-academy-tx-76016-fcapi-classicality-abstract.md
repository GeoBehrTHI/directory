## ABSTRACT

Arlington Classics Academy demonstrates a clear alignment with classical education in Curriculum and Pedagogy, with strong textual references to grammar, logic, rhetoric, Great Books, and Socratic/seminar approaches. The school presents a formation-minded orientation, supported by explicit Christian/classical alignment from companion materials, signaling a Religious & Metaphysical Foundation that is present but not uniformly foregrounded across all public pages. The integration of Core Knowledge content in K-5 shows a hybrid approach, which sits alongside classical elements rather than fully displacing them. The Faculty and Parents & Community categories show evidence of strong governance and parental involvement, though the public materials do not always articulate a fully developed “living curriculum” narrative or a fully explicit liturgical framework. Overall, ACA is BEST DESCRIBED AS SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL, with several robust classical elements in Curriculum and Pedagogy and meaningful but less explicit religious-metaphysical grounding, parental engagement, and faculty narrative. The six-category balance supports this overall classification, with significant potential to advance toward CLASSICAL through enhanced explicit articulation of metaphysical foundations, more continuous Great Books integration, and stronger public-facing “living curriculum” storytelling.
