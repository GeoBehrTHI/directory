## Executive Summary

Thales Academy Cary/Holly Springs (Thales Academy, NC network) demonstrates a clearly Classical framework across most elements of the Six Essential Elements rubric. Public materials consistently foreground the Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric), the Great Books and the Great Conversation, and a commitment to virtue, wisdom, and the formation of character within a Judeo-Christian Western civilizational inheritance. Direct Instruction is acknowledged for foundational concepts in early grades, but the overall pedagogy emphasizes Socratic dialogue, seminars, and the senior thesis in history and literature, aligning with classical pedagogy from grammar to rhetoric stages. The school emphasizes Latin study, interdisciplinary continuity, and a robust emphasis on community, family partnership, and parental engagement. Evidence across multiple Thales Academy sources shows a coherent classical identity with explicit attention to truth, goodness, and beauty as real and knowable, anchored in the Western canon and religious-ethical formation. This yields an overall assessment of CLASSICAL across the six categories.

- The religious/metaphysical foundations are strongly grounded in a Judeo-Christian frame, with explicit references to natural law, the imago Dei, and the Western canon as the inherited tradition shaping education. 
- The school’s purpose/goals stress formation, virtue, and pursuit of truth, goodness, and beauty rather than mere job preparation. 
- The curriculum explicitly centers the liberal arts (grammar/logic/rhetoric), Great Books, history taught chronologically, and a “Great Conversation.” 
- Pedagogy encompasses both contemplative/restful elements and Socratic/inquiry-based modes, with upper-level seminars and rhetoric, while lower grades include memory-oriented practice and singing/recitation (Scholé) as foundational memory work. 
- Faculty are portrayed as virtuous, mentors who embody and model the school’s aims, serving as the living curriculum, with explicit emphasis on character formation and professional leadership. 
- Parents and community are foundational partners, with explicit statements about parental involvement, home-school collaboration, and open access for visits and resources.
