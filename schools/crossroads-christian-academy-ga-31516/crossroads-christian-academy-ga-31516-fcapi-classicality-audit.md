# Classicality Audit: Austin Classical School

Website used: https://austinclassical.org/ 

## Executive Summary
Overall classification: CLASSICAL

Austin Classical School (ACS) presents clear, repeated indicators of a robust classical Christian identity across purpose, theology, curriculum, and pedagogy. The site explicitly centers “truth, goodness, and beauty,” names the Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric), highlights Latin, logic, rhetoric, Great Books and the “Great Conversation,” and describes classroom practices such as memorization, recitation, songs, debate/discussion, the Socratic method, imitation, and a senior thesis defense—hallmarks of a mature classical program. Its mission underscores co-laboring with families within a Christ-centered framework. Evidence for a formal, public “portrait of a teacher” or faculty-as-mentors statement is thinner on the website, and explicit parent-formation programming is not highlighted in the crawl material. Nevertheless, ACS’s overall alignment with the Six Essential Elements is strong.   

Method note: Scoring and keyword comparisons below follow the positive/negative indicators in Six Essential Elements of a Classical School and the supporting lexicon used by this GPT.
