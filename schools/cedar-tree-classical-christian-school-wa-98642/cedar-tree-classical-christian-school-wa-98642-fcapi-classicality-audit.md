## Executive Summary

Cedar Tree Classical Christian School (Ridgefield, WA 98642) presents a robust, clearly classical-leaning program embedded in a Christian worldview. Public materials emphasize formation of virtuous, eloquent graduates rooted in Scripture, liturgical life, and a time-honored liberal-arts approach. The school foregrounds Great Books and literary classics, Latin (and Greek in high school), public speaking from early grades, a thesis-driven senior year, and a consistent focus on virtue, wisdom, and beauty. Parental partnership and donor/community engagement are integral through structured fundraising and parent communications.

Overall classification: CLASSICAL.

Key strengths

- Explicit religious/metaphysical commitments (Bible as authority; daily prayer; statement of faith; liturgical life cues such as hymns and morning devotions) that align with transcendental moral realism and a theological grounding.
- Curriculum and pedagogy anchored by Great Books, literary classics, Latin/Greek study, and a heavy emphasis on public speaking and rhetoric (Upper/Lower School).
- Formation-focused goals (virtue, wisdom, character) with explicit statements about formation over information and a Christian worldview permeating subjects.
- Evidence of a living, mentored faculty and a community-forward model that positions parents as co-teachers and partners.

What would move the needle (for SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL cases only)

- Not applicable here; all six categories align with classical cues. If any area were to be nudged, it would be to further explicitify a formal, named sequence of grammar-logic-rhetoric progression in the published curriculum map (beyond references to “classical method” and “public speaking”) to reinforce the Trivium framework more transparently.

    - The “Our Philosophy” highlights integration of liberal arts with a Christian Gospel framework.
- Positive/Negative keyword analysis
    - Positive keywords present: theology, Scripture/Bible, Biblical worldview, prayer, worship, virtue/theory of truth, etc., along with explicit clerical/faith commitments. Likely 6–8 distinct positive indicators identified in the public materials.

- Why this rating
    - The school’s public materials foreground theological grounding, liturgical life cues, and a Bible-centered epistemology, matching the Positive Keywords in the Six Essential Elements and rejecting neutral/denominational pluralism in favor of a theologically grounded formation. The evidence shows transcendental moral realism and communal worship components, aligning with a Classical/Scholastic foundation rather than secular or purely information-based approaches.

### Category 2: School Purpose and Goal — CLASSICAL

- Evidence

    - Central aim of human excellence and the cultivation of a Christian worldview; graduates described as embodying wisdom and eloquence for God’s glory.
    - Explicit Christian formation lifecycle: Scriptural grounding, prayer, and a goal of shaping character before vocational outcomes.
- Positive/Negative keyword analysis

- Why this rating
    - The mission and profile articulate formation and the pursuit of transcendent goods (truth, goodness, beauty) within a Christian framework, which aligns strongly with Classical aims, even though the site emphasizes college-readiness via advanced coursework and SAT results. The balance leans heavily toward formation and virtue-cultivation rather than utilitarian job-prep.

The presence of a strong classical ethos and Great Books emphasis supports a high classical ranking, but the exact published grammar-logic-rhetoric sequencing is not fully explicit.
