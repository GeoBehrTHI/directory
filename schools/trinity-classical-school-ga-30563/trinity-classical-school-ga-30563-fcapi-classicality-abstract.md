## ABSTRACT

Trinity Classical School (Mt Airy, GA, 30563) demonstrates a clear CLASSICAL orientation across all six categories examined. The school’s materials foreground a Christ-centered, covenantal educational philosophy rooted in the Word of God, with a mission to form virtuous, wise individuals rather than focusing merely on information or vocational training. The curriculum is explicitly classical (grammar/logic/rhetoric; Latin; senior capstone) and includes memory-based pedagogy (Narration, Copywork, Dictation) alongside upper-grade rhetorical emphasis, with living-virtue faculty and strong parental partnership. The presence of these elements aligns with the Positive Keyword indicators for each category and shows minimal to no engagement with Negative Keyword indicators in the public-facing content.

- Element 1 (Religious and Metaphysical Foundations): CLASSICAL — strong theological grounding, Christ-centered framing, Word of God as foundation; covenantal admissions; explicit theology language.  
- Element 2 (Purpose and Goal): CLASSICAL — formation-oriented aims emphasizing virtue, wisdom, and truth under a theistic frame; glorifying God as a central purpose. 
- Element 3 (Curriculum): CLASSICAL — explicit classical structure (grammar/logic/rhetoric; Latin; capstone); living books/integrated approach; memory-based pedagogy.  
- Element 4 (Pedagogy): CLASSICAL — mix of contemplative/memory-based practices in early years and rhetorical/cognitive training in later years; evidence of public speaking/logic. 
- Element 5 (Faculty): CLASSICAL — faculty described as living curriculum and virtuous mentors; Christ-centered personal commitment; staff pages.  
- Element 6 (Parents & Community): CLASSICAL — covenantal partnerships; parental involvement; family engagement as part of the school’s ethos. 

Overall assessment: CLASSICAL

- The school aligns strongly with the core components of classical education (traditional canons, Trivium, Great Books/Great Conversation orientation to the extent present in public materials, formation of virtue and wisdom, and a covenantal Christian community with engaged parents).

- The abstract of the Six Essential Elements resources further supports the indicators used in this evaluation, notably the emphasis on truth/goodness/beauty, formation, theology, liturgical practices, and family-partnered community dynamics.
