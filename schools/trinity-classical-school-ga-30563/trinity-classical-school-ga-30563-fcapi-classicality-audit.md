## Executive Summary

Trinity Classical School (Mt. Airy, GA 30563) presents a robust Classical Christian program that aligns strongly with the Six Essential Elements framework for classical education. Public materials from the school consistently emphasize a Christ-centered, covenantal, virtue- and wisdom-focused aims, grounded in Christian theology and the pursuit of truth, goodness, and beauty. The curriculum explicitly incorporates traditional classical structures (grammar, logic, rhetoric; Latin; senior capstone), a historically informed and integrated approach to content, and memory-based pedagogy (Narration, Copywork, Dictation, Composer Study, Nature Study). Faculty are portrayed as living witnesses to virtue and faith, and parents are positioned as covenantal partners in education. Taken together, the school scores CLASSICAL across all six categories.

Key highlights:
- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: The Word of God and Christ-centered formation are foundational, with covenantal language governing admissions and curriculum; theology is foregrounded in mission and vision statements.   
- Purpose and Goal: Education is framed as formation toward virtue and wisdom, anchored in truth and divine order rather than purely vocational outcomes; the mission explicitly centers glorifying God and forming the whole person.  
- Curriculum: Clear classical structure (grammar/logic/rhetoric; Latin; senior capstone) plus exposure to living books via Veritas Press Omnibus in some courses; robust lower-school memory-and-nature-based practices.  
- Pedagogy: A mix of contemplative/memory-based practices in the early years (Narration, Copywork, Dictation; nature study) and rhetoric-oriented work in the upper grades, with opportunities for reasoned discourse and public speaking. 
- Faculty: Public statements position faculty as both professional educators and virtuous exemplars, the “living curriculum,” and “mentors” who model Christian virtue. 
- Parents & Community: Strong emphasis on partnership with Christian families, covenantal commitments, and parental involvement as co-educators within a Christ-centered framework.
