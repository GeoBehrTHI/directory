## ABSTRACT

Trinity Classical Academy (TCA) presents a robust CLASSICAL profile across all six categories. The school’s public materials emphasize a Christian, formation-centered approach (Religious & Metaphysical Foundations), a purpose oriented to virtue and wisdom (School Purpose & Goal), a curriculum built on liberal arts and Great Books (Curriculum), a pedagogy that includes rhetoric, Socratic elements, and contemplative learning (Pedagogy), a faculty culture modeled on virtue and mentorship (Faculty), and an active partnership with families (Parents & Community). The public content provides explicit quotes about Christian formation, the non-value-neutral nature of education, the ordering of the soul, and the Great Books/great conversation framing, aligning with the Positive Keyword Indicators identified in the Six Essential Elements framework. The site also communicates practical classical structures, such as a Rhetoric Stage and collaboration with parents in a co-educator model, reinforcing the classical orientation. Taken together, these signals justify a CLASSICAL rating with a coherent, integrated classical educational vision that aligns with the “Six Essential Elements” lexicon and the school’s own materials. The aggregate evidence supports a strong classical orientation with explicit commitments to virtue, truth, beauty, and human flourishing through a Christian classical education.
