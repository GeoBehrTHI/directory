## Executive Summary

Trinity Classical Academy (TCA) in Nebraska operates with a clearly classical Christian identity that is visible across its public materials. The school emphasizes formation of character through a Christian framework, a liberal-arts oriented curriculum, and a pedagogy that combines contemplative elements with Socratic-style discussion and rhetoric. The institution markets itself as a collaborative model that partners with parents as co-educators, integrates Great Books and liberal arts thinking, and presents a high level of intentional formation rather than narrow job-preparatory outcomes. Public statements stress that education is not value-neutral, that theology informs the curriculum, and that students are formed through virtue, philosophy, and the great conversation of Western civilization. The presence of “Christian education,” a stated aim toward wisdom and virtue, and explicit calls for parental partnership align strongly with classical ideals as defined by the Six Essential Elements framework.

Overall classification: CLASSICAL

- Evidence from the school’s public materials shows explicit religious grounding and the ordering of the soul toward truth, goodness, and beauty (Element 1). 
- The school’s stated purpose emphasizes formation, virtue, and contemplation rather than mere information or workforce readiness (Element 2). 
- The curriculum is framed around liberal arts and Great Books concepts, with references to classical education resources and reading lists (Element 3). 
- Pedagogical methods include rhetoric, reasoning, and a high school emphasis on persuasive expression (Element 4). 
- Faculty/faculty leadership language implies a model of virtue and mentorship in the classroom (Element 5). 
- Parental partnership and community formation with open engagement and joint formation of affections are foregrounded (Element 6).

Positive/Negative keyword analysis

Where present, collaborative/home-based models are framed as a supplement, not a replacement for classical teaching modes. 

Why this rating
- The pedagogy aligns with classical indicators (Socratic/dialogic inquiry, memorization, rhetoric and oratory, restfulness in learning, and formation through models), giving CLASSICAL.
