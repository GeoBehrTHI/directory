## Executive Summary

Acacia Academy (Kokomo, IN; 46902) presents a robust classical Christian program that aligns closely with the Six Essential Elements of a Classical School rubric. Across all six categories, the public materials emphasize a Trivium-based curriculum (grammar, logic, rhetoric), engagement with Great Books and the Great Conversation, integrated disciplines, worshipful and liturgical practices, and a partnership model with families. The school explicitly frames its mission around character formation and the cultivation of truth, goodness, and beauty within a Christian worldview. Public materials also document a Prayer/Chapel culture, weekly worship opportunities, and a visible faculty leadership structure. These characteristics collectively support a CLASSICAL assessment for all six categories, with a clearly defined aggregate CLASSICAL profile.

Key insights:
- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations are explicitly rooted in a Christian framework, with liturgical worship, prayer, and a Biblical worldview embedded in daily life (Our Prayer; Core Principles; SPIRITUALITY).   
- Curriculum centers on the Trivium, Great Books, Latin/English study, and a connected, liberal-arts approach (Grammar/Logic/Rhetoric; The Great Conversation; The Great Books). 
- Pedagogy includes mind-building methods such as narration and chants, with a clear emphasis on formation and memory, complemented by Socratic/seminal elements at higher levels (Teaching methods: narration, chants, nature study; etc.). 
- Faculty and community aspects show leadership and a living-structure where teachers model virtue and are central to the curriculum (Head of School; Living curriculum concepts in classical pedagogy lexicon).  
- Parent partnership and family involvement are foregrounded, with explicit appeals to parents as co-educators and open invitations for visits and engagement (Partnership with Parents; Parent Covenant).
