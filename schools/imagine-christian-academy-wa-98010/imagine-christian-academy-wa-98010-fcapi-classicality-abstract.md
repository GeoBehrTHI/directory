## ABSTRACT
- Overall classification: CLASSICAL
- Summary: Austin Classical School’s published materials consistently foreground a Christ‑centered telos (“glorify Jesus Christ”), articulating education as the cultivation of wisdom and virtue ordered to truth, goodness, and beauty; it implements the Trivium with Latin, logic, rhetoric/thesis; integrates learning under theology; and maintains daily Chapel and a collaborative parent partnership. The community culture (choir, theater, house competitions, Fun Run, Recitatio) reinforces formation beyond academics.    
- Category summaries:
  - Religious & Metaphysical: Moral realism (truth, goodness, beauty), theological integration, and liturgical practice (Chapel) are explicit. CLASSICAL. 
  - Purpose & Goal: Formation in wisdom/virtue, not careerism; “for life” not merely college prep. CLASSICAL. 
  - Curriculum: Trivium explicit; Latin/logic/rhetoric present; Great Books/Great Conversation; integrated knowledge under theology; robust math/science. CLASSICAL.  
  - Pedagogy: Lower school memory work (memorization, recitation, song), upper school debate/rhetoric/thesis; community recitations. CLASSICAL.  
  - Faculty: Teachers as Christian models of virtue; ongoing training; form students’ souls. CLASSICAL. 
  - Parents & Community: Co‑teaching parents trained/formed; vibrant communal practices; accessible visits. CLASSICAL.
