# Classicality Audit Report: Austin Classical School

Executive Summary
- Overall classification: CLASSICAL
- School: Austin Classical School (Austin, TX)
- Website used: https://austinclassical.org/ 
- Why: The school explicitly centers its mission on glorifying Jesus Christ and forming “wise, virtuous, and humble” students through a classical, Christian education; it teaches toward truth, goodness, and beauty, employs the Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric), teaches Latin and logic, emphasizes memorization/recitation in the lower grades and debate/rhetoric/thesis in upper grades, and begins on‑campus days with Chapel—clear signs across all six elements of a classical school.    

Note on method: For each category, we first list keywords found on the school site, then compare them to the positive/negative indicators from the “Six Essential Elements of a Classical School” lexicon to quantify hits and justify ratings.
