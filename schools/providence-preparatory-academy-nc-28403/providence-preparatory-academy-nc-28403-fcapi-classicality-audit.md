## Executive Summary

Providence Preparatory Academy (PPA) in Wilmington, NC (28403) presents a robust Classical Christian program that aligns with the Six Essential Elements of a Classical School. Public-facing materials emphasize a Christian, classical core—centered on formation of character through a Biblical worldview, reverence for truth, goodness, and beauty, and a curriculum rooted in the Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric) alongside Great Books, classical languages, and rigorous Socratic/recitation-based pedagogy. The school also foregrounds a collaborative model that intentionally partners with parents in the educational process, and it positions its faculty as the living curriculum within a house-culture framework. Across catechetical language, explicit calls to virtue and wisdom, a history-rich and literature-forward curriculum, and a pedagogy that alternates between contemplative and discursive modes, PPA exhibits a clearly Classical orientation.

Aggregate assessment: CLASSICAL.

Key evidence points include: explicit Christian-theological framing and reliance on Scripture as truth; formative education and the pursuit of virtue, wisdom, and the true/good/beautiful; a curriculum built around grammar/logic/rhetoric with emphasis on reading Great Books (Homer, Plato, Shakespeare, Dickens) and studying history in a Western civ frame; pedagogy that includes contemplative/memory work in early grades and Socratic/seminar-based work in upper grades; a faculty structure that includes a headmaster, dean of students, and curriculum leadership; and a structured, parent-partnered model that invites parent involvement through admissions, open houses, and ongoing communication. These traits appear consistently across multiple public-facing pages and internal adoptions and align with the Positive keyword indicators in the classical lexicon.
