## ABSTRACT

Providence Preparatory Academy (PPA) in Wilmington, NC exhibits a CLASSICAL orientation across all six canonical domains of classical education. Religious and Metaphysical Foundations are explicitly Christian and theologically anchored, with Scripture cited as the pillar of truth and Christ-centered formation central to the school's mission. The School Purpose and Goal emphasize formation, virtue, wisdom, and the True/Good/Beautiful as real and knowable through a classical telos. Curriculum is clearly grounded in the Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric) with a strong emphasis on Great Books, classical languages (Latin and Greek), history, and Western civilization. Pedagogy integrates contemplative/restful elements with Socratic seminars, memory work, and rhetoric, especially in the upper grades. Faculty structure and culture emphasize mentors, living curriculum, and collegiality, while Parents & Community are engaged as co-educators through a collaborative model, parent formation activities, and open access to school life. The site’s content consistently features keywords aligned with classical education as defined by the Six Essential Elements, with positive signals across all six categories and no substantive negative signals evident in the public materials reviewed. The aggregate classification is CLASSICAL.
