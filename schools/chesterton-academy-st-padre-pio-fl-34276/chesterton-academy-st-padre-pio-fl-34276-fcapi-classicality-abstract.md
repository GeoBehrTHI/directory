## ABSTRACT

Chesterton Academy of St. Padre Pio (Sarasota, FL) is CLASSICAL across all six classical-education categories. The public materials show a Catholic, incarnational foundation tied to liturgical life and a faith-centered formation. The school’s purpose centers on virtue, wisdom, and a liberal-arts telos rather than workforce-prep alone. The curriculum is built on liberal-arts scaffolding, Great Books/primary sources, and an integrated humanities program, reinforced by a Socratic pedagogy and traditional memory-work. The faculty is characterized as experienced mentors and living exemplars, and parents/community engagement is a sustained pillar of the school’s model. The aggregate assessment places the school solidly in the CLASSICAL cluster, with all six categories aligning with the classical-education lexicon and indicators described in the Six Essential Elements of a Classical School (1) framework.
