# Classicality Audit Report: Austin Classical School

School website audited: https://austinclassical.org/

Executive Summary
- Overall classification: CLASSICAL
- Why: Austin Classical School explicitly anchors its mission in Christ and in the cultivation of wisdom and virtue; it foregrounds Truth, Goodness, and Beauty; it structures curriculum around the Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric), includes Latin, logic, and rhetoric, and employs Socratic discussion, memorization/recitation, and imitation. Its philosophy and language align closely with the Six Essential Elements positive keyword indicators across Religious Foundations, Purpose, Curriculum, and Pedagogy. Faculty and Parents/Community evidence is positive but thinner in explicit classical phrasing, so those two categories score “Somewhat Classical.”
- Most salient evidence from the site:
  - “exists to glorify Jesus Christ … cultivate wise, virtuous, and humble students … classical, Christian education” 
  - “Truth, Goodness, and Beauty: Classical Education Basics … cultivate wisdom and virtue … contemplation of the True, the Good, and the Beautiful … great books … great conversation … the Trivium” 
  - Trivium stages with memorization, recitation, songs; logic via debate/discussion; rhetoric via thesis defense and eloquent speech; Latin beginning in 5th; logic and rhetoric in upper grades 
  - “less about training for a specific, temporal vocation … learning to think and live with an eternal purpose” (formation over workforce prep) 
  - “We employ the Socratic method” and explicit Trivium-based pedagogy; “Bible … at the center of the great conversation about the true, the good, and the beautiful … we study those great books” 

Method note: The audit follows the Six Essential Elements of a Classical School keyword framework—positive and negative indicators for each category—and the supporting lexicon used by this GPT. These served as the scoring backbone for all six categories, as instructed  .
