## ABSTRACT
Austin Classical School (https://austinclassical.org/) is classified as CLASSICAL. It explicitly or implicitly matches the Six Elements benchmark language in four of six categories and shows no contradictory negative-signal language. Religious and metaphysical foundations are explicit (Christ-centered, Scripture central, pursuit of Truth, Goodness, Beauty). The purpose and aim of education is virtue/wisdom formation, not workforce prep. The curriculum centers on the Trivium, Latin, logic, rhetoric, Great Books, and an integrated approach. Pedagogy includes memory work (songs/recitation), Socratic discussion/debate, and rhetoric exercises (thesis/oral defense) with contemplative aims. Evidence for a formal “portrait of a teacher/mentor collegium” and an ongoing program of parent formation is positive but limited in the crawl. Category ratings:

- Purpose and Goal: CLASSICAL (formation in wisdom/virtue; TGB; eternal telos)  
- Curriculum: CLASSICAL (Trivium, Latin/logic/rhetoric, Great Books, integration)
