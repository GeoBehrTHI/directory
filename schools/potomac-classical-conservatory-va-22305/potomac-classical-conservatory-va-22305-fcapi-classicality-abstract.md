## ABSTRACT

Potomac Classical Conservatory (Alexandria, VA, 22305) scores as CLASSICAL across all six classical-education categories, with strong signals in Religious & Metaphysical Foundations, School Purpose & Goal, Curriculum, Pedagogy, Faculty, and Parents & Community. The school presents a Christ-centered, Trivium-based curriculum that includes Great Books, The Great Conversation, and Latin, alongside a covenantal partnership with families and a “paideia” formation approach. Public materials emphasize formation over mere information, virtue, and the cultivation of Truth, Goodness, and Beauty, anchored in Scripture and a Reformed Biblical worldview. The collaborative model (school plus home instruction) demonstrates a nuanced, family-centered approach to classical education rather than a rigid, all-in-school classical program. The keywords and signals from PCC’s materials align closely with the Positive keyword indicators of a classical model, with negligible occurrences of Negative keywords in public text.
