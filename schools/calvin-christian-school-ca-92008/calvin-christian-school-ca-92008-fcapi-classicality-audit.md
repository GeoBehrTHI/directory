## Executive Summary

Calvin Christian School (Escondido, CA) presents a strongly theocentric, biblically grounded program that emphasizes formation, virtue, and a classical-informed trajectory, though its public materials do not consistently spell out all hallmark classical cues (e.g., explicit Great Books pedagogy or explicit Trivium/Quadrivium references). Across categories, the public materials signal a classical-adjacent orientation rooted in Reformation-era liberal-arts tradition, with clear emphasis on covenantal formation, worship-witness, and a life oriented toward truth, goodness, and beauty as real and knowable through a biblical worldview. The school’s messaging strongly foregrounds theology, formation, virtue, and a holistic, faith-centered education, with concrete evidence of community life, parental partnership, and a faculty corps that includes credentialing and leadership roles. Where the materials are most explicit about classical structure (e.g., liberal-arts language, paideia concepts, and a portrait of a graduate oriented to wonder, worship, work, and wisdom), they align with Classical/Somewhat Classical marks. There is less explicit public articulation of core classical curriculum components (Great Books, Trivium, Quadrivium) in the site’s posted materials, though the school repeatedly references liberal-arts heritage and Christian scholastic aims. Overall, the school sits between CLASSICAL and SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL, with stronger alignment in Religious Foundations, Purpose/Goal, and Community/Parents than in a fully spelled-out, book-by-book classical curriculum or explicit high-school pedagogy labeled as Socratic/seminar-based.
