## ABSTRACT

CSW demonstrates a robust CLASSICAL orientation across all six categories. The Religious and Metaphysical Foundations are anchored in a Christ-centered framework with a clear articulation of Truth, Goodness, and Beauty, theology, liturgy, and communal worship. The School Purpose and Goal emphasize formation, virtue, wisdom, and human excellence over job-focused outcomes. The Curriculum centers on the Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric) and Quadrivium with Great Books/Great Conversation via Omnibus, and it treats History as a core integral across disciplines. Pedagogy blends contemplative, memory-based practices in the early years with Socratic seminars, rigorous rhetoric, and thesis-based assessment in the upper grades. Faculty are framed as living exemplars and mentors who model virtue, while Parents & Community are actively engaged as co-educators with clear pathways for parental formation and involvement. Taken together, CSW’s public materials align with the classical education ideal, emphasizing formation, virtue, and the Great Conversation within a Christian worldview.
