## Executive Summary

Paideia Academy Knoxville (37932) presents a robust classical Christian program that aligns strongly with the Six Essential Elements of a Classical School rubric. Public materials from the school's site consistently frame education through a Christian worldview, a liberal arts / Trivium-based curriculum, and a pedagogy that blends Socratic method with rigorous rhetoric and senior-level work. The evidence includes explicit references to the Great Books, the Omnibus program, Latin and Greek study, and Socratic-led inquiry, as well as formal mentions of a parent-partnered model and a faculty as the living curriculum. Across the six categories, the school demonstrates a clear classical orientation with Christian theological grounding, and there are ample signals of Great Conversation, formation through the liberal arts, and an emphasis on virtue and character formation.

Overall classification: CLASSICAL

Key insights:
- Religious and metaphysical foundations are explicit (Christian worldview, theology references, and a historically grounded liberal arts approach).
- Purpose and goal emphasize formation, virtue, wisdom, and human flourishing (not job preparation alone).
- Curriculum centers on grammar, logic, rhetoric; Great Books; classical languages; and Western history.
- Pedagogy includes Socratic/seminar-based discourse, memory work, and opportunities for rhetoric and oratory.
- Faculty are portrayed as living exemplars and mentors, with a clearly defined teacher profile and community orientation.
- Parents and community are positioned as co-teachers and engaged partners, with active parental participation and open campus culture.

The following report provides category-by-category keyword analyses, followed by a concluding assessment and a final abstract.
