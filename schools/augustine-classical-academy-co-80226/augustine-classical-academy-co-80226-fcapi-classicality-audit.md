## Executive Summary

Augustine Classical Academy ( ACA ), Lakewood, CO 80226, presents a clearly identifiable classical orientation in its public materials, notably through repeated emphasis on chapel-based communal life, a rhetoric/logic-focused upper school, and grammar- and logic-focused tours. However, the site’s public content provides only partial, indirect evidence of key classical-education foundations such as explicit Great Books study, a defined telos or transcendent formation, and a thoroughly articulated living-faculty pedagogy. On balance, ACA is best characterized as SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL: it demonstrates several core classical markers (liturgical/community life; grammar/logic/rhetoric framing; high-school rhetoric/seminar potential) but does not consistently or explicitly affirm the full spectrum of Positive keywords identified for a fully classical program.

Executive takeaways
- Strengths aligned with classical aims: liturgical/communal practices (All-School Chapel), explicit emphasis on rhetoric and logic (Logic & Rhetoric entries), and public-facing pathways for upper-level classical study (Upper School Tours, Grammar School Tours) that reflect the Trivium architecture. Evidence: All-School Chapel references; Logic & Rhetoric Tour language; grammar/logic/rhetoric framing on public pages.  
- Gaps relative to a fully classical program: limited explicit public statements about Great Books, Great Conversation, telos/theology integrated into coursework, and a fully articulated “living curriculum” in faculty practice. Evidence in site content appears lighter on direct Great Books language and on explicit faculty-pedagogy pledges beyond general guidance. 
- Parent/community engagement is comparatively strong and visible in public pages (open houses, parent orientations, etc.), which aligns with the classical element of strong parental partnership, though not always couched in classical vocabulary. Evidence: Preschool/parent events; open houses; parent orientation materials.
