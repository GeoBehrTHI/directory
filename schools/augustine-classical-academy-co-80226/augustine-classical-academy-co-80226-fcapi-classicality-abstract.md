## ABSTRACT

- Overall classification: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL
- Core rationale: Augustine Classical Academy demonstrates several classical anchors (chapel-based communal practices; grammar/logic/rhetoric curricular scaffolding; upper-school rhetoric and public-facing seminars). However, the public materials do not consistently articulate the full suite of classical foundational commitments (notably explicit Great Books/Great Conversation, fully spelled-out telos/theology integration, and an explicit “living curriculum” faculty model). This mix of explicit classical signals with notable gaps yields a SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL profile.
