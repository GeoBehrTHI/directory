# Classicality Audit: Berean Christian Academy (Katy, TX)

Website used: https://bereanchristianacademy.org

Executive Summary
- Overall classification: CLASSICAL
- Why: The school explicitly grounds itself in a Christ-centered worldview ordered to Truth, Goodness, and Beauty; integrates theology across the curriculum; offers the Trivium (Grammar–Logic–Rhetoric) with Latin, Great Books, Harkness discussion, debate, and a defended Senior Thesis; and describes a parent–school partnership and robust communal life with chapels and service. These map tightly to the positive indicators in the Six Essential Elements keyword framework used for this audit   . 
- Affiliations: ACCS and SCL (among others) reinforce the classical identity .
- Highest strengths: Religious and metaphysical foundations; purpose and telos; curriculum content (Trivium, Latin, Great Books, history by eras); rhetoric capstones (Senior Thesis)  .
- Main gaps to watch: Fewer explicit lower-school pedagogical markers such as chanting/memory work and scholé/restful learning language in public materials.

Note on method and lexicon
- This audit used the Six Essential Elements keyword framework and lexicon of positive and negative indicators across six categories (Religious/Metaphysical; Purpose/Goal; Curriculum; Pedagogy; Faculty; Parents & Community). Keyword hits were counted from the school’s public materials and compared to the positive/negative lists to compute category scores and coverage estimates  .

  - “Christ-centered worldview”; “Truth, Goodness, and Beauty”; “for the glory of Christ” (mission)

  - “Christmas Chapel,” “Bridge Ceremony Chapel,” “Commencement Chapel”; Good Friday/Easter breaks (liturgical/chapel calendar) 
- Mapping to Six Elements lexicon
  - Positive indicators matched: truth, goodness, beauty, virtue, theology, Christian worldview, apologetics, chapel (liturgical practices) .
  - Negative indicators: none observed.
- Quantitative estimate
  - pos_neg_hits: +10/-0
  - percent_positive: 1.00
  - percent_coverage: 0.95 (all three sub-parts—transcendentals, theology, communal worship—appear; worship language is present mainly via chapel events)
- Score: CLASSICAL

Category 2: School Purpose and Goal

  - Educate in a Christ-centered worldview; equip to recognize Truth, Goodness, Beauty; formation for the “glory of Christ and benefit of others” (telos)

- Mapping to Six Elements lexicon

  - Negative indicators (e.g., “college and career readiness” emphasis): not present; notably, the school does not offer AP and orients upper-school capstones toward rhetoric and public defense instead .
- Quantitative estimate
  - pos_neg_hits: +8/-0
  - percent_positive: 1.00
  - percent_coverage: 0.85 (strong telos/formation language; room to make “formation” terminology even more explicit across pages)
- Score: CLASSICAL

Category 3: School Curriculum
- School keywords found (examples)
  - Seven liberal arts; Trivium (Grammar–Logic–Rhetoric) with Latin; Great Books and “original ancient texts”; formal Logic, Rhetoric I–II; defended Senior Thesis; history by eras (Ancient/Medieval/Modern) 
  - Extensive upper-school humanities reading list (Odyssey, Herodotus, Augustine, Shakespeare, Federalist/Anti-Federalist, Lewis, etc.) evidences Great Books/primary texts orientation 
- Mapping to Six Elements lexicon
  - Positive indicators matched: liberal arts, Trivium, Latin, Great Books/primary sources, rhetoric/oratory, the Great Conversation; robust history (not “social studies”) .
  - Negative indicators: none (no “social studies” terminology displacing history; projects like Science Fair/Mock Trial appear as capstones within a classical spine, not as a project-based ideology) .
- Quantitative estimate
  - pos_neg_hits: +12/-0
  - percent_positive: 1.00
  - percent_coverage: 0.95
- Score: CLASSICAL

Category 4: School Pedagogy or Teaching Methods
- School keywords found (examples)
  - Harkness Table conversation; formal Logic and Debate; oratory and public defense (Senior Thesis)  
  - Oral presentations in Logic capstones; thesis defense to a panel and community (rhetoric-in-action) 
- Mapping to Six Elements lexicon
  - Positive indicators matched: Socratic/Harkness discussion, dialectic, debate, rhetoric/oratory, disputation/defense .
  - Missing/less explicit in public materials: lower-school chanting/singing/memory work; explicit scholé/restful learning language.
  - Negative indicators (e.g., constructivist/project-based rhetoric dominance): not observed.
- Quantitative estimate
  - pos_neg_hits: +6/-0
  - percent_positive: 1.00
  - percent_coverage: 0.60 (upper school pedagogy is very strong; elementary-specific markers like chanting/recitation are not clearly advertised)
- Score: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL

Overall Assessment
- Aggregate rating: CLASSICAL
- Rationale: Strong evidence across five categories with a classical telos, theological integration, and a liberal-arts curriculum anchored by the Trivium, Latin, Great Books, and rhetoric culminations. Pedagogy is clearly dialectical and rhetorical in upper school; lower-school memory/chanting/scholé language is less visible publicly and represents the principal documentation gap to address  .
