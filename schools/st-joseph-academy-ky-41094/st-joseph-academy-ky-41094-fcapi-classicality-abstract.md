ABSTRACT
- The evaluation of St. Joseph Academy (Walton, KY 41094) positions it as SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL overall, with strong CLASSICAL signals in Religious & Metaphysical Foundations and in Parents & Community. The school demonstrates meaningful classical-adjacent features in Curriculum and Pedagogy (e.g., Classical Homeschooling, Captivating Literature, K–8 program structure) but does not consistently manifest a unified, fully classical, trivium/quadrivium-centered curriculum across all grades. Faculty signals lean toward formation and Catholic mentorship, while parent involvement is robust and aligns with classical family formation ideals. The aggregate profile suggests a school that embraces classical education principles selectively, anchored by a solid Catholic identity and active family partnership, but not yet a fully realized classical curriculum for all grades.

- Needle-mover recommendations (for a move toward CLASSICAL across more categories):
  - Implement a school-wide Great Books/Great Conversation framework, with explicit trivium/quadrivium alignment across K–12 (Curriculum).
  - Introduce regular Socratic seminars and scholé-based restfulness pedagogy across more grades (Pedagogy).
  - Develop a formal “living-curriculum” portrait of the teacher and a faculty-development plan aligned with classical pedagogy (Faculty).
  - Expand parent formation programming and explicit home-school catechesis resources (Parents & Community).
