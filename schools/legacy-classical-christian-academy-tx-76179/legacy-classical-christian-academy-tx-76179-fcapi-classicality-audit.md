## Executive Summary

Legacy Classical Christian Academy (Fort Worth/Haslet, TX) demonstrates strong Classical Christian alignment across most rubric categories. The public materials consistently frame education as formation in truth, goodness, and beauty within a biblical framework, and emphasize the Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric), the Quadrivium, the liberal-arts curriculum, and the Great Books tradition. The school also foregrounds a University-Model approach that engages families as co-educators and places a premium on liturgical/community practices (daily chapel, Scripture memorization, Biblical worldview, prayer). While Pedagogy is broadly Classical, some aspects (notably explicit modern Socratic-centric seminars across core grades) are less overt in the public-facing materials, which nudges Element 4 toward “Somewhat Classical” in places. Taken together, the six-category assessment overwhelmingly lands in CLASSICAL, with nuanced room for growth in pedagogy explicitness.
