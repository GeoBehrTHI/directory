## Executive Summary

Grace Academy Boone (Grace Academy NC 28607) presents a robust classical Christian program with explicit theological grounding, a clearly stated mission of formation, and a curriculum and pedagogy aligned with the Trivium and Great Books tradition. Public materials consistently frame education as formation in truth, goodness, and beauty within a Biblical worldview, and they foreground parental partnership, a collaborative faculty culture, and a community-oriented approach. The site repeatedly emphasizes a Biblical worldview, Christian discipleship, and classical aims (grammar-logic-rhetoric, Latin, Great Books, and Socratic/recitation-based practices), with visible articulation of core values, a Statement of Faith, and a home–school partnership model. On balance, the evidence strongly supports a CLASSICAL classification across all six categories of the classical rubric.

Key highlights and textual anchors:

- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations are foregrounded by Core Values (Christian, Classical, Collaborative) and explicit Biblical grounding (Proverbs 9:10; Biblical worldview; Statement of Faith) [Grace Academy Overview; Christian Core Value; Statement of Faith].  
- The School Purpose/Goal centers on formation, virtue, and wisdom within a Biblical framework, not mere job preparation (mission statements, Biblical worldview, and long-horizon formation language). 
- Curriculum emphasizes the liberal arts (grammar, logic, rhetoric), Latin, Great Books, and a history taught in a Christian Tavern of time (Creation to today); explicit Great Books/Great Conversation framing appears in classical education materials on the site.  
- Pedagogy features singing/recitation in the Grammar Stage, memory work, and upper-school Socratic discussion and rhetoric training; explicit Recitation and Curriculum pages document these methods.  
- Faculty is depicted as faith-informed mentors, with a living-education ethos; explicit references to Christian commitments of staff and a mission-driven faculty culture are visible.  
- Parents & Community are positioned as co-teachers and partners in education, with a collaborative framework, home-classroom integration, and community engagement programs. 

Overall aggregate assessment: CLASSICAL.
