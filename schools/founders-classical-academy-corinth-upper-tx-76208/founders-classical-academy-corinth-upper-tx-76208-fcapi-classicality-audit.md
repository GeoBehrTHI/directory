## Executive Summary

Founders Classical Academy Corinth Upper (Corinth, TX 76210 area; associated with the ResponsiveEd Founders network) presents a distinctly classical program in its public materials, anchored by virtue, a knowledge-rich curriculum, and engagement with great books and classical languages. The public materials emphasize formation of character, virtue, and civic-mindedness, along with a curriculum centered on the liberal arts (notably Latin and literature rooted in primary sources and Great Books). However, explicit religious/metaphysical foundations (theology, liturgy, sacred practice) are not foregrounded in the public materials, and some aspects of classical pedagogy (e.g., explicit Socratic seminars, formal “living curriculum” faculty identity, and explicit parent-co-teacher structures) are not clearly stated. Taken together, the school demonstrates substantial classical alignment, but with some gaps that prevent a full CLASSICAL rating across all six categories. The overall classification is SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL.
