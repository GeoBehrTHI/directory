## Executive Summary

St. John Paul II Independent School (JPII) in Colwich, Kansas presents a distinct classical-inflected approach anchored in Catholic liberal arts education. Public pages and crawl-derived materials repeatedly foreground a Catholic identity, formation of character, and a liberal-arts framework that integrates Common Arts with traditional liberal arts pedagogy. The school explicitly emphasizes relationship with God, a Logos-centered intellectual program, and the cultivation of wonder, virtue, and truth, goodness, and beauty throughout its messaging. Pedagogical statements point to Socratic discussion, memory work, and formation through practice, with a visible faculty culture described as mentors and living curriculum. There is also explicit emphasis on parental partnership and community formation, underscoring a classical ethos that sees families as co-teachers in the education of children. Taken together, the public materials strongly align with a CLASSICAL orientation across all six classical elements.
