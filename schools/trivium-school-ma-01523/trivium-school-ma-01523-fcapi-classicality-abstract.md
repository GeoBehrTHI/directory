## ABSTRACT

Trivium School (Lancaster, MA 01523) demonstrates a robust CLASSICAL profile across all six categories of the classical school rubric. Religious and metaphysical foundations are explicit through a Catholic identity, liturgical life, and “truth, goodness, and beauty” formation. The school’s purpose centers on formation and virtue rather than mere job preparation. The curriculum adheres to Seven Liberal Arts and Quadrivium, with Great Books/primary texts and a commitment to the Great Conversation, reinforced by a Senior Thesis and a strong Fine Arts component. Pedagogy emphasizes Socratic discussion in the upper grades and expressive arts in the lower grades, complemented by contemplative elements in liturgical life. Faculty are portrayed as model mentors and the living curriculum, with leadership by the Headmaster and a dedicated faculty body. Parents and community are engaged through open houses and involvement in school life. The overall assessment is CLASSICAL, with strong alignment to all six criteria and an evident commitment to a coherent, formation-focused classical education.
