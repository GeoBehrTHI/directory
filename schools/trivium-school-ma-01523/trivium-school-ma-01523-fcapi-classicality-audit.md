## Executive Summary

Trivium School, Lancaster, MA 01523, presents a robust Catholic classical education that clearly aligns with the Six Essential Elements framework. The school openly espouses a Catholic metaphysical vision, foregrounds virtue and wisdom as aims of education, and implements a curriculum anchored in the liberal arts (grammar, logic, rhetoric; plus quadrivium), Great Books/primary texts, and the Great Conversation. Pedagogy centers on Socratic discussion in the upper grades and a strong fine-arts emphasis, with a culture of reverence for liturgy and communal practice (Mass weekly, chapel, Benedictine monastery connections). The faculty structure emphasizes mentors and a living curriculum, and community engagement is evidenced through open houses and parent participation opportunities. Across all six categories, the evidence supports a CLASSICAL classification, with explicit signals of religious formation, formation-centered aims, a canonical curriculum, Socratic pedagogy, a faculty modeled as the living curriculum, and active parent/community involvement.
