## ABSTRACT

Overall classification: CLASSICAL

Ovilla Christian School demonstrates strong alignment with a classical vision in key areas:

- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: CLASSICAL. The school’s mission and public messaging center on biblical truth, a Christ‑centered worldview, and liturgical life (chapels, worship, church partnership), with explicit theological framing and a Statement of Faith. This aligns with transcendental moral realism and a theology‑driven approach found in classical education understandings.  

- School Purpose and Goal: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL. The formation emphasis (biblical thinkers, lifelong learners, servant leaders) aligns with the classical emphasis on formation over mere information. However, the language is more explicitly theological/Christian than the standard classical lexicon of virtue and wisdom as transcendentals, which places it in the middle tier rather than fully Classical on the canonical rubric. 

- Curriculum: NOT CLASSICAL. Public materials focus on faith integration and Christian worldview across PreK–12, but lack explicit classical curriculum signals (grammar–logic–rhetoric progression, Great Books/Great Conversation, history as a liberal-arts through-line). This category would require a more explicit classical curriculum map to move toward CLASSICAL. 

- Pedagogy: NOT CLASSICAL. While lower‑grade practices include memory/chanting, the site does not publicly articulate a sustained classical pedagogy (Socratic seminars, scholé, rhetoric instruction) across grades. The pedagogy signals are more devotional/formation‑oriented than canonical classical. 

- Faculty: CLASSICAL. The faculty are described as model teachers and mentors, living the curriculum, with credentialing (Masters degrees) and strong Christian formation (born‑again Christians, ACSI‑certified). This aligns with the classical ideal of the teacher as living embodiment of virtue and knowledge. 

- Parents & Community: CLASSICAL. The school emphasizes partnership with families, parental engagement, and community involvement (Visiting, study groups, family resources), consistent with classical education’s emphasis on family partnership and communal formation. 

Aggregate assessment: CLASSICAL

The Ovilla Christian School exemplifies a classical orientation with strong religious foundations, clear formation aims, and a robust faculty and parental/community ecosystem. To reach a fuller, textbook‑classical profile, the school would need to publicly articulate and implement explicit classical curriculum and pedagogical structures (grammar/logic/rhetoric progression; Great Books and Socratic pedagogy) in its public materials and on the campus.

End of ABSTRACT
