## Executive Summary

Great Hearts Irving Lower School (TX 75038) demonstrates strong, multi-faceted alignment with the classical education model as defined by the Six Essential Elements of a Classical School. Across the six categories, public-facing materials consistently emphasize the core classical aims: the pursuit of Truth, Goodness, and Beauty; formation of character and intellect; a liberal-arts curriculum anchored in the Great Books and the Great Conversation; Socratic pedagogy and disciplined memory work; a faculty committed to living the curriculum; and a robust partnership with parents and the local community. The school’s stated core purpose—cultivating minds and hearts through Truth, Goodness, and Beauty—frames its goals beyond mere information transfer, aligning with formation and the classical telos. Public materials repeatedly reference the Socratic method, a liberal-arts curriculum, Great Books and Great Conversation, and a strong emphasis on virtue and wonder, while actively engaging parents and families as co-educators. These indicators map well to all six categories of the classical rubric, resulting in an overall CLASSICAL designation.

Key textual signals include: a mission centered on Truth, Goodness, and Beauty; explicit use of the Socratic Method and a classical liberal-arts curriculum; calls to cultivate virtue and human excellence; reference to Great Books and the Great Conversation; emphasis on formation (not just information); and explicit statements about parent partnerships and community involvement.
