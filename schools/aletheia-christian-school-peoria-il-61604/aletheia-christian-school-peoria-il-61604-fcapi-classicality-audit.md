## Executive Summary

Aletheia Christian School Of Peoria Il 61604 presents a coherent classical-Christian model rooted in a Christ-centered worldview, explicit Trivium framing, and active parental partnership. Public materials demonstrate a deliberate integration of classical aims (truth, goodness, beauty) with a Christian worldview, a defined grammar-logic-rhetoric curriculum, and a pedagogy that alternates contemplative, memory-based lower-division practice with Socratic, rhetoric-focused work in the upper grades. The school frames its mission around formation of character and wisdom in light of a biblical worldview, rather than mere information transfer or career preparation. Relative to the classical rubric, the school displays strong CLASSICAL alignment in most categories, with Category 5 (Faculty) showing only partial alignment and thus a potential area for further strengthening. The aggregate assessment is CLASSICAL.

Key evidence includes: explicit articulation of a Christian, Word-centered education with a biblical worldview (Christ-centered / Word-centered) and a Statement of Faith alignment (Protestant-evangelical) in Academics and Christ-centered sections; a clear articulation of the Trivium (Grammar, Logic, Rhetoric) and Great Books/Great Conversation framing in K–8 materials; a pedagogy that blends memorization, singing, and contemplation (scholé) in lower grades with Socratic discussion and rhetoric in upper grades; and ongoing parental partnership as core to the mission. The school also features a living staff presence across multiple levels, illustrating a culture of mentorship and teacher leadership, though the explicit language of “living curriculum” is not as overt as in some classical schools. Citations from the school’s site and the classical lexicon support these observations.

Evidence highlights:
- Christ-centered, Word-centered education and a biblical worldview (Academics, Christ-centered Education)  
- Trivium framework with Grammar, Logic, Rhetoric stages (K–8) and a focus on Great Books/Great Conversation lineage (K-8) 
- Lower-division memory, chant, song with a contemplative dimension; upper-division Socratic seminars and rhetoric experience (pedagogy) 
- Parental partnership and invitation to participate (About Us / Admissions)  
- Faculty pages and staff involvement illustrating mentorship and leadership (staff profiles) 

Overall, Aletheia aligns strongly with classical education principles and earns CLASSICAL in aggregate, with Category 5 (Faculty) representing the primary area for potential enhancement to reach full CLASSICAL status across all six categories.

- Create a standardized “Teacher Portraits” page that outlines what a classical-Christian teacher at Aletheia should be and do, including observable practices (model virtue, mentorship, classical pedagogy mastery), contributing to a transparent living curriculum narrative.
- Expand professional development aligned with classical pedagogy and publish annual or biennial reports detailing faculty development in Trivium pedagogy, memorization techniques, and Socratic/seminar facilitation.
