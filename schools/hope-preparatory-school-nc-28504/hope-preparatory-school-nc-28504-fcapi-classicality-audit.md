## Executive Summary

Hope Preparatory School NC 28504 presents a robust classical Christian program with explicit religious foundations, a clearly articulated classical purpose, a curriculum built on the liberal arts, and pedagogy that foregrounds memory, Socratic discussion, and disciplined rhetoric. Public materials emphasize a Christ-centered, God-centered frame, daily worship and Bible integration, Great Books and Great Conversation, Latin from early grades, and a strong parental-partnership model. Across six classical criteria, the school demonstrates strong alignment with classical objectives and methods, with little or no evidence of modern non-classical signals.

Overall classification: CLASSICAL.

- Faculty are framed as virtuous, exemplary teachers who model the curriculum and mentor students; the public content emphasizes character formation and teacher-student partnerships.
- Parents & community are central partners, with explicit mention of parent covenant, involvement requirements, book clubs, and family-centered activities.
