## ABSTRACT

Cedar Hall Classical Academy TX 76021 demonstrates a fully CLASSICAL educational model. The school foregrounds gospel-centered, Christian-theological foundations, a formation-focused purpose emphasizing virtue and wisdom, and a curriculum anchored in the seven liberal arts, Great Books, and Western civilization. Pedagogy blends Scholé/restful learning with Socratic dialogue and rhetoric, while faculty operate as living curriculum through mentor-teacher roles. Parent and community engagement is central, with a defined partnership model that treats parents as co-educators and participants in a shared classical mission. In sum, the school aligns with the Six Essential Elements of a Classical School, yielding a CLASSICAL rating across all six categories and an overall CLASSICAL classification.

- Gospel-centered, local church emphasis and Christ-focused mission with Great Books and liberal arts framing: public materials (Key Questions for Parents Considering CHCA; Great Books/western tradition; seven liberal arts).  
- Formation/virtue orientation and “true, good, beautiful” teleology: public materials (formation language; virtue emphasis). 
- Charlotte Mason–influenced, restful pedagogy and living books; public statements about pedagogy: Lower School inspiration, “How it Works at Home,” and “About Us.” 
- Seven liberal arts, Great Books, Western tradition, and history integrated into curriculum: explicit references to the seven liberal arts and Great Books, curriculum structure, and humanities focus.  
- Mentor-Teachers and living curriculum; parental partnership: “Mentor-Teacher Role” language and parent-partnership materials.
