## Executive Summary

Cedar Hall Classical Academy TX 76021 presents a robust classical Christian model across all six categories of the classical rubric. Public materials foreground gospel-centered, theologically grounded education anchored in the Western classical tradition, the seven liberal arts, and a strong emphasis on the formation of virtue, wisdom, and character. The school explicitly centers on a living faith, partnership with families, and a mentor-based faculty structure, with Curricular and Pedagogical commitments that align with Great Books, Latin/Greek-inspired study, and the integration of faith with reason. Public-facing materials consistently emphasize the Great Books, the seven liberal arts, and a collaborative, home–school partnership model, with strong parental involvement and a two-day hybrid format that preserves rest and contemplative learning (Scholé) alongside rigorous study. Overall, Cedar Hall Classical Academy is CLASSICAL.
