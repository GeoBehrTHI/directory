## Executive Summary

Veritas Academy Savannah (Veritas Savannah; ZIP 31412) presents a clearly classical Christian framework. Public-facing materials emphasize a Christ-centered, covenantal approach, the classical Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric), Great Books and Great Conversations, and a explicit partnership with families. The school reveals strong incorporation of theology and biblical worldview across disciplines, a curriculum that foregrounds liberal arts with historically oriented study, and pedagogy that combines chant and memorization in the early grades with Socratic discussion and rhetoric in the upper grades. Parental involvement is highlighted as a core component of the model, including covenantal family-based education and multiple lines of parent formation and engagement. Overall, the six-category assessment places Veritas Savannah as CLASSICAL across all six dimensions, with broad alignment to the classical education rubric and evidence of sustained classical practice in curriculum, pedagogy, faculty posture, and community partnerships. Evidence is drawn from multiple school pages and documents (e.g., Our Mission and Foundational Commitments, curriculum pages, the Trivium framework, and admissions/board materials), and aligned with the positive/negative keyword indicators in the classical education lexicon.
