## Executive Summary

Providence Classical Christian Academy (Arkansas, 72758) presents a robust classical Christian profile across the six traditional elements of classical education. Public-facing materials from the school emphasize Christ-centered, university-model education, a classical curriculum, and active parental partnership. The materials frequently reference great-books instruction, rhetoric and oratory, Socratic-style dialogue in upper grades, and the integration of faith with learning. The school also foregrounds faculty as mentors within a living curriculum and positions parents as co-teachers and partners in the educational journey. Taken together, these signals align strongly with the Classical / fully Classical rubric.

Key textual evidence includes explicit claims of being Christ-centered, biblically integrated instruction, and a classical, college-preparatory curriculum; explicit mentions of the University-Model with heavy parental involvement; references to Great Books and the Great Conversation; descriptions of a Rhetoric phase with debates and public speaking; faculty as mentors and core of the learning community; and formal calls for parent participation, visits, and partnership. See supporting citations throughout.     

Overall classification: CLASSICAL.
