## ABSTRACT

Providence Classical Christian Academy (AR 72758) demonstrates a classical Christian education profile across all six rubric categories. Religious and metaphysical foundations are foregrounded through Christ-centered mission and worship-oriented practices. The school’s purpose emphasizes formation, virtue, wisdom, and a telos rooted in truth, goodness, and beauty. Curriculum signals include grammar, logic, rhetoric, Great Books, and Latin, with a history approach framed as part of a Great Conversation. Pedagogy blends contemplative, memory-based lower-school activities with Socratic dialogue, seminars, and rhetoric in the upper school. Faculty are described as mentors and living curriculum, and parents/ community are integral partners via the University-Model with strong parent involvement and regular campus participation.
