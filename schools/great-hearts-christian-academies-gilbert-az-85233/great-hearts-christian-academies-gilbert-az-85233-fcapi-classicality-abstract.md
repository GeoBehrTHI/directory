## ABSTRACT

GHCA Gilbert demonstrates a CLASSICAL classification across all six criteria. Religious and Metaphysical Foundations are grounded in Christian formation, with explicit references to Christ-centered education, Scripture, and liturgical rhythm (morning prayer). The School Purpose and Goal category is classical, focusing on formation, virtue, wisdom, and the belief in truth, goodness, and beauty as real. The Curriculum category aligns with classical pedagogy via Great Hearts’ classical curriculum, liberal-arts orientation (grammar, logic, rhetoric), Great Books/reading lists, and integration of faith with reason. Pedagogy emphasizes Socratic inquiry, contemplative learning (scholé), memory work, and a path from lower- to upper-school rhetoric and dialectic. Faculty are portrayed as mentors and living exemplars, forming the core of the school’s culture and curriculum. Parents and Community are engaged as partners in formation, with open access to events, tours, and a family-centered ethos.

Cited evidence from GHCA Gilbert materials (mission, foundation, curriculum, academy life, and admissions) supports these conclusions. The school’s public-facing content explicitly references Truth, Goodness, Beauty; Christ-centered and faith-based education; Scripture and morning prayer; Great Books and canonical-reading practices; Socratic instruction; and a family-partnered approach to education. These patterns map cleanly onto the Positive Keyword indicators in the classical lexicon and the Six Essential Elements framework, reinforcing a robust CLASSICAL classification across all six categories.
