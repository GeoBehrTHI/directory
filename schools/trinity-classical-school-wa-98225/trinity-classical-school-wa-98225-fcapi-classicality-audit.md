## Executive Summary

Trinity Classical School WA 98225 (Trinity Bellingham) presents a strong CLASSICAL orientation across all six classical-education categories. The school explicitly grounds its program in Christian theology, treats education as formation in virtue and wisdom, centers the Trivium and Quadrivium within a Great Books framework, and embraces a pedagogy that includes Socratic dialogue, memorization/recitation, and contemplative “Scholé” as part of a living-curriculum approach. The aggregate assessment is CLASSICAL.
