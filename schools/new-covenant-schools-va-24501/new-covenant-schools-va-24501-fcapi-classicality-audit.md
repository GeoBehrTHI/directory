## Executive Summary

New Covenant Schools (Lynchburg, VA 24501) presents a robust, textually grounded alignment with classical Christian education across all six elements of the classical rubric. Public materials consistently foreground a three-part Trivium-based curriculum (grammar, dialectic, rhetoric) rooted in liberal arts, a strong emphasis on Great Books and foundational texts, and a faith-infused community life centered on liturgy, Scripture, and chapel. Evidence from the school’s site shows explicit commitments to virtue formation, intellectual rigor, and a lifelong love of truth, goodness, and beauty, with concrete practices such as Harkness discussions, Senior Theses, liturgical worship, catechesis, and a central role for the arts in education. Public materials also emphasize the partnership with parents and an open, involved school community, including dedicated rituals (Lessons & Carols, Chapel) and parent-facing programs (Center for Paideia, Moms-in-Touch, parent relations). All six categories therefore meet the criteria for CLASSICAL under the Six Essential Elements rubric.

Key evidence highlights:
- Religious/metaphysical foundations are explicit in mission and daily life (Christian, Anglican/Reformed Episcopal identity; Scriptures and Book of Common Prayer; chapel and liturgy).  
- Purpose/goal centers formation, virtue, and the pursuit of truth, goodness, beauty, not job-prep alone. 
- Curriculum emphasizes grammar/dialectic/rhetoric, liberal arts core, Great Books, Latin/Greek, and history within a Western tradition frame.  
- Pedagogy features Socratic/dialectic methods, memory work, liturgical/chanting traditions, and upper-level rhetoric/theater by design.  
- Faculty materials portray mentors living the curriculum, with a strong emphasis on character formation and long-tenured, highly qualified staff. 
- Parents and community engagement is a formal pillar, with ongoing parent formation and an open invitation to campus visits, plus a Center for Paideia that partners with families.
