## Executive Summary

Eukarya Christian Academy (Stephens City, VA, 22655) presents a clearly Christian-influenced classical framework, with explicit triadic Trivium pedagogy (grammar, dialectic, rhetoric) and a program that emphasizes formation, virtue, and a Christian worldview. Public materials show a Christ-centered program, biblical pedagogy, and a curriculum described as classical (grammar/dialectic/rhetoric) with an Omnibus cross-curricular approach. Still, while the school demonstrates several core classical signals (notably in pedagogy and curricular structure), certain elements (notably explicit Great Books emphasis, formal community/parent partnerships, and a fully articulated faculty-formation narrative) are less overt in public materials. Overall, the school aligns most closely with SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL, with strong Classical signals in religion, purpose, and pedagogy, and partial signals in curriculum and community engagement. The school could move toward CLASSICAL by foregrounding explicit Great Books engagement, strengthening explicit living- curriculum language for faculty, and expanding visible parental partnership practices.
