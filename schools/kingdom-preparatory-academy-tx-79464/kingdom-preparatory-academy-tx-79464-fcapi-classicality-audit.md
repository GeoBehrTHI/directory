## Executive Summary

Kingdom Preparatory Academy (KPA) presents a strongly classical, Christian, and collaborative model blended into a single cohesive identity. Public-facing materials frame KPA as “Lubbock’s only Classical, Christian, Collaborative school,” with a curriculum and pedagogy drawn from the liberal arts tradition (grammar, logic, rhetoric) and a curriculum built around Great Books, Western civilization, and a clearly articulated theology-informed worldview. Texts on the site emphasize discipleship, a Christ-centered approach to education, a partnership with families, and a structured progression through the Trivium and Quadrivium, including a senior thesis in rhetoric. Taken together, these signals align with a CLASSICAL ranking across all six rubric categories.

Key observations driving the CLASSICAL assessment:

- Religious and metaphysical foundations are explicit: Christ-centered education, Christian worldview, and liturgical/community practices evident in statements of faith, core values, and campus programming (e.g., Advent Chapel). Positive anchors around theology, worship, and virtue appear in school materials. Evidence: “We are Lubbock’s only Classical, Christian, Collaborative school” and “unapologetically Christ-centered” with explicit theology-infused formation.
- Purpose and goal emphasize formation, virtue, wisdom, and the pursuit of truth, goodness, and beauty, consistent with classical telos rather than merely job-preparation. Evidence: explicit classical mission language, and senior-thesis expectations in rhetoric that signal formation through liberal-arts formation and virtuous aims.
- Curriculum centers the liberal arts and Great Books with a concrete structure (grammar/logic/rhetoric; history woven through the curriculum; Western civilization; Latin and primary sources). Evidence: descriptions of the School of Rhetoric with ancient/medieval/early modern/modern sequences and “great books” emphasis.
- Pedagogy traces a classical arc (grammar → logic → rhetoric) with emphasis on memory, recitation, and eventual rhetoric, plus a high school senior thesis anchored in classical rhetoric; lower-school experiences also align with traditional modes of instruction. Evidence: rhetoric-phase focus, college-style pacing, and senior-thesis requirement.
- Faculty expectations emphasize virtuous character, Biblical worldview, and classical pedagogy; the employment page specifies Christian commitments and alignment with classical methods.
- Parents & community emphasis partnership with families, formation of affections, and open engagement (visits, parent formation, and collaborative models). Evidence: explicit statements about partnership with families and collaborative education, along with opportunities for parent involvement.

Overall aggregate classification: CLASSICAL
