## ABSTRACT

Kingdom Preparatory Academy (KPA) demonstrates a robust classical orientation anchored in Christian theology, a Trivium-based curriculum (grammar, logic, rhetoric), Great Books, and Western-civilization framing. Across six categories, the school shows strong alignment with classical education in Religious and Metaphysical Foundations, School Purpose and Goal, Curriculum, and Parents & Community, while Pedagogy and Faculty show only partial explicit alignment, resulting in SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL classifications for those two categories.
