## Executive Summary

Kenai Classical School (Kenai, AK) presents a clearly Christian, classical-education orientation with a pronounced emphasis on virtue, wisdom, and a transcendent sense of truth, goodness, and beauty. Public-facing materials consistently frame education as formation of character in service of faith, with explicit statements about glorifying God, Christ-centered pedagogy, and a Paideia-inspired aim to cultivate virtue and reason. The curriculum includes liberal-arts foundations (grammar, logic, rhetoric), a humanities-focused approach, history throughout the program, and a formal emphasis on rhetoric in upper levels. There is a structured diploma path with multi-year requirements in humanities, sciences, languages (including Latin), and logic, reinforcing a classical backbone. Public materials stress parent partnership and community engagement as central to the educational mission.

Overall classification: CLASSICAL.

- Purpose and Goal aligned with classical aims: formation of virtue and wisdom as the telos of education, rather than employment-focused outcomes. 
- Curriculum anchored in classical liberal arts and languages, with grammar, logic, rhetoric, history, and emphasis on original sources in humanities.
