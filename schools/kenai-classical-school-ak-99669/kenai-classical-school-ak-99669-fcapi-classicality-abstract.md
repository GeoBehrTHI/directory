## ABSTRACT

Kenai Classical Alaska presents a robust CLASSICAL profile across most of the classical education spectrum: a distinctly Christian, virtue-formation-focused mission; a curriculum centered on the liberal arts (grammar, logic, rhetoric) and the seven liberal arts, with explicit Latin study and original-text engagement; and strong parent/community involvement. The public materials show clear commitments to truth, goodness, and beauty, formation over information, and a "living curriculum" housed in faculty who embody and mentor classical virtues. The school’s emphasis on partnership with families and a community-centered approach aligns with the classical education tradition and the Six Essential Elements lexicon.

- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: CLASSICAL. Evidence includes explicit Christ-centered language, “glorify God,” “nurture and admonition of the Lord,” and a Paideia framing in churchly terms .
- School Purpose and Goal: CLASSICAL. The school targets wisdom and virtue formation with a teleological aim toward truth/goodness/beauty rather than mere job-readiness .
- Curriculum: CLASSICAL. Liberal Arts focus (grammar, logic, rhetoric), Latin, Great Books/original texts, and a historical-humanities emphasis are evident in public content .
- Pedagogy: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL. The school demonstrates discussion-based, original-source-driven learning and rhetoric development, but explicit signs of full Paideia/Socratic-seminar pedagogy are not prominently documented in public pages; a more explicit articulation of Scholé/restful learning and Os-based Socratic/seminar structures would push this to CLASSICAL  .
- Faculty: CLASSICAL. Faculty is positioned as central to the living curriculum with a teacher-centered, mentorship-oriented ethos in alignment with classical formation; explicit quotations about faculty as mentors/models appear in public materials and the Six Elements framework supports this lens .
- Parents & Community: CLASSICAL. Strong partner language with families; parent formation and involvement are highlighted in Kenai materials and echo the Six Elements’ expectations that families participate and are co-educators in the classical tradition .

Overall, Kenai Classical Alaska aligns closely with the CLASSICAL rubric, with Category 4 (Pedagogy) as the principal needle-mover opportunity. Implementing a more explicit, publicly documented pedagogy (Socratic/Scholé/memory-work chants and explicit upper-school rhetoric programs) would move that category definitively to CLASSICAL and further deepen the school’s aggregate alignment with the classical model.

End of report.
