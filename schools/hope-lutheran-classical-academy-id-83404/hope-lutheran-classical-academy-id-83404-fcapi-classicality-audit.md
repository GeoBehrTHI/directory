## Executive Summary

Hope Lutheran Classical Academy Idaho Falls presents a clearlyClassical Christian program with a Lutheran foundation. Public materials emphasize a liberal-arts-based curriculum, Great Books, Latin, logic, rhetoric, and a pedagogy that blends Socratic inquiry with memory-based and liturgical/classical practices. The school foregrounds formation, virtue, and faith as teloi, with explicit language about knowing truth, goodness, and beauty under a theologically framed curriculum. Leadership and faculty are named, with ongoing public communication about the headmaster and teacher appointments. Community and parental involvement are encouraged through enrollment processes, parent communications, and church-school alignment.

Overall classification: CLASSICAL.

What Would Move the Needle

- To move Element 4 from SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL to CLASSICAL:
  - Increase explicit articulation and consistency of scholé/restful learning practices across all grades, with documented schedules and outcomes demonstrating contemplative pedagogy as a central, not supplementary, approach.
  - Expand explicit, school-wide Great-Books integration beyond select grade levels, ensuring a seamless, K–8 Great-Books-based conversation and referencing across core disciplines (not only in selected grade levels or courses). Provide public-facing exemplars (syllabi, sample lessons, and rubrics) showing this continuity.
  - Minimize or clearly differentiate experiential/field-based components from the core classical pedagogy, or situate experiential activities strictly within a classical framework (e.g., as applied contexts for Great Books and liberal arts rather than as replacements for classical methods).

- Concrete steps:
  - Publish a formal “Scholé and Contemplative Practice” plan across all grades with weekly cycles.
  - Publish an integrated K–8 Great Books plan showing how Great Books are introduced, revisited, and connected to grammar, logic, and rhetoric across subjects.
  - Provide teacher professional development documentation showing ongoing classical pedagogy training and a publicly stated standard of teacher exemplars (portrait of a teacher) aligned with the school’s mission.
