## Executive Summary

Telos Classical Academy Utah (ZIP 84098) presents a coherent, biblically centered classical program with strong emphasis on virtue, truth, and the classical liberal-arts framework. Public materials consistently foreground a Christian worldview, a mission of virtue formation, and a curricular/pedagogical posture anchored in classical education (great books, liberal arts, and a classical conversation). The school explicitly anchors its mission in God, virtue, and wisdom and presents graduates who pursue truth, lead with character, and demonstrate resilience. Across categories, the material signals a robust alignment with classical aims, notably in religious foundations, formation-focused goals, curriculum orientation toward liberal arts and Great Books, and a pedagogy that blends timeless methods with a clearly Christian context. The faculty model and parent/community partnership are also highlighted as core pillars. Overall, Telos Classical Academy Utah aligns with CLASSICAL education across all six rubric categories.

# ABSTRACT

Telos Classical Academy Utah demonstrates a robust CLASSICAL orientation across six categories. The religious/metaphysical foundations are clearly anchored in Christian theology and a Creed, with explicit aims to cultivate truth, goodness, and beauty through a God-centered formation. The school’s purpose and goals foreground formation (virtue, wisdom) over mere workforce preparation, aligning with classical teleology. The curriculum emphasizes liberal arts (grammar, logic, rhetoric), Great Books/Great Conversation, and interdisciplinary unity, all hallmarks of classical pedagogy. Pedagogy, while not exhaustively detailed with explicit Socratic seminars in public materials, clearly reflects classical aims via the emphasis on virtuous leadership, memory-work in some contexts, and a historically informed approach to learning. Faculty are described as living curriculum and mentors who continually develop professionally, and the Parents & Community category highlights deep family partnership and engagement in formation. Overall, Telos Classical Academy Utah ranks CLASSICAL across all six categories, with a notable consistency in mission, curriculum, and community engagement.
