## Executive Summary

Medina Christian Academy (Medina, OH, 44256) is represented in the crawl data as Heritage Classical Academy (HCA) with campuses in Pepper Pike, Peninsula, Bainbridge, and Northfield. The material presents Heritage Classical Academy as a classical Christian school operating on a Trivium-based framework (Grammar/K-5, Logic/6-8, Rhetoric/9-12) with a strong emphasis on Christian worldview formation, virtue formation, and a “Great Conversation” with Western civilization’s canonical texts. Public materials repeatedly frame education as formation in Christ and in virtue, with explicit liturgical and communal practices (chapel, hymnody, worship), strong family/parent partnership, and a living-faculty-centered model. The school consistently positions itself against purely information-transfer or career-prep aims, instead foregrounding truth, goodness, beauty, and the cultivation of character.

- Core aggregate classification: CLASSICAL.
- Category-by-category snapshot: all six categories show alignment with classical aims, with explicit evidence of Trivium-based curriculum, liturgical/community practices, formation-first aims, and robust faculty/parent partnership.
- Abstract takeaway: Heritage Classical Academy (as reflected in the Medina/Ohio presence within the crawl content) demonstrates a comprehensive classical Christian program: three-stage grammar/logic/rhetoric curriculum; integrated history and Great Conversation framing; contemplative/socratic pedagogy at the high school level; faculty as living curriculum; and active parent partnerships and church/community alignment.
