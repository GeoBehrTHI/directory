## Executive Summary

Our Savior Lutheran School TX 77091 demonstrates a clear alignment with classical-Lutheran education in its public-facing materials. The school foregrounds religious-theological aims, formation of virtue and wisdom, and a structured trinity of grammar, logic, and rhetoric through distinct divisions (Grammar, Logic, Early Childhood). Public materials consistently frame education as formation centered on truth, goodness, and beauty, anchored in Lutheran faith and liturgical life. Evidence from the website confirms explicit statements about a classical framework, a Christ-centered pedagogy, and a commitment to memory work, rhetoric, and disciplined study. However, while several core elements of classical education are strongly present (religious/metaphysical foundations; formation-focused aims; explicit reference to the grammar-logic-rhetoric continuum and memory-based practice), some hallmark features of a fully classical program—particularly explicit references to Great Books, the Great Conversation, and widespread Socratic/seminar-driven pedagogy across grade levels—are less visible in the public materials. Overall, the school leans toward CLASSICAL in several key areas but remains more accurately characterized as SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL in a few others, producing a balanced overall classification of SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL.
