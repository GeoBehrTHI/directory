## Executive Summary

Toledo Christian Schools (Toledo, OH 43614) presents a robust Classical Christian education that aligns strongly with traditional classical criteria. Public-facing materials frame the school as a distinctly Christian, Bible-centered institution that explicitly embraces the classicalTrivium model (grammar, logic, rhetoric), a history-rich curriculum, Great Books/Great Conversation themes, Latin (and Koine Greek) study, and a pedagogical arc from memory-focused grammar work to Socratic/seminar-style inquiry in the upper grades. The school also foregrounds liturgical and communal practices (Bible classes, chapel, pastor-led spiritual focus), a living-faculty model, and active partnerships with parents and the broader Christian-community network. Taken together, these elements map closely onto the Six Essential Elements rubric and its Positive Keyword indicators, with few if any explicit Negative Keyword signals in public statements.

Key signals include:
- Religious/metaphysical foundations woven into every level of schooling (Bible-centered worldview, chapel, pastor on staff, Statement of Faith)  
- Formation-focused purpose (educate, disciple, prepare; formation of the whole person) and explicit affirmation that truth, goodness, and beauty are real and knowable through a Biblical lens  
- Curriculum centered on the Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric), Great Books/Great Conversation, history studied across disciplines, and classical languages (Latin, Koine Greek)   
- Pedagogy that includes memory/recitation in early years, and Socratic/seminar-oriented discourse, with rhetoric training in the upper grades  
- Faculty described as highly qualified, mentoring, and central to the living curriculum; strong sense of teacher-student partnership 
- Active parent and community partnership; opportunities for family involvement, shadow visits, and open admissions channels 

Overall classification: CLASSICAL

Executive decisions are grounded in direct textual evidence from the school’s site and internal program descriptions, cross-referenced with the Six Essential Elements Positive Keyword indicators. The school’s content demonstrates the core features of classical Christian education: the Trivium, Great Books/Great Conversation, Latin/Greek, biblical worldview integrated across disciplines, liturgical/communal practices, and a faculty-centered living curriculum, all within a framework that emphasizes formation over mere information and partnership with families.
