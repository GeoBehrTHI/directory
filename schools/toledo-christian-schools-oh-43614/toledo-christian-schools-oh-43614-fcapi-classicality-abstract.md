## ABSTRACT

Toledo Christian Schools presents a CLASSICAL classification across all six categories. The Religious and Metaphysical Foundations are grounded in a biblically centered, chapel-led framework that permeates curriculum and culture. The School Purpose and Goal prioritizes formation, virtue, and wisdom, with a telos anchored in truth, goodness, and beauty within a Biblical worldview. The Curriculum explicitly follows the Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric), emphasizes Great Books and the Great Conversation, and includes classical languages (Latin, Koine Greek) along with a history-rich, integrated approach. Pedagogy aligns with scholé-inspired practices in the grammar stage and Socratic/seminar-style engagement in higher grades, culminating in rhetoric training. The Faculty is presented as model teachers and mentors who embody the living curriculum, while Parents & Community are positioned as co-educators and active partners in education. The school’s public materials consistently employ the Positive Keyword indicators associated with classical education (truth, goodness, beauty; virtue; Great Books; Trivium; liberal education; liturgy; chapel; living curriculum; parent partnership). The presence and alignment of these elements indicate a coherent Classical framework with strong evidence across all six categories.

- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: CLASSICAL. Evidence includes Bible-centered worldview, chapel, pastor on staff, and Statement of Faith.  
- Purpose and Goal: CLASSICAL. Evidence shows formation-centered aims (Educate, Disciple, Prepare) and a Biblically grounded telos. 
- Curriculum: CLASSICAL. Evidence of Trivium progression, Great Books/Great Conversation, history emphasis, and Latin/Koine Greek study.  
- Pedagogy: CLASSICAL. Evidence of memory-centered grammar pedagogy, and Socratic/seminar-style upper-level pedagogy with rhetoric training.  
- Faculty: CLASSICAL. Evidence of a living curriculum with highly qualified faculty and mentoring roles. 
- Parents & Community: CLASSICAL. Evidence of strong parent engagement, co-teaching partnerships, and community involvement. 

Overall, Toledo Christian Schools demonstrates a coherent Classical framework across all six categories, with explicit textual signals supporting a robust Classical Christian education model.
