## ABSTRACT

Liberty Common Schools presents a CLASSICAL profile across most rubric categories. The school foregrounds core knowledge, Great Books, liberal arts, a knowledge-based curriculum, and a pedagogy rooted in memory, recitation, narration, and Socratic dialogue. Public materials emphasize virtue, wisdom, and the true/good/beautiful as aims of education, and there is strong evidence of parent partnership and community involvement, consistent with classical formation. The few gaps relate to explicit religious/theological grounding and documented faculty portrait/formation language, which temper a full, doctrinally religious alignment and a fully explicit “living curriculum” framework for faculty. Overall, Liberty Common Schools aligns with Classical education principles and emphasizes character formation, rigorous content, and active parent engagement alongside a robust classical curriculum.

- Evidence of Classical Alignment: Core Knowledge Sequence, Great Books/Great Conversation, liberal-arts framing, memory-based pedagogy, Socratic-style upper-level pedagogy, explicit emphasis on virtue and the true/good/beautiful.   
- Positive/Negative Keyword Signals (from the Six Essential Elements lexicon): Positive: truth; goodness; beauty; virtue; liberal-arts; Great Books; formation; telos; paideia; classical; memory work; recitation; Socratic; scholé. Negative: anti-intellectual; progressive. The site contains explicit uses of positive classical language and occasional negative signals (anti-intellectualism, progressive critiques) to describe opponents of classical education. 
- Notable Evidence Citations: Our Philosophy and Great-Books/Classical Education pages; Core Knowledge emphasis; parent-partner language; high-school classical orientation.   
- Needle-Mover: If the school chooses to explicitly codify and publicly articulate a more formal faculty-portrait and theology-liturgical dimension, and to more clearly claim a fully “living curriculum” for faculty, the CLASSICAL classification would become even more robust. For now, the aggregate assessment remains CLASSICAL.
