## Executive Summary

Grace Lutheran School Brenham (Grace Lutheran School, Brenham, TX 77833) presents a strongly classical Christian profile with clear religious foundations, a purpose oriented toward formation and virtue, a curriculum anchored in liberal arts and classic Christian pedagogy, and an active partnership with parents and the broader church community. The school explicitly identifies itself as “Private, Classical, Christian” and emphasizes Bible-centered teaching, daily prayer/chapel, and Lutheran faith formation within a classical framework. Evidence from the school’s public materials shows explicit alignment with many core classical indicators—Trivium/Quadrivium framing, classical curriculum language, and Great Books-like orientation through integrated Omnibus-style courses—while some aspects of pedagogy appear less explicitly canonical (e.g., explicit Socratic/Seminar methods are not consistently documented across all grade levels). Overall, the school reads as CLASSICAL with a notable, but not complete, alignment in Pedagogy that trends toward SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL in that dimension.

Key observations:

- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: Strong Lutheran/theological grounding; daily prayer, chapel, and catechesis are central; doctrinal grounding is explicit.
- Purpose and Goal: Formation and virtue-centered aims with a clear formation emphasis and a faith-based telos.
- Curriculum: Explicit classical elements (grammar/logic/rhetoric, liberal arts) with a clear Christian integration; presence of Latin study; Omnibus-style high school curriculum; explicit reference to classical education language.
- Pedagogy: Evidence of memory work, singing, and liturgical/recitation elements; public documentation of Socratic seminars or debates is less explicit; overall pedagogy aligns with classical aims but requires more explicit documentation of high school Socratic/seminar work to be fully “CLASSICAL” in this category.
- Faculty: Teachers appear as mentors and living exemplars within a faith-scaffolded community; limited but present evidence of a “living curriculum” through teachers’ roles.
- Parents & Community: Strong parent involvement infrastructure (PTL), open campus/tours, and clear partner-formation opportunities.

The faculty are portrayed as mentors and living exemplars within a faith-based, classical framework, and parent/community engagement is robust through PTL, tours, and open-campus opportunities, aligning with CLASSICAL expectations for Parents & Community.

Overall, Grace Lutheran School Brenham is CLASSICAL, with meaningful strengths in religious foundations, formation-oriented aims, and classical curriculum, and a clearly documented pathway for greater Pedagogy transparency (Socratic/seminar-based practice) would move it toward a fully CLASSICAL status in that category.
