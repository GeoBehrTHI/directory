# Classicality Audit Report: Austin Classical School

Website audited: https://austinclassical.org

Executive Summary
- Overall classification: CLASSICAL
- Why: The school explicitly grounds its mission in Christ (“glorify Jesus Christ”) and in cultivating “wise, virtuous, and humble” students through a “classical, Christian education,” and it frames the aim, method, and tools of education around Truth, Goodness, and Beauty and the Trivium with Great Books and the Great Conversation. Clear evidence for classical curriculum and pedagogy appears across multiple pages. Evidence for faculty formation language and parent formation programming is thinner in the public pages sampled.   
- Method note: This audit follows the Six Essential Elements framework and its Positive/Negative Keyword Indicators (for example: “truth, goodness, beauty,” “Trivium,” “Socratic,” “scholé,” and negatives like “project-based learning” when used as a dominant paradigm), cross‑checked against the classicality lexicon.  

How to read the scores
- Each category below includes: (1) school keywords found; (2) comparison to the positive/negative indicators from the Six Elements; (3) a positive vs. negative tally and estimated coverage; and (4) a rating per category.

Aggregate Rating: CLASSICAL
- Rationale: Robust classical language and implementation in mission, aims, curriculum, and pedagogy (Categories 1–4) outweigh thinner public evidence in Faculty and Parents/Community (Categories 5–6). Using the Six Elements keyword framework—e.g., truth/goodness/beauty; Trivium; great books; Latin; recitation/chanting; rhetoric; and the absence of negative markers like “21st‑century skills” or “project‑based learning” as a dominant paradigm—the school clearly aligns with classical education.
