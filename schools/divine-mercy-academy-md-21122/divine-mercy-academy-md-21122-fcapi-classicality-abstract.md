## ABSTRACT

Divine Mercy Academy (DMA) in Maryland presents a clear CLASSICAL profile, anchored in Catholic liberal education, formation, and a robust Great Books-centric curriculum. The school’s public materials emphasize Catholic liturgy, theology, and moral formation as foundational, with daily Mass, adoration, and catechetical instruction shaping the school culture. The curriculum is explicitly classical: a Trivium-based progression (Grammar, Logic, Rhetoric), integration of theology and the liberal arts, and a strong focus on Great Books and the Great Conversation. Pedagogy aligns with classical methods, including memory-based learning and singing in the lower grades, coupled with Socratic discussions, seminars, debates, and rhetorical training in the upper grades. The faculty are portrayed as a living curriculum and mentors, and parents are positioned as co-educators in a strong partnership with the school. DMA’s alignment with the Newman Guide and explicit Catholic identity reinforce its classical orientation.
