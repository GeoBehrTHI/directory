## ABSTRACT
Overall classification: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL. Bishop Ryan’s public materials strongly exhibit:
- Category 1: Religious and Metaphysical Foundations — explicit theological grounding, daily prayer/Mass, liturgical year, and TGB language (Classical).
- Category 2: Purpose and Goal — mission centered on formation in virtue and the pursuit of truth, goodness, and beauty; education oriented toward God and human flourishing (Classical).
- Category 3: Curriculum — no public evidence of Trivium/Quadrivium, Great Books, Latin, or the Great Conversation framing (Not Classical).
- Category 4: Pedagogy — no public evidence of Socratic discussion, rhetoric training, chanting/recitation, or scholé/restful learning (Not Classical).
- Category 5: Faculty — virtue-oriented formation led visibly by chaplain; otherwise limited classical faculty language (Somewhat Classical).
- Category 6: Parents & Community — partnership with parents and shared liturgical life (Somewhat Classical).

The school would likely attain a CLASSICAL designation with public articulation of a classical curriculum (Trivium, Latin, Great Books, history), classical pedagogy (Socratic, rhetoric, memory/chanting, scholé), and faculty/parent formation language to match its strong theological and formative core.
