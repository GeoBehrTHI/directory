# ABSTRACT

True North Classical Academy Florida demonstrates a robust Classical education orientation with strong alignment to the six essential elements of classical education as evidenced by a Trivium-centered curriculum (grammar, logic, rhetoric), Great Books and the Great Conversation emphasis, and a pedagogy that blends chant/memory work in the early grades with Socratic seminars and rhetoric in the upper grades. The school emphasizes formation, virtue, truth, goodness, and beauty as central teloi, and presents a living, teacher-centered faculty culture. Parent and community engagement is substantial, including fundraising and open-campus opportunities. Category-wise analysis indicates CLASSICAL alignment in Curriculum, Pedagogy, Faculty, and Parents/Community, with Element 2 (Purpose and Goal) and Element 3 (Curriculum) fully CLASSICAL, Element 4 (Pedagogy) CLASSICAL, Element 5 (Faculty) CLASSICAL, Element 6 (Parents & Community) CLASSICAL, and Element 1 (Religious and Metaphysical Foundations) AS SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL due to the strong transcendental focus but less explicit theology or liturgical practice in public materials. The aggregate classification remains CLASSICAL.

Evidence sources (highlights, mapped to categories):

- General Classical framing, mission, and telos: “Our purpose is to create scholars that exemplify the classical ideal – excellence in soul, mind, and body.”
- Pursuit of Truth, Goodness, and Beauty; virtue formation: “pursuit of the True, Good, and Beautiful” and virtue-oriented language.
- Great Books, Great Conversation, and the Trivium: “grammar, logic, and rhetoric” and Great Books emphasis.
- Pedagogy (Socratic Seminar, seminars, debates; memory work; chanting): explicit references to Socratic pedagogy in upper grades and chant/memorization in grammar.
- Faculty as living curriculum; teacher training: “elite, world-class faculty; training in classical pedagogy.”
- Parents & Community partnership and engagement: strong parent involvement, fundraising, open sessions.
