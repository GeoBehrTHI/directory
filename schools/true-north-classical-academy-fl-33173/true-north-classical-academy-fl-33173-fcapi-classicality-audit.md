## Executive Summary

True North Classical Academy Florida (Miami-Dade area, 33173) shows a clear and robust Classical education orientation across most rubric categories, anchored in the liberal arts tradition, Great Books emphasis, and the Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric). Public materials repeatedly emphasize truth, goodness, and beauty, virtue and character formation, a mastery-focused curriculum, and a pedagogy that blends memorization and chant in early grades with Socratic discussion and rhetoric in higher grades. The school presents a strong faculty culture (described as living curriculum and mission-driven), and a collaborative, parent-inclusive community model (parents as co-participants in fundraising and open-campus engagement). While explicit mentions of formal theology, liturgical practice, or organized worship are not pervasive in the public materials, the transcendental aims (truth, goodness, beauty) and explicit classical pedagogy are prominent, supporting a classification of CLASSICAL with minor caveats in Element 1 (Religious and Metaphysical Foundations).

Key signals from public materials include:

- The Great Books framework, grammar-logic-rhetoric progression, and emphasis on the Great Conversation (Classical core).
- Clear statements about formation, virtue, character, and pursuit of Truth, Goodness, and Beauty.
- Pedagogy that includes singing/chanting in the grammar stage and Socratic/seminar/rhetoric in the upper grades.
- Faculty as the heartbeat of the school and a living curriculum, with explicit emphasis on teacher training in classical methods.
- Strong parent/community partnership, fundraising structures, and opportunities for parent involvement and campus visits.

Overall classification: CLASSICAL.

Note: Some of Element 1 evidence reflects a strong transcendental and moral orientation without explicit public theology or liturgical practice in the materials reviewed. This yields a nuanced but still CLASSICAL classification for Element 1, with the other five categories meeting Classical criteria more fully.
