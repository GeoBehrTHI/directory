## Executive Summary

Regents School of Austin (Regents) presents a robust, highly aligned CLASSICAL model grounded in a Christian worldview. Public materials from the school emphasize a classical, Christian formation that centers on virtue, truth, goodness, and beauty, and that seeks to form character and intellect together rather than merely transmit information. Evidence shows explicit commitments to the classical trio of grammar–logic–rhetoric, the Great Books/Great Conversation tradition, and a liturgical, communal life that includes regular chapel and ministerial/theological framing. The faculty are positioned as a living part of the curriculum, and parents are positioned as partners in education. In short, Regents demonstrates strong adherence to the Six Essential Elements of Classical Education, with explicit language and practices across all six categories.

Aggregate classification: CLASSICAL
