## ABSTRACT

Regents School of Austin presents a fully classical education in practice: a Christian, theologically grounded formation aimed at truth, virtue, and beauty; a curriculum organized around the liberal arts (grammar, logic, rhetoric) with strong integration of Great Books and the Great Conversation; a pedagogy that combines contemplative elements with Socratic/Harkness-based discussion and senior-level rhetoric projects; a faculty described as living curriculum and mentors; and a vibrant parent/community partnership. The school publicly emphasizes formation over information, virtue-centered aims, and Western civilizational transmission through a classical lens. Collectively, these features yield a CLASSICAL rating across all six categories, with strong alignment to the “Six Essential Elements of a Classical School (1)” framework. The public materials consistently reference truth, goodness, beauty; theology; liturgical and communal practices; formation of affections; Great Books/Great Conversation; Socratic/Harkness pedagogy; meaningful faculty roles; and robust parental partnership. The aggregate assessment is CLASSICAL.
