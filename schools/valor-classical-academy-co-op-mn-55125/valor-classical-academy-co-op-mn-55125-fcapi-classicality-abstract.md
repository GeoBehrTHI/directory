## ABSTRACT

Valor Classical Academy Co-op (Woodbury, MN, 55125) exhibits a CLASSICAL core in Curriculum and Religious/Metaphysical Foundations, with formation toward virtue and wisdom and explicit Trivium/Quadrivium framing. The Public materials show strong theological grounding (Bible-infused education and Scripture as a truth standard) and a Liberal Arts-centric curriculum anchored by Trivium and Quadrivium. However, several classical indicators—such as explicit Great Books/Great Conversation, Socratic pedagogy in practice across grades, a clearly articulated “living curriculum” faculty culture, and robust parent-formation/partnership language—are not consistently demonstrated in the publicly available content, resulting in a mixed profile across all six categories. Taken together, Valor Classical Academy Co-op (Woodbury, MN, 55125) is best described as SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL, with clear strengths in Curriculum and Religious Foundations and opportunities to deepen classical signaling in Pedagogy, Faculty, and Parents/Community for a full CLASSICAL standing.
