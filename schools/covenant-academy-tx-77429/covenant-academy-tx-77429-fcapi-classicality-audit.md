## Executive Summary

Covenant Academy Cypress (TX 77429) presents a robust classical Christian education profile that aligns strongly with the Six Essential Elements of a Classical School rubric. Public-facing materials emphasize Christ-centered formation, a clearly stated theology, and a commitment to virtue, wisdom, and the liberal arts through a Trivium-aligned curriculum. The school explicitly frames its mission as formation and character development grounded in biblical doctrine, while also highlighting Great Books/Great Conversation-oriented elements and a pedagogy that includes memory work, rhetoric training, and community-with-parents as core to the model. The public materials consistently describe faculty as living exemplars and mentors, and they position parents as partners in the educational process. Overall, Covenant Academy Cypress is CLASSICAL, with some granular pedagogical aspects that warrant explicit strengthening to reach full classical pedagogy at all grade levels.

Key evidentiary signals include: Christ-centered classical education and explicit theology integration; a stated purpose of character formation and virtue cultivation; a Trivium-based curriculum with emphasis on grammar/logic/rhetoric and historical/humanities integration; pedagogy that includes memory work, recitation, and rhetoric training; a living-faculty and mentorship orientation; and active parent/teacher partnership with structured parent formation and open-campus communications.
