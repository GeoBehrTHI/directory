## ABSTRACT
Austin Classical School is CLASSICAL overall. Highlights by category:
- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: Explicit transcendental moral realism (truth, goodness, beauty); Christ‑centered mission; theology and Scripture integrated across the curriculum; communal singing. Liturgy/chapel not explicitly detailed. (Rating: CLASSICAL)  
- Purpose and Goal: Formation toward wisdom/virtue and the transcendentals; education oriented to eternal purpose rather than job training. (Rating: CLASSICAL) 
- Curriculum: Trivium articulated; Latin/Logic/Rhetoric; Great Books/Great Conversation; history (not social studies) progression; Bible central. (Rating: CLASSICAL)  
- Pedagogy: Memory/recitation/chanting in lower grades; debate/discussion in middle grades; Socratic method; rhetoric/thesis defense; language of leisure/wonder present. (Rating: CLASSICAL)  
- Faculty: Clear Christian/qualified teachers and mentoring posture, but fewer explicit classical faculty descriptors (portrait/mentor language). (Rating: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL) 
- Parents & Community: Robust parent partnership via collaborative co‑teaching, structured support and training, and open campus tours. (Rating: CLASSICAL)
