## Executive Summary
Overall classification: CLASSICAL
School: Austin Classical School (austinclassical.org)
Bottom line: Austin Classical School explicitly grounds its mission and model in Christian classical education. Its public materials repeatedly invoke truth, goodness, beauty; wisdom and virtue; the Trivium; Great Books; Latin; Socratic discussion; memory work; and a collaborative partnership with parents. The only area with less direct evidence is Faculty formation language (e.g., “portrait of a teacher,” “living curriculum”). Nonetheless, the school’s faculty claims and practices still align broadly with classical aims. URL for this audit: https://austinclassical.org/ 

— Overall rating: CLASSICAL
