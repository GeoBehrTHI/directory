## ABSTRACT

Ozark Catholic Academy demonstrates a comprehensive classical-education orientation anchored in Catholic faith and a rigorous liberal-arts curriculum. The religious and metaphysical foundations are explicit through liturgical life, sacraments, and Catholic identity; the school’s purpose emphasizes formation, virtue, and wisdom rather than vocational training; the curriculum foregrounds humane letters, great books-informed study, and interdisciplinary inquiry; pedagogy integrates Socratic discussion with memory work and rhetoric; faculty are portrayed as mentors and living exemplars; and parents are framed as partners and co-educators within a faith-based community. Collectively, these features align with the Six Essential Elements’ positive indicators across all six categories, yielding a CLASSICAL classification for Ozark Catholic Academy.

Key primary evidence across the six categories includes:
- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: Catholic faith as central to culture; liturgy and sacraments integrated into school life.  
- School Purpose and Goal: Formation and virtue-forward aims, anchored in faith and reason. 
- Curriculum: Humane Letters, Socratic method, interdisciplinary humanities focus, Latin, philosophy.  
- Pedagogy: Socratic seminars, memory work, rhetoric, and contemplative elements. 
- Faculty: Mentoring teachers, living curriculum, collegial culture. 
- Parents & Community: Partnership with families and parental involvement as co-educators.
