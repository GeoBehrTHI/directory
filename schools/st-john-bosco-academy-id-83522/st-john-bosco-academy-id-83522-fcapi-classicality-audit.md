## Executive Summary

St. John Bosco Academy (Idaho 83522) presents a robust Catholic classical education profile with explicit commitments to the liberal arts, Great Books, Latin, history, and rhetoric, alongside a clearly defined religious foundation rooted in Catholic formation. Public-facing materials consistently emphasize formation of character, virtue, and leadership within a Catholic framework, with recurring language about truth, the mind, the heart, and the formation of Catholic leaders. The school foregrounds a classical curriculum, the Great Books/Great Conversation tradition, grammar-logic-rhetoric, and a mission of faith-integrated education. While the pedagogy leans into restful, formation-centered aims in places and highlights strong family engagement, it also communicates a clear and mission-driven approach to rigorous classical formation. Overall, the school aligns with CLASSICAL criteria across most categories, with only minor ambiguities in explicit pedagogical nomenclature that could edge toward SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL in isolated respects.

- Overall classification by category: CLASSICAL for categories 1, 2, 3, 5, and 6; SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL for category 4.
- Aggregate ranking: CLASSICAL.
