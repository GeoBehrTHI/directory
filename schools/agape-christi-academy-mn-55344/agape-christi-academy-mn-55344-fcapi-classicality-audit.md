## Executive Summary

Agape Christi Academy (Eden Prairie, MN, 55344) presents a robust alignment with the classical education rubric across all six categories. The school explicitly markets itself as a PK–12 classical Christian school, centers its curriculum on the Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric) and liberal-arts integration, channels its pedagogy through rigorous capstones like a Senior Thesis and rhetoric-focused seminars, and frames its mission around formation, virtue, and a biblical worldview. Public materials repeatedly emphasize truth, goodness, beauty, the Great Books/Great Conversation, classical languages (Latin, Greek exposure), and the partnership with families and the church. Evidence drawn from the school’s public content shows a consistent pattern of classical content, paired with explicit doctrinal commitments and a mission to form character in light of a Christian framework. This yields a formal CLASSICAL rating across all six categories. Key insights:

- Curriculum emphasis on grammar-logic-rhetoric, Great Books, Omnibus, Latin/Greek, and historical cycles (Ancient–Medieval–Modern) aligns strongly with classical standards.
- Pedagogy features indicative elements: singing/recitation in lower grades, memorization, and upper-school rhetoric and senior thesis processes, with clear capstone opportunities and Socratic-like engagement implied through seminars and debates.
- The school explicitly frames education as formation in a Christian, biblical context, with an emphasis on virtue, character, and a Christian worldview, and it positions parents as partners in the educational enterprise.
- Some traditional classical signals (e.g., explicit liturgical practices) are less overt in the site copy; however, the combination of explicit theological framing, Great Books content, and classical pedagogy more than suffices for CLASSICAL status.
