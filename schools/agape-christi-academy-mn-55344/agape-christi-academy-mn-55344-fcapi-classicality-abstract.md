## ABSTRACT

Agape Christi Academy (Eden Prairie, MN, 55344) exhibits a comprehensive CLASSICAL orientation across all six classical elements. The school positions itself as a PK–12 classical Christian institution with explicit emphasis on formation, virtue, and a biblical worldview, anchored in the Great Books framework and Trivium pedagogy (grammar, logic, rhetoric). Curriculum signals include Omnibus-style integrative study of history, literature, and theology, cycles through Ancient–Medieval–Modern periods, and explicit focus on Latin (beginning in 3rd grade) and classical languages. Faculty are framed as living exemplars and mentors forming the living curriculum, with a clearly defined leadership team. Parental partnership is a consistent theme—parents are considered co-teachers, and school life invites open houses, shadow visits, and ongoing parent education.
