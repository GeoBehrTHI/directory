# ABSTRACT

Corpus Christi Catholic Academy (CCCA) demonstrates a strong Catholic identity tightly integrated with a classical-education orientation. The school explicitly anchors education in Catholic theology, liturgy, and sacramental life, signaling strong Religious and Metaphysical Foundations. The school’s mission emphasizes formation and truth-seeking through Truth, Beauty, and Goodness, with formation of character and virtue as central aims, supporting a CLASSICAL orientation in the School Purpose and Goal category. The Curriculum reflects classical content (classical languages, literature, history, philosophy, mathematics, sciences) and a liberal-arts orientation, though explicit, cross-grade articulation of grammar, logic, and rhetoric is not as consistently stated across all pages, suggesting a SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL rating for Curriculum. Pedagogy is described as rooted in a classical teaching model and formation focus, but the site does not always publish explicit, repeatable classical-method signals (e.g., regular Socratic seminars or scholé days) across all grade levels, leading to SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL. Faculty signals show a public commitment to classical instruction and visible staff leadership, with limited explicit “living curriculum” portrayals, yielding SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL. Parents & Community are strongly engaged, with explicit statements about parent partnership, parental primacy in education, and formal parent governance structures (SAC), which yields CLASSICAL in this category.

Overall Aggregate: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL.

What would move the needle (summary)

- Publicly codify and publish explicit grammar–logic–rhetoric architecture (Trivium) across grades as core curriculum, including assessment alignment.
- Make Great Books/Great Conversation a central, recurring component with explicit references across grade levels and public-facing materials.
- Publish concrete pedagogy details (Socratic seminars, scholé days, memorization/recitation protocols) with examples and outcomes.
- Publish a formal Faculty Portrait/Living Curriculum description with documented professional development in classical pedagogy.
- Expand and publicize parent-formation programs and formal covenants, ensuring ongoing parental visitation and co-teaching alignments.
