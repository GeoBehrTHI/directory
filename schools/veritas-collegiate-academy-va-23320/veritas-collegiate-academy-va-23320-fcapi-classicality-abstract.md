What would move the needle (concrete steps)
- Publicly commit to a formal Great Books program and Great Conversation framework across grades, with primary-source engagement and cross-disciplinary integration.
- Document and publish explicit pedagogical methods: Socratic seminars, Scholé days, memory work, and disciplined dialogue; provide snapshots or case studies from classrooms.
- Elevate the Faculty narrative: publish a clear portrait of the teacher as mentor, the living curriculum, and the “faculty as core” messaging with concrete examples.
- Formalize liturgical/spiritual practices: articulate a defined liturgical life plan and its integration into academic disciplines.
- Deepen parental formation: publish a formal plan and schedule for parent education and co-educator roles, including book studies and family catechesis.
