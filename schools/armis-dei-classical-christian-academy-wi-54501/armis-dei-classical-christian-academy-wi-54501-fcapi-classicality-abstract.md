## ABSTRACT

Armis Dei Classical Christian Academy demonstrates a CLASSICAL orientation overall, with strong signals in Religious and Metaphysical Foundations, School Purpose and Goal, Curriculum, Faculty, and Parents & Community, and a solid but slightly more implicit Pedagogy profile. The school presents a biblically grounded mission, a formation-focused aim, and a curriculum centered on grammar/logic/rhetoric, Latin, and the study of great literature as part of a classical canon. The parental partnership model and the faculty living-curriculum approach reinforce the classical ecosystem. The primary area for enhancement is the Pedagogy category, where more explicit, publicly documented practices (e.g., Socratic seminars, scholé days, and formal Great Conversation structures) would further strengthen alignment with the fullest classical pedagogy. Overall, Armis Dei sits firmly within CLASSICAL as defined by the Six Essential Elements lexicon, with concrete opportunities to move toward full alignment in pedagogy through more explicit public articulation of seminar-based and contemplative instructional methods.
