## Executive Summary

Armis Dei Classical Christian Academy (WI 54501) presents a robust Classical Christian profile across most of the Six Essential Elements rubric. Public-facing materials emphasize a biblically grounded, virtuous formation trajectory, partnership with families, and a classical curriculum focused on grammar, logic, rhetoric, Latin, and the reading of great literature. The school frames its mission around leading students with biblical truth and a historically informed Christian worldview, with explicit commitments to parental partnership and faculty formation in a Christ-centered, classical pedagogy. The strongest signals lie in religious/metaphysical foundations, school purpose/goals, curriculum, faculty, and parent/community alignment. Pedagogy shows substantial classical roots (grammar/logic/rhetoric, classical model, reading of great books, cooperative/home-education hybrid), but has less explicit, publicly stated detail about Socratic seminars, scholé, or formal Great Conversation structures compared to the strongest classical exemplars. Overall, the school meets the criteria for CLASSICAL with room to more explicitly codify some pedagogical practices that are hallmark traits of the fullest Classical model.

## What Would Move the Needle

- If the school wants to move from SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL to CLASSICAL in Pedagogy, it could publicly codify explicit Socratic/seminal seminar structures for upper grades (e.g., explicit Harkness-style discussions, seminar formats, and Great Conversation-style curricula) and provide a clear Scha of restfulness (Scholé) days in the schedule with evidence of contemplative, memory-based practice.
- Expand explicit references to the Great Conversation and Great Books across more grade levels, with sample syllabi or yearly cycles that connect literature to history, philosophy, and theology.
- Provide publicly accessible exemplars or case studies of how Great Books are integrated with Great Books discussions, including assessment rubrics that emphasize contemplation, moral reasoning, and intellectual virtue.
- Normalize more explicit liturgical or communal practices in publicly accessible pages (e.g., chapel programs, liturgical calendar, or communal prayer glimpses) to further align with Liturgical/Communal Practices expectations.

Note: The aggregate classification remains CLASSICAL, with Element 4 representing the primary area where explicit, public articulation could be strengthened to reach the highest Classical rubric across Pedagogy.
