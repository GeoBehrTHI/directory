# Executive Summary
Overall classification: CLASSICAL
School: Austin Classical School (austinclassical.org)
Why: The school’s public materials repeatedly affirm truth, goodness, and beauty; virtue and wisdom as aims; the Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric); Great Books; Latin; memory work with songs and chants; upper‑school debate, discussion, and senior thesis; explicit Christian theological integration; and a deep parent co‑teacher model. These are strong positive matches to the Six Essential Elements classical lexicon and criteria used in this audit    .  
Method: For each of the six categories below, I extracted keywords and phrases from the school’s materials, compared them against the positive/negative indicators in Six Essential Elements, tallied positive vs. negative hits, estimated percent coverage of the sub‑criteria, and assigned a category rating accordingly  .

URL used for audit: https://austinclassical.org
