## ABSTRACT

Ancient Paths Christian Classical Academy presents a strong Classical-Christian orientation with explicit theological grounding, a formation-focused mission, and a liberal-arts curriculum anchored in the seven liberal arts and Western civilization. Public materials emphasize virtue, wisdom, and a Biblically centered worldview, alongside active parent partnership and independence from state systems. Based on the six-category rubric, Religious and Metaphysical Foundations, School Purpose and Goal, and Parents & Community clearly align with Classical norms; Curriculum and Pedagogy show robust classical elements but lack fully explicit Great Books/Great Conversation and Socratic-practice documentation in public materials. Faculty is clearly oriented toward a classical-Christian identity, but a formal “living curriculum” articulation is not fully evident. Overall, Ancient Paths Christian Classical Academy is BEST described as SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL, with strong Christian formation and classical content, and targeted opportunities to deepen explicit canonical and pedagogical Classical elements.
