## ABSTRACT

Abiding Savior Academy (Sioux Falls, SD, 57103) demonstrates a consistent CLASSICAL orientation across all six classical-element categories: Religious and Metaphysical Foundations, School Purpose and Goal, Curriculum, Pedagogy, Faculty, and Parents & Community. The school publicly embeds a Christian, classical worldview, a Trivium-based curriculum, Great Books, and a layered pedagogy that moves from memory work in the Grammar stage to rhetoric and debate in the Rhetoric stage, with chapel-based liturgical life and an integrated biblical worldview in daily practice. Faculty are portrayed as moral exemplars and mentors, while parents are positioned as co-teachers and engaged community partners. These signals—supported by explicit mission language, curricula, and community practices—align with the positive keyword indicators in the classical lexicon, and there are few to no negative indicators in the public materials.
