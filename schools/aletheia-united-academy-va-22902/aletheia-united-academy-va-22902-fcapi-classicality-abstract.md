## ABSTRACT

Aletheia United Academy Virginia (Charlottesville, 22902) demonstrates a coherent CLASSICAL orientation across all six classical elements. Religious and metaphysical foundations are clearly rooted in a biblical worldview, with Christ-centered education and theology embedded in daily practice. The school’s purpose and goals emphasize formation, virtue, wisdom, and transcendent truth, rather than mere job-readiness, aligning with classical teleology. The curriculum centers on the Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric) and a liberal-arts framework, with integration of Great Books/Great Conversation and a biblically anchored worldview. Pedagogy follows classical methods through a teacher-guided, memory-rich, and sequential approach; the faculty are framed as mentors within a collaborative governance structure, and parents are engaged as co-teachers in a home/classroom partnership. The school explicitly promotes partnership with families, parental formation, and an open, ongoing invitation for parental involvement, aligning with the classical community ethos.

- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: CLASSICAL
  Evidence includes biblical worldview, Christ-centered framing, and theology integrated into education. Positive indicators: biblical worldview, gospel, Word of God, Jesus Christ, Scripture; negative indicators are not foregrounded.

- Purpose and Goal: CLASSICAL
  Evidence includes formation-focused aims, virtue and wisdom, teleology oriented toward truth/goodness/beauty; formation rather than pure information.

- Curriculum: CLASSICAL
  Evidence includes Trivium (Grammar/Logic/Rhetoric), humanities core (History, Literature, Theology, Writing), Great Books/Great Conversation framing, and integration with Scripture.

- Pedagogy: CLASSICAL
  Evidence includes Dorothy Sayers influence, Trivium-based progression, and a collaborative home/classroom with a focus on classical methods; Scholé and Socratic-style elements are present in the broader classical framework, though explicit Sequences may be less overt in every page.

- Faculty: CLASSICAL
  Evidence includes professional teaching, board governance, living curriculum, and mentor-like teacher roles within a collaborative culture.

- Parents & Community: CLASSICAL
  Evidence includes partnership with families, co-teacher model, and parental involvement in education and formation.

Overall, the school merits a CLASSICAL designation, with a strong alignment across all six elements.
