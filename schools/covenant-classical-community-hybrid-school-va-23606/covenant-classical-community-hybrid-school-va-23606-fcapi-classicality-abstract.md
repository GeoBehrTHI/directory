## ABSTRACT

Covenant Classical Community Hybrid School Virginia (23606) demonstrates a CLASSICAL profile across all six rubric categories. The school’s public materials emphasize biblically grounded, theologically ordered education anchored in Scripture and confessional standards (Word of God, Statement of Faith, Westminster Confession, catechesis), with a clearly articulated formation-centered purpose oriented toward wisdom, virtue, and a teleology of truth, goodness, and beauty. The curriculum adheres to a classical liberal-arts framework (grammar-logic-rhetoric), includes Great Books references and a canonically grounded history program, and offers Latin electives. Pedagogy blends scholé-like, contemplative memory work in the lower grades with Socratic seminars and a rhetoric-focused upper program. The faculty are presented as living exemplars and mentors who embody the school’s classical aims, and parents are positioned as co-educators in a robust partnership with the school, including open houses and parent formation opportunities. The school’s content aligns with the Positive Keyword indicators in the Six Essential Elements, and remains clear of secular or anti-theology signals in its public-facing materials.
