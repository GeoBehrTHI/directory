## Executive Summary

Covenant Classical Community Hybrid School Virginia (CCC Virginia 23606) presents a coherent classical Christian program across all six rubric categories. The public materials clearly articulate a biblically integrated, virtue-formation oriented education rooted in a theologically explicit framework, with a solid emphasis on classical pedagogy (grammar, logic, rhetoric), a commitment to Great Books and liberal arts foundations, and a partnership model with families. Evidence from CCCS materials emphasizes biblical authority, theological foundations, and liturgical-style communal commitments, while the curriculum and pedagogy foreground the classical suite of grammar-logic-rhetoric, Socratic inquiry in upper grades, and a rhetoric-focused senior program. The faculty and parent/community language consistently frame teachers as mentors and the school as a partner with families in the formation of character and intellect. Public materials repeatedly describe the school as biblically integrated, theologically grounded, and mission-driven toward virtue and wisdom rather than mere information delivery.

- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: Strong emphasis on God-centered, biblical authority, and confessional grounding (e.g., Word of God, Statement of Faith, Westminster Confession and Catechisms) as the basis for all learning.  
- Purpose and Goal: Education framed as formation in virtue and wisdom, truth-goodness-beauty, and a teleological aim beyond workforce preparation. 
- Curriculum: Clear liberal-arts orientation with grammar-logic-rhetoric, Great Books references, Latin options, and a history-centered approach tied to a Christian canon. 
- Pedagogy: Mix of contemplative/restful elements (scholé-like) in lower grades with Socratic, seminar, and rhetoric-focused instruction in upper grades; explicit rhetoric program; emphasis on memory work, chant, and disciplined reasoning. 
- Faculty: Descriptions of faculty as virtuous mentors and the living curriculum; explicit emphasis on teacher character and pastoral, collegial teacher-student relationships; active faculty opportunities and postings reflect a traditional, mentorship-driven model. 
- Parents & Community: Strong partnership with families, parent formation, and visible calls for parent involvement, ongoing communication, open campus/visits, and community integration. 

Overall classification: CLASSICAL.
