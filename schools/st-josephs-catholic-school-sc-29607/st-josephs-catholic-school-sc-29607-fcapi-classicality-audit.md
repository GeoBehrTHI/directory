# Classicality Audit Report: Bishop Louis Reicher Catholic School

Website audited: https://bishopreicher.com

Executive Summary
- Overall classification: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL
- Why: The school shows strong Religious and Metaphysical Foundations (daily prayer, weekly Mass, explicit sacramental and theological formation) and a curriculum that includes classical elements (Latin, “classic literature” with authors such as Homer, Sophocles, Shakespeare). Purpose/Goal language includes robust “virtue” formation but is mixed with heavy college-prep framing. Public materials show limited explicit classical pedagogy (e.g., “Socratic,” “scholé,” “chant/recitation”) and do not surface a classical faculty profile or parent formation typical of classical schools.
- Standout positives: Four-year theology sequence; explicit “virtues” formation (cardinal and theological virtues); Latin and “classical literature”; regular liturgy (weekly Mass, Eucharist); references to catechesis and sacred scripture    .
- Key gaps: No visible “Trivium/Quadrivium” claims, no clear “Socratic seminar,” “scholé/restful learning,” or “chant/recitation” practices in the lower school; faculty ethos not framed as “models/mentors” or “living curriculum”; parent formation not clearly articulated.

What would move the needle
If Bishop Louis Reicher aims to present as fully Classical in public materials:
- Name the Trivium and Rhetoric: Publish explicit references to grammar, logic, rhetoric; list formal logic/rhetoric courses (with student speeches, theses, or capstone orations) and sample rubrics/outcomes .
- Make pedagogy visible: Describe Socratic/seminar methods in upper grades and chant/recitation/memory work in lower grades; show examples (photos/videos, schedules) and use the terms “Socratic,” “seminar,” “recitation/chant,” “scholé/restful learning,” and “progymnasmata” in Academics pages .
- Emphasize formation over metrics: Rebalance public copy by foregrounding “wisdom and virtue,” “truth, goodness, and beauty,” and “wonder,” while moving test/college metrics to secondary positions .
- Faculty as the living curriculum: Publish a “Portrait of a Teacher” (virtue language, mentoring, lifelong learning, great books seminars for faculty) and “Portrait of a Graduate” (virtue, wisdom, rhetoric, civic friendship), using classical lexicon cues (faculty of friends, mentors, exemplars) .
- Parent formation: Offer visible parent education (book clubs on classical ed, great books evenings, talks on TGB/scholé) and highlight these on the site under “Parents/Community” to deepen the partnership beyond admissions events .
