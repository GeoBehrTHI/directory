## ABSTRACT

Covenant Classical School NC 28027 presents a CLASSICAL profile across all six rubric categories. Evidence from the school’s public-facing materials shows:
- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: a covenanted, Christian, biblically-grounded approach with covenantal partnership with families.
- School Purpose and Goal: formation of virtue and wisdom; shaping learners toward a classical telos rather than job-prep alone.
- Curriculum: explicit emphasis on Seven Liberal Arts (grammar/logic/rhetoric, Latin), Great Books, and integrated history across grades.
- Pedagogy: a balanced mix of contemplative Scholé-like elements, singing/memory work in lower grades, and Socratic seminars plus rhetoric in upper grades.
- Faculty: teachers as mentors and exemplars within a living curriculum and a collegial educational culture.
- Parents & Community: robust parent partnership, open campus policies, tours, and family involvement through PTO and other channels.

Key textual anchors include Covenant’s Covenantal approach and Christ-centered framing, the integration of biblical worldview in education, Great Books and classical pedagogy discussions, and explicit references to Socratic and rhetorical teaching methods, as well as the living curriculum and parent-partnership cues. Citations drawn from Covenant Classical School materials indicate the presence of these elements.
