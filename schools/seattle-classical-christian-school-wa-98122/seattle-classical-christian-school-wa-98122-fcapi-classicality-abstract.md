# ABSTRACT

Seattle Classical Christian School WA 98122 presents a CLASSICAL profile across all six categories of the classical-education rubric. The Religious and Metaphysical Foundations category is strongly classical, anchored by embedded biblical perspective, gospel-centered formation, worship/community practices, and language about truth, goodness, and beauty. The School Purpose and Goal category is CLASSICAL, highlighting formation, virtue, wisdom, and the reality of transcendent truths. Curriculum signals are robustly CLASSICAL: grammar, logic, rhetoric; liberal-arts orientation; Latin; history as organizing principle; Great Books and Great Conversation. Pedagogy evidences Socratic seminars, rhetoric training, and a living-curriculum faculty model. Faculty are portrayed as mentors and core to the school’s mission, with teacher ideals and portraits guiding practice. Finally, Parent & Community engagement is emphasized through partnerships, family involvement, and opportunities for parent education and campus visits. The overall assessment places SCCS firmly in CLASSICAL territory, with no category requiring a needle-move toward a more classical stance.
