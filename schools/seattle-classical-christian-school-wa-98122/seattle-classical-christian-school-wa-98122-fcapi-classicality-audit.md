## Executive Summary

Seattle Classical Christian School WA 98122 (SCCS) demonstrates strong alignment with classical education as defined by the Six Essential Elements. Public materials from SCCS consistently emphasize a Christian, virtue-centered framework, a curriculum built around the liberal arts (grammar, logic, rhetoric) and Latin, the study of Great Books and a “Great Conversation,” and pedagogy that includes Socratic seminars alongside traditional instruction. The school also foregrounds the teacher as the living curriculum, explicit faculty mentorship, and an intentional partnership with families and the broader school community. Evidence across multiple pages and posts highlights a Gospel-centered, biblically integrated approach to learning, communal worship and devotion, and a history-led approach to curriculum. Overall, SCCS is CLASSICAL across all six categories, with particularly strong signaling in religious/metaphysical foundations, curriculum, and pedagogy.

Key evidence highlights include:

- Religious/metaphysical foundations: embedded biblical worldview across disciplines; worship and devotionals; gospel-centered formation; Scripture as the lens for all study.
- Purpose and goal: explicit aim to cultivate virtue, wisdom, and a resilient faith; education as formation, not merely information.
- Curriculum: emphasis on grammar, logic, rhetoric; history as organizing principle; study of Great Books and Great Conversation; Latin as core; education designed to evoke wonder.
- Pedagogy: a blend of contemplative/restful elements with rigorous inquiry; lower grades' memory through singing/devotionals; upper grades’ Socratic seminars and rhetoric training; public-speaking opportunities.
- Faculty: teachers described as mentors and living curriculum; portraits of teacher ideals; faculty/staff pages emphasize mentorship and accountability.
- Parents & Community: explicit family partnerships; emphasis on parent involvement and education about classical education; invitations for visits and engagement.
