# Classicality Audit: Austin Classical School (Austin, TX)

School website: https://austinclassical.org/ 

## Executive Summary
Overall classification: CLASSICAL

Austin Classical School (ACS) presents clear, repeated, and substantive evidence of a classical Christian identity across mission, curriculum, and pedagogy. Public materials explicitly center truth, goodness, and beauty; the cultivation of wisdom and virtue; the Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric); Latin; integrated studies; memory work; discussion/debate; and upper‑school rhetoric/thesis—all core positive indicators in the Six Essential Elements framework and lexicon. The school’s mission (“glorify Jesus Christ,” “cultivate wise, virtuous, and humble students”) and curricular pages (Trivium, Latin, Great Books, Christian Studies) strongly align with classical norms.   

Six-category ratings:
- Religious & Metaphysical Foundations: CLASSICAL
- Purpose & Goal: CLASSICAL
- Curriculum: CLASSICAL
- Pedagogy: CLASSICAL
- Faculty: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL
- Parents & Community: CLASSICAL

Method note: Keyword analysis and scoring use the Positive/Negative Keyword Indicators described in the Six Essential Elements document and the classicality lexicon; judgments below reference both the ACS site and these indicator sets.
