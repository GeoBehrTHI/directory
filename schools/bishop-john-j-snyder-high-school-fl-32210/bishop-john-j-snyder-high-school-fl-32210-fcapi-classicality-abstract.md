## ABSTRACT
Austin Classical School is classified as CLASSICAL. Highlights include:
- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: Christ-centered telos; transcendental focus on truth, goodness, and beauty; theology integrated across learning.  
- Purpose and Goal: Strong emphasis on formation—cultivating wisdom and virtue; participation in the “great conversation” over careerist aims. 
- Curriculum: Explicit Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric), Latin instruction, Great Books/Western tradition, integrated studies, Christian Studies.  
- Pedagogy: Memory work (memorization, recitation, songs) in lower grades; debate/discussion and thesis defense in upper grades; orientation to wonder and contemplation.  
- Faculty: Public framing is positive (teachers serving God via classical approach) but briefer on explicit teacher‑formation language; rated SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL. 
- Parents & Community: School “co‑labors with families” and provides resources that educate parents in classical principles; rated CLASSICAL.
