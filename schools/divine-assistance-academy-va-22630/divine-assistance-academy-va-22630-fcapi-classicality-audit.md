## Executive Summary

Divinum Auxilium Academy (Divine Assistance Academy), Front Royal, VA 22630 (Divine Assistance Academy VA 22630) presents a coherent classical-leaning program that blends Montessori foundations with a clearly stated aim toward the classical liberal arts and Catholic formation. Public materials emphasize Christ-centered education, catechesis, liturgical life, a multi-age classroom model, and the integration of faith with rigorous academics (Montessori-primary through Grammar School; Catechesis of the Good Shepherd; Latin and classical history content). The school articulates a formation-focused aim (virtue, wisdom, and love of truth), ongoing formation of faculty within a Catholic framework, and a partnership with parents as co-educators. Across six classical criteria categories, the school demonstrates strong alignment with classical education principles, though the explicit emphasis on certain classic components (e.g., explicit Great Books/Great Conversation or a consistent Socratic program in upper eras) is present primarily through the school’s stated classical framing and catechetical approach rather than through direct, external Great Books prescriptions. Overall assessment: CLASSICAL.

Key insights driving the classification:

- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations are strongly evident: Catholic identity, liturgical life, CGS (Catechesis of the Good Shepherd), and religious formation integrated with academics. Evidence includes Christ-centered language, the CGS atrium, and an oath of fidelity to the Magisterium.
- School Purpose and Goal centers on formation, virtue, and knowledge of transcendentals (truth, goodness, beauty) consistent with classical aims. The mission emphasizes formation and encounter with Christ, virtue formation, and the classical tradition.
- Curriculum is framed as liberal-arts oriented (Montessori-to-Classical transition, classical content via grammar-lower grammar-grammar-school pathways, Latin, world history). The program explicitly notes a classical liberal-arts trajectory.
- Pedagogy includes both contemplative/restful (Scholé) and active, Socratic-style elements, with strong emphasis on memorization/recitation and catechesis. Lower-division singing, prayer, and memory work align with the classical pedagogy framework.
- Faculty are presented as living curriculum, Catholic exemplars, and mentors with formal catechetical and Montessori credentials; staff oath signals alignment with ecclesial norms.
- Parents & Community engagement is explicit through parent observation, rolling admissions with parent involvement, and a stated belief that parents are primary educators, with ongoing opportunities for parent formation and campus visits.
