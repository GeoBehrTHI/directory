## Executive Summary

Runnels Academy TX 79720 presents a robust CLASSICAL Christian educational model across all six rubric categories. Public-facing materials consistently foreground a biblical, theologically grounded curriculum centered on the Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric), Great Books, Latin/Greek, and a Christ-centered worldview. The school communicates formation of character and virtue as central aims, emphasizes rigorous classical content, and embeds active parental partnership within a community oriented around shared faith and governance. Evidence from the school’s site repeatedly ties academics to theology, virtue, and a Christian worldview, with explicit statements about a Bible-centered curriculum, a Great Books emphasis, and a deliberate, formation-focused philosophy. While liturgical practices are not heavily foregrounded in public materials, the integration of faith with learning, the Great Conversation, and parental partnership are consistently stressed.

Executive Summary — Element-by-Element Snapshot

Element 1: Religious and Metaphysical Foundations — CLASSICAL
- Evidence: Explicit statement of faith, biblical worldview, and theology at the center of education; Christ-centered mission; statement of faith detailing core beliefs.
- Positive/Negative keyword analysis: Strong presence of positive religious/theological keywords (Bible, God, theology, biblical worldview, Christ-centered, Gospel). No clear negative indicators in public materials.
- Why this rating: The school’s materials foreground transcendental religious aims, theological grounding, and communal worship-oriented culture through faith-centered statements.

Element 2: School Purpose and Goal — CLASSICAL
- Evidence: Mission to form virtuous, wise students; aim to shape culture through wise and victorious Christian living; emphasis on formation, not merely information; pursuit of truth and beauty as real.
- Positive/Negative keyword analysis: Positive indicators include formation, virtue, truth, beauty, and a unified teleology around human flourishing under a Christian worldview. Limited negative signals in public materials.
- Why this rating: The purpose centers on formation, virtue, and the cultivation of truth and beauty as real, aligning strongly with classical aims.

Element 3: School Curriculum — CLASSICAL
- Evidence: Explicit description of the Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric); emphasis on primary sources in history; Great Books/Great Conversation; Latin; Greek; Story of the World as history text; integration of classical arts and languages.
- Positive/Negative keyword analysis: Strong presence of positive curriculum keywords (grammar, logic, rhetoric, Great Books, Trivium, Latin, Greek, history with primary sources). Minimal or no negative signals in public content.
- Why this rating: Curriculum aligns closely with liberal arts core elements and the Great Conversation framework central to classical education.

Element 4: School Pedagogy or Teaching Methods — CLASSICAL
- Evidence: Lower school activities include singing, memorization, and recitation; upper school includes Socratic discussion, debates, formal logic, and rhetoric; songs and memory work highlighted; hands-on projects; field trips; presentations.
- Positive/Negative keyword analysis: Positive pedagogical keywords include recitation, memorization, Socratic dialogue, debate, logic, rhetoric, and living-books approaches. Some notes of contemplative/restful pedagogy are present in formation emphasis, with a blend of restfulness and rigor.
- Why this rating: Pedagogy reflects a described progression from memory-based, contemplative lower-forms to dialectic, rhetoric-based upper-forms consistent with classical practice.

Element 5: School Faculty — CLASSICAL
- Evidence: Emphasis on model teachers and mentors, the living curriculum, a professional community; parent-school partnership; statements about faculty as core to the school; portraits of teacher standards and expectations.
- Positive/Negative keyword analysis: Positive indicators include living curriculum, model teachers, mentorship, and professional, godly conduct. The materials stress the role of faculty as anchors of the school’s ethos.
- Why this rating: The faculty framing emphasizes virtue, mentorship, and the faculty’s central role in delivering formation, aligning with classical expectations.

Element 6: Parents & Community — CLASSICAL
- Evidence: Explicit statement that parents are primary in education; emphasis on parental partnership, co-teaching role, and active parent involvement; invitation to visits; materials about family involvement in admissions and school life.
- Positive/Negative keyword analysis: Positive indicators include “parents are primary,” “parents as co-teachers,” involvement in admissions, and open invitations for community engagement; low or no negative signals detected.
- Why this rating: The institution positions parents as essential partners in a classical education and promotes active parental involvement and learning opportunities for families.
