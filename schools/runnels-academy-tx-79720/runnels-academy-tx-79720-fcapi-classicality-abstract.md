## ABSTRACT

Runnels Academy TX 79720 demonstrates a cohesive CLASSICAL framework across all six classical education categories. The Religious and Metaphysical Foundations are anchored by a robust theological core and a biblical worldview evident in statements of faith and mission (CLASSICAL). The School Purpose and Goal emphasize formation, virtue, truth, and beauty as real, aligning with classical teleology (CLASSICAL). The Curriculum centers on the Trivium, Great Books, Latin/Greek, and primary-source history, with a clear liberal-arts orientation (CLASSICAL). Pedagogy combines memory-based lower-school practices with Socratic discourse, debates, and rhetoric in upper grades, reflecting the classical progression from grammaticals to rhetorics (CLASSICAL). Faculty are presented as living curriculum and mentors, with a strong emphasis on character formation and professional governance (CLASSICAL). Parents and Community engagement is a foundational element, with explicit positioning of parents as co-teachers and partners in a faith-centered educational journey (CLASSICAL).

Overall, Runnels Academy is CLASSICAL in its public materials, with explicit commitments to theology, the Trivium, Great Books, virtuous formation, and active parental involvement. The alignment with the Six Essential Elements indicators is strong, and no major gaps in the classical criteria emerge from the available site content. The school’s posture toward formation, truth, goodness, and beauty—coupled with a clearly stated biblical framework and community integration—solidifies its CLASSICAL designation.
