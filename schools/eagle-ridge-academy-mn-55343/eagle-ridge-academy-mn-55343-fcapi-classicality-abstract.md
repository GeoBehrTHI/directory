## ABSTRACT

Eagle Ridge Academy presents a CLASSICAL orientation in its public materials, anchored by a time-tested Classical Liberal Arts mission, a grammar-logic-rhetoric curricular scaffold, and a visible emphasis on virtue, character, and truth-seeking. The school communicates a clear telos toward formation of wisdom and virtue within a classical tradition, supported by explicit Pillars (Veritas, Pulchritudo, Probitas) and a values-based framework (CIPHER). Public materials show strong curriculum alignment with classical norms and robust parent/community engagement, while explicit theological grounding and liturgical practices are not strongly evidenced in the public domain. The aggregate assessment places ERA as SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL, with stronger signals in Curriculum, Purpose/Goal, and Parent/Community; moderate signals in Religious Foundations; and partial signals in Pedagogy and Faculty that would benefit from more explicit articulation of classical methods (e.g., Socratic pedagogy, Souled memory, and a formal living-curriculum portrait). Moving toward full CLASSICAL would entail codifying and publicly sharing explicit Socratic/seminar pedagogy, a more overt Great Books/Great Conversation program across grades, a formal articulation of the living curriculum and teacher portraits, and (if appropriate) a theologically grounded or liturgical dimension aligned with ERA’s mission and policy context.
