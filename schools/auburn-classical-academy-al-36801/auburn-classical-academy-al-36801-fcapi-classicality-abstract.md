## ABSTRACT

- Classification: CLASSICAL
- Core justification: ACA demonstrates strong classical alignment across Religious/Metaphysical Foundations, School Purpose & Goal, Curriculum, Faculty, and Parents & Community, with evidence of Veritas/theology integrated into curriculum, Trivium framing, Latin/Greek study, and a classical literature canon. Public materials emphasize liturgical practice, community partnerships with parents, and a family-centered approach consistent with classical education principles.
- Category-by-category highlights:
  - Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: Explicit grounding in truth/theology; liturgy/chapel; doctrinal creeds; Christ-centered framing. CLASSICAL.
  - School Purpose and Goal: Formation, virtue, wisdom; classical telos; formation over mere information. CLASSICAL.
  - Curriculum: Grammar/Logic/Rhetoric; canonical literature; Latin/Greek; Western civilization; historical content across grades. CLASSICAL.
  - Pedagogy: Memory/recitation in grammar stage and rhetoric emphasis in upper grades; some evidence of teacher-led, contemplative pedagogy; limited explicit documentation of Socratic seminars. SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL.
  - Faculty: Clear faculty/governance structure; teachers as core/central to the program; living-curriculum framing is implied through Distinctives. CLASSICAL.
  - Parents & Community: Strong parent partnership; learning opportunities for parents; family-centered events and open campus. CLASSICAL.

- Needle-moving opportunities (for SOMEWHAT to CLASSICAL shifts): Implement explicit, regular Socratic seminars; publish a formal Great Books/Great Conversation program; articulate the “living curriculum” with concrete examples and faculty development practices.

Citations to source material include ACA’s Veritas/theology framing (Distinctives), liturgical practices (FAQ), Trivium framing (Academics), canonical literature and language curriculum (Curriculum pages), and parent/community materials (Familia, Parent resources, Family Events).
