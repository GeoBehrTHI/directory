## Executive Summary

Restoration Academy, Alabama 35064, presents a notably religious, virtue-formation–driven model of schooling with a strong emphasis on Christ-centered education, virtue formation, and community involvement. The public materials reviewed reveal a cohesive alignment with several classical education sensibilities (notably: formation of character, memory-based pedagogy, and explicit parental partnership). However, the curriculum responsiveness to the canonical classical triad (grammar, logic, rhetoric) and the explicit Great Books / Great Conversation components are not clearly foregrounded in the RA public materials. Taken together, the school demonstrates substantial classical-influenced aspects—especially in its character formation, liturgical/communal emphasis, and emphasis on vocations and virtue—while not fully meeting all core classical criteria (notably the explicit Great Books / trivium emphasis in curriculum). Overall, Restorations Academy is CLASSICAL with meaningful, category-specific strengths and a few gaps that prevent it from achieving a full, textbook “classical” classification in every category.
