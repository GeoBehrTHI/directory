## Executive Summary

Leman Academy of Excellence – Parker Stroh (Colorado, 80134) presents as a public charter K–8 that explicitly identifies itself as offering a tuition-free classical education built on a foundation of wisdom and virtue. Public-facing materials emphasize a classical orientation, small-class benefits, and a strong partnership with families. The school clearly communicates goals around character formation and virtuous learning, while highlighting parent involvement and a supportive community. However, while the school markets itself as classical and uses language aligned with classical education, public materials do not consistently detail core classical curricular components (e.g., grammar, logic, rhetoric; Great Books) or explicit Socratic pedagogy in the K–8 context. Pedagogical specifics such as cycles of memorization/recitation, Socratic discussion, or explicit liturgical/community practices are not prominently evidenced in the public content available from the crawl.

Overall classification (aggregate): SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL.

Key takeaways:
- Strong signals of classical branding and virtue-oriented aims (e.g., “classical education,” “foundation of wisdom and virtue”) with explicit emphasis on parental partnership and community (cited evidence).   
- Explicit, named classical curriculum components (grammar/logic/rhetoric; Great Books; deep history) are not clearly stated in the publicly crawled materials, suggesting only partial alignment with the full classical curriculum rubric. 
- Parent engagement and community orientation are pronounced (positive alignment with Six Elements).
