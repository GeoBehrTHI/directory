## Executive Summary

Palm Beach Christian Academy Palm Beach Gardens (PBCA) presents itself prominently as a Christian, classical school that centers on the Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric), great books, Socratic dialogue, and a Christ-centered community. Public-facing materials describe PBCA as Distinctively Christian, Distinctively Classical, and Distinctively Community, with explicit emphasis on Scripture-centered formation, chapel and worship, and a mission-driven partnership with families. The website repeatedly positions itself as a classical, faith-formed education that partners with parents and seeks to form virtue and wisdom, not merely to deliver information. These elements strongly align PBCA with core criteria in the Six Essential Elements framework, especially around religious/metaphysical foundations, formation as a goal, a Trivium-centered curriculum, Socratic pedagogy, living/mentoring faculty, and robust parent-community partnership.

Overall classification: CLASSICAL

- PBCA’s public materials foreground theological grounding, communal worship, spiritual formation, and a Trivium-based, great-books approach to curriculum. There is consistent emphasis on virtue formation, biblical worldview, and partnership with families, which map directly onto the Positive Keyword indicators in the Six Essential Elements lexicon. The absence of explicit secular or value-neutral framing in the public materials further reinforces the Classical alignment.  

Executive observations by category (Element-by-Element Snapshot): PBCA demonstrates strong alignment across all six classical criteria, with explicit signals for each of the six categories. The strongest signals come from religious/metaphysical foundations, curriculum (Trivium + Great Books), and pedagogy (Socratic dialogue, seminars in higher grades implied by the site’s classical framing). The parent/community partnership and faculty signals are also explicit and robust. No evidence of secular-only framing or project-based-only approaches appears in the public-facing PBCA materials.

- What would move the needle: If the school is classified as CLASSICAL, no major needle-moving changes are required. PBCA already embodies the core classical markers; marginal gains would come from explicit, published alignment statements tying day-to-day practices to the specific Positive Keyword indicators in the Six Essential Elements lexicon (e.g., explicit "great conversation" articulation in curriculum documents, a formalized statement of “formation of character” across grade levels, and a clearly documented “living curriculum” language in faculty materials). However, this would be enhancements rather than essential changes.

Citations note: PBCA content explicitly describing Christian, classical, and community identity; Trivium and great books; chapel and Scripture; and family partnership are drawn from PBCA public materials (Palm Beach Gardens Campus pages and related PBCA content). See sources detailing Distinctively Christian/Classical/Community framing and the Trivium/Great Books with Socratic dialogue, as well as Chapel and Scripture-based formation.

## What Would Move the Needle

- PBCA is categorized as CLASSICAL across all six elements. No category is currently in the SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL range. Therefore, no major needle-moving changes are required. If desired, minor refinements could include:
  - Publish explicit cross-walk documents mapping day-to-day programs to the Positive Keyword indicators in the Six Essential Elements lexicon (e.g., “Great Conversation” formalization in a curriculum guide).
  - Provide a concise, public articulation of “formation of virtue” across all grade levels (K–12) with explicit outcomes and assessment aligned to the classical virtues.
  - Expand faculty-facing documents to explicitly describe the living curriculum and mentorship roles in teacher job descriptions and recruitment materials.

- These are incremental enhancements, not necessary to achieve CLASSICAL status, but could strengthen the clarity and consistency of the Classical identity.
