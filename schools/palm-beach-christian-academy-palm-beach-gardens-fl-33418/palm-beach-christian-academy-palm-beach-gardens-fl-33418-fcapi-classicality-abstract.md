## ABSTRACT

Palm Beach Christian Academy Palm Beach Gardens presents a robust CLASSICAL profile, anchored in Christian orthodoxy and a classical curriculum. The school explicitly proclaims Distinctively Christian, Distinctively Classical, and Distinctively Community, with a Trivium-centered program, Great Books, and Socratic dialogue. Public materials emphasize chapel, Scripture, and a biblical worldview, signaling a strong religious/metaphysical foundation. The school targets formation—virtue, wisdom, and character—through a teacher-student partnership and family involvement, aligning with the Six Essential Elements’ emphasis on formation, teleology, and community. The curriculum centers on the liberal arts (grammar, logic, rhetoric) and the Quadrivium in a Great-Books framework; pedagogy includes Socratic discussion and memory-based practices, while faculty and parental partnerships appear as core to the school’s mission. Parent/community engagement, living curriculum, and teacher mentorship are consistently highlighted.

Overall classification: CLASSICAL

- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: CLASSICAL (Distinctively Christian; Scripture-centered; liturgical practices; theocentric worldview). Positive indicators include Christ-centered formation, worship/chapel, and biblical worldview.  
- School Purpose and Goal: CLASSICAL (formation and wisdom with truth, goodness, and beauty as real). Positive orientation toward virtue formation and teleology beyond mere job prep. 
- Curriculum: CLASSICAL (Trivium, Great Books, Socratic dialogue, history integrated with literature). 
- Pedagogy: CLASSICAL (Socratic dialogue, memorization, rhetoric, and contemplative pedagogy). 
- Faculty: CLASSICAL (model teachers/mentors; living curriculum; collaborative culture). 
- Parents & Community: CLASSICAL (partners with families; open campus; parent formation).
