# ABSTRACT

Harrisonville Classical Christian Academy (HCCA) presents a CLASSICAL orientation in a Christian framework, anchored by biblical worldview language, Memoria Press curriculum, and engagement with Great Books and the Western canon, while also highlighting parental partnership and a leadership-focused mission. The strongest signals occur in Religious & Metaphysical Foundations and Parents & Community, with robust formation-oriented aims across School Purpose and Goal. The Curriculum shows strong classical content through Memoria Press, Great Books, and Latin, but explicit public articulation of the full Trivium/Quadrivium and a fully developed Pedagogy (Socratic/seminar-based methods and Scholé) are less consistently documented in the public-facing content, yielding a SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL rating for Curriculum and Pedagogy. Faculty presentation is present but lacks a strongly codified living-curriculum portrait in public materials, resulting in a SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL rating for Faculty. Overall, the aggregate classification is SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL, with concrete steps outlined in the “What Would Move the Needle” section to push toward full CLASSICAL alignment.

- Faculty: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL

- Parents & Community: CLASSICAL
  Strong signals of parental partnership, co-teaching, and community engagement.
