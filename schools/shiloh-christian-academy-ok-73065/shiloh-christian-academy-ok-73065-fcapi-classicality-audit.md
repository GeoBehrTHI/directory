# Executive Summary
Overall classification: CLASSICAL  
School: Austin Classical School (ACS)  
Website used for this audit: https://austinclassical.org/ 

ACS presents a robust classical Christian identity in mission, curriculum, and pedagogy. The site repeatedly affirms the pursuit of truth, goodness, and beauty; the cultivation of wisdom and virtue; and a Trivium-centered program with Latin, logic, and rhetoric culminating in thesis defense and oratory. The collaborative model explicitly “co-labors with families,” and faculty are framed as Christian exemplars who model intellectual and moral formation. Notably, the school reports ACCS accreditation as of Spring 2025, further corroborating its classical Christian orientation.  

Below is the required six-category keyword analysis and scoring, using the Positive and Negative Keyword Indicators from “Six Essential Elements of a Classical School,” plus the classicality lexicon as the scoring framework.
