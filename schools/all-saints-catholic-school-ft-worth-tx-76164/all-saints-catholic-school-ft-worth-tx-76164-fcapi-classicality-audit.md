## Executive Summary

All Saints Catholic School Fort Worth (76164) presents a pronounced classical-Catholic orientation across its public-facing materials. The school consistently frames its mission and daily practice around a Catholic, classical liberal-arts approach, with explicit emphasis on formation of virtue, wisdom, and character within a dual-language (English/Spanish) program. Public content highlights include: a stated aim to form students in the Classical tradition guided by the Gospel, integration of liturgical life and Catholic pedagogy, a curriculum anchored in the trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric) and Latin, a commitment to Great Books/readings, a house-based community model, a robust parental partnership through a Home & School Organization, and a clear emphasis on Catholic identity (Mass, adoration, prayer, catechesis, service).

Key evidence from the school’s public materials includes:
- Religious and metaphysical foundations are foregrounded by language about the Gospel-guided formation of students and a liturgical life (Mass, adoration) embedded in weekly rhythms. 
- The school explicitly identifies itself as Catholic and classical, with language about truth, goodness, beauty, and a dual-language classical program. 
- Curriculum centers on classical-trivium elements (grammar, logic, rhetoric), Latin study, Great Books-inspired reading, and a curriculum aligned with Catholic standards. 
- Pedagogical notes emphasize both contemplative/restful and rigorous approaches (lower-school singing/memory work; upper-school Socratic/seminar-style work and rhetoric), with explicit emphasis on mastery and traditional pedagogy. 
- Faculty references emphasize dedicated, virtuous mentorship and the “living curriculum.” 
- Parental/community engagement is strong via the Home & School Organization (HSO) and activities/clubs that connect family life to the school ethos. 

Overall category-by-category assessment (using the Six Essential Elements framework):
- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: CLASSICAL
- Purpose and Goal: CLASSICAL
- Curriculum: CLASSICAL
- Pedagogy: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL
- Faculty: CLASSICAL

Aggregate ranking: CLASSICAL

Notes on needle-moving opportunities (for sites ranked SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL): The Pedagogy category is the closest to a potential improvement, with room to sharpen explicit evidence of Socratic/seminar-based practice in upper grades and to foreground the Great Books/Great Conversation framework more consistently across grade levels. See “What would move the needle” for concrete steps.
