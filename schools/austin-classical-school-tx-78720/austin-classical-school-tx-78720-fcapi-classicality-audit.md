# Austin Classical School — Classicality Audit

Website: https://austinclassical.org

## Executive Summary
Overall classification: CLASSICAL

Austin Classical School is explicitly and comprehensively classical and Christian. The school’s public materials consistently invoke core classical aims (cultivating wisdom and virtue; pursuing truth, goodness, and beauty), the liberal arts (Trivium with Latin, logic, and rhetoric), classical pedagogy (memorization, recitation/chanting in Grammar; debate/Socratic discussion in Upper School; senior thesis/oral defense), and a robust Christian, liturgical rhythm (daily/all‑school chapel; faculty bound to a Statement of Faith). Parents are positioned as partners and co‑teachers in a collaborative model. These markers align tightly with the positive keyword indicators in the Six Essential Elements framework (Religious/Metaphysical Foundations; Purpose and Goal; Curriculum; Pedagogy; Faculty; Parents & Community)  .

Highlights
- Mission centers on Christ and the cultivation of “wise, virtuous, and humble” students within a “classical, Christian” model, co‑laboring with families .
- Purpose emphasizes formation over careerism; “less about…a specific, temporal vocation” and “not…a traditional college preparatory institution…our goal is not to prepare students for college but for life”  .
- Curriculum explicitly names Trivium (Grammar, Logic, Rhetoric), Latin, logic, rhetoric, primary sources/Great Books, Socratic discussion, integrated humanities and sciences, and a capstone senior thesis/oral defense    .
- Pedagogy includes memorization, recitation, songs/chant in Grammar; debate/discussion and Socratic seminars in Upper School; rhetoric/senior thesis defense; “depth over breadth,” chapel, and “ruminate on texts” signal contemplative learning (scholé‑aligned)    .
- Religious/liturgical life is evident via All‑School chapel (for tours and daily rhythm) and the requirement that board/faculty/staff subscribe to the Statement of Faith   .
- Parent partnership is central (co‑teaching model; prospective‑parent formation events; chapel + campus tours; community events such as Recitatio)  .

The analysis below follows the “Six Essential Elements” keyword method (positive/negative indicators) and quantifies hits/coverage per category  .

## Aggregate Classification
- Overall: CLASSICAL
- Rationale: All six categories meet or exceed the positive indicators from the Six Essential Elements framework, with no conflicting negative signals. The site repeatedly affirms T‑G‑B, virtue/wisdom formation, the Trivium with Latin/logic/rhetoric, Socratic pedagogy and rhetoric theses, chapel/prayer, and parent partnership. The school’s ACCS accreditation further corroborates fidelity to classical Christian norms .
