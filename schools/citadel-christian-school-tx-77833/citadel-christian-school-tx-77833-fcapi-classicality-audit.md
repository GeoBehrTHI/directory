## Executive Summary

Citadel Christian School (Brenham, TX 77833) presents a robust Classical Christian program that aligns closely with the Six Essential Elements of a Classical School rubric. The school identifies itself publicly as a Classical Christian institution and embeds classical pedagogy (grammar, logic, rhetoric) alongside a biblically grounded framework. Notable public signals include explicit references to classical curriculum, weekly chapel, biblical authority as foundational to teaching, and a dual-path classical model (a traditional 5-day program and a University-Model® option). In public-facing materials, you can see the school describing itself as a Classical Christian school and detailing its mission to equip students with a biblical worldview, while also describing the three-part Trivium approach (grammar, logic, rhetoric) and corresponding grade-band pedagogy. These signals appear across multiple pages and documents within the school’s site content. Citadel also highlights formation of virtue, character, and spiritual aims as core outcomes, rather than purely vocational preparation.

Category-by-category, the school demonstrates strong Classical alignment:

- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: Clear emphasis on biblical authority, theology, chapel, and a biblical worldview integrated across subjects.
- School Purpose and Goal: Emphasis on formation, virtue, wisdom, and a teleological (classical) aim beyond mere information transfer.
- Curriculum: Trivium-based structuring (grammar/logical/rhetoric stages) and a cognitively coherent, classical curriculum; explicit public mention of grammar-stage memorization, logic-stage reasoning, and rhetoric-stage writing/speaking.
- Pedagogy: A mix of contemplative/restful elements (Scholé-like memory work) and Socratic/dialogical methods in upper grades; chanting/memory in early grades; rhetoric-focused opportunities in upper grades.
- Faculty: Emphasis on teachers as living exemplars aligned with a classical, biblical mission; explicit statements about staff and their role in forming character.
- Parents & Community: Strong partnership with families, University-Model® program described as parent-guided, and explicit invitation for parents to engage and participate.
