## Executive Summary

Holy Spirit Preparatory School (GA 30327) presents a robust Classical Catholic program anchored in the liberal arts, Great Books, and formation in virtue. Public materials portray a unified vision: education as formation of the whole person—intellect, will, and spiritual life—within a Catholic, classical framework. The school emphasizes the Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric), the seven liberal arts, integration of sacred Scripture and Tradition, liturgical life, campus ministry, and virtue formation through a deliberate Roadmap to Virtue. Parental partnership and a living faculty are highlighted as core practices. Overall, the school is CLASSICAL across categories, with Pedagogy showing strong classical elements but slightly less explicit public articulation of Socratic/seminar pedagogy in the public materials. The aggregate assessment is CLASSICAL.

Key insights
- Strong religious foundations: explicit Catholic identity, theology, liturgy, sacraments, and campus faith life are embedded in daily life and academics.
- Curriculum and pedagogy align with classical education: emphasis on Great Books, Trivium, liberal arts, history throughout the curriculum, and rhetoric education.
- Formation focus: explicit language around virtue, formation, and the Roadmap to Virtue, with prayer, Mass, and chapel integrated into school life.
- Family and community role: parents positioned as co-educators and partners with the school; open invitation for campus visits and engagement.
