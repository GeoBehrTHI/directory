## ABSTRACT

Heritage Classical Academy (Pepper Pike, OH, 44023) demonstrates a CLASSICAL orientation across all six rubric categories. The Religious and Metaphysical Foundations category is strongly anchored in Christ-centered theology, liturgical practice, and a Word-centered worldview. The School Purpose and Goal category emphasizes formation, virtue, wisdom, and the true/good/beautiful telos over mere information or job prep. The Curriculum category foregrounds the Seven Liberal Arts (Grammar, Logic, Rhetoric), Great Books, Great Conversation, and Latin within a history-rich, integrated program. Pedagogy highlights memory-work and recitation in the grammar stage, with Socratic/seminar-style and rhetoric-focused higher-level work, aligning with classical methods. Faculty materials portray teachers as the living curriculum and core to school culture, and Parents & Community materials show active partnership with families, two-track options for homeschool families, and robust parent-facing resources.

In short, Heritage Classical Academy presents a coherent Classical education profile with explicit signals of formation, tradition, and a robust classical curriculum and pedagogy. The school’s public materials consistently align with the core keywords and patterns of classical education as defined by the Six Essential Elements framework.

END OF ABSTRACT
