## Executive Summary

Heritage Classical Academy Oh 44023 (HCA) exhibits a robust Classical Christian orientation across all six classical categories. Public materials from the school consistently foreground Christ-centered, virtue-formation aims, a trivium-centered curriculum (grammar, logic, rhetoric), engagement with Great Books and the Great Conversation, and a pedagogy that blends contemplative forms with Socratic/seminar-style inquiry in upper grades.
