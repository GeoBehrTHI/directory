## ABSTRACT

Oaks Classical Academy CA 92009 demonstrates a foundational Christian classical orientation, with explicit public language describing a Christ-centered, classical liberal arts education and a goal of forming virtue and discernment in truth, goodness, and beauty. However, public-facing materials do not fully disclose the core classical curriculum components (e.g., grammar, logic, rhetoric; Great Books; Trivium/Quadrivium), nor explicit pedagogical practices (Socratic seminar, scholé, memory-based recall), nor comprehensive faculty and parent-community commitments as used in classical education rubrics. Accordingly, the school is NOT CLASSICAL overall, with pockets of CLASSICAL alignment (Religious Foundations; Purpose & Goal) and a clear but incomplete public depiction of Curriculum (SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL). To reach a FULL CLASSICAL status, Oaks Classical Academy would need to publicly articulate and demonstrate canonical curriculum structure, classical pedagogical methods, a living-curriculum faculty approach, and explicit parent-partnership practices.

Citations used in assessment:
- Christ-centered mission and truth/beauty framing. 
- Deep Hope rooted in Christ; commitment to truth. 
- Public description of a “classical liberal arts education.” 
- Classical-education keyword framework and categories (positive/negative indicators) for rubric references. 
- Public-facing resources describing parent partnership as normative in classical education (for context).
