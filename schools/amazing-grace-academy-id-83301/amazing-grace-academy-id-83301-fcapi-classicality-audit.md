# Executive Summary

Overall classification: CLASSICAL

Austin Classical School (https://austinclassical.org) presents exceptionally strong evidence across all six domains of the classical school framework—religious foundations; purpose and goal; curriculum; pedagogy; faculty; parents and community—consistently using the core classical lexicon (truth, goodness, beauty; wisdom; virtue; trivium; Latin; Socratic; great books; chapel; formation) and avoiding negative indicators (e.g., “values-neutral,” “college-and-career readiness” as the primary aim, project-based dominance). Highlights include: a mission that aims to “glorify Jesus Christ” while “cultivat[ing] wise, virtuous, and humble students” through a classical, Christian education; a clearly articulated trivium model with Latin, logic, and rhetoric; routine chapel; explicit use of Socratic discussion; and a collaborative model that positions parents as co-teachers.    

Methodology note: The keyword audit below compares the school’s public language to the positive/negative indicators in “Six Essential Elements of a Classical School” and the companion lexicon, and then quantifies positive vs. negative hits and coverage before assigning ratings.
