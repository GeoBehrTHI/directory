## Executive Summary

Trinity Christian School Montville, NJ (07045) presents a clearly classical Christian model. Public-facing materials consistently frame education as biblically-grounded, threefold in aim (spiritual, relational, intellectual), and anchored in the liberal-arts tradition (grammar, logic, rhetoric) with explicit attention to Great Books, Western thought, and classical pedagogy. The school describes its mission as Biblically-based, academically rigorous, and classical, and emphasizes partnership with Christian parents. Evidence includes explicit statements about Christ-centered foundations, profiles of graduates, and a curriculum that foregrounds Great Books, Latin, logic, and rhetoric, alongside chapel and spiritual formation. Across categories, the school's materials align with classical principles, with particularly strong signals in religious/metaphysical foundations, purpose and goals, curriculum, and pedagogy. The faculty and parent/community components likewise show classical-flavored expectations (mentoring faculty, biblical leadership, and a parental-partnership model). Overall, the school earns a confident CLASSICAL designation, with consistent indicators of traditional classical education throughout K–12.

Evidence: Vision/Mission statements and gospel-centered language; Profile of a Graduate; Statements of Faith.
- Purpose and Goal centers on virtue, wisdom, and truth as knowable realities; formation over information; focus on character rooted in Biblical truth. Evidence: Vision and Mission; Profile of a Graduate; biblically-based approach.
- Curriculum is explicitly liberal-arts oriented (grammar, logic, rhetoric) with continuous history study, Great Books/western thought, and language study (Latin). Evidence: Enhancements listing Great Books/Western Thought/Logic/Rhetoric; Elementary program details; Latin instruction.
- Pedagogy balances rest/contemplation with rigorous discussion; lower grades incorporate memory/chanting/memory work; upper grades emphasize Socratic inquiry and rhetoric. Evidence: Not Just the Mind, But the Whole Person; Socratic/seminar framing; memory/calm/rest in classroom approach.
- Faculty described as mentors, living curriculum, and partners with students; alignment with classical aims; clear emphasis on character formation and virtue. Evidence: Faculty/staff language in mission/values; “Profile of a Graduate” pillars; head-of-school messaging.
- Parents & Community are positioned as co-educators/partners; active invitation for parental involvement and learning about classical education; wide visibility of partnerships with Christian parents. Evidence: Partnership with Christian Parents; Vision/Mission; Inquiries and tours highlighted.
