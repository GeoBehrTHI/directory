## ABSTRACT

Christ Covenant School (Baton Rouge, LA, 70808) demonstrates strong alignment with classical education in a Christian framework, notably in its religious foundations, formation-focused mission, and parental partnership. The school presents a Christ-centered, liturgical, and memory-rich pedagogy with “gently classical” curricular framing, and explicit emphasis on truth, goodness, and beauty as real and knowable. Evidence from public materials shows weekly chapel, Scripture memory, catechesis, and a liturgical rhythm, alongside a curriculum described as liberal-arts oriented with great texts and classical ideals. Element 1 (Religious and Metaphysical Foundations) and Element 2 (School Purpose and Goal) clearly align with CLASSICAL standards. Element 3 (Curriculum), Element 4 (Pedagogy), and Element 5 (Faculty) show substantial classical signals but lack full, consistent, externally verifiable articulation of every classical criterion (e.g., explicit Trivium/Quadrivium, comprehensive Great Books pipelines, or explicit faculty portraits) in the publicly accessible site. Element 6 (Parents & Community) aligns strongly with classical norms, emphasizing parental partnership and community formation.

Overall, the school earns a SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL classification. The strongest anchors are its explicit Christian-theological foundations and formation-driven purposes, with clear evidence of liberal-arts framing and engagement with great texts. The most actionable needle-movers to progress toward CLASSICAL would be publicly detailing full classical pedagogy (Trivium/Quadrivium), explicit Great Books sequencing, Socratic or seminar-based pedagogy in upper grades, and a more explicit portrayal of the living, mentoring faculty within the classical frame.
