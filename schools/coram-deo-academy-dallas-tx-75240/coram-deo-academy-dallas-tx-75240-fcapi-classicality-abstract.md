## ABSTRACT

Coram Deo Academy Dallas (75240) demonstrates a consistently Classical profile across all six rubric categories. The Religious and Metaphysical Foundations are deeply Christian, with theology woven through academics, chapel practices, and biblical worldview integration. The School Purpose and Goal centers on virtue, wisdom, and formation rather than solely job preparation, aligning with Classical teleology. The Curriculum emphasizes the Liberal Arts via grammar/logic/rhetoric progression, Latin, and engagement with historical and literary Great-Works concepts. Pedagogy blends contemplative memory work with Socratic discussion and rhetoric, moving from memory-based learning in the Grammar stage to dialectic and rhetoric in the Rhetoric stage. The Faculty are portrayed as mentors and a living curriculum, and Parents & Community are actively partnered with the school through a collaborative learning model and open-access community. The public materials consistently map to the Positive Keyword indicators in the classical lexicon, with few or no Negative Keyword indicators detected.
