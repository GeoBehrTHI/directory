## Executive Summary

Coram Deo Academy (Dallas, TX 75240) presents a robust Classical Christian program with strong religious and metaphysical foundations, a clearly stated telos oriented toward virtue and wisdom, a trinidade curriculum (grammar–logic–rhetoric), and pedagogy that blends contemplative memory work with Socratic discourse in upper grades. Public materials emphasize Christ-centered, biblical worldview integration across subjects, a collaborative model that partners with families, and a Portrait of a Graduate focused on ethical servant leadership and wise thinking. Across six rubric categories, the school demonstrates pervasive classical commitments with very few signals of non-classical approaches.

Overall, the school is CLASSICAL.

- Evidence highlights include: Christ-centered learning with theology woven into academics and chapel-based realities (Religious and Metaphysical Foundations) ; a mission centered on virtue, wisdom, and shaping culture for God’s glory (Purpose and Goal) ; explicit Grammar/Logic/Rhetoric progression, Latin, and attention to historical study and great works (Curriculum) ; a pedagogy featuring memory work, chanting, Socratic discussion, and rhetoric in upper grades (Pedagogy) ; a faculty culture described as living curriculum and mentors (Faculty) ; and strong parent partnership with at-home learning and open campus/community engagement (Parents & Community) .
