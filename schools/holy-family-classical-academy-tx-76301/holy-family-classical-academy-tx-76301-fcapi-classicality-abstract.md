## ABSTRACT

Holy Family Classical Academy (HFCA) demonstrates a strong CLASSICAL orientation across Religious and Metaphysical Foundations, School Purpose and Goal, and Curriculum, with notable but not fully explicit alignment in Pedagogy and Parents & Community. The school’s Catholic identity—daily prayer, sacramental life, and a mottos-centered devotion to truth, goodness, and beauty—anchors its religious/classical vision. Its mission and curricular choices emphasize formation, virtue, wisdom, and the Great Books/Western Canon, supported by Latin, grammar-stage content, and canonical literature. The public materials show robust evidence of teacher quality and mentorship in line with the Classical model, and a vibrant family/community culture through testimonials and engagement opportunities.

- School Purpose and Goal: CLASSICAL

The school’s strengths strongly support CLASSICAL status, with two categories requiring targeted enhancements to reach full CLASSICAL status (Pedagogy and Parents & Community).
