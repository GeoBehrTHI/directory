## Executive Summary

Lumen Gentium Academy (LGA) presents a robust, historically rooted classical education within a distinctly Catholic framework. Public materials describe LGA as a classical liberal arts high school in the Catholic tradition, with explicit emphasis on formation of wisdom and virtue, a liberal arts curriculum, Great Books/Great Conversation, and a pedagogy that foregrounds dialogue, contemplation, memory, and liturgical life. The school’s own communications repeatedly tie education to the formation of character and the pursuit of truth, goodness, and beauty through a Catholic worldview, reinforced by liturgical and communal practices, strong faculty mentors, and active parental partnership. Taken together, these signals align strongly with classical education as defined in the Six Essential Elements framework. Overall classification: CLASSICAL.

Key indicators include: a named Liberal Arts approach, a Humanities Seminar centered on conversation with canonical authors (Shakespeare, Aristotle, Burke), explicit Catholic identity and liturgical elements (Mass, morning prayer, retreat culture), a teacher as living curriculum and a faculty of mentors, strong parental partnership and open-door engagement, and a mission statement framing life-long formation rather than mere information transfer. These facets are consistently referenced across multiple public-facing materials (homepage, academics pages, weekly updates, and graduate/post-graduate profiles).
