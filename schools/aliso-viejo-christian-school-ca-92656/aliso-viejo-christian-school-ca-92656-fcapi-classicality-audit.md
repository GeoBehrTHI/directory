## Executive Summary

Aliso Viejo Christian School CA 92656 (as represented in the school’s public materials, associated with the Pacifica OC network) demonstrates a robust alignment with classical education in its religious, formation-focused orientation and its emphasis on the liberal arts within a faith-centered framework. Public materials emphasize a Judeo‑Christian theological grounding, regular liturgical/communal practices (e.g., weekly chapel), and a formation-first approach that seeks to cultivate character, virtue, wisdom, and reverence for truth, goodness, and beauty. The school also foregrounds the liberal arts and the aim of education as formation in the likeness of a “Great Conversation” and a life of service. While there is strong classical alignment in categories of Religious Foundations, Purpose & Goals, Faculty, and Parents/Community, elements of Curriculum and Pedagogy show more partial alignment (i.e., SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL) due to explicit emphasis on liberal arts and formation alongside less explicit, book‑level commitments to Great Books, formalized Socratic method, or full Seven Liberal Arts articulation on the public-facing materials. The aggregate classification is CLASSICAL, with room for needle-moving improvements in explicit curriculum structure and pedagogy signals.
