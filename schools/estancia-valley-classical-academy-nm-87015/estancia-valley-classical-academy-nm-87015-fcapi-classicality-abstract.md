## ABSTRACT

Estancia Valley Classical Academy (EVCA) presents a CLASSICAL education profile in line with the classical education tradition. The school’s public materials emphasize formation of virtue and wisdom within a liberal-arts framework, with explicit pedagogy that includes Socratic dialogue, Scholé, memorization/recitation, and imitation—the classical sequence from memory and contemplation to rhetoric and debate. The curriculum is anchored in liberal-arts traditions, Great Books culture, and interdisciplinary integration, signaling strong alignment with the classical education mission. Foundationally, EVCA’s mission and founder statements articulate formation, virtue, and character as core teloi, while governance and family/community roots demonstrate sustained parental partnership. Some areas—such as explicit theological/the liturgical framing and a clearly codified “living curriculum” faculty portrait—could be strengthened in public materials to reach a fuller classical standing across all six categories.
