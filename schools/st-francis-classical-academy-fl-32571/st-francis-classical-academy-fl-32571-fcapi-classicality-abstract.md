## ABSTRACT

St Francis Classical Academy (Milton/Pace area, FL) demonstrates a robust CLASSICAL orientation across all six evaluation categories: Religious and Metaphysical Foundations, School Purpose and Goal, Curriculum, Pedagogy, Faculty, and Parents & Community. The public materials reveal a creedal Christian framework anchored in theology, liturgical-like communal practices, and baptismal/Nicene Creed commitments. The school emphasizes formation, virtue, and telos toward human flourishing, within a liberal-arts framework that features grammar, logic, rhetoric, and Great Books-style engagement, with a history- and culture-rich curriculum. Pedagogy blends contemplative practices (scholé, memorization, singing) with Socratic dialogue and rhetoric, particularly in upper forms. Faculty are presented as mentors within a living-curriculum model, with a strong tutor culture and small student-to-teacher ratios. Parent partnership is foregrounded, with open opportunities for involvement and field activities that welcome families.
