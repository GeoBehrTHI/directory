## ABSTRACT

Veritas Christian Academy of Houston (Veritas CA) is classified as CLASSICAL. The school’s public materials emphasize a Christ-centered, biblical-ly grounded classical education with a rigorous curriculum rooted in the liberal arts (grammar, logic, rhetoric; Latin; Great Books; primary sources) and a Great Conversation orientation. Pedagogy includes a mix of memory-based practices (recitation, chanting) in the Grammar Stage and Socratic seminars/rhetoric training in the Logic and Rhetoric Stages, consistent with classical pedagogy. The faculty identity centers on virtuous role models and a living curriculum, and the school actively partners with families as co-educators, inviting parental involvement and ongoing communication. The public materials also highlight a liturgical, communal dimension (biblical literacy, worship-language, etc.) that aligns with the religious-metaphysical foundations of classical education. This synthesis yields a consistently CLASSICAL ranking across all six categories: Religious and Metaphysical Foundations; School Purpose and Goal; Curriculum; Pedagogy; Faculty; and Parents & Community.

Key supporting signals from the school site include: mission statements framing a classical Christian education; explicit Trivium/Quadrivium framing; Latin and Great Books emphasis; memory-based and Socratic pedagogies; a “living curriculum” faculty emphasis; and a robust parent-partnership model. These are complemented by references to a shared mission with biblical literacy and a Christ-centered approach across primary materials. The Six Essential Elements document provides the rubric of positive/negative keywords used to evaluate these signals (e.g., truth, virtue, Great Books, liturgy, Socratic dialogue, formation, partnership with families; and signals of relativism or project-based approaches as negative). The combination of strong positive signals across all six rubric categories supports the CLASSICAL designation.
