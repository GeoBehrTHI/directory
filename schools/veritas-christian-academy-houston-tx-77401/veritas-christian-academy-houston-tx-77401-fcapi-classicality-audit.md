## Executive Summary

Veritas Christian Academy of Houston (Veritas CA) presents itself publicly as a PK–8 private school delivering a Christ-centered, classical education. Across its site, the school emphasizes a Trivium-based curriculum, Great Books engagement, Latin study, and a memory-centric pedagogy that includes singing, chanting, recitation, and Socratic-style inquiry in the upper grades. The public materials also foreground biblical literacy, virtue formation, and a community-oriented, parent-partnered model of education. Taken together with the broader classical-education rubric (positively framed by the Six Essential Elements and related lexicon), Veritas CA displays strong alignment with classical education in most categories, particularly in curriculum, pedagogy, religious/metaphysical framing, and family/community partnership. The aggregate judgment is CLASSICAL.

Key observations driving the assessment:
- Clear articulation of a classical curriculum (grammar–logic–rhetoric; Latin; Great Books; memorization through recitation and chant) and a coherent articulation of the Trivium, Quadrivium, and Great Conversation as core axes. Evidence appears in multiple site sections describing the curriculum and the Trivium framework (grammar-to-rhetoric progression) and in admissions materials that define the school’s classical aims.  
- Public materials explicitly position Veritas CA as Christ-centered and biblically grounded, with a stated mission to form wisdom and virtue for the glory of God, rather than mere information transfer. This is reinforced by core-values pages and mission statements. 
- Pedagogy features described on the site include lower-school chanting/memory practices, and upper-school Socratic seminar-based work, with rhetoric training and public speaking opportunities. This aligns well with classical pedagogical norms. 
- Faculty and community materials emphasize a mature, virtuous teaching model and a partnership with families as co-educators, which aligns with the classical-education lexicon’s expectations for a robust “living curriculum” and parent-formation orientation. 
- The school situates itself in a broader classical-education ecosystem (e.g., Hillsdale curriculum references in admissions/info materials), signaling engagement with established classical frameworks. 

Notes about sources:

See, for example, the Classical Curriculum framing and Great Books emphasis in the Information Packet excerpts and the Kindergarten/Grammar/Lexicon references on site.

Aggregate Assessment

- Overall classification: CLASSICAL
- Rationale: Across all six categories, Veritas CA demonstrates robust alignment with classical education principles as defined by the Six Essential Elements. It presents a truth-oriented religious/metaphysical grounding; a formation-focused purpose; a curriculum centered on the liberal arts, Great Books, Latin, and the Trivium; a pedagogy that blends memory work with Socratic inquiry and rhetoric; a faculty model anchored in virtue and mentorship; and a strong, ongoing partnership with families and the broader parent community. The public materials consistently show explicit classical markers and scarce, if any, signals that would indicate a move toward not-classical or only somewhat-classical approaches. This yields a CLASSICAL verdict overall.
