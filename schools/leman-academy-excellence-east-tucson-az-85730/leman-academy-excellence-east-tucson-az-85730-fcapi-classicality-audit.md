## Executive Summary

Leman Academy East Tucson presents as a tuition-free K–8 charter school that explicitly markets a classical approach to education and emphasizes character formation, virtue, and wisdom. Public-facing materials describe a “classical education” that aims to build a foundation of wisdom and virtue, with a focus on a close-knit community, personalized learning, and enrichment experiences in the arts and athletics. The site also highlights strong partnerships with families and an active, parent-inclusive culture. Taken together with the school’s explicit emphasis on virtue, character formation, and a rigorous classical framework, the school aligns most clearly with classical aims in several domains, while some elements (notably explicit mention of theology, liturgical practices, or the full traditional Great Books/grammar–logic–rhetoric curriculum) are less obviously documented on the public-facing pages.

Executive classification by category (based on the Classical School rubric and keyword indicators):

- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL
- Purpose and Goal: CLASSICAL
- Curriculum: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL
- Pedagogy: NOT CLASSICAL
- Faculty: CLASSICAL
- Parents & Community: CLASSICAL

Aggregate assessment: CLASSICAL

Direct references to theology, liturgical practice, and explicit Great Books/grammar–logic–rhetoric language are limited or not overt in the public pages we can access, which informs category-level judgments in several places.
