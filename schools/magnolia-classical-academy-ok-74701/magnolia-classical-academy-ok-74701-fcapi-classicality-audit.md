Where nuances exist (e.g., hybrid/University Model and some contemporary enrollment logistics), they still sit within a traditional classical formation model rather than diverging toward secular or purely information-based education.

- The ACCS description on the MCA Durant site frames classical Christian education as sharping reasoning, language, and rhetorical skills within a Christian vision of truth and knowledge, rooted in a long tradition. This reinforces Grounded in Theology and Transcendental Realism aims. 
- Statements such as “Genesis 1:27 … God created man in his own image” and emphasis on the Triune God occur in public content about Classical Christian Education, signaling doctrinal grounding.

Why this rating

The ACCS framing also emphasizes a classical formation over purely informational or vocational aims. Therefore: CLASSICAL.

Why this rating
