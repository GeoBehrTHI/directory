ABSTRACT
Saint Agnes School presents a comprehensive CLASSICAL profile: robust Trivium-based curriculum (grammar/logic/rhetoric) with Latin K-12, extensive Great Books/Western canon study, a liberal-arts integration, and a prominent Catholic formation program (Education in Virtue; Catechesis of the Good Shepherd) embedded within liturgical life. The school’s parent-community partnership is strong and explicit, aligning with classical-community models of formation. Across all six classical categories, the evidence supports CLASSICAL classification, with no substantive indicators of non-classical direction in the publicly available materials. The overall classification is CLASSICAL.
