## Executive Summary

Saint Agnes School (St. Paul, MN, ZIP 55103) demonstrates a robust classical-Catholic framework across most of the Six Essential Elements. The school foregrounds a liberal arts, Trivium-based curriculum (grammar/logic/rhetoric), sustained engagement with Great Books and the Western intellectual tradition, and explicit formation in faith, reason, and virtue. Pedagogy emphasizes Socratic dialogue in upper grades, memorization and chant in various forms, and a strong liturgical/catholic life integrated into daily learning. The faculty are presented as the living curriculum and stewards of a Catholic formation, and the school actively partners with families in formation and community life. There are explicit entries on Latin across K-12, a Catholic theological framework, and a catechetical life (including Catechesis of the Good Shepherd). These features point strongly toward CLASSICAL status, with few signs of the negative indicators listed in the Six Elements lexicon.

Key evidence drawn from the school’s public materials includes: grammar/logic/rhetoric divisions, a liberal arts integration that ties faith, reason, and virtue, and explicit commitment to the Western canon, Great Books, and Latin. The school also evidences Great Books/Great Conversation framing, and a liturgical, communal, and catechetical culture anchored in Catholic theology. Quantitatively, positive keyword indicators across categories substantially outnumber any negative signals; where present, negative signals are minimal (often absent) relative to the pro-classical signals.
