## ABSTRACT

Coram Deo Academy (CDA) demonstrates a robust CLASSICAL model across all six categories of the classical-education rubric. Religious and Metaphysical Foundations are clearly grounded in Christian theology and liturgical practice (Christ-centered, biblical formation, worship, and the “coram Deo” framing). The School Purpose and Goal centers on formation, virtue, wisdom, and the Portrait of the Graduate, underscoring truth, goodness, and beauty as real and knowable. The Curriculum foregrounds the liberal arts through the Trivium (grammar/logic/rhetoric), Latin, history studied chronologically, and Great Books within a Christian framework. Pedagogy combines contemplative elements (memory-based recitation; singing) with Socratic discussions, seminars, and rhetoric training in upper grades. Faculty are portrayed as model teachers and mentors, integral to a living curriculum and a core part of the school community. Parents and the broader community are positioned as partners in a covenantal relationship, with active opportunities for involvement, education, and co-teaching.
