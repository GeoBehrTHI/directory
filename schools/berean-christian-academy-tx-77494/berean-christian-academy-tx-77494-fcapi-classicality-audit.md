# Classicality Audit: Berean Christian Academy (Katy, TX)

School website used: https://bereanchristianacademy.org 

## Executive Summary
Overall classification: CLASSICAL

Why: Berean Christian Academy’s public materials repeatedly emphasize transcendental moral realism (“Truth, Goodness, and Beauty”), a Christ-centered theological foundation, and a curriculum ordered to the liberal arts (Trivium) and Great Books. Upper School pedagogy features seminar dialogue (Harkness), formal logic and debate, and a Senior Thesis—clear rhetoric-stage markers. Parent partnership is explicitly named. While we did not find explicit references to liturgical practices, restful learning (scholé), or lower-school chanting/memory work in the provided crawl, the core signals across mission, curriculum, and upper-school pedagogy strongly align with the Six Essential Elements of a Classical School  and the classicality lexicon .

Key evidence found in the crawl data:
- “Christ-centered worldview,” “recognize Truth, Goodness, and Beauty,” “for the glory of Christ” (mission) 
- “Independent, interdenominational, classical Christian school,” ACCS and SCL memberships 
- “Seven liberal arts,” “Grammar, Logic, and Rhetoric,” “Latin,” “great books,” “original ancient texts,” “theology,” “philosophy,” “Harkness Table conversation,” “formal logic and debate,” “Senior Thesis,” and “virtue development” 

Method note: We scored the school against the Six Essential Elements positive/negative keyword indicators (Religious/Metaphysical Foundations; Purpose and Goal; Curriculum; Pedagogy; Faculty; Parents & Community) , cross-checked with the classicality lexicon , and applied the category criteria provided.
