## ABSTRACT

Alexandria Classical Christian Academy (ACCA) VA 22302 demonstrates CLASSICAL alignment across all six categories of the classical school rubric. Evidence from ACCA’s public materials shows explicit Religious and Metaphysical Foundations (anchored in Christ, liturgical practices, Scripture-centered formation), a purpose geared toward formation, virtue, and wisdom (not merely job preparation), and a curriculum built around the liberal arts (grammar, logic, rhetoric; Quadrivium; Great Books; Latin; history as living narrative). Pedagogy integrates contemplative, memory-based grammar-stage activities with Socratic/seminar-driven upper grades and rhetoric training, while faculty are described as living curriculum and mentors within a relational culture. Parent and community engagement is foregrounded as a core element, with parents as co-teachers and open campus access.

Quantitatively, keyword indicators drawn from ACCA’s materials align with Positive Keyword signals across all six categories, while explicit Negative Keyword signals are minimal or absent. The keyword analysis, anchored to the Six Essential Elements lexicon (including terms like truth, beauty, virtue, liturgy, great books, the Trivium, Paideia, scholé, memory work, Socratic dialogue, formation, and parent partnership), indicates a strong CLASSICAL orientation in each category. ACCA’s public materials also reinforce a formation-first ethos (education as formation) and a robust classical curriculum and pedagogy, with explicit notes on liturgical practice, Great Books/primary sources, the Trivium/Quadrivium, and a relational faculty culture. Consequently, the overall evaluation for ACCA is CLASSICAL.
