## Executive Summary

Chapel Field Christian Schools (Chapel Field) presents a coherent classical Christian orientation across all six rubric categories. The school's public materials frequently foreground the transcendental aims of truth, goodness, and beauty; a Christ-centered, theology-infused framework; formation of character; and a curriculum and pedagogy steeped in classical tradition (great books, commonplaces, and historical breadth). The school explicitly states its mission to partner with parents, to cultivate virtue and wisdom, and to engage students in a culture of learning described in terms such as “Scholé” (restful study) and “great books.” These elements align strongly with the Positive Keyword indicators for Classical education and show little evidence of the Negative Keyword signals that would indicate secularization or a departure from classical aims.

- Core public statements signaling the religious/metaphysical foundations: Christ-centered culture; truth, goodness, beauty; the transcendentals; theology and sacred tradition.  
- Explicit articulation of forming virtue and wisdom rather than merely preparing for college/work. 
- Curriculum signals aligning with classical emphasis: Great Books/Great Books reading; grammar, elective focus on classical storytelling; commonplace books; direct statements about classical, Christian, Western tradition. 
- Pedagogy signals: emphasis on contemplation, “Scholé,” and a language of reading, discussing, and debating great ideas; in practice, a community-oriented, worshipful culture with a focus on formation. 
- Faculty as a living curriculum and strong leadership/administration presence; explicit parent partnership and parent education signals.  
- Active parent partnership with opportunities for engagement, tours, and open access to campus. 

Overall classification (aggregate): CLASSICAL

What would move the needle: Not applicable here, as all six categories align with Classical indicators. No major shifts are required to reach a fully Classical rating.

Abstract-level takeaway: Chapel Field presents a clearly Classical Christian program across religious foundations, purpose and goal, curriculum, pedagogy, faculty identity, and parent/community engagement, anchored in Christ-centered formation, virtue, and wisdom, with explicit Great Books-driven sensibilities and community formation.
