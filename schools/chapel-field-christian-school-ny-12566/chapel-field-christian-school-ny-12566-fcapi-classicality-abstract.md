## ABSTRACT

Chapel Field Christian Schools demonstrates a robust Classical Christian orientation across all six rubric categories. Religious and Metaphysical Foundations are anchored by Christ-centered culture, the transcendental triad of truth, goodness, and beauty, and theology-infused formation. The School Purpose and Goal foreground formation and virtue over vocational preparation, aligning with classical aims of human flourishing and wisdom. Curriculum clearly embraces classical elements, including Great Books/primary sources, grammar/logic/rhetoric, and a strong emphasis on reading and commonplaces. Pedagogy emphasizes contemplative, dialogic, and orderly learning (Scholé) with an explicit emphasis on great ideas and moral formation. Faculty identity centers on mentors and living curriculum, while Parents & Community collaboration underscores partnership with families as co-educators and active engagement. In sum, Chapel Field meets or exceeds the Positive Keywords of the classical framework across all six categories, with minimal evidence of Negative Keyword signals.

- Category 1 (Religious and Metaphysical Foundations): CLASSICAL
  - Evidence: Christ-centered culture; truth, goodness, beauty; sacred tradition; theology; Logos. Positive keyword hits dominate; no negative signals observed. 
- Category 2 (Purpose and Goal): CLASSICAL
  - Evidence: Formation and virtue; derivate from truth and wisdom; explicit formation over job prep. Positive keywords present; no negative signals observed. 
- Category 3 (Curriculum): CLASSICAL
  - Evidence: Great Books/primary sources; grammar/logic/rhetoric in emphasis; commonplace book; stories and Bible; Western tradition. Positive keywords present; minimal if any negative signals. 
- Category 4 (Pedagogy): CLASSICAL
  - Evidence: Scholé, contemplation, debate, great ideas; emphasis on reading and discussing, and a “living curriculum.” Positive keywords present; no negative signals. 
- Category 5 (Faculty): CLASSICAL
  - Evidence: Living curriculum, mentors, portrait of teacher, staff culture; leadership and faculty identity anchored in classical aims. Positive keywords present; no negative signals. 
- Category 6 (Parents & Community): CLASSICAL
  - Evidence: Partnership with families; parent formation; open-campus and family engagement. Positive keywords present; no negative signals.
