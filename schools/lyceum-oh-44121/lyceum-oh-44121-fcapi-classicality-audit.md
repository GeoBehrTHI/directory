## Executive Summary

Lyceum (South Euclid, OH, 44121) (The Lyceum – Classical Catholic Education in Cleveland, OH) presents a robust classical framework as evidenced by its publicly available materials. The school’s mission, curriculum, pedagogy, and community practices align strongly with transcendentally grounded, formation-focused classical education. Public materials emphasize a Catholic, theology-grounded approach to education, a liberal-arts core (grammar, logic, rhetoric; arithmetic, geometry, astronomy, music), engagement with Great Books, and Socratic teaching methods. The school also foregrounds liturgical life, communal worship, and a visible partnership with families. Taken together, these signals position Lyceum as CLASSICAL across the six traditional elements of classical education.
