# ABSTRACT

The Lyceum (South Euclid, OH, 44121) is classified as CLASSICAL across all six classical elements: Religious and Metaphysical Foundations, School Purpose and Goal, Curriculum, Pedagogy, Faculty, and Parents & Community. Public materials emphasize Catholic theology, truth-goodness-beauty as real ideals, a fixed liberal-arts curriculum (grammar, logic, rhetoric; arithmetic, geometry, music, astronomy), Great Books and classical languages, Socratic pedagogy, memory-based Scholé, and a living-faculty culture with strong parent involvement. The school’s public messaging aligns with transcendental moral realism, grounded theology, liturgical/community life, formation over information, and a culture of mentorship and partnership with families. The aggregate signals place Lyceum as fully CLASSICAL.

- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: Catholic teaching and theology are central; truth-goodness-beauty are professed as real goods; liturgical/community impulses are present via Catholic life and related activities. Evidence supports a CLASSICAL rating in this dimension.
- School Purpose and Goal: Formation and liberal education oriented toward virtue rather than purely job preparation; pursuit of truth-great-good-beauty is foregrounded. CLASSICAL signal strong.
- Curriculum: Seven Liberal Arts, Great Books, classical languages, and a historically grounded curriculum are explicit; integration of faith and reason is central. CLASSICAL signal strong.
- Pedagogy: Socratic method, seminars, and rhetoric training; lower-school chants/memory-based learning (Scholé) are described; upper-school rhetoric and public speaking featured. CLASSICAL signal strong.
- Faculty: Faculty described as living curriculum, mentors, and exemplars; portrait of teacher expectations; collaborative culture. CLASSICAL signal strong.
- Parents & Community: Strong partnership with families; parental involvement emphasized; open campus and community life. CLASSICAL signal strong.
