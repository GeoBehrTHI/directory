## Executive Summary

Archway Classical Academy Arete Az (Arete) presents a robust classical-education orientation anchored in Great Hearts/Great Books-style liberal arts, with explicit emphasis on forming “hearts and minds in virtue and truth” and cultivating the habits of character that accompany a classical curriculum. Public-facing materials repeatedly foreground classical content and the aims of developing virtue, wisdom, and a love of truth, beauty, and goodness. That said, explicit religious-theological framing and liturgical/community practices are less overt in the visible public materials, which yields a rating that leans CLASSICAL overall, with Element 1 showing some room for stronger explicit religious/metaphysical articulation.

Key drivers of the CLASSICAL assessment
- Content centers on a classical liberal arts approach, with a stated aim to cultivate virtue, wisdom, and the triad of Truth, Beauty, and Goodness (positively signaling transcendental aims) as core to formation, not merely information transfer. This is evidenced in Arete’s public-facing curriculum language and Great Hearts framing. 
- The materials emphasize formation and character (“habits of the heart,” virtue, leadership, human flourishing) and highlight Great Books/the liberal-arts tradition as core, aligning strongly with classical education norms. 
- Pedagogical language and historical framing emphasize an intentional, traditional approach (ancient/paideia-like lineage, memory-based practices, emphasis on classics and rhetoric) consistent with classical pedagogy concepts. 
- Community and family involvement are clearly valued (volunteer culture, parent engagement, open campus, family events), which aligns with the classical-school model’s emphasis on parental partnership and the living curriculum.
