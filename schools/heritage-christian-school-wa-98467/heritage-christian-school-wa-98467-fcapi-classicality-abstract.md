## ABSTRACT

Heritage Christian School (WA 98467) demonstrates a strong CLASSICAL orientation across most dimensions, anchored by a Christ-centered mission, biblical formation, and community partnerships. The Religious and Metaphysical Foundations are clearly Classical due to explicit theology, catechetical structures, daily Bible instruction, and chapel life, underpinned by Westminster Confession and catechisms. The School Purpose and Goal strongly emphasize formation, virtue, and wisdom, aligning with classical aims beyond mere job preparation. The Faculty and Parents/Community categories show robust Classical signals: teachers as mentors and “living curriculum,” and a formation-focused, open, parent-partner model.

In Curriculum and Pedagogy, Heritage exhibits substantial classical elements—classical literature in curricula, historical continuity, and formation-focused pedagogy—but the explicit, widespread articulation of a full Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric) and Socratic/Paideia-style pedagogy is not consistently visible in public-facing pages. This yields SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL in those two categories, though the overall rubric leans CLASSICAL given the weight of the other elements.

Aggregate classification: CLASSICAL.

Key sources from Heritage materials include:

- Christ-centered mission and daily Bible instruction with chapel life (Mission and Purpose; Middle School content).
- Doctrinal standards (Westminster Confession and Catechisms) and catechetical practice (First/Shorter Catechisms).
- Classical-literature framing and history emphasis within the curriculum.
- Parent-Partner formation and a clearly stated Parent Code, with open-campus access.

Note on rubric and keyword framework

- The analysis uses the Positive/Negative Keyword Indicators from the Six Essential Elements of a Classical School (1) as the scoring framework, with explicit mentions of terms like truth, virtue, theology, liturgy, catechesis, Great Books, history, grammar, logic, rhetoric, Socratic, scholé, wonder, chant, and related items as indicators. The lexicon of Positive/Negative Keywords from the document (Six Essentials) is applied to the Heritage public materials to generate category scores and the aggregate assessment.
