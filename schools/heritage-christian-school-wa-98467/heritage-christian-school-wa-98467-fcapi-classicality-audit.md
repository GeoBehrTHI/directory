## Executive Summary

Heritage Christian School in University Place, WA (98467) presents a robust Christ-centered program that strongly emphasizes formation, virtue, and character within a Christian framework. Public materials show explicit integration of Biblical instruction, daily spiritual life, and catechetical structures (Westminster catechisms and confessional standards), alongside a curriculum that places classical literature, history, and a rigorous academic ethos at the core. While the school clearly aligns with many elements of classical education (notably in religious/metaphysical foundations, purpose/goal, and community/faculty commitments), certain pedagogy and curriculum signals (e.g., explicit Socratic seminars, grammar/logic/rhetoric emphases, and some elements of Great Books framing) are less overt in the public materials. Overall, the school is CLASSICAL, with some notable strengths and a few areas where classical markers are less explicit.

- Strong CLASSICAL signals across Religious & Metaphysical Foundations, School Purpose & Goal, Faculty, and Parents & Community, anchored by a Christ-centered mission, daily Bible instruction, chapel, and catechetical standards.   
- Moderate to clear CLASSICAL signals in Curriculum (history continuity; presence of “classical literature” in the middle grades; repetition of character formation themes), with some gaps in explicit Trivium emphasis (grammar, logic, rhetoric) not always stated outright in public materials. 
- Pedagogy shows traditional strengths (memory, devotional rhythm, community formation) but fewer explicit indications of Socratic seminars or heavy project-based/experiential learning signals in the public content. This yields a SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL reading for Pedagogy, even as other areas are clearly classical. 
- Overall, Heritage is CLASSICAL, with some room to deepen explicit classical pedagogy markers (Socratic inquiry, Great Books framing, Trivium emphasis) in public literature.
