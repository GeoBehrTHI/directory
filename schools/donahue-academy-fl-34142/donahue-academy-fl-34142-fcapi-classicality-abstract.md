## ABSTRACT

Donahue Academy of Ave Maria demonstrates a consistent, strong CLASSICAL alignment across all six classical-education categories. The school is explicitly Catholic and classical, with a formation-first mission that centers on encountering Christ and pursuing truth, goodness, and beauty. The curriculum follows the Trivium progression (grammar, logic, rhetoric) with a clear emphasis on liberal education, original sources, and a Great-Books-like interpretive axis, while pedagogy integrates contemplative and Socratic methods alongside rhetoric training. Faculty are portrayed as living, virtuous mentors forming the whole person, and parents/communities are positioned as partners in formation, with ample opportunities for family involvement and education. The aggregate assessment places Donahue Academy in the CLASSICAL category.
