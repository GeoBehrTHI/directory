## ABSTRACT

St. Mary’s Catholic School Taylor TX 76574 demonstrates a strong CLASSICAL orientation across most dimensions. The school grounds its education in a Catholic worldview, liturgical life, and a “truth, goodness, beauty” formation that aligns with classical aims. The curriculum centers on the Trivium (Grammar, Logic, Rhetoric), CGS, and Spirit of Truth, with upper-level pedagogy featuring Socratic discussion and rhetoric training. History is integrated across disciplines, and Great Books-like inquiry is evident in the program through philosophy, logic, and classical authors. The faculty is grounded in Catholic tradition (founded by Dominican Sisters; credentialed faculty), and the school fosters parent partnership and community involvement through a house system and open visitor policies.
