## ABSTRACT

Kingdom Collegiate Academy demonstrates a strong religious-metaphysical foundation and a clear classical framing in its public materials, anchored by a biblical/Kingdom worldview, weekly worship-like practices (chapels), and a mission centered on formation and virtue within a Christian context. The school explicitly describes its curriculum as classical and integrates faith deeply into instruction, while also highlighting modern, 21st-century approaches and career preparation. Across the six classical criteria, Element 1 (Religious and Metaphysical Foundations) and Element 5 (Faculty) score CLASSICAL, Element 3 (Curriculum) and Element 4 (Pedagogy) are SMALL-TO-MODERATEly CLASSICAL, and Categories 2 (Purpose & Goal), 6 (Parents & Community), and Element 4 (Pedagogy) fall into SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL, reflecting a robust classical flavor with some gaps relative to the full Great-Books-based, liberal-arts-centric classical model. The aggregate classification is SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL.

Overall, Kingdom Collegiate Academy presents a compelling classical-leaning program that is deeply integrated with a Christian worldview and strong faculty and community connections. To move firmly into CLASSICAL across all six categories, the school should publicly publish explicit Great Books/Great Conversation elements, a formal liberal-arts sequence (grammar-logic-rhetoric) across grades, more explicit Socratic pedagogy, and stronger, documented parent-as-co-teacher initiatives.
