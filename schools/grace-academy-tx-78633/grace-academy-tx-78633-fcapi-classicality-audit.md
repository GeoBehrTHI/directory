## Executive Summary

Grace Academy TX 78633 presents a coherent, strongly classical, and overtly Christian approach to education. Public-facing materials emphasize a Christ-centered, biblically grounded formation built around the Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric), a campus-wide sense of liturgical-life through chapel and Scripture memory, and a deliberate partnership with parents as co-educators. Evidence across the school’s site shows explicit commitments to formation over mere information, the liberal-arts trajectory (grammar/logic/rhetoric) across K–12, a clearly articulated Portrait of a Graduate oriented to love of God, truth, goodness, and beauty, and a living-faculty model that is deeply intertwined with students’ formation. The six classical criteria categories—Religious and Metaphysical Foundations, Purpose and Goal, Curriculum, Pedagogy, Faculty, and Parents & Community—are all addressed in ways that align Grace Academy with CLASSICAL to CLASSICAL-leaning (i.e., Classical). While the school does not foreground Great Books or explicit “Great Conversation” language on every page, the explicit Trivium framework, formation-centric mission, liturgical life, and robust faculty-mentoring model strongly support a CLASSICAL ranking.
