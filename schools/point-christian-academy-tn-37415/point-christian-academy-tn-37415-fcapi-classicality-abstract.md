## ABSTRACT

Point Christian Academy (PCA) demonstrates a strong CLASSICAL orientation in religious foundations, purpose/goal, faculty, and parent/community engagement, with Curriculum and Pedagogy showing substantial classical elements but not yet fully meeting every Great Books/Great Conversation criterion across all grade levels.
