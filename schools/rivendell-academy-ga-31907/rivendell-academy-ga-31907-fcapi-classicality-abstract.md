## ABSTRACT

Rivendell Academy presents a clearly Christian, classical-leaning formation program anchored in truth, goodness, and beauty, with explicit theology and a formation-focused mission. The public materials show strong alignment with Religious and Metaphysical Foundations and School Purpose/Goal, plus visible Faculty and Parent/Community structures. However, curriculum and pedagogy signals are not yet fully classical as defined by canonical elements (notably explicit Great Books involvement, the Trivium/Quadrivium, and documented Socratic pedagogy). Overall, Rivendell Academy lands in SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL, with a clear trajectory toward CLASSICAL through targeted curricular and pedagogical enhancements, particularly around Great Books, dialectical methods, and a more explicit integration of classical memory/recitation and liturgical practices. The school’s Christian formation, liberal-arts emphasis, and family-centered community are its strongest features, and with the needle-moving changes outlined above, Rivendell Academy could advance to a full CLASSICAL designation.

- Key evidence includes statements of pursuing truth through Classical Christian Education and a mission centered on true/good/beautiful discernment, along with Bible-centered curriculum and a 3-core-days instructional structure. These signals support CLASSICAL in Categories 1 and 2, and to a strong extent in Category 5. Public references to parent involvement (Parent Portal) and faculty structure support Category 6 and Category 5. Public documentation of Great Books, rigorous Trivium/Quadrivium sequencing, and explicit Socratic pedagogy are less evident, yielding SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL for Categories 3 and 4. Citations drawn from Rivendell Academy public materials: mission/Three Core Days (throughout), explicit theology/discernment language, and the site navigation showing a Faculty and Parent Portal presence.
