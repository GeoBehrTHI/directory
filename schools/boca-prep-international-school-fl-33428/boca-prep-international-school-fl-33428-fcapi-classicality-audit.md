# Austin Classical School — Classicality Audit

Website audited: https://austinclassical.org

## Executive Summary
Overall classification: CLASSICAL

Austin Classical School (ACS) presents a robust, coherent expression of classical Christian education across mission, curriculum, and pedagogy. The school explicitly states a telos of cultivating wisdom and virtue under Christ, foregrounds the transcendentals (truth, goodness, beauty), integrates theology across the curriculum, and employs the trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric) with Socratic discussion, memory work, Latin, and a culminating senior thesis and defense. Regular communal worship (All‑School chapel) evidences a liturgical life. The collaborative model explicitly partners with parents as co‑teachers in the grammar years. In every audit category, positive indicators from the Six Essential Elements lexicon and framework predominate, with no meaningful negative indicators observed (e.g., “21st‑century skills,” “project‑based learning” dominance, “values‑neutral” framing)      .

Below is the category-by-category keyword analysis and scoring, using the Positive/Negative Keyword Indicators from the “Six Essential Elements of a Classical School” framework and lexicon  .
