## Executive Summary

Incarnation Catholic School (Sarasota, FL 34239) presents a robust classical Catholic education aligned with the Six Essential Elements framework. Across categories, the school emphasizes Catholic identity, virtue formation, a liberal-arts oriented curriculum, and pedagogy that blends contemplative practice with Socratic discussion and rhetorical training. Public-facing materials portray a formation-focused approach (rather than mere information transfer), a history-rich and Great-Books–adjacent curriculum, and an active partnership with parents and parish life. The evidence supports a strong Classical orientation in all six categories, with no clear signals of non-classical influences in the materials provided. Consequently, the aggregate assessment is CLASSICAL.

Key insights:
- Religious and metaphysical foundations are explicit through Catholic identity, liturgical emphasis, catechesis, and Eucharistic framing.
- The school’s purpose centers on formation, virtue, wisdom, and the pursuit of truth, goodness, and beauty, not merely job-readiness.
- Curriculum highlights include a Catholic liberal-arts framework with historically centered pedagogy and a calendar of historical “years” (Greek, Roman, Medieval, etc.), implying a Great-Books–style trajectory.
- Pedagogy combines contemplative elements with Socratic discussion, memory work, and rhetoric, signaling a structured classical pedagogy with opportunities for public speaking and debate.
- Faculty framing as mentors and living curriculum, with an emphasis on virtuous exemplars and partnership between faculty and parish life.
- Strong parental and community engagement, open visits, and a formal role for families as co-educators and partners.
