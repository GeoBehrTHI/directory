## Executive Summary

California Republic Leadership Academy Capistrano (CRLA Capistrano) presents a clear classical-leaning profile in public materials. The school positions itself as a tuition-free, public charter grounded in classical liberal arts, with explicit emphasis on virtue, leadership, and a content-rich curriculum. Public materials stress formation of character and leadership through a virtue-based education, pursuit of truth, and love of the true, good, and beautiful. At the same time, explicit theological or liturgical foundations are not foregrounded, suggesting a nonsectarian classical framing rather than a religious-metaphysical program. On curriculum, pedagogy, and the teacher’s role, CRLA Capistrano demonstrates strong classical markers (grammar-logic-rhetoric, Great Books/Great Conversation, Socratic method, rhetoric).カテゴリー-wise, the school evidences many classical elements, though with some gaps in explicit religious/metaphysical language and in documented parent-partnership practices within public-facing materials.

- Overall classification (aggregate): SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL

- Why this aggregate rating: The school robustly satisfies core classical criteria in Curriculum and Pedagogy, and it aligns with the School Purpose/Goal. It shows meaningful evidence of Virtue-based formation and leadership-focused aims (Element 2) and strong classical Curricular structures (Element 3) and Pedagogy (Element 4). However, explicit Religious/Metaphysical Foundations and sustained, public-facing Parent/Community partnerships (Element 1 and Element 6) are less clearly documented in the materials provided. Faculty presence is evident (Element 5) but the public materials do not consistently present the “living curriculum” or the same depth of faculty-character articulation found in some classical schools, which tempers a full Classical ranking. See category-by-category notes for details.

- What would move the needle (for SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL schools): 
  - Strengthen explicit articulation of religious/metaphysical foundations or liturgical/community practices in public-facing materials where appropriate (without compromising non-sectarian status if that is maintained).
  - Expand public-facing language around parent partnership and co-education (co-teaching roles, home-school collaboration, family formation activities) to align with the “Partnership with Families” and “Formation of Affections” cues in classical lexicons.
  - Highlight more explicit statements about the faculty as living curriculum and the portrait of teacher ideals to reinforce Element 5 as CLASSICAL.
