The Religion/Metaphysical Foundations category shows a strong orientation to transcendentals (truth, goodness, beauty) and virtue but lacks explicit theology or liturgical practices in public materials, placing Element 1 as SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL. The Faculty category appears less explicit in articulating the faculty as the living curriculum, enabling a SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL rating there. The aggregated assessment across the six categories is CLASSICAL, with identified needle-movers (if pursued) being explicit theological grounding and a clearer public articulation of faculty as the living curriculum to move Element 1 and 5 from SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL to CLASSICAL.
