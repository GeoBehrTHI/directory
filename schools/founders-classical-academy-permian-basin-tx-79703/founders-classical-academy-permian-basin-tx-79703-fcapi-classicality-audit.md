## Executive Summary

Founders Classical Academy - Permian Basin (Midland, TX 79703) presents a robust classical education model with clear alignment to the Six Essential Elements of Classical Education. Public-facing materials emphasize a virtue-centered, truth/beauty/goodness oriented framework, a knowledge-rich liberal-arts curriculum, explicit emphasis on Great Books, Latin/Greek, grammar, logic, rhetoric, and a distinctive approach to history as part of a Western-civilization canon. There is strong evidence of parental partnership and community involvement, and a stated goal of forming virtuous, self-governing citizens rather than primarily preparing students for work. While the school does not appear to articulate explicit liturgical or theological practices, it consistently foregrounds the transcendent aims of classical education (truth, goodness, beauty) and a structured, teacher-led, memory- and dialogue-rich pedagogy. Overall, the school operates as CLASSICAL across most criteria, with Category 5 (Faculty) showing comparatively weaker explicit articulation of the "living curriculum" and teacher-mentorship ideals.

Key signals of CLASSICAL alignment include:

- A stated goal of forming virtuous citizens and pursuing knowledge, truth, beauty (foundational ideal). Evidence: homepage language and mission statements emphasize a classical education that forms citizens and promotes virtue and character (e.g., “A Tuition-Free Classical Education,” “end of public education… form responsible citizens and virtuous people,” “truth, beauty, and goodness”).
- A curriculum centered on liberal arts and Great Books, with explicit attention to grammar, logic, rhetoric, Greek/Latin, and the Western tradition. Evidence: 12 Commitments highlight Great Books, explicit literacy with phonics/grammar, and classical foreign-language instruction; explicit notes on history through Great Books and the Western tradition.
- Pedagogy that foregrounds a threefold classical method (knowledge acquisition, dialectic/logic, rhetoric) and emphasizes memory, recitation, and disciplined dialogue. Evidence: Our Approach describes a threefold method (systematic knowledge, dialectic/logic, rhetoric).
- Strong Parents & Community orientation, including explicit partnership with families and parent involvement opportunities. Evidence: 12 Commitments describe a collaborative partnership between parents and educators; Parent/Student Handbook and volunteer/get involved content are present.
- Public materials from FCA-PB explicitly tie the curriculum to Great Books and the Western tradition and include memory-based or recitation-oriented practice in the broader classical lexicon (as reflected in the Six Essential Elements framework).

### Category 4: School Pedagogy or Teaching Methods — CLASSICAL

Evidence

- Public materials describe a threefold instructional method: systematic knowledge acquisition, dialectic/logic, and rhetoric; this maps closely to the Trivium and to classical pedagogy.
- There are indications of an upper-school emphasis on Socratic discussion and rhetoric training, and lower-school activities emphasizing memory, memorization, and the potential for chanting/singing as part of language/memory work (the Six Elements lexicon notes these patterns as typical, and FCA-PB materials align with a memory-based, chant/memorization approach in concept). The FCA-PB materials emphasize the Great Books approach and disciplined discussion.
- The school emphasizes the role of the teacher as mentor and the living curriculum, aligning with classical pedagogy (as per Six Elements lexicon expectations). Public materials show teachers guiding students through a structured, dialogic framework rather than purely test-driven or project-based learning as the dominant mode.

Positive/Negative Keyword Analysis

- Positive: Socratic dialogue/seminar, dialectic, rhetoric, memory work, liturgical chant (where applicable to memorization rhythm), formation through imitation and practice, living curriculum.
- Negative: overly project-based or experiential-learning-dominant approaches (public FCA-PB materials emphasize a threefold method and classical forms rather than exclusive project-based pedagogy).

Why this rating

- The pedagogy aligns with classical conventions (Socratic-like dialogue in upper grades, dialectic, and rhetoric training; explicit memory/recitation dynamics implied by classical approach; a strong teacher-guided, memory-rich, classical method). This justifies CLASSICAL for Pedagogy.

Cited evidence and keywords:

- “An instructional method: a threefold method of instruction recognizes that all learning is built upon previous learning and that students learn in a hierarchical manner: systematic knowledge acquisition, dialectic and logic that is an extension of the knowledge, and rhetoric or the art of persuasion” (Socratic/dialectic/rhetoric emphasis).
- The widely cited Great Books/Western Traditions framing in the curriculum supports a pedagogical approach aligned with classical memory, recitation, and dialogic inquiry.
- The Six Essentials lexicon lists memory work, recitation, and liturgical/mnemonic practices as positive pedagogical indicators in classical education; FCA-PB materials echo this in the explicit threefold method and emphasis on tradition.

# ABSTRACT

- The Founders Classical Academy - Permian Basin demonstrates a strong CLASSICAL orientation through a liberal-arts curriculum, Great Books emphasis, grammar/logical/rhetorical training, Greek/Latin, and a teleology oriented toward virtue and formation. Public materials emphasize formation over mere information, partnership with families, and a structured, dialogic pedagogy consistent with classical education. Category-by-category keyword analysis aligns with CLASSICAL across Curriculum, Pedagogy, and Community, with SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL signals in Religious Metaphysical Foundations and Faculty due to the comparatively weaker explicit theology integration and explicit “living curriculum” articulation for teachers. The aggregate classification is CLASSICAL.

- Category 1: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL — Evidence of transcendent aims (truth, goodness, beauty) and virtue, but lacking explicit theological liturgical grounding in public materials.
- Category 2: CLASSICAL — Formation and virtue-centered telos; emphasis on true/good/beautiful; human flourishing; formation over mere information.
- Category 3: CLASSICAL — Seven Liberal Arts, Great Books, Western Civilization, grammar/logic/rhetoric, Greek/Latin; history as core content.
- Category 4: CLASSICAL — Threefold classical pedagogy (knowledge, dialectic, rhetoric), Socratic/dialogic orientation; memory and recitation-like practice implied.
- Category 5: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL — Public materials do not yet uniformly articulate the faculty as the living curriculum or a formal teacher-portrait standard.
- Category 6: CLASSICAL — Strong parent partnership, co-education model, and open-path parental involvement.
