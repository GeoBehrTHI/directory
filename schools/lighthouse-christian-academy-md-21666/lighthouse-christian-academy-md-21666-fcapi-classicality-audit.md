## Executive Summary

Lighthouse Christian Academy (MD 21666) presents a robust Classical Christian profile. Public materials consistently foreground classical methods, liberal-arts orientation, and integration of Scripture, with explicit emphasis on formation of character, virtue, wisdom, and a Biblical worldview. The school highlights aims such as truth, goodness, and beauty;Great Conversation; Latin, logic, rhetoric, and Great Books; and a pedagogy that blends contemplative memory work (e.g., singing, memorization) with Socratic inquiry and debate in the upper grades. The community emphasizes partnership with parents, a family-centered culture, and a campus anchored in Christ and local sponsoring churches. Overall, the school demonstrates CLASSICAL alignment across most categories, with one category showing only partial/Somewhat Classical alignment due to explicit articulation gaps around certain faculty-formation language. See category-by-category synthesis below for details and evidence.
