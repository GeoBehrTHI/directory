## Executive Summary

Riverstone Classical Academy (Bradenton, FL 34209) presents a compact, Christ-centered, classically oriented K–8 program delivered through a hybrid private homeschooling model hosted by Gulf Coast Church. The school explicitly anchors its mission in classical education—grammar, logic, and rhetoric—while emphasizing virtue, character formation, and a Christian worldview. Public materials emphasize truth, goodness, and beauty as transcendental goods, the living curriculum role of faculty, and a deep partnership with parents as co-teachers. Great Books, the Great Conversation, Latin, and history-centric integration appear as core curricular anchors. The school’s public statements consistently describe a formation-oriented aim (virtue, wisdom, human flourishing) rather than a sole workforce-preparation aim, and they foreground parent involvement, communal worship, and a shared moral framework.
