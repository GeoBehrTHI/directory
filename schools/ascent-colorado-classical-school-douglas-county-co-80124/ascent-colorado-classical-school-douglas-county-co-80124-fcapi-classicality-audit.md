## Executive Summary

Ascent Classical Academy of Douglas County (Ascent Colorado Classical School) presents a robust, text-rich classical education model across all six rubric categories. Public materials emphasize truth, goodness, beauty; formation of character; a liberal-arts curriculum built on Great Books and Western civilization; teacher-led pedagogy that leverages classical methods (grammar, logic, rhetoric; Socratic inquiry at higher levels); a faculty conceived as a living curriculum; and a strong, collaborative partnership with parents and the broader school community. The aggregate assessment is CLASSICAL, with consistent alignment across categories to classical aims. Key evidence includes explicit articulation of classical hallmarks on the school’s site (hallmarks such as truth, beauty, virtue, great books, and wonder), explicit policy statements about curricular priorities (Core Knowledge sequences, Western civilization emphasis, Latin, and a content-rich liberal arts program), and practices around parent partnership and faculty culture.
