## ABSTRACT

New Covenant Christian Academy (NCCA) demonstrates a robust CLASSICAL orientation across all six classical-education categories. The school's public materials foreground Christ-centered, biblical-worldview formation, a liberal-arts curriculum, the Trivium (grammar/logic/rhetoric), and Socratic-influenced pedagogy at the upper levels, complemented by memory work, chanting, and liturgical worship. The school emphasizes formation, virtue, and human flourishing within a framework of biblical truth and beauty, with active partnership with families and a community-centric ethos. The presence of weekly chapel services, a Trivium progression, and explicit references to classical canon elements (great conversation, liberal arts) align with the Positive Keywords in the Six Essential Elements lexicon and related classicality resources. Faculty, governance, and parent-community engagement are described in ways that emphasize mentorship, character formation, and shared mission. Collectively, these signals place NCCA in the CLASSICAL category across all six categories, with a cohesive, church-informed classical Christian pedagogy and curriculum.
