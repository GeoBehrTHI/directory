Classification: CLASSICAL

Summary of primary findings:
- Relational Foundations (Religious and Metaphysical Foundations): CLASSICAL. Public materials foreground truth, goodness, beauty, and Western tradition, suggesting transcendent aims in character formation, though explicit theology or liturgical practice is not foregrounded. 
- Purpose and Goal: CLASSICAL. The school emphasizes formation, virtue, human flourishing, and telos, with a classical mission and Great Books emphasis. 
- Curriculum: CLASSICAL. Strong Great Books, liberal-arts framing, and Western civilization/history content. 
- Pedagogy: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL. Public materials do not consistently enumerate explicit classical pedagogical approaches (Socratic dialogue, Scholé, etc.), though canonical classical signals exist in the broader lexicon. 
- Faculty: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL. Formation-focused language around teachers exists, but explicit “living curriculum”/faculty portraits are not clearly articulated in public materials. 
- Parents & Community: CLASSICAL. Clear engagement with families through tours, information nights, and community events, aligning with partnership and formation themes. 

Overall aggregate: CLASSICAL.

This evaluation synthesizes public materials from American Legacy Academy with the classical-education keyword framework (truth, beauty, virtue, Great Books, liberal arts, Western civilization, formation, Great Conversation, and parental partnership) to determine alignment with classical education criteria. The school demonstrates strong classical alignment in curricular content, purpose, and community engagement, while signaling for Pedagogy and Faculty could be strengthened through explicit articulation and public-facing statements of classical teaching methods and teacher formation.
