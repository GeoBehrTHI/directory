## Executive Summary

American Legacy Academy (ALA), Severance, CO, presents a robust classical orientation in its public-facing materials. The school emphasizes a back-to-basics, liberal-arts–driven education with explicit commitments to truth, goodness, beauty, and Great Books, alongside a clear focus on civic virtue and moral character. Public materials repeatedly describe a Classical/Western-Civilization framed curriculum that centers on the liberal arts (grammar, logic, rhetoric) and a traditional literacy sequence, with attention to history, literature, philosophy, and the fine arts. Evidence from the school’s mission, vision, and curriculum language shows alignment with core classical aims (truth, virtue, Great Books, Western heritage, formation of character). The most substantial evidence resides in statements about Great Books, liberal arts, Western civilization, and civic virtue. The school’s materials also emphasize parent involvement and community engagement, with open tours and parent information nights, which aligns with the classical emphasis on partnerships with families.

Overall assessment: CLASSICAL.

Key supporting evidence drawn from public materials:
- Explicit articulation of a return to classical education with emphasis on truth, goodness, beauty, and Great Books; Western tradition framing; and civic virtue in mission statements.  
- Vision and curricular language describing a classical orientation rooted in liberal arts, civic virtue, and moral character; emphasis on Great Books and a balanced core curriculum (math, science, literature, history).  
- Concrete curricular commitments to Great Books, primary sources, and a Western-civilization-centered history, with explicit literacy instruction and language development.
