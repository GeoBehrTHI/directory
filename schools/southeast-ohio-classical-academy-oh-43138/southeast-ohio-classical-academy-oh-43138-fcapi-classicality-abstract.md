## ABSTRACT

Southeast Ohio Classical Academy demonstrates a CLASSICAL orientation in mission/goal, and in its faculty and parent/community engagement, with strong virtue-formation and truth/beauty commitments. The curriculum and pedagogy show a robust classical bent, including reading the classics and teacher-led instruction, and a community emphasis on formation of character. However, explicit theological/metaphysical grounding and some canonical classical pedagogy aspects (e.g., explicit Seven Liberal Arts framing, high-school Socratic pedagogy, and explicit Great Books/Great Conversation language) are less evident in public materials, resulting in an overall SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL classification. The six-category assessment yields: Category 1 – SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL; Category 2 – CLASSICAL; Category 3 – SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL; Category 4 – SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL; Category 5 – CLASSICAL; Category 6 – CLASSICAL. The aggregate is SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL.

What would move the needle (needle-moving changes):
- Add explicit theological or liturgical framing to reinforce the religious/metaphysical foundations.
- Introduce explicit high-school Socratic seminars and structured rhetoric training to elevate Pedagogy to CLASSICAL.
- Formalize a Strong Great Books/Great Conversation program with cross-subject integration.
- Publicly articulate the Seven Liberal Arts as the curricular backbone and map courses to grammar, logic, rhetoric; arithmetic, geometry, music, astronomy.
- Continue expanding and documenting the “living curriculum” as a normative practice with explicit faculty exemplars and mentorship expectations.
