## ABSTRACT

Cornerstone Classical Academy Kentucky 41022 exhibits a predominantly CLASSICAL orientation across most of the classical-elements rubric, with notable evidence of formation and liberal-arts orientation, and strong pedagogy signals (Socratic discussion; memory/recitation; rhetoric training). The public-facing materials show a clear emphasis on truth, goodness, beauty, virtue, liberal education, and a classical approach to language and history, including Latin instruction, and a high-school sequence with literature/history/Latin/world-language components. There are explicit signals of Great Books/Western-civilization engagement in places and a core emphasis on formation and virtue in public materials.

- Religious & Metaphysical Foundations: PARTIAL alignment (transcendental ideals are present in motto and virtues; theology and liturgical practices are not evidenced in public-facing materials). NOT CLASSICAL.

- Purpose & Goal: STRONG classical alignment (formation, virtue, classical-mission language, telos). CLASSICAL.

- Curriculum: STRONG classical cues (Latin, history, Western civilization, core-knowledge-like sequences, and reference to great books in parts of the site). SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL (explicit Trivium/Quadrivium articulation is not uniformly explicit across all curriculum pages). SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL.

- Pedagogy: STRONG classical signals (Socratic discussion; rhetoric training; memory and recitation). CLASSICAL.

- Faculty: Some classical signals (virtue formation and teacher accountability) but lack of a fully public-facing “living curriculum” portrait of faculty. SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL.

- Parents & Community: Strong COMMUNITY signals and parent-partnership framing (ICE pamphlet content used to supplement the public site). CLASSICAL.

Overall assessment: CLASSICAL. The aggregate signals across the six categories show a robust classical orientation with significant strengths in Purpose/Goal, Pedagogy, and Community, and solid Curriculum and Faculty cues, tempered by gaps in explicit theological grounding and some public articulation of a living-faculty portrait. Needle-moving steps listed above would strengthen Element 1 (theology/ liturgy) and Element 5 (living-faculty portrait) to push all six categories firmly into CLASSICAL.
