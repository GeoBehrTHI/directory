## Executive Summary

Cornerstone Classical Academy Kentucky 41022 (publicly represented in the crawl data as Cornerstone Classical Academy in Duval County, Florida) shows a predominantly classical orientation with clear strengths in formation, virtue, and the liberal arts emphasis, but with notable gaps in explicitly theological grounding and liturgical/community practices. Across the six classical-element categories, the school demonstrates strong alignment in School Purpose & Goal, Pedagogy, and Community/Parent engagement, while Religious & Metaphysical Foundations shows partial alignment (transcendentals appear in motto and virtues, but theology and liturgical practices are not evident). Curriculum and Faculty show mixed signals, with robust curricular commitments (Latin, history through high-school sequence, great literature emphasis in some materials) but less explicit articulation of the full Trivium/Quadrivium framework, or a living-faculty portrait in public materials. Overall, the school ranks CLASSICAL, with some important gaps in explicit theological grounding and certain faculty-pedagogy articulations. See category-by-category notes below for detailed keyword analyses, evidence, and rationale.

- Executive note on evidentiary basis: The public materials frequently express transcendent values (truth, goodness, beauty) and virtue-focused character formation (motto, virtues). They also explicitly describe classical aims, liberal arts emphasis, and Socratic-style pedagogy in public materials. They also reveal nonsectarian stance, a public-charter context, and a broad curriculum (Latin, history, core knowledge sequences, and Great Books-oriented language emphasis in parts of the site). Citations anchor these observations to the site and knowledge-base materials.    

- What would move the needle for SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL categories: emphasize explicit Great Books/Trivium content in curriculum pages, articulate a stronger “living curriculum” portrait of teachers as models, and foreground parents’ formal co-educator role in materials. See category sections for specifics.

- Abstract: The school displays a classical orientation with strong virtue and liberal-arts emphasis and Socratic elements in pedagogy, plus robust parent/community interfaces, but with gaps in explicit theological grounding and some detailed classical-curriculum language (e.g., explicit Trivium/Quadrivium articulation). Aggregate assessment: CLASSICAL, with room for needle-moving improvements in theology-liturgical grounding, explicit Great Books articulation, and a more defined living-faculty portrait.

## What Would Move the Needle

- For Element 1 (Religious and Metaphysical Foundations): Introduce explicit theology/theological grounding and liturgical/community practices in public-facing materials (e.g., statement of faith or explicit liturgical practices, even if the school remains nonsectarian, to provide a more robust religious-metaphysical grounding visible to the public). Concrete steps: add a theology anchor statement and a community worship/rite description to the site.

- For Element 5 (Faculty): Publicly articulate the living-curriculum portrait of teachers, including how teachers model virtue and carry the classical curriculum forward in practice; publish faculty development practices grounded in classical pedagogy; feature teacher exemplars and guides on the website.

- For Element 3 (Curriculum): More explicit articulation of Trivium/Quadrivium content in the curriculum pages; clearly label and describe Seven Liberal Arts components (grammar, logic, rhetoric; quadrivium) and how they structure the K-12 curriculum; emphasize Great Books and primary sources, with direct examples in each grade band to show a full Great-Books cadence.

- For Element 6 (Parents & Community): Expand public-facing materials to explicitly describe parent-formation programs, book studies, and co-teacher roles with concrete schedule and signup information; reinforce open-campus/visitation policies on the site, with a dedicated section detailing parent involvement opportunities.
