## ABSTRACT

The Christ Classical Academy South Campus materials demonstrate a CLASSICAL orientation across all six rubric categories. The school foregrounds Christian theological foundations, formation-focused aims (the Portrait of a Graduate), a clearly articulated Classical Curriculum (grammar/logic/rhetoric, Latin, primary sources, Great Books, ad fontes), and a pedagogical approach that blends memory work with Socratic inquiry and rhetoric. Faculty are represented through leadership and the living-curriculum framing, and parents are actively engaged as covenant partners and co-educators. The aggregate profile aligns with the traditional classical education model described in the knowledge-base lexicon, including the transcendental aims (truth, goodness, beauty), formation over information, and a robust partnership with families. The school’s public materials corroborate canonical classical indicators: Trivium-based structure, Great Books/primary sources, liturgical/theological grounding, and parent-community partnership.

Key evidence anchors:
- The Trivium-centered curriculum (grammar, logic, rhetoric) with Latin and Great Books orientation. 
- The Nicene Creed and explicit Christian formation language, including a “Portrait of a Graduate” focused on following Christ, loving neighbor, discerning truth and beauty, humble service, and excellence. 
- Covenant partnership with parents and a family-centered mission that frames education as formation within a Christian worldview. 

Note: All six classical-element categories align with the school’s public messaging, supporting a General Rating of CLASSICAL.
