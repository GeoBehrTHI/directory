## Executive Summary

Christ Classical Academy South Campus (Tallahassee, FL 32308) presents a clearly classical, Christian-formed educational model. The public materials consistently foreground a Trivium-based pedagogy (grammar, logic, rhetoric), a theology-centered worldview, a covenantal partnership with families, and a mission to form students who discern and practice what is true, good, and beautiful. The school frames its work around a Portrait of a Graduate that emphasizes Christ-centered formation, virtue, wonder, service, and excellence. Across the six classical-element categories, the school demonstrates strong alignment with classical criteria (CLASSICAL) with few if any gaps that would push it toward “Somewhat Classical” or “Not Classical.” The aggregate assessment is CLASSICAL.

Key evidentiary notes, aligned to the public materials and the classical rubric, include:
- Explicit Christian theological grounding and liturgical-cultural expressions (Nicene Creed, creedal language, Christ-centered worldview) and a mission and covenant-partnering stance with families. This is evident in mission statements, creedal content, and governance pages.  
- A clearly articulated classical curriculum and pedagogy structure: Pre-Grammar / Grammar / Logic / Rhetoric divisions, explicit Trivium emphasis, Latin, primary sources, and a “Great Books/Great Conversation” orientation in philosophy of education.  
- A robust “Portrait of a Graduate” and covenant-partnered parent culture that positions parents as co-educators and emphasizes formation, virtue, and the love of learning.  
- Evidence of structured, teacher-led leadership and governance (Head of School, Board, accreditation).
