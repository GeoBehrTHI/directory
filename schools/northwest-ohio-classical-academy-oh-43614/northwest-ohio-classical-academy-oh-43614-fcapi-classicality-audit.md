## Executive Summary

Northwest Ohio Classical Academy (NOCA) exhibits a strongly classical orientation across public-facing materials, with clear emphasis on a content-rich liberal-arts curriculum, virtue formation, and engagement with classical methods (e.g., Socratic dialogue, memorization, and memory work). The school foregrounds truth, goodness, and beauty as real and knowable, centers formation over mere information, and presents a curriculum and pedagogy aligned with classical ideals (Great Books, Trivium/Quadrivium, classical languages, and a rinse of Western civilizational study). The Hillsdale College Curriculum designation on NOCA’s site further anchors its classical identity, and public materials emphasize virtue, civic responsibility, and character formation. Several facets common to classical practice, such as Socratic dialogue in upper grades, memory- and chant-based review in younger grades, and a robust K–12 program with a clear graduation pathway, are evident. There is less explicit, on-site liturgical or theological apparatus described in public pages, though the language of virtue, moral education, and reverence for truth and beauty is prominent.

Overall classification: CLASSICAL
