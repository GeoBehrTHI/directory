## Executive Summary

Lumen Christi Catholic School (Indianapolis, IN 46203) demonstrates a robust classical orientation anchored in explicit Catholic identity. Across six categories, the public materials indicate strong alignment with classical education’s core aims: faith-informed virtue, the liberal arts, and a pedagogy rooted in timeless discourse and communal worship. The school presents itself as an independent Catholic PreK–12 institution with daily Mass, a Newman Guide recognition, and a stated mission to unite faith and reason in formation and virtue. While the school clearly embodies classical aims in most areas, there is less explicit, codified language around traditional Socratic pedagogy or the detailed operant practices of scholé in every setting. Taken together, the school sits squarely in CLASSICAL for most categories, with Pedagogy showing signs of classical method but not always stated in explicit, conventional terms.
