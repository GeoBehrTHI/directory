## Executive Summary

Vintage Christian Academy TX 76548 (VCA) presents a robust alignment with classical education principles, anchored in a biblically informed worldview and a clearly articulated Christian community. Across most categories, public materials emphasize formation of virtue, study of great ideas, and a classical core, reinforced by statements about a biblical worldview, Christian discipleship, and partnership with families. The school highlights a structured Classical Core, emphasis on great books and Western civilization, and a family-church partnership that includes parental involvement and open campus-style engagement. Taken together, these signals place VCA primarily in the CLASSICAL category, with Element 4 (Pedagogy) showing some gaps where explicit Socratic/seminar-based practices or the full Scholé/restful-learning vocabulary are not clearly evidenced in the public materials. The aggregate classification for VCA is CLASSICAL.

See statements like “our curriculum is rooted in biblical principles” and emphasis on a biblical worldview, including discipleship language and explicit Christian identity.

- Purpose & Goal: emphasis on formation, virtue, and the cultivation of wisdom within a Christian framework, not primarily job preparation. Evidence includes language about forming character and honoring God, and a mission described as a biblically based, discipleship-oriented school.

- Curriculum: explicit reference to a Classical Core with study of great books and ideas, and a program description that presents classical education as a core aim. Evidence includes “Classical Core” program, and statements about studying great books and ideas.

- Parents & Community: explicit partnership with families, parent involvement, and open-campus events, including family meet-and-greet, previews, and a Doctrinal/Parent Conduct framing for admission.

Overall, Vintage Christian Academy TX 76548 demonstrates CLASSICAL alignment, with a notable strength in religious/metaphysical foundations, purpose, and curriculum, and a strong faculty/community framework. Pedagogy shows some signs of classical practice but is less explicit in the public materials as containing full Socratic/seminar-based methods, hence the category-specific nuance for Pedagogy.
