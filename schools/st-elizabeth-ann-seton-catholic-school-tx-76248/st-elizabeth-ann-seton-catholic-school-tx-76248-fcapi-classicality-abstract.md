## ABSTRACT

- Classification: CLASSICAL
- Summary: St Elizabeth Ann Seton Catholic School (Keller, TX) demonstrates a strong classical orientation grounded in Catholic identity. Its public materials emphasize liturgical life, catechesis, the integration of Catholic doctrine across the curriculum, and the Great Books/liberal-arts framework (including Latin). The school emphasizes formation, virtue, and wisdom, partnering with families, and a living, virtuous faculty. While there are clear classical signals in all six categories, Curriculum and Pedagogy show SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL alignment due to the absence of explicit, public articulation of certain elements (notably a formal Trivium/Quadrivium framing and explicit high-school Socratic pedagogy in the public materials). The aggregate assessment remains CLASSICAL due to the predominance of classical signals across categories.

- Element-by-Element Summary:
  - Religious and Metaphysical Foundations — CLASSICAL: strong Catholic theological grounding; liturgical life; catechesis; sacramental worldview.
  - School Purpose and Goal — CLASSICAL: formation and wisdom; Great Books; classical liberal arts; teleological aims.
  - Curriculum — SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL: Great Books and Latin present; history integrated; explicit Trivium/Quadrivium framing not clearly enumerated in public content.
  - Pedagogy — SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL: contemplative pedagogy and liturgical/biblical formation; explicit Socratic pedagogy not clearly described for K–8.
  - Faculty — CLASSICAL: faculty as virtue-models; living curriculum; mentorship in a Catholic-classical frame.
  - Parents & Community — CLASSICAL: parents as partners; PSA; open campus; parental formation.

- Accessibility of Classical Signals: The site presents explicit public-facing statements about Great Books, Latin, and a classical liberal-arts approach, with Catholic formation as a core. This is reinforced by Mission/Philosophy materials and the explicit “First Principles” that place parents at the center of formation and partnership with the school.
