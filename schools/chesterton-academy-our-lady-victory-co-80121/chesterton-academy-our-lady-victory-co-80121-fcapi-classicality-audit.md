## Executive Summary

Chesterton Academy of Our Lady of Victory (Colorado 80121 area; public domain material centers on Centennial/Littleton, CO) presents a distinctly Catholic, classical-school profile. The public-facing materials emphasize a chapel-based, liturgical rhythm (Mass each morning, adoration, formation Fridays, Cultura Vitae family programming), a clear Catholic identity, and a stated aim of forming holy, virtuous students within a classical framework. The curriculum evidence includes explicit attention to Latin and sacral arts, plus a focus on integrated, virtue-centered formation; there is also visible attention to liberal-arts style habits (creative arts, music, and drama) and a rigor of content (science and mathematics sequences). While the school convincingly conforms to several core classical elements, certain explicit classical-canon indicators (e.g., explicit, published Trivium/Quadrivium emphasis, and Socratic pedagogy in high school) are less overt in the public materials. Taken together, the school demonstrates substantial alignment with classical education across most criteria, with some categories showing only partial (somewhat classical) alignment.

Overall classification: CLASSICAL

- Important note: The school’s Catholic identity and liturgical practices, together with an intentional formation focus and public emphasis on virtue and truth, anchor Category 1 (Religious & Metaphysical Foundations) and Category 2 (Purpose & Goal) firmly in the CLASSICAL bucket. Category 3 (Curriculum) and Category 4 (Pedagogy) show strong classical intentions (great books, Latin, formation, ritual life) but lack explicit, comprehensive public articulation of the entire Trivium/Quadrivium framework and Socratic pedagogy in all levels; those gaps place Category 3 and Category 4 into SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL rather than fully CLASSICAL. Categories 5 (Faculty) and 6 (Parents & Community) exhibit strong classical signals (clear teacher leadership, mentorship framing, and active family/parent engagement), pushing the aggregate toward CLASSICAL despite the two somewhat-lower-scoring categories.

What Would Move the Needle

- For Category 3 (Curriculum)
  - Publish explicit articulation of the Seven Liberal Arts framework (grammar, logic, rhetoric; arithmetic, geometry, music, astronomy) and connect Great Books with a concrete Great Conversation program.
  - Provide a clearly stated list of canonical Great Books, primary sources, and a cross-curricular plan showing how history, literature, philosophy, and sciences interrelate in a unified curriculum.
  - Emphasize a published plan for Trivium/Quadrivium elements and demonstrate integration with Latin, rhetoric, and logic in public materials.

- For Category 4 (Pedagogy)
  - Provide explicit public commitments to Socratic dialogue/seminars (Harkness-style discussions) for upper grades, with a plan showing when Socratic pedagogy begins and how students progress in rhetoric and public speaking.
  - Maintain and publicly document Scholé/restful-learning days and ensure a clear cadence of contemplative and challenging learning, with metrics to show outcomes tied to such rhythms.
  - Expand evidence of memory-work, recitation, and chant in the lower grades, linking these practices to formation goals public-facing.

- For Category 1 and 2 (Religious Foundations and Purpose)
  - Continue to foreground liturgical life and sacred tradition while articulating how these foundations shape liberal-arts formation (to strengthen the synergy of faith and reason as a formalizing anchor).
  - Consider publishing brief, structured statements tying the Catholic theological framework to specific learning outcomes, ensuring consistent messaging across grade levels.

- For Category 5 and 6 (Faculty & Community)
  - Maintain visible, named “living curriculum” faculty profiles and collectable testimonials showing formation through exemplary teaching practice.
  - Expand community-facing materials that demonstrate ongoing parent education, family catechesis, and community partnership opportunities beyond fund-raising and events.
