## ABSTRACT

Logos Christian School Plano demonstrates a consistent CLASSICAL orientation across six core dimensions: Religious and Metaphysical Foundations; School Purpose and Goal; Curriculum; Pedagogy; Faculty; and Parents & Community. The school’s public materials foreground a Christ-centered, biblical worldview, a Trivium-based curriculum, Charlotte Mason influences (living books, nature study, character formation), and a strong parental-partnership model with chapel participation and active family involvement. Evidence indicates a formation-oriented mission that seeks truth, goodness, and beauty through a synthesis of Christian theology, classical philosophy, and liberal-arts study. Faculty are framed as living mentors, and the community emphasizes collaboration with parents as co-educators. The aggregate assessment is CLASSICAL.

Key evidence points include: Biblical worldview integrated in all subjects and chapel-based liturgical life; the Trivium in curriculum (grammar, logic, rhetoric) with Western canon literature, Latin, Spanish, and history; Charlotte Mason-inspired pedagogy and living-books approach; a Conservatory of Fine Arts and Children’s Garden as core curricular elements; and explicit parent-partnership language in admissions and ongoing school life. The school’s public materials consistently articulate virtue, wisdom, and formation as central aims, aligning with the Six Essential Elements’ Positive Keyword indicators and demonstrating strong classical alignment across all six categories.
