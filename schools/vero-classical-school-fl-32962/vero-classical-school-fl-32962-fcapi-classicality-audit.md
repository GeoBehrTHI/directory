## Executive Summary

Vero Classical School (VCS) in Vero Beach, FL presents a robust classical framework that aligns strongly with traditional classical-education criteria. Public materials from the school emphasize a Christ-centered, liturgically-inflected, community-focused environment; a Trivium-based curriculum (Grammar, Logic, Rhetoric); a history-rich and integrated approach; Socratic discourse at the upper levels; and a “living curriculum” conveyed by faculty who model virtue and intellectual rigor. The school also foregrounds parental partnership and a welcoming, grace-filled community, signaling alignment with classical-parent-community expectations.

Overall, based on the rubric drawn from the classical-school knowledge base, VCS is CLASSICAL across all six categories. The site consistently showcases: (1) Religious and metaphysical foundations anchored in Christ-centered community and virtue; (2) a purpose/goals frame oriented toward formation, wisdom, and the pursuit of truth, goodness, and beauty; (3) a curriculum built on the Trivium (Grammar, Logic, Rhetoric) with historical study and field experiences; (4) pedagogy that blends contemplative/scholastic approaches with Socratic inquiry and memory work; (5) a faculty model that acts as the living curriculum and mentors; and (6) strong parent/community partnerships with open campuses and formal parent-engagement opportunities. Direct textual anchors include references to Christ-centered education, the Trivium, Socratic dialogue, memory-rich pedagogy, and parent involvement. The evidence is drawn from multiple school pages, including sections titled The Classical Model of Education, The Trivium (Grammar/Logic/Rhetoric), The Grammar/Logic/Rhetoric sequences, Community of Grace, and the Parent/Community materials. See cited sources throughout the category notes.

What would move the needle section is not required here since the aggregate classification is CLASSICAL across all six categories. No major systemic changes are needed to reach a fully classical standing per the rubric.
