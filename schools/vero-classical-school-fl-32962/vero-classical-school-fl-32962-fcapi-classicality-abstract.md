## Abstract

Vero Classical School in Vero Beach, FL demonstrates CLASSICAL alignment across all six evaluation categories. Evidence across the site shows a Christ-centered, community-of-grace environment (Element 1) and a clear telos oriented toward formation and wisdom (Element 2). The curriculum centers on the Trivium (Grammar/Logic/Rhetoric) with historical study and integrated humanities (Element 3). Pedagogy combines contemplative/restful elements with Socratic discussion and rhetoric training (Element 4). Faculty are portrayed as living exemplars and mentors central to the curriculum (Element 5). Parent and community engagement is explicit, with formal opportunities for parental involvement, tours, and education (Element 6).
