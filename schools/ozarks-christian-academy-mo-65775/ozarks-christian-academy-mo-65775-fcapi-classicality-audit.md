## Executive Summary

Ozarks Christian Academy (West Plains, MO, 65775) (OCA) presents a robust Classical Christian program rooted in a Christ-centered, biblical worldview. Public materials describe a Trivium-based curriculum (Grammar, Logic, Rhetoric stages) with strong emphasis on virtue, wisdom, and formation of character, rather than mere information transfer. The school frames education as formation in mind, heart, and faith, integrates biblical principles across subjects, and explicitly partners with families in a discipleship-focused community. Across six evaluation domains, OCA demonstrates strong classical affinities in Religious Foundations, Purpose, Pedagogy, Faculty, and Parent/Community engagement, with solid alignment in Curriculum (notably the Grammar/Logic/Rhetoric framework and emphasis on history) but more modest explicit signaling of Great Books/Great Conversation in published materials. The aggregate reading places OCA solidly in CLASSICAL territory, with Element 3 (Curriculum) being the most ambiguous area, leaning toward SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL due to less explicit Great Books signaling.
