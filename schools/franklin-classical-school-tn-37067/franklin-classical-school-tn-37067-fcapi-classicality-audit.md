## Executive Summary

Franklin Classical School TN 37065 (FCS) presents a clearly Christian, classical-inflected program with strong emphasis on character formation, virtue, and a liberal-arts oriented Humanities approach. Public materials consistently frame the school as Biblically grounded, theologically explicit, and oriented toward virtue and truth, with a strong parent-partnership component and a university-model division that blends campus study with at-home learning options. Across categories, the school most clearly demonstrates a Classical orientation in religious/metaphysical foundations, school purpose/goal, faculty culture, and parent/community engagement, while some facets of curriculum and pedagogy are less explicitly articulated in the classical lexicon (notably explicit Great-Books orientation and fully articulated Socratic/seminar-based pedagogy). Overall, the school aligns with CLASSICAL in aggregate, with some areas showing only SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL strength pending further documentation of canonical classical elements.

This would help stakeholders understand the full classical continuity from K–12.
