## Executive Summary

Arrow Classical Education Kentucky (ZIP 42420) presents a clearly classical, Christian-oriented program anchored in traditional liberal arts, Great Books, and a biblically grounded worldview. Public-facing materials emphasize partnership with families, a focus on Latin and the liberal arts, and a mission to form students in faith and virtue rather than mere information or job preparation. The school’s materials consistently foreground biblical faith, classical pedagogy, and the Great Books canon, with explicit statements about partnering with parents and maintaining a biblical worldview as the organizing principles of the school. Taken together, these signals align strongly with a CLASSICAL classification across most of the classical criteria, with Pedagogy showing strong classical elements but somewhat less explicit articulation of certain traditional pedagogical practices (e.g., specific Socratic or scholé rituals) in the readily visible materials.

Key evidence points include:
- Mission & Vision statements tying education to loving and serving God and grounding learning in a biblical worldview, with a commitment to classical excellence and Great Books (turn0file11).
- Explicit emphasis on Latin, artes liberales, and the Great Books as foundational to coursework and upper-grade excellence (turn0file11).
