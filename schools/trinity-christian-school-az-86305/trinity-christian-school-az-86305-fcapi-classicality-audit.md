## Executive Summary

Trinity Christian School (Prescott, AZ, 86305) presents a robust classical Christian orientation across most dimensions of the Six Essential Elements rubric. The website and public materials consistently foreground Christ-centered, biblically grounded classical education, with explicit emphasis on the trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric), formation of virtue and wisdom, and a strong partnership with parents. Evidence of liturgical and communal practice appears in worship-oriented language and prayerful campus moments, while the faculty narrative emphasizes mentoring and “living curriculum.” Some areas show less explicit alignment (e.g., named Great Books/Great Conversation programs are not clearly advertised on the site), which yields a couple of category-level nuances, but the overall pattern is predominantly classical. The aggregate assessment is CLASSICAL.

Key takeaways:
- Religious and metaphysical foundations are clearly present (Christ-centered, biblical worldview; worship; discipleship).
- The school’s purpose/goal centers on formation, virtue, wisdom, and nurturing the whole child rather than mere job preparation.
- Curriculum centers on the Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric) with explicit grammar-stage language; stronger evidence for the classical approach is evident than for explicit Great Books mention.
- Pedagogy includes memory work and contemplative/restful dimensions, with strong evidence of classical methods, though explicit Socratic seminars and high-school-level rhetoric/deliberation are not uniformly demonstrated in public materials.
- Faculty messaging positions teachers as mentors and living curriculum; a clear emphasis on virtue and mentorship is present.
- Parents and community are treated as partners, with explicit calls for parent involvement, campus tours, and ongoing engagement.
