# Classicality Audit: Hillsdale Academy

School website audited: https://academy.hillsdale.edu

## Executive Summary
Overall classification: CLASSICAL

Hillsdale Academy presents exceptionally strong alignment to the six essential elements of a classical school. Its public materials explicitly affirm transcendental moral realism (“objective standards of truth, goodness, and beauty”), theological grounding (Christian mission and worship practices), and a liberal arts curriculum centered on Latin, logic, rhetoric, and the Western tradition. Pedagogy references Socratic dialogue, memory and recitation, and culminating public orations/defenses. Faculty are described as moral and intellectual models and mentors, and the school clearly states a partnership with parents as primary educators. Key strengths include: explicit TGB language; required Latin; logic and rhetoric; chapel and communal prayer; Socratic dialogue and public defenses; “virtue and wisdom” as telos; and parent partnership. Minor gaps include limited explicit use of scholé/restful-learning language and limited explicit mention of lower‑school chanting in classroom pedagogy. These do not materially diminish the overall classical alignment, which is clear and comprehensive.

Evidence highlights:
- “Objective standards of truth, goodness, and beauty” and Christian faith commitments (mission, chapel, prayer)  
- Required Latin; logic and rhetoric; “Socratic dialogue” and primary sources; great conversation; public thesis defense  
- Portrait of a Graduate emphasizes virtue, wonder, intellectual humility, memorization, and pursuit of the True, Good, and Beautiful 
- Faculty portrayed as mentors and models; explicit parent partnership (“parents are the primary educators”)
