## Executive Summary

The Geneva School (Laguna Woods, CA; referred to here as Geneva School CA 92637) presents a solidly CLASSICAL profile across all six classical-school categories. Public-facing materials from the school consistently foreground a distinctly classical Christian pedagogy built on the Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric), the study of great books, Latin, and Socratic methods, all framed within a Christian worldview. The faculty are described as faith-driven exemplars, and the school emphasizes formation (not just information), virtue, wisdom, and wonder as core aims. Parent engagement is clearly valued through admissions processes and community-building language, while the curriculum and pedagogy emphasize memory, chant, recitation, Socratic dialogue, and rigorous classical disciplines. Together, these cues align with the Positive Keyword indicators and the six-element rubric for a Classical School.

Key evidentiary points drawn from Geneva School materials include: a distinctly classical Christian framework with a Christian worldview across subjects; explicit Trivium framing (grammar/logic/rhetoric) and Great Books emphasis; use of Socratic seminars and memory-based pedagogy; and strong statements about formation, virtue, wisdom, and a Christ-centered community supported by qualified faculty and faith-aligned parent partnerships. These signals are repeatedly documented in The Geneva School’s public content, including “Why a Classical Christian Education?”, “The Geneva Difference,” “Culitvating Wisdom and Virtue,” and related program pages, as well as admissions and history sections. See, for example, explicit notes on a Christian worldview and biblical formation, Trivium stages, and Great Books, as well as faculty and parent-partnership language.
