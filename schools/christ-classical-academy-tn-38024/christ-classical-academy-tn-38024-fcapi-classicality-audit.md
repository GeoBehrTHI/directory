## Executive Summary

Christ Classical Academy (Tn 38024) presents a robust classical Christian program with clear alignment to the Six Essential Elements of a Classical School rubric. Public materials from the school emphasize a Christ-centered, classical Trivium-based curriculum (grammar, logic, rhetoric) delivered through Memoria Press resources, a Great-Books orientation, and explicit liturgical and communal practices (Focus time, chapel, Salt & Light, mission trips). The school also foregrounds formation over mere information, a strong partnership with families, and a living, virtuous faculty. Collectively, these signals place the school squarely in the CLASSICAL category across all six rubric categories, with particular strength in Curriculum, Pedagogy, Faculty, and Parents & Community. Evidence drawn from the school’s public materials includes: explicit statements of a Christ-centered classical curriculum, the Trivium, Great-Books/Great Conversation framing, liturgical and prayer practices, and an active, partnership-oriented approach to parents and community. Positive signals such as “Christ-centered,” “classical curriculum,” “grammar–logic–rhetoric,” “Great Books / Great Conversation,” “Socratic seminar” (as described in higher-grade pedagogy), “Recitation and memory work,” “Latin,” and “Salt & Light” are repeatedly present. Countervailing negative signals are minimal; the school does not appear to rely on relativism, secular-skill framing, or purely project-based pedagogy in lieu of classical methods.
