# ABSTRACT

Saint Monica Academy (Montrose, CA 91020) demonstrates robust alignment with classical education principles as defined by the Six Essential Elements. Across the six categories—Religious and Metaphysical Foundations, School Purpose and Goal, Curriculum, Pedagogy, Faculty, and Parents & Community—SMA exhibits explicit Catholic formation, a formation-centric telos, a trivium-based curriculum, a memory- and dialogue-rich pedagogy, faculty who model virtue as the living curriculum, and a strong partnership with parents as co-educators. The public materials repeatedly emphasize Faith, Reason, and Virtue; a classical liberal-arts curriculum; Great Books and Socratic methods; Latin study; integrated Western civilization history; and liturgical life on campus. The keyword indicators from the Six Essential Elements lexicon align with SMA’s signals (e.g., theology, liturgy, virtue, Great Books, trivium, scholé, memory, and liturgical formation), yielding a comprehensive CLASSICAL rating across all six categories. SMA’s public-facing materials present a coherent, historically grounded approach to classical education that integrates faith and reason within a communal, family-centered model.
