## Executive Summary

Saint Monica Academy (SMA), located in Montrose, CA 91020, presents a robust alignment with classical education as defined by the Six Essential Elements framework. Public materials from SMA consistently frame the school as a Catholic, classical institution that foregrounds faith, reason, and virtue; emphasizes the trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric); centers Great Books and Socratic discourse; employs memory-based pedagogy (recitation, chanting, mnemonic devices); and promotes a literate cultivation of character with strong parental partnership. Across six rubric categories, SMA demonstrates strong fidelity to classical aims rather than a more secular or purely vocational model.

Key observations driving the overall CLASSICAL assessment:

- Religious and metaphysical foundations are explicit and central (Catholic identity, theology, liturgy, sacraments, daily prayer, Mass, magisterium alignment).
- The school purpose emphasizes formation (virtue, wisdom, contemplation) over mere information or job preparation, aligned with the transcendental aims of truth, goodness, and beauty.
- Curriculum centers the liberal arts via the trivium, Great Books, Western civilization, Latin, and integrated humanities; history is woven through courses rather than treated as mere social studies.
- Pedagogy includes contemplative/restful elements (scholé) alongside Socratic discussion, seminars, and rhetoric training; lower grades engage in memory-centered activities and chant, while upper grades practice debate and oration.
- Faculty are portrayed as exemplary models and living curriculum; there is explicit emphasis on virtue, mentorship, and a collaborative culture.
- Parents and community are positioned as co-teachers and partners in formation, with active parent involvement and events, along with a welcoming campus.

Overall classification by category: CLASSICAL in all six categories.

This evaluation draws on SMA’s public-facing materials (classical pedagogy, integrated humanities, Great Books, trivium, Catholic formation, liturgical practices, and parent/community engagement) and triangulates those signals against the Positive/Negative keyword indicators in the Six Essential Elements lexicon. See the cited SMA materials for evidence and the Six Essential Elements keyword framework for the scoring rationale.
