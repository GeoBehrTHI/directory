## Abstract

Valor San Antonio (78228) demonstrates a strong CLASSICAL orientation in its mission, curriculum signals, and community commitments, with explicit emphasis on virtue, truth, goodness, and beauty, as well as Great Books, poetry memorization, Latin, and nature study. The public materials show a robust formation-centric purpose and a robust parental/community partnership, both of which align with classical aims. However, explicit articulation of the Trivium/Quadrivium as foundational liberal arts, a fully developed Socratic pedagogy across grades, and concrete liturgical/theological integration are not consistently clear in the public-facing content, resulting in a SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL overall assessment.

- Purpose and Goal: CLASSICAL, with explicit formation aims, virtue cultivation, and a telos toward wisdom and moral excellence.

The Valor San Antonio public materials substantiate several classical pillars (Great Books, memorization, virtue-focused aims, parental partnership) while leaving room for more explicit articulation of the full classical pedagogy and theological-liturgical identity. Focused enhancements in the areas above would push Valor toward a full CLASSICAL rating across all six categories.

ABSTRACT

Valor San Antonio presents a robust CLASSICAL orientation through explicit aims (virtue and wisdom formation), a curriculum featuring Great Books, memorization, Latin and nature study, and a strong family/community partnership. However, explicit articulation of the Trivium/Quadrivium as foundational, a fully articulated Socratic pedagogy, and theatre-style liturgical/theological integration are not consistently evident in public-facing materials, resulting in a SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL overall rating. Strengthening theology integration, making the classical pedagogy framework explicit across all grades, and detailing liturgical/community practices would move Valor toward full CLASSICAL status.
