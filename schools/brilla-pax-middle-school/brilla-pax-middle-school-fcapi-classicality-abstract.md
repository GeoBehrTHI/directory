Overall Classification: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL

## ABSTRACT

Brilla Pax Middle School displays a SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL orientation, evidenced by explicit virtue-centered aims and a stated classical approach, and a modern blended-learning pedagogy. However, it lacks robust, public documentation of key classical elements such as a Seven Liberal Arts curriculum, explicit Great Books/Great Conversation commitments, pronounced Socratic pedagogy, liturgical/theological grounding, and explicit parent-as-co-educator partnerships. Consequently, its overall classification across the six classical elements is SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL.
