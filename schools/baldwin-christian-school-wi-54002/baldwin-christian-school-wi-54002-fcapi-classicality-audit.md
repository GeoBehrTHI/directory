# Classicality Audit Report: Baldwin Christian School

Website audited: https://www.baldwinchristianschool.org/

Executive Summary

- Overall classification: CLASSICAL
- Why: Baldwin Christian School (BCS) explicitly anchors its mission and curriculum in Christian theology, the transcendental ideals of truth, goodness, and beauty, and the liberal arts tradition (Trivium; Great Books), with age-appropriate pedagogy (chanting, memory work; Socratic discussion; thesis defense) and clear parent partnership language. Evidence appears consistently across admissions, academics, and program pages, and aligns closely with the Six Essential Elements positive indicator lexicon.
- Notable strengths: Robust Trivium framing; Great Books lists; Latin and Greek; explicit Socratic discussions; liturgical elements (Bible, prayer, hymns; choir); explicit parent partnership.
- Opportunities: Faculty section could strengthen by publishing an explicit portrait of a teacher, stated mentor/collegial formation practices, or faculty classical formation. (Indicator rationale per the Six Elements faculty lexicon.)

Method note: This audit follows the Six Essential Elements of a Classical School keyword framework (positive/negative indicators), applying it to the school’s public materials. The element-by-element keyword lists and criteria are taken from the provided “Six Essential Elements” materials and supporting lexicon.
