# Classicality Audit: Bishop Louis Reicher Catholic School (Waco, TX)

Website audited: https://bishopreicher.com/

## Executive Summary
Overall classification: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL

Bishop Louis Reicher (BLR) presents a strongly Catholic, virtue-centric identity with robust theological integration, daily/weekly liturgical life, and visible reference to classical authors (Homer, Sophocles, Shakespeare) and Latin in the middle school program. These are all positive classical indicators. However, the site places significant emphasis on contemporary college-prep metrics (PSAT/National Merit), AP Calculus, state-of-the-art labs, and hands-on STEM/robotics—signals that, without balancing classical pedagogy (e.g., Socratic/seminar, rhetoric orations, memory/recitation, scholé/restful learning), dilute the classical profile. Evidence for classical pedagogy, faculty-as-mentors language, and structured parent formation is sparse or absent on the public pages reviewed. Consequently, BLR is rated SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL overall, with Category 1 (Religious Foundations) clearly CLASSICAL; several other categories show mixed signals. Keyword analyses and category scores below are anchored to the “Six Essential Elements” keyword framework and lexicon provided for this audit  .

## Overall Assessment
Aggregate classification: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL

- Strong religious/theological/liturgical foundations (Category 1) clearly align with classical Christian education frameworks (virtue language; theology as a governing discipline; schoolwide worship) .
- Curriculum shows classical elements (Latin; classical literature; theology and scripture), tempered by modern STEM and college-prep positioning (robotics, labs, AP/PSAT marketing) .
- Classical pedagogical signals (Socratic/seminar, rhetoric orations, recitation/chant, scholé/rest) are not visible; project-based/experiential language dominates descriptions of STEM, pulling pedagogy away from classical norms .
- Evidence for faculty-as-mentors and parent formation is limited on public pages.

---

## What would move the needle (to “CLASSICAL”)
- Pedagogy: Publish explicit descriptions of classical methods:
  - Upper School: Socratic seminars, dialectic debates, rhetoric speeches/orations (e.g., capstone theses), and text-centered colloquy .
  - Lower School: Daily recitation/chanting/memory work and hymnody; articulate “restful learning/scholé” practices (unhurried contemplation)  .
- Curriculum clarity:
  - Name the Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric) and, where fitting, Quadrivium; post reading lists featuring Great Books/primary sources to anchor the “Great Conversation” .
  - Continue Latin and consider describing progression (e.g., start grades, objectives) to underscore classical languages’ role .
- Re-balance marketing:
  - Continue STEM excellence but temper hands-on/project framing with classical telos and methods; shift from outcomes-first (PSAT/AP/National Merit) to wisdom/virtue formation as the first purpose of schooling, per the classical goal set .
- Faculty and Parent formation:
  - Publish a “Portrait of a Teacher” and “Portrait of a Graduate” oriented to virtue/wisdom, and outline faculty development in classical pedagogy (e.g., Socratic leader training) .
  - Add parent-formation offerings (book clubs, seminars, primers on classical education), and state “parent partnership” explicitly as a school distinctive .
