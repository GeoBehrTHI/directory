## Executive Summary

Masters Academy NC 28043 (The Master’s Academy) presents a clear orientation toward classical Christian education, with strong religious foundations, a stated classical curriculum framework, and a mission of character formation alongside intellectual development. Public materials emphasize faith-based formation, a weekly chapel program, and a classical curriculum framed by ACCS membership. Pedagogical practices highlighted on the site include outdoor, inquiry-driven experiences and a commitment to dedicated, “living” faculty who model virtue. The school’s public materials show strong alignment in Religious and Metaphysical Foundations, School Purpose and Goal, and Faculty, with credible evidence for Curriculum and Pedagogy that is indicative but not uniformly explicit about all canonical classical practices (e.g., explicit Great Books/Great Conversation references or a robustly Socratic high-school pedagogy). Overall, the school is CLASSICAL in its orientation, with some areas showing only SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL signals and one category (Parents & Community) showing limited classical cues.

- Aggregate classification: CLASSICAL

- Key insights:
  - Religious foundation is explicit and central: faith-based education, weekly Chapel, Bible curriculum, and ACCS membership. This strongly supports Element 1.
  - Purpose and formation are foregrounded: noble purpose, formation of character, and a mind trained through classical aims. This supports Element 2.
  - Curriculum is described as classical, with explicit mention of a classical curriculum and traditional subject areas; explicit Great Books references are not shown in public materials, which yields SOMEWHAT signals for Element 3.
  - Pedagogy includes flow-based, inquiry-driven outdoor learning, which provides SOMEWHAT classical signals but is not fully aligned with some canonical classical methods (e.g., explicit Socratic seminars in public materials). Element 4 is SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL.
  - Faculty are depicted as model teachers and living curriculum, which aligns with CLASSICAL signals in Element 5.
  - Parent/community engagement is not clearly demonstrated as a co-teaching model within the public materials, aligning with NOT CLASSICAL in Element 6.
