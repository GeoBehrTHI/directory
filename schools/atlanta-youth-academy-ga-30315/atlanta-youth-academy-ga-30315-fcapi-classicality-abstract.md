# ABSTRACT

Atlanta Youth Academy presents a robust Christian foundation with a Christ-centered mission, daily prayer, and a clear emphasis on character formation and service. The school demonstrates CLASSICAL alignment in Religious and Metaphysical Foundations and Parents & Community, while Curriculum, Pedagogy, and Faculty show mixed signals, producing an overall SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL rating. The school’s public materials emphasize prayer, Biblical worldview, servant-teacher ethos, small-class attention, and family engagement, which align with classical formation ideals. However, explicit classical curriculum elements (Seven Liberal Arts, Great Books, Great Conversation) and formal Socratic pedagogy are not clearly documented, resulting in a NOT CLASSICAL rating for Curriculum and a SOMETIMES-assertive Classical pedagogy for Pedagogy and Faculty. The aggregate assessment indicates SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL, with clear, concrete opportunities to move toward CLASSICAL by explicitly embedding Great Books, Trivium-based curriculum, Scholé pedagogy, and a fully articulated living-curriculum framework.

Key takeaways:

- Strong religious and community orientation: CLASSICAL.
- Formation and virtue emphasis: present and meaningful, leaning toward CLASSICAL in spirit.
- Explicit classical curriculum and pedagogy: not yet clearly evidenced in public-facing materials.
- Parent/Community partnership: CLASSICAL.
