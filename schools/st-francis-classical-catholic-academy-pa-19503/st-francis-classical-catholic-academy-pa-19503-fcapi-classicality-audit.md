## Executive Summary

Saint Francis Classical Catholic Academy (SFCCA) demonstrates strong alignment with classical education as defined by the Six Essential Elements. The school presents a robust religious and metaphysical foundation (Catholic identity at the center of learning, liturgical life, catechesis), a clearly formed purpose (formation through virtue, wisdom, and the true good and beautiful), a curriculum built around the liberal arts, a pedagogy that blends contemplative and Socratic methods, a faculty model oriented toward virtue and mentorship, and a parents-and-community partnership that actively involves families in faith-informed, classical formation. Evidence across SFCCA materials highlights explicit Great Books, a Four-School Within the Academy (Pre-Grammar to Rhetoric), integration of liturgy and sacred practice, and a pedagogy that includes memorization, chant, and memory-driven recitation alongside Socratic discussion in upper grades. Collectively, these signals place SFCCA in the CLASSICAL category across all six categories of assessment.
