## Executive Summary
Overall classification: CLASSICAL

Hillsdale Academy (https://academy.hillsdale.edu) presents clear, repeated evidence of a classical Christian school across all six essential elements. Its public materials explicitly affirm objective standards of truth, goodness, and beauty; a mission of formation in virtue and wisdom; a traditional liberal arts curriculum including Latin, logic, and rhetoric; upper‑school Socratic dialogue and capstone defenses; faculty who model and mentor toward virtue; and a robust parent partnership and liturgical life. These features align tightly with the positive indicators in the “Six Essential Elements of a Classical School” keyword framework and the supporting lexicon used for this audit  .

School website used: https://academy.hillsdale.edu

## Aggregate Assessment
Overall rating: CLASSICAL

Hillsdale Academy’s website evidences alignment with all six elements of a classical Christian school. It explicitly anchors education in transcendental moral realism and Christian theology with communal liturgical practices; defines education as formation in wisdom and virtue; implements a liberal‑arts curriculum with Latin, logic, rhetoric, Great Books, and primary sources; employs Socratic dialogue, recitation/memorization, wonder, and rhetorical capstones; presents faculty as mentors and exemplars; and actively partners with parents as primary educators     .
