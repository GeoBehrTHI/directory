## ABSTRACT

Founders Classical Academy – Corinth (K–5) Grammar Campus TX 75065 demonstrates substantial alignment with classical education principles, especially in formation, virtue, liberal-arts curriculum, and strong parent-community partnerships. The school articulates a formation-centric purpose (formation of scholars, virtue, and Western classical curriculum) and presents a credible faculty and community framework. However, explicit theology/the liturgical integration, consistent Great Books/Great Conversation engagement, and an explicit Scholé/restful pedagogy language are not uniformly foregrounded in the publicly accessible materials, which places Element 1, Element 3, and Element 4 as SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL rather than CLASSICAL. The aggregate across all six categories lands in SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL, with clear strengths in Element 2 (Purpose and Goal), Element 5 (Faculty), and Element 6 (Parents & Community), and strong signals in Element 3 (Curriculum) and Element 4 (Pedagogy) that could be elevated to CLASSICAL through targeted public articulations.

Targeted enhancements around explicit theology integration, Great Books engagement, and a transparent, public pedagogy map would move the school toward a robust CLASSICAL standing across all six categories.
