## Executive Summary

Redeemer Classical School Ga 30269 presents a clearly articulated classical Christian vision. The public materials emphasize a Christ-centered education anchored in the classical liberal arts, with explicit reference to the Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric), Great Books, and a formation-focused purpose that aims at virtue and wisdom in harmony with faith. The school also foregrounds a partnership with families and churches, a visible living-curriculum approach, and a 3-day-on-site model that integrates faith and academics. Taken together, these signals align strongly with classical education as defined by the Six Essential Elements framework, indicating a CLASSICAL rating across categories with minor ambiguity in pedagogy details that are not explicit on the site but do not undermine the overall classical orientation. See category-by-category analysis below for the keyword-driven justification and evidence. Sources include Redeemer’s own site content and the Six Essential Elements of a Classical School rubric (positive/negative keyword indicators) to ground the assessment. The analysis draws on the following: Christ-centered and scripture-based framing; Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric); Great Books and Western/cultural heritage; curriculum choices (Mystery of History, Berean Builders, Singapore Primary, Harold Jacobs Algebra/Geometry, Novare Science); explicit teacher-as-living-curriculum concepts; and strong parent/community partnership language. Key material references are cited inline with the corresponding evidence.
