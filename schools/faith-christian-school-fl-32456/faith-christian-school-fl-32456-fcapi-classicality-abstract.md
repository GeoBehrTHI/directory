## ABSTRACT

Faith Christian School (Port St Joe, FL, 32456) exhibits a clearly Christian, classical-leaning program anchored in a biblical-theological framework, a Trivium-based curriculum, and a church-affiliated, Christ-centered educational identity. The school’s public materials emphasize biblical authority, doctrinal commitments, and classical pedagogy (grammar, logic, rhetoric) with integrated biblical instruction. This yields a categorization close to CLASSICAL in the Religious and Metaphysical Foundations and Curriculum categories, while the overall profile across all six categories sits at SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL due to gaps in fully explicit Great Books/Great Conversation signaling, formalized living-curriculum faculty language, and explicit parent-formation structures.

- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: CLASSICAL
  Evidence includes a formal Statement of Faith and explicit biblical/theological commitments, Church affiliation, and mission language (Word of God, Trinity, biblical authority). Positive keyword signals are strong; negative signals are absent.

- School Purpose and Goal: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL
  Evidence shows formation-oriented aims within a classical-Christian frame (mind/body/spirit), but explicit articulation of virtue, wisdom, and telos beyond formation is less overt. Positive cues outweigh negatives, yielding a partial CLASSICAL alignment.

- Curriculum: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL
  Evidence shows a Trivium-based, biblically integrated curriculum with grammar/logic/rhetoric and historical study across subjects. The absence of explicit Great Books/Great Conversation language reduces it from CLASSICAL to SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL.

- Pedagogy: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL
  Evidence supports grammar/recitation/memory and rhetoric-focused upper-level expression, but explicit Socratic/seminar pedagogy or scholé-centered practices are not clearly described in public-facing materials. Positive signals exist; the absence of explicit Paideia-style pedagogy signals lowers classification.

- Faculty: NOT CLASSICAL
  Evidence shows standard faculty presence but a lack of explicit “living curriculum” framing, portrait-of-a-teacher, or faculty-mentorship language that typifies classical “living curriculum” pedagogy.

- Parents & Community: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL
  Evidence indicates parent engagement via open-admissions and financial-aid connections (Step Up for Students, FES) and a church-centered community context; however explicit formal parent-formation programs or a stated “co-teaching” partnership with parents are not clearly documented.

Overall Classification: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL

This assessment leverages the dual-source framework: the Faith Christian School site (crawl content) and the classical-education knowledge base (Six Essential Elements of a Classical School) to map keywords and practices against the six categories. The school demonstrates clear classical-and-theological alignment in several areas, with room to strengthen the explicit Great Books/Conversation, living-curriculum faculty signaling, and parent-community formation to reach full CLASSICAL in all six categories.
