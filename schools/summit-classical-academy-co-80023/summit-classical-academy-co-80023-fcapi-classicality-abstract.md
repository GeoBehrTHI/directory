## ABSTRACT

Summit Classical Academy (SCA) demonstrates a robust CLASSICAL profile across all six classical criteria.

- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: Strong Christ-centered and biblically grounded framing with explicit God-centric language (e.g., God’s truth, goodness, and beauty; biblical worldview; worship-like community life). The public materials foreground theology, Christian community, and liturgical practices in alignment with classical education’s religious foundation. CLASSICAL.
- School Purpose and Goal: Clear formation/virtue-oriented telos, with an emphasis on formation over information, virtue development, and a teleological aim toward human flourishing under a Christian worldview. CLASSICAL.
- Curriculum: A true classical curriculum is described, centering the Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric), Great Books, Great Conversation, and a liberal-arts framework, with history engaged through integrated, cross-disciplinary study. CLASSICAL.
- Pedagogy: Emphasis on Socratic dialogue, seminars, debates, and rhetoric into the upper grades; a model where memory work and disciplined discourse lead to philosophical and theological inquiry. CLASSICAL.
- Faculty: Living-curriculum orientation with faculty as mentors and exemplars; ACCS accreditation; public teacher profiles illustrating professional and spiritual formation of students. CLASSICAL.
- Parents & Community: Strong emphasis on parental partnership, parental formation through guidance and reading, and open involvement in the school’s mission. CLASSICAL.

Overall classification: CLASSICAL

- The school presents a coherent and well-supported classical approach across all six categories, integrating classical pedagogy, Great Books, a Trivium-centered curriculum, and a robust theological framework with a strong parent-community partnership. The evidence across the school's site and the classical-education knowledge base supports a unified CLASSICAL designation.
