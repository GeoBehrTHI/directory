## Executive Summary

Summit Classical Academy (SCA) presents a cohesive, well-articulated Classical Christian program anchored in a biblically-informed worldview. Across the six classical criteria, SCA demonstrates clear Religi ous & Metaphysical Foundations, a purposeful and virtue-first aim, a Trivium-based Curriculum with Great Books engagement, a pedagogy that blends Socratic inquiry with rhetoric, a faculty culture described as mentors living the curriculum, and an active partnership with parents and the broader community. The public materials consistently emphasize God, Christ, virtue, truth, formation, and engagement with Great Books, along with explicit statements about partnering with families and involving parents as co-educators. The aggregate assessment across all six categories is CLASSICAL.

Key higher-signal indicators include: explicit Christ-centered messaging and biblical worldview; formation of character and virtue as core aims; Trivium-based curriculum with Great Books and a focus on philosophical and historical thought; upper-school emphasis on Socratic dialogue, seminars, and rhetoric; a living-faculty/mentorship model and ACCS accreditation; and documented partnerships with parents and a strong parent/community orientation. There is consistent use of terms such as truth, goodness, beauty; virtue; formation; Great Books; Trivium; Socratic dialogue; public affirmation of parental partnership; and explicit theological framing.

- Why this rating
  - The pedagogy explicitly features Socratic, seminar-based, and rhetorical training in its upper grades and a Trivium-centered progression, which are central to classical teaching methods. This qualifies as CLASSICAL pedagogy.

Aggregate Assessment

- Relative weight of signals across all six categories strongly supports CLASSICAL. The presence of classic elements—Great Books, Trivium, Socratic/seminar pedagogy, virtuous formation, a teacher-as-mentor living curriculum, and a robust parent/community partnership—comprise a coherent classical-education profile.

What Would Move the Needle

- Given the CLASSICAL rating across all six categories, no major needle-moving changes are required to achieve a stronger CLASSICAL standing. If anything, further explicit articulation of Great Books reading lists, a published scope-and-sequence showing Trivium progression by grade bands, and more explicit (and citable) statements of a liturgy/worship cadence could provide even greater clarity for external evaluators; however, these are already present in the substantive content.
