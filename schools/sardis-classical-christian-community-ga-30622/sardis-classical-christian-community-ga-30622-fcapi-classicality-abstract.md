## ABSTRACT

Sardis Classical Christian Community GA 30622 demonstrates a CLASSICAL orientation across most criteria. Religious and metaphysical foundations are strongly evident through a Christian, theology-centered mission, worship/contextual communal life, and Biblical literacy emphasis. The school’s purpose and goal emphasize formation, virtue, wisdom, and the pursuit of truth, beauty, and goodness. The curriculum shows emphasis on Great Books and history, with logic visible in public materials, and a broader reference to trivium/quadrivium signals through network content. Pedagogy reflects contemplative/restful elements and a hybrid in-class/home model, with some dialogic teaching signals in related materials, but explicit, consistently documented Socratic pedagogy remains less evident on Sardis’ own site. Faculty content highlights model teachers and a living curriculum with a Christian ethos, and parent/community evidence shows strong partnership, parent formation, and open-campus access.
