## Executive Summary

Sardis Classical Christian Community in Athens, GA (ZIP 30622) presents a clearly classical orientation with a strong Christian and biblical framework. Public materials emphasize a covenant, Christ-centered educational vision, classical methods, and a hybrid model that includes home-based days for some grades. The school foregrounds elements aligned with the Great Books tradition, the trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric) in its broader network, and a pedagogy that blends contemplative/restful approaches with Socratic/seminar-style inquiry in upper levels, though explicit evidence of all six classical elements is variably demonstrated in the public materials.

Overall, the school is CLASSICAL. Evidence across categories shows substantial alignment with religious/metaphysical foundations, purpose/goal, curriculum, faculty expectations, and parent/community partnership. Category-wise nuance exists in Curriculum and Pedagogy, where explicit mentions of grammar, logic, rhetoric and continuous Socratic pedagogy are less consistently documented in Sardis’ own site content (some signals are present via the network’s published materials and related pages). This yields a few category-specific notes, but not enough to move the aggregate rating away from CLASSICAL.
