## Executive Summary

Faith Christian Academy (Kansas City, MO, 64164) (FCA) presents a robust classical Christian program that aligns strongly with the Six Essential Elements of Classical Education. Across all six rubric categories, FCA demonstrates explicit religious grounding, formation aims, a Trivium-based curriculum, contemplative and dialogic pedagogy, a faculty culture oriented toward virtue and mentorship, and deep parent/community partnership. Public materials repeatedly emphasize Christ-centered formation, Biblical study, Latin and the liberal arts, Socratic-style inquiry in the secondary program, and active parental involvement as co-educators. This convergence supports a CLASSICAL overall classification with clear, category-consistent evidence. Evidence excerpts drawn from FCA’s site and related materials show clear signals in each category, including explicit Trinity-era classical pedagogy anchors (grammar, logic, rhetoric; Great Books/Great Conversation; Socratic dialogue; memory work with singing/recitation), and a culture of parental partnership and communal formation. See below for category-by-category keyword analyses and ratings. The aggregate assessment is CLASSICAL.

- As an aggregate, FCA is CLASSICAL.

- What would move the needle? No major needle-moving changes are required given the current findings. If anything, minor enhancements could emphasize explicit language around “Great Books” and formal Socratic seminars in more grade levels beyond what is already present, but the existing materials already strongly support classical aims (see Category 4 for explicit Socratic/dialogic pedagogy in the secondary). For a SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL result, you would seek more uniform articulation of all six element indicators in public-facing materials; FCA already satisfies those indicators in most cases. No such needle-moving changes are required for this school to be CLASSICAL.

- The school’s public-facing history and mission materials frame education as formation within a Christian worldview, including references to image bearers of Christ and a relationship between educators and parents in a Christian frame.

Positive/Negative keyword analysis

- Positive keywords present: Bible, Christ-centered, LORD, Creator, theology, Christian, God, Gospel framing. These reflect Transcendental Moral Realism and Grounded Theology signals in the Six Elements framework. Approximately 5–6 distinct positive signals are present across materials.
- Negative keywords: 0 clear instances of secular or relativistic framing; no explicit values-neutral or secular-neutral language identified in the public materials reviewed.

Why this rating

- FCA’s materials foreground a transcendent moral framework anchored in Christian theology, worship/communal practice implied via Bible-centered formation, and explicit Christ-centered language. These align with the Positive Keywords for Transcendental Moral Realism, Grounded Theology, and Liturgical/Communal Practice in the Six Elements rubric. The absence of secular/neutral framing strengthens the CLASSICAL rating. The secondary materials show explicit theological integration through Bible/studies, and a liturgical/communal rhythm (celebrations in Grade School narratives, etc.). Therefore, this category is CLASSICAL.

Evidence citations: Christ-centered formation text; Bible-centered grammar school narrative; Our Story/mission language.

Why this rating

This aligns with the Pedagogy element’s Positive Keywords and signals a CLASSICAL rating.
