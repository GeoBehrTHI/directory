# Classicality Audit: Candeo Schools (candeoschools.com)

## Executive Summary
- Overall Classification: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL
- Why: Candeo Schools explicitly centers its mission on “growing wisdom and virtue” and the pursuit of “truth, goodness, and beauty,” aligns its curriculum with the Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric), teaches Latin (4–8), and references “recitation,” “wonder,” and “Western Civilization.” It integrates Core Knowledge with Hillsdale’s K–12 “classical and American” curriculum. However, the site provides no evidence of theological grounding or liturgical life, and only partial evidence of the distinctively classical pedagogies (Socratic seminars, chanting/singing, restful scholé) and faculty/parent formation expected in the tradition. These gaps keep the school from a fully CLASSICAL designation.    
- Website used: https://candeoschools.com/

## What would move the needle (to CLASSICAL)
- Add explicit theological grounding and liturgical life (if appropriate to the school’s governance model), or at minimum, a principled statement of metaphysics that grounds the transcendentals beyond civic virtue; integrate chapel, morning prayer, hymnody, or a coherent sacred aesthetic if faith‑based (or a clear metaphysical posture if not) to fully satisfy Category 1. 
- Make pedagogy explicit: publish descriptions of Socratic seminars (upper grades), formal debates, orations/capstones, and restful scholé practices; in lower school, name chanting/singing and routine recitation techniques. 
- Publish a “Portrait of a Teacher” and “Portrait of a Graduate,” and describe faculty formation in classical pedagogy (“teacher as mentor,” “living curriculum”). 
- Offer parent formation: classical education reading groups, seminars, or workshops so parents can share the school’s classical philosophy, not only policies.
