Overall Classification: CLASSICAL

## Executive Summary

Caritas Christian Academy Az 85244 presents a coherent Christian classical education profile across the six classical-school categories. Public-facing materials emphasize a Christ-centered, theology-infused approach to learning, a purpose oriented toward formation and the cultivation of virtue, and a curriculum built around the liberal arts (Trivium and Quadrivium), Great Books, Latin, and a clearly staged pedagogy (Socratic discussion in the upper grades, rhetoric and public speaking, and a strong emphasis on memory-work and liturgical culture). There is explicit attention to family partnership, church community, and a hybrid model that engages parents as co-educators in the educational project. Taken together, the school aligns strongly with classical education norms and ideals, showing robust evidence of both religious metaphysical foundations and a formation-first educational aims that pervade curriculum and pedagogy.

Key textual signals include: explicit Christ-centered stance and biblical/theological integration across disciplines; strong emphasis on the Great Conversation and Great Books; explicit Trivium and Quadrivium structure with Latin; Socratic/logic pedagogy in middle grades and rhetoric in upper grades; a visible “living curriculum” conveyed through faculty stories and a staff-centric, virtue-focused description; covenanted partnership with parents and a welcoming stance toward family involvement; and a hybrid model that ties on-campus instruction to parent-led remote days, all framed within a Christian worldview. These signals are consistently documented in Caritas Academy’s site materials (e.g., references to The Great Conversation, The Trivium, Great Books, Latin, Socratic discussions, and a strong emphasis on virtue and biblical worldview) and reinforced by admissions, curriculum highlights, faculty bios, and governance language.
