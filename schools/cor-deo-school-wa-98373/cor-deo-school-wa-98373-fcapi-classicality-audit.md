## Executive Summary

Cor Deo School (Tacoma, WA, 98373) presents itself as a distinctly classical Christian school, with clear commitments to the Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric), a Great-Conversation orientation, Scripture-informed pedagogy, and active parent partnership. Public materials consistently emphasize truth, goodness, and beauty as real and knowable, a Christ-centered mission, and formation of character through a classical liberal-arts framework. Across six categories, the school demonstrates strong alignment with classical aims in religious foundations, purpose, curriculum, pedagogy, and community involvement. The only category with more tentative/partial alignment is Faculty, where the public materials reference administrators and staff and the broadened idea of “faculty and staff” but provide less explicit, text-level portrayal of the faculty as the living curriculum or as archetypes of virtuous teaching beyond standard roles. Overall classification: CLASSICAL.

Key insights:
- Religious and metaphysical foundations are strongly evident through Christ-centered language, biblical instruction, and explicit references to truth, goodness, and beauty as real ideals.
- Purpose and goal articulate formation, virtue, wisdom, and participation in the Great Conversation, with explicit emphasis on human excellence and timeless questions.
- Curriculum is overtly classical (grammar/logic/rhetoric), the trivium, and the Great Books/Great Conversation orientation, plus explicit attention to history, classical languages (Latin), and memorization/chanting traditions.
- Pedagogy foregrounds both contemplative/restful elements and rigorous Socratic-seminar-like engagement in upper grades, with lower grades engaging memory-focused, singing/chanting routines.
- Community and parental partnership are explicit, with language about parent involvement, covenant-like relations, and access for visits and engagement.
- The aggregate evidence supports a CLASSICAL ranking across categories, with Element 5 (Faculty) being the mildest departure from the strongest classical alignment.
