## Executive Summary

Aristoi Classical Academy Cypress (ACA Cypress) presents itself as a tuition-free public charter that explicitly frames its offering as classical liberal arts education for grades Kindergarten through 10th with a stated mission to form virtuous citizens. The school heavily markets classical language around the grammar–logic–rhetoric (Trivium) tradition and emphasizes critical thinking, persuasive writing, and a love of learning. Public materials show clear alignment with several core elements of classical education—most notably the liberal arts framework and a pedagogy that foregrounds structured language disposition (grammar, logic, rhetoric) and memory via recitation-like practice in lower grades, with Socratic-style inquiry and rhetoric in upper grades implied by the naming of “Logic & Rhetoric” and related weekly announcements.

However, ACA Cypress does not consistently foreground theological grounding, liturgical or communal religious practices, or explicit Great Books/the Great Conversation language in its publicly accessible materials. The content leans toward formation through classical disciplines and virtue-informed aims, but it does not closely articulate theology, liturgy, or explicit theological grounding, nor does it consistently articulate a distinctive, written “great books” curriculum. On balance, ACA Cypress most closely aligns with a classical orientation, but with notable gaps in explicit articulation of several pillars (especially religious metaphysics and explicit Great Books emphasis). Consequently, the school is best characterized as SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL overall, with particular strength in curriculum structure (Trivium) and formation aims, and room to stronger demonstrate the full six-element classical rubric in a few categories.
