## Executive Summary

Our Lady of Lourdes Catholic Classical School (Denver, CO, 80210) presents a robust Classical orientation anchored in Catholic, theologically integrated education. Public materials consistently foreground Catholic identity, the Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric), and a vision of education that aims at forming virtue, wisdom, and a love of truth, beauty, and goodness. The school explicitly links character formation to its religious mission, integrates liturgical life and communal practices, and highlights parental partnership as a core feature. Pedagogy leans into Socratic dialogue, memorization/recitation, and a memory-work orientation in early grades, with upper grades featuring Socratic seminars and rhetoric development. Faculty are portrayed as models of virtue and living exemplars within a relational, community-centered culture. Parental partnership and family formation are repeatedly emphasized, with explicit programming to educate and involve parents as co-educators.

Overall assessment: CLASSICAL. Across the six categories, public materials consistently align with the core indicators of a classical school as defined in the Six Essential Elements rubric: a religious/metaphysical foundation, formation-focused goals, a curriculum built on the liberal arts and Great Books/tradition, a pedagogy that balances contemplative and dialectic methods, a faculty culture anchored in virtue and mentorship, and an active, covenantal partnership with parents and the broader faith community.
