## ABSTRACT

Classical Consortium Academy (CCA) demonstrates strong alignment with classical education principles across all six rubric categories. Religious & Metaphysical Foundations are anchored in a Christian worldview with explicit Truth-God-Beauty language and a formal Statement of Faith. The School Purpose & Goal centers on formation, virtue, and wisdom, prioritizing the formation of character over mere information. The Curriculum centers on the Seven Liberal Arts (Trivium and Quadrivium), Omnibus/Great Books, Latin, and integrated history-literature-philosophy strands. Pedagogy blends contemplative and memory-based practices (Scholé, chant/memorization) with Socratic dialogue and rhetoric in upper grades. Faculty are portrayed as living exemplars and mentors who form the living curriculum, while Parents & Community are integrally involved as co-educators and partners in learning. Public materials consistently align with the classical canon (great books, liberal arts, and virtuous formation) and declare a unified mission of forming wise, virtuous, and faithful graduates. Overall, the school is CLASSICAL.
