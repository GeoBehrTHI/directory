## Executive Summary

Classical Christian Academy (Id 83858), operating as Coeur d’Alene Classical Christian School (CCA Classical) in North Idaho, presents a strong classical Christian framework embedded in scripture, covenant with parents, and a distinctly Catholic–Protestant–evangelical style of theology typical of many classical Christian schools. Public-facing materials emphasize a Christ-centered, Biblical worldview, a partnership with families, and a classical pedagogy that includes Latin study and a broad, integrated curriculum. Evidence from the crawl content shows explicit statements about a Biblical foundation, a Statement of Faith, and a mission to form character and wisdom alongside intellectual growth. Overall, the school achieves a robust Classical profile in most categories, with Category 3 (Curriculum) and Category 4 (Pedagogy) showing partial alignment due to fewer explicit references to some canonical classical elements (e.g., explicit “Great Books” framing or detailed Socratic/Scholé practices on the site). In aggregate, the school is CLASSICAL, with some Category 3/4 nuances that are "Somewhat Classical" rather than fully explicit in every Classical criterion.

Key takeaways by category (brief):

- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: CLASSICAL. Strong Christ-centered posture, Statement of Faith, biblical worldview, and theological framing are clear across site content.
- Purpose and Goal: CLASSICAL. Emphasis on formation, virtue, wisdom, and living a life aligned with truth, goodness, and beauty; formation over mere information is evident.
- Curriculum: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL. Classical signals are present (Latin, classical pedagogy, integrated curriculum), but explicit Great Books/Great Conversation and full articulation of the Seven Liberal Arts appear less overt in public pages.
- Pedagogy: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL. The program is described as classical and in line with perennial pedagogy, but explicit, high-visibility Socratic seminars or Scholé-focused routines are not consistently detailed on the site.
- Faculty: CLASSICAL. Faculty are portrayed as mentors and living curriculum; leadership (e.g., Principal Michael Howard) is highlighted, with teachers framed as central to the pedagogy.
- Parents & Community: CLASSICAL. Strong, explicit emphasis on parent partnership, covenant with families, and parent involvement; multiple pages invite parent engagement and visits.
