## ABSTRACT

Chesterton Academy of St. Scholastica (CASS), Fort Wayne, IN 46825, demonstrates a robust CLASSICAL profile across all six categories. Religious and Metaphysical Foundations are grounded in Catholic theology, liturgical life, and an incarnational educational framework (evident in daily Mass and Catholic identity; Six Hallmarks emphasize Incarnational environments). The School Purpose and Goal centers formation and virtue, with a Three Pillars Model (Intellect, Character, Sacred Life) and explicit aims at truth, goodness, and beauty. The Curriculum is explicitly liberal-arts driven (grammar, logic, rhetoric; integration across history, literature, philosophy, theology, languages) with a classical, Great-Books-informed orientation within an integrated framework. Pedagogy emphasizes Socratic Seminar and dialectic, memory work, and a contemplative yet rigorous approach consistent with scholé. Faculty are presented as model teachers and mentors who form the living curriculum and who embody classical pedagogy. Finally, Parents & Community involvement is central, with active parent engagement, open campus opportunities, shadow days, and alignment with a broader Chesterton School Network ethos of family collaboration.

Six-category keyword analysis, grounded in the Six Essential Elements of a Classical School framework, shows abundant positive indicators across all six domains, with negative indicators effectively absent. The school consistently references truth, goodness, beauty; formation and virtue; the trivium/quadrivium and Great Books; Socratic pedagogy; mentors in the faculty; and active parental partnership. The aggregate assessment therefore supports CLASSICAL classification for Chesterton Academy of St. Scholastica.
