## ABSTRACT

OSLS (Our Savior Lutheran School, Grand Rapids, MI) demonstrates a classical Christian program rooted in Lutheran theology, liturgical practice, and a formation-focused telos. The school’s public materials emphasize daily Word of God, scripture-based catechesis, and liturgy; a curriculum built on the liberal arts (grammar, logic, rhetoric) and Great Books within a Western-civilization framework; pedagogy that blends Scholé/restful learning with Socratic dialogue and rhetoric; a faculty culture centered on virtue and mentorship; and a robust partnership with parents. Across six classical criteria, OSLS is classified as CLASSICAL: Religious and Metaphysical Foundations are explicitly theology-centered; School Purpose and Goal emphasize formation, virtue, and truth; Curriculum centers on Liberal Arts and Great Books; Pedagogy integrates contemplative and dialectical methods; Faculty described as virtuous guides within a living curriculum; and Parents & Community are engaged as co-partners in formation. The aggregate assessment is CLASSICAL.

Key evidentiary anchors include: daily worship and Scripture-focused instruction and Lutheran confessions; formation-focused language (virtue, wisdom, human flourishing); seven liberal arts and Great Books framing; explicit Socratic and rhetorical pedagogy; teacher-mentored formation; and explicit parental partnership and openness to visits.
