## Executive Summary

St Gabriel Classical Academy (SGCA) presents a robust classical-Catholic educational model anchored in Catholic doctrine, liturgical life, and a clearly articulated Trivium-based curriculum. Public-facing materials repeatedly emphasize a dual commitment to faith and classical liberal arts, with explicit references to Catholic identity, daily Mass, sacraments, and a formal mission that foregrounds faith, reason, and virtue. The school’s curriculum is organized around grammar, logic, and rhetoric stages, with attention to Great Books-adjacent texts, history study integrated across the curriculum, and a strong emphasis on memory work, chanting, and liturgical life as part of pedagogy. Family partnership and servant leadership in the community are also foregrounded, including a Family Service Investment Fee and explicit parent involvement opportunities.

Overall classification: CLASSICAL

Key insights:

- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations are clearly articulated through a Catholic identity, token statements of faith, daily Mass, liturgical life, and catechetical formation. This aligns strongly with the Positive Keyword indicators for Transcendental Truth-Realism, Theology, Liturgical Practice, and Sacramental worldview.
- The School Purpose and Goal centers on formation of virtue and wisdom, not vocational preparation alone, with explicit language about faith, reason, virtue, and human flourishing.
- Curriculum is explicitly framed around liberal arts-informed structures (grammar-logic-rhetoric), integration of history across disciplines, Great Books/primary-source orientation, and an emphasis on awe and wonder.
- Pedagogy combines contemplative elements (Scholé), memory-based practices, and Socratic/seminar-style discourse at the upper levels, including rhetoric training and public speaking.
- Faculty is positioned as mentors and living curriculum, with emphasis on character formation and exemplary models.
- Parents & Community are positioned as partners in formation, with a formal Family Service Investment Fee, open tours, and parent engagement opportunities.
