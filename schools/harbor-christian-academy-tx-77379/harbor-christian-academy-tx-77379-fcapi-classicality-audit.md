## Executive Summary

Harbor Christian Academy (HCA) in Houston’s urban Fifth Ward/Denver Harbor area presents a robust classical Christian program that aligns closely with the Six Essential Elements of a Classical School. Across all six categories, the school demonstrates clear elements of classical education: a Trivium-based curriculum integrated with Biblical worship, explicit emphasis on virtue and wisdom as aims of education, a history-and-literature-rich curriculum, Socratic and dialectic pedagogy in the upper grades, a visible living-faculty-and-community ethos, and a strong partnership with parents and the surrounding community. Public materials emphasize the end of education as glorifying God, formation of character, mastery of the liberal arts (grammar, logic, rhetoric), Great Books-like engagement with literature, and high levels of parental involvement through partnerships and open campus initiatives. The domain content consistently frames education as a sacred, communal endeavor anchored in Christian doctrine and liturgy, with explicit references to worship, prayer, and a daily Bible-centered culture. These signals collectively support a CLASSICAL rating across all six categories.

Key takeaways:
- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations are strongly reinforced by explicit statements about God, the Bible, worship, and the goal to glorify God.
- School Purpose and Goal are framed around formation, virtue, wisdom, and a telos that transcends vocational preparation.
- Curriculum centers on grammar, logic, rhetoric, a biblically informed humanities emphasis, Latin, and engaging with classic literature and historical study.
- Pedagogy combines contemplative/restful elements with Socratic dialogue, debates, and rhetoric-focused activities in the upper grades.
- Faculty is presented through a Christian, mentoring lens with visible teacher roles and a living-curriculum flavor via a Christian faculty.
- Parents & Community are integrated through partnerships, parent formation opportunities, and open access to engage with the school.

Evidence is drawn from Harbor Christian Academy’s public materials, including statements about the Trivium, daily worship, Latin and logic study, senior theses, and explicit parent partnerships. See the cited sources throughout for specifics.
