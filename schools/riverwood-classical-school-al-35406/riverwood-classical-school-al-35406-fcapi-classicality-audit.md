## Executive Summary

Riverwood Classical School (operating as Omnia Classical School in the public materials) presents a strong Classical Christian profile. Its public materials repeatedly frame education as formation grounded in a Christian worldview, with a clearly articulated classical method that centers the Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric) and seeks integration across disciplines. The school emphasizes parental partnership, a covenantal community, and a teacher-stewardship model, with explicit language about truth, goodness, and beauty shaping learning. It also references accreditation and membership in classical education networks, and it provides curricula and diploma structures aligned with a classical Christian vision. Taken together, the school demonstrates a comprehensive Classical framework across foundations, goals, curriculum, pedagogy, faculty, and parent/community engagement. Evidence draws from the Omnia/Classical materials (Educational Philosophy and Distinctives; Christian/Covenant Orientation; Master Curriculum materials; Faculty pages; and Parent/Community content) as well as the public-facing classically oriented documents. Key sources include language about God’s truth as the core of learning, biblical worldview, the integration of classical subjects, and the Trivium-focused curriculum, all of which align with the Six Essential Elements framework. Citations reflect the public materials that substantiate these observations.
