## ABSTRACT

Riverwood Classical School/Omnia Classical School presents a robust CLASSICAL profile across all six categories. Religious and Metaphysical Foundations are rooted in a Biblical worldview and theology-driven learning; Purpose and Goal emphasize formation, virtue, and truth through a Christian classical framework. Curriculum centers on the Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric) and classical disciplines with master curriculum documentation indicating K–12 progression and Latin study. Pedagogy includes Socratic dialogue and rhetoric training, with a restive, contemplative element in early education and a seminary-style discourse approach in upper grades. Faculty are presented as living curriculum with mentorship roles and a covenantal community ethos, while Parents & Community are oriented toward partnership, parental involvement, and open engagement with school activities. The public materials demonstrate alignment with the Six Essential Elements’ positive indicators (transcendentals and formation) and display minimal or no negative indicators, supporting a CLASSICAL rating overall. The aggregate assessment across categories yields CLASSICAL, with the strongest signals in Curriculum, Pedagogy, and Religious Foundations, and equally strong signals in Faculty, Parents & Community, and Purpose.

Key sources include the Educational Philosophy and Distinctives documents and the Christian/classical framing (God’s truth; biblical worldview) as well as Master Curriculum materials showing dialectic/rhetoric progression and Latin study, and Parental Partnership content.
