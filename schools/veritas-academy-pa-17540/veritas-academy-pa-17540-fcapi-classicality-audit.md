## Executive Summary

Veritas Academy, located in Lancaster County, PA (zip 17540), demonstrates a strong classical Christian identity across its public materials. The school emphasizes the Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric), Great Books, a truth-goodness-beauty teleology, and formation of character within a Christian framework. Public pages repeatedly frame education as a virtue- and wisdom-forming enterprise anchored in Scripture, theology, and Christian anthropology, with explicit references to family partnership, in loco parentis, and liturgical-style communal rhythms (music, worshipful tone, and shared practices). Financial aid and accessibility are presented as priorities to broaden access to a classical Christian model.
