## Executive Summary

Second Baptist School University-Model (SBSUM) West demonstrates a robust classical orientation across most of the Six Essential Elements of Classical Education. Public materials from SBSUM West emphasize a Christ-centered, biblically grounded education that adopts the University-Model structure framed by classical grammar–logic–rhetoric pedagogy. Evidence across the site highlights virtues such as truth, goodness, beauty, reverence, wonder, and the pursuit of wisdom, alongside explicit commitments to formation, liberal arts, and a shared parent-school partnership. The curriculum foregrounds the Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric), while pedagogy includes contemplative (Scholé) elements and Socratic dialogue in the high-school context; fine arts are integrated through music, choir, drama, visual arts, and arts pedagogy anchored in a Christian worldview. The faculty reflect a structured leadership model (Head of School, Dean of Academics, etc.), and parents are explicitly positioned as partners and co-educators, with active opportunities for parent formation and campus visits.

Overall classification: CLASSICAL.

Rationale: The school coherently aligns with the core themes of religious foundations, formation-oriented aims, Classical Curriculum (grammar–logic–rhetoric), and a pedagogy that includes Socratic inquiry and contemplative learning, combined with a mission-driven faculty and strong parent/community partnership. The primary area where SBSUM West shows more limited explicit articulation (relative to the strongest classical exemplars) is the explicit call for Great Books/Great Conversation within the public materials; the curriculum clearly centers on the Trivium and classical arts rather than a stated, program-wide emphasis on Great Books. This leads to Element 3 being the relatively weaker link, i.e., SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL for Curriculum, while all other categories trend CLASSICAL. See category-by-category sections for the detailed keyword analyses and evidence.
