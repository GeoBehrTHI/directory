# ABSTRACT

SBSUM West presents a coherent Classical Education model embedded in a University-Model framework. Religious and Metaphysical Foundations are strong (Christ-centered, gospel-centered, Word of God; reverence toward truth, beauty, and virtue). The School Purpose and Goal centers on formation, virtue, wisdom, and a biblical worldview, aligning with Classical aims. The Curriculum foregrounds grammar–logic–rhetoric, with explicit Trivium-based pedagogy and language of classical arts; however, explicit Great Books/Great Conversation emphasis is less documented in public content, resulting in a SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL rating for Curriculum. Pedagogy blends contemplative (Scholé) learning with Socratic dialogue and rhetoric in upper grades, consistent with classical practice. Faculty are presented as a structured leadership team (Head of School, Deans, Academic Coordinators) within the living-curriculum paradigm. Parents & Community are positioned as essential partners—parents co-teach and participate in campus life, with a welcoming, open-campus policy.
