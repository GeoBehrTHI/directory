## ABSTRACT

Axios Jonesboro Christian Academy (Axios JCA) demonstrates a classical Christian orientation with strong CLASSICAL signals across multiple dimensions. The school presents itself as a private Christian institution embedding faith and classical education in its mission, curriculum, and pedagogy. Evidence includes explicit Christian framing (pursuit of Christ; Non Sibi; private Christian identity) and a classical framework (Latin, formal logic, classical subjects). Pedagogy emphasizes a balance of contemplative/restful elements with Socratic-style inquiry and rhetoric training, while faculty are described as living exemplars and mentors in a living curriculum. Parent involvement is framed as a core partnership, with open communication and co-teaching dynamics.

- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL, with strong Christian framing and virtue-oriented language; explicit liturgical practices are less foregrounded in public texts, though theological commitments underpin the model. Key signals: private Christian identity, pursuit of Christ, non sibi, truth/goodness/beauty framing.   
- Purpose and Goal: CLASSICAL, foregrounding formation, virtue, and telos-oriented development rather than sole job-preparation; emphasis on shaping leaders and a Christian mission.  
- Curriculum: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL, with explicit Latin and formal logic and a classical framework; Abeka core usage and classical subjects signal a classical orientation, but explicit Great Books language is less foregrounded in all public materials. 
- Pedagogy: CLASSICAL, with a blend of Socratic-style inquiry, memory-work, and a charged emphasis on questions and disciplined discourse; hands-on exploration and wonder are present.  
- Faculty: CLASSICAL, model teachers and living curriculum; mentoring culture; portrait of a teacher. 
- Parents & Community: CLASSICAL, strong partner-with-parents stance, co-teaching language, and open-communication culture. 

Overall classification: CLASSICAL

What would move Axios JCA toward fully CLASSICAL:
- Strengthen explicit liturgical/community signals (liturgical practices, communal worship, sacred music) to enhance Religious and Metaphysical Foundations.
- Surface explicit Great Books/Great Conversation language in curriculum descriptions and admissions materials to strengthen Curriculum.
- Continue to surface and articulate the full grammar/logic/rhetoric progression in public-facing content to deepen the Curriculum/Pedagogy fit.
