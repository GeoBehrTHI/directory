Overall, the school leans toward CLASSICAL in several components (notably Faculty and Parents/Community) but shows only partial alignment in others (notably explicit Great Books/Great Conversation emphasis and some specific pedagogical practices). The aggregate assessment lands in SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL.

This is strong evidence for a solid theological foundation within the classical rubric.
- Faculty: The site highlights a teacher-relationship model (small classes and long-term teacher relationships) and presents staff as a core part of the living curriculum, which aligns with classical norms of virtuous, exemplary mentors.
- Parents & Community: Parent involvement is foregrounded via a Parent Covenant and documented family-centered programming, a hallmark of classical culture in which parents are co-educators and partners in formation.

This yields a SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL signal.
- Curriculum: The curriculum is described as “all classical in nature” with explicit mention of Elemental Classical Science and other classical resources; however, the public materials do not consistently spell out Great Books/Great Conversation or the full Seven Liberal Arts framework in the same way as canonical classical schools. This result is SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL.
- Pedagogy: The public-facing pedagogy is framed around classical language (classical in nature) and multigrade structure, but explicit evidence of Socratic seminars, rigorous grammar-logic-rhetoric progression, or a sustained emphasis on Scholé-style contemplative practice is not consistently documented on the site. This yields a SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL reading.
- History and Great Books emphasis: While history and world-culture elements appear in curriculum notes, explicit Great Books/Great Conversation language is not consistently evident in the public materials; the category thus receives a SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL signal.
- Great Books/Scholé (memory, chanting, contemplative learning): Public materials do not show recurring chanting/memorization rhythms or Scholé-driven contemplative pedagogy as a core feature, limiting a full classical fit in this category. A SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL designation is warranted.
