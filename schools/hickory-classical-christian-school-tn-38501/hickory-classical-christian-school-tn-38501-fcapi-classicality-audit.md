## Executive Summary

Hickory Classical Christian School (Cookeville, TN 38501) presents a robust classical Christian program with clear alignment to the Six Essential Elements of Classical Education. Public materials emphasize a commitment to the liberal arts (Trivium and Quadrivium), Great Books/Great Conversation, Socratic and rhetoric-based pedagogy, a formal Statement of Faith, and a strong emphasis on virtue, character, and Christian formation. The school also highlights ACCS-certified teachers, a visible faculty community, and active parental engagement. Taken together, these signals place the school squarely in the CLASSICAL category across all six assessment domains.

Key signaling items include: the explicit aim for a classical, Christian education shared with Protestant Evangelical families, a formal Statement of Faith grounded in Holy Scripture and Trinitarian theology, the curricular scaffolding around grammar, logic, rhetoric with both Trivium and Quadrivium, and pedagogy that moves from singing and memory in the lower grades to Socratic discussion and rhetoric in the upper grades. The faculty section emphasizes living curriculum and teacher mentorship, and the parents/community section stresses partnerships with parents and open, ongoing engagement. Citations: Home and About pages with the school’s stated aims and religious framing; the Statement of Faith; the Our Approach pages detailing liberal arts, Trivium/Quadrivium, and pedagogical progression; and team bios confirming ACCS-certified faculty and a community approach. See sources cited inline below.
