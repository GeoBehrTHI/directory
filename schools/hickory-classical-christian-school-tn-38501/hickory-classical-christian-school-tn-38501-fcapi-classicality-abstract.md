## ABSTRACT

Hickory Classical Christian School is CLASSICAL across all six categories. Its Religious and Metaphysical Foundations are anchored by a Protestant Evangelical Christian framework and a formal Statement of Faith grounded in Holy Scripture and the Trinity. The School Purpose and Goal prioritize virtue formation and wisdom within a Christian formation model. Curriculum centers on the liberal arts, with explicit Trivium and Quadrivium structure and engagement in Great Conversation. Pedagogy blends contemplative, memory-forward approaches in the lower grades with Socratic, seminar-style discussion and rhetoric in the upper grades. Faculty are described as dedicated, ACCS-certified, and central to the living curriculum, and Parents & Community engagement is highlighted as a core principle through welcoming visits and visible partnerships. In sum, Hickory Classical Christian School demonstrates strong alignment with classical education principles, including virtue formation, a robust liberal arts curriculum, disciplined classical pedagogy, a living-faculty model, and active parental partnership, all anchored by a Christ-centered theological framework.
