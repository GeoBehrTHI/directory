## Executive Summary

Ambrose Academy (Manhattan, KS 66503) presents a clearly Christian, classical-leaning program with a strong emphasis on formation of virtue, wisdom, and a Christian worldview. Public materials emphasize Christ-centered discipleship, a theology-grounded curriculum, small class sizes, and active partnership with parents. The school demonstrates substantial alignment with classical education principles—especially in its stated goals, curriculum emphases (Latin, classic literature, theology, worldview, and a long historical timeline), and a governance/teacher formation approach that models virtue. The Pedagogy category shows partial alignment (the school highlights classical content and teacher training) but does not repeatedly and explicitly document core classical teaching modalities (e.g., Socratic seminars, scholé, or explicit Great Books/Great Conversation practices) in the public materials currently available, which places Pedagogy in the “Somewhat Classical” range rather than fully Classical. The aggregate posture across all six categories lands squarely in CLASSICAL, with one category requiring further explicit articulation to reach full alignment.

 Evidence shows a strong classical content focus and teacher training, plus small class size. However, explicit, repeated articulation of core classical pedagogy (Socratic seminars, scholé/restful learning, memorization/narration, or firm Great Books/Great Conversation practices) is not consistently documented in the public materials.  The faculty is presented through concrete personnel (e.g., 2nd Grade Teacher with classical educator training) and a culture of mentorship and character formation. The staff are described as seasoned, trained in classical pedagogy, and as living exemplars of the school’s ethos. - Element 6: School Parents & Community — CLASSICAL
 The school publicly emphasizes partnering with parents, parental formation, and open, ongoing parental involvement. Statements about co-teaching, parental primacy in formation, and invitations for families to engage align strongly with the classical model’s emphasis on a robust parent-school partnership. 

Overall aggregate classification: CLASSICAL
The Ambrose Academy public materials demonstrate robust Classical Christian alignment across Religion/Metaphysics, Purpose/Goal, Curriculum, Faculty, and Parents/Community. Pedagogy is the lone area with a need for more explicit articulation of established classical teaching methods to move from SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL to CLASSICAL in that category.

What would move the needle
- Pedagogy: Explicitly publish and emphasize the use of Socratic seminars, scholé days, and narration/dictation as standard upper-elementary/secondary practices, and connect these methods directly to student outcomes. Document an ongoing, school-wide pedagogy plan that links Great Books/Great Conversation with grade-level pedagogy.
- Great Books/Great Conversation: Publicly articulate a Great Books/Great Conversation framework, including sample reading lists, sequence, and how memory, narration, and discussion reinforce the curriculum—to strengthen Element 4 and Element 3 alignment.
- Liturgical/Communal Practices: If not already, provide more explicit references to liturgical or communal practices (e.g., morning prayers, liturgical calendar, chant or sacred music) within school routines to align with the Positive Indicators in the religious foundations and to reduce any ambiguity about liturgical life. 
- Documentation of Great Books/Scholé in Public Materials: Add a public-facing page detailing Great Books, paideia, and the Great Conversation as central pillars of the curriculum, with examples of text selections and how they connect to virtues and wisdom.
