## Executive Summary

Geneva Academy Roseburg (Geneva Academy OR 97470) presents a robust, classroom-centered model that aligns strongly with classical education and a Christian/catholic-liberal-arts heritage. Public materials from the Geneva site consistently describe a classical Christian mission, with explicit emphasis on faith-integrated learning, virtue formation, and the liberal arts tradition (grammar, logic, rhetoric) taught through a Great Books-oriented curriculum and socratic pedagogy. Evidence from mission statements, curriculum descriptions, and documented pedagogical practices indicates a comprehensive classical orientation across categories. The site likewise emphasizes parental involvement, a living faculty, and community engagement, all framed within a Christ-centered worldview.

Overall classification: CLASSICAL.

- Why this matters: Geneva Academy Roseburg clearly identifies itself as a classical Christian school, foregrounds faith-informed pedagogy and formation, and emphasizes canonical classical elements (Great Books, liberal arts triad, Socratic dialogue, capstone-style senior work, and a living-faculty model). The programmatic emphasis on virtue, wisdom, contemplation (scholé), and communal practices reinforces the alignment with classical education. The school’s public materials consistently cite formation and virtue as primary goals, not merely vocational preparation, supporting a CLASSICAL ranking across all six categories. For example, mission statements emphasize faith-integrated classical education and the formation of virtuous character, while curricular and pedagogical notes highlight classical tools like Great Books, grammar/logic/rhetoric, and Socratic methods.   

- Note on needle-mover: As a fully Classical rating, there is no mandatory “What Would Move the Needle” section required here.
