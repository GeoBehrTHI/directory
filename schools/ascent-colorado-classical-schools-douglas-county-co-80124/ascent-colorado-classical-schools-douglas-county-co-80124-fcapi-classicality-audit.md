## Executive Summary

Ascent Colorado Classical Schools – Douglas County (CD: 80124) demonstrates a robust classical-education orientation across all six rubric categories. Public-facing materials consistently foreground the liberal-arts core: a content-rich, Western-civilization-centered curriculum that integrates literature, history, philosophy, languages (notably Latin), and the arts; explicit emphasis on virtue and character formation; teacher-led instruction with traditional methods; and a clear partnership with families and communities to sustain a classical ethos. The school anchors its mission in forming minds and hearts toward truth, goodness, and beauty, and it operationalizes this through hallmarks like a Core Knowledge-based K–8 sequence, high regard for great books and original sources, and a governance commitment to Western-tradition pedagogy. Communal practices (opening ceremonies, Scholé-inspired rhythms, Constitution Day, pledges) and structures (House System; parent partnerships; Core Virtues) reinforce a liturgical-style cadence and shared tradition. The aggregate assessment across all categories is CLASSICAL.

Evidence for this conclusion is drawn from the school’s public-facing materials and policy documents, including explicit statements about truth/goodness/beauty, the Western-civilization focus, the liberal-arts Trivium/Quadrivium framework, Latin study, great books/common texts, and a pedagogy that combines Socratic elements with traditional teacher-led instruction, plus a strong emphasis on parental involvement and a civically virtuous culture. Therefore CLASSICAL is warranted.

There is also explicit mention of memory-focused recitation/memorization (as part of classical pedagogy) and the practice of Socratic discussion in upper grades. Scholé-inspired rhythms (restful learning) are also described. 
- The school’s model includes a robust emphasis on classic pedagogy, including direct instruction with Socratic elements, memorization/recitation, and a living-curriculum approach, all consistent with classical pedagogy. 
- Communal and ritual practices (Constitution Day, Scholé-oriented celebrations, a House System) contribute to a disciplined, tradition-based classroom culture that supports classical pedagogy. 

Positive/Negative Keyword Analysis

- Negative keywords: not evidenced in public materials (no primary emphasis on purely student-centered, project-based, or constructivist pedagogy dominating classroom practice). The materials emphasize classical methods over student-led-only approaches. 

Why this rating
- The pedagogy is clearly anchored in traditional/classical methods (teacher-led, Socratic inquiry, memorization/recitation, classical rhetoric development), reinforced by Scholé-inspired rhythm. This aligns with CLASSICAL rather than SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL or NOT CLASSICAL.
