# Classicality Audit: Austin Classical School

Website audited: https://austinclassical.org/ 

## Executive Summary
Overall classification: CLASSICAL

Austin Classical School (ACS) presents strong textual evidence of a classical, Christian identity across all six elements. Its public materials repeatedly emphasize truth, goodness, beauty; the cultivation of wisdom and virtue; Trivium-driven curriculum (grammar, logic, rhetoric), Latin, Great Books, and Socratic pedagogy; robust Christian Studies; daily chapel; and a collaborative “co‑teacher” partnership with parents. Collectively, these signal comprehensive alignment with the “Six Essential Elements of a Classical School” framework and its keyword indicators. Key examples include: “glorify Jesus Christ,” “wise, virtuous, and humble,” “Truth, Goodness, and Beauty,” “Trivium,” “memorization, recitation, and songs,” “Socratic method,” “Senior Thesis,” and “All School chapel.”     

Method note: The audit applies the “Six Essential Elements” keyword lexicon—positive and negative indicators for each category—to ACS’s published materials, tallying keyword hits and estimating coverage relative to the framework.
