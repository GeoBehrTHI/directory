## ABSTRACT
Overall classification: CLASSICAL

Most important insights:
- Religious/Theological core with daily communal worship: ACS integrates Scripture and theology (Christian Studies, exegesis, apologetics) and anchors campus life in daily chapel.  
- Purpose and telos are explicitly classical: “cultivate wisdom and virtue” directed by the true, good, and beautiful; not primarily “college prep.”  
- Curriculum is distinctly classical: Trivium, Latin, logic, rhetoric, Great Books, integrated humanities, and Senior Thesis.  
- Pedagogy reflects classical methods: chanting/recitation in lower grades; debate/discussion and Socratic method; rhetorical imitation and thesis defense.  
- Faculty framed as Christian exemplars and mentors; ongoing teacher training.  
- Parent partnership is robust via a collaborative “co‑teacher” model, structured communication, and chapel participation on tours.
