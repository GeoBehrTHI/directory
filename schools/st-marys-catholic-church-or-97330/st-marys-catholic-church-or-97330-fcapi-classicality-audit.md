## Executive Summary

St. Mary's Catholic Church (Corvallis, OR, 97330), operating Chesterton Academy of St. Andrew in Corvallis, OR, presents a strongly Catholic, classical educational model. Public-facing materials emphasize a Catholic intellectual tradition, a focus on truth, goodness, and beauty, and a pedagogy centered on Socratic inquiry, memorization, and a liberal-arts curriculum that includes Great Books and the Great Conversation. The school is explicit about its Catholic identity and liturgical rhythms, while also advertising a rigorous classical curriculum and Socratic seminars in the upper grades. Public materials also show parent leadership as a core organizational frame, and evidence of parental involvement through events like Information Nights, Shadow Days, and donations.

Overall classification: CLASSICAL.

Rationale: Across six categories, the school demonstrates substantial alignment with classical-education criteria, especially in Religious Foundations, Purpose/Goal, Curriculum, and Pedagogy, with strong alignment in Parents/Community and evident alignment in Faculty. Element 5 (Faculty) shows meaningful evidence but is slightly lighter than other categories due to fewer explicit textual claims about the faculty as “living curriculum” or portraits of teacher archetypes, though the site does present a “Meet Our Faculty” pathway and a clearly teacher-centric approach. Element 6 (Parents & Community) is solid (parent-led and open engagement), though some phrases (e.g., “co-teachers” phrasing) are not always explicit in the public text. The aggregate assessment remains CLASSICAL.
