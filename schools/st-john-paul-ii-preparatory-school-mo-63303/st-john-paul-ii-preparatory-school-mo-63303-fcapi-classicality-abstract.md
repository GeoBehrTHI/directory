# ABSTRACT

St John Paul II Preparatory School (MO 63303) demonstrates a strong CLASSICAL orientation across most of the classical-school criteria, anchored by a distinctly Catholic formation and a liberal-arts curriculum centered on the trivium, Great Books, and Western-Christian civilization. The school’s public-facing materials reveal a clear commitment to formation, virtue, and truth-realities, with liturgical practices and a robust parental-partnership model embedded in the school’s hybrid approach. The curriculum and faculty messaging align with classical expectations, and the Parents & Community category shows a well-developed system for parental involvement, including Parents As Co-Educators (P.A.C.E.). The Pedagogy category is the one area with slightly lesser explicit documentation of full classical teaching modalities (e.g., explicit Socratic seminars in public content), resulting in a SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL designation for Element 4, while all other categories meet or exceed the classical criteria. Taken together, the school earns a GENERAL CLASSICAL classification, supported by substantial textual evidence across Catholic formation, classical curriculum, Great Books, and a strong family-centered learning community.

Key evidence and terms cited:

- Classical education framework with Great Books and the trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric).
- Catholic formation, liturgy, Mass, adoration, confession, and magisterial alignment.
- Mission-focused formation, virtue, wisdom, and the pursuit of truth, goodness, and beauty.
- Curriculum details (Latin, history/Western civilization, Great Books).
- Faculty as living curriculum and mentors; collaborative culture.
- Family partnerships and parental co-education through P.A.C.E. and hybrid model.
