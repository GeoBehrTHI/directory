## ABSTRACT

Covenant Classical Academy NC 27909 is evaluated as CLASSICAL across all six categories: Religious and Metaphysical Foundations, School Purpose and Goal, Curriculum, Pedagogy, Faculty, and Parents & Community. The school presents a Christ-centered, biblically grounded education rooted in covenant theology and the Five Solas, with daily Bible integration, creedal affirmations (Apostles’ Creed, Nicene Creed), and liturgical practices in its communal life. The curriculum follows the Trivium (Grammar, Logic, Rhetoric) and centers on Great Books and the Great Conversation, with history integrated across subjects and classical languages (Latin/Greek) taught. Pedagogy blends contemplative, scholé-oriented approaches with Socratic seminars and vigorous rhetoric training (including senior theses). Faculty are depicted as mentors and living curriculum, and parents are positioned as co-teachers fostering an intentional partnership with the school and church. Across the six categories, Covenant Classical Academy demonstrates strong, coherent alignment with classical education principles integrated within a robust Christian framework, yielding a general CLASSICAL classification.
