## ABSTRACT

Heritage Oak School (Tehachapi, CA 93561) demonstrates strong alignment with classical Christian education across the six essential elements. The Religious and Metaphysical Foundations are anchored in biblical formation, Word of God, and liturgical life (chapel, gospel-informed mission). The School Purpose and Goal emphasize Bildung, virtue, wisdom, and paideia as the telos of education. The Curriculum centers on Liberal Arts via the Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric), Great Books/Great Conversation, history-as-Western civilization framing, and memory-based learning. Pedagogy integrates contemplative, memory-based lower-years methods with Socratic seminars and rhetoric in upper grades. Faculty are portrayed as living exemplars and mentors who model virtue and form the living curriculum, while Parents & Community are positioned as co-educators partnering in formation.
