## Executive Summary

Heritage Oak School (Tehachapi, CA 93561) presents itself as a distinctly classical Christian school rooted in a biblical worldview, the Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric), and a Great Books-oriented pedagogy. Public materials repeatedly emphasize a mission of forming hearts and minds to be “all for Christ,” a commitment to classical liberal arts education, and a policy framework built around a Portrait of a Teacher and a Portrait of a Parent. The school foregrounds features typical of classical education: memory work and singing in the early years; Socratic discussion and rhetoric in the upper grades; engagement with Great Books and the Great Conversation; and a pedagogy anchored in a living curriculum taught by faculty who model virtue. The school also highlights strong parental partnership (in loco parentis), parent formation activities, and open campus opportunities. Evidence from multiple public materials shows explicit alignment with classical education signals (grammar/logic/rhetoric, Great Books, Paideia, memorization, Socratic seminars) as well as robust religious foundations (Word of God, Bible-based instruction, Statement of Faith, chapel). Taken together, Heritage Oak School aligns strongly with classical education principles across all six categories of the Classical rubric.
