## Executive Summary

John Paul the Great Academy (JPG) in Lafayette, LA presents a robust classical-Catholic framework across its public materials. The school identifies itself as a PK-12 independent Catholic school delivering a rigorous classical curriculum anchored in the Roman Catholic faith, with explicit reference to Trivium and Quadrivium, Great Books/Great Conversation, and a strong emphasis on the family as the “Domestic Church.” It integrates liturgical life (Mass and chapel) with academic formation and highlights a governance structure (Board of Trustees, Parent Advisory Committee) that positions parents as active stakeholders and co-educators. This combination—Catholic theology and liturgy, classical curriculum, emphasis on virtue and wisdom, and clear family partnership—aligns with classical-school criteria in multiple dimensions. Evidence drawn from JPG’s public materials shows direct statements about Catholic formation, great-books-esque engagement with Western civilization, Trivium/Quadrivium, and a pedagogy that blends memory work, chant, and communal liturgy with rigorous intellectual work.   

Overall classification by category (based on keyword analysis and alignment with the Six Essential Elements) tends to be CLASSICAL across four of six categories, with Element 4 (Pedagogy) showing the strongest signs of CLASSICALNESS but not uniformly explicit in all required pedagogical forms in the public narrative. The aggregate assessment is CLASSICAL.
