## ABSTRACT

Veritas Christian Academy presents a public profile that is clearly Classical or strongly Classical in its Christian, Trivium-based curriculum and its Christ-centered, liturgical orientation. The site emphasizes Intelligent Faith, Biblical Christianity, and a classical Trivium, indicating robust Religious and Metaphysical Foundations as well as a Curriculum aligned to the liberal arts tradition. However, public-facing materials do not fully enumerate or demonstrate the complete six-element classical framework (especially explicit Great Books/Great Conversation, explicit Socratic pedagogy, and formal Parent & Community partnership language). The keyword- indicators from the Six Essential Elements align strongly with a Classical orientation in several domains, but not all, creating an overall assessment of SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL for Veritas Christian Academy.

- Strengths across the six classical elements:
  - Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: Clear Christ-centered script and liturgical signals (e.g., Lessons & Carols). 
  - Curriculum: Explicit Trivium-based approach mentioned in public content. 
  - Formation/Character orientation through formation language (though explicit articulation of telos and Great Conversation is less visible publicly). 

- Gaps to address to move toward CLASSICAL:
  - Publish explicit Great Books/Great Conversation and Quadrivium alignment in public materials. 
  - Publicly articulate a defined telos across disciplines and provide a formal curriculum map.
  - Publish explicit pedagogical practices (Socratic seminars, scholé, memorization/recitation) and a formal portrait of the teacher as living curriculum.
  - Build a public Parent & Community program, detailing parent partnerships and opportunities for parent education and involvement.

Citations: Primary evidence from Veritas site (Intelligent Faith, Trivium, Biblical Christianity, liturgical event) and the Six Elements lexicon for indicators and signs.  

End of ABSTRACT.
