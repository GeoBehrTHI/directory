# ABSTRACT

Wilberforce is assessed as CLASSICAL across all six categories. The school’s public materials repeatedly emphasize a distinctly Christian, classical framework, with explicit references to formation (not information), virtue cultivation, and the Great Books tradition manifested through a grammar–logic–rhetoric progression. Pedagogy includes memory work, recitation, and dialogue at the upper levels, with lower grades engaging in collaborative, experiential, and liturgical forms of learning. Faculty are portrayed as living exemplars of virtue, mentorship, and shared inquiry, while the community engages with parents as co-educators through the PTF and other avenues. Taken together, these elements align Wilberforce with a robust classical-education model rather than a secular or purely skills-based approach. The aggregate assessment is CLASSICAL.
