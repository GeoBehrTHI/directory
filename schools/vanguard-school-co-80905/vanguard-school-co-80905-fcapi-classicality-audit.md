## Executive Summary

The Vanguard School (Colorado Springs, CO, 80905) (Colorado Springs) presents a clearly classical orientation in its public materials. The curriculum centers on a classical liberal arts framework, Latin study, and a course of study that aligns with great-books tendencies and a traditional, character-formation emphasis. Public materials emphasize virtue, character development, and leadership, and they describe an academically rigorous program designed to prepare students for college within a classical paradigm. Parental involvement is actively encouraged, and the faculty are portrayed as virtuous mentors and living exemplars in a community that values discipline and civility. While the school foregrounds classical aims in curriculum, pedagogy, and community life, explicit theological or liturgical foundations are not prominently stated in the publicly available materials.

Overall classification (based on the Six Essential Elements rubric): CLASSICAL. The strongest alignments are in Curriculum, Faculty, and Parents & Community, with notable, but less explicit, alignment in Religious/Metaphysical Foundations and Pedagogy. The School Purpose/Goal and Curriculum demonstrate robust classical aims; Pedagogy shows classical practices alongside some modern approaches; Faculty and Community are clearly oriented toward a classical, virtue-centric model.
