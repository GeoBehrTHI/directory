# Austin Classical School — Classicality Audit

School website used: https://austinclassical.org/ 

Executive Summary
- Overall classification: CLASSICAL
- Why: The school publicly centers its mission on “glorify[ing] Jesus Christ,” partners “with families to cultivate wise, virtuous, and humble students,” and explicitly frames learning around truth, goodness, and beauty using the Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric), Latin, great books, debate/discussion, and thesis defense—strong alignment with the “Six Essential Elements” positive indicators and lexicon for classical schools    .  
- Highlights:  
  - Religious and metaphysical foundations are explicit (Christ-centered, Bible, theology; TGB language)  .  
  - Purpose centers on formation in wisdom and virtue (not college/career prep), with “eternal purpose” emphasis  .  
  - Curriculum reflects the Trivium, Latin, great books, and Western tradition  .  
  - Pedagogy includes memory work, chanting/songs, debate/discussion, and rhetoric/thesis defense .  
  - Parents & community partnership is structurally central (collaborative “co-teacher parent” model, office hours, tours)  .  
  - Faculty evidence is strong but less explicit than other areas; language about teachers “helping form the souls of our children” and ACCS accreditation still indicate alignment  .
- Method: The audit follows the “Six Essential Elements of a Classical School” keyword indicators (positive/negative) and the classicality lexicon (truth, goodness, beauty; Trivium; Socratic/debate; chanting; etc.) to quantify hits and coverage before scoring each category  .

Note: “Positive” and “Negative” terms below are taken from the Six Essential Elements indicators and the lexicon; presence/absence and frequency inform the category scores, per instructions  .

4) Rating: CLASSICAL
