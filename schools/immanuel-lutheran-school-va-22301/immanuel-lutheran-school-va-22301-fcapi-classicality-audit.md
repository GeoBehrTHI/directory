## Executive Summary

Immanuel Lutheran School (ILS) in Alexandria, VA (ZIP 22301) presents a robust classical-Christian program that aligns strongly with the core features of classical education as defined by the Six Essential Elements rubric. Public materials describe a Christ-centered, Lutheran, liberal-arts oriented program with explicit emphasis on Latin, logic, rhetoric, history, great-books-like engagement, and a Socratic/ dialectical pedagogy in the upper grades. The school emphasizes formation of character (virtue, wisdom, faith) over vocational training, fosters a liturgical and communal life, and actively partners with families through a robust Parent-Teacher League and structured parental involvement. Across six categories, the public materials consistently indicate CLASSICAL orientation, with clear signals in religious/metaphysical foundations, educational purpose, curriculum, pedagogy, faculty, and parent/community engagement. The aggregate classification for Immanuel Lutheran School is CLASSICAL.

Key evidence highlights include: a stated commitment to a classical, Christian, Lutheran education with low-tech, character-centered formation; explicit references to Latin (3rd grade) and logic (5th grade), and a liberal-arts emphasis; the presence of Socratic inquiry, dialectic work, and opportunities for oral presentations and speeches in upper grades; a liturgical life with Matins and liturgical traditions; and formal structures for parent partnership (PTL) and open campus visits. These elements appear repeatedly across the school’s public materials, including the About/ILS Today pages, academics, admissions, and parent engagement sections. Citations demonstrating these signals are drawn from the school’s public pages and the specific articulation of classical aims, pedagogy, and community involvement.
