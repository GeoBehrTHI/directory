# ABSTRACT

Immanuel Lutheran School (Alexandria, VA) embodies a CLASSICAL educational framework across all six categories. Religious and metaphysical foundations are foregrounded through Christ-centered, Lutheran catechesis, liturgical life (Matins), and a foundational Good–True–Beautiful teleology. The school’s purpose prioritizes formation, virtue, and wisdom within a liberal-arts context rather than vocational training. Curriculum features include Latin (3rd grade), logic (5th grade), grammar, history, literature, and rhetoric, with a strong emphasis on the Great Conversation and Socratic inquiry in the upper grades. Pedagogy blends contemplative elements (scholé) with Socratic seminars, dialectic, and oral rhetoric, alongside memory- and chant-based lower-school practices. Faculty signals emphasize mentorship, a living curriculum, and favorable class sizes (9:1), with active involvement in classical-education networks. Parent and community engagement is robust, including a Parent-Teacher League, parent formation activities, and open-campus opportunities. Collectively, these signals position Immanuel Lutheran School as CLASSICAL in perpetuating traditional classical education within a Christian-Lutheran framework.
