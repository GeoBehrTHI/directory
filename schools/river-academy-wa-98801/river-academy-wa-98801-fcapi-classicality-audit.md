## Executive Summary

River Academy WA 98801 (The River Academy) presents a robust classical Christian education profile. Public materials describe a mission focused on forming virtuous, wise Christian leaders within a Trivium-centered curriculum (Grammar–Logic–Rhetoric) and a strong emphasis on the Great Books/Paideia tradition, integrated with a distinctly Christian worldview. The school explicitly calls itself “Thoroughly Christian, Innovatively Classical, Boldly Joyful, Intentionally Relational,” and frames education as formation of character in a community that partners closely with families. Evidence from core materials highlights: a biblical worldview and statement of faith; a strong Trivium orientation; engagement with primary/Great Books (e.g., Beowulf); commitment to the formation of virtue and character; and liturgical/communal practices such as psalm singing. The school emphasizes community-building elements such as a house system and a relational faculty model, and it actively invites parental involvement as co-educators. Taken together, these signals align most strongly with CLASSICAL, with a notable but smaller emphasis on certain pedagogical practices (e.g., lower-school singing, pastoral relationality) that render one category as SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL rather than fully rigid in every classical rubric detail. Overall assessment: CLASSICAL.
