## ABSTRACT

River Academy WA 98801 (The River Academy) demonstrates a CLASSICAL orientation across most criteria: a clearly stated Christian, transcendent-friendly mission; explicit Trivium-cored curriculum; engagement with Great Books/paideia; a formation-focused purpose; a living-teacher/mentoring faculty model; and strong parental partnership. The strongest signals come from Religious and Metaphysical Foundations, School Purpose and Goal, Curriculum, Faculty, and Parents/Community. Pedagogy shows substantial classical components (memory/chanting in early grades; grammar/logic/rhetoric in later grades) but public documentation of a consistent, full-scale Socratic instructional program across all grades is less explicit, which leads to a SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL rating for pedagogy. Overall, the school is CLASSICAL.

Key evidence themes drawn from the school’s public materials:
- Core Values state Thoroughly Christian, Innovatively Classical, and a Gospel-centered approach to all subjects.
