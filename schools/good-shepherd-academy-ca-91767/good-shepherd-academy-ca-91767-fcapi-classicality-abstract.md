## ABSTRACT

The Good Shepherd Academy (Pomona, CA 91767) demonstrates a robust Catholic classical orientation, with strong religious foundations, a formation-centric purpose, and a firm commitment to parental partnership. Public content highlights daily Mass, sacramental life, Catholic doctrine, Latin instruction, Western civilization and virtue formation, and parent collaboration, aligning well with the Six Essential Elements’ Religious & Metaphysical Foundations, and School Parents & Community categories. The school’s mission and materials also emphasize truth, beauty, and goodness as real and knowable, anchored in Catholic theology and liturgical life. These features strongly support a CLASSICAL orientation in two categories and a CLASSICAL framing in the parent/community category.

However, explicit canonical classical markers are more variably documented in public materials: explicit Great Books/Great Conversation language, a formal Trivium progression (grammar, logic, rhetoric) across all grades, and fully detailed Socratic pedagogy across grade bands are not consistently evident in the publicly accessible pages. Consequently, Curriculum, Pedagogy, and Faculty show emerging classical signals rather than full canonical articulation in public content. This places the overall assessment at SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL, with clear needle-moving opportunities if the school makes the canonical elements more explicit in public-facing materials.

In sum:
- Overall classification: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL
- Executive evidence highlights: Catholic school identity; formation over information; virtue and truth/beauty/goodness; Latin and Western-civilization emphasis; family partnership; sacramental life.
- Primary areas for needle-moving change: explicit Great Books/Great Conversation articulation; formal Trivium-based curriculum mapping; explicit Socratic pedagogy in upper grades; and a publicly stated “living curriculum” framework for faculty.
