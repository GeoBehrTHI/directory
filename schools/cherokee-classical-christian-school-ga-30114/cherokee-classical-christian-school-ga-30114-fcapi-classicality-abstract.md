## ABSTRACT

Cherokee Classical Christian School Georgia (30114) presents a public materials narrative strongly rooted in religious and metaphysical foundations, with explicit creedal grounding and a mission centered on truth, goodness, and beauty within a classical Christian framework. The school demonstrates a classical orientation in its stated curriculum and mission, supported by explicit references to a classical curriculum and theological formation. However, the crawl artifacts rarely provide explicit demonstrations of all six classical elements in a uniform, detailed manner (notably Great Books/Great Conversation, precise Trivium/Quadrivium structure, and explicit Socratic pedagogy). Consequently, the school is best characterized as SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL across multiple categories, with Category 1 (Religious and Metaphysical Foundations) closer to CLASSICAL and Categories 2–6 showing partial alignment.

- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: CLASSICAL, with explicit creed/theology statements and a mission centered on transcendent truths (truth, goodness, beauty) and God-centered formation. Positive keyword indicators (truth, goodness, beauty, virtue, theology, creed) appear in the public materials.  
- School Purpose and Goal: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL, grounded in virtue and formation but with less explicit articulation of wisdom telos and scholé-oriented formation in crawl text. Positive indicators include virtue and formation language in mission; absence of explicit Great Conversation language reduces the score. 
- Curriculum: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL, evidenced by “classical Christian curriculum” with Curriculum Scope and Sequence and Portrait of a Graduate, but lacking explicit and consistent Great Books/Great Conversation language in crawl artifacts. 
- Pedagogy: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL, with general classical framing but limited explicit references to Socratic discussion or scholé-style practices in crawl content. 
- Faculty: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL, with emphasis on virtuous, formative teachers; explicit portrait-based living-curriculum language exists in mission/hiring materials but not fully detailed in crawl. 
- Parents & Community: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL, with evidence of partnership and parent visitation; lack of explicit recurring “co-teacher” framing or formal parent-education programs in crawl content. 

This analysis leverages the Public-Crawl materials from Cherokee Classical Christian School (GA 30114) and applies the Six Essential Elements of a Classical School (1) rubric, using the Positive/Negative keyword indicators and the canonical Classical lexicon to ground category judgments. The school demonstrates a robust religious/metaphysical core and a clear classical identity, but explicit, comprehensive demonstration of all six classical criteria across categories is not uniformly evident in the crawl content, resulting in an overall SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL classification.
