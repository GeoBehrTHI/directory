## Executive Summary

Cherokee Classical Christian School Georgia (30114) presents a classical Christian educational vision anchored in historic Western Christian doctrine, virtue, and a curriculum described as classical Christian. Public-facing materials emphasize truth, goodness, and beauty as foundational ideals, and explicitly articulate a faith-centered framework (e.g., ecumenical creedal statements and a Statement of Faith). The school presents itself as a classical institution, with references to a classical curriculum and a mission to cultivate virtue and character within a Christian worldview. Evidence points to strong Religious and Metaphysical Foundations and clear alignment with some aspects of the Classical framework, particularly around virtue, formation, and a traditional ecclesial orientation. However, explicit, category-specific classical practices such as sustained Great Books engagement, explicit Trivium/Quadrivium emphasis, or explicit public-facing statements about Socratic seminars and Great Conversation are not consistently demonstrated in the publicly accessible crawl content.

Overall classification (aggregate): SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL
