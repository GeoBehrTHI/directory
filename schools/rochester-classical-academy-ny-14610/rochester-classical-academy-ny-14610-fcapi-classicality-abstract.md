## ABSTRACT

Rochester Classical Academy is classified as CLASSICAL. The school demonstrates a coherent classical Christian program across six domains:

- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: Public materials show a Christian metaphysical frame, a mission centered on godly character and inquiry into God, biblical truth integration, and a faith-based community. The presence of a formal Statement of Faith and Christ-centered mission supports transcendentals and theological grounding. Evidence: mission statements, Statement of Faith, biblical framing.  

- School Purpose and Goal: The school foregrounds formation, virtue, wisdom, and contemplation, with an aim for lifelong learning. There is explicit emphasis on forming human flourishing through liberal education rather than mere job preparation. Evidence: mission statements and philosophy of formation. 

- Curriculum: RCA centers on liberal arts and Great Books, grammar/logical/rhetorical progression (grammar, logic, rhetoric), and a schedule of reading Classics (Homer, Plato, Augustine, Shakespeare) alongside Latin/Greek and Western history. Evidence: Academics Overview, Tools for a Life of Learning, reading lists.  

- Pedagogy: The school combines restful, contemplative days with rigorous study; lower grades employ memory work, chants, and singing; upper grades emphasize Socratic discussion and rhetoric training. Evidence: Tools for a Life of Learning; pedagogy descriptions. 

- Faculty: The faculty are described as living curriculum and mentors; leadership and teachers are presented as aligned with the classical mission. Evidence: Faculty & Staff excerpts. 

- Parents & Community: RCA champions a partnership with parents, parent formation opportunities, and open access to visits, indicating a communal, participatory model consistent with classical schooling. Evidence: Connect with RCA, admissions materials.
