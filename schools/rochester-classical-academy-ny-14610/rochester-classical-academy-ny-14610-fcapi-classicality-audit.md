## Executive Summary

Rochester Classical Academy (RCA) presents a clearly classical, Christian model of education. Public materials from RCA articulate a mission of forming godly character and a joyful spirit of inquiry into God and His creation, with education designed to engage the world for Christ through wisdom, virtue, and eloquence. The school foregrounds classical elements—grammar, logic, rhetoric; the study of Great Books; liberal-arts formation; and a pedagogy that blends contemplative/Scholé rhythms with Socratic-style discussion and rhetoric-rich upper grades. The faculty pages highlight model teachers who embody and live the curriculum, while parental partnership and community involvement are repeatedly emphasized (tours, open admissions processes, and parent involvement). Taken together, RCA aligns strongly with the six elements of classical education in the knowledge base and the school’s own materials.

Key evidence includes RCA’s mission statement tying character formation to a spirit of inquiry into God, explicit articulation of biblical truth across subjects, a curriculum rooted in grammar/logic/rhetoric and Great Books, and pedagogy that alternates contemplative/recitation memory work with Socratic dialogue and rhetorical training. The site also emphasizes a living curriculum through its faculty, and a parental partnership model that treats parents as co-teachers in a faith-based classical program. In short, RCA demonstrates broad-based alignment with classical education across all six categories, with a particularly strong match in religious foundations, curriculum, pedagogy, and the formation of a living, intergenerational learning community.
