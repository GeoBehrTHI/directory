## ABSTRACT

Zion Lutheran School (Nampa, ID, 83686) (Nampa, ID) demonstrates a strong CLASSICAL orientation across all six classical-education categories. The Religious and Metaphysical Foundations are anchored by liturgical practice, Gospel-centered theology, catechesis, creeds, and sacraments. The School Purpose and Goal foreground formation—eternal life and virtuous living—over mere job-readiness, consistent with classical paideia. The Curriculum emphasizes liberal arts, Trivium/Quadrivium foundations, Great Books, and integrated history/literature with a strong musical/arts dimension. Pedagogy features Socratic inquiry, recitation/memory, and contemplative scholé, complemented by opportunities to develop public rhetoric. Faculty are presented as the living curriculum and core community, with a clear staff structure and teacher-mentored program. Parents and Community are deeply involved through PTF, open houses, and family education initiatives, reflecting a classical model of home-school partnership.

- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: Gospel-centered, liturgical life, Word of God, chapel, creeds, catechesis, sacraments, and vocation framing. CLASSICAL.
- School Purpose and Goal: Formation and virtuous living as core aims; eternal life as the ultimate telos. CLASSICAL.
- Curriculum: Liberal arts emphasis; Great Books; Trivium/Quadrivium; memorization/recitation; integration with Western civilization. CLASSICAL.
- Pedagogy: Socratic inquiry; recitation/chanting; memory-based learning; scholé/restful pedagogy; rhetoric opportunities. CLASSICAL.
- Faculty: Living-curriculum culture; identified leadership (Headmaster) and teachers; collaborative staff structure. CLASSICAL.
- Parents and Community: Strong parental partnership; open visits; ongoing parent education and involvement. CLASSICAL.
