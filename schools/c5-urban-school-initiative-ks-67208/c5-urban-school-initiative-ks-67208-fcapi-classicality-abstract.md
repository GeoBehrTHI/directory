## ABSTRACT

C5 Urban School Initiative Kansas (67208) demonstrates a robust classical Christian orientation across six classical-education categories. Evidence shows explicit religious metaphysical foundations anchored in Christian theology, Scripture, and liturgical/community practice, with a formation-centered purpose oriented to virtue, wisdom, and lifelong learning. The curriculum is clearly framed within liberal arts and the Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric), with grade-band structures and classical curricular trajectories. Pedagogy is described in terms of contemplative/restful and rigorous elements, though explicit public detailing of high-school Socratic seminars and Great Books integration is not consistently documented. Faculty are presented as model, mission-aligned mentors who function as the living curriculum, and the community emphasis centers on active parental partnership and church-based community engagement. The bank of programs (Prayer Partnerships, Book Buddies, Student Partners, Bridge Partners, Ambassadors, Cornerstone Partners) shows a strong ecosystem for parent involvement and support.
