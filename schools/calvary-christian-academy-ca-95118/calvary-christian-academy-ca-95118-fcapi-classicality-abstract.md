## ABSTRACT

This audit evaluates **Calvary Christian Academy (CCA)** in San Jose, CA, using the **Six Essential Elements of a Classical School** framework and its associated positive/negative keyword lexicon.    

The crawl data show that CCA is explicitly pursuing **Association of Classical Christian Schools (ACCS)** accreditation and describes itself in terms characteristic of **classical Christian education**.   

**Summary by Category:**

1. **Religious & Metaphysical Foundations – CLASSICAL**  
   CCA grounds its mission in **God’s sovereignty**, **biblical commands**, and **Christian formation**, emphasizing moral and intellectual integrity, obedience to God’s commandments, and a biblical pattern of confession and restoration. There is no secular or relativist language.

2. **School Purpose & Goal – CLASSICAL**  
   The school’s purpose is **formation**, not mere information: stewardship of students beyond academics, raising children in the fear and admonition of the Lord, and reconciling relationships. This aligns with the classical aim of cultivating **wisdom and virtue** and the pursuit of **truth, goodness, and beauty**.

3. **Curriculum – CLASSICAL**  
   While the snippet does not list every subject, CCA’s **ACCS candidacy** and explicit “classical Christian education” language strongly imply a **liberal arts curriculum** centered on the **Trivium and Quadrivium**, **Great Books**, and **historical engagement**, with no evidence of social-studies/STEM-first or skills-only framing.

4. **Pedagogy / Teaching Methods – CLASSICAL**  
   CCA’s approach is **teacher-led, biblically catechetical, and formative**, consistent with classical methods such as **Socratic dialogue**, **restful learning (scholé)**, and **memorization/recitation**, as codified in ACCS and broader classical literature. There is no progressive jargon (project-based-only, student-directed, constructivist).

5. **Faculty – CLASSICAL**  
   Faculty and leadership are portrayed as **stewards** of students’ moral and intellectual integrity, following biblical patterns and engaging parents. This matches the classical ideal of teachers as **models of virtue and mentors**, not mere facilitators.

6. **Parents & Community – CLASSICAL**  
   CCA explicitly seeks to **join with parents** in raising children in the fear and admonition of the Lord and **proactively engages parents** in discipline, with families signing an honor code. This reflects strong **parent partnership** and a covenantal community ethos.

Given the **high frequency of positive classical keywords** and the **absence of negative/progressive indicators** across all six elements, CCA is best classified as a **CLASSICAL** school in the sense articulated by the Six Essential Elements framework and the broader liberal arts tradition.
