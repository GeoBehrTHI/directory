## Method and Keyword Framework

I used the **Six Essential Elements of a Classical School** keyword lists (positive and negative indicators) across all six categories—Religious & Metaphysical Foundations, Purpose & Goal, Curriculum, Pedagogy, Faculty, Parents & Community.    

For each category I:

1. **Extracted exact words/phrases** from the CCA crawl (especially the handbook and mission/vision language).   
2. **Matched them** against the positive/negative lexicon (e.g., truth, goodness, beauty, virtue, theology, Socratic, scholé, wonder, chanting, project-based, experiential learning, etc.).   
3. **Estimated positive vs. negative keyword frequency** (rough percentages).  
4. **Assigned a rating**: CLASSICAL / SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL / NOT CLASSICAL.
