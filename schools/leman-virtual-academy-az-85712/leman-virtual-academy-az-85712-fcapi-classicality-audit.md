## Executive Summary

Leman Virtual Academy Arizona (AZ 85712) presents a hybrid that clearly leans toward classical education, with explicit emphasis on a science of classical pedagogy—especially in its branding as a “classical education” delivered in an online charter setting and in explicit grade-level references to logic, classical studies, and composition. However, the public materials from the school stop short of evidencing several core elements that accompany a fully classical program in the traditional sense (notably explicit Great Books lineage, robust liturgical/theological foundations, and the full spectrum of classical pedagogy such as uninterrupted Socratic seminars, scholé-oriented rests, chanting, and a broad, book-centered curriculum across all grades). The school does demonstrate meaningful alignment in some areas (curriculum emphasis on logic and classical studies; parent partnership; live teacher-led instruction; and a cultivated sense of character formation). Overall, the school sits squarely in the SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL category, with clear doors toward CLASSICAL potential if it deepens certain practices and language in its public communications.

- Strengthen teacher as living curriculum: Adopt explicit language and practices that teachers are “models of virtue” and “the living curriculum,” with professional development in classical pedagogy and a public statement of the teacher profile and student ideals.
- Deepen historical-cultural transmission: Emphasize Western civilization, classical texts, and primary sources in history and literature across grades (beyond project-based work) to align with the “Great Books” and “cultural transmission” aspects.
