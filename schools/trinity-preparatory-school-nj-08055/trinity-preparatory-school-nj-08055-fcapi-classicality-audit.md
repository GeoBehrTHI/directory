# Classicality Audit: Austin Classical School (Austin, TX)

Website used: https://austinclassical.org

## Executive Summary
Overall classification: CLASSICAL

Austin Classical School (ACS) exhibits comprehensive, explicit alignment with classical Christian education across mission, aims, curriculum, and pedagogy. The school centers on truth, goodness, and beauty; the cultivation of wisdom and virtue; and implements the Trivium with Latin, formal logic, rhetoric, Great Books, and Socratic teaching. Program artifacts (e.g., Senior Thesis, Christian Studies sequence, Latin through upper grades, logic and rhetoric, “Rhetoric Choir,” and the explicit use of memorization/recitation and debate/discussion) strongly match the positive indicators in the Six Essential Elements framework and lexicon. Parents are positioned as co-laborers and co-teachers, especially in the Grammar years, further strengthening the classical ethos at home and school. Evidence for “Faculty” as the “living curriculum” is present but comparatively lighter on the public site; thus that single category is scored Somewhat Classical with suggestions below. Key evidence includes: “glorify Jesus Christ… cultivate wise, virtuous, and humble students” and explicit commitment to “Truth, Goodness, and Beauty” and the Trivium, Great Books, Latin, logic, rhetoric, and Socratic method (ACS pages) which map directly to the positive keyword indicators of the “Six Elements” and classicality lexicon      ;  .

Aggregate result across six categories: 5x CLASSICAL, 1x SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL → Overall: CLASSICAL.
