## ABSTRACT
Austin Classical School (https://austinclassical.org) is classified as CLASSICAL. The school’s mission and program consistently reflect classical Christian aims—formation in wisdom and virtue under the transcendentals; theological grounding; Trivium-based curriculum including Latin, logic, and rhetoric; Great Books and Western canon engagement; and pedagogy that features memorization/recitation, debate/discussion, imitation, and Socratic method. Parents are co-laborers and co-teachers, with explicit structures supporting household participation.

- Religious/Metaphysical Foundations: CLASSICAL. Christ-centered mission; Scripture central; moral realism; Christian Studies sequence; choir/community life (positive matches to truth/goodness/beauty, theology, and liturgical/communal indicators)   .
- Purpose/Goal: CLASSICAL. “Cultivate wisdom and virtue,” “formation, not information,” eternal purpose, true/good/beautiful—strong positive alignment with lexicon; no careerist framing  .
- Curriculum: CLASSICAL. Trivium (Grammar-Logic-Rhetoric), Latin, Great Books/Great Conversation, history (not “social studies”), Senior Thesis, integrated humanities, Christian Studies   .
- Pedagogy: CLASSICAL. Memory/recitation/songs, debate/discussion, Socratic method, imitation in rhetoric, capstone thesis; clear alignment with classical pedagogy indicators  .
- Faculty: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL. Evidence of faithful, classically trained teachers is implicit; public articulation of teachers-as-mentors/“living curriculum” could be strengthened (see “move the needle”) .
- Parents/Community: CLASSICAL. Co-laboring with families; co-teacher model; parent formation resources; hospitable admissions practices; house system   .
