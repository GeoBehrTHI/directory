## Executive Summary

Holy Angels Academy (Louisville, KY) presents a robust Catholic, classical education framework. Public materials consistently emphasize a formation-oriented mission rooted in Catholic tradition, a liberal arts approach, Latin and grammar-based pedagogy, memorization and recitation, and a climate of sacramental life and daily prayer. The school hosts daily liturgical life, a full chaplaincy program, and a heritage of classical approaches in curriculum and assessment. Taken together, these signals align strongly with classical education as defined by the Six Essential Elements framework.

Overall, the school scores CLASSICAL across all six categories. The religious and metaphysical foundations are clearly integrated with liturgical life and sacramental practice. The school purpose and goal foreground formation, virtue, and knowledge of truth, goodness, and beauty through a Catholic, classical lens. The curriculum emphasizes grammar, logic, rhetoric, Latin, Western civilization, and engagement with great literature (notably Shakespeare and other canonical authors). Pedagogy shows early emphasis on memorization, recitation, and a structured classical progression, with Socratic methods present in upper levels but not described as the sole modality. Faculty signals include a living-education, mission-driven culture and a priestly/chaplaincy presence. Parent and community engagement is positioned as a partnership, with family formation and open access to involvement in school life.
