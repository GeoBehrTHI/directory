## ABSTRACT

Imago Dei Neighborhood School in Richmond, VA displays a strong Christian, liberal-arts orientation, with explicit emphasis on formation, virtue, and a holistic education. It demonstrates classical elements such as virtue formation, cross-curricular integration, memory-based pedagogy (narration/recitation), and a robust family/parent partnership model. However, explicit canonical markers—such as a formal Great Books program, a defined grammar-logic-rhetoric sequence, comprehensive Socratic pedagogy across all grades, and a fully articulated “living curriculum” in teacher practice—are not uniformly documented in public materials. Therefore, the overall evaluation places the school as SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL, with Element 1 (Religious & Metaphysical Foundations), Element 2 (Purpose & Goal), and Element 6 (Parents & Community) leaning CLASSICAL, while Categories 3 (Curriculum), 4 (Pedagogy), and 5 (Faculty) show strong classical tendencies but lack full canonical articulation in the publicly available content.

Notes on methodology
- Keyword analysis leveraged the Positive and Negative Keyword Indicators from the Six Essential Elements of a Classical School (1). Positive Keyword indicators include Conserved themes such as virtue, wisdom, formation, cultural transmission, partnership with Families, and the cultivation of affections, while Negative Keyword indicators include absence or diminution of traditional classical practices (Socratic dialogue, memorization, liturgical study, and imitation-based formation). The school’s public materials were scanned for these indicators and mapped to the six categories. The relevant lists and terms used in the scoring were taken from the Six Essential Elements documents:
  - Memorization/Recitation and related terms (category 4 pedagogy) 
  - Formation through imitation and practice (category 3–4 related) 
  - Community and Cultural Practices (category 6) with Positive Keywords (family partnership, formation of affections, cultural transmission) 
  - Positive Keywords (cultivation of wisdom and virtue; virtue, wisdom, contemplation, etc.)
