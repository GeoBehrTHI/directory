## ABSTRACT

Providence Classical Christian School WA 98011 demonstrates a strong CLASSICAL orientation across most of the classical education criteria. The school clearly grounds its mission in Christian theology and virtue formation, with explicit Christ-centered language and a Bible/theology emphasis in public materials. The curriculum centers on the liberal arts and the trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric), and there is a strong emphasis on formation, virtuous character, and a Christian worldview as the telos of education. The faculty are portrayed as moral exemplars within a living-curriculum framework, and parents are engaged as co-teachers and partners in the educational project.

Category-by-category summary:
- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: CLASSICAL. Evidence includes Christ-centered identity, faith integration, and a Christian worldview in mission and curriculum. 
- School Purpose and Goal: CLASSICAL. Emphasis on formation, virtue, wisdom, and a holistic purpose beyond information. 
- Curriculum: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL. Trivium and liberal-arts emphasis are clearly stated, but explicit Great-Books/Great Conversation elements are not consistently identified in public materials. 
- Pedagogy: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL. Core classical methods are implied (grammar/logic/rhetoric), but explicit documentation of Socratic seminars, scholé, or memorization practices is not consistently stated in public content. 
- Faculty: CLASSICAL. Faculty are described as virtuous, teacher-mentors and living curriculum; committed to Christ and to formation. 
- Parents & Community: CLASSICAL. Explicit partnership with families and open avenues for parent involvement and education. 

Executive-level takeaway: Providence Classical Christian School WA 98011 presents a strong CLASSICAL orientation with clear religious foundations, a formation-focused purpose, and robust faculty/parent-community alignment. The primary gaps lie in explicit articulation of Great Books integration and explicit pedagogical methods like Socratic seminars and scholé in public-facing materials. Addressing these two areas would elevate Curricular and Pedagogical alignment to full CLASSICAL.
