## Executive Summary

Providence Classical Christian School WA 98011 presents a robust classical orientation with clear religious foundations, a stated focus on formation through a liberal-arts curriculum, and strong engagement with families and the wider school community. Public materials consistently emphasize Christ-centered education, pedagogy aligned with the trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric), and a commitment to virtue and character formation. The faculty is portrayed as morally dedicated and integrated into the living curriculum, and parent partnership is foregrounded as essential to classical education. Taken together, these signals point to a CLASSICAL overall classification, with particularly strong alignment in Religious and Metaphysical Foundations, School Purpose & Goal, Faculty, and Parents & Community. The curriculum and pedagogy show solid classical alignment (grammar/logic/rhetoric and history across disciplines) but are not always explicit about Great Books, Great Conversation, or fully articulated Socratic pedagogy in every public-facing document; thus Curricular and Pedagogical signals are GENERAL to CLASSICAL, with some elements more fully developed than others.
