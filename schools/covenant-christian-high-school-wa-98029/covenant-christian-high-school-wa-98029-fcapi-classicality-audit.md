# Classicality Audit Report: Austin Classical School (Austin, TX)

School website used: https://austinclassical.org

## Executive Summary
Overall classification: CLASSICAL

Austin Classical School (ACS) clearly and consistently articulates a classical, Christian philosophy and practice. Its public materials emphasize transcendental moral realism (“truth, goodness, and beauty”), the cultivation of wisdom and virtue, a Trivium-shaped curriculum with Latin, logic, and rhetoric, and a pedagogy that includes memorization/recitation in the Grammar years, debate/discussion and formal rhetoric with thesis work in the upper grades. ACS is explicitly grounded in theology through a robust sequence of Christian Studies (Exegesis & Hermeneutics; Applied Exegesis; Apologetics & Ethics) and daily Chapel, and it intentionally “co-labors” with parents in a collaborative model. On our six-category classicality framework, ACS scores CLASSICAL in five categories and SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL in Faculty (the only area where the public site offers fewer explicit signals about a formal “portrait” of the teacher and collegial faculty culture).

Key evidence:
- Mission and aim: “cultivate wise, virtuous, and humble students” and teach through the “contemplation of the True, the Good, and the Beautiful” .
- Trivium-centered curriculum (Grammar, Logic, Rhetoric), Latin study, Great Books/Great Conversation language; integration of knowledge under theology   .
- Theology and liturgy: Christian Studies sequence (Exegesis, Apologetics), daily Chapel and “All School chapel” for visitors   .
- Pedagogy: memorization, recitation, songs in lower school; debate/discussion in Logic stage; rhetoric with thesis defense in upper school  .
- Parents/community: collaborative model with co-teachers; prospective parent meetings and tours (with chapel), open campus rhythms; strong community life (athletics, theater, Recitatio)  .

Reference framework: Positive/negative keyword indicators and category criteria from Six Essential Elements of a Classical School and the classicality lexicon were used to ground keyword matching and scoring across all six categories    .

## What would move the needle (Faculty)
Because Faculty is rated SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL:
- Publish a “Portrait of a Teacher” (and “Portrait of a Graduate”) that explicitly names virtues, habits of mind, and collegial expectations (Socratic collegium, lifelong learning, faculty seminars) to align with Six Elements’ faculty ideals .
- Publicly describe faculty formation rhythms (e.g., regular great-books seminars for teachers; classical pedagogy training; mentorship/apprenticeship for new faculty) using classical vocabulary from the lexicon (e.g., “faculty of friends,” “collegium,” “master-apprentice”)  .
- Share exemplars of faculty-led liturgical/recitation practices or teacher scholarship (papers, talks, in-house colloquia) to make the culture visible.
