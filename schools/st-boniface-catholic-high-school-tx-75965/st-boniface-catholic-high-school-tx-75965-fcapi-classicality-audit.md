## Executive Summary

St. Boniface Catholic High School (St. Boniface) presents itself as a Catholic, classical secondary school operating under a Chesterton Academy model. Its public-facing materials emphasize formation of the whole person through a rigorous liberal-arts curriculum, strong Catholic formation, and partnership with families. The school explicitly anchors its mission in forming Catholic missionary disciples and operates within a framework that foregrounds truth, goodness, and beauty as real and knowable. The curriculum highlights the liberal arts (grammar, logic, rhetoric), Great Books, Latin, and a history-centered program taught through a Catholic lens, with Socratic dialogue, seminars, and opportunities for rhetoric and public speaking. Liturgy, Mass, Rosary, and other liturgical practices are referenced as integral to the school culture. Faculty roles are framed around mentorship and formation, while parents are portrayed as co-teachers and active partners. Overall, the materials align strongly with classical education principles and Catholic formation, yielding a CLASSICAL designation across all six categories.
