## ABSTRACT

St. Boniface Catholic High School, Texas (75965) demonstrates a robust CLASSICAL orientation across all six classical-subject categories. The school’s public materials consistently emphasize Catholic formation, a formation-centered mission, a liberal-arts curriculum with Great Books, Latin, and classical history, Socratic pedagogy, rhetoric and public speaking, and strong parental partnership. The keyword analysis, grounded in the Six Essential Elements framework and its Positive/Negative Keyword Indicators, shows a predominance of positive classical indicators with negligible or no negative signals across all six categories. The aggregate assessment is CLASSICAL, with a fully aligned program of Catholic formation, classical curriculum and pedagogy, and engaged faculty and parents.

Key insights:
- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: Clear incarnational, liturgical, and theological grounding with a sacramental worldview.
- Purpose and Goal: Formation-first aims, virtue cultivation, and a teleological orientation toward truth, goodness, and beauty.
- Curriculum: Liberal-arts emphasis, Great Books, Latin, and a canon-rich history/philosophy sequence.
- Pedagogy: Socratic seminars, rhetoric training, and contemplative-mindful pedagogy integrated with liturgical life.
- Faculty: Teachers framed as mentors and living exemplars contributing to a living curriculum.
- Parents and Community: Strong partnership with families, parent formation opportunities, and open campus engagement.

This evaluation relied on content from the school’s public materials reflecting Chesterton-style classical education and from the knowledge-base lexicon for classical keywords, and it integrated both the school-specific crawl content and the classical rubric.
