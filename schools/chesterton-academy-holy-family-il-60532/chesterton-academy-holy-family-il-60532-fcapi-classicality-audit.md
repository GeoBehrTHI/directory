## Executive Summary

Chesterton Academy of the Holy Family (CATHF) presents a coherent, clearly Catholic, and classical program that aligns strongly with the core tenets of the classical education rubric. Public-facing materials emphasize a joyful, liturgical, and faith-infused education rooted in the Catholic tradition, with explicit commitments to daily Mass, formation in virtue, and exposure to liberal-arts breadth through a classical curriculum. The school describes its mission as forming virtuous leaders and saints within a culture of life, educated in the classical tradition and grounded in Catholic truth. This is reinforced by references to daily Mass, a chaplain, alignment with the Diocese, and a curricular emphasis on the liberal arts and the Great Books tradition. In short, the school signals a robust alignment with Religious and Metaphysical Foundations, Curriculum, and Purpose/Goal, with compelling elements in Pedagogy (Socratic-like dialogue, memorial/recitation practices), Faculty (teacher-mentors and living examples), and Parents & Community (strong parental partnership and community involvement).

Key strengths
- Explicit Catholic identity and liturgical life (Mass, chaplain, diocese affiliation) deeply embedded in daily life and mission. This is supported by statements that the school is “a Joyfully Catholic, Classical High School” and that Mass is central to the day.  
- Curriculum framed through a classical, liberal-arts paradigm with emphasis on Great Books, Western thought, and formation in virtue within a Catholic framework. Evidence includes “a rigorous, integrated and classical curriculum taught through the lens of the Catholic faith,” and explicit cues to the Great Books/Western thought orientation.  
- Pedagogical approach integrates Socratic-style inquiry and rhetoric within a framework that also honors liturgical, contemplative, and memory-based practices. The presence of a dedicated “Socratic Seminar” in the school materials signals alignment with classical pedagogy. 
- Strong home-school partnership and parental involvement as core to the model (Cultiva Vitae, family involvement, affordability supported by parent participation).
