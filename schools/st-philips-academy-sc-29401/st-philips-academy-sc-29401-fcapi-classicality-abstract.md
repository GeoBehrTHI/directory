## ABSTRACT

St Philips Academy Sc 29401 presently shows no public signals of a classical education orientation across the six rubric categories based on the available crawl materials. The only accessible material appears to be a 404 page from the school’s site, offering no explicit statements about religious/metaphysical foundations, the school’s formation-focused purpose, a classical curriculum, trape 'pedagogy' or teaching methods, faculty culture, or parent/community partnerships. Consequently, the evaluation classifies all six categories as NOT CLASSICAL. Positive keyword indicators drawn from the Six Essential Elements (truth, virtue, beauty, theology, liturgy, Great Conversation, scholé, and related signals) are not evidenced in the crawl materials, nor are their negative signals detectable in the accessible text. This lack of classical cues yields a universal Not Classical rating for the school under the rubric.

What would move the needle section provides concrete, grounded steps that would shift St Philips Academy Sc 29401 toward CLASSICAL status if the school elected to adopt the classical framework. The recommended steps include publicly documenting a transcendental/metaphysical orientation, a virtue-centered telos, a liberal-arts curriculum with Great Books, a pedagogy blending scholé and Socratic inquiry, a living-faculty vision, and explicit parent/community partnerships. These changes align with the keywords and signaling patterns described in the classical framework and would reorient the school’s public materials toward a Classical or Somewhat Classical standing.
