## Executive Summary

Abilene Classical Academy (ACA) presents a predominantly CLASSICAL orientation across its public materials. The school explicitly identifies a Christian, classical education framework (the Trivium: grammar, logic, rhetoric) and emphasizes cultivation of truth, goodness, and beauty, alongside a worldview formed by Scripture. Public materials highlight: a Christ-centered, classical mission; a curriculum built on Great Books sensibilities; classical languages (Latin) and history-centered study; and a distinctive teacher-families-home partnership in pedagogy. The school furthermore foregrounds parental partnership (parents as co-teachers on home days) and a structure that ties home, church, and school together in formation of disciples. These features align closely with the core Positive Keyword indicators of classical education while showing limited signs of the negative indicators in the classical lexicon.
