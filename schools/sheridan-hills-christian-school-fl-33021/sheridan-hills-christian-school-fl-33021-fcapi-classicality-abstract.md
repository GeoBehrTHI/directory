## ABSTRACT

Sheridan Hills Christian School (SHCS) demonstrates a CLASSICAL orientation across all six classical-category criteria. Its religious and metaphysical foundations are clearly anchored in Christ-centered faith and Biblical worldview commitments, reinforced by liturgical practices (weekly chapels) and Gospel mission activity. The school’s purpose and goals center on formation, virtue, wisdom, and human flourishing, with a strong telos aligned to the true, good, and beautiful. The curriculum foregrounds the Trivium (grammar/logic/rhetoric), biblical-history integration, and classical content (great books/classics, primary sources, and liberal-arts framing), complemented by classical languages and rigorous upper-school options. Pedagogy emphasizes both contemplative/restful elements and dynamic, Socratic/seminar-based discussions, memory work, and rhetoric training. Faculty are presented as mentors and living curriculum, and parents/community are positioned as co-educators with robust partnerships and opportunities for formation. Collectively, SHCS satisfies the SIX ELEMENTS criterion for CLASSICAL education.
