## Executive Summary

Liberty Classical Academy (White Bear Lake, MN, 55110) presents a robust classical, Christian approach to education. The school clearly embraces core classical elements—the Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric), Latin and classical literature, Great Books, and Socratic-style inquiry—while foregrounding faith as the lens through which all learning is interpreted. Public materials emphasize classical formation, virtue, and the aim of educating for character and wisdom rather than solely for work-readiness. The six-category evaluation below, based on the Six Essential Elements of a Classical School framework, indicates a consistently CLASSICAL orientation across Religious & Metaphysical Foundations, School Purpose & Goal, Curriculum, Pedagogy, Faculty, and Parents & Community. The strongest signals come from explicit statements about the Trivium, Great Books, classical method, and faith-based formation; weaker signals relate to liturgical practices, which are referenced less directly on site. Overall, Liberty Classical Academy is CLASSICAL, with limited need for needle-moving reforms to reach full alignment.

Key textual signals include: explicit Christian faith integration in the curriculum and worldview, the Trivium (Grammar–Logic–Rhetoric) as the organizing framework, emphasis on Great Books and original sources, memory-work and recitation as pedagogical tools, and a visible commitment to virtue and character formation. The public materials also stress a two-campus model, small class sizes, and a focus on speech and writing as core outcomes, consistent with classical formation. Citations drawn from Liberty Classical Academy’s site content and the accompanying classical-education lexicon support these characterizations.
