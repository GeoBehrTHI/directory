## ABSTRACT

Liberty Classical Academy (White Bear Lake, MN, 55110) exhibits a CLASSICAL orientation across all six classical-education categories (Religious & Metaphysical Foundations; School Purpose & Goal; Curriculum; Pedagogy; Faculty; Parents & Community). Public materials emphasize a Christian, classical foundation, with explicit Trivium-based curricular design (grammar, logic, rhetoric), the use of Great Books and original sources, and a pedagogy that blends memory work, recitation, communal chanting, and Socratic-style discussion and debate. The school portrays faculty as living exemplars and mentors and promotes active parental involvement and community engagement. The aggregate assessment is CLASSICAL, with strong signals of faith-informed formation, virtue development, and a deep commitment to classical content and methods. Some minor opportunities to more explicitly document liturgical practices and to foreground “Great Conversation” language could further strengthen the public alignment with the full Six Elements rubric.

- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: CLASSICAL. Evidence includes biblical integration and faith-centered interpretation of learning. 
- School Purpose and Goal: CLASSICAL. Evidence includes formation of virtue and wisdom as aims, with telos toward truth, goodness, and beauty.

- Faculty: CLASSICAL. Evidence includes living-curriculum faculty, mentorship, and exemplary teacher model. 
- Parents & Community: CLASSICAL. Evidence includes parent partnership, formation opportunities, and open-campus visitation.
