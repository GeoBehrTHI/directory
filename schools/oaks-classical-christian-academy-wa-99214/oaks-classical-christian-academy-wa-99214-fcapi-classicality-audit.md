## Executive Summary

Oaks Classical Christian Academy WA 99214 (The Oaks) presents a robust classical Christian program that centers faith, virtue formation, and a Trivium-based curriculum. Public-facing materials consistently emphasize a biblical worldview, theology as foundational to all disciplines, and liturgical/community practices, alongside a clearly articulated Trivium pedagogy (grammar, logic, rhetoric) and a strong emphasis on memorization, primary sources, and Great Books-like reading. The school also foregrounds parental partnership, discipleship, and a family-based culture with ongoing opportunities for parent formation and campus involvement. Taken together, these signals align strongly with classical education as outlined in the Six Essential Elements framework.

- Foundational religious/theological framing and liturgical/community practices are evident in mission statements, statements of faith, and explicit references to Christian worship and prayer as part of daily life. 
- The curricular structure and pedagogy explicitly mirror the classical Trivium with emphasis on primary sources, memorization, and a memory-centered culture, as well as a focus on virtue and formation. 
- Parental partnership and a school culture oriented toward virtue, character formation, and community life are repeatedly highlighted. 

Overall classification: CLASSICAL.
