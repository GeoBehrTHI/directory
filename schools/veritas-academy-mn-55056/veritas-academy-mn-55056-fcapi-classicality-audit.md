## Executive Summary

Veritas Academy (North Branch, MN, 55056) (Veritas Academy Classical) demonstrates strong alignment with classical education principles across all six rubric categories. Public materials from the school emphasize a Christian, biblically grounded, classical framework (grammar–logic–rhetoric) with explicit mentions of the Great Books, Latin, and a Biblical worldview; portfolio pieces emphasize virtue, wisdom, and formation, not mere information. Extracurriculars, small class sizes, caring faculty, and active parental partnership further reinforce a classical pattern of education centered on formation and character. The aggregate assessment places Veritas firmly in CLASSICAL territory across Religious and Metaphysical Foundations, School Purpose and Goal, Curriculum, Pedagogy, Faculty, and Parents & Community.

Key textual anchors include: the school’s articulation of three-triad classical education (grammar–logic–rhetoric) and Latin/great books exposure; explicit statements about a Biblical worldview informing all subjects; a mission to form virtuous, wise graduates; a strong emphasis on small classes and caring, credentialed faculty; and ongoing parent partnerships and opportunities for on-campus visits and involvement. Citations reflect the crawl content and the knowledge-base rubric elements that ground these conclusions.
