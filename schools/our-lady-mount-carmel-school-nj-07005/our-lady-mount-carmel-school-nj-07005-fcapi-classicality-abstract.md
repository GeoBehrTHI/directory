## Abstract

Our Lady of Mount Carmel School (NJ 07005) demonstrates a Catholic, virtue-centered educational mission with a strong sacramental life and liturgical rhythm. The public materials show a clear classical orientation in Religious and Metaphysical Foundations, School Purpose and Goal, and Faculty/Parents/Community engagement, while Curriculum and Pedagogy exhibit a hybrid model that blends Montessori elements with classical aims (notably Shakespeare and a “classical approach” branding). This results in an overall classification of SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL, with robust CLASSICAL signals in faith, virtue formation, and community, and partial classical alignment in curriculum/pedagogy due to Montessori integration.

- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: CLASSICAL
  Evidence includes explicit Catholic identity, sacramental life, liturgy, and Catholic traditions across grades; virtue formation is central. Positive keywords heavily outnumber any negative indicators (none clearly present).

- School Purpose and Goal: CLASSICAL
  Evidence shows formation, virtue, wisdom, and a telos toward human flourishing. The emphasis on formation over information and impact-based assessment aligns with classical ideals.

- Curriculum: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL
  Evidence shows Montessori structures and a classical literary emphasis (Shakespeare, Great Books) with explicit classical branding, but a lack of a fully articulated, grade-spanning Trivium/Quadrivium framework. The hybrid curriculum reduces to SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL.

- Pedagogy: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL
  Evidence includes contemplative/scholé practices, memory work, chanting, and some upper-grade seminars, but lacks consistent, universal Socratic/seminar-based pedagogy across all grades. The blend with Montessori pedagogy contributes to the mixed signal.

- Faculty: CLASSICAL
  Evidence highlights model, virtuous, living-curriculum teachers with classical preparation and mentorship emphasis.

- Parents & Community: CLASSICAL
  Evidence of strong parental partnership, formation opportunities, and open-campus/community events, aligned with classical community principles.

Aggregate classification: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL

- Expanding explicit classical language and rhetoric components across the K–12 spectrum.
