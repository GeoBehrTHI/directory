## Executive Summary

Logos School in Moscow, Idaho presents a robust classical Christian program anchored in a Christ-centered worldview, explicit Trivium-oriented pedagogy, and a curriculum that foregrounds liberal arts, Great Books sensibilities, and a historical-Western-Christian intellectual tradition. Public materials consistently frame education as formation of character and the cultivation of wisdom, truth, goodness, and beauty, with Scripture-centered integration across disciplines. The school’s publicly stated philosophy emphasizes that there is no neutrality in education and that subjects are taught within a Christian framework, which strongly aligns with classical education criteria. Multiple visible signals—the motto “a classical and Christ-centered education,” explicit emphasis on grammar/logic/rhetoric, and a stated commitment to a biblical worldview—support a classical orientation across core dimensions: religious/metaphysical foundations, overarching purpose, curriculum structure, pedagogy, faculty, and parent/community partnership. Given the available content, Logos School earns a predominantly CLASSICAL rating across all six categories.
