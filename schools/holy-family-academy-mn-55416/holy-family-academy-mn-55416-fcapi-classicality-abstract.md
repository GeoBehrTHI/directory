## ABSTRACT

Holy Family Academy Mn 55416 is a Catholic, Montessori-inflected classical school (Pre-K–8) that centers formation, virtue, and Catholic liturgical life. Its public materials emphasize the Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric), Great Books, Latin, and a curricular integration of faith, history, and language arts, with liturgical life (Mass and liturgical calendar) interwoven into daily schooling. The school’s mission points toward the true, the good, and the beautiful within a Catholic telos (salvation in Jesus Christ). Faculty are portrayed as capable and faith-aligned, with strong teacher expertise highlighted, though explicit articulation of a fully developed “living curriculum” and a robust Socratic pedagogy in public materials remains less evident. Parent and community involvement is actively cultivated through enrichment nights and family liturgy, underscoring a strong classical-community alignment.

Category-wise, HFAMN scores CLASSICAL in Religious & Metaphysical Foundations, School Purpose & Goal, and Curriculum; in Pedagogy and Faculty it demonstrates substantial classical cues but lacks full public documentation of Socratic pedagogy and living-curriculum articulation, yielding SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL ratings for those two categories. Parents & Community show strong alignment with classical expectations, earning CLASSICAL. The aggregate rating is SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL, driven by the solid classical-curriculum and religious foundations, with room to document pedagogy and faculty more fully in classical terms.

- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: CLASSICAL. Evidence: Catholic identity, liturgical life, truth/goodness/beauty language, Incarnation framework. 
- School Purpose and Goal: CLASSICAL. Evidence: formation, virtue, telos, true/good/beautiful; Catholic formation. 
- Curriculum: CLASSICAL. Evidence: Trivium, Great Books, Latin, history, classical sciences/languages. 
- Pedagogy: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL. Evidence: recitation, memory, liturgical rhythm; limited explicit Socratic/seminar documentation. 
- Faculty: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL. Evidence: teacher expertise; lack of explicit living-curriculum language. 
- Parents & Community: CLASSICAL. Evidence: parent formation, enrichment nights, open-campus style engagement. 

This abstract reflects the school’s strong classical cues in curriculum and religious formation, with opportunities to deepen explicit classical pedagogy and faculty articulation to reach a fully CLASSICAL standing across all six categories.
