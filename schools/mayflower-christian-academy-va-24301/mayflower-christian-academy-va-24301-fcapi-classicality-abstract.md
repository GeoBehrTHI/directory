## ABSTRACT

Mayflower Christian Academy Virginia (Pulaski, 24301) presents a Christian formation-centered program with strong theological grounding and a clear emphasis on character formation and leadership. Public-facing materials show robust Religious and Metaphysical Foundations (Bible-based curriculum, Statement of Faith, mission to influence the world for Jesus Christ) and a strong Parents & Community orientation (parents as co-partners in education). These signals mark the school as CLASSICAL in Category 1 and Category 6.

However, in Curriculum and Pedagogy, the school does not publicly foreground the central Classical elements such as the Seven Liberal Arts (grammar, logic, rhetoric; Quadrivium), Great Books/Great Conversation, or explicit Socratic pedagogy. The Curriculum relies on Bible-based Christian publishers (My Father’s World, IEW, BJU Press, Apologia, etc.) rather than canonical classical texts; Pedagogy emphasizes Mastery-based, small-class instruction rather than traditional Socratic seminars and recitation. These factors yield a NOT CLASSICAL signal for Category 3 and a SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL signal for Category 4, with Category 5 showing mixed signals about the faculty’s classical role.

Taking all six categories into account, the school falls into SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL overall. The strongest levers to move toward CLASSICAL would be to publicly integrate Great Books and the Great Conversation into the curriculum, adopt explicit Socratic and rhetoric training in appropriate grade levels, and systematize a “living curriculum” approach across faculty—with a robust articulation of classical faculty formation and parental engagement that aligns with traditional Classical aims.

Key sources and evidence include MCA public materials documenting the mission, Bible-based curriculum, and liturgical practices; the Statement of Faith; and admissions materials; compared with the Classical criteria and keyword indicators from the Six Essential Elements of a Classical School (1) and related lexicons. Citations: MCA materials (mission, curriculum, pedagogy), Statement of Faith, and admissions materials.
