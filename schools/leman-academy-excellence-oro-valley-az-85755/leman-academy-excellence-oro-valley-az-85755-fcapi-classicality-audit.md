## Executive Summary

Leman Academy of Excellence, Oro Valley (K-8) presents a broadly classical framing in public-facing materials. It explicitly positions itself as a “classical education” school that emphasizes virtues such as wisdom and virtue, and aims to form the whole student (character, intellect, and the person). The public materials foreground formation of character and memory, with terms like “wisdom and virtue,” “timeless values,” and “character” recurring across pages. However, the site does not consistently disclose explicit traditional classical elements such as a stated Great Books program, explicit mention of grammar/logic/rhetoric (Trivium/Quadrivium), or a clear Socratic pedagogy across all grade levels in readily visible content. Taken together, the school aligns with many core classical signals but does not always meet all six element criteria in a fully explicit way.

Overall classification: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL.

Key insights:
- Strengths: Clear emphasis on virtue, wisdom, and character; vocabulary of formation and a “classical education” frame; public materials describe a rigorous, values-driven program and small-class, personalized learning. Evidence of faculty and family partnership language that aligns with classical schooling ideals (e.g., “dedicated teachers,” “safe, supportive environment,” and “partner with parents”).
  - Evidence examples: “Leman Academy of Excellence in Oro Valley is a tuition-free public charter school serving scholars in kindergarten through 8th grade. Our classical education model builds a strong foundation of wisdom and virtue.”; “Our curriculum builds both academic knowledge and character.”; “Our teachers and staff at the Oro Valley campus are dedicated to creating a safe, supportive environment where every scholar can thrive.” fileciteturn0file0turn1file16
- Gaps to needle-move: Explicit Great Books/Great Conversation signals, explicit Trivium/Quadrivium framing, explicit Socratic pedagogy in upper grades, and explicit theology-liturgical language are less visible in the public-facing OV site. Increasing explicit articulation of those elements would move the school toward a more fully CLASSICAL profile.
  - Notable absence: Direct statements about Great Books, Great Conversation, grammar/logic/rhetoric as core curricular pillars, or explicit liturgical/theological practices are not readily visible in the cited OV content. 

Abstracted takeaway: The school strongly frames education as formation with virtue, wisdom, and character at the center, and operates as a tuition-free classical program (K–8). It falls into SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL due to meaningful classical framing and formation emphasis, but would benefit from clearer, explicit articulation of canonical classical elements (Great Books, Trivium/Quadrivium, Socratic pedagogy, liturgical-community practices) to achieve a CLASSICAL designation.

## What Would Move the Needle

- For Element 3 (Curriculum): Add explicit, public articulation of canonical classical curriculum components, such as:
  - Clear statements about grammar, logic, and rhetoric as core curricular strands (Trivium) and about the Quadrivium (arithmetic, geometry, music, astronomy) as applicable at specific grade levels.
  - Explicit commitment to Great Books and the Great Conversation (or a formal list of core works) in the curriculum documentation.
  - Publicly publish a reading list aligned with Great Books and primary sources across grades.
- For Element 4 (Pedagogy): Publicly commit to and describe explicit pedagogical methods aligned with classical education, such as:
  - Socratic seminars in upper grades, narration/recitation in appropriate grade bands, and the use of memory work and catechesis where relevant.
  - Documentation of “Scholé” or contemplative days and a structured rhythm of study that balances rigor with contemplation.
- For Element 5 (Faculty): Publish a formal teacher-portrait framework and living-curriculum concept:
  - Provide a public “Portrait of a Classical Teacher” and a “Living Curriculum” articulation showing how teachers embody and enact virtue, wisdom, and classical pedagogy.
- For Element 6 (Parents & Community): Expand explicit parent-formation and co-teaching language:
  - Create and publicize parent-education offerings (book studies, seminars, and liturgy-based family activities) and emphasize visitor-friendly campus policies to welcome parent involvement as co-educators.

These needle-moving steps would largely shift the profile from SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL toward fully CLASSICAL.
