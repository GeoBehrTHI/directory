## Executive Summary

Archbishop Rummel High School in Metairie, LA demonstrates a predominantly classical orientation, anchored in Catholic Lasallian formation and a clear emphasis on character, virtue, and communal life. The public materials strongly foreground faith, community, and service as the educational telos, with explicit commitments to formation of students within a liturgical and faith-based framework. The school’s stated mission and culture reflect key classical signals (the sense of formation, virtue, and a religiously grounded community), though the publicly available content (as represented in the crawl content) does not overtly spell out the full Trivium/Quadrivium curriculum, Great Books, or Socratic pedagogy in the manner traditional classical schools often articulate. Across the six categories, Archbishop Rummel registers as CLASSICAL in three areas, SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL in one, and NOT CLASSICAL in two, with an overall CLASSICAL orientation because the category results as a group tilt toward classical formation and shared virtues.

Key takeaway: The school’s strength lies in religious and metaphysical foundations and formation-oriented aims (Classical in Categories 1, 2, and 5), with visible engagement of parents/community (Element 6) and some signs of classical formation through policies and mission (but lacking explicit Great Books/Trivium-focused curriculum in the public materials). The aggregate assessment is CLASSICAL.
