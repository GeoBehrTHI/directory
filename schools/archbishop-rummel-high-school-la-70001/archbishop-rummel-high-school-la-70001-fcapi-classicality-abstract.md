# ABSTRACT

- The Archbishop Rummel High School assessment indicates a CLASSICAL orientation overall, with strong alignment in Religious and Metaphysical Foundations (Catholic Lasallian formation and liturgical life), School Purpose and Goal (formation and moral development framed by virtue and service), and Faculty (model teachers, living curriculum). The school shows SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL signals in Parents & Community (active parent engagement), and NOT CLASSICAL signals in Curriculum and Pedagogy (explicit classical curriculum and classical teaching methods are not evidenced in public materials). The aggregate classification is CLASSICAL.

- Primary insights:
    - Religious formation and moral formation are central, with explicit Lasallian faith-based formation and integrity policies.
    - The school emphasizes formation and virtue as core goals, aligning with classical aims beyond vocational training.
    - The Curriculum and Pedagogy categories lack explicit classical elements (Great Books, Trivium/Quadrivium, Socratic pedagogy) in the public materials provided, suggesting room for growth to be considered fully Classical in those domains.
    - Parent/community engagement exists, but a stronger articulation of parents as co-teachers would strengthen the CLASSICAL signal in Element 6.

- The six-category synthesis yields an overall CLASSICAL designation, with attention needed in Curriculum and Pedagogy to move toward a more complete classical articulation and a clearer articulation of Great Books/Trivium-based curricular structure.
