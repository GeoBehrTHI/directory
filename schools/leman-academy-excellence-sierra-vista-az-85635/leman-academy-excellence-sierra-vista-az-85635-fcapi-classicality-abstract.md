## ABSTRACT

Leman Academy of Excellence Sierra Vista is a publicly funded K–8 charter school that positions itself as offering a classical education focused on building knowledge and character. Public materials emphasize a tuition-free, community-centered environment with small class sizes, personalized learning, and whole-child enrichment in the arts and academics. However, explicit public articulation of core classical elements—such as theology and liturgical practice, Great Books/Great Conversation, and explicit triad/trivium-based pedagogy—is not clearly evident, which leads to an overall classification of SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL rather than CLASSICAL.

- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL. Evidence shows classical framing and character emphasis but no explicit theological or liturgical program in the accessible public content.
- School Purpose and Goal: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL. Formation through character is evident, but explicit transcendental aims (truth, goodness, beauty as real) are not fully articulated in public materials.
- Curriculum: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL. The school communicates a classical orientation and reading-focused aims, yet explicit mention of Seven Liberal Arts, Great Books, or Great Conversation is not found in the accessible content.
- Pedagogy: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL. Personalized learning and a collaborative culture are present, but explicit classical pedagogical methods (Socratic seminars, scholé, memorization, etc.) are not publicly described.
- Faculty: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL. Visible emphasis on caring, small-campus teachers and partnership with families aligns with classical expectations, but explicit “living curriculum” language is not stated.
- Parents & Community: CLASSICAL. Strong evidence of partnership with parents, parent formation, open campus/vistor-friendly culture, and family involvement aligns well with classical-education values.

General assessment: The Sierra Vista campus demonstrates a solid classical orientation and best-practice community engagement, but the public-facing materials do not yet consistently and explicitly articulate the full classical canon (theology, liturgy, Great Books, and explicit pedagogical sequences). The aggregate classification is SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL.
