## Executive Summary

Holy Family Academy (Phoenix, AZ 85016) presents a robust classical-Catholic school profile. The publicly available site content emphasizes a Catholic, traditional, liberal-arts orientation, a clearly articulated mission to form virtuous citizens, and a curriculum rooted in the liberal arts with explicit attention to Trivium/Quadrivium and a historically grounded scope. Liturgical practices (daily prayer, Rosary, weekly Mass, Confession) and the integration of faith across disciplines are central, aligning strongly with the Religious and Metaphysical Foundations category. The school also foregrounds formation of character, virtue, and a teleology toward truth, goodness, and beauty, indicating strong alignment with the School Purpose and Goal category.

Most categories show CLASSICAL alignment. A careful keyword check indicates Element 4 (Pedagogy) is the only area with partial alignment, yielding a SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL signal, largely due to less explicit documentation of Socratic seminars or discussion-based pedagogy on the public site (though the Trivium/Quadrivium emphasis and classical approach are clearly present). The overall aggregate rating remains CLASSICAL.
