## ABSTRACT

Holy Family Academy (Phoenix, AZ 85016) exhibits a CLASSICAL orientation across six evaluation categories, with particularly strong alignment in Religious and Metaphysical Foundations, School Purpose and Goal, Curriculum, Faculty, and Parents & Community. The school’s public materials emphasize Catholic formation, a truth-goodness-beauty teleology, a liberal-arts curriculum, and a Trivium/Quadrivium framework, all of which align with classical education principles. Pedagogy stands as SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL — the site clearly articulates classical curriculum and Trivium/Quadrivium, but explicit Socratic/seminar-based pedagogy is not extensively documented in public-facing content. The aggregate assessment across categories yields CLASSICAL, with a minor needle-mover in Pedagogy (and minor potential enhancement in Curriculum regarding explicit Great Books alignment). The school’s architecture—strong faith integration, parent partnership, and a teacher-centric living-curriculum model—supports a robust classical education ethos.
