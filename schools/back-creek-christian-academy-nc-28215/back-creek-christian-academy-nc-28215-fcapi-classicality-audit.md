## Executive Summary

Back Creek Christian Academy (Charlotte, NC, 28215) (operating as Christ Classical Academy of Charlotte) presents a robust alignment with classical education principles in several key areas, particularly around religious/theological foundations and a purpose-driven, formation-focused approach. Public materials emphasize Christian worldview integration, the centrality of Bible-based instruction, and a Trivium-based framework (grammar, logic, rhetoric) as the backbone of the curriculum and pedagogy. Evidence from the school’s site shows explicit statements about Bible integration in every subject, weekly chapel, and a mission statement centered on forming students within a Christ-centered, classical framework. These elements support a CLASSICAL orientation in multiple categories, especially where religious/metaphysical foundations and purpose/goals converge with classical pedagogy and a Trivium-informed curriculum.

However, there are areas that limit a full CLASSICAL rating. For example, while there is clear articulation of the Trivium and Socratic-style upper-school dialogue, explicit references to some hallmark classical components—such as a broad Great Books program and a fully articulated “Great Conversation” across history and literature—are not consistently evident on the public materials. Some categories show partial alignment (SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL), and one category shows little explicit evidence of classical practice (NOT CLASSICAL). Taken together, the school leans toward CLASSICAL overall, with meaningful strengths in religious foundations, formation-oriented goals, and Trivium-based curriculum and pedagogy, but with room to strengthen explicit Great Books integration, faculty formation language, and deeper community/parent formation practices in public-facing materials.

Overall classification: CLASSICAL
