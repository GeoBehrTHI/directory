## Executive Summary

Veritas Preparatory School (Greenville, SC, 29604) presents a strong classical-education profile in public-facing materials, anchored in Christian faith, a University-Model structure, and a deliberately articulated Trivium-Quadrivium curriculum. The school emphasizes character formation, virtue, and the pursuit of truth, goodness, and beauty through a liberal-arts framework, with explicit mention of Great Books, grammar-logic-rhetoric (the Trivium), and the Quadrivium (arithmetic, music, geometry, calculus). Pedagogy centers on debate, discussion, and rhetoric, with a living-faculty model and a partnership with parents in a home-school–school hybrid. The public materials consistently tie formation of affections and moral imagination to intellectual development, and they foreground a Christian theological orientation, communal liturgy/ritual, and a family-centered, open-campus ethos. Overall, the school is decisively CLASSICAL across all six categories.

- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: Clear Christian framing, with explicit telos tied to Christ and theology; emphasis on God, Christian identity, and biblical formation. Evidence includes “Our Telos” and the Christian, classical, University-Model identity, and biblical framing of purpose. 
- Purpose and Goal: Formation-first aims (virtue, wisdom, love of God/self/neighbor) aligned with a classical telos; critique of mere information transfer; emphasis on liberal-humane formation and contemplation. 
- Curriculum: A robust liberal-arts core (grammar/logical/rhetoric; Great Books; Latin/Greek; history; Quadrivium elements; Singapore Math). Evidence shows explicit Trivium and Quadrivium treatment, Great Books emphasis, and classic-curriculum content. 
- Pedagogy: Seminars, debates, and rhetoric training; Socratic/humanities-oriented discussion; disciplined memory work and chant-like elements appear in classic-education discourse; University-Model structure emphasizes disciplined, ritually integrated pedagogy with parental partnership. 
- Faculty: Living curriculum and mentors; professional teachers in their fields; a faculty culture framed as a core of the school and a portrait of teacher-formation. 
- Parents & Community: Explicit partnership with parents as co-teachers; open, welcoming campus; parental formation opportunities; home–school integration.
