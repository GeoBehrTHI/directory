## ABSTRACT

Veritas Preparatory School (Greenville, SC, 29604) earns a CLASSICAL designation across all six classical-elements categories. Evidence shows a Christian telos and theology-linked ends, formation-centered goals (virtue; wisdom; pursuit of truth, goodness, and beauty), a robust classical curriculum (Trivium; Quadrivium; Great Books; Latin/Greek; history), a pedagogy anchored in Socratic discourse, memorization, and rhetoric, a faculty model described as a living curriculum, and a strong, ongoing partnership with parents and the surrounding community. The Positive Keyword Indicators from the Six Essential Elements document align with the school’s stated aims and practices, while Negative Indicators are not evidenced in the public materials. The school presents a coherent classical-education program with explicit ties to faith, formation, and liberal-arts excellence, and it does so in a University-Model, home–school integrated format that reinforces its classical mission.
