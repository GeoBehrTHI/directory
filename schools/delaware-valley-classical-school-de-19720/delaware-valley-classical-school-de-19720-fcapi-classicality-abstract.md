## ABSTRACT

Delaware Valley Classical School (DVCS) demonstrates a thoroughly classical Christian orientation across all six rubric categories. Religious and Metaphysical Foundations are anchored in Christ, theology, Bible-based practice, and liturgical rhythm, aligning with transcendental realities and a Biblical worldview. The School Purpose and Goal emphasize formation, virtue, and the good life, consistent with classical aims to cultivate humanity and wisdom rather than mere job preparation. The Curriculum centers on the Seven Liberal Arts (grammar, logic, rhetoric) and the Quadrivium, integrates Great Books and Western civilization, and emphasizes wonder and a unified, cross-disciplinary approach. Pedagogy blends contemplative/restful lower grades with Socratic and rhetorical instruction in the upper grades, including singing, memory work, and formal dialogue. Faculty are described as living curriculum—model teachers and mentors—within an accreditation framework. Finally, Parents & Community are treated as co-educators through active partnerships, parent education, and family engagement, including content aimed at grandparents and a broader school community.

In summation, the analysis yields a CLASSICAL classification for DVCS, with all six categories meeting the core criteria of classical education as defined by the Six Essential Elements of a Classical School (1) and the associated lexicon. The public materials consistently reference truth, goodness, and beauty; formation of virtue; Great Books and the Trivium/Quadrivium; Socratic pedagogy; a living, virtuous faculty; and active parental/community partnership. The aggregate assessment is CLASSICAL, with no immediate category requiring a needle-moving adjustment.
