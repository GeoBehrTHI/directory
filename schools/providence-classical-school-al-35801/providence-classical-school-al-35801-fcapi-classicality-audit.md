## Executive Summary

Providence Classical School Alabama (PCS Huntsville, 35801) presents a clearly classical Christian program that aligns strongly with the Six Essential Elements framework. Public-facing materials emphasize a biblically grounded, virtue-centered vision and a Trivium-based curriculum (grammar, dialectic, rhetoric) taught through integrated humanities, classical languages, and exposure to Great Works. The school foregrounds formation of character and affections in tandem with intellectual development, treats parents as partners in the educational project, and employs a grade-band pedagogy that begins with contemplative, memory-rich work in the early grades and expands into Socratic/dialectic discourse and rhetorical expression in the upper grades. Evidence from PCS’s site shows explicit references to biblical foundations, a shared Christian worldview, classical pedagogy (Grammar-Dialectic-Rhetoric), memorization and chant-based memory in the early years, and a public statement of parental partnership. The faculty data point (average teaching experience) and multiple accreditations/associations further support a robust classical profile. Collectively, PCS Huntsville is classified as CLASSICAL across all six evaluated categories, with its public communications consistently signaling traditional, virtue- and wisdom-centered aims anchored in a Christian worldview and a Trivium-informed curriculum.
