## Executive Summary

Brilla Veritas Elementary Ny 10455 (Bronx, NY) operates as part of Brilla Public Charter Schools, a network described publicly as eight free, high-performing K-8 schools in The Bronx. The public materials emphasize academic rigor coupled with a virtue-centered approach and characterize the school as an accessible, community-focused option (no entrance exams; enrollment is free). Evidence from the Brilla Veritas site shows the school’s stated aim to develop students’ minds and hearts through virtue-centered formation and character development, with explicit mention of college readiness as a goal. The site also highlights a staff-facing, teacher-centered public-facing presence (e.g., “TEACHER” pages), and a Families/Community orientation centered on open visits and family inquiries.

Overall, by the classical rubric, Brilla Veritas Elementary demonstrates some classical impulses (notably virtue-centered formation and a character-focused framing) but does not consistently articulate core classical elements such as the Trivium/Quadrivium, Great Books, or explicit Socratic pedagogy. Across the six categories, the school shows limited alignment with classical education in curriculum, pedagogy, and parent/community practices, with modest alignment in religious/metaphysical tone and formation aims. The aggregate assessment is NOT CLASSICAL. See category-by-category notes below for the supporting evidence and keyword analysis.
