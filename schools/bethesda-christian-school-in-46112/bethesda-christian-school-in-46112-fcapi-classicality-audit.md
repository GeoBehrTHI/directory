## Executive Summary
Overall classification: CLASSICAL

Austin Classical School (austinclassical.org) presents a robustly classical, Christian, collaborative model across mission, curriculum, pedagogy, faculty culture, and parent partnership. The site explicitly emphasizes truth, goodness, and beauty; the Trivium and Latin; Socratic and rhetorical training; daily chapel and Scripture; teachers as Christian exemplars; and parents as co‑teachers. Their own language—“glorify Jesus Christ,” “cultivate wise, virtuous, and humble students,” “contemplation of the True, the Good, and the Beautiful,” “Socratic method,” “thesis defense,” and “collaborative model”—aligns directly with the Six Essential Elements framework and its positive keyword indicators.    

School website audited: https://austinclassical.org 

Highlights
- Mission and theology: Christ-centered mission, daily chapel, Scripture as “holy, inerrant, and infallible Word,” and staff who “joyfully submit” to a Statement of Faith; integration of Christian studies, apologetics, and exegesis.    
- Purpose and telos: Formation toward wisdom and virtue; TGB (truth, goodness, beauty); “less about training for a specific, temporal vocation… more about learning to think and live with an eternal purpose”; not a college-prep posture.   
- Curriculum: Trivium, Latin, logic, rhetoric, Great Books/Western tradition; history (ancient to modern) named as history (not social studies).    
- Pedagogy: Memorization/recitation/chanting, debate/discussion, Socratic method, rhetoric via imitation, senior thesis defense; communal recitation events and choir.   
- Faculty: Teachers are professed Christians, called to model proper behavior and humility; school-day chapel with teachers and students together. 
- Parents & community: “Collaborative model” with parents as co‑teachers; office hours for homeschool days; explicit parent partnership and community events.
