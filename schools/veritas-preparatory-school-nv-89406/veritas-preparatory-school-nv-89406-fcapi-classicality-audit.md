## Executive Summary

Veritas Preparatory School NV 89406 (Fallon, NV) presents itself publicly as a classical Christian school serving Kindergarten through 8th grade, with a stated aim to form character through classical education “to the glory of God” and a Christ-centered approach. The site emphasizes a Trivium-based curriculum (grammar, logic, rhetoric) and a joyful, virtues-centered classroom culture, along with Bible-based authority and small-class learning. This combination signals a strongly Classical (and explicitly Christian) orientation in line with the core Classical School rubric.

Evidence from public materials shows:

- Religious/metaphysical framing and theology-integrated purpose (God-centered education; Bible as final authority; Soli Deo Gloria) anchored in a classical framework.
- A clear emphasis on virtue, beauty, truth, and goodness as ends of education, with formation emphasized over purely informational aims.
- A curriculum described in terms of the Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric) and a commitment to classical educational architecture, but with no explicit public claim of Great Books or a full Great Conversation on the site.
- Faculty described as experienced and capable; “experienced teachers” with a mentoring framework.
- Limited public emphasis on parents as co-educators or a formal community-partnership program on the site itself, though classical-education materials elsewhere emphasize parent involvement and partnership.

Quantitatively, the site demonstrates strong positive indicators in Religious/Metaphysical Foundations, Purpose/Goal, and Faculty, with clear but partly incomplete evidence for Curriculum (no explicit Great Books or history integration stated on the site) and Parents/Community (public materials do not foreground parent partnership). When benchmarked against the Six Essential Elements of a Classical School rubric, the school overall aligns closest to CLASSICAL, with some gaps that prevent a full endorsement in every category without additional public evidence.

Overall ranking: CLASSICAL.

What would move the needle (only if the school were SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL)

- Publicly articulate and publish a Great Books/Great Conversation component within the curriculum, including representative titles and reading plans.
