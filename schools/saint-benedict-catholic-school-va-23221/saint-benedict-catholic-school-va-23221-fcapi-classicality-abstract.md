## ABSTRACT

Saint Benedict Catholic School (Richmond, VA 23221) demonstrates a robust CLASSICAL orientation across all six classical-education categories. Religious and Metaphysical Foundations are anchored in a faithful Catholic framework with liturgical life and theology integrated into daily learning. The School Purpose and Goal emphasize formation, virtue, and wisdom, consistent with classical telos. The Curriculum centers on liberal-arts foundations (grammar, logic, rhetoric) and Great Works, Latin, and integrated learning. Pedagogy blends contemplative Scholé-style memory work with Socratic/seminar elements, supported by rhetoric training at higher levels. Faculty are portrayed as mentors embedded in a living-curriculum model within a small, family-like community. Parents & Community involvement is explicit and ongoing, with formal channels for parent participation and education.

Key evidence from the school’s public materials includes explicit statements about Catholic classical education, the Great Works, and Latin (Grade 1), as well as a liturgical life that includes daily prayer and Mass, which together ground the school’s metaphysical, moral, and intellectual aims. The six-category assessment yields CLASSICAL across Religious & Metaphysical Foundations, School Purpose & Goal, Curriculum, Pedagogy, Faculty, and Parents & Community. The aggregate result supports a CLASSICAL classification for Saint Benedict Catholic School in the context of the classical education rubric.
