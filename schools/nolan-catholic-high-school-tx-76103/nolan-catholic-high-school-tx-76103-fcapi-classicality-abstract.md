## ABSTRACT

Nolan Catholic High School (Fort Worth, TX 76103) demonstrates a CLASSICAL orientation across most of the six classical-education categories, with two areas showing partial alignment (Category 3 Curriculum and Category 4 Pedagogy). The school’s Catholic formation is explicit and central, supported by mission language (Education in Faith, Formation in Hope, Perseverance in Charity), liturgical life (weekly Mass) and theology-centric coursework. The Core curriculum (five subject core plus electives) and Trivium-inspired course elements (Grammar & Composition; Logic & Composition) align with liberal-arts priorities and the Trivium doctrine. There is evidence of Great Books/Great Conversation language and explicit classical-education-series resources, but not always a fully articulated Great Books program in public materials, placing Category 3 in SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL. Pedagogy shows classical tendencies, including memory/recitation-like traditions and a contemplative emphasis, but lacks explicit uniform Socratic seminars across all grades, placing Category 4 in SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL.

- Category 1 — Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: CLASSICAL
  Evidence: Catholic mission; Mass; theology; Marianist heritage; formation in Gospel virtues. Positive keywords include faith, theology, liturgy, Mass, sacred, truth/goodness/beauty.  
- Category 2 — School Purpose and Goal: CLASSICAL
  Evidence: Formation-based mission; servant leadership; faith-formation language; pursuit of truth/goodness/beauty as real. 
- Category 3 — Curriculum: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL
  Evidence: Core liberal-arts framing; Trivium elements; reading-list reference; public-facing Great Books language is not consistently explicit.  
- Category 4 — Pedagogy: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL
  Evidence: Trivium-based coursework; contemplative/experiential elements; less explicit universal Socratic pedagogy across grade bands. 
- Category 5 — Faculty: CLASSICAL
  Evidence: Faculty as living curriculum; strong professional culture; mentorship and virtue-focused framing. 
- Category 6 — Parents & Community: CLASSICAL
  Evidence: Partnership with families; parent formation; open campus; fundraising and community engagement. 

Aggregate assessment: CLASSICAL

Overall, Nolan Catholic High School presents a strong classical orientation, anchored in Catholic formation and liberal-arts pedagogy, with explicit elements of Trivium-based curriculum and a pronounced commitment to family partnership and community. The main opportunities for strengthening toward full canonical classical education lie in making Great Books/Great Conversation explicit in public materials and expanding uniformly articulated Socratic pedagogy and Quadrivium integration within the formal curriculum.
