## Executive Summary

Emmaus Academy IL 61101 presents a robust classical Christian orientation, with clear emphasis on the Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric), living books and Habits-based formation, and a distinctive partnership model that actively involves parents in the educational formation of their children. Public materials consistently articulate a mission of pursuing truth, goodness, and beauty within a Christian framework, and the school foregrounds virtue, character formation, and wisdom as central aims of education. The curriculum and pedagogy showcase classical elements (grammar-logic-rhetoric, memory work, nature study, picture study, composer study, Shakespeare) alongside Charlotte Mason influences (living books, habit formation, at-home/onsite blend). There is explicit language about a Christian curriculum centered on Christ, with governance and faculty explicitly professing Christian faith. Across six classical criteria, Emmaus Academy demonstrates a substantially classical orientation, with little evidence of the negative signals that typically accompany non-classical approaches.

- Overall classification: CLASSICAL
- Core supporting cues: Trivium-based curriculum, living books, memory work, nature study, Shakespeare, composer study, and a Christian, liturgical-inflected community life. Strong signals of formation over mere information, and a deliberate partnership model with parents.
