## Executive Summary

Holy Trinity Academy Lower School (Schriever, LA, 70395) presents a robust classical-Catholic program that aligns strongly with the Six Essential Elements framework. Public materials describe it as a Classical School in the Catholic Tradition, with a clear emphasis on formation, virtue, and the pursuit of truth, goodness, and beauty. The public-facing content consistently foregrounds Catholic formation, a classical liberal-arts curriculum, the Great Books/Great Conversation ethos, and pedagogies such as Socratic dialogue, disciplined memory work, and a memorializing, liturgical rhythm. Evidence also highlights the school’s intentional partnerships with parents and a communal, incarnational approach to education. Taken as a whole, the school demonstrates a predominantly CLASSICAL orientation across all six categories.

Key signals across categories include: the Catholic groundings (Magisterium, daily Mass, Incarnation), the classical curriculum (grammar–logic–rhetoric, liberal arts, Great Books discussions, Socratic pedagogy), the focus on formation and virtue (pillar of formation, formation plan, virtue-centered aims), the living faculty as mentors and models, and strong parent/community engagement (parent formation, open campus, family involvement). The site explicitly notes the Pillars of Formation (Spiritual, Intellectual, Human), emphasizes the Magisterium, and presents a robust classical curriculum with a strong communal and liturgical character. Notable textual anchors include phrases such as “A Classical School in the Catholic Tradition,” “pursue truth, goodness, and beauty,” “daily Mass,” “Pillars of Formation,” “An Incarnational Environment,” and “The Humanities Program ... utilizing the Socratic Method.” These are all consistent with a Classical framework, not merely a subset, and there is little to no material in the public-facing materials suggesting a move away from classical aims.
