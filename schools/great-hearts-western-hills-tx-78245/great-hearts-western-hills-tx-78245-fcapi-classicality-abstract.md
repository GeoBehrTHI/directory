## ABSTRACT

Great Hearts Western Hills (GHWH) is a university-adjacent classical-leaning charter school guided by liberal-arts traditions, moral formation, and a Western-civilizational heritage. Across six classical criteria, GHWH displays strong alignment in Religious and Metaphysical Foundations, School Purpose and Goal, Faculty, and Parents & Community, with Curriculum and Pedagogy showing substantial classical signals but some gaps in explicit public articulation of certain classical elements (e.g., explicit Great Books/Great Conversation commitments and explicit Socratic pedagogy in upper grades). The categorical analysis yields:

- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: CLASSICAL
  - Evidence includes True-Good-Beautiful framing, cardinal-virtues language, and liturgical/communal practices in handbooks. Positive keywords (truth, goodness, beauty, virtue, wonder) are explicit in public materials. 
- School Purpose and Goal: CLASSICAL
  - Formation, virtue, wisdom; classical mission; telos; and a Great-Conversation ethos are reflected in GHWH messaging and Great Hearts’ classical framing. 
- Curriculum: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL
  - The school is positioned within the classical liberal-arts tradition and Western focus, but explicit public statements about grammar/logic/rhetoric and Great Books/Great Conversation usage are less consistently visible in the public content provided. 
- Pedagogy: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL
  - Emphasis on depth of inquiry, wonder, and contemplative learning suggests scholé-like pedagogy, yet explicit high-school Socratic/seminar pedagogy and rhetoric training are not robustly demonstrated in the public-facing pages we reviewed. 
- Faculty: CLASSICAL
  - Faculty as mentors and living curriculum, with public pages dedicated to staff and faculty leadership, align with classical expectations. 
- Parents & Community: CLASSICAL
  - Strong parent-partnership language, family-formation opportunities, and open-campus culture align with classical community-building principles. 

- General Assessment: CLASSICAL
  - GHWH demonstrates a classical orientation across the majority of the rubric. The strongest signals come from the religious/metaphysical foundations, purpose/goal, faculty, and parent/community. The curriculum and pedagogy show robust classical intent but would benefit from more explicit public articulation of specific classical curricular elements (grammar/logic/rhetoric; Great Books/Great Conversation) and explicit Socratic pedagogy to fully realize a pure Classical designation in those areas.

- The six-element lexicon and the canonical classical indicators provided in the source documents strongly corroborate GHWH’s classical orientation. The school’s emphasis on tradition, virtue, and a shared cultural heritage—coupled with active family partnerships and a living-teacher model—redounds to a Classical classification, with minor gaps in publicly stated curricular specifics that, if remedied or made more explicit on the site, would push the Curriculum and Pedagogy categories firmly to CLASSICAL. The executive assessment is supported by the presence of explicit phrases and programmatic commitments aligned with the Six Essential Elements of a Classical School (1) and the accompanying lexicon.
