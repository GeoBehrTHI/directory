# Classicality Audit Report: Berean Christian Academy (Katy, TX)

Website audited: https://bereanchristianacademy.org

## Executive Summary
Overall classification: CLASSICAL

Berean Christian Academy (BCA) presents strong, explicit commitments to the classical Christian tradition across mission, curriculum, and upper‑school pedagogy. The school names Truth, Goodness, and Beauty in its mission, frames theology as the “Queen of the Sciences,” organizes schooling by Grammar–Logic–Rhetoric, assigns Latin and Great Books, and culminates in a public Senior Thesis. Evidence of Socratic/Harkness discussion and debate is clear in the upper school. While explicit references to liturgical practices (e.g., daily chapel, hymnody) and a formal portrait of the teacher are less visible on public pages, the school’s memberships (ACCS, SCL) reinforce its classical identity. On balance, BCA’s public materials substantively align with the Six Essential Elements’ positive indicators.   

Highlights
- Mission explicitly names Truth, Goodness, Beauty and partnership with parents. 
- Curriculum structured by Trivium; Great Books, theology, philosophy, Latin; Senior Thesis capstone; Harkness/debate. 
- Theology framed as queen of the sciences; apologetics and Christian studies in sequence. 
- Affiliations: ACCS and SCL. 

Method note: Keywords from BCA’s site were compared to the positive/negative indicators in Six Essential Elements and the companion lexicon to support each category rating.

## What would move the needle (focused improvements)
Because Pedagogy and Faculty scored SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL:
- Pedagogy: Publish lower‑school “memory work” practices explicitly (e.g., “singing, chanting, recitation” in K–6), and describe any scholé/restful learning rhythms. Showcases of oral recitations, catechesis, or hymnody would enhance visibility. 
- Faculty: Add a “Portrait of a Teacher” that names teachers as moral/intellectual exemplars who model the classical virtues; describe faculty formation in classical pedagogy and collegial seminars.
