## ABSTRACT

Providence Christian School (PCS) in Dothan, AL, demonstrates a CLASSICAL orientation across six classical-education categories, with Pedagogy representing the only category showing more nuanced signaling (SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL). Evidence across PCS materials includes: a biblically anchored mission and Statement of Faith (the Bible as final authority), explicit reference to the Trivium (Grammar, Logic, Rhetoric), and a humanities-forward curriculum featuring primary sources, Western civilization content, and Latin. The school portrays itself as a classical, Christ-centered ministry in partnership with First Presbyterian Church, emphasizing character formation and wisdom as primary aims, rather than job-prep alone. Faculty credentials are substantial (all hold bachelor degrees; a significant subset hold advanced degrees), and a strong parent/community partnership is evident.
