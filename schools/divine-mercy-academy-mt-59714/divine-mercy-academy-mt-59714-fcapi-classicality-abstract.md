Overall Classification: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL

## ABSTRACT

Divine Mercy Academy (Belgrade, MT, 59714) presents a robust Catholic classical profile with strong signals in Religious and Metaphysical Foundations, School Purpose/Goal, and Parents & Community, and solid but more incremental signals in Curriculum and Pedagogy. The school’s public materials emphasize truth, goodness, beauty; virtue formation; Trivium-based curriculum (grammar, logic, rhetoric); liturgical life (CGS, Eucharist, atria); and family-centric governance and parent partnership. The aggregate assessment places the school in the SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL category.

Key categories and signals:
- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: Catholic identity, liturgical life, catechesis, and sacramental formation strongly align with classical aims (CLASSICAL). 
- Purpose and Goal: formation and virtue- and wisdom-oriented aims are explicit (CLASSICAL). 
- Curriculum: Trivium-based curriculum and integrated history/literature, with Great-Books framing present in classical vocabulary, though not always explicit; signals lean CLASSICAL but not uniformly across all public materials (SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL). 
- Pedagogy: memory/recitation, contemplative elements, and rhetoric appear; Socratic dialogue is suggested in some contexts but not uniformly across all grades (SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL). 
- Faculty: mentoring and formation signals exist, but a formal “living curriculum” faculty portrait is not consistently spelled out publicly (SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL). 
- Parents & Community: strong parental partnership and family-centered approach (CLASSICAL).
