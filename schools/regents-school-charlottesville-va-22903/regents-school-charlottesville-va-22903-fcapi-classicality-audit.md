## Executive Summary

Regents School Charlottesville (Regents School of Charlottesville, Charlottesville VA 22903) demonstrates a robust classical Christian orientation across public materials on its site. The school presents itself as a Christ-centered, liberal-arts–influenced program that explicitly aligns with the Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric), Great Books, classical languages (notably Latin), and a biblical worldview integrated across disciplines. Evidence from their site includes the motto Soli Deo Gloria, a mission in terms of truth, beauty, and wisdom, explicit reference to the Trivium and Dorothy Sayers’ Lost Tools of Learning, and a weekly rhythm of liturgical/communal practice (Chapel) and catechetical formation (catechesis, liturgy). The school also emphasizes formation of virtue, character, and eloquence (senior theses, public speaking, and rhetoric) and frames parent involvement as a core part of the educational project (In Loco Parentis, Regents Parent Fellowship). The curriculum spans K–12 with Great Books and primary sources, Latin, logic, rhetoric, and a strong emphasis on memory work and disciplined discourse (Socratic seminars in upper grades; living-history/collegiate-style pedagogy). Public materials also showcase a strong sense of community and partnership with parents and grandparents (e.g., Grandparents’ Day, parent involvement). Taken together, the six classical-element categories classify Regents School Charlottesville as CLASSICAL, with few to no explicit negative indicators in the school’s public materials.
