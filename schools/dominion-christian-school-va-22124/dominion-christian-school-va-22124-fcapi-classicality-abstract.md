## Abstract

Dominion Christian School (Oakton, VA, 22124) exhibits a CLASSICAL orientation across all six classical-education categories. The school identifies itself as a K-12 classical Christian institution, foregrounding a faith-based formation (theology, Augustine, Jesus Christ) and virtues such as wisdom and virtue. The curriculum centers on the Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric) and Great Books, with explicit attention to formation of character and an integrated, liberal-arts approach that studies history through classical canonical texts. Pedagogy appears rooted in classical methods, including a Trivium framework and disciplined discussion, while faculty structures (Head of School, named subject specialists, leadership/faculty pages) reflect the “living curriculum” model. Parent and community engagement is evidenced by parent-focused resources and open-access campus information (e.g., admissions, tours, and current-family portals). Overall, the school demonstrates a strong classical alignment with minimal negative indicators in the public materials. Abstractly, Dominion Christian School stands as a CLASSICAL institution with robust evidence across religious foundations, purpose, curriculum, pedagogy, faculty, and parental/community engagement.
