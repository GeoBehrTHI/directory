## Executive Summary

Barry County Christian School (BCCS) Middleville, MI presents a clearly classical Christian educational model. Across the six classical elements, BCCS consistently frames itself as a Classical Christian School, foregrounding a biblical worldview, the Trivium, Great Books, and a virtue-centered formation. Public materials emphasize devotions, prayer, chapel, theology, and a structured liberal-arts curriculum, including history studied through primary sources, Latin/Greek language, and integrated fine arts. The faculty paradigm emphasizes living teachers and mentorship, and parental partnership is foregrounded as a central value. Based on the public materials analyzed from the school’s site, combined with the classical rubric and keyword indicators from the knowledge base, the aggregate rating for BCCS is CLASSICAL.

Key insights:
- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations are explicit: biblical framework, theology, worship elements (devotions, prayer, chapel) and a Christ-centered worldview pervade daily life and academics. 
- The School Purpose and Goal is classical in aim: formation through virtue, wisdom, and the pursuit of truth, goodness, and beauty within a Christian frame. The explicit phrase “Education is the Cultivation of Virtue” anchors category 2. 
- Curriculum centers on liberal arts, Trivium, Great Books, history through primary sources, and classical languages; clear alignment with classical pedagogy. 
- Pedagogy shows both contemplative/restful elements (devotions, prayer, chapel) and active, rhetoric-focused training (Rhetoric training, memorization-related practices, public speaking). The site explicitly mentions "train them to be winsome as they write and speak with eloquence." 
- Faculty is presented as mentors and living curriculum; dedicated staff and multiple campuses emphasize teacher quality and relational learning. 
- Parents & Community are positioned as partners, with emphasis on relationships with parents and opportunities to visit and engage, indicating a cooperative model with the community. 

Overall Classification: CLASSICAL
