## ABSTRACT

Barry County Christian School Middleville, MI demonstrates a robust CLASSICAL orientation across all six classical education categories. Public materials present a clearly Christian, virtue-centered formation anchored in a biblical worldview, the Great Books tradition, and the Trivium (Grammar, Logic, Rhetoric). The curriculum emphasizes liberal arts, classical languages, and primary-source history, while pedagogy integrates both contemplative and rhetorical approaches, including public speaking and memory-based practices. Faculty are portrayed as living exemplars who form the core of the living curriculum, and parents are positioned as partners in education, with opportunities to engage and visit. The overall assessment is CLASSICAL, with strong alignment between the school’s stated mission and the classical rubric.

Key findings by category:
- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: Explicit Christian framing, liturgical practices (devotions, prayer, chapel), theology, and biblical worldview support a Classical evaluation. 
- School Purpose and Goal: Virtue- and wisdom-centered formation, with explicit reference to virtue being cultivated and a classical mission. 
- Curriculum: Trivium, Great Books, primary sources, Latin/Greek, and history treated as a central curriculum element.
