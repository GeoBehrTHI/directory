## ABSTRACT

Category-wise, Religious and Metaphysical Foundations, School Purpose and Goal, Faculty, and Parents & Community align with CLASSICAL markers, driven by Christ-centered pedagogy, formation of character, mentorship, and family partnership. Curriculum and Pedagogy show partial alignment; the school emphasizes biblically integrated curricula and college preparation but does not publicly articulate the Seven Liberal Arts, Great Books, or formal Socratic pedagogy, which pulls these categories toward NOT CLASSICAL or SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL. The overall assessment is CLASSICAL, with explicit opportunities to strengthen classical curricular structures and pedagogical methods to achieve a fully classical profile.
