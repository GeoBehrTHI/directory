## Executive Summary

Faith West Academy (Katy, TX 77493) presents itself as a Christ-centered private Christian school that emphasizes spiritual formation, character development, and biblically integrated academics. Public-facing materials consistently frame education as formation of the person in relation to faith, virtue, and service, with chapel, Bible study, and daily devotionals highlighted as core practices. The school also emphasizes family partnership, parental involvement, and a welcoming campus culture.

Strengths aligned with classical aims:
- Religious and metaphysical foundations are explicitly foregrounded (Christ-centered, Bible-based, weekly chapel, discipleship/mentorship). This maps strongly to the Positive Keywords for Religious and Metaphysical Foundations in the Six Essential Elements framework (e.g., theology, liturgy/worship, virtue, moral formation, spiritual depth, mentorship) and is reinforced by a clear statement of faith and biblical authority. Evidence includes explicit doctrinal statements and weekly worship practices.  
- School purpose and goals center on formation, virtue, and the cultivation of character within a Christian framework, with a stated aim to form minds, hearts, and souls. This aligns with the classical emphasis on formation and virtue rather than mere career-readiness. Evidence in the public materials emphasizes “formation,” “Christ-centered education,” and character development.  

Key caveats relative to a strict classical rubric:
- Curriculum and pedagogy sections emphasize biblically integrated academics, AP/dual-credit pathways, and modern instructional practices, but do not clearly articulate explicit classical elements such as the Seven Liberal Arts (grammar, logic, rhetoric; trivium), the Quadrivium, or a rigorous Great Books/Great Conversation orientation. There is limited public evidence of Great Books, formal Socratic seminars, or a history-program framed as “Western civilization through the Great Conversation.” This suggests not-classical in the Curriculum and Pedagogy categories by strict classical standards, with potential for “Somewhat Classical” depending on how one weighs biblically integrated liberal arts and character formation.  

Overall classification: CLASSICAL
- The evaluation below uses a keyword-driven rubric derived from the Six Essential Elements of a Classical School (1). Faith West Academy demonstrates strong alignment in Religious and Metaphysical Foundations, School Purpose and Goal, and Faculty/Parents & Community via explicit doctrinal commitments, liturgical practices, and a culture of mentorship and parental partnership. It shows partial alignment in Curriculum and Pedagogy (due to biblically integrated and college-preparatory programs rather than explicit classical structures). The aggregate result places Faith West Academy in CLASSICAL territory, with room to strengthen classical curriculum and pedagogy signals to achieve a fully classical profile.

What would move the needle (for a SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL assessment only):
- If the school were to explicitly articulate and incorporate classical curriculum elements (Seven Liberal Arts; Great Books/Great Conversation; explicit history across the curriculum in a Western-civilizational frame) and identify classroom practices such as Socratic seminars, recitation, and memory work as core methods, its category would shift more clearly toward CLASSICAL.

Abstract takeaway: Faith West Academy is fundamentally rooted in a Christian formation model with strong moral and spiritual formation signals, robust parental partnership, and a college-preparatory academic posture. It already aligns well with several Positive Keywords in the classical rubric and outperforms in Religious/Metaphysical Foundations, Purpose/Goal, and Faculty/Community. It would reach a more purely classical standing with explicit classical curriculum elements and traditional pedagogical practices (Socratic dialogue, great books, memory work) clearly framed as central components.
