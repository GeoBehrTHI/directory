## Executive Summary

Appalachian Christian School Virginia (Waynesboro/Afton area) demonstrates a predominantly classical orientation, with clear religious/metaphysical foundations, a formation-focused purpose, and strong faculty/parental partnership elements. Public-facing materials emphasize a Christian worldview, daily engagement with Scripture, chapel worship, character formation, and disciplined leadership development. While the school foregrounds formation, virtue, and fidelity to theological commitments, its explicit curricular architecture does not consistently articulate the full liberal-arts Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric) or a canonical Great Books program. Taken together, the school fits predominantly Classical or deeply Classical in spirit across most areas, with partial alignment in curriculum structure and pedagogy that suggest Somewhat Classical characteristics in those dimensions. The aggregate classification is CLASSICAL.

Evidence highlights include: repeated emphasis on Bible classes, daily engagement with Scripture, weekly chapel and student-led worship, and a Biblical worldview integrated across subjects; a core value proposition centered on love of God, wisdom, service, and integrity; strong emphasis on mentoring and the faculty as living exemplars; explicit openness to parent partnership and frequent parent-focused engagement; and detailed admissions processes that encourage shadowing and family involvement. However, the curriculum and pedagogy sections do not consistently reveal the grammar–logic–rhetoric structure, Great Books participation, or classic seminar formats typically associated with a fully classical model. This nuanced mix supports a strong Classical frame with Somewhat Classical aspects in curriculum and pedagogy.
