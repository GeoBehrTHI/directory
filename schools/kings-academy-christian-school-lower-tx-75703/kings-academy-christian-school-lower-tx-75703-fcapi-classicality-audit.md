This makes Element 1 (Religious and Metaphysical Foundations) and Element 2 (School Purpose and Goal) clearly CLASSICAL in orientation.

Element 3 (Curriculum), Element 4 (Pedagogy), and Element 5 (Faculty) lack explicit classical indicators in public-facing materials, yielding NOT CLASSICAL readings for these areas. Element 6 (Parents & Community) shows partnership with families, which aligns with some classical expectations, though with limited evidence of broader parent formation or campus openness.

Overall, the school leans toward NOT CLASSICAL, with some CLASSICAL elements in its religious foundations and stated mission, and a partial, but insufficient, alignment with classical parent-community partnership. The aggregate ranking is NOT CLASSICAL.
