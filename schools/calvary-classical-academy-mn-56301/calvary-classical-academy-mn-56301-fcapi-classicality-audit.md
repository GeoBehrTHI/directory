## Executive Summary

Calvary Classical Academy Mn 56301 presents as a classical Christian academy operating within Calvary Church St. Cloud. Public materials emphasize classical framing (Latin, traditional subjects, aesthetic beauty through the arts) alongside explicit Christian formation (discipleship, worship, missions). The site repeatedly foregrounds a religious purpose for education (disciples of Jesus Christ) and a commitment to traditional, classroom-based subjects with hands-on learning. However, explicit references to core classical-curriculum fixtures such as Great Books/Great Conversation, the full Trivium/Quadrivium framework, or Socratic seminars are not clearly stated on the public pages. The school’s materials do show concrete classical attributes (Latin, aesthetics, classical naming) and a robust parish-community scaffolding (worship, community groups, missions), but several hallmark elements of a fully articulated classical program (e.g., explicit Trivium/Quadrivium pedagogy, detailed Great Books emphasis, student-teacher as living curriculum, formal parental formation processes) are not overtly documented on the site.

Overall classification (aggregate): SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL

Key observations driving the assessment:
- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations are strongly present via explicit discipleship, worship, and mission-minded activity (CLASSICAL in evidence and tone).  
- Curriculum signals include classical markers like Latin and traditional subjects, but lack explicit Great Books/Great Conversation and full Trivium/Quadrivium articulation in public pages (SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL). 
- Pedagogy, Faculty, and Parent/Community indicators exist (e.g., leadership bios, community/missions pages, worship/ministry pages), but explicit classical pedagogy prescriptions (Socratic seminars, Scholé, living curriculum) are not clearly spelled out on the site (SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL).  
- The Public Materials show a genuine commitment to formation, virtue, and beauty in a Christian framework, but the site does not consistently foreground the full classical lexicon (Great Books, Trivium/Quadrivium, scholé language) in a way that would push all six categories to CLASSICAL. This yields an overall SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL classification, with Element 1 leaning CLASSICAL.
