## ABSTRACT

PBCA demonstrates a CLASSICAL profile across its public-facing materials. The school emphasizes Distinctively Christian, Scriptural, and Christ-centered foundations, with liturgical/community practices (chapel, worship) and church partnerships that ground its religious metaphysics. Its purpose emphasizes formation and virtue, aligning with the classical aim of truth, goodness, and beauty as real and knowable. The curriculum centers on grammar, logic, and rhetoric (Trivium), Great Books, and Socratic dialogue, indicating a strong classical curriculum. Pedagogy combines Socratic discussion and rhetoric training with contemplative and memory-based approaches (lower grades and Conservatory experiences with recitals and chapel). Faculty presence is clearly communicated through a structured team, though explicit language that faculty embody the living curriculum is less evident, placing Category 5 at SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL. Parental and community involvement is robust, with a clear emphasis on partnership and community culture.

Overall, PBCA is CLASSICAL in its public materials, with nuanced opportunities to deepen explicit faculty formation language to reach full CLASSICAL status in all six categories.
