## Executive Summary

Prudentia Classical Academy (Lake Chales, LA, 70605) presents a clearly classical Christian program, with strong alignment to the six essential elements of classical education. Public-facing materials emphasize classical Christian formation, truth-seeking, virtue, and wisdom, often framed within a theology-driven, God-honoring worldview. The curriculum centers on the Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric), humanities, memory work, and recitation, with explicit upper-school Socratic-style dialogue and a culminating rhetoric emphasis. There is consistent emphasis on parent partnership and a public-facing mission to partner with families in educating children to think and act biblically, seek truth and virtue, and pursue wisdom. Taken together, the school's public materials indicate a CLASSICAL orientation across most categories, with particularly strong evidence in Curriculum, Pedagogy, and Parents & Community. Some elements related to Faculty portrayal are less explicit, contributing to a plausible but somewhat weaker signal there, which places Element 5 as SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL. Overall, the school earns an aggregate CLASSICAL rating.
