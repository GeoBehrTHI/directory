## Executive Summary

Cabrini Co-op Id 83814 presents a robust Catholic, homeschool-cooperative model that strongly foregrounds religious formation, liturgical life, and family partnership. Public materials repeatedly emphasize Catholic identity, sacramental life, and the domestic church as central to learning, while curriculum highlights memory work, faith-infused humanities, and reverence for tradition. While the school demonstrates many core classical markers, it does not consistently foreground the full Great Books framework, the Seven Liberal Arts explicitly, or explicit Socratic pedagogy across all levels. Overall classification across categories largely centers in the CLASSICAL space, with especially strong alignment in Religious/Metaphysical Foundations, School Purpose & Goal, and Community/Parent Partnership. Some categories show only partial alignment (SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL), notably Curriculum and Pedagogy. The aggregate assessment leans CLASSICAL, with a clear emphasis on formation, virtue, liturgy, and Catholic intellectual heritage.
