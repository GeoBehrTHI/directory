## ABSTRACT

DHCA presents a CLASSICAL profile with strong religious-metaphysical foundations (biblical truth, Christ-centered worldview), a formation-centric school purpose and goal (virtue, wisdom, leadership under a Christian telos), and a robust faculty/parent/community framework that aligns with classical expectations (model teachers, living curriculum, parental partnership). The curriculum and pedagogy show clear classical signals (emphasis on wonder, historical-consciousness within a Christian framework, and rhetoric-related skill development) but are not uniformly explicit about canonical classical elements (such as explicit Great Books, grammar/logic/rhetoric progression, or fully documented Socratic seminars) in public-facing materials. Pathways and career-readiness components indicate a hybrid model, blending classical aims with modern workforce-oriented elements, which keeps Categories 3 and 4 as SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL, while the others remain CLASSICAL. Overall, DHCA’s public-facing content places it firmly within CLASSICAL territory, with particular strength in religious foundations, formation-driven goals, faculty culture, and parental/community engagement.
