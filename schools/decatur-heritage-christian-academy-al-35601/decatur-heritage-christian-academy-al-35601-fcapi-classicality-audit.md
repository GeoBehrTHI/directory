## Executive Summary

Decatur Heritage Christian Academy (DHCA) in Decatur, AL 35601 demonstrates a clearly Christian, formation-oriented mission with strong theological framing, explicit instruction in biblical truth, and intentional parent and community involvement. Across the six classical-education criteria, DHCA shows robust Religious and Metaphysical Foundations and a purposeful, formation-focused rationale for education. However, its public materials do not systematically foreground the Seven Liberal Arts (grammar, logic, rhetoric) or Great Books as foundational curricular pillars, and explicit, sustained Socratic pedagogy in the upper grades is not uniformly documented in the website copy available through the crawl. Taken together, the school leans toward CLASSICAL in several domains, but the absence of explicit Great Books/Trivium-Quadrivium framing and primarily job-formation language in curricular discourse push the aggregate toward SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL.

- Strong Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: explicit theology, biblical truth, and a Christian worldview; formal Statement of Faith; discipleship language; chapel offerings. Evidence includes statements about Jesus Christ, biblical truth, and a Christian worldview shaping education.
- Clear Purpose and Goal oriented to formation and virtue, with a Christ-centered telos: “Know, Believe, Transform” framework and mission/vision statements emphasizing formation, wisdom, and Christian identity.
- Curriculum is broad and career-path oriented, with interdisciplinary offerings and faith-formational aims, but lacks explicit Great Books or Trivium/Quadrivium emphasis in the public-facing material. DHCA presents Foundations Programs, career pathways, AP/dual enrollment, and foreign language tracks rather than a traditional liberal-arts triad.
- Pedagogy shows elements compatible with some classical approaches (integrated faith formation, occasional memory-like practices via structured memory work in classical schemas via external doc; weekly chapels; service opportunities). Explicit Socratic/seminar pedagogy is not consistently documented in the public pages, though the classical lexicon is used as a reference in the knowledge base.
- Faculty portrayal emphasizes living-the-lesson quality, Christ-centered mentorship, and a strong professional readiness; however, explicit “living curriculum” phrasing appears more in classical lexicon than in the school copy. Still, DHCA describes teachers as Christ-centered and capable of influencing students toward virtue.
- Parents & Community are strongly integrated: the school emphasizes partnership with parents, parent formation, and open campus/visit opportunities; a robust Parent Association and volunteer culture are documented.

Executive classification: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL. While DHCA grounds education in biblical truth and formation with strong faculty and parent engagement, the explicit liberal-arts (Trivium/Quadrivium) curriculum and sustained Great Books/Socratic pedagogy are not consistently foregrounded in the publicly crawled website content. The DHCA materials align closely with classical formation ideals but stop short of a fully explicit classical-curriculum declaration across all six categories.

- What would move the needle: To reach a full CLASSICAL designation, DHCA could publicly foreground (a) the Seven Liberal Arts (grammar, logic, rhetoric) as core curriculum anchors, (b) a Great Books/Great Conversation program interwoven with history and literature, (c) explicit Socratic pedagogy in upper grades with documented seminars, (d) signaling of liturgical practices as communal worship in a structured way, and (e) a consistent articulation of the “formation not information” motif across all grade levels with explicit memory-work, catechesis, and imitation-of-exemplars language in site materials.
