# Classicality Audit: Hillsdale Academy

Executive Summary
- Overall classification: CLASSICAL
- Why: The school’s public materials repeatedly and explicitly affirm the hallmarks of classical education across all six categories: religious/metaphysical grounding (daily prayer, chapel), purpose and ends (formation in virtue and wisdom over mere college/career prep), curriculum (liberal arts with Latin, logic, rhetoric; primary sources; Great Conversation), pedagogy (teacher‑led, Socratic dialogue, memorization/recitation), faculty (teachers as models and mentors), and parents/community (parents as primary educators and genuine partnership). See especially: “objective standards of truth, goodness, and beauty,” required Latin, rhetoric and logic in the Upper School, Socratic dialogue and primary sources, daily communal prayer, and weekly chapel services   .
- School URL audited: https://academy.hillsdale.edu/ 

Note on method and lexicon
- This audit applies the Six Essential Elements framework and its Positive/Negative Keyword Indicators for each category, cross‑checking school language against that lexicon (e.g., truth, goodness, beauty; theology; liturgy; liberal arts; trivium; great books/Great Conversation; Socratic; scholé; wonder; chanting; project‑based/experiential as possible negatives)     .

Aggregate Rating
- Overall: CLASSICAL
- Rationale: Five of six categories rate CLASSICAL with abundant positive indicators and zero negatives. Parents & Community is strong on partnership but lighter on explicit parent‑education programming and open‑campus language; nevertheless, overall classicality is unambiguous and robust, with explicit statements about truth, goodness, beauty; a classical liberal arts curriculum with Latin, logic, rhetoric; Socratic pedagogy; and liturgical/communal practices integral to school life   .
