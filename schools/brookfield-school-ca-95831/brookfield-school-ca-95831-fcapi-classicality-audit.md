# Classicality Audit Report: Austin Classical School

Website audited: https://austinclassical.org

Executive Summary
- Overall classification: CLASSICAL
- Why: The school explicitly or implicitly matches the Six Essential Elements framework across mission, curriculum (Trivium, Latin, logic, rhetoric, Great Books), pedagogy (memorization, recitation, songs and chants; debate/discussion; senior thesis/orations), Christian theological grounding (mission to glorify Jesus Christ; Christian Studies sequence; chapel), and a strong parent partnership model (co‑teacher parents in a collaborative schedule). The presence of ACCS accreditation further corroborates classical Christian alignment. Evidence includes frequent use of terms such as truth, goodness, beauty; virtue; Trivium; Latin; rhetoric; chanting; chapel; and explicit statements about formation over utilitarian, career‑prep aims (e.g., “less about training for a specific, temporal vocation and more about…eternal purpose”)     .
- Most important insights:
  - Religious and metaphysical foundations are explicit, Christ‑centered, and practiced communally (All‑School chapel noted in tour schedule; Christian Studies from doctrine to apologetics)  .
  - Purpose centers on cultivating wisdom and virtue through contemplation of the true, good, and beautiful—aligned with classical moral realism and formation (not mere information)  .
  - Curriculum explicitly names the Trivium, Latin, logic, rhetoric, Great Books, and integrated knowledge—all core positive indicators in the Six Elements lexicon  .
  - Pedagogy shows grammar‑stage memory work with “songs and chants,” logic‑stage debate/discussion, and rhetoric‑stage thesis and oratory—precisely the practices the framework calls out   .
  - Parents and community are central due to the collaborative model with “co‑teacher parents,” a hallmark of classical parent partnership .
- Reference framework used: Six Essential Elements positive/negative keyword indicators for all six categories (Religious and Metaphysical Foundations, Purpose and Goal, Curriculum, Pedagogy, Faculty, Parents & Community)      and the consolidated classicality lexicon .

Aggregate Classification
- Overall: CLASSICAL
- Rationale: Five of six categories are clearly CLASSICAL with decisive keyword evidence from mission, curriculum, pedagogy, theology, and parent partnership. Faculty language is positive but thinner in explicit classical phrasing; still, nothing contradictory was found, so it scored SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL, not due to deficiency but due to lighter published specificity relative to the other areas.

Supporting Notes and Additional Evidence
- ACCS Accreditation (Spring 2025): “accredited by the Association of Classical Christian Schools (ACCS)”—a strong external signal of classical Christian identity .
- Upper School course progression shows integrated humanities and Christian studies anchored in primary sources and theological inquiry (e.g., Development of Christian Doctrine; Exegesis/Hermeneutics; Apologetics & Ethics; Senior Thesis) .
- Great Books/Great Conversation language and Trivium explanation are explicit and aligned with the Six Elements lexicon and classical frameworks  .
- Liturgical/communal practice: Visitors attend “All School chapel” before tours; this signals customary communal worship practice on campus days .
