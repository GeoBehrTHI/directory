## Executive Summary — Element-by-Element Snapshot

### Element 1: Religious and Metaphysical Foundations — CLASSICAL

Evidence
- School materials are embedded in a Christian context, with explicit God-centered content visible in crawl items (e.g., discussions of names of God and biblical passages). This indicates a religious-metaphysical orientation that aligns with a theologically grounded education. 
- The Headwaters Academy materials appear within a church site, linking the school to a Christian worshiping community, which supports liturgical and communal dimensions of education. 

Positive/Negative keyword analysis
- Positive indicators present: theology, God, Jesus, biblical content, worship-oriented framing. These map onto the Positive Keyword set for Religious and Metaphysical Foundations (theology, sacred scripture, worship, etc.). The explicit biblical framing and the church context supply multiple positive signals.
- Negative indicators: none evident in the public crawl for this category (no explicit movement away from transcendental moral realism or from sacred tradition in the visible public materials).

Why this rating
- The public materials demonstrate a clear theological frame and liturgical/community orientation consistent with Grounded in Theology and Liturgical/Communal Practices. While the language of “truth, goodness, beauty” as a formal philosophical frame is not overtly stated, the explicit Christian metaphysical framework provides a robust religious grounding that, in classical terms, supports a favorable reading for this category. The combination of explicit biblical/theological content and church-based community practice aligns with the rubric’s expectations for Religious and Metaphysical Foundations. 

### Element 2: School Purpose and Goal — NOT CLASSICAL

Evidence
- Public materials show a Christian-educational context and a church-linked program, but there is no explicit articulation of formation for virtue and wisdom as the telos of education, nor a clear claim that truth, goodness, and beauty are real and knowable in a classical sense. The clearest public signals are religious rather than philosophical or humanistic aims. 

Positive/Negative keyword analysis
- Positive keywords for Classical Education (e.g., formation, virtue, liberal education, telos, the true/good/beautiful) are not clearly present in the public Headwaters materials.
- Negative signals: The content lacks explicit statements about human excellence, paideia, or the Great Conversation as the telos of education.

Why this rating
- Without explicit articulation of classic aims (virtue formation as telos, formation over mere information, or the real knowledge of truth/goodness/beauty in a classical sense), the category fails to meet the core Element 2 criteria in the classical rubric. The religious framing alone is not sufficient to meet the classical purpose/goal benchmarks.

What Would Move the Needle
- If the school were to shift toward a CLASSICAL profile, substantive changes would include:
  - Explicit articulation of a classical purpose and telos (virtue formation, pursuit of truth/goodness/beauty) beyond church affiliation.  
  - Development and public articulation of a classical curriculum (grammar, logic, rhetoric; Great Books; history as Western Civilization; Latin/Greek as offered).  
  - Adoption and public description of classical pedagogy (Scholé/restful learning, memory work, narration, rhetoric training, Socratic seminars in upper grades).  
  - Clear faculty model statements (the living curriculum, teacher portraits, professional development in classical pedagogy).  
  - Strong parent/community scaffolding (parents as co-teachers, ongoing formation opportunities, open campus visits, formal partnerships and book clubs).  
  - Evidence of a Great Conversation with canonical texts and disciplined study in the curriculum.  
  - Public documentation of grade-level practices in K-12 that align with classical education (including upper-grade Socratic engagement and lower-grade chant/memory practices).  

Note: No “needle-moving” changes are mandated by the current evaluation because the aggregate rating remains NOT CLASSICAL; the rubric section designed to guide improvements for SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL schools is not triggered by this result.
