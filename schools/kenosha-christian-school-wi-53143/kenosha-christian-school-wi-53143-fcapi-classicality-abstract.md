## ABSTRACT

Kenosha Christian School (KCA) demonstrates a Christian, relationally oriented approach with clear religious foundations (CLASSICAL in Category 1) and a community-and-formation emphasis that permeates the school culture (SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL in Categories 2 and 6). However, the public-facing materials do not consistently present classical curriculum signals (NOT CLASSICAL for Category 3) nor classical pedagogy signals (NOT CLASSICAL for Category 4) or explicit faculty portraits and mentorship frameworks (NOT CLASSICAL for Category 5). Overall, the school earns a SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL classification, with meaningful religious and relational strengths and room to deepen canonical classical curricular and pedagogical elements.

- Categories and classifications:
  - RELIGIOUS AND METAPHYSICAL FOUNDATIONS: CLASSICAL
  - PURPOSE AND GOAL: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL
  - CURRICULUM: NOT CLASSICAL
  - PEDAGOGY: NOT CLASSICAL
  - FACULTY: NOT CLASSICAL
  - PARENTS & COMMUNITY: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL

- Aggregate classification: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL

- Key takeaways: The strongest classical signals are religious/theological foundations and family/community orientation. To move toward full CLASSICAL, the school should publicly adopt and articulate a robust liberal arts curriculum (grammar/logic/rhetoric and Great Books) and accompany it with explicit classical pedagogy and faculty development, while expanding parent formation and co-educator signaling.
