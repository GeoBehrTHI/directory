## ABSTRACT

Saint Sophia Classical Academy exhibits a robust CLASSICAL orientation across Religious and Metaphysical Foundations, School Purpose and Goal, Curriculum, and Pedagogy, with Parents & Community also aligning strongly to classical formation norms. Public materials emphasize Gospel-centered Christian formation, virtue, wisdom, and the truth/goodness/beauty triad as real and knowable, coupled with a liberal-arts–driven curriculum that includes Latin/Greek studies, Memoria Press materials, and engagement with Great Books and the Great Conversation. Pedagogy blends traditional teacher-led instruction in the lower grades with Socratic discourse and rhetorical training in the upper grades, consistent with classical teaching methods. Faculty is credentialed and supported by ongoing training, though explicit articulation of a “living curriculum” portrait of the teacher is less prominent in the public materials, making Category 5 only SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL at present. Parental partnership is clearly endorsed, and school-community engagement is evident through events and campus visits.

- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: CLASSICAL

- Curriculum: CLASSICAL

Overall, Saint Sophia Classical Academy is CLASSICAL, with concrete opportunities to strengthen the Faculty category’s explicit living-curriculum language and to more fully foreground Great Books/Great Conversation in public materials to solidify the CLASSICAL standing across all six categories.
