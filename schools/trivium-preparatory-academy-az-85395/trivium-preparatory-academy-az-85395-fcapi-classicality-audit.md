## Executive Summary

Trivium Preparatory Az 85395 (Trivium Prep), a Great Hearts Academy in Goodyear, AZ, demonstrates strong classical education orientations across the six canonical elements. Public materials consistently emphasize Truth, Goodness, and Beauty as foundational aims; a liberal-arts curriculum anchored in the Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric) and Great Books; Socratic pedagogy; character formation; highly invested faculty; and an active partnership with parents and the broader community. Taken together, these signals indicate a CLASSICAL orientation with high fidelity to the classical school rubric, though explicit references to liturgical theology or formal liturgical practices are less prominent in the visible site content. The aggregate assessment across all six categories is CLASSICAL.

Key overall signals include:
- Mission and branding foreground Truth, Goodness, and Beauty as transcendent aims of education (evidence from multiple Great Hearts sources associated with Trivium Prep)   .
- Curriculum claims center on a classical liberal-arts program and engagement with Great Books and the Great Conversation (evidence in Academics/Curriculum descriptions) .
- Pedagogy emphasizes Socratic dialogue and memory-based practices, aligned with the classical model (e.g., “The center of our school is the classroom and the Socratic dialogue…”) .
- Faculty and community aspects highlight teacher as models of virtue and learning, small class sizes, and parental involvement as co-educators (evidence in charter/affiliations content and handbook tone)  .
- Parental engagement and community orientation appear as central to the school’s ethos (handbook language and mission statements) .

The following section-by-section analysis details keyword alignment with the classical-education rubric, including primary visible evidence, quantified keyword signals, and rating rationales for each category.
