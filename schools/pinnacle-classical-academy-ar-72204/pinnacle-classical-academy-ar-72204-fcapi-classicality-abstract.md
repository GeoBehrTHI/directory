## ABSTRACT

Pinnacle Classical Academy (Arkansas 72209) exhibits a cohesive CLASSICAL profile across all six categories of the classical-school rubric:

- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: Strong Christian/theological grounding with explicit statements that truth is rooted in Christ and that education forms character within a faith-centered framework. The school’s ethos and statements about a community of faith reinforce classical metaphysical commitments. Evidence includes “All truth and knowledge are rooted in Jesus Christ” and governance by a Statement of Faith. 
- School Purpose and Goal: Formation of virtue and wisdom is foregrounded, with a telos oriented toward love of God, service, and human excellence rather than mere job prep. The mission emphasizes lifelong learning and service in love to God and neighbor. 
- Curriculum: The Trivium (grammar/logic/rhetoric), liberal arts framing, Latin, and a focus on Great Books/Western civilization are explicitly present. The structure of Grammar (K4–6), Logic (7–9), Rhetoric (10–12) demonstrates a canonical classical progression. 
- Pedagogy: A blended approach of contemplative/restful pedagogy (Scholé) with memory work (singing/chanting) in the grammar stage and Socratic-style rhetoric development in the upper grades aligns PCA with classical teaching methods. Rhetoric focus and the aim to train students to articulate truth persuasively are evident. 
- Faculty: The faculty are presented as active agents in forming character, mentoring, and living the curriculum; leadership emphasizes in-house training and a living, mentorship-based culture. 
- Parents & Community: A strong partnership with parents as co-educators is central, with active PTA involvement, parent formation opportunities, and open-campus policies. This aligns with classical education’s emphasis on family and community formation. 

Aggregate assessment: CLASSICAL

Key sources drawn from PCA’s site and blog content illustrate a consistent classical-Christian orientation, including Trivium-based pedagogy, formation-focused goals, Latin and Great Books emphasis, and a living-curriculum faculty culture. Positive keyword indicators from the Six Elements lexicon (e.g., truth, virtue, theology, liturgy, wonder, formation, Great Books, grammar/logic/rhetoric) are evident in PCA materials; negative signals are not prominent in PCA’s public materials. The school’s explicit self-description as a classical Christian school with a mission to form hearts and minds in Christ solidifies its CLASSICAL standing.
