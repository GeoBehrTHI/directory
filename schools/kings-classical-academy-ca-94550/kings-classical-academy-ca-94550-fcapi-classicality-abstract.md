## ABSTRACT

Kings Classical Academy (CA 94550) demonstrates a robust CLASSICAL alignment across all six rubric categories. Religious and Metaphysical Foundations are explicitly Christian and theologically grounded, with chapel/community practices and a Biblical worldview permeating the curriculum. The School Purpose and Goal emphasize formation, virtue, wisdom, and Love of Learning as ends of education rather than mere job preparation. The Curriculum centers on grammar, logic, and rhetoric (the Trivium) with Bible/History, Latin, Mathematics, Science, and Civics/Economics threaded through the grades; Great Books and Great Conversation are explicitly highlighted as a core tradition. Pedagogy includes Socratic discussion, seminars, and rhetoric training in upper grades. Faculty language emphasizes virtuous educators, mentorship, and shared mission, while Parents & Community reflects strong parental partnership, co-teaching with families, and access to visits and faith-informed educational material. Across the six categories, there is clear evidence of direct alignment with “truth, goodness, and beauty” realized through formed character, classical discourse, and faith-integrated learning. The aggregate assessment is CLASSICAL.

Key category signals:
- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: Christ-centered, Biblical worldview, chapel, worship, Great Books/Great Conversation, theological framing.   
- Purpose and Goal: Formation and virtue as educational ends; wonder and wisdom as outcomes.  
- Curriculum: Trivium-based structure; Bible/History; Latin; Great Books; Civics/Economics; scope TK–12.  
- Pedagogy: Socratic/seminars/debates; rhetoric training; contemplative elements consistent with scholé. 
- Faculty: Mission-aligned, virtuous educators; recruitment language signaling moral formation and integrity. 
- Parents & Community: Strong partnerships with parents; Statement of Faith; openness to campus visits; parent education materials.
