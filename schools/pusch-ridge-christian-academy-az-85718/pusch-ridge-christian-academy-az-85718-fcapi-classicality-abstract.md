# ABSTRACT

- Classification: CLASSICAL
- What this means: PRCA demonstrates a classical, Christian education orientation across all six rubric categories—Religious Foundations, Purpose/Goal, Curriculum, Pedagogy, Faculty, and Parents & Community. The public materials foreground covenantal partnership, biblical worldview, Great Books / Great Conversation, grammar-logic-rhetoric, living books, Shakespeare and classical literature, Latin beginnings, recitations, chapel, and a family-centered governance model. The school uses a model in which faculty act as the living curriculum within a covenantal community and parents are co-teachers in a strong partnership with the school. The overall posture is deeply classical and Christian-oriented, with virtuous formation as its core aim. Keywords such as Christ, biblical worldview, covenantal community, Great Books, Great Conversation, trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric), living books, Shakespeare, Latin, recitations, chapel, and Covenant Parent Fellowship repeatedly surface in PRCA materials.

- Category-by-category snapshot (highlights)
    - Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: CLASSICAL — Christ-centered mission, covenantal community, biblical worldview, theology, chapel/worship, covenantal partnership.
    - Purpose and Goal: CLASSICAL — formation toward virtue, wisdom, truth-seeking; formation over mere information; real-knowing of truth/goodness/beauty.
    - Curriculum: CLASSICAL — Great Books, Great Conversation, grammar-logic-rhetoric (Trivium), living books, Shakespeare/Cervantes, Latin, integrated Bible across disciplines.
    - Pedagogy: CLASSICAL — lower-school memory/recitations and singing; upper-school seminars, debates, and rhetoric; chapel and community-learning rhythm.
    - Faculty: CLASSICAL — living-curriculum model, mentorship, professional development, leadership training; portrait-of-graduate alignment with virtue.
    - Parents & Community: CLASSICAL — Covenant Parent Fellowship, family involvement, co-teaching partnership, broad community engagement.

Note on sources

- The category signals and textual evidence cited above come from PRCA’s public materials that describe the mission, curricular framework, pedagogy, and community partnerships (including Covenant Parent Fellowship and Portrait of a Graduate). See mission statements and curriculum discussions in PRCA materials.

Abstract

- The evaluation concludes that PRCA operates as a CLASSICAL institution in six categories: Religious Foundations, Purpose/Goal, Curriculum, Pedagogy, Faculty, and Parents/Community. Textual evidence from PRCA materials demonstrates a covenantal Christian foundation, formation-focused aims, a liberal-arts curriculum anchored by Great Books and the Trivium, a pedagogy mixing memory-verse/recitations with Socratic-style seminars and rhetorical training, a faculty modeled as a living curriculum with professional development, and a strong family/community partnership ethos. The aggregate is CLASSICAL, with all six categories aligned to classical norms.
