## Executive Summary

Providence School of Tifton (Providence School of Tifton GA 31794) demonstrates strong alignment with classical education in its public presentation and curricular commitments, particularly around its Christian religious foundations, formation-focused goals, and dialogic pedagogy. The website materials emphasize classical Christian education, the Trivium and Quadrivium as the educational framework, and a pedagogy that blends Socratic inquiry in the upper years with memory- and singing-based approaches in the lower grades. There is clear evidence of a partnership ethos with families and an active community culture.

Overall, the school is best described as SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL. It clearly embodies core classical aims (virtue- and wisdom-centered formation; liberal arts orientation; Socratic and rhetorical elements in pedagogy; strong family/community alignment). It falls short of a full, explicit Great Books/global Great Conversation emphasis in its published materials and lacks some explicit articulations found in the fullest classical lexicon (e.g., some metrics of a mature “living curriculum” expressed in teacher portraits and a more explicit, cross-grade Great Books program). Nevertheless, the presence of Trivium/Quadrivium, the Christian teleology, and the mix of contemplative memory-ritual and Socratic discussion place Providence School of Tifton squarely within the classical spectrum.

- The site repeatedly references “classical Christian education” and a Christ-centered approach to learning, including external references to classical education and theology.
