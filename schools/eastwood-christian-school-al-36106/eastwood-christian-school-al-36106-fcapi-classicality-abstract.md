## ABSTRACT

Eastwood Christian School demonstrates a robust CLASSICAL alignment across categories, with particular strength in Religious and Metaphysical Foundations, School Purpose and Goal, Curriculum, and Parents & Community. The school emphasizes Christ-centered, biblically-based education rooted in Scripture, theology, and liturgical practices, alongside a Trivium-centered curriculum and a strong parental partnership. The Pedagogy and Faculty areas show strong classical intent, though explicit public articulation of certain scholé/restful learning practices and a more explicit “living curriculum” portrait of the faculty would further strengthen the overall classical alignment. The aggregate assessment remains CLASSICAL, with opportunities to further crystallize Pedagogy and Faculty in line with the classical lexicon.
