## Executive Summary

Aquinas Academy Pittsburgh, as reflected in its public materials, presents a strongly classical and Catholic educational orientation. The school foregrounds authentic Catholic formation, virtue, and a classically oriented program, and it emphasizes high-quality faculty and active parental engagement. Taken together with the classical rubric’s criteria, the school registers as CLASSICAL in most domains, with two areas showing more limited explicit alignment (Curriculum and Pedagogy) where the evidence is less explicit rather than non-existent. Overall, Aquinas Academy earns a Classically oriented designation with some room for deeper alignment in explicit Great Books/Great Conversation and explicit Socratic/Scholé-based pedagogy on the public-facing materials. This assessment relies on the school’s mission statements, explicit references to Catholic identity and virtue, and evidence of faculty quality and parent involvement, as documented in the crawl content. Key sources include the school’s mission and theology-focused framing and virtue-focused programming, as well as explicit statements about faculty quality and parent engagement.
