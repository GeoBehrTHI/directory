## ABSTRACT

Palm Beach Christian Academy (PBCA) Bridges presents a CLASSICAL orientation across Curriculum, Pedagogy, Faculty, and Parents & Community, anchored by explicit Christian-theological framing in Religious and Metaphysical Foundations, and formation-centered aims in School Purpose and Goal. The school emphasizes the Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric), Great Books, and Socratic dialogue as core curricular and instructional modalities, with a one-room schoolhouse structure that supports cross-age collaboration and a community-centered framework.

- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: Strong CHRIST-centered Scriptural grounding with Christ at the center and pursuit of truth; substantially classical in orientation, with explicit theology/Scripture signals and a lack of overt secular signals in public-facing materials.
- School Purpose and Goal: Formation and virtue orientation are evident, though explicit articulation of telos and contemplative scholé could be strengthened to achieve full CLASSICAL status in this category.
- Curriculum: Explicit Trivium, Great Books, and Socratic dialogue signals align with classical curriculum expectations; historic and liberal-arts emphasis is clear.
- Pedagogy: Socratic dialogue, seminars, and classical rhetoric training are emphasized, supporting CLASSICAL status; restful/Scholé and memory-based practices are implied, but would benefit from more explicit, across-grade articulation.
- Faculty: Public materials highlight a team-oriented, teacher-centered culture with “Meet the Team” and specialized teacher roles, aligning with the classical faculty ideal.
- Parents & Community: The partnership with families and open engagement signals strong classical alignments and family formation opportunities.

Aggregate classification: CLASSICAL, with Element 2 as SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL. The school demonstrates a strong classical orientation in theory and public-facing practice, and further explicit articulation in Element 2 (School Purpose and Goal) would push the overall evaluation toward a fully CLASSICAL standing.
