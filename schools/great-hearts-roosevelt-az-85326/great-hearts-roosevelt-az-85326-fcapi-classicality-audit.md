## Executive Summary

Great Hearts Roosevelt Buckeye (AZ 85326) demonstrates strong alignment with classical education as defined by the six essential elements. Public-facing materials from the school emphasize Truth, Goodness, and Beauty, the formation of character and virtue, and a liberal-arts orientation anchored by Great Books, Socratic inquiry, and a classical curriculum. The school also foregrounds parental partnership and community involvement, including a visible Parent Service Organization and ongoing parent engagement. Across the six categories, the public materials show a coherent classical orientation with limited evidence of modern utilitarian or purely job-centric aims.

Overall classification: CLASSICAL
