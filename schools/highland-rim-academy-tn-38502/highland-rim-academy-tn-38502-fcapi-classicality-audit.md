## Executive Summary

Highland Rim Academy (HRA) presents a robust CLASSICAL profile across all six classical criteria. Public materials consistently foreground a distinctly Christian, Protestant worldview and a classical framework (grammar, logic, rhetoric) as the backbone of education. The school emphasizes formation of character and virtue, not merely information, and explicitly centers on truth, goodness, and beauty as real and knowable. Great Books / classical resources are integrated into its curricular philosophy, and teaching methods span from memory- and chant-oriented routines in the early grades to Socratic-style engagement in the upper grades, with an explicit emphasis on disciplined study, catechesis, and disciplined behavior. Family partnerships and parental formation are positioned as core to the mission, with frequent reminders that parents are co-educators and are welcomed into the school community.

- Overall classification: CLASSICAL

- Key takeaways: HRA openly commits to a Christian-formative, virtue-centered education grounded in the classical Trivium; the curriculum foregrounds grammar, logic, rhetoric, and Great Books; pedagogy blends memory-based routines in younger years with higher-order dialogue; faculty are framed as moral exemplars within a living-curriculum model; parents are invited into partnership and formation.

Evidence spans multiple public-facing pages and materials, including our Vision & Statement of Faith, the “Why Classical?” page, admissions and program descriptions, and parent-focused materials. See representative lines and sections cited in the category-by-category breakdown below.
