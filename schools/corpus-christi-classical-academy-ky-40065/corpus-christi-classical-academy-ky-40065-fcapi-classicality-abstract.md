## ABSTRACT

Corpus Christi Classical Academy (KY 40065) is a Catholic, classical-liberal-arts school whose public materials demonstrate clear alignment with all six elements of the classical education rubric. Religious and Metaphysical Foundations are robust, with explicit Catholic formation, liturgical life (Mass, Rosary, Angelus), Scripture-based teaching, and Magisterial guidance. The School Purpose and Goal centers on virtue, wisdom, and human flourishing through a formation-first teleology. The Curriculum foregrounds the liberal arts (grammar, logic, rhetoric) and the Great Books / Great Conversation tradition, with a strong emphasis on Latin, classical history, and integrated learning. Pedagogy blends contemplative Scholé-leaning approaches with Socratic dialogue, rhetoric instruction, and memory-based learning in the early grades. Faculty are portrayed as living exemplars and mentors at the core of the living curriculum, and Parents & Community are positioned as essential partners in the educational enterprise, with formal opportunities for parent formation and active involvement.

Final rating: CLASSICAL across all six categories, with an aggregate CLASSICAL classification.

Cited evidence across the analyses includes mission/Philosophy statements, liturgical life descriptions, curriculum outlines, pedagogy notes, faculty descriptions, and parent/community engagement materials.
