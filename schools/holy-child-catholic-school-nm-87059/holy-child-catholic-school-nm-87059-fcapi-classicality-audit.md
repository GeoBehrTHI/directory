## Executive Summary

Public-facing materials emphasize Christ-centered formation, the Catholic intellectual tradition, and a stated goal of forming the whole person—mind, soul, and body—while prioritizing truths such as truth, goodness, and beauty, as well as devotion to the Eucharist. The site also highlights a Great Books orientation (e.g., a course featuring Dostoevsky) and a focus on Western civilization, which aligns with core classical-education aims. Overall, the school sits solidly in the Classical camp, with significant strengths in religious/metaphysical foundations and formation-focused goals, while certain granular curricular and pedagogical details are less explicit in public materials. The aggregate assessment falls into CLASSICAL, with room for more explicit articulation of the full classical curriculum (grammar, logic, rhetoric) and traditional pedagogy (e.g., socratic seminars, scholé) in public-facing content.

The site also references a formal role for parents and families in the formation process, and a “Current Families” portal indicating ongoing family involvement.
