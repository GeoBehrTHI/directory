## ABSTRACT

Veritas Academy CA 92122 presents a CLASSICAL overall classification, with strong signals across all six classical categories. Religious and Metaphysical Foundations are anchored in Christian formation and a Trivium/Quadrivium curriculum, reflecting a transcendent teleology common to classical schools. The School Purpose and Goal emphasize formation, virtue, and wisdom, rather than mere information or job-readiness. The Curriculum centers on the liberal arts with the Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric) and Quadrivium (arithmetic, geometry, music, cosmos). Pedagogy blends contemplative, memory-based approaches in the lower grades with Socratic discussion, seminars, and rhetoric in the upper grades, consistent with classical education. Faculty are portrayed as living exemplars and core to the school’s mission, and parents/community are actively engaged as co-educators and partners in formation.

- Faculty: Leadership profiles emphasize classical-Christian formation and mentorship; faculty as central to the curriculum. 
- Parents & Community: Emphasis on partnership with families and parental involvement in learning and school life.
