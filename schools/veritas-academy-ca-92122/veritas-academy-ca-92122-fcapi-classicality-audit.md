## Executive Summary

Veritas Academy CA 92122 presents a robust classical education profile anchored in Christian formation. Public materials from the school emphasize a Trivium-Quadrivium curriculum, a formation-centered purpose, and pedagogy that blends contemplative, memory-based practices in the lower grades with Socratic, rhetoric-focused instruction in the upper grades. Leadership pages frame teachers as core to the living curriculum, and parent partnerships are foregrounded as a key element of the school’s community model. Collectively, these signals align strongly with classical education as defined in the Six Essential Elements framework. Overall classification: CLASSICAL.

Key takeaways
- Religious and metaphysical foundations are foregrounded through explicit Christian framing and a doctrinally resonant education approach (e.g., Christian formation, Trivium/Quadrivium). Evidence includes explicit statements about Christian education and the Trivium/Quadrivium framework. 
- The school’s purpose centers on formation and virtue rather than mere information or job preparation, with language pointing to formation of character and a Christian telos. 
- Curriculum centers on the liberal arts (grammar, logic, rhetoric) and the Trivium/Quadrivium, signaling a classical curriculum architecture. 
- Pedagogy is distinctly classical: lower grades engage in memory-based and chant-like activities; upper grades employ Socratic discussion, seminars, and rhetoric-focused activities. 
- Faculty are presented as living exemplars and core to the school’s mission; leadership materials emphasize classical-formation orientation. 
- Parents and community partnerships are central, with explicit calls for family involvement and open engagement, indicating a co-educative model.
