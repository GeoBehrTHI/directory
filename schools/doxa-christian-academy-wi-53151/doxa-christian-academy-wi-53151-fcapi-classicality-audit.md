## Executive Summary

Doxa Christian Academy Wisconsin 53151 presents as a clearly classical Christian school, oriented toward formation in virtue and wisdom within a Christian-theological frame. Public-facing materials consistently frame education as a Christian vocation and a classical enterprise (not merely information transfer), with explicit references to the Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric), Great Books oriented study, biblical languages (Biblical Greek), and a governance/culture anchored in Reformed theology and a Statement of Faith. The school emphasizes partnership with families, parental formation, and open campus-style engagement, aligning with the classical model of a school as a living, communal project rather than a purely institutional setting. These signals are reinforced by statements about “All truth is God’s truth,” the integration of theology across disciplines, and a commitment to classical pedagogy and culture rather than secular, utilitarian schooling. Evidence drawn from the Doxa site materials shows: clergy/teachers modeling virtuous teaching and the school positioning itself as collaborative with families and churches; a clear commitment to Great Books, the Trivium, Biblical Greek, and Socratic/seminar-style inquiry in upper grades; and explicit statements about avoiding secular/state-driven approaches in favor of a Christ-centered, classical framework. The knowledge-base rubric (Six Essential Elements) corroborates this orientation by listing transcendental moral realism, theology, liturgical/community practices, Great Books, the Trivium, and strong parental partnership as core indicators of classical education.

Key textual signals include: “Doxa Christian Academy is a classical Christian school” and “We are Classical” with a linked description of the classical method aligned to child development; explicit affirmation that “All truth is God’s truth” and that the curriculum includes the Trivium, Great Books, Biblical Greek, and a formation-first aim; roots in Reformed theology with a Statement of Faith; and a stated partnership with families (parents as co-teachers) and an open-campus/visit approach. These signals appear across multiple pages (Admissions, About/Christian Education, FAQ, Employment bios, and faculty pages).     

In sum, Doxa Christian Academy Wisconsin 53151 scores CLASSICAL across all six categories, with particular strength in religious/philosophical foundations, purpose and goal, curriculum, pedagogy, faculty, and parent/community partnerships. The public materials demonstrate a coherent classical-Christian program anchored in virtue, truth, and beauty, with a robust Trivium-based curriculum, Great Books orientation, and a strong parental-partnership model that aligns with classical norms.
