## ABSTRACT

Doxa Christian Academy Wisconsin 53151 presents a robust CLASSICAL profile across all six classical-education criteria:

- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: The school anchors education in Christian-theological commitments, explicitly asserting “All truth is God’s truth,” leveraging Reformed theology, and linking curriculum to a biblical worldview. This aligns with transcendental realism and theology signals in the Six Elements framework.  

- School Purpose and Goal: Education is framed as formation of virtue, wisdom, and human flourishing, consistent with classical aims (formation over information). Great Books/Great Conversation and telos are reflected in the public materials and the classical-education framing. 

- Curriculum: A Trivium-centered liberal-arts curriculum, Great Books, original texts in original languages (Biblical Greek), and a history-driven, integrated approach anchor the curriculum in classical structures. The materials emphasize grammar, logic, rhetoric, and study of primary sources.  

- Pedagogy: A blended pedagogy of contemplative/restful learning (Scholé) in younger grades with Socratic discussion, seminars, debates, and rhetoric in upper grades maps directly to classical teaching methods described in the Six Elements lexicon. 

- Faculty: The living-curriculum model and teacher-mentors that exemplify virtue and scholarship align with classical expectations of the faculty as exemplars and living exemplars of the curriculum. 

- Parents & Community: A strong partnership with families, parental formation, and open-campus access reflect the classical emphasis on parents as co-teachers and engaged participants in the school’s mission. 

Overall, the school exhibits a fully CLASSICAL orientation across the six categories, with textual signals corroborating the core elements described in the Six Essential Elements of a Classical School (1). The present materials—focused on truth-governed, theologically grounded formation; classical curriculum and pedagogy; virtuous faculty; and active parent/community partnership—provide a cohesive CLASSICAL portrait that aligns with the canonical classical-education rubric.

Cited basis for the abstract: comprehensive corroboration across school content (Christian/theological framing, classical curriculum and pedagogy, Great Books/Trivium focus, and parent-partnership models) and the Six Elements keyword framework.
