## Executive Summary

Ann Arbor Orthodox Classical Academy (A2OCA) presents a robust alignment with classical education ideals, anchored in an Orthodox Christian framework. Public-facing materials consistently emphasize formation of wisdom and virtue, a Christian telos, and engagement with classical curriculum and pedagogy (Trivium/Quadrivium, Great Books, memorization, and Socratic dialogue in appropriate grade bands). The school foregrounds a liturgical, communal, and parent-partnered approach, with explicit statements about being Christ-centered and about parents as first educators. Overall, the school demonstrates CLASSICAL alignment across most categories, with particularly strong emphasis in religious foundations, purpose/goal, and curriculum, and clear indicators in pedagogy and community. The Faculty category shows substantial presence (staff, teachers, leadership), but lacks consistently explicit language about the “living curriculum” or teacher as exemplar in the public materials, suggesting a SLIGHTLY less explicit fit in that category. Aggregate assessment: CLASSICAL.

What would move the needle

- Strengthen explicit articulation of the faculty as the living curriculum: add statements on teacher exemplarity, mentorship, and the role of teachers as living embodiments of classical formation (e.g., portraits of a teacher, teacher training in classical pedagogy, and teacher-student mentor relationships).
- Expand public-facing language to consistently tie faculty roles to classical formation (e.g., “the faculty are the living curriculum” and “great books seminars for faculty”).
- Maintain and amplify explicit language around “Scholé” and contemplative/restful learning as a core pedagogical principle across more grade levels, with concrete examples and policy statements.
