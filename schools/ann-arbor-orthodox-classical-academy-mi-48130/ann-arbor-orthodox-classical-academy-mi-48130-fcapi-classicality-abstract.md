## ABSTRACT

Ann Arbor Orthodox Classical Academy (A2OCA) demonstrates CLASSICAL alignment across the majority of classical criteria. The school positions itself within an Orthodox Christian framework, foregrounding truth, goodness, beauty, and virtue as transcendent arereal ideals. Its mission centers on wisdom and virtue in Christ, with education framed as formation of mind and soul, not mere information. The curriculum emphasizes liberal arts (grammar, logic, rhetoric; quadrivium), Great Books, and historical study, reflecting classical content and structure. Pedagogy includes contemplative/restful elements (scholé) alongside Socratic discussion and rhetoric training, aligned with higher-grade classical teaching methods. Public materials show robust parental partnership and community integration, with explicit language about parents as first educators and family involvement in classical education.

However, the Faculty category shows substantial organizational presence (staff, instructors, board) but lacks explicit public articulations of the teacher as the “living curriculum” or of teachers as exemplar models within the classical framework. This leaves a slight gap relative to a fuller CLASSICAL fit in the Faculty criterion. Despite this, the aggregate assessment remains CLASSICAL, driven by clear signals in religious foundations, purpose, curriculum, pedagogy, and parent/community.
