## Executive Summary

Great Hearts Harveston LA 70810 presents a strong classical-education orientation across most rubric categories, anchored by a public mission to cultivate minds and hearts through truth, goodness, and beauty. Public materials emphasize virtue formation, a liberal-arts curriculum, Socratic inquiry, and a high regard for memory, language study, and the Western intellectual tradition. Taken together, the school demonstrates a predominantly classical profile with some areas that are less explicit in the public materials (notably explicit liturgical/theological foundations and explicit parent-as-co-teacher language). The aggregate classification across the six categories is CLASSICAL, with Category 6 (Parents & Community) showing only partial alignment due to limited explicit wording about parental co-teaching and formal parent-education programs in the public materials.

Key textual evidence includes: the mission statement and school materials stating a pursuit of truth, goodness, and beauty and a commitment to virtue formation and a liberal-arts education; explicit reference to Socratic instruction, memory work, and a two-teacher model in the lower school; a curriculum featuring humane letters, Latin, Euclid, and other classical elements; and strong parent-facing activities such as open enrollment, information nights, and campus tours. See cited passages for details.
