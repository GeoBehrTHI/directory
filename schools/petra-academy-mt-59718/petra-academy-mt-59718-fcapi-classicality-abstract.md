## ABSTRACT

Petra Academy (Bozeman, MT, 59718) demonstrates a comprehensive CLASSICAL profile across all six categories of the classical education rubric. Its religious and metaphysical foundations are robust, anchored in Christian theology, Scripture, creedal traditions, and a paideia-centered vision that integrates truth, goodness, and beauty with worship and community life. The school’s stated purpose emphasizes formation and virtue over mere information or workforce preparation, aligning with the transcendental aims of classical education. The curriculum explicitly centers grammar, logic, and rhetoric, complemented by the Quadrivium, Great Books, primary sources, Latin, and Bible integration, producing a deeply classical academic program. Pedagogy blends contemplative, memory-based practices in early years with Socratic dialogue, seminars, and rhetorical training in upper grades. Faculty are portrayed as virtuous mentors who embody and deliver the living curriculum, and parents are viewed as co-teachers and partners in a faith-informed community. Across all six categories, Petra Academy (Bozeman, MT, 59718) presents a coherent, faith-rooted classical approach that aligns closely with the Six Essential Elements of a Classical School (1).
