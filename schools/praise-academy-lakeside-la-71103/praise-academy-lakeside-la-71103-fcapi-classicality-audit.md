## Executive Summary

Praise Academy Lakeside (Lakeside, LA 71103) presents clear Christian orientation in its public-facing materials, with emphasis on literature, mathematics, science, and fine arts. The site conveys a character-formation emphasis through biblical framing (e.g., “A Neighborhood Christian School” and references to God and scripture). However, explicit classical-grounded elements such as a formal Great Books program, a stated Trivium/Quadrivium curriculum, Socratic pedagogy, a “living curriculum,” or an explicit partnership framework with parents are not clearly evidenced on the public materials that address curriculum structure, pedagogy, or community engagement. Using the Six Essential Elements rubric (with positive/negative keyword indicators) as a lens, the school demonstrates some classical orientation (especially in religious/metaphysical tone and some curricular emphasis) but does not consistently meet the core criteria across all six categories. Overall, Praise Academy Lakeside falls into the category: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL.

Key insights
- Strong religious framing is evident and pervasive (Christian school identity; biblical reference). This aligns with the Religious and Metaphysical Foundations category in part, but there is limited explicit attestation to specific classical metaphysical theses in the public materials beyond a Christian worldview.
- The curriculum speakers emphasize Great Literature (imagination) and solid Math/Science and Fine Arts, which hints at some classical elements (especially in curricular emphasis on beauty and literature). However, there is no explicit articulation of the Trivium/Quadrivium framework, Great Books program, or explicit Great Conversation language in public materials.
- Pedagogy, faculty, and parent/community engagement practices are not clearly described in classical terms (e.g., Socratic seminars, living curriculum, parent co-education). Therefore, Pedagogy, Faculty, and Parents & Community categories do not demonstrate strong classical signals in the available public materials.
- The aggregate assessment is therefore SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL, with room to advance toward CLASSICAL through explicit articulation and implementation of core classical elements.
