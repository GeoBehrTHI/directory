## Executive Summary

Oakdale Academy (Waterford, MI; Oakdale Academy MI 48329) presents a deliberately classical, Christian model of education. Public-facing materials repeatedly foreground a classical, liberal-arts orientation (grammar-logic-rhetoric via the Trivium and Quadrivium), a capitally religious framework (Statement of Faith/Apostles’ Creed, daily prayer, chapel rituals), and a mission centered on character, truth, and wisdom. The school emphasizes virtue formation, Great Books-oriented study through a structured curriculum, and explicit parent partnership. Taken together across six rubric categories, Oakdale Academy demonstrates a robust Classical alignment with strong religious/metaphysical foundations, formation-driven purpose, a curriculum steeped in liberal-arts and Great-Books sensibilities, a pedagogy that includes both contemplative and Socratic-style elements, a faculty culture framed by traditional teacher mentoring and governance, and an active, faith-informed engagement with parents and the broader community.

- Overall classification: CLASSICAL
- Strengths: Clear religious/theological grounding; explicit classical curriculum elements (Trivium/Quadrivium, Great Books orientation); explicit formation language (Character, Truth, Wisdom); strong parent-community orientation and formal governance structures.
- Areas that are already well-aligned but could be communicated even more explicitly (without departing from core philosophy): continuing explicit Socratic pedagogy in upper grades; broader public articulation of Great Books program and its integration with history/lumanities; sustained emphasis on a liturgical cadence and communal worship as formal schooling practices.

- Upper levels emphasize seminars, Socratic discussion, and the development of rhetorical skills and public speaking; the Curriculum maps reference rhetoric training and capstone-style presentations.

Representative terms observed include Grammar School, Logic School, Rhetoric School, recitation, Socratic seminar, capstone, and rhetoric training.

Oakdale materials show classical sequencing and memory-based/recitation components rather than exclusively project-based pedagogy, which aligns with classical indicators rather than negative signals (no explicit “experiential-only” or “constructivist-only” framing in the public materials).
