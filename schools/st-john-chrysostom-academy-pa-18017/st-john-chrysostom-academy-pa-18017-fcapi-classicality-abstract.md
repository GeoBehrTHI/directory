## ABSTRACT

St John Chrysostom Academy (Bethlehem, PA 18017) is classified as CLASSICAL. The school presents a robust Orthodox Christian formation integrated with a knowledge-centered classical curriculum. Public materials emphasize Truth, Goodness, and Beauty as real and knowable, liturgical life and communal worship, and the Orthodox tradition as the ethical and metaphysical backbone of education. Curriculum evidence demonstrates the liberal-arts foundation (grammar, logic, rhetoric; Quadrivium), Great Books, history across the curriculum, Latin/Greek, and a history-education emphasis, all within a unified, wonder-filled pedagogy. Pedagogy blends memory-based practices (recitation, chanting, memory work) with Socratic seminars and rhetoric training in upper grades. Faculty are framed as living exemplars and mentors, and parents are positioned as co-educators in an engaged community. Collectively, these signals meet the classical rubric across all six categories: Religious and Metaphysical Foundations; School Purpose and Goal; Curriculum; Pedagogy; Faculty; and Parents & Community. The aggregate assessment is CLASSICAL, with no category requiring a needle-moving move toward another classification.
