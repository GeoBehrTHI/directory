## Executive Summary

St John Chrysostom Academy (Bethlehem, PA 18017) presents a robust classical Orthodox education model. The public materials consistently emphasize a knowledge-centered curriculum anchored in Orthodox Christian formation, with explicit attention to Truth, Goodness, and Beauty as real and knowable realities. The school publicly foregrounds classical elements (Great Books, Trivium/Quadrivium, memory work, liturgical life) and a pedagogy that blends memorization and chant in lower grades with Socratic seminars and rhetorical training in upper grades. Family partnership and a liturgical, communal life are repeatedly highlighted, aligning with the classical rubric’s expectations for religious/metaphysical foundations, formation of character, and a communal school culture. Overall, the school aligns with a CLASSICAL orientation across all six categories. Evidence from multiple sources on the site demonstrates explicit commitments to classical education, Orthodox formation, great books and memory-based pedagogy, and active parental involvement.
