## Executive Summary

Gulf Coast Classical Academy (GC Classical) presents a robust classical-leaning profile with clear Christian grounding and a curriculum emphasis on liberal arts, Great Books, and classical languages. Public-facing materials emphasize wisdom and virtue, objective truth, and beauty as organizing ideals, all anchored in Christian theology and the Nicene Creed. The school highlights a traditional, teacher-led framework with a tutor-coop model for homeschooling, a strong emphasis on character formation, and a focus on family involvement. Taken together, these signals align GC Classical with a classical education framework, though explicit, high-school–level Socratic pedagogy and some formal curricular rituals (e.g., scholé days, formal Great Conversation sequencing) are less overt in the site copy. Overall, GC Classical is evaluated as CLASSICAL across the six rubric categories, with some Element 4/5 areas showing partial alignment and room for further alignment to a fuller, more explicit classical pedagogy and parent-education ecosystem.
