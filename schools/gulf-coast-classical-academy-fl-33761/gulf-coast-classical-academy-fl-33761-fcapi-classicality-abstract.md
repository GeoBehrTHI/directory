## ABSTRACT

Gulf Coast Classical Academy (GC Classical) demonstrates classically aligned signals across the six categories, anchored by a Christian-theological foundation, a formation-focused purpose, a curriculum centered on liberal arts and Great Books, and strong parent/community engagement. Public materials express a clear commitment to objective truth, goodness, and beauty; a Great Books/liberal-arts curriculum with classical languages; and a tutor-led homeschooling/co-op model that foregrounds character formation. Pedagogy and faculty signals are present but less explicit in canonical classical pedagogy terms; thus Pedagogy and Faculty are marked as SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL, while Religious Foundations, Purpose/Goal, Curriculum, and Parents/Community are CLASSICAL. Overall, GC Classical is classified as CLASSICAL with room to articulate more explicit classical pedagogy and a more formalized faculty-living-curriculum language in public materials to achieve full alignment.

Cited sources and evidence:
- Core Christian grounding and Nicene Creed framing. GC Classical is grounded in Christianity: “GC Classical is grounded in the precepts of Christianity, and we uphold Christian values and the Nicene Creed.” 
- Formation language and truth/goodness/beauty framing. “We equip them with wisdom and virtue to recognize the objective truth, goodness, and beauty that exist in the world all around us.” 
- Classical-canonical curriculum signals (Great Books, liberal arts, classical languages). “In classical education, students study the classics and the classical languages that produced them and the liberal arts: the best that has been thought and said, and the intellectual skills that equip a student to think critically. A classical education cultivates wisdom and virtue through the study of the Great Books and develops basic critical thinking skills to recognize and defend truth, beauty, and goodness.” 
- Co-op/tutor-led and family-centered model. “Tutor-led homeschool support,” and related materials. 
- Community/discipline signals with biblical grounding. Public discipline policies emphasize biblical standards (“Everything that is worn, and the manner in which it is worn, should support learning… biblical principles”). 
- Faculty signals (public team rosters). The site showcases named faculty roles (Latin Tutor, History/Curriculum Advisor, etc.). 

End of Abstract.
