## Executive Summary

Veritas Academy (Dublin, OH, 43017) presents a clearly Classical Christian model of education rooted in the Trivium (grammar, dialectic, rhetoric) and a university-model approach that serves homeschooling families with three in-person classroom days per week. Public-facing materials emphasize a Christian worldview, adherence to classical methods, and a formation-focused aim (virtue, wisdom, and character) rather than mere vocational training. The school also foregrounds a robust “biblically-based curriculum” anchored in Scripture, with a stated mission to proclaim and defend the faith, and is affiliated with the Association of Classical and Christian Schools (ACCS). Across the six classical elements rubric, Veritas Academy demonstrates strong alignment with Classical ideals, with consistent emphasis on formation, virtue, truth, and Western-historical orientation, supported by explicit notes on the Trivium, Great Ideas of Western civilization, and shared parental partnership. Based on the textual evidence from the public-site materials and the Precepts in the classical rubric, Veritas Academy is rated CLASSICAL overall.

Key highlights:
- Religious and metaphysical grounding is explicit: Statement of Faith, biblical curriculum, and the Word of God at the center. 
- Purpose and goals emphasize formation, virtue, wisdom, and a Christian worldview (not just job training). 
- Curriculum centers on the Trivium (grammar, dialectic, rhetoric) and western-civilizational study; explicit emphasis on the Great Ideas shaping Western civilization. 
- Pedagogy aligns with classical practice through three-stage progression, rhetoric development, and opportunities for memory, recitation, and later Socratic-style inquiry (implied through the rhetoric stage and classical structure). 
- Faculty appears as a living curriculum with a visible leadership team and multiple faculty profiles, reinforcing mentor-figure roles. 
- Parents & Community are foregrounded via homeschooling partnerships, family involvement, and parental covenant/admissions processes.
