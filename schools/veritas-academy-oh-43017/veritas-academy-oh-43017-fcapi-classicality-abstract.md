## ABSTRACT

Veritas Academy (Dublin, OH, 43017) is categorized as CLASSICAL across all six classical-education criteria. The school is clearly anchored in a Christian worldview (Statement of Faith; biblically-based curriculum) and explicitly adopts classical education through the Trivium (grammar, dialectic, rhetoric), with a university-model, three-day classroom structure for homeschooling families. The curriculum emphasizes Western civilization, great ideas, and a formation-focused aim—virtue, wisdom, and truth—consistent with the Positive keyword indicators for a Classical school. The pedagogy follows the three-stage Trivium, with rhetoric-focused development in upper grades, aligning with the Classical Teaching Method criteria. The faculty is presented as a living curriculum, with a clear leadership team and a network of teachers who model virtue and knowledge. Parent and community involvement is foregrounded through partnerships with homeschooling families, admissions covenants, and parent engagement, aligning with the Classical model of family partnership and cultural transmission. Overall, Veritas Academy demonstrates a robust CLASSICAL alignment with explicit religious foundations, formation aims, a Trivium-centered curriculum, classical pedagogy, faculty as mentors, and active parent-community collaboration.
