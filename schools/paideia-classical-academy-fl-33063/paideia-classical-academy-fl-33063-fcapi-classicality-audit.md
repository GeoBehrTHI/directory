## Executive Summary

Paideia Classical Academy (Coconut Creek, FL 33063) presents a cohesive, strongly classical framework anchored in Orthodox Christian assumptions and a tripartite Trivium-based curriculum. Public materials from the school consistently emphasize Christ-centered, Orthodox identity, liturgical life (Saint Luke Chapel), and a classical pedagogy that foregrounds memory, virtue, and mastery. Across six classical criteria categories, the school demonstrates a robust Classical alignment, with explicit religious foundations, formation-focused purpose, a grammar-logic-rhetoric curriculum, explicit classical pedagogy, a faculty described as masterful mentors, and an explicit partnership model with parents and community.

- Pedagogy: explicit tripartite pedagogy (master-disciple-parent), with lower- and upper-school differentiation, and a memory/recitation emphasis (chanting, narration) consistent with scholé and contemplative elements. Evidence includes tripartite pedagogy and a stated emphasis on memory work and classical pedagogy.
