## Abstract

Kootenai Classical Academy presents a CLASSICAL model across Curriculum, Pedagogy, Faculty, and Parents/Community, with Category 1 (Religious and Metaphysical Foundations) showing SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL alignment due to strong emphasis on truth, goodness, beauty, and virtue, but less explicit theological/liturgical grounding in public-facing materials. The school’s materials consistently articulate formation over information, a liberal-arts orientation, Great Books, Trivium/Quadrivium concepts, Socratic inquiry, and a living-faculty culture. The public-facing evidence supports a robust classical education identity with explicit focus on virtue, wisdom, freedom, and civic virtue, alongside parent partnerships and active community involvement. Overall, KCA fits CLASSICAL, with notable strengths in Curriculum and Pedagogy, and with room to more explicitly foreground theological and liturgical dimensions in its public materials to reach a fully integrated religious/metaphysical alignment.
