## Executive Summary

Kootenai Classical Academy (KCA) presents a robust classical-education profile, with strong alignment in curriculum, pedagogy, and parental/community partnership. Public-facing materials emphasize a liberal-arts orientation, Great Books, memorization and recitation, Socratic inquiry, and a formation-of-character ethos centered on truth, goodness, and beauty. The school also foregrounds civic virtue, self-government, and a Hillsdale College Curriculum framework, indicating a structured, tradition-minded approach to classical education. While explicit theological grounding or liturgical practices are not prominently featured in the public materials, the core classical aims—formation of character, pursuit of transcendent truths, and a living, intergenerational culture of learning—are clearly present. Overall, the evidence places KCA in the CLASSICAL category across most categories, with Element 1 showing some tension due to limited explicit theological/liturgical framing in public materials, but still strong in moral-realist orientation and virtue formation.
