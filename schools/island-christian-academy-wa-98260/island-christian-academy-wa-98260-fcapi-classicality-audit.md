## Executive Summary

Island Christian Academy (ICA) in Langley, WA 98260 presents itself as a classical, Christian school operating within a faith-based community (including a campus hosted at a local church) and aligned with the Association of Classical and Christian Schools (ACCS). Public materials emphasize a classical identity and a mission to integrate faith and learning, with explicit statements about being a “classical school” and about preparing college-bound students. The site highlights a mission to partner with parents and to foster a Christian worldview, and it includes a visible commitment to a public-facing classical framework (via a “Classical Approach” page) and a staff-led leadership structure. That said, several classic elements of a rigorous classical program—such as explicit reference to liberal arts triads (grammar, logic, rhetoric), widespread deployment of Great Books/Great Conversation, and explicit pedagogical practices (Socratic seminars, scholé, etc.)—are not clearly documented in the public ICA materials provided here. The net result is a school that strongly signals classical and Christian aims in mission, branding, and governance, but with only partial explicit articulation of core classical curriculum and pedagogy details in the publicly accessible content.

- Aggregate classification: NOT CLASSICAL
- Overall rationale: The school clearly advertises classical and Christian orientation and a church-based context, with explicit statements like “we are a classical school focused on preparing college-bound students.” However, the public materials do not consistently document all six classical criteria (notably explicit Great Books/Great Conversation commitments, explicit liberal arts curriculum (grammar–logic–rhetoric), and fully described Socratic/Scholé pedagogy and a fully developed faculty/community formation model). This mix yields a global assessment of NOT CLASSICAL, with some category-by-category signals of classical alignment.
