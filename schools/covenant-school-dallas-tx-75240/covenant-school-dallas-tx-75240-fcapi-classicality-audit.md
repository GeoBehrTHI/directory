## Executive Summary

Covenant School Dallas (75240) presents a coherent classical Christian formation, deeply rooted in a Christ-centered worldview, and organized around the Trivium (Grammar–Logic–Rhetoric). Public materials emphasize formation, virtue, truth, and beauty, and the curriculum is clearly built on Great Books and classical disciplines, with explicit architecture for the three Trivium stages and a strong Charlotte Mason influence on aims like the cultivation of wonder and moral formation. The school also foregrounds partnership with families and active parental involvement. Overall, the school aligns strongly with classical education on most measures, with Element 4 (Pedagogy) representing the most notable gap toward full Classical status. The aggregate assessment is CLASSICAL, with targeted improvements outlined below.

Key evidence highlights:
- Religious and metaphysical foundations are explicit and central: mission states glorifying God and defending truth in service to Jesus Christ; history note emphasizes Christ-centered worldview and the Word of God.  
- Purpose and goal emphasize formation, virtue, wisdom, and the pursuit of truth, beauty, and goodness within a Christian, classical frame. The mission text foregrounds formation and lifelong learning toward discernment and truth. 
- Curriculum is recognizably Trivium-based (Grammar, Logic, Rhetoric) with mentions of Great Books and a Charlotte Mason influence; explicit description of the Triune structure (Grammar School, Logic School, Rhetoric School) appears across campus pages.  
- Pedagogy shows strengths in rhetoric, memory, and classical discussion at the upper levels, along with contemplative/memorable practices in lower grades, though explicit Socratic/seminar references are less overt in public materials. This category shows the most room for full alignment. 
- Faculty are described as experienced, with a living-curriculum approach and strong emphasis on mentorship, character formation, and a teacher-centered but collegial culture; the school highlights “100+ faculty” and leadership messages. 
- Parents & Community are foregrounded as co-educators and partners; the site repeatedly notes primacy of parents and invites ongoing parental involvement and visits.
