## ABSTRACT

Covenant School TX 75240 demonstrates a robust CLASSICAL profile across all six classical categories. Evidence from public materials shows: (1) Religious and Metaphysical Foundations anchored in a Christ-centered, theologically integrated framework with liturgical/community rhythms; (2) a School Purpose and Goal focused on formation, virtue, wisdom, and the pursuit of truth, goodness, and beauty; (3) Curriculum centered on the Trivium (grammar/logic/rhetoric), Great Books, living books, and a liberal-arts orientation; (4) Pedagogy that blends contemplative (Scholé) with Socratic discussion, memorization/recitation, and rhetoric training; (5) Faculty described as living curriculum and moral exemplars; and (6) Parents & Community characterized by active partnership, parent formation, and openness to visitation. This alignment is substantiated by Covenant’s mission/history pages, Trivium-based program descriptions, and evidence of Great Books and living-books pedagogy, as well as by the Six Essential Elements keywords framework, which identifies the positive indicators Covenant demonstrates in each category. Positive keyword signals across Covenant materials include truth, virtue, beauty, theology, liturgy, Great Books, Trivium, formation, and partnership with families, while negative signals are not prominently present in Covenant’s public communications.
