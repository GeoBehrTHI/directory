## ABSTRACT

Chesterton Academy of Atlanta is a Catholic, classical high school in the Atlanta area that foregrounds liturgical life, theology, and formation as its core mission. Evidence from public materials shows strong Religious and Metaphysical Foundations (daily Mass, Catechism, Magisterium fidelity), a teleology oriented toward formation and virtue, and a liberal-arts curriculum with Latin and Western-canon emphasis. The school’s public narrative also highlights a three-pillar pedagogical framework (Intellect, Character, Spirituality) and a faculty/governance structure consistent with classical education norms. While the school clearly exhibits CLASSICAL characteristics in most categories, Pedagogy appears to be the least explicit in public materials, suggesting a Somewhat Classical signal there. Collectively, CAATL earns an overall CLASSICAL designation, with a concrete path forward to move Category 4 into full CLASSICAL status.

Key evidence sources:
- Spirtual Formation: Catholic identity, daily Mass, theology from Bible and Catechism, adoration/retreats, and Magisterium fidelity. 
- Mission and Purpose: “joyful leaders and saints,” classical tradition, truth of Catholic faith, formation toward college/life. 
- Curriculum and Liberal Arts: Classical approach, Latin requirement, intellectual formation through Western thought.  
- Pedagogy signals: Three Pillars, emphasis on contemplative/restful elements and memory/chanting in lower grades, with rhetoric/public-speaking emphasis in arts; explicit Socratic/seminar language not consistently documented.  
- Faculty: Qualified Headmaster and governance (Board of Directors), canonical alignment, accreditation trajectory.  
- Parents & Community: Open houses, shadow days, parent information sessions, accessible admissions process.
