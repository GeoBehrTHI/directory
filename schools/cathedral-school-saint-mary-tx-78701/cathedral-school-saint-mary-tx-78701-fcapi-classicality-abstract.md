Overall Classification: CLASSICAL

# ABSTRACT

Cathedral School of Saint Mary (Austin, TX 78701) presents a robust CLASSICAL profile across six dimensions of classical education as defined by the Six Essential Elements rubric.

- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: Strong Catholic identity, liturgical life, and theology-informed formation are evident in mission, philosophy, worship practices, and daily life. The school integrates faith life with learning, aligning with transcendental truths and liturgical communal practices. Evidence includes weekly Mass, adoration, rosary recitations, and a Christ-centered formation ethos.
- School Purpose and Goal: The school foregrounds formation of virtue and wisdom through a Catholic classical framework, emphasizing the whole-child development and the pursuit of truth, goodness, and beauty. The mission/vision language highlights formation over mere information or job preparation.
- Curriculum: The curriculum explicitly centers on the seven liberal arts and classical pedagogy (grammar, logic, rhetoric; Latin; Great Books; Great Conversation). There is explicit mention of Great Books and a classical model rooted in the trivium/quadrivium.
- Pedagogy: Teaching methods include contemplative/restful elements in early grades (singing, memorization) and Socratic/seminar-style inquiry and rhetoric training in higher grades. This mix aligns with classical pedagogy signals.
- Faculty: Public materials emphasize formation and collaboration with families; however, explicit language on the faculty as “the living curriculum” is not consistently deployed in the public-facing content, placing it as SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL rather than CLASSICAL at this time.
- Parents & Community: Strong emphasis on parental partnership, co-educator role, and open invitation for parent involvement and visits. This aligns with the classical model’s emphasis on family partnership and formation.

Overall, the Cathedral School of Saint Mary demonstrates a CLASSICAL orientation with a very strong Catholic-classical blend. The sole category with partial alignment (Faculty) reduces the aggregate to CLASSICAL, with concrete opportunities for the school to strengthen explicit language about the faculty as the living curriculum to move toward a fully CLASSICAL designation in that category.
