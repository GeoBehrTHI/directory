# ABSTRACT

Washington Classical Christian School Florida (St. Augustine) demonstrates a robust CLASSICAL orientation across all six rubric categories:

- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: explicit Christian/theological grounding (God’s Word, Bible, biblical worldview) and liturgical/congregational language, indicating transcendental moral realism and grounded theology. Evidence shows explicit integration of faith with learning.
- School Purpose and Goal: formation-focused aims with lifelong learning and a Great Books approach (per Bible/Theology, History and Literature) that aligns with virtue and wisdom-focused telos.
- School Curriculum: clear Trivium framework (grammar, dialectic, rhetoric); Great Books and primary sources; Latin instruction; integrated historical-literary study.
- Pedagogy or Teaching Methods: Trivium-driven pedagogy with rhetoric, public speaking, and memorization; upper-school rhetoric/seminar dynamics; lower-school memory and song-based reinforcement.
- Faculty: teacher-formation culture (godly teachers; living curriculum; portraits of teachers and collaborative faculty) that positions educators as central to the learning experience.
- Parents & Community: explicit partnership with parents as co-teachers; parent formation opportunities; welcoming open-campus environment.

Overall, this school meets the criteria for CLASSICAL by mapping strongly to the Six Essential Elements’ positive indicators and showing minimal presence of negative indicators in the public materials. The school’s publicly visible commitments to the Trivium, Great Books, Latin, biblical worldview, and parental partnership align with classical education norms, and the content demonstrates a coherent and formative approach to education that emphasizes truth, goodness, and beauty within a Christian framework.
