## Executive Summary

Founders Classical Academy Leander (FCA Leander) presents a robust alignment with classical education in its public-facing materials. The school foregrounds a liberal-arts, Virtue-centered mission rooted in truth, beauty, and goodness, and emphasizes formation over mere information. Its stated commitments include a traditional liberal arts curriculum (grammar, logic, rhetoric) and exposure to Latin, philosophy, and classical literature, with a strong emphasis on character formation, virtue, and the formation of wise and virtuous citizens. The school likewise highlights parental partnership and a community-oriented ethos, including a “co-educator” stance with families.

Using the Six Essential Elements rubric as a rubric, FCA Leander shows strong Classical alignment in several domains (Curriculum; Purpose and Goal; Faculty; Parents & Community), with Element 1 (Religious and Metaphysical Foundations) and Element 4 (Pedagogy) showing more partial alignment. The aggregate assessment places FCA Leander in the CLASSICAL range, with some room for deeper explicit integration of theology, liturgical practices, and explicit Socratic pedagogy in the upper levels to achieve full alignment.
