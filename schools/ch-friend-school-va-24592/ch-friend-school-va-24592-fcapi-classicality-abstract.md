## ABSTRACT

CH Friend School (Ch Friend School Va 24592) demonstrates a Christian-centered, teacher-led, hands-on approach with small-class advantages and clear parental partnerships. The school places a strong emphasis on virtue, character, and Christ-centered formation, with a structured sequence in early literacy and a high-school program that includes dual enrollment and career-technical offerings. In the classical-education rubric, CH Friend exhibits notable Classical touchpoints in Religious & Metaphysical Foundations, School Purpose & Goal, Faculty, and Parents & Community. However, the Curriculum and Pedagogy categories lack explicit classical pillars (no documented Great Books/Great Conversation, no clear Trivium/Quadrivium framing, and no sustained Socratic/Scholé pedagogy in the public materials).

Overall, CH Friend School is BEST described as SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL. The school displays core virtues of classical formation (virtue-centered aims, teacher-as-model, strong family partnership), but it stops short of a full Classical model due to absence of explicit Great Books, Trivium-based curriculum, and a constitutive Socratic pedagogy. Strengthening those elements, as suggested in the needle-move section, would elevate CH Friend toward CLASSICAL status.
