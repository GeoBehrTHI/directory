## ABSTRACT

Chesterton Academy Annunciation MD 21788 demonstrates a CLASSICAL profile across all six classical school criteria, grounded in Catholic doctrine and liturgical life, and organized around a formation-centered liberal-arts curriculum. Evidence from public materials shows daily Mass, Confession, Rosary, Examen, Adoration, and a novena; a formal commitment to formation of wisdom and virtue; a classical curriculum with Socratic inquiry and a Great-Books sensibility; a faculty described as virtuous mentors and living curriculum; and strong parent/community partnership. Public materials also indicate formal MSDE/NAPCIS recognition, confirming institutional alignment with classical standards. The Pedagogy category shows strong classical signals (Socratic method), while the Category 4 assessment notes room for more explicit documentation of the full spectrum of classical pedagogy (Scholé, memorization, etc.) in public-facing materials, hence the “SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL” designation for Pedagogy. Overall, the Chesterton Academy Annunciation MD 21788 earns a robust CLASSICAL rating, with room to more explicitly document some pedagogical routines in public materials to reach full canonical documentation.

- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: CLASSICAL — Catholic liturgical life, Theology integration, daily Mass, liturgy, and sacred life are explicit and central. 
- School Purpose and Goal: CLASSICAL — formation in virtue and wisdom, true/good/beautiful telos. 
- Curriculum: CLASSICAL — classical framing, liberal-arts orientation, Socratic method; Great Books/Great Conversation implied by curriculum language. 
- Pedagogy: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL — Socratic method explicit; further classical-pedagogy signals exist in materials but are not exhaustively cataloged publicly. 
- Faculty: CLASSICAL — living curriculum and mentorship; virtuous teachers; Magisterium-aligned teaching. 
- Parents & Community: CLASSICAL — partnership with families; parent formation; open interactions; community/Church-linked life; external recognition. 

In sum, the school is CLASSICAL.
