# ABSTRACT

St. Therese Classical Academy (Chester, NY, 10918) shows a coherent classical orientation within a Catholic framework, with strong signals in Religious Foundations and School Purpose/Goal and meaningful signals in Curriculum and Pedagogy via Chesterton-inspired practices. However, explicit, fully documented evidence of core classical elements—especially grammar/logic/rhetoric as curricular pillars, a fully articulated Great Books program, and explicit, systematic Scholé practices and a well-defined “living curriculum” for faculty—are not consistently evident in the public-facing material. The aggregate classification is SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL, with CLASSICAL indicators strongest in Foundation and Purpose, and more conditional signals in Curriculum, Pedagogy, Faculty, and Parents & Community. Concrete public articulation of the missing core elements would move the school toward a full CLASSICAL designation.
