## ABSTRACT

Chesterton Academy of Orlando presents a CLASSICAL model anchored in Catholic identity and a liberal-arts, Great Books-centered curriculum. The school features a faith-forward pedagogical approach that includes Socratic discourse, memory-based elements, and rhetoric training, alongside a strong emphasis on formation, virtue, and the transcendentals (truth, goodness, beauty). Faculty are depicted as living exemplars within a faithful community, while parents are positioned as co-educators and active participants in school life. Across six categories—Religious and Metaphysical Foundations, School Purpose and Goal, Curriculum, Pedagogy, Faculty, and Parents & Community—the materials consistently indicate CLASSICAL alignment, with explicit statements about Catholic theology, liturgical practices, the Great Books curriculum, Socratic pedagogy, lifelong mentoring, and robust parental partnership. The aggregate assessment is CLASSICAL, reflecting a coherent classical education framework rather than a hybrid or project- or career-first approach.

Direct textual anchors include: a classical-Catholic identity and mission (Catholic faith, Bible/Catechism, four years of Catholic theology/philosophy), liturgical and communal practices (Morning Prayer, feast-day celebrations), the Great Books and Great Conversation curriculum (Homer, Augustine, Dante, Shakespeare, Aquinas; Socratic discussions), a pedagogy that blends contemplative and rigorous methods (Socratic seminars, memory work, rhetoric, debate), a living/faithful faculty model, and a partnership-driven parents/community culture (Board governance, parental involvement, open campus). These signals collectively justify a CLASSICAL rating across all six categories.
