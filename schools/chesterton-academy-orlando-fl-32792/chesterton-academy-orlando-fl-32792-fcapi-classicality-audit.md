## Executive Summary

Chesterton Academy of Orlando (Winter Park, FL) presents a robust classical-Catholic educational model, with clear emphasis on the Great Books, Socratic discourse, and formation of virtue within a Catholic framework. Public materials depict a deliberately faith-centered curriculum in which theology, philosophy, and literature are woven into all subjects; community and family engagement are central, and the school emphasizes a life-oriented rather than career-focused aim for education. Taken together, the six-category rubric yields a predominantly CLASSICAL rating across RMF, Purpose & Goal, Curriculum, Pedagogy, Faculty, and Parents & Community. The school shows explicit commitments to truth, goodness, and beauty; formation of character through virtue; a Great Books/liberal-arts curriculum; Socratic pedagogy and rhetoric; a living, faithful faculty; and active parental partnership and community involvement.

In brief:
- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: CLASSICAL, with explicit Catholic identity, Biblical literacy, catechesis, liturgical practice, and daily prayer culture.
- Purpose and Goal: CLASSICAL, emphasizing formation, virtue, wisdom, and a life-oriented telos rather than mere job-prep or credentialing.
- Curriculum: CLASSICAL, centering on Great Books, liberal arts, and a Great Conversation orientation; history integrated through a classical lens; emphasis on wonder.
- Pedagogy: CLASSICAL, with contemplative/restful elements, strong Socratic seminars, and explicit rhetoric training.
- Faculty: CLASSICAL, featuring faithful, mentoring teachers and a living-curriculum approach.
- Parents & Community: CLASSICAL, with formal partnership with families and active parental involvement in campus culture and governance.
