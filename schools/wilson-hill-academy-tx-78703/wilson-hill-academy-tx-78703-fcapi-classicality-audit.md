## Executive Summary

Wilson Hill Academy (WHA) presents a public-facing stance and program framework that align strongly with Classical Christian education principles. The school identifies itself explicitly as offering Classical Christian Education and emphasizes formation of virtue, wisdom, and character rooted in a Christian worldview. Public materials frequently reference God, Scripture, virtue, and the Kingdom of Christ, and they highlight language study (Latin & Greek), engagement with classical forms of inquiry, and a community-centered approach (including family partnerships and open community life). These signals are reinforced by WHA’s own blog and program pages, including statements about being “rooted in Scripture” and training students to love what is true, good, and beautiful.

- Executive classification: CLASSICAL (overall)
- Notable strengths: Religious/metaphysical foundations; formation-focused purpose; explicit classical-language emphasis; engagement with Great Books/Great Conversation themes in at least some posts (e.g., Cloelia reference to The Great Conversation).
- Needle-movers for full classical status: explicit, consistent articulation of Great Books/Great Conversation as central to the curriculum across multiple grades; explicit Socratic/seminar-based pedagogy in upper grades; formal “living curriculum” and teacher portraits in public materials; sustained, long-term parent/community formation signals beyond a few posts.
