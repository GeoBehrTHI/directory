## ABSTRACT

Geneva Academy (West Monroe, LA, 71291) demonstrates a CLASSICAL orientation grounded in Christian theology, the Trivium-based curriculum, and a formation-focused mission. Religion and metaphysical foundations are explicit (Triune God, worship, liturgical life), and the school emphasizes formation of virtue, wisdom, and truth as central aims. The curriculum centers on grammar–logic–rhetoric with Latin and a strong sense of historical and moral formation, aligning with the classical emphasis on the Seven Liberal Arts and Great Books through its approach to knowledge in relation to faith. Pedagogy includes memorization, chant, and memory work, with upper grades potentially engaging in Socratic dialogue, though explicit public articulation of such pedagogy is less evident. Faculty are described in terms of mentorship and living curriculum, and the school actively partners with parents and emphasizes parent formation and open-campus access. Collectively, Geneva Academy scores CLASSICAL across categories, with Element 4 representing the area with partial alignment and room for additional public articulation of Socratic/secolé pedagogy to reach full classical status.
