## ABSTRACT

Kalos Christian Academy (KC A), located in Kansas City, MO 64117, demonstrates robust alignment with classical Christian education across all six classical criteria: religious/metaphysical foundations, purposeful formation, curriculum, pedagogy, faculty, and parent/community engagement. The school explicitly foregrounds truth, goodness, and beauty within a Biblical, Christ-centered framework, integrating these classical aims with a Charlotte Mason-inspired approach. The curriculum centers on the Trivium (grammar/logic/rhetoric), Great Books and the Great Conversation, with Latin study and living books across grades. Pedagogy blends contemplative and rigorous elements, using singing/chanting and memory work in the grammar stage, with Socratic seminars, debates, and rhetoric in the upper grades. Faculty are portrayed as mentors and living curriculum, supported by a governance and leadership structure, while parents are positioned as co-educators and partners in formation. The school’s mission emphasizes forming virtuous students who pursue wisdom and serve God, and community partnerships with families are actively pursued through events, seminars, and homeschool partnerships.

- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: CLASSICAL. The school’s Christ-centered, Biblical worldview and emphasis on truth, goodness, beauty align with transcendentals and theological grounding. Evidence includes statements about Scripture as authority and a Biblical worldview. 
- School Purpose and Goal: CLASSICAL. Formation and virtue-centered aims, coupled with a telos of wisdom and human flourishing, are explicit in mission and philosophy.  
- Curriculum: CLASSICAL. The Trivium, Great Conversation, Great Books, Latin, and living books are central, with history integrated across the curriculum.  
- Pedagogy: CLASSICAL. A balanced pedagogy with Konol (Scholé/restful) elements in lower grades and Socratic/seminar/rhetoric elements in upper grades is described. 
- Faculty: CLASSICAL. Faculty are depicted as mentors and living curriculum with a community culture and named leadership. 
- Parents & Community: CLASSICAL. Strong emphasis on parental partnership, formation, and community engagement; open campus and parent involvement are evident. 

Overall classification: CLASSICAL
