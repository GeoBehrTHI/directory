## Executive Summary

Kalos Christian Academy (KC A) in Kansas City, MO 64117 presents a strong alignment with classical Christian education across all six rubric categories. Public-facing materials consistently emphasize a Christ-centered, Biblical worldview integrated with classical pedagogy (Grammar-Logic-Rhetoric of the Trivium), a commitment to the Great Conversation and Great Books, habit-formation and virtue, living books, and rigorous, virtue-centered character formation. The school foregrounds Great Conversation, Latin study, and a Trinity of truth, goodness, and beauty as organizing ideals, while partnering with families and honoring parental formation. Evidence from KC A’s public materials indicates explicit engagement with Trinity-era classical aims (grammar-logic-rhetoric, Great Books, chant and memory in lower grades, Socratic/seminar activities in upper grades), and a clear emphasis on formation over mere information. The school’s mission, philosophy statements, and program descriptions consistently map onto the Positive Keyword indicators for each category and show minimal, if any, engagement with Negative Keyword indicators in the Classical lexicon.

Overall classification: CLASSICAL

Notes on aggregate: Across all six categories, the available public materials demonstrate the core features of classical education as defined by the Six Essential Elements framework: transcendental foundations, formation through the Trivium, Great Books, Socratic and rhetoric work in higher grades, a faculty embodying the living curriculum, and a robust partnership with parents and community. Where KC A aligns with the framework, it does so clearly and consistently, with a few elements—such as liturgical practices—being more implicitly present through Biblical worship and community life rather than formal liturgical schedules. The result is a robust CLASSICAL designation with no evident substantial weaknesses relative to the rubric.
