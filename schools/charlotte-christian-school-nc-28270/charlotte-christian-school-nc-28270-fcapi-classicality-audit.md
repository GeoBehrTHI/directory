# Classicality Audit Report: Austin Classical School

Website audited: https://austinclassical.org/ 

## Executive Summary
Overall classification: CLASSICAL

Austin Classical School presents a strongly classical, Christian identity. The mission centers on glorifying Jesus Christ and co-laboring with families to cultivate wise and virtuous students through classical education. Public materials emphasize truth, goodness, and beauty; the Trivium; Great Books; Latin; Socratic method; and a culminating senior thesis defense. Parent partnership is structurally embedded via a collaborative “co-teacher parent” model. Evidence for explicitly liturgical school-wide practices (e.g., daily chapel) is lighter than other elements, but the theological and metaphysical foundation is explicit and robust.    

Key strengths
- Metaphysical and theological clarity: mission to “glorify Jesus Christ,” Scripture-centered curriculum, formal Statement of Faith guiding instruction.   
- Purpose and telos: “cultivate wisdom and virtue,” education ordered to the true, the good, and the beautiful and to an “eternal purpose.”  
- Curriculum: Trivium by stages; Latin I–IV; primary-source/Great Books; history as history (not “social studies”).    
- Pedagogy: memorization/recitation and songs in Grammar; debate/discussion in Logic; explicit Socratic method; senior thesis defense in Rhetoric.    
- Parents & community: co-teacher parent model and regular tours/parent meetings.  

Method note: Keyword analysis was performed against the Positive/Negative Keyword Indicators for the “Six Essential Elements of a Classical School” framework and supporting lexicon.
