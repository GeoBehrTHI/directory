## ABSTRACT
Berean Christian Academy (https://bereanchristianacademy.org) is CLASSICAL. The school’s mission anchors education in a Christ-centered worldview aimed at recognizing truth, goodness, and beauty, and it partners intentionally with parents. The curriculum features the liberal arts (Trivium), Latin through advanced levels, Great Books and primary texts, logic and rhetoric sequences, and a capstone Senior Thesis. Pedagogy includes Harkness-style conversation, debate, memory work (songs, chants), daily hymn studies, catechism, and Scripture memory, with visible emphasis on wonder. Faculty are framed through an explicit “Portrait of a Teacher” emphasizing virtue and lifelong learning; a “Portrait of a Parent” and parent-formation steps reinforce a home-school partnership. Across the six categories—Religious/Metaphysical foundations, Purpose/Goal, Curriculum, Pedagogy, Faculty, and Parents/Community—the school exhibits the positive keyword indicators drawn from the Six Essential Elements lexicon and avoids negative signals. Result: CLASSICAL.
