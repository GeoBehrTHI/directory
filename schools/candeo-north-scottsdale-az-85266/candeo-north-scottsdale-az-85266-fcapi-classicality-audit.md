# Austin Classical School — Classicality Audit

Website audited: https://austinclassical.org/ 

## Executive Summary
Overall classification: CLASSICAL

Austin Classical School (ACS) clearly positions itself as a classical, Christian institution that partners closely with parents. The school’s public materials explicitly foreground the transcendentals (truth, goodness, beauty), the cultivation of wisdom and virtue, the Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric), memory work and recitation, debate/discussion and thesis defense, Latin, a theology-rich curriculum, and communal worship (chapel). ACS also highlights its collaborative “co‑teacher parent” model and recently announced accreditation by the Association of Classical Christian Schools (ACCS), reinforcing its adherence to the classical Christian tradition (ACCS accreditation announcement) .

Scoring across the Six Elements shows strong alignment in all categories, with particularly high coverage in Religious/Metaphysical Foundations, Purpose/Goal, Curriculum, and Parents & Community. Pedagogy is robust (memory work, chants, recitation; debate/discussion; thesis defense), though the site does not explicitly use terms like “Socratic” or “scholé,” which slightly lowers Pedagogy coverage. Faculty language strongly emphasizes formation and Christian exemplarity.

Note on method: Keyword judgments align with the positive and negative indicators provided in the Six Essential Elements lexicon and companion lists (e.g., truth, goodness, beauty; virtue; Trivium; Socratic, chanting; scholé; project‑based, experiential learning)    .

## Aggregate Assessment
- Category ratings: All six categories = CLASSICAL
- Overall classification: CLASSICAL

ACS strongly exemplifies the classical Christian model across mission, curriculum, pedagogy, community life, and theological grounding, consistent with the keyword indicators in the Six Elements lexicon and supportive resources such as The Scholé Way and ICE documents that define contemplative, memory‑rich, rhetorically oriented instruction alongside parent partnership and virtue formation    .
