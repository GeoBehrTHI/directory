## Executive Summary

Anthem Preparatory Academy (AZ 85086) demonstrates strong alignment with classical education across most rubric categories, with particularly robust evidence in curriculum, pedagogy, faculty, and parent/community engagement. Public-facing materials from the school emphasize a liberal-arts core, Great Books, and a telos oriented toward Truth, Goodness, and Beauty, paired with Socratic inquiry and rigorous memory/recitation practices. These signals align closely with classical education as defined by the Six Essential Elements and accompanying keyword lexicons. While the materials foreground transcendental aims (truth, goodness, beauty) and formation of character, explicit references to theology, liturgical practices, or a formal ecclesial framework are less conspicuous in the snippets available, which places Element 1 in the “Somewhat Classical” band rather than fully Classical on the religious/metaphysical dimension. Overall, Anthem Prep AZ is CLASSICAL in aggregate.

Key exemplar signals from the crawl and knowledge sources include:

- Emphasis on the Great Books, liberal arts core, and Western canon as foundational to learning.
- Explicit telos centered on truth, goodness, and beauty, and formation of virtue in students.
- Pedagogical methods featuring Socratic discussion, memory work, narration/recitation, and upper-school rhetoric; lower-school memory-related chants and singing.
- Public statements about parent partnership and community involvement as integral to the classical project.
- The Six Elements framework and lexicon provide the positive/negative keyword scaffolding used to score each category.
