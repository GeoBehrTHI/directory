# Classicality Audit Report: Bethel Classical Academy

Website used: https://bethelclassical.org/

## Executive Summary
Overall classification: CLASSICAL

Bethel Classical Academy presents clear classical Christian identity and practice. The school explicitly centers its model on truth, goodness, and beauty, uses the Trivium (Grammar, Logic, Rhetoric), teaches Latin, emphasizes integrated studies, and engages families through worship, catechism, and parent formation resources. Liturgical and communal practices (chapel, hymns, creeds, Scripture memorization) are explicit, and faculty are described as modeling Christian faith. Using the Six Essential Elements keyword indicators as our framework, each category scores Classical with strong positive keyword coverage and virtually no conflicting signals (e.g., “values‑neutral,” “project‑based learning” marketing) in the public materials reviewed   . 

Method note: All judgments are grounded in the positive/negative keyword indicators from Six Essential Elements and the supporting lexicon used for classical audits  .
