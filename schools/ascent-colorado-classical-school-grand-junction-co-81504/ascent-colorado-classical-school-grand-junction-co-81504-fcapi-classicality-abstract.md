## ABSTRACT

Overall Classification: CLASSICAL

ACAGJ demonstrates strong alignment with classical education across Purpose & Goal, Curriculum, Faculty, and Parents & Community, supported by Mission/Vision statements, core virtues, a canonical (Great Books) orientation, and Hillsdale-supported teacher development. The Pedagogy category shows classical elements (Socratic/seminar work and rhetoric in upper grades) but also includes lower-year practices (e.g., singing/chanting, memory-based activities) that temper a fully uniform Scholé-like approach across all grades, resulting in a SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL signal for that category. The Religious & Metaphysical Foundations category also leans toward classical virtue formation rather than explicit theological liturgy, placing it in SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL. The Curriculum category is firmly CLASSICAL, given the explicit grammar, logic, rhetoric framework, Latin, Western Civ, Moral Philosophy, Great Books, and Senior Thesis components.

Aggregate: CLASSICAL

Key textual anchors include the Vision/Mission emphasis on moral character and civic virtue, core virtues, Great Books/Great Conversation framing, a Hillsdale-supported curriculum, and explicit teacher development and family-partnership language. Public-facing materials highlight the Great Conversation and Western Civilization through canonical texts, explicit Latin and Moral Philosophy coursework, and a Senior Thesis, reinforcing the classical trajectory. The school’s approach to pedagogy reflects both Socratic upper-level practices and traditional, teacher-led instruction, producing a nuanced but overall CLASSICAL profile.
