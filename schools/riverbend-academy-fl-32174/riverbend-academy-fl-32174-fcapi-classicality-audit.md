## Executive Summary

Riverbend Academy (Ormond Beach, FL) presents a robust CLASSICAL orientation across all six rubric categories, anchored in a Christ-centered, classical framework. Website materials and school communications emphasize a Trivium-based curriculum (grammar, logic, rhetoric), integration of a Biblical worldview, Great Books-oriented aims, and a community that views parents as partners. Documentation on the site also highlights teacher as mentors, a living curriculum, and strong parent partnership, all of which align with classical benchmarks. Evidence includes explicit references to a classical Christian philosophy of education, the Grammar-Logic-Rhetoric progression (K3–12), Progymnasmata memory work, and dual-enrollment pathways, as well as a mission centered on character formation in a Christlike context. The school also describes itself as partnership-oriented with families and a community that invites parent involvement. Taken together, these signals place Riverbend Academy squarely in the CLASSICAL category.

Key evidence anchors:

- Religious and metaphysical foundations: Biblical worldview, Christian formation, and Christ-centered mission (e.g., “Creating Christlike Character, Christlike Culture, Christlike Community.”)
- Purpose and goal: Emphasis on formation, virtue, wisdom, and the true/good/beautiful; education as a holistic formation rather than mere information.
- Curriculum: Trivium (Grammar/Logic/Rhetoric) with K–12 progression; Bible-chronological history; core study areas; emphasis on Progymnasmata and memory work.
- Pedagogy: Socratic discussion in upper grades, explicit rhetoric training, and memory/recitation practices; contemplative/restful aspects alongside rigorous study.
- Faculty: Staff described as “Kind, Loving, Christian Teachers and Assistants”; concept of teachers as mentors and living curriculum.
- Parents & Community: Explicit partnership with families; opportunities for parent involvement and education; open-campus style engagement.
