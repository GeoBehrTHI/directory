## Executive Summary

Providence Academy (Green Bay, WI) presents a robust classical Christian program that aligns very strongly with the Six Essential Elements of Classical Education. The school emphasizes a Trivium-based curriculum (grammar, logic, rhetoric), integrated Biblical/theological formation, Latin study, and a clearly defined Christian worldview embedded across lower and upper school programs. Public-facing materials consistently frame education as formation of character, virtue, and human flourishing within a Christian framework, while maintaining a rigorous academic program and a family-partnered model. Evidence from the school’s materials shows explicit commitment to theology, liturgical practice (weekly chapel), Great Books/Western canon engagement in humanities, memory work (recitation, singing), and a clearly articulated Trivium-aligned progression (grammar in early years, logic in middle school, rhetoric in high school), as well as a strong emphasis on leadership, house systems, and parental involvement.

Overall classification: CLASSICAL

- The school clearly embraces transcendental truths (truth, goodness, beauty) within a Christian/metaphysical frame, with theology as a core element. Evidence includes God-centered language, biblical worldview, and liturgical practices (weekly chapel). 
- The goal of education emphasizes formation, virtue, wisdom, and human flourishing, not merely career preparation. This is reinforced by statements about forming Godly leaders, virtue, and a biblical worldview guiding all disciplines. 
- The curriculum and pedagogy align with classical methods (grammar, logic, rhetoric; Latin; Great Books/western-canon emphasis; worldviews/apologetics). Public materials describe Latin from grade 3, logic/rhetoric progression, and humanities centered on primary sources from the Western canon. 
- Pedagogy includes elements of memorization/recitation and a memory-formation approach via grammar-stage singing, chanting, and recitation in the early years, with Socratic/seminar-style discussion emphasized in upper grades. While explicit quotations of Socratic seminars appear in internal materials, the Trivium framing and senior thesis requirements reflect a classical pedagogy. 
- Faculty materials emphasize teachers as mentors and living exemplars, with profiles and leadership structures that reflect a community of teachers who model virtue and form the “living curriculum.” 
- Parent/community engagement is a core element (partnership with families, parent formation opportunities, campus visits, open events). The school highlights strong parent involvement through house systems, parent-teacher engagement, and tours/open houses.
