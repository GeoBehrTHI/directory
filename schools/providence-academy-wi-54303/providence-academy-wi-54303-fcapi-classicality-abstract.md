## ABSTRACT

Providence Academy (Green Bay, WI) exhibits a strong CLASSICAL profile consistent with traditional classical Christian education. Across the six categories, the school demonstrates:

- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: Clear Christ-centered framing, biblical worldview integration, liturgical practices (Weekly Chapel).
- School Purpose and Goal: Explicit formation of virtue and wisdom, emphasis on human flourishing and Godly leadership.
- Curriculum: Trivium progression (grammar, logic, rhetoric), Latin from grade 3, humanities anchored in Western canon, primary-source engagement, and a biblically integrated worldview.
- Pedagogy: Classical methods including memorization/recitation in early years, logic/rhetoric progression, and thesis work; Socratic-style discourse appears in upper grades; alignment with scholé in at least some forms.
- Faculty: Faculty as living curriculum and mentors; strong professional profiles and faith-aligned leadership.
- Parents and Community: Strong parent partnership, co-educator model, open campus events, and family involvement in school life.

Overall, Providence Academy is CLASSICAL, with evidence of a robust classical model across all six dimensions. The school clearly adheres to the Transcendental/Truth-Goodness-Beauty frame, formation-focused goals, Trivium curriculum, Socratic/ rhetorical pedagogy, practicing teachers as role models, and a cloud of parent-community engagement that aligns with classical education principles.

Cited sources and representative content include
- Foundational Christian/Classical framing and biblical worldview emphasis. 
- Christ-centered mission and partnership with parents. 
- Weekly Chapel and liturgical/communal practices. 
- Trivium-based curriculum and language/Great Books emphasis. 
- Humanities, Western canon, and primary-source emphasis. 
- Latin from grade 3 and language core across upper school. 
- Adherence to classical pedagogy with Master-Teacher/mentorship culture. 
- Parental partnership and campus/community engagement.
