## ABSTRACT

Berean Baptist Academy Utah demonstrates a CLASSICAL orientation across all six categories, anchored by a robust religious/theological framework and a Trivium-based curriculum and pedagogy. It emphasizes virtue and wisdom as core goals, uses chant-based memory work in the grammar years, dialectic/debate in the middle years, and rhetoric/apologetics in the upper grades, with Latin study and a strong teacher/mentoring culture. Parents are positioned as co-educators in a collaborative community, with visible openness to parental involvement. While the program exhibits many classical signals, it would benefit from an explicit, sustained Great Books/Great Conversation component to strengthen Element 3’s classical signals further. Overall, Berean Baptist Academy Utah is CLASSICAL, with strong signals of a carefully designed Classical Christian education and opportunities for even deeper alignment through targeted enhancements in Great Books integration and explicit Great Conversation framing.
