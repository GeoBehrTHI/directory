## Executive Summary

Great Hearts Invictus TX 78023 presents a robust alignment with classical education principles as articulated in the classical education rubric. The school’s public materials emphasize a liberal-arts, Great Books–driven, Socratic-centered program that foregrounds Truth, Goodness, and Beauty, strongly shaping curriculum, pedagogy, and school culture around character formation and intellectual virtue. Public-facing materials frequently invoke the Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric), Great Books, Socratic dialogue, and a formation-focused mission, and they describe a community-wide commitment to families as partners. While the school foregrounds transcendental aims and a classical curriculum, it also identifies itself as a non-sectarian charter school, which introduces a nonliturgical, broadly Christian-informed classical framework rather than church-centered liturgy. This combination yields a predominantly Classical orientation with some notes of nonsectarian structure. Overall classification: CLASSICAL.
