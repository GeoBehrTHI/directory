## Executive Summary

Kardia Classical School WA 98272 demonstrates a thoughtful alignment with classical education concepts while operating as a Christian, family-partnered program. The public materials emphasize a Christian worldview, the triad of truth–goodness–beauty, and a structured Parent Partnership model that integrates home-based and in-school learning. There is clear evidence of curricular emphasis on grammar/history/writing and a two-days-in-person / three-days-at-home format, plus enrichment in arts and community life. However, explicit references to several hallmark classical elements—such as a full Great Books program, explicit Socratic seminars across the 9–12 range, and formal attention to the liberal arts sequence (grammar, logic, rhetoric) as a core curriculum—are less consistently articulated in the site content. Taken together, the school sits squarely in the Classical realm, but with notable room to deepen and explicitly codify traditional classical practices. Overall classification: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL.

The absence of explicit secular relativism signals and the presence of a liturgical/communal flavor (through faith-based community life) reinforce a Classical alignment, albeit with room to further embed liturgical practices in a formal way.

Positive/Negative keyword analysis

Why this rating

The presence of a strong formation/virtue frame qualifies as Classical-inclined, but the explicit, comprehensive classical triad of elements (including detailed Great Books/Great Conversation and a full liberal arts progression) is not uniformly stated.
