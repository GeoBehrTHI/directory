# ABSTRACTCT

Kardia Classical School WA 98272 demonstrates a strong Christian, parent-partnered model with a discernible classical orientation. The school foregrounds a Christian worldview and the triad of truth, goodness, and beauty, forming the Religious and Metaphysical Foundations core. Its stated goals emphasize formation and virtue, aligning with classical aims, though the articulation of a full classical telos (including explicit Great Books and a comprehensive liberal-arts progression) is not consistently codified in core materials. The Curriculum shows a liberal-arts focus with grammar, history, science, and writing, supplemented by a Great Books-leaning discourse on the site’s blog, but lacking consistently explicit Great Books programming across the curriculum. Pedagogy is blended (in-person + at-home with video lessons), incorporating memory and review elements, yet not uniformly detailing Socratic seminars or primitive rhetoric across all grade levels. Faculty presence is clear (Head of School and bios), but the explicit living-curriculum language is less overt in core pages. Parent & Community engagement is a strong Classical signal, with formal Partner Partnership language, parental co-teaching, and active involvement.

Overall, the school sits in the SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL category, with multiple strong classical signals (religious foundations, formative aims, parent partnership) and several areas where deeper, more explicit classical practices could be codified and expanded (Great Books/Great Conversation, liberal-arts sequence, explicit Socratic pedagogy, liturgical practices). Implementing the needle-moving enhancements outlined above would move the school toward a full CLASSICAL classification.

Key Classical Signals Present
- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: Christian worldview, truth/goodness/beauty, faith-based community. 
- School Purpose and Goal: Formation and virtue; culture of lifelong learners; emphasis on truth, goodness, beauty. 
- Curriculum: Grammar/history/science/writing; classical framing; Great Books discourse referenced in blog content. 
- Pedagogy: Blended in-person/at-home model with enrichment; memory-focused, restorative elements suggested. 
- Faculty: Named Head of School and bios; faculty-led instruction. 
- Parents & Community: Strong parent-partnership model; faith-based co-educators; open parental involvement.
