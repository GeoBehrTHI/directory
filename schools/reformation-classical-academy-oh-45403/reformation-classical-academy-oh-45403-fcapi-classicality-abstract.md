# ABSTRACT

Reformation Classical Academy (Dayton, OH 45403) is a predominantly CLASSICAL school, deeply anchored in Reformed/theological foundations and classical Christian pedagogy. The school’s public materials foreground Scriptural authority, confessional commitments, and a formation-focused goal oriented toward virtue and wisdom. The curriculum emphasizes grammar, logic, rhetoric, memory-work, and the classical method, while upper-grade instruction highlights rhetoric and public discourse through a Senior Thesis. Faculty are depicted as living exemplars of virtue, and parents are treated as co-educators in a partnership model. Across the six classical criteria, RCA demonstrates strong alignment with classical education norms, with Curriculum and Pedagogy showing SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL alignment due to less explicit public emphasis on Great Books and explicit Socratic-style seminars. The aggregate classification is CLASSICAL.

Key sources include RCA’s Mission/Vision, Foundations (Historical & Theological), Doctrinal Foundations (Creeds/Confessions), and Foundational Documents (including The Lost Tools of Learning). The RCA Curriculum and upper-grade pedagogy emphasize grammar/logic/rhetoric and rhetoric-focused work, while lower grades integrate memory-work, singing, and a contemplative pace consistent with scholé ideas. The school also shows a robust parental-partnership model and a strong community ethos.
