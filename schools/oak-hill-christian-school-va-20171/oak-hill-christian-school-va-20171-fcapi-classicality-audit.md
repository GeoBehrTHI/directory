## Executive Summary

Oak Hill Christian School (OHCS), Herndon, VA, presents a robust classical-Christian program rooted in the grammar–logic–rhetoric framework, strong engagement with Great Books and biblical instruction, and an explicit emphasis on parent partnership and formation of character. Public materials show clear alignment with the six classical elements in many respects: a Christian theocentric worldview, a formation-focused aim, a curriculum built around the trivium and Great Books, and a pedagogy that blends memory work, chant, and Socratic-style inquiry in later grades. The school also emphasizes family involvement and a shared mission with parents, consistent with the classical model’s emphasis on the living teacher and living curriculum.

Overall, OHCS is CLASSICAL across most categories, with Element 4 (Pedagogy) showing some room for fuller alignment with every hallmark of the canon (notably explicit, sustained Socratic/seminar-style practice in all upper grades). The aggregate assessment leans CLASSICAL, with a notable strength in religious and metaphysical foundations, purpose and goal, and curriculum, plus solid faculty and parent/community alignment. See category-by-category notes below for the keyword-driven justification and the evidence trail from OHCS public materials and the classical-education lexicon.

# ABSTRACT

Oak Hill Christian School demonstrates strong classical-education alignment across most categories. The school’s religious and metaphysical foundations are explicit, with a Christian worldview permeating curriculum and pedagogy, and an emphasis on virtue and truth in alignment with classical aims. The curriculum is strongly classical, employing the grammar–logic–rhetoric progression and compelling engagement with Great Books, Latin, Bible study, and history integrated across grades. Faculty are positioned as living exemplars and central to the school’s mission, with a family-led, parent-partnered model that mirrors classical education’s pedagogy of formation through community and mentorship. The public materials show a robust commitment to forming character and intellect in a Christian framework, with a clear emphasis on forming students who can engage in deep reading, thoughtful discussion, and confident public speaking.

# ABSTRACT

(concise synthesis)
Oak Hill Christian School demonstrates a robust CLASSICAL orientation, with a Christian, formation-centered mission, a Trivium-based curriculum (grammar–logic–rhetoric), and widespread use of Great Books, Latin, and history interwoven throughout the program. The teaching approach blends memory-based and liturgical elements (lower grades through recitation and singing) with higher-level reasoning (Socratic-like dialogue and rhetoric in upper grades) and a clear emphasis on teacher as exemplar and parent partnership. The program aligns with classical education’s core aims—truth, goodness, beauty; formation of character; and integration of faith with reason—while some pedagogical documentation could be strengthened with explicit, sustained use of Socratic seminars across all upper grades. Overall, OHCS is CLASSICAL, with a pathway to full CLASSICAL status as Pedagogy documentation becomes more explicit and comprehensive.
