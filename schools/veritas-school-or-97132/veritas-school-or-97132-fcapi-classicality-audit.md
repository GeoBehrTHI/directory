## Executive Summary

Veritas School, Newberg, Oregon (97132) presents itself as a robust Classical Christian school. Public-facing materials emphasize a Christ-centered mission, formation of virtue and wisdom, and a Trivium-based curriculum that foregrounds liberal arts, Great Books, and a memory-rich pedagogy in the lower grades. The school repeatedly anchors its identity to formation (wisdom, virtue, Godliness) rather than mere information, and it ties curriculum and pedagogy to a long-standing classical tradition (grammar, logic, rhetoric; Great Books; The Lost Tools of Learning). These themes align strongly with the Six Essential Elements framework for a classical education.

Overall, the six categories consistently demonstrate Classical alignment, with explicit religious/metaphysical foundations, formation-focused purpose, a Trivium-based curriculum centered on liberal arts and Great Books, a pedagogy that blends rote/memory-rich practice with Socratic-style inquiry in upper grades, and clearly defined teacher and parent/community roles. The aggregate assessment is CLASSICAL.
