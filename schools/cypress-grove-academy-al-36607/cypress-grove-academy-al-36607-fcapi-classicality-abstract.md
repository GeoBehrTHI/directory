## ABSTRACT

Cypress Grove Academy is classified as CLASSICAL across five of the six categories, with Element 3 (Curriculum) and Element 4 (Pedagogy) currently BEST characterized as SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL due to explicit classical elements (Trivium, liberal arts, formation) present but not uniformly foregrounded as Great Books/Great Conversation and Socratic pedagogy in public-facing materials. - Purpose & Virtue Formation: Formation of character and virtue as telos, with a focus on truth, beauty, and goodness in daily life and learning.

- Pedagogy: Foundational classical pedagogy elements exist, but explicit public-facing evidence of Socratic seminars and scholé-based pedagogy is not consistently demonstrated in the crawl materials. 
- Faculty: Strong teacher-steward presence and Christian-mission alignment; living curriculum and mentor-like faculty portrayals. 
- Parents & Community: Deep partnership and parental formation with active family involvement and a welcoming campus for family engagement. 

Overall classification: CLASSICAL.
