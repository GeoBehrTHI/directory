## ABSTRACT

Golden View Classical Academy (GVCA) exhibits a robust classical orientation across mission, curriculum, and faculty culture, with explicit commitments to virtue, formation, and liberal-arts study. The school’s public materials foreground formation (virtue, moral character, civic virtue) and a content-rich, classical curriculum (grammar/logical/rhetoric focus; Latin and other classical languages; study of Great Books/primary sources; whole-book reading; senior thesis). Faculty language emphasizes teachers as moral exemplars and central to a living curriculum, while parent/community engagement is visible through family handbooks and governance communications.
