## ABSTRACT

Saint Paul Catholic Classical School (SPCCS) demonstrates a consistently classical orientation across all six rubric categories. Religious and metaphysical foundations are anchored in Catholic orthodoxy and liturgical life (Mass, Adoration, Baltimore Catechism) with Christ as the Logos directing all study. The school’s purpose and goals center on formation—virtue, wisdom, and the pursuit of truth, goodness, and beauty—over vocational preparation, aligning with the classical telos described in classical education literature. The curriculum foregrounds the Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric), Great Books, Latin, and Western/Christian history, with integrated religious content. Pedagogy combines contemplative/restful elements (Scholé, memorization/recitation, liturgical integration) with active discourse and rhetoric training. Faculty are described as living the curriculum through long-tenured, highly qualified teaching staff and a culture of mentorship. The Parents & Community category emphasizes partnership with families as co-educators, open-house opportunities, and active parental involvement in the educational journey.

In aggregate, SPCCS meets and in many respects exceeds the Six Essential Elements’ indicators for a classical education. The textual evidence from SPCCS public materials consistently aligns with the positive keyword indicators across categories, while negative signals are minimal or absent. The school ranks CLASSICAL across all six categories.

Key sources informing this assessment include SPCCS public-facing materials on Academics, curricular content, and mission statements, which describe the Trivium, Great Books, Latin, liturgical life, and formation-focused aims.
