# ABSTRACT

Westminster School at Oak Mountain demonstrates a CLASSICAL orientation in its public materials, with explicit Christian formation and a classical pedagogical frame. Core religious foundations are clear (gospel-centered, covenantal community; church affiliation), and formation is foregrounded as a telos alongside intellectual development. Curriculum signals include grammar, logic, rhetoric, and a capstone thesis, indicating strong classical strands, though explicit Great Books/Great Conversation language and continuous Socratic pedagogy are less consistently documented in public content. Faculty messaging emphasizes a living curriculum and classical formation, and parent-community partnership is central to the school's model. Aggregated, WSOM falls solidly in CLASSICAL territory, with Curriculum and Pedagogy as the primary areas of partial alignment (SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL). The school’s overall trajectory supports continued growth in public articulation of canonical classical elements (Great Books, Great Conversation, Socratic pedagogy) to further strengthen the public-facing CLASSICAL profile.
