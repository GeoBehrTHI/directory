## ABSTRACT

Providence Academy TX 75032 is evaluated as CLASSICAL across the six classical-school categories. The school’s materials indicate a religiously grounded, theology-infused program (biblical worldview, chapel, liturgical practices, and Logos-based epistemology), a purpose oriented toward formation, virtue, wisdom, and the pursuit of truth, good, and beauty, and a curriculum organized around liberal arts, the Trivium, Great Books, Latin, and Western history. Pedagogy combines Socratic seminars and rhetoric with memory-based lower-level work and liturgical elements, while faculty are portrayed as mentors who embody and deliver the living curriculum. Parental partnership and community involvement are positioned as central to the school’s model, with parents described as co-teachers and participants in formation activities and school life. Evidence drawn from the school’s statements and materials demonstrates alignment with the six elements: Religious and Metaphysical Foundations, School Purpose and Goal, Curriculum, Pedagogy, Faculty, and Parents & Community. The aggregate result supports CLASSICAL status for Providence Academy TX 75032.

Key evidence anchors:
- Religious & Metaphysical Foundations: biblical worldview, theology, chapel, worship, wonder, and Logos language appear throughout mission and program materials.    
- School Purpose & Goal: formation/virtue/wisdom emphasis with Great Conversation and classical aims articulated in graduate profiles.  
- Curriculum: explicit Trivium, Great Books, Latin, and history emphasis; primary sources and liberal-arts structure.  
- Pedagogy: a blend of Socratic discussion, memory-based practices, and rhetoric training; lower grades emphasize wonder and chant/memory.  
- Faculty: small-class/mid-range ratios, credentialed teachers, living curriculum emphasis. 
- Parents & Community: explicit partnerships with parents, parent formation, and open-campus/community engagement. 

Notes on sources
- The analysis draws on publicly available materials within the Providence Academy site and related documents in the provided crawl, which include mission statements, graduate profiles, curriculum descriptions, faculty pages, and parent/handbook materials. Citations indicate the primary sources used to substantiate claims in each category.

- All six categories were evaluated using keyword analysis against the Positive/Negative keyword indicators from the Six Essential Elements of a Classical School lexicon, and the presence of terms such as truth, virtue, theology, liturgy, Great Books, Trivium, Socratic pedagogy, and parents as co-teachers were used to ground the assessments. The school’s public materials show strong alignment with classical education principles, particularly in religious/theological grounding, formation-focused goals, classical curriculum and pedagogy, and active parental engagement.
