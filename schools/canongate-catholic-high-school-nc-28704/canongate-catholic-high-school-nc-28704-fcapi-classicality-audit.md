# Classicality Audit: Austin Classical School

URL used: https://austinclassical.org/

## Executive Summary
Overall classification: CLASSICAL

Austin Classical School presents a robust classical Christian identity rooted in transcendental moral realism, explicit theological grounding, liberal-arts curriculum (Trivium foregrounded with meaningful Latin, logic, and rhetoric), and pedagogy that features memory work, Socratic discussion, and capstone rhetoric defenses. Its collaborative schedule structurally partners parents as co‑teachers, and the school community includes chapel, recitations, and house life. Across the six categories, positive classical keywords (truth, goodness, beauty; virtue; Scripture; chapel; trivium; Latin; Socratic; rhetoric; memorization/recitation) are frequent; negative-progressive signals (e.g., project‑based learning marketing, 21st‑century skills, college‑and‑career first orientation) are essentially absent. Representative evidence includes the mission “to glorify Jesus Christ… cultivate wise, virtuous, and humble students” and the Classical Education Basics page centering “Truth, Goodness, and Beauty” with the Trivium as the primary toolset  . The school explicitly states use of the Socratic method and culminates with a senior thesis and oral defense in the Rhetoric stage  . Daily chapel further evidences liturgical practice .

Method note: Keyword analysis and scoring follow the “Six Essential Elements” framework and lexicon of positive/negative indicators for each category  .

- Aggregate verdict: CLASSICAL

- Present in ACS materials: truth, goodness, beauty; virtue; theology/Scripture; Socratic; memorization, recitation, songs; rhetoric; thesis/defense; Latin; Great Books/primary sources; chapel; parent partnership/co‑teachers.
- Not explicitly observed in sampled pages: scholé, wonder (the ideas are implied via depth‑over‑breadth and non‑utilitarian purpose), chanting as a term (songs/recitation present), project‑based/experiential learning marketing (absent)  .
