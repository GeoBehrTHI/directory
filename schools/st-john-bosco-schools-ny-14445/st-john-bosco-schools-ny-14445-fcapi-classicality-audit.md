## Executive Summary

St. John Bosco Schools in East Rochester, NY 14445 demonstrates a clear orientation toward classical Catholic education. Public-facing materials emphasize a religious and metaphysical foundation aligned with Catholic doctrine, a formation-focused mission, a classical liberal-arts curriculum, and a pedagogy that blends contemplative practice with rigorous academic work. The school presents a strong faculty structure and a mission that positions parents as partners in formation. Some elements, such as explicit high-school-style Socratic seminars or a widely advertised parent-education program beyond informational admissions material, are less evident in the public materials. Overall, the school aligns with Classical education reasonably well, with room to strengthen explicit pedagogical practices and broader parent-education initiatives. Aggregate assessment: CLASSICAL.

What Would Move the Needle

- Pedagogy (Element 4)
  - Explicitly publish and publicize a high-school-level of Socratic pedagogy or a documented, formalized Socratic/seminal discussion approach appropriate for high school grades, and adapt it to upper middle-grade levels where feasible.
  - Provide a visible, explicit plan for memory-work, recitation, or other scholé-aligned practices (chanting, dictation, or classical memory routines) in the lower grades, with concrete examples in curriculum materials.
- Parents & Community (Element 6)
  - Establish and advertise formal parent-education programs (e.g., classical book studies, lectures, workshops) and provide a schedule or calendar of these offerings on the website to demonstrate ongoing commitment to parent formation beyond admissions communications.
  - Create and publish a “Portrait of a classical parent” or “co-teacher” guidelines to strengthen the parent-school partnership, including open campus policies, and formal opportunities for parental involvement beyond visits.
- Curriculum (Element 3)
  - More explicitly name and describe Great Books, primary-sources reading, and classical texts within the curriculum pages, including a map of which Great Books or canonical works are engaged at each grade level, to tighten alignment with explicit classical-curriculum vocabulary.
- Pedagogical Documentation
  - Add explicit statements or pages detailing how memory work, recitation, and classical pedagogy are implemented in different grade bands, with sample lesson structures or rubrics.
