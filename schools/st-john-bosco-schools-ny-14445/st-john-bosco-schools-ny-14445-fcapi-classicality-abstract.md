## ABSTRACT

St. John Bosco Schools in East Rochester, NY 14445 demonstrates a clear alignment with Classical education across most of its six categories. The school presents a robust religious and metaphysical foundation through explicit Catholic identity, liturgical life, and sacramental formation (Category 1: CLASSICAL). Its purpose and goals center on formation, virtue, wisdom, and a classical liberal-arts orientation (Category 2: CLASSICAL). The curriculum showcases classical framing, a history-forward, Western civilization-centric approach, and Latin study with external validation (Category 3: CLASSICAL). Pedagogy shows contemplative and formation-focused elements, with evidence of a scholé-inspired atmosphere, but lacks explicit high-school–style Socratic seminars in the public-facing materials (Category 4: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL). The faculty are visible and central to the living curriculum, supported by named leadership and faculty pages (Category 5: CLASSICAL). Parent and community engagement is evident, especially in positioning parents as primary educators and inviting family involvement, though deeper formal parent-education activities are not consistently highlighted (Category 6: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL). 

Overall, the school is CLASSICAL, with Category 4 and Category 6 marking the areas most in need of explicit articulation to reach a full classical standing in every category. The school would move toward full CLASSICAL alignment by expanding explicit pedagogical practices (Socratic/Scholé detailing) and by building out structured parent-education offerings visible on the website and in materials.
