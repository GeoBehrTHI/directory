## ABSTRACT

Overall Classification: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL

APA WV2 demonstrates a classical-liberal-arts orientation with emphasis on virtue, character formation, and a community-centered, college-prep framework. This combination yields a SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL rating across categories, with particular strengths in Faculty and Parents/Community (Element 5 and Element 6) and notable formation emphasis (Element 2) but opportunities to strengthen explicit curriculum-method signals (Categories 3 and 4) and theological/liturgical foundations (Element 1).
