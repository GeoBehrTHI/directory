## ABSTRACT

Petra Christian Academy Id 83713 positions itself as a Christian, tuition-free classical school with online delivery and a focus on formation and leaders within a Christian worldview. Public materials emphasize theology (Statement of Faith), Christian leadership formation, and a classical branding, but explicit classical curriculum and pedagogy details (e.g., Seven Liberal Arts, Great Books, Great Conversation, and Socratic/Scholé methodologies) are not consistently spelled out in the public facing content. The school clearly signals religious foundations, formation goals, and family/community engagement, but falls short of fully articulating a comprehensive classical curriculum and pedagogical model in publicly available content. Overall classification: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL.
