## Executive Summary

Northwest Classical Academy (NCA) in Kennesaw, GA (ZIP 30152) presents a clearly classical orientation in its stated mission, virtues, and curricular emphasis, with strong evidence of a liberal-arts, Trivium-based approach, virtue formation, and active family/community involvement. The public materials emphasize virtues, a content-rich classical curriculum, a three-stage Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric), and a civically oriented, purpose-driven education. There are explicit references to a pledge that highlights the true, the good, and the beautiful, and to virtues such as courage, courtesy, honesty, humility, perseverance, self-government, and service. The school also situates itself within the Liberty Classical Schools network, underscoring a broader classical-education framework.

However, there are notable gaps with respect to explicit religious/theological grounding and liturgical/community practices within the public-facing materials. The site strongly communicates virtue, telos, and the classical curriculum, but it does not consistently foreground liturgical or theological commitments, nor does it publicly describe liturgical worship or a theology-centered program. The pedagogy also shows partial alignment: strong emphasis on memory work, recitation, and classical memory practices in lower grades, with less explicit, public articulation of Socratic/seminar-based pedagogy in upper grades. Taken together, these factors yield a verdict of SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL: the school is clearly classical in mission and curriculum, with substantial classical alignment, but not yet fully explicit across all six canonical elements in every category (notably in Religious/Metaphysical Foundations and Pedagogy as publicly evidenced).
