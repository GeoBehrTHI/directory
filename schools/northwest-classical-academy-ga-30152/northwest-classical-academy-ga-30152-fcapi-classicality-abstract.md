## ABSTRACT

Northwest Classical Academy (NCA) demonstrates a clear CLASSICAL orientation in Curriculum, Purpose/Goal, Faculty, and Parents & Community, with explicit Trivium-based pedagogy in curriculum and a virtue-formation teleology. The school uses a pledge and virtues to anchor moral formation, emphasizes a content-rich classical curriculum, and features active parental involvement and a networked classical framework (Liberty Classical). However, the public materials do not consistently foreground theological grounding or liturgical practices, and explicit Socratic/seminar pedagogy across all grade levels is less transparent in public content. Taken together, the aggregate assessment is SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL, with strong classical elements overall and room for fuller public articulation of Foundations and Pedagogy to reach full CLASSICAL status.

Cited evidence from the school’s public materials includes:
- Mission and pledge language focusing on truth, goodness, beauty and virtues (Our Mission; Our Pledge; Our Virtues). 
- Three-stage Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric) and memory-based pedagogy; public mention of a content-rich curriculum (What is Classical Education?; Lower School materials). 
- Faculty and staff context highlighting a living curriculum and mentorship ethos (Northwest Classical Academy Faculty & Staff). 
- Strong parent/community engagement and open-campus/volunteer structure (Enrollment pages; After-School pages; Open House references).
