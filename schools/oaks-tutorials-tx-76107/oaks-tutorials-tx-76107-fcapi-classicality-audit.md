## Executive Summary

The Oaks Tutorials TX 76107 (Fort Worth area) presents a robust Christian classical education model that aligns strongly with the Six Essential Elements of a Classical School. Public-facing materials describe a K–12 Classical Christian program that centers on virtue formation, the Great Books, liberal arts (grammar, logic, rhetoric), and a pedagogy that blends contemplative practice with Socratic inquiry. The school emphasizes collaboration with families, liturgical practices, and a community of teachers who model virtue. Across categories, the school demonstrates clear classical characteristics, with all six categories rated CLASSICAL. Evidence drawn from the school’s public materials (classical pedagogy, Great Books, liturgical elements, and family partnership) is consistent with the Positive Keyword indicators in the Classical Education lexicon, while explicit negative signals are not present in the materials reviewed.

Key contextual signals from public materials include:
- Explicit Christian, biblical framework and liturgical elements (e.g., morning liturgy; catechesis; worship-like language) alongside a classical framework (grammar, logic, rhetoric; Great Books; Great Conversation).  
- Emphasis on formation, virtue, wisdom, and a teleological view of education anchored in Christian teaching, not mere job preparation.  
- Curriculum explicitly centers grammar, logic, rhetoric; strong emphasis on history through Great Books and a cyclical history approach. 
- Pedagogy includes Socratic dialogue, Harkness-style discussion, and Schole (restful/voluntary contemplation) with a blended model of in-class and home study.  
- Faculty narratives highlight living curriculum, virtue modeling, and teacher as mentor in a collaborative atmosphere. 
- Strong parental partnership ethos, with parents as co-teachers and active involvement in the learning process. 

Overall classification: CLASSICAL
