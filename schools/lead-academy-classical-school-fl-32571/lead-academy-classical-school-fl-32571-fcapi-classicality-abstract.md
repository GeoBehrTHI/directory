# ABSTRACT

Lead Academy Classical School (L.E.A.D. Academy Lions) demonstrates a robust CLASSICAL orientation across religious foundations, purpose, curriculum, and faculty/community engagement, with pedagogy that leans toward traditional memory-based and Socratic elements in line with classical Christian schooling. The most pronounced strengths are a clear Christ-centered, biblically integrated framework; a Trivium-based curriculum (grammar, logic, rhetoric) with Latin in the lower grades; and a living-curriculum faculty culture. Public communications emphasize parent partnership and community life (Joppa Project, SHO, and family resources) and continually frame learning as formation in truth, goodness, and beauty.

- Overall classification: CLASSICAL
- Notes: Pedagogy shows strong classical elements (memory, chant, and structured discussion) but with less explicit, consistently documented Socratic seminars across all grades in public materials. This yields a category of SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL for Pedagogy, while the other five categories clearly meet Classical criteria.

What would move the needle

- If there were a desire to push Pedagogy fully into a more explicit, school-wide, documented Socratic/seminar-based model across all grade bands, the school could publish and emphasize systematic use of Socratic discussions in more grade bands (K–12) with concrete exemplars, rubrics, and timetables to demonstrate consistent implementation.
- More explicit references to Great Books or Great Conversation in curriculum pages (beyond foundational classical framing) would align Curriculum more tightly with the Great Books/Great Conversation axis of the classical rubric.
- Further articulation of liturgical or communal worship practices as an element of spiritual formation (where appropriate within the school’s doctrinal stance) could strengthen the Religious and Metaphysical Foundations category.

Abstract (Bottom section)
Lead Academy Classical School demonstrates a robust CLASSICAL orientation across six of the six analyzed categories (Religious and Metaphysical Foundations, School Purpose and Goal, Curriculum, Faculty, and Parents & Community) with Pedagogy positioned as SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL due to explicit, but less uniformly documented, classroom-wide Socratic practice. The school’s public materials emphasize a Christ-centered, biblical worldview; a Trivium-based curriculum (grammar, logic, rhetoric) with Latin in lower grades; biblically integrated content; and a strong community/family engagement framework. Taken together, the school aligns with classical education principles and presents a coherent, formation-focused program that places truth, goodness, and beauty at the center of its mission. The aggregate assessment is CLASSICAL.
