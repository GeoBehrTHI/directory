## Executive Summary

Lead Academy Classical School (L.E.A.D. Academy Lions, Pace, FL 32571) presents a robust classical Christian program rooted in a Christ-centered worldview. Public-facing materials consistently emphasize classical elements (grammar, logic, rhetoric), a biblical framework, and a strong emphasis on virtue, wisdom, and beauty. The school frames its mission around formation of character and the cultivation of truth, goodness, and beauty, with explicit statements about a biblical worldview, theology, and community-life practices. The curriculum highlights include biblically integrated content, Latin in the lower grades, and a broad co-curricular program that includes fine arts and athletics within a Christian framework. Public materials also signal a serious commitment to parent partnership and community life (e.g., “L.E.A.D. Families,” Joppa Project, prayer-related content, and parent resources).

Overall, and across all six categories, the evidence strongly supports CLASSICAL classification, with a consistently classical Christian orientation evidenced by explicit Trivium framing (grammar, logic, rhetoric), biblical and theological foundations, and a coherent classical-pedagogical approach. Where the site shows practical connections to modern supports (e.g., dual enrollment, scholarships, and fine arts programming), these are presented as adjuncts to the core classical formation rather than core vocational emphasis. Therefore, the aggregate rating is CLASSICAL.

Negative indicators (secular or relativistic signals) are not present in public materials.

  Evidence: formation of character and virtue emphasized; vision of education as formation, not mere information; commitment to truth, goodness, and beauty; occasional evidence of college-preparatory pathways (dual enrollment) but framed within a formation-first approach.

  Negative indicators: project-based learning and other constructivist, student-led, or heavy experiential approaches are not foregrounded as primary, and there is limited explicit documentation of pervasive Socratic seminars across grades in the public materials.
  Why: The public-facing materials strongly emphasize classical methods (grammar-logic-rhetoric, memory work, choral/musical memory), with more explicit Socratic practice appearing in higher-level classical education literature rather than consistently documented on the site.

- Element 5: School Faculty — CLASSICAL

  Why: The faculty is presented as moral exemplars and living curriculum, with explicit emphasis on virtue and character—central to classical education.

- Element 6: Parents & Community — CLASSICAL
