## ABSTRACT

Overall classification: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL

- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: CLASSICAL. CHA demonstrates a clearly Christ-centered framework with Bible-based instruction, chapel, liturgical rhythms, and faith-integrated educational aims. This aligns with the Classical School’s religious/metaphysical indicators (theology, liturgy, sacred scripture, reverence). Evidence includes Christ-centered language, Bible classes, and GO! Week anchored in the Great Commission.  
- School Purpose and Goal: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL. CHA emphasizes formation of mind and character within a Christian framework, including a trajectory toward higher education. The explicit Great Books/Paideia vocabulary is not documented; however, formation and godly telos are present. 
- Curriculum: NOT CLASSICAL. The public materials show traditional subject areas and a strong Bible emphasis but do not document the liberal-arts Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric), Great Books, or the Great Conversation. Documentation of Great Books or a Paideia-style curriculum is not evident. 
- Pedagogy: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL. Lower-grade pedagogy includes memory/recitation and singing (which align with scholé and contemplative learning), while upper-grade pedagogy shows depth and potential for discourse, yet lacks explicit, public documentation of Socratic seminars and formal Trivium sequencing.  
- Faculty: CLASSICAL. The living-curriculum approach and teacher-mentorship emphasis align with classical expectations of teachers as virtuous role models and core to the curriculum. 
- Parents & Community: CLASSICAL. CHA presents a robust parent-partnership model, with explicit language about the three-legged stool of parents, school, and church, and sustained parental engagement in governance and daily school life. 

Executive Summary — Summary of Six Categories
- Classical alignment is strongest in Religious Foundations, Faculty, and Parents/Community.
- Pedagogy shows meaningful classical elements in lower grades; Curriculum and Purpose/Goal are mixed (strong formation/faith orientation but lacking full Great Books/Paideia articulation).
- Overall, CHA sits between CLASSICAL and SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL, with a majority of the six categories leaning CLASSICAL or SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL, producing an aggregate SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL designation.
