## Executive Summary

Garden State Classical Academy (GSCA) in New Jersey presents a distinctly classical educational model anchored in a Christian/Biblical worldview, with explicit commitments to virtue formation, a three-part Trivium pedagogy (grammar, logic, rhetoric), and a curriculum that emphasizes liberal arts, Western heritage, and character formation. Public material from GSCA routinely references truth, virtue, beauty, and the cultivation of moral character, alongside liturgical and communal practices (opening prayers, Nicene Creed, and other faith-centered rituals). The school explicitly positions itself as a Hillsdale College K-12 Candidate Member School, aligning with Hillsdale’s American classical education framework. Taken together, GSCA demonstrates a robust classical profile across all categories of the rubric.
