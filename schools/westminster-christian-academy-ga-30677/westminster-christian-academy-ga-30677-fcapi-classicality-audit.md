Still, evidence exists of a strong faculty presence, a faculty/staff focus, and a visible commitment to formational goals. Element 6 (Parents & Community) is particularly strong, with explicit statements about partnership with parents, parent education, and open-campus access.

Overall, the school falls within CLASSICAL with notable strength across most dimensions and some room for stronger explicit articulation in Pedagogy and Faculty narratives. The aggregate assessment: CLASSICAL.

# ABSTRACT

Westminster Christian Academy (GA 30677) demonstrates a CLASSICAL orientation across Religious and Metaphysical Foundations, School Purpose and Goal, Curriculum, and Parents & Community, with more modest explicit signals in Pedagogy and Faculty. The school’s biblically grounded mission, the presence of a classical grammar/logic/rhetoric progression, Latin study, and history instruction through a Christian worldview, align strongly with classical catechism and Trivium-based models. Parental partnership and community engagements further reinforce the classical ethos. To reach a fully CLASSICAL categorization across all six areas, the school could more explicitly document Socratic/Scholé pedagogy in practice and provide a formal, public-facing portrait of the living curriculum embodied by faculty.
