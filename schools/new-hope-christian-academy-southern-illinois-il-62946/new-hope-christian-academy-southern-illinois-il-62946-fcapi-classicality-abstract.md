## Abstract

New Hope Christian Academy (NHCA) in Southern Illinois demonstrates a robust CLASSICAL orientation across all six rubric categories. The Religious and Metaphysical Foundations are strongly anchored in Christian theology, worship, and Scripture, with explicit claims that “truth is God’s truth.” The School Purpose and Goal emphasizes formation of character and wisdom, not just job-readiness. The Curriculum centers the liberal arts (grammar, logic, rhetoric), Great Books, and the Great Conversation, with history taught throughout. Pedagogy emphasizes the Trivium, Socratic dialogue, and contemplative dimensions aligned with scholé and mnemonic practices. Faculty are described as virtuous mentors who model the school’s formation ideals, and the Parents & Community framework highlights partnership with families, parent education, and open-school engagement. This assessment integrates NHCA public materials with the Six Essential Elements knowledge base, mapping direct terms such as Christ-centered, Scripture, Great Books, Socratic discussion, grammar/logic/rhetoric, and parent partnerships to the Positive Keyword indicators, while noting the absence of negative signals in NHCA’s materials. The aggregate rating is CLASSICAL.

- Categories rated CLASSICAL: Religious and Metaphysical Foundations; Purpose and Goal; Curriculum; Pedagogy; Faculty; Parents & Community.
- Key evidence sources include NHCA materials on Christian formation, the trivium-based curriculum, Great Books and Socratic methods, the House System, faculty faith commitments, ACCS membership, and parent education initiatives.     

References to the classical-school knowledge base (positive/negative keyword indicators) accompany the analysis to ground the assessment in the Six Essential Elements framework.
