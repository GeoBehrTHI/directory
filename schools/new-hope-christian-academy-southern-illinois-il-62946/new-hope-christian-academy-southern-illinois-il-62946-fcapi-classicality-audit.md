## Executive Summary

New Hope Christian Academy (NHCA) in Southern Illinois presents a robust CLASSICAL Christian education profile across all six rubric categories. Public-facing materials repeatedly foreground Christ-centered learning, Scripture as foundational truth, worship and biblical formation, history through a Western civilizational lens, and a classical pedagogy (grammar/logic/rhetoric; Socratic seminars; rhetoric training). Faculty hiring emphasizes shared faith and formation, while parent involvement and ACCS membership reinforce a sustained classical community around the school. Based on the Six Essential Elements of a Classical School rubric, NHCA earns CLASSICAL across all six categories, with strong corroboration from multiple NHCA sources and corroborating elements in the classical-education knowledge base.
