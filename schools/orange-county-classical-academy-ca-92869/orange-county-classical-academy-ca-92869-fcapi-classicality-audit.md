## Executive Summary

Orange County Classical Academy (OCCA) presents a clearly classical orientation across most elements of its publicly described program, with strong emphasis on virtue formation, a content-rich liberal arts curriculum, and a classical classroom ethos. Based on the Six Essential Elements framework, the school exhibits robust classical cues in Curriculum, Pedagogy, Faculty, and Parents & Community, while Religous/Metaphysical Foundations show a notable but not fully explicit alignment (lacking overt theology or liturgical practices). Overall, the school registers as CLASSICAL with a single category showing only partial alignment (SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL) due to the absence of explicit theological/liturgical grounding, despite a coherent classical formation throughout its mission, curriculum, and pedagogy.

- Aggregate classification: CLASSICAL.
- The strongest exposures to classical aims appear in Curriculum (grammar/logic/rhetoric, Core Knowledge within a liberal-arts frame, and emphasis on the Great Conversation via classical texts), Pedagogy (Socratic-style inquiry, frequent discussion, memory work and recitation), Faculty (the school as living curriculum; teacher as exemplar), and Parents & Community (active parent involvement and partnership). These elements are repeatedly reflected in the site’s Vision/Mission statements, grade-level curricula, and governance materials. See evidence below for category-by-category justification. 

What would move the needle (for a category currently marked SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL): 
- Introduce explicit theological/metaphysical framing or liturgical-cultural practices as formal elements of the program (without compromising nonsectarian status if desired). 
- More explicit articulation of Great Books-oriented Great Conversation within core curriculum (beyond Core Knowledge and Hillsdale alignment) to strengthen the Great Books/Great Conversation signal. 
- For transparency, publish a short, explicit alignment map tying each category criterion to on-site practices (Religious/Metaphysical Foundations and Great-Books signals).

- These needle-moves would push Element 1 (Religious and Metaphysical Foundations) from SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL toward CLASSICAL, while keeping other categories firmly CLASSICAL.
