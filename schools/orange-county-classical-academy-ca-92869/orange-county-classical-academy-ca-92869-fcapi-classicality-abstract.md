## ABSTRACT

Orange County Classical Academy demonstrates a robust CLASSICAL orientation across Curriculum, Pedagogy, Faculty, and Parents & Community, with a strong articulation of virtue, a Trivium-centered curriculum, and a governance/teacher culture aligned with classical education. The Religious and Metaphysical Foundations category shows partial alignment (SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL) due to the lack of explicit theology and liturgical practices in public-facing materials, though the school clearly emphasizes truth, goodness, and beauty, and a conception of virtue and moral formation as central aims. Aggregate rating: CLASSICAL.

- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL — signals around truth/goodness/beauty and virtue, but no explicit theology or liturgy in public materials. Evidence: public statements about classical aims and virtue; absence of overt theological content. 
- Purpose and Goal: CLASSICAL — clearly formation-oriented with virtue, wisdom, and civic character as telos. 
- Curriculum: CLASSICAL — explicit liberal-arts orientation (grammar/logic/rhetoric), plus Core Knowledge and Great-Books signaling in curriculum language. 
- Pedagogy: CLASSICAL — Socratic/seminal discussion, memory work, and restfully-paced Scholé elements described in public materials. 
- Faculty: CLASSICAL — living curriculum and faculty as exemplars; governance language supports the classical teacher formation model.
