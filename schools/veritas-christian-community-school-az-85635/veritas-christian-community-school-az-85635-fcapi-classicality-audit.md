## Executive Summary

Veritas Christian Community School (Veritas CCS) presents a robust Classical Christian education consistent with a full Classical rubric. The public materials consistently frame the school as a K–12 Classical Christian program anchored in a biblical worldview, with explicit reference to the Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric), engagement with the Great Books (The Great Conversation), classical languages, liturgical practices (Chapel), and a governance model that positions teachers as mentors and the school as a living, faith-informed curriculum. These features align strongly with the six essential elements of classical education in the knowledge-base rubric.

Key indicators from the school’s materials include: a stated Latin root meaning for truth, Distinctly Classical and Distinctly Christian branding, explicit discussion of the Trivium and the Great Conversation, instruction in the classics and Great Books, and liturgical/community practices such as weekly Chapel and parental involvement. The site also emphasizes formation of character and virtue within a biblical framework, not vocational workforce training, and frames parents as partners in education. Taken together, these signals place Veritas CCS squarely in the CLASSICAL category across all six rubric categories.
