## ABSTRACT

Chesterton Academy of Wichita Falls (CAWF) presents a comprehensive CLASSICAL profile across all six evaluation categories. Evidence from CAWF’s public materials shows a Catholic, formation-focused mission (Religious and Metaphysical Foundations; Element 1), a formation-centered purpose (Element 2), a classical liberal-arts curriculum (Element 3), a Socratic-based pedagogy (Element 4), credentialed teachers who serve as the living curriculum (Element 5), and active parental involvement and community integration (Element 6). The school is explicitly identified as a Catholic classical high school in the Catholic tradition, emphasizes formation through virtue and the liberal-arts aesthetic, and integrates liturgical life (e.g., on-site Mass) with a pedagogy centered on dialogic inquiry and classical study. Key public statements include the motto Cultura Vitae; the Three Pillars (Intellect, Character, Spirituality); the mission to raise joyful saints and leaders; and direct references to Socratic seminars and classical Western thought. The school’s location within a Catholic parish context (St. Jude Thaddeus Catholic Church) and the presence of daily Mass further reinforce the religious-metaphysical and liturgical dimensions of its program. The faculty bios illustrate a cadre of credentialed educators who anchor the curriculum, while parent engagement events and open-house formats demonstrate a robust community-partner model.

Overall, CAWF satisfies the six elements of classical education criteria and surpasses the threshold for a full CLASSICAL designation. The combination of Catholic formation, classical curriculum, Socratic pedagogy, and engaged faculty/parents forms a coherent classical ecosystem.
