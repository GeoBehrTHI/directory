## Executive Summary
Overall classification: CLASSICAL

Austin Classical School presents strong, explicit alignment with the classical, Christian tradition across mission, curriculum, and pedagogy. Its public materials repeatedly emphasize cultivating wisdom and virtue, the transcendentals (Truth, Goodness, Beauty), the Trivium (Grammar, Logic, Rhetoric), Latin, formal logic, rhetoric, recitation, debate, and a capstone senior thesis. The mission explicitly centers on glorifying Jesus Christ and partnering with families through a collaborative model that includes at‑home “co‑teachers.” While we did not find explicit references to school‑wide liturgy/chapel on the pages crawled, theological integration is clear (Christian Studies, Exegesis & Hermeneutics, Apologetics & Ethics) and biblical texts are front‑and‑center. Faculty language tilts positive but is less detailed than other categories.

Website used: https://austinclassical.org/ 

Method note: Keyword findings were matched against the Positive/Negative Keyword Indicators in the “Six Essential Elements” framework and supporting lexicon to estimate positive vs. negative indicators in each category (Religious & Metaphysical; Purpose & Goal; Curriculum; Pedagogy; Faculty; Parents & Community)  .
