## Executive Summary

Grace Christian Academy NJ 08093 (GCA) presents a robust Classical-leaning profile anchored in a Christian worldview. Public materials from the school emphasize a Christ-centered mission, regular prayer and Bible study, a structured Trivium-inspired curriculum (grammar, logic, rhetoric), and explicit pedagogical practices that include Socratic dialogue, memory work (singing/recitation), and contemplative learning. There is clear attention to parent partnership and a community ethos, with the school describing itself as a ministry of a home church and inviting ongoing parental involvement. Taken together, these elements align closely with classical education as defined by the Six Essential Elements framework.

Key strengths
- Religious and metaphysical foundations are explicit: Christ-centered mission, Bible study, daily prayer/worship, and a theology-informed approach to education. This is evidenced by the mission statement and classroom devotional practices.  
- Curriculum and pedagogy mirror classical design: Grammar, Logic, and Rhetoric stages are named explicitly, with corresponding grade ranges, plus Latin and literature components. Upper-level rhetoric and discussion are described, consistent with Socratic/seminar traditions.  
- Pedagogical methods include contemplative (Scholé) learning, memorization/recitation with singing in the lower years, and Socratic dialogue in the upper years. This combination is a hallmark of classical pedagogy as described in the rubric. 
- Strong parental partnership emphasis and community orientation are present, including a stated model of teachers as co-educators with families and opportunities for parent involvement. 
- Evidence of a ministry-driven, faith-based formation aim (formation of character, virtue, and spiritual development) aligns with the classical emphasis on education as the formation of the whole person, not merely information transfer.
