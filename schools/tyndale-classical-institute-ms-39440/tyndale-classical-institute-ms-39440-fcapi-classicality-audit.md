## Executive Summary

Tyndale Classical Institute (Laurel, MS) demonstrates strong alignment with classical Christian education in its public materials, particularly in its explicit emphasis on virtue-forming aims, the liberal-arts Trivium approach, and engagement with Great Books and classical languages. The school foregrounds a Christian, theological frame (e.g., Gospel of Christ, Word of Truth) and a curriculum centered on Latin, Greek, literature, logic, and music, all framed as pathways to truth and beauty. However, the public materials provide limited, explicit evidence of formal classical-pedagogy practices such as Socratic seminars, scholé/restful learning structures, or a clearly described “faculty as living curriculum” and robust parent-community partnerships. Because categories with strong classical signals balance categories with ambiguous or absent signals, the overall classification lands in SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL.

Key signals of classical alignment
- Religious and metaphysical foundations are explicitly Christian, with references to the Gospel, Word of Truth, catechism, and a theology-informed view of learning (e.g., "truth," "beauty," "God," "Christ"). These are clear positive indicators for this category. 
- Curriculum is clearly rooted in classical strands (Latin, Greek, logic, literature, Western civilization, Great Books ideals) and a stated aim to discern truth and beauty, aligning with the liberal-arts Trivium/Quadrivium tradition. 
- The materials reference Great Minds and great authors of Western civilization, and repeatedly connect study to truth and beauty, consistent with classical aims. 

Gaps to address for a fully CLASSICAL rating
- Pedagogy: No explicit mention of Socratic discussion, scholé (restful learning), or other detailed, high-school–intensity classical teaching methods in the public materials. This category lacks explicit pedagogical practices beyond traditional subject offerings. 
- Faculty: The materials do not clearly describe a “living curriculum,” named teacher portrait, or a profile of teacher excellence/virtue in the public content. 
- Parents & Community: No explicit statements about parent partnerships, co-teaching, or open-campus/community formation in the public site copy.
