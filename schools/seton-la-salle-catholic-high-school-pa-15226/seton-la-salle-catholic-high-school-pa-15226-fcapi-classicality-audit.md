## Executive Summary

Seton La Salle Catholic High School in Mt. Lebanon (Pittsburgh area) presents a coherent Catholic classical orientation embedded in its mission, curriculum, and pedagogy. The school explicitly positions itself as a Catholic institution guided by faith, liturgical life, and character formation, while offering a classical track that foregrounds the humanities, Great Books and discussion-based learning within a seminar-style framework. Evidence from the school site shows mass, sacraments, daily prayer, and retreats as regular entry points for spiritual formation; a classical curriculum emphasizes theology, philosophy, history, and literature, with seminar-style teaching and opportunities to study Great Books and classical humanities alongside AP and honors options. Faculty highlight a classical-teaching emphasis and a commitment to character formation, including teachers who are actively engaged in classical pedagogy. The school also demonstrates active parental engagement and community partnerships through programs like Grade School Partnerships, shadow days, and admissions-tied parent involvement. Overall, the school exhibits a robust Classical orientation across all six categories, with explicit curricular and pedagogical choices aligned to classical education as defined in the provided rubric.

Executive rating: CLASSICAL
