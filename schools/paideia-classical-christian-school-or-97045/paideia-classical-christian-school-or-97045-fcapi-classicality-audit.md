## Executive Summary

Paideia Classical Christian School (Oregon City, OR, 97045) presents a robust Christian, classical-leaning program that foregrounds character formation, liturgical-infused practice, and a classical curricular sensibility within a K–12 framework. Public materials from the school's site repeatedly emphasize Christ-centered learning, formation, and a paideia-based approach, with explicit references to worship, Scripture memory, and a governance/teacher-mentorship model. While the school clearly adopts classical language (e.g., “Classical Approach,” Paideia framing, and a structured curriculum for memory, catechesis, and liturgy), explicit weekly evidence of core classical lexicon items like extensive Great Books exposure or explicit Socratic seminars across the full K–12 range is less visible in the public-facing materials provided here. Taken together, the school appears predominantly CLASSICAL with meaningful CLASSICAL-leaning attributes, but with room to more fully articulate and document classic elements such as extensive Great Books integration and explicit high-school Socratic pedagogy across curricula.

- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations are clearly foregrounded through explicit Christ-centered framing, Scripture memory, liturgical language, and paideia-driven aims. Evidence includes the school’s Christ-centered framing, reference to the paideia of the Lord, and liturgical-style teaching emphasis.
- School Purpose and Goal centers on formation, virtue, wisdom, and a teleological reading of education, with strong emphasis on Christian character formation and the pursuit of truth, goodness, and beauty as real.
- Curriculum shows clear liberal-arts framing and a curriculum built around Scripture, memory work, and catechesis, with explicit elementary content that includes biblical text, catechisms, and historical study; however, explicit Great Books coverage is not strongly evidenced in the public materials examined.

- Parents & Community emphasis is strong, with explicit framing of partnership with parents, parent formation, and open-campus/volunteer opportunities, aligning with classical-community partnership signals.
