## Executive Summary

Adel Classical Academy (ACA) presents a clearly classical, biblically anchored educational approach with a hybrid/collaborative model that partners with families. The public materials consistently emphasize truth, goodness, and beauty; a liberal-arts framework (grammar–logic–rhetoric); foundational biblical/theological commitments; and a parental partnership through the Collaborative model. Evidence from ACA’s site shows explicit statements about Biblical worldview, Gospel-centered education, God's creation, and a vision of forming virtuous, thoughtful students through a classical curriculum (grammar–logic–rhetoric, history, Latin, and Great Books-leaning elements are foregrounded in coursework). The school also highlights a parental covenant, two-days-on-campus instruction with professional teachers, and regular engagement through family events and open communications.

Overall classification: CLASSICAL.

Notes on the six-category rubric (highlights and cautions)
- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: Strong CLASSICAL signals (Biblical truth, gospel-centered formation, creation theology, worship-like rhythm in memory work). Some elements of liturgical/community practice are present through memory songs and communal events; not a fully liturgical program, but clearly a theologically grounded environment.
- School Purpose and Goal: Clear formation-oriented aims (virtue, wisdom, character; liberal arts; truth-goo dness-beauty). The explicit telos language is present in the classical lexicon, and the site emphasizes formation over mere information.
- Curriculum: Strong CLASSICAL alignment (grammar–logic–rhetoric; liberal arts; history integration; Latin; worldviews). Great Books emphasis is implied through classical canon ideas, even if the explicit “Great Books” label isn’t always used.
- Pedagogy: Mixed signals. There is strong classical structure (grammar–logic–rhetoric; memory and recitation in early years; Socratic-style discussions are not heavily explicit in the site content). Some lower-school pedagogy features are memory-based; higher-year pedagogy suggests more dialogic/faculty-led inquiry, but explicit Socratic seminars are not consistently documented.
- Faculty: Documented presence of Headmaster and professional teachers; collaborative staffing; and explicit references to “professional, qualified teachers.” Evidence supports a classical–mentored model, though “living curriculum” language is implied rather than always explicit.
- Parents & Community: Extremely strong. Parental partnership is a core feature (Family Covenant, two-days-on-campus with parents co-teaching, frequent family events, open visits). This aligns well with classical education’s emphasis on home-school partnership and formation of affections through family involvement.

  - The model is described as “Collaborative” with classroom teachers two days per week and parents teaching on homeschool days; this indicates a mixed pedagogical approach blending classical pedagogy with home-based instruction. The site explicitly describes the collaborative model and its structure. 
  - Upper-grade pedagogy is referenced in adjunct/subject teaching with a focus on disciplined, integrated study and cross-subject planning. 
- Positive/Negative keyword analysis
  - Positive keywords present: Socratic/seminar-style discussions (implied in “classical pedagogy” and in the broader classical lexicon), memory work, rhetoric, recitation, contemplation, scholé (classical concepts)—though explicit “Socratic seminars” are not consistently documented in public texts; there are indications of the classical sequence and seminars in the materials.

The knowledge-base lexicon notes negative signals for experiential/project-based focus and non-traditional pedagogy; ACA’s materials show a blend with collaboration, which is not inherently anti-classical but indicates a hybrid approach. 
  - Estimated counts: Positive ~4–5 indicators (memory/recitation; rhetoric; classical sequence; some Socratic implications); Negative ~1–2 indicators (collaborative/home-based model blurring traditional classroom-only pedagogy).
  - Status justification: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL.
- Why this rating
  - ACA emphasizes key classical pedagogy elements (grammar–logic–rhetoric, memory work, recitation) but also integrates a collaborative/home-school hybrid that moderates the pure classical pedagogy model. This mixed approach places it in the middle rather than fully “Classical” in pedagogy terms.
