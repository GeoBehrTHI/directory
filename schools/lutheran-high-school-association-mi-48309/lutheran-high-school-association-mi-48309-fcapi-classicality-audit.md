## Executive Summary

Based on the public materials from Lutheran High School Association Michigan (LHSA), the school demonstrates a Christian, parish-connected orientation and a longstanding network of Lutheran schools. However, the available public content does not evidences a classical liberal-arts orientation (i.e., explicit Trivium/Quadrivium, Great Books, Socratic pedagogy, or a living-curriculum ethos) that characterizes a full Classical school. Across the six classical criteria, LHSA shows clear religious formation and communal aims but limited or no explicit Classical curriculum or pedagogy. The aggregate result is NOT CLASSICAL.

Key takeaways:

- Religious and Metaphysical Foundations: The LHSA materials foreground Christian formation and a Christian worldview, which signals a religiously grounded education, but explicit classical markers (theology as a formal curricular axis, liturgical practices, etc.) are not clearly documented in the public LHSA content. Evidence points to a Christian formation orientation rather than a formally stated classical metaphysical framework.
- School Purpose and Goal: The mission emphasizes shaping a Christian worldview and laying a foundation for students’ futures, aligning with formation and purpose, but without explicit articulation of virtue-wisdom-beauty as the telos in classical terms. This yields a SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL reading in purpose, not a fully classical one.
- Curriculum: There is no explicit public indication of a liberal-arts curriculum (grammar/logic/rhetoric; Great Books; Great Conversation) on LHSA’s site excerpts. This category is rated NOT CLASSICAL based on current public content.
- Pedagogy: No explicit mention of Socratic seminars, Scholé/restful learning, or upper-level rhetoric education in the public LHSA materials. This category is NOT CLASSICAL.
- Faculty: Public-facing descriptions focus on mission and institutional structure rather than a profile of teachers as “living curriculum” or mentors in the Classical sense. This category is NOT CLASSICAL.
- Parents & Community: LHSA materials show partnership framing (e.g., “Partner with us;” “Our Mission”) but without explicit, repeated parent-education or co-teaching language typical of classical models. This category is NOT CLASSICAL.

Overall classification: NOT CLASSICAL.
