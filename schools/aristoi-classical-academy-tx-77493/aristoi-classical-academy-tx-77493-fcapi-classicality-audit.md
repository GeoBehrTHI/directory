## Executive Summary

Aristoi Classical Academy (TX 77493) presents a distinctly classical framework across governance, curriculum, pedagogy, and community engagement. Public materials consistently emphasize the core aims of classical education: the cultivation of virtue and wisdom, engagement with the Great Books and the Great Conversation, and a three-stage Trivium approach (Grammar, Logic, Rhetoric) that culminates in speech, writing, and public debate. The school also foregrounds character formation — embodied in its motto Truth, Goodness, and Beauty and in mission statements that stress virtuous citizenship and lifelong reasoning. Public materials reveal active examples of the classical pedagogy (Socratic dialogue, memorization and chant in lower grades, and rhetoric-focused experiences in the upper grades) and a strong sense of community partnering with families. The Katy and Cypress campuses articulate clear pathways through K-12 with explicit references to Latin study, living-history projects, and the Habits of Mind developed through classical instruction.

Overall classification: CLASSICAL

- Categories that drive the CLASSICAL rating are evidenced by: explicit Trivium structure (grammar/logic/rhetoric), Great Books/Great Conversation framing, Socratic teaching approaches, and a mission that emphasizes virtue, wisdom, and beauty as real goods. See evidence from mission/vision/philosophy statements, program descriptions, and pedagogy sections. Citations: Mission, Vision, and Philosophy statements; Great Books and Socratic-method references; explicit K-12 structure and Latin study.
