## Executive Summary

Providence Christian School of Texas (Dallas area) presents a coherent classical-Christian profile in public materials. The school repeatedly emphasizes classical instruction, character formation, virtuous living, and a Christian worldview as foundational to its identity. Public pages describe a time-tested curriculum, a focus on shaping character and affections, and a partnership model with families that aligns with classical education norms. The school foregrounds virtues such as truth, goodness, beauty, and wisdom, and ties learning to faith, Scripture, and worship language. Across the site, phrases such as “classical, Christian education,” “virtue,” “the Word of God,” and “great ideas” recur, and there is explicit attention to a liberal-arts–driven curriculum with logic, Latin, and history taught through a biblical lens. Notably, while the materials strongly align with classical aims in Religion/Metaphysics, Purpose/Goal, and Faculty/Parents, explicit public references to some classical pedagogy indicators (e.g., explicit mention of Great Books, explicit Socratic seminars, or scholé pedagogy) are less evident. Overall, Providence presents a CLASSICAL orientation with some room for fuller public articulation of certain classical pedagogy specifics.
