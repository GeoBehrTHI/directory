## ABSTRACT

Greenville Classical Academy Sc 29681 is a fully CLASSICAL, Christian institution. The school’s public materials consistently articulate a Christ-centered, biblical, and classical education with a rigorous Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric), a Great Books framework, and a Western-civilization-informed curriculum. Pedagogy blends memorization and recitation in the grammar stage with Socratic dialogue and rhetoric in the upper grades, aligning with classical methods. Faculty messaging highlights living exemplars and the formation role of teachers, and the community emphasizes a covenantal partnership with families, open visitation, and parental formation. Together, these elements map cleanly onto the Six Essential Elements of a Classical School (1), yielding a CLASSICAL rating across all six categories.

- Religious/metaphysical foundations are deeply embedded through a Christ-centered worldview, chapel, and integration of theology across disciplines.  
- The school’s purpose centers on formation, virtue, wisdom, and the true/good/beautiful as real, not merely job preparation. 
- Curriculum is explicitly classical: Trivium, Great Books, Latin, Western history, and a culture of wonder.  
- Pedagogy integrates contemplative/restful elements with rigorous Socratic and rhetorical work, plus memory/recitation and singing in early years.  
- Faculty is positioned as mentors and the living curriculum within a covenantal community. 
- Parents and community are central partners, with explicit invitation for parental formation and open campus engagement.
