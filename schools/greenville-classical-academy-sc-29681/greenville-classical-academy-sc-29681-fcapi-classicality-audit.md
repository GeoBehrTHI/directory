## Executive Summary

Greenville Classical Academy Sc 29681 (GCA) is a fully CLASSICAL, Christian school that presents a coherent classical-Christian program across grammar, logic, and rhetoric phases, with explicit emphasis on Great Books, a Christian worldview, and a robust partnership with families. Public-facing materials consistently frame the school as Christ-centered, biblically grounded, and forming students toward virtue, wisdom, and the good. The Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric), Great Books, Latin, history anchored in Western civilization, and a pedagogy that blends memorization, recitation, singing, and Socratic discourse align strongly with classical criteria. Faculty messaging emphasizes living exemplars of virtue and a teacher-as-curriculum role, while parents are positioned as co-educators in covenantal community. The school also foregrounds liturgical-like aspects (chapel/memorial traditions) and a theology-infused approach to all disciplines. Overall, GCA meets the six-element classical rubric as CLASSICAL, with evidence across all six categories in favor of classical aims.

Key evidence highlights:
- Religious and metaphysical foundations are explicit: Christ-centered, Christian worldview, biblical integration, theology-informed learning, liturgical practices (chapel), and a mission to glorify God.   
- Purpose and goal emphasize formation, virtue, wisdom, and the true/good/beautiful as real, not mere preparation for work.  
- Curriculum centers on the liberal arts, the Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric), Great Books, Western history, Latin, and a paideia that fosters wonder.   
- Pedagogy includes both contemplative rests and rigorous discourse: singing/recitation in the lower years, and Socratic discourse and rhetoric in the upper years.  
- Faculty are described as living exemplars and mentors; “living curriculum” and covenantal staff messaging is evident. 
- Parents and community are central partners; families covenant, parent involvement is encouraged, and open campus/visitation is available.
