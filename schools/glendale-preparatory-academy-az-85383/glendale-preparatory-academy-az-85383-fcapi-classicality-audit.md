## Executive Summary

Glendale Preparatory Academy (AZ 85383) presents a robust classical-education profile anchored in the Great Hearts framework. Public materials from the school emphasize Truth, Goodness, and Beauty as guiding ideals, a strong formation/virtue emphasis, a liberal-arts curriculum with heavy emphasis on Great Books, Latin, and historical study, and a pedagogy that includes Socratic dialogue, memory work, and public speaking. Across six categories, the school demonstrates many classical-excellence indicators, with Element 1 (Religious and Metaphysical Foundations) showing some gaps (explicit theology and liturgical practice are not clearly stated in the public materials), while Categories 2–6 show clear classical alignment (formation of virtue and wisdom; liberal-arts curriculum; Socratic and rhetoric-focused pedagogy; Living-teacher/mentorship model; and active parental/community partnership). Taken together, Glendale Prep earns an overall CLASSICAL classification.

Executive takeaway:

- Strong CLASSICAL alignment overall, with Element 1 marking as SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL due to limited explicit theological/liturgical cues in the public materials.
- The school clearly foregrounds virtue, great-books pedagogy, Latin, history as a through-line, Socratic dialogue in upper levels, and an active parent/community ecosystem.
- The public materials provide concrete textual evidence for most classical criteria (Great Conversations, original texts, Latin, rhetoric, wonder, mentorship, and family partnerships). See cited evidence throughout.

What would move the needle (only required when some categories are SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL)

- For Element 1, explicitly foreground theology and liturgical/community practices in public materials (e.g., morning prayer, chapel, liturgical calendar, sacred music) to align more fully with the theological and liturgical dimension of classical foundations.
- Add explicit language tying the foundation to transcendentals beyond virtue (e.g., explicit appeal to theology or sacramental worldview) while continuing to emphasize Truth, Goodness, Beauty and virtue.
- Document more direct references to the Great Conversation as a sustained, cross-disciplinary reading program across grades, with representative Great Books as core requirements visible on the site.

# ABSTRACT

Glendale Preparatory Academy displays CLASSICAL alignment across five of six classical elements, with Element 1 showing SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL due to partial explicit theological/liturgical framing in public materials. The school emphasizes virtue and formation over mere information, features a liberal-arts core (Great Books, Latin, history, mathematics through calculus, science with a classical bent), employs Socratic pedagogy and rhetoric, positions teachers as mentors and living exemplars, and pursues active parental involvement. The aggregate profile is CLASSICAL, with room to strengthen explicit theology/liturgy in public communications to reach full alignment in Element 1.

- Language emphasizes wonder and a transcendent frame (e.g., education beginning in wonder and rising toward mastery). This aligns with transcendental, moral-realism sensibilities.

Positive/Negative keyword analysis

- Positive keywords present: truth, goodness, beauty, virtue, wonder, courage, temperance, justice, wisdom.
- Negative keywords: none detected in the public-facing materials (no explicit relativism, secular humanism, or morally neutral signals appear in the cited excerpts).
- Quantitative note: Positive indicators appear multiple times; negative indicators appear to be absent in the accessible public materials.

Citations: The core claim about Truth, Goodness, Beauty and virtue appears in Glendale Prep content describing “pursuit of Truth, Goodness, and Beauty” and virtue-centered formation. The reference to “Academics begin in wonder and rise toward mastery” supports the wonder/realism dimension.
