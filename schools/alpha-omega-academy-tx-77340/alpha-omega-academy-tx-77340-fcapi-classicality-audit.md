## Executive Summary

Alpha Omega Academy TX 77340 presents a demonstrable classical framework in its public materials. The school positions itself as a classical, Christ-centered institution serving Pre-K–12 and emphasizes foundational classical elements such as the trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric), Latin, Great Books, and Western Civilization within a Christian worldview. Public statements highlight a partnership with parents and a family-friendly, community-oriented approach (e.g., parent involvement and volunteer opportunities). Taken together, these elements align strongly with the core components of classical education, particularly in Religious & Metaphysical Foundations, School Purpose & Goal, Curriculum, and Parents & Community. Pedagogy and Faculty show partial alignment, with clear classical signals (the trivium, Great Books, etc.) but less explicit articulation of practices like Socratic seminars, scholé, or a fully articulated “living curriculum.” Overall, the school is best categorized as CLASSICAL, with room to deepen adherence in pedagogy and explicit faculty portrayal.

Key signals include:
- Religious & Metaphysical Foundations: Christ-centered framing; Biblical worldview; theological orientation implicit in curriculum and purpose. Evidence includes explicit statements about being Christ-centered and about a Biblical worldview within the published materials.  
- Purpose & Goal: Formation-oriented language (virtue, formation, lifelong learning) and emphasis on forming Christian leaders who will impact the world. 
- Curriculum: Clear articulation of classically-oriented elements (grammar/logic/rhetoric; Latin; Great Books; Western Civilization). 
- Parents & Community: Notable emphasis on parent involvement, partnerships, and open access to engagement (Parent Links; PIE concept referenced). 
- Pedagogy: Core signals point to classical content (trivium) and memory-based practice, but explicit modern classical-pedagogy rituals (Socratic seminars, scholé, etc.) are less clearly described in the public materials. 
- Faculty: Some indicators of a traditional teacher-model culture (small student/teacher ratio, emphasis on virtue-led education) are present in public materials, with less explicit language about the faculty as living curriculum or portrait of teacher ideals. 

Overall aggregate classification: CLASSICAL.

What would move the needle? For a fully CLASSICAL outcome, the school could explicitly codify and communicate: (a) a consistent faculty portrait and “living curriculum” language; (b) explicit Socratic/seminar-based and scholé pedagogy in upper grades; (c) more overt references to contemplative/restful learning as a daily mode; (d) deeper articulation of parent formation activities and a structured plan for broader community engagement beyond basic involvement.
