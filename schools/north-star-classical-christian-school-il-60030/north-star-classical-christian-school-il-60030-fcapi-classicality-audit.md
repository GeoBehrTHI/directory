## Executive Summary

North Star Classical Christian School IL 60030 demonstrates a robust alignment with the classic six-element rubric for classical education. Public-facing materials consistently foreground the three liberal arts tools of the Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric), Christian/theological integration, formation of intellectual virtues, and a governance/teaching culture that positions faculty as the living curriculum and parents as partners in a classical education. Across categories, the school’s materials emphasize virtue, truth, and beauty, sustained through a Gospel-centered worldview, contemplative pedagogy, Socratic inquiry in upper grades, and parent involvement as co-educators. This strong confluence of catechetical mission, curriculum, pedagogy, faculty emphasis, and community engagement supports a classification of CLASSICAL for all six categories, with no category evidencing a clear move toward not-classical or only somewhat-classical.

Key evidentiary threads include: a mission anchored in the Gospel with explicit emphasis on formation of the soul and intellectual virtues; a curriculum built on grammar, logic, and rhetoric with references to a classical method and the existence of a senior/thesis-oriented capstone; pedagogical practices that mix contemplative/restful learning with Socratic discussion and rhetoric training; faculty described as master teachers and the living curriculum; and a strong parent/communal partnership model that treats parents as co-teachers and invites ongoing involvement and formation. See mission statements and program descriptions emphasizing “Anchored in the hope of the Gospel, equipped by the classical method, prepared to lead and live wisely” and the Trivium framework, along with explicit references to Socratic discourse and rhetoric in upper grades.  

- Category-level snapshot (summary): All six categories align with CLASSICAL criteria, with explicit textual anchors to transcendental truth, virtue, beauty, formation, and classical pedagogy. The aggregate assessment is CLASSICAL.

- What would move the needle: Not required at this time given the CLASSICAL rating across categories. If desired, minor, non-normative enhancements could include explicit cross-grade articulation of Great Conversation/Great Books and a formal liturgical integration, though current materials already signal strong theology, worship-like community rhythm, and traditional forms of communal learning. No major changes are required to shift the classification.
