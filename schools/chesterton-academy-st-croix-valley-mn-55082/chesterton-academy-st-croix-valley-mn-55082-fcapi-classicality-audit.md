## Executive Summary

Chesterton Academy of the St. Croix Valley (CASCV) presents itself as a robustly classical, Catholic high school operating in Stillwater, Minnesota. Public materials systematically foreground a classical, Catholic educational approach—emphasizing formation, virtue, and a liberal-arts-infused curriculum grounded in faith. The school articulates a mission to form joyful leaders and saints through a rigorous, integrated curriculum, anchored in Catholic theology and liturgy, and organized around a Three Pillars Model (Intellect, Character, Spirituality). Evidence across multiple CASCV pages highlights liturgical life (Mass, daily prayer), a strong faculty culture (oath of fidelity to Catholic tradition), a catechetical and Schöné-like communal rhythm, and a humanities-centered, Socratic-method emphasis in the upper grades. These features align CASCV closely with a classical education framework, including integration of Great Books sensibilities, Latin study, a history-forward and interdisciplinary curriculum, and active parental partnership.

Aggregate assessment: CLASSICAL
