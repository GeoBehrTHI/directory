## Executive Summary

Upper Valley Christian School (Leavenworth, WA, 98826) (UVCS) presents a THEOLOGICAL and CLASSICAL orientation per public-facing materials. The school self-describes as offering Classical Christian Education, integrates a biblically-grounded worldview, and foregrounds the Trivium (Grammar, Logic, Rhetoric) alongside Great Books, paideia language, and liturgical life (e.g., chapel, statement of faith). Mission statements foreground partnership with families in a Christ-centered, classical education intended to form virtuous, thinking students who live for the glory of God and the good of others. These elements align UVCS with a Classical education posture in the knowledge base, particularly through explicit references to Trivium, Great Books, virtue formation, theology, liturgy, and a communal/church-aligned life of learning. Public materials repeatedly emphasize formation of character, the objective truth of biblical truth, and a community rooted in Christian worship and doctrinal commitments. This combination supports a CLASSICAL classification across the rubric.

Key public signals include: explicit Classical Christian framing (Trivium, Great Books, Paideia), a biblically grounded worldview, liturgical/covenantal elements (Chapel, Statement of Faith), and a mission centered on virtue and wisdom within a Christian frame. The presence of a clearly defined K–12 program with Grammar/Logic/Rhetoric tracks (and rhetoric in the 9–12 program) further anchors UVCS in the classical pedagogical tradition, while formal aspects like “our mission … Christ-centered education, in the classical tradition” and explicit doctrinal commitments strengthen the religious/metaphysical foundation. Taken together, the school demonstrates a coherent classical education profile rather than a partial or secular reform approach.
