## ABSTRACT

Veritas Classical Academy (Marietta, OH 45750) demonstrates a CLASSICAL orientation in its stated aims, curriculum scaffolding, and community ethos, with strong signals of religious/theological grounding, formation-focused goals, and parent/community engagement. The site emphasizes truth, goodness, and beauty as real goods, virtue formation, a grammar-logic-rhetoric curriculum, and Western-civilization–oriented study. Public materials also show active parental involvement (board/family interviews in admissions) and a vibrant community life (house system, dress code, fine arts). However, explicit public signaling of Great Books/Great Conversation, Socratic seminars, and scholé (restful learning) pedagogy is less overt, which leads to a SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL rating in several categories (Curriculum, Pedagogy, Faculty). The aggregate classification falls into SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL, with concrete steps outlined above that would strengthen any movement toward full CLASSICAL status.
