# Classicality Audit Report

Below are full audits for the newly crawled schools. Each audit includes an Executive Summary, six-category keyword analysis tied to the Six Essential Elements framework and lexicon, category ratings, an overall classification, and supporting citations. The keyword step is performed explicitly for every category, using positive/negative indicators defined in Six Essential Elements and the classicality lexicon (truth, goodness, beauty, virtue, theology, Socratic, scholé, wonder, chanting, project-based, experiential learning, etc.) to justify scores  .

---

## AUDIT 1: Austin Classical School
Website used: https://austinclassical.org 

### Executive Summary
- Overall classification: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL
- Strong evidence in religious foundations, purpose/goal, and curriculum: explicit pursuit of God’s “goodness, truth, and beauty,” integration of Bible and theology, and a trivium-based, Great Tradition curriculum with Latin and classical rhetoric/thesis defense  .
- Pedagogy shows memorization/recitation, logic, rhetoric, and oratory, but there is no explicit mention of Socratic seminars or scholé; faculty formation and parent partnership evidence is limited in the crawl excerpts.
- Priority improvements: public articulation of Socratic seminar practice and scholé/restful learning, a published “portrait of a teacher,” and explicit parent-formation/partnership pages.

### Method note on scoring
- Percent_positive = positive keyword hits / (positive + negative hits) observed in site excerpts for that category.
- Percent_coverage = proportion of that category’s sub-elements evidenced on the site (e.g., Religious Foundations has 3 sub-elements: moral realism, theology, liturgical life).
- Keyword lists and polarity are drawn from Six Essential Elements and the lexicon  .
