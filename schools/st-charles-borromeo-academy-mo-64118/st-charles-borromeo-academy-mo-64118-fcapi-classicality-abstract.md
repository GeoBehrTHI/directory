## ABSTRACT

- Austin Classical School: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL. Strengths in religious foundations (TGB, Bible/theology), classical purpose, and a Trivium/Latin/Great Tradition curriculum. Pedagogy shows memory and rhetoric/oratory but lacks explicit Socratic/seminar and scholé language; faculty formation and parent partnership are not articulated in the excerpts. Improvements in those areas would likely move ACS to a clear CLASSICAL rating  .

- Berean Christian Academy: CLASSICAL. Clear commitments to TGB, parent partnership, the Trivium, Latin, Great Books, Harkness/Socratic practices, chanting/memorization, and a thesis defense. Faculty formation language is the only thinner area; otherwise the evidence is comprehensive and robust   .

Category summary across both schools:
- Religious & Metaphysical: Both emphasize TGB and theology; explicit liturgy/chapel is unconfirmed in the excerpts .
- Purpose & Goal: Both focus on formation and TGB over careerism; no “workforce-first” rhetoric found .
- Curriculum: Strong Trivium/Latin/Great Books orientation for both; history taught as history, not “social studies” .
- Pedagogy: Berean explicitly features Harkness, chants, and thesis defense; ACS features memory/recitation and rhetoric/oratory but lacks explicit seminar/scholé language in excerpts .
- Faculty: Neither school surfaces a rich public “portrait of a teacher” in the excerpts; Berean shows strong program-level mentorship (student-oriented) .
- Parents & Community: Berean publicly affirms partnership; ACS excerpts do not display this language .
