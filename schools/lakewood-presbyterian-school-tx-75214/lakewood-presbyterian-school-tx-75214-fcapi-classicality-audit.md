## Executive Summary

Lakewood Presbyterian School (Dallas, TX 75214) presents a robust CLASSICAL profile rooted in a Christian worldview. The public materials consistently frame the school as a classical Christian institution that uses the Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric), emphasizes virtue-centered formation, and integrates biblical faith with intellectual pursuits. The school explicitly situates itself as “over 30 years of providing a classical Christian education” and describes its mission as forming resilient, thoughtful, capable Christians who will influence society for the glory of God. It foregrounds the Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric) across grades, teaches Latin and other classical texts/periods, and blends memory work, Scripture, and poetry with rigorous argumentation and rhetoric in upper grades. The community emphasizes partnership with parents, Christ-centered identity, and a living-learning model where teachers and staff model virtue and serve as mentors. These elements align strongly with the Six Essential Elements rubric for a classical school and place this school solidly in the CLASSICAL category.

Key textual anchors include: the school’s mission to form Christians who will “build, reform, and lead society to the glory of God,” the explicit triad of grammar/logic/rhetoric across grade bands, the presence of Latin/Greek/World history taught through a classical Christian worldview, and the emphasis on memory work (poetry, Scripture) and debate/seminar-style discourse in upper grades. The school also emphasizes parent partnership and a Christ-centered community, which map to the classical model’s sixth element (Parents & Community) as described in the normative rubric. Citations drawn from the school’s site confirm these core attributes and practices.
