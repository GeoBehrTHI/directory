## Executive Summary

Great Hearts Monte Vista North (TX 78212) presents a program that aligns strongly with classical-education principles across most rubrics, with only partial alignment in a couple of categories. Public materials emphasize a liberal-arts core, a curriculum oriented toward the Western tradition, exposure to Great Books and the Great Conversation, and a mission framed by truth, goodness, and beauty. The school also highlights character formation, virtue, and community partnership with families. Taken together, the public-facing materials indicate CLASSICAL alignment in most domains, with SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL signals in a couple of areas where theology, liturgical practice, or explicit Socratic pedagogy are not overtly stated, though they are common motifs in the broader classical-education framework used by Great Hearts and the Six Essential Elements lexicon.

Key findings driving the assessment
- Core mission and curriculum emphasize truth, goodness, and beauty and a liberal-arts foundation (grammar, logic, rhetoric; history; natural science; philosophy), consistent with classical aims. This is reinforced by explicit statements about a rigorous liberal-arts curriculum in the Western tradition and engagement with authors such as Aristotle and Dostoevsky. 
- Great Books / Great Conversation, and a Western-tradition core are repeatedly highlighted as central to the curriculum and mission. This matches core classical criteria. 
- There is clear emphasis on character formation, virtue, and a community of learners—consistent with classical educational aims in the School Purpose and Goal and Faculty/Community pillars. 
- Pedagogical specifics like Socratic seminars, chanting, or rigid, memory-based pedagogy are not explicitly documented in the public homepage content, though they are part of the classical-education discourse in the accompanying knowledge-base elements. This results in SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL signals in Pedagogy, while other categories show strong Classical alignment.

Overall assessment: CLASSICAL

- When all six categories are weighed, the Great Hearts Monte Vista North public materials exhibit robust classical alignment across most facets, with only partial alignment in the Religious/Metaphysical Foundations and Pedagogy categories due to explicit theological liturgical language and explicit pedagogy terminology not being as overtly documented in the public-site content. The aggregate assessment, based on category-by-category analysis and the weight of classical indicators across curriculum, mission, and community, is CLASSICAL.

What would move the needle

- For Category 1 (Religious and Metaphysical Foundations): Increase explicit articulation of theological grounding and liturgical or communal practices in public-facing content (e.g., a statement about theology integrated into disciplines, or concrete references to liturgical or communal worship or religious formation within the curriculum). Explicitly naming theology or liturgical practices would elevate this from SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL to CLASSICAL in evidence rather than inference. This would align the site more strongly with the Grounded in Theology and Liturgical/Communal Practices criteria. 
- For Category 4 (Pedagogy): Publicly document and describe explicit classical-pedagogy methods (e.g., Socratic seminars, scholé days, recitation/chanting, memory-work, and a stated progression from lower to upper-school rhetoric and debate). Publishing a concise pedagogy section or curriculum narrative that names these elements would move this from SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL to CLASSICAL.
