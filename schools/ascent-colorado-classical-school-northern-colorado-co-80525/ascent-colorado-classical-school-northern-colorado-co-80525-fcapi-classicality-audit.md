## Executive Summary

Ascent Colorado Classical School Northern Colorado (ACAC NC) demonstrates a robust alignment with classical education principles across multiple rubric categories, with especially strong emphasis on curriculum content (Great Books, liberal arts framework), pedagogy (Socratic discourse, disciplined seminars, rhetoric training), and community involvement (active parent partnership and visible faculty as the living curriculum). Public materials consistently articulate a mission centered on virtue, wisdom, truth, beauty, and character formation, alongside a content-rich liberal arts approach and a disciplined, orderly learning environment. While explicit references to theology or liturgical practices are not prominent in the public materials, the school foregrounds transcendental qualities (truth, goodness, beauty) and moral formation as core aims, which aligns well with classical-humanist aims. Overall, ACAC NC is CLASSICAL, with Element 1 showing some tension between explicitly religious liturgy/theology and a broad classical formation through secular channels.

Evidence drawn from school communications includes the mission statements and hallmarks of classical education (truth, beauty, virtue; moral character; civic virtue; a content-rich liberal arts program) as well as explicit commitments to Great Books and the Great Conversation, the Trivium and Quadrivium, and a structure that includes Socratic discussion, memory work, and public speaking. The school also emphasizes direct parent partnership and community involvement, and describes the faculty as mentors who model virtue and serve as the “living curriculum.” See sources indicating mission and hallmarks (turns: 6, 9, 16), curriculum emphasis (turns: 5, 9, 18, 12), Great Books/Great Conversation (turns: 5, 6, 12), pedagogy (turns: 3, 6, 11), and parent/community partnership (turns: 6, 11).
