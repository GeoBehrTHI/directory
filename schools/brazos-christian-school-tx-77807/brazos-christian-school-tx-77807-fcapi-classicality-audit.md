# Classicality Audit: Austin Classical School (austinclassical.org)

School website audited: https://austinclassical.org/

Executive Summary
- Overall classification: CLASSICAL
- Why: The school’s public materials consistently emphasize a Christ-centered telos (“glorify Jesus Christ”), the cultivation of wisdom and virtue, the transcendentals (truth, goodness, beauty), and the liberal arts (Trivium with Latin, Logic, Rhetoric). They describe memory work, recitation, chanting in Grammar grades; debate, discussion, and Socratic methods in Logic/Rhetoric; senior thesis with oral defense; extensive use of primary sources; and an explicit partnership-with-parents “collaborative” model. The site also evidences chapel and corporate worship practices.       
- Most consequential signals:
  - Purpose: “cultivate wisdom and virtue … contemplation of the True, the Good, and the Beautiful” and “less about training for a specific, temporal vocation and more about … an eternal purpose.”  
  - Curriculum: Trivium by stages; Latin 5–8; formal Logic; Rhetoric; primary sources; Great Books/Western tradition; senior thesis/defense.   
  - Pedagogy: Memorization, recitation, songs and chants (Grammar); debate, discussion, Socratic methods (Logic/Rhetoric); oration and thesis defense; school-wide choir.    
  - Foundations: Mission, Scripture, doctrine/exegesis/apologetics, chapel.    
  - Parents & community: Deliberate co-teacher model with training, parent choir in chapel, “partner with your family.”  

The keyword evaluation below uses the Positive/Negative Keyword Indicators from “Six Essential Elements of a Classical School” as its scoring framework.
