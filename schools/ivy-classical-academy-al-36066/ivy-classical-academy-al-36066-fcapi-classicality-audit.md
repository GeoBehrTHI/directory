## Executive Summary

Ivy Classical Academy, Prattville (Alabama, 36066) presents a largely classical profile across Curriculum, Pedagogy, Faculty, and Parents & Community, with a notable exception in Religious and Metaphysical Foundations. Public materials emphasize formation, virtue, and a content-rich liberal arts approach, anchored by Hillsdale-affiliated curricula and a commitment to virtue-driven civic education. Evidence includes explicit mission language about training minds and improving hearts through moral character and civic virtue, and a public emphasis on truth, goodness, and beauty as real and knowable. The school also highlights Socratic-style pedagogy, memory-based recitation practices, a strong liberal-arts curriculum (grammar-logic-rhetoric, Great Books, Great Conversation), teacher-expert instruction, and an active Parent Service Organization. However, the materials reviewed do not reveal explicit liturgical or theological grounding, worship practices, or a liturgical communal rhythm, which is a hallmark asserted by some classical models.

Overall classification: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL

- Element 1 (Religious and Metaphysical Foundations): NOT CLASSICAL
  - Evidence: Mission emphasizes moral character and civic virtue rather than explicit theology or liturgical life; no overt liturgical practices are described in the public materials. See mission statement language emphasizing moral character and civic virtue. 
  - Positive indicators observed: virtue, moral character, formation (present in mission); general emphasis on transcendent aims appears more in Element 2 language (truth/goodness/beauty) than explicit theological grounding. 
  - Rationale: Without explicit theological grounding or liturgical communal practices in public-facing materials, Element 1 falls short of a full Religious & Metaphysical Foundation with transcendent moral realism or liturgical life as defined in the classical rubric.

- Element 2 (Purpose and Goal): CLASSICAL

  - Positive keyword indicators observed: formation, virtue, character, wisdom, moral imagination, liberal education, eternal aims (truth/goodness/beauty) as guiding ideals. The “What is Classical Education?” articulation explicitly anchors truth, goodness, and beauty as real. 
  - Why this rating: The school foregrounds formation of virtue and wisdom as the telos of education, not merely job-readiness, and identifies truth/goodness/beauty as guiding ideals.

- Element 3 (Curriculum): CLASSICAL

- Element 5 (Faculty): CLASSICAL

- Element 6 (Parents & Community): CLASSICAL
