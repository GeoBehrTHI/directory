## ABSTRACT
Overall classification: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL. Bishop Louis Reicher Catholic School demonstrates strong classical foundations—transcendentals (true/good/beautiful), virtue formation, integrated theology, and rich liturgical life. The curriculum includes Latin and classic literature (Great Books authors). However, the site provides minimal explicit evidence of classical pedagogy (Socratic seminars, scholé/rest, chanting/recitation, rhetoric orations) and features modern‑school emphases (AP/PSAT, 21st‑century technology, robotics; “Social Studies”). To reach a solid classical profile, the school should publicly foreground classical pedagogy, replace “Social Studies” with “History,” strengthen explicit Trivium/Quadrivium language, publish a “Portrait of a Teacher” and faculty formation practices, and create visible parent formation offerings. Category summary: [1] Religious Foundations—Classical; [2] Purpose/Goal—Somewhat Classical; [3] Curriculum—Somewhat Classical; [4] Pedagogy—Not Classical; [5] Faculty—Somewhat Classical; [6] Parents/Community—Somewhat Classical.
