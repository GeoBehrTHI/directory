# Classicality Audit: Bishop Louis Reicher Catholic School

School website used for this audit: https://bishopreicher.com/

Executive Summary
- Overall classification: SOMEWHAT CLASSICAL
- Why: The school explicitly frames education around truth, goodness, and beauty, virtue formation, and a Catholic liberal arts identity with integrated theology and robust liturgical life—strong classical signals. It also includes Latin and classic literature (Great Books authors). However, the site gives limited evidence of classical pedagogy (Socratic seminars, scholé/restful learning, chanting/recitation, rhetoric orations), and features prominent modern‑school markers (21st‑century technology, robotics, AP/PSAT emphasis, “Social Studies”) that dilute the classical profile. On balance, the philosophical/theological foundations are classical; pedagogy and some curriculum signaling are weak to mixed.     
- Method: Keyword analysis aligned to the Six Essential Elements framework and lexicon (positive/negative indicators) was performed across the school’s public site copy and compared against the reference lists.  

How to read this report
- For each category, we list: (1) keywords found on the school site, (2) alignment to the Six Elements positive/negative keyword lists, (3) quantitative hit estimates, and (4) a category rating.

2) Alignment

- Negative: numerous terms aligning with experiential/project/skills‑first framing.

What would move the needle
- Publish classical pedagogy evidence:
  - Name and describe Socratic seminars, dialectic practices, and rhetoric (speeches/orations, debates) in Middle/Upper School syllabi and pages; show exemplars/theses. 
  - Surface memorization/recitation (poetry, catechism, hymns) and lower‑school chanting practices. 
  - Articulate “scholé/restful learning” and “wonder” as pedagogical commitments (festina lente; multum non multa). 
- Strengthen curriculum signaling:
  - Explicitly use Trivium/Quadrivium language; map courses to grammar/logic/rhetoric; spotlight Quadrivium elements where present. 
  - Replace “Social Studies” with “History” on elementary pages; highlight primary sources across grades. 
- Clarify telos over pragmatics:
  - Reframe AP/PSAT/“21st‑century skills” within a subordinate role to wisdom/virtue and the pursuit of truth, goodness, beauty (keep data, change emphasis/tone). 
- Faculty formation:
  - Publish a “Portrait of a Teacher” and evidence of ongoing classical pedagogy formation (e.g., Great Books seminars for faculty, classical workshops). 
- Parent formation:
  - Offer and advertise parent reading groups or classical education seminars; share reading lists (Great Books, liberal arts).
