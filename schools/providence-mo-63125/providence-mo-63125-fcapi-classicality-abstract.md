# ABSTRACT

Providence Academy (Providence Classical Christian Academy) in Saint Louis, MO 63125 demonstrates a CLASSICAL orientation across all six rubric categories: Religious and Metaphysical Foundations, School Purpose and Goal, Curriculum, Pedagogy, Faculty, and Parents & Community. The school’s public materials articulate a Christ-centered, theology-informed worldview; a formation-focused aim toward virtue, wisdom, and human flourishing; a curriculum anchored in liberal arts, the Trivium (grammar, logic, rhetoric), Great Books, Latin/Greek, and Western civilization; a pedagogy that blends contemplative (Scholé) practices with Socratic discussion and eloquence training; a faculty described as living curriculum and mentors; and a strong partnership with parents and the broader Christian community. The absence of explicit negative signals in the public materials further reinforces CLASSICAL status. The school’s emphasis on transparency, parental involvement, and public curriculum documentation aligns with classical education expectations for a community of learners formed for virtue and wisdom within a Christian framework. Overall, Providence (St Louis, MO, 63125) aligns closely with classical education principles and practices.
