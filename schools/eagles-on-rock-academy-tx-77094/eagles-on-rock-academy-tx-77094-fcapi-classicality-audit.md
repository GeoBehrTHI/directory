## Executive Summary

Eagles On The Rock Academy (Houston, TX 77094) presents a robust Classical/Education-with-a-Biblical-Worldview approach that aligns strongly with the Six Essential Elements of a Classical School. Across all six categories, the school demonstrates substantial Classical alignment through explicit use of the Trivium (Grammar Stage) in the Elementary program, a commitment to Great Books and a Biblical worldview, and a pedagogy that includes memorization, recitation, chanting, and Socratic-inflected discourse at appropriate levels. Public materials repeatedly emphasize Christ-centered formation, moral development, and a robust family/school partnership, which map to the Positive Keyword indicators in the Classical lexicon. The overall assessment for Eagles On The Rock Academy is CLASSICAL.
