Strengths

This aligns Element 1 (Religious and Metaphysical Foundations) and Element 2 (School Purpose and Goal) with CLASSICAL indicators. Evidence is seen in statements such as “Christ-centered, classical education that shapes students’ minds and hearts” and “we work closely with families, caregivers, and the local church” to support a holistic formation approach. 

Challenges
- The public-facing materials do not consistently enumerate core classical curriculum and pedagogy details (e.g., grammar/logic/rhetoric; Great Books/Great Conversation; Socratic seminars; scholé; a history-centered integrated curriculum). Element 3 (Curriculum) and Element 4 (Pedagogy) lack explicit, verifiable statements in the accessible content, resulting in a NOT CLASSICAL rating for those categories.
