## Executive Summary

Public materials on the school’s site emphasize a Christian formation orientation, partnership with families, and liturgical elements such as chapel and church involvement. The school also hosts a program called “The Well,” expressly designed to serve students with special needs within a Christ-centered framework, and it emphasizes weaving biblical truth into every part of the learning journey. This demonstrates clear Religious and Metaphysical Foundations and a formation-focused School Purpose.
